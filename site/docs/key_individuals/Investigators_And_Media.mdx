## Role in the investigation

Citizen investigators are **not suspects** — but their **source networks** (French official, NSA tip, witness footage) define which **key individuals** stay in public focus.

Every figure here is a **living private individual or public commentator**, discussed only for their **published work** — none is accused of a crime. Where an account frames a claim as opinion (e.g. Stew Peters on foreknowledge), we **preserve that framing** and do not restate it as fact.

Censorship counter-thread: [Censorship](/Censorship/overview) (X bans, FBI delete-video).

## Who shapes the public record on X

**Candace Owens** remains the highest-reach alternative-narrative voice (French/Israeli/Egyptian threads; patsy framing of Robinson). **Ian Carroll**, **Tony Seruga**, **MuppetMasher**, and others supply OSINT and video analysis. Mainstream and conservative media (Posobiec, Fox pool at prelim) push the **state's camera/DNA package**. Readers should **compare claims**, not merge them.
