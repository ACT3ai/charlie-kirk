# Key Individuals

A murder case is a **network of people** — victim, accused, security, officials, witnesses, decoys, donors, and investigators. This section groups them by **investigative role** so you can see who touches the killing **peripherally** without reading sixty separate bios. Every name here is here for one reason: Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025, and the [question of how](/Cause_of_Death/overview) still shapes who matters to the record. Full profiles: [People](/People/overview). Start with the [investigation index](./Investigation_Index).

The **inner ring** is [Charlie](/People/charlie-kirk), Erika, and [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) ([Central Figures](./Central_Figures)) — plus the **security SUV** and **[UVU police](/People/jeff-long)** who were supposed to keep the courtyard safe ([Security](./Security_And_Protection)). The **official ring** is **[Kash Patel](/key_individuals/Officials_And_Law_Enforcement)**, **[Spencer Cox](/key_individuals/Officials_And_Law_Enforcement)**, **[Tony Graf](/Locations/Fourth_District_Court_Provo)**, and **[Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent)'s [halted NCTC thread](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC)** — people who controlled press conferences, custody timing, gag orders, and foreign-lead follow-up.

The **peripheral ring** is where citizen investigation lives: **[Dylan Hope](/Locations/Construction_Site_785_College)** (all-black eyewitness), **[George Zinn](/distraction_people/George_Zinn)** and **Zachariah Qureshi** (distraction / early custody), the **[Hamptons donor names](./Donor_Meeting_Circle)**, and **[Candace / MuppetMasher / Seruga](./Investigators_And_Media)** who keep surfacing new documents. **[Mikey McCoy vs Blake Neff](/People/mikey-mccoy)** call-timing at **12:23–12:24** is a staff-level puzzle that may never require guilt — only **consistent timelines**.

Use **[Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index)** → pick your role cluster → open the linked **[People](/People/overview)** profile → follow outbound links into [Security_Team](/Security_Team/overview), [distraction_people](/distraction_people/overview), or [Tyler_Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview). We **do not** claim any living person committed a crime unless court-proven.
