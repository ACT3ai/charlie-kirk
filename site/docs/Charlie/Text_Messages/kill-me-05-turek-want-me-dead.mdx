# KM-05 — I Know They Want Me Dead

> "I know they want me dead… I feel like I'm under the gun… please pray for me."

**Instance 05** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

## Best understanding of when he said this

Best understanding: Turek’s primary on-record framing is **weeks before** SEPT 10 (broad hump across mid–late AUG). Separate packaging says **days before** or a SEPT 9 security exchange (see KM-12). A counter-claim says the “under the gun” and “want me dead” bubbles were **months apart**. Likelihood stays deliberately wide.

**Date range (working model):** Wide: early AUG → SEPT 9 · Primary claim: “weeks before” · Secondary: day-before / “days before”

## What is claimed

The **strongest-sourced** entry in this index by witness quality. [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek) is a named mentor who has described these texts **in his own voice, in public interviews**, rather than through anonymous leaks. The wording is a prayer request, not an accusation: Kirk reportedly wrote that he knew people wanted him dead and asked to be prayed for.

## How it entered the record

1. Turek recounts the texts in on-camera interviews after September 10, 2025.
2. Commentary compilations pick the phrase up and often merge it with the September 9 material.
3. Turek is one of the public figures whose account is cited as contradicting the phone denial (KM-11 context).

## Verification status

- **A named, identifiable witness** stands behind it — the highest bar cleared by anything in this set.
- **No screenshot has been published** by Turek in the sources reviewed here.
- The wording is about a **state of fear**, not about a specific plot, date, or named person.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** date this to September 9. Turek describes **weeks before**. A separate day-before exchange attributed to him is tracked as KM-12.
