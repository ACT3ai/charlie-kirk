## What is claimed

The **fuller, more conditional** version of the Israel-split fear: not a flat prediction but a hedged one ("I *think* they will kill me"). Investigation indexes date it **August 16, 2025** (row **KB155**), while a large share of public retellings and reels put it on **August 13** — the same day as KM-02 and KM-03.

## How it entered the record

1. Attributed to conversations with friends rather than to a document; see [Friend Conversation Claims](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/August_16_Go_Against_Israel/friend-conversation-claims).
2. Amplified heavily through Instagram and X reels that pose the question "when did he say this?" — secondary packaging, not primary record.
3. Frequently spliced after September 10 into the September 9 material, producing a false single-event story.

## Verification status

- **No native export or recording.** This is a recollected sentence.
- The **date is contested inside the record itself** — Aug 16 by index, Aug 13 by popular retelling.
- The hedge ("I think") is often dropped in re-postings, hardening a worry into a prediction.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** merge with KM-03. And do not let the Aug 13 / Aug 16 collision be used to argue the whole cluster is fabricated, or to argue it is corroborated — a date conflict proves neither.
