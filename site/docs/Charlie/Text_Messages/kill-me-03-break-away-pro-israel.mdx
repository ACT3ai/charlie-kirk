## What is claimed

The **shortest and most slogan-like** of the Israel-split quotes, dated **August 13, 2025** in investigation indexes (row **KB156**). Its compact form is exactly why it spread: it fits a graphic, a thumbnail, and a caption. That same compression is why its provenance is thin.

## How it entered the record

1. The line enters circulation in mid-August 2025 alongside the same-day Harrison Smith post (KM-02), which is a separate artifact.
2. It is picked up by high-engagement reels and meme accounts, often with no source attribution at all.
3. After September 10 it is retro-fitted into 48-hour and day-before timelines it does not belong to.

## Verification status

- **No screenshot, recording, or named first-hand witness** has been published for this exact wording.
- Whether it was spoken aloud or written has never been settled — see [Verbal vs Text vs Discord](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/Other_Fear_Channels/verbal-vs-text-vs-discord).
- Its August 13 date comes from investigation indexing, not from a timestamp on any document.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** merge with KM-04 ("if I go against Israel"). They are different sentences with different index rows and different dates, and several viral carriers date KM-04 to August 13 as well, which compounds the confusion.
