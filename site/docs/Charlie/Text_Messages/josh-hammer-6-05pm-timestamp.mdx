# Josh Hammer's 6:05 PM Post — A September 9 Timestamp

Almost everything in the final-day record is **described** rather than **shown**. Warnings
are recalled in interviews, texts are paraphrased on podcasts, and the hours they happened
in are reconstructed from memory. This page is about one of the few items that carries its
own clock reading on its face.

## What the screenshot shows

A post from the verified **@josh_hammer** account consisting of a single word — "based" —
quoting a [Donald Trump](/People/donald-trump) post dated **April 19, 2013** that reads:
"Should be public execution for all to see-you will end this bullshit fast!"

The screenshot's footer reads **6:05 PM · Sep 9, 2025 · 27K Views**.

<div style={{clear:'both'}} />

## Why the timestamp matters

The [circulated final-day timeline](./final-day-timeline-sept-9) places a Zoom call at
**6:00 PM** with Charlie Kirk, Josh Hammer, [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek), a rabbi,
and two Israeli officials. According to @idontgiveadom, this post went out **five minutes
into that meeting**.

That is worth isolating, because it is a different *kind* of evidence from everything
around it. The rest of the day is reconstructed testimony. This is a public post with a
machine-generated timestamp that anyone can still check. And it reads **September 9** —
which is the correction the [final-day timeline page](./final-day-timeline-sept-9) makes.
The graphic circulating with those hours on it is labeled September 10; a public post
inside the same hour block is dated September 9.

So the value here is narrow and real: **an independent, checkable clock reading that
anchors that evening to September 9, 2025.** It does not depend on anyone's memory, and
it does not depend on this site being right about anything else.

## What this page does not claim

This is where the page has to be careful, because the screenshot invites a reading it does
not support.

* **The 2013 Trump post is almost certainly about the Boston Marathon bombing.** April 19,
  2013 is the day of the Boston manhunt and the capture of the surviving bomber. Read in
  its own context, that post is a twelve-year-old reaction to a terror attack, not a
  statement about anyone in this investigation.
* **"Based" is ordinary internet approval slang**, used constantly and casually across
  political X. Nothing in the screenshot shows what the poster understood himself to be
  agreeing with, or that he had the events of the following day in mind.
* **A timestamp establishes when, never why.** That a post went out at 6:05 PM on
  September 9 says nothing about the content of any meeting, about who was on it, or
  about what anyone intended.
* **No claim is made here that Josh Hammer knew of, participated in, or foresaw any
  harm to Charlie Kirk.** He is a living person, he has not been charged with anything,
  and to our knowledge he has not been asked about the timing of this post. If he
  addresses it, that response belongs on this page.

The screenshot is being carried here for its **clock reading**, and for nothing else. A
reader who wants the timing argument should take the timestamp and leave the rest.

## Open verification steps

1. Pull the original post from the @josh_hammer account and confirm the timestamp against
   the archived copy rather than against the screenshot alone.
2. Confirm independently that the 6:00 PM Zoom call occurred and who was on it — currently
   sourced only to the circulated timeline.
3. Establish whether the timestamp displays in the viewer's local timezone or the poster's,
   which decides whether "6:05 PM" is Mountain, Eastern, or the reader's own.

Point 3 matters more than it looks: X renders timestamps in the **viewer's** timezone, so
the screenshot's 6:05 PM is 6:05 PM *to whoever took the screenshot*. Until the capturing
timezone is known, the five-minutes-into-the-call alignment is suggestive rather than
established. The **date** is the durable part; the **hour** is not yet nailed down.

### The two screenshots corroborate each other on the minute

This capture renders the first post as **18:05** on a 24-hour clock and reports **15K
views**. The earlier capture on this page renders the same post as **6:05 PM** on a
12-hour clock and reports **27K views**. Different locale settings, different capture
times, same account, same post — **and the same minute**. That is two independent
screen captures agreeing, which is a stronger position than the page held before.

### The timing: a few minutes into the meeting

The evening of **September 9, 2025** is when Charlie Kirk was on the call —
the Zoom or Discord meeting the [final-day timeline](./final-day-timeline-sept-9) puts at
**6:00 PM**, with Josh Hammer, [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek), a rabbi, and two Israeli
officials. That is the same stretch of hours in which Charlie was sending the messages this
section catalogues — [KM-06 "worried Israel will kill me"](./kill-me-06-worried-israel-kill-me),
[KM-07 "THEY ARE GOING TO KILL ME"](./kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps),
[KM-04 "go against Israel"](./kill-me-04-go-against-israel) — and the
[3:54 PM text about leaving the pro-Israel cause](./leave-pro-israel-whatsapp) sits earlier
the same day.

**18:05 is five minutes past 18:00.** So on the face of the two documents, the "based" post
was sent **a few minutes into that meeting** rather than before it or after it. The question
the record does not yet settle is whether it went out **during** the call — someone posting
from inside the meeting — or in the minutes immediately **after** it began and away from it.
Nothing in the screenshot distinguishes those two.

### The second post lands after the killing

Charlie Kirk was shot at roughly **12:20 PM Mountain** on September 10. The second panel is
stamped **15:57 · 9/10/25**. X renders timestamps in the **viewer's** timezone, so the exact
offset depends on where the capture was taken — but the direction does not: 15:57 in any US
timezone is after 12:20 PM Mountain. Eastern would put it at 13:57 Mountain, Mountain at
15:57, Pacific at 16:57. On every reading, "Public execution." was posted **hours after
Charlie Kirk was killed**, not before.

So the pair brackets the event: one post minutes into the September 9 call, one post the
afternoon of the killing. Both consist of the same phrase pulled from a twelve-year-old
Trump post about a terror manhunt.

### What this does and does not establish

The caveats in the section above still apply to panel 1, and they apply to panel 2 as well:
a timestamp fixes **when**, never **why**, and neither post says what its author had in mind.
Josh Hammer is a living person, has not been charged with anything, and to our knowledge has
not publicly addressed the timing of either post. If he does, that response belongs on this
page.

What is now on the record, and was not before, is the **second clock reading** and the fact
that the same two words were posted on both days.
