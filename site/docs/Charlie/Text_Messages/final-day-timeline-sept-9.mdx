# Final-Day Timeline — September 9, Not September 10

**This was September 9th, 2025, not September 10th.**

That is the correction this page exists to make. An hour-by-hour timeline of Charlie
Kirk's last full day has been circulating on X carrying the date **9/10/2025**. The
hours printed on the graphic itself rule that date out. The events belong to
**September 9, 2025** — the day before he was killed.

## Why the date on the graphic cannot be right

Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University at approximately **12:20 PM Mountain
Time on September 10, 2025**. Everything on this timeline from the early afternoon
onward is therefore impossible for that date:

* A **3:54 PM** text about leaving the pro-Israel cause.
* A **6:00 PM** Zoom call with Josh Hammer, Frank Turek, a rabbi, and two Israeli officials.
* A **6:48 PM** message to security about the left trying to kill him.
* A dinner **after 7:00 PM** with a priest, Erika, and his children — and a message to
  Andrew Kolvet during or after it.

By 3:54 PM on September 10 he had been dead for more than three hours. None of those
hours existed for him on the 10th. They existed on the **9th**.

## What that shifts

Read on the correct date, this is not a timeline of the day he died. It is a timeline of
**the day before** — the last full working day of his life, and the day on which the
site's existing record already places several of the strongest reported warnings:

* [KM-07 — "THEY ARE GOING TO KILL ME"](./kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps), dated September 9.
* [KM-12 — the security exchange](./kill-me-12-sept-9-security-exchange), also placed on
  or about September 9.
* [KM-08 — "they will kill me" to Andrew Kolvet](./kill-me-08-kolvet-they-will-kill-me),
  which matches the after-dinner entry on this graphic.
* [KM-11 — "The Left will kill me"](./kill-me-11-the-left-will-kill-me), which matches
  the 6:48 PM security entry.

The graphic, once redated, stops being a loose set of anecdotes and becomes an
**hour-by-hour ordering of a single day** — one in which a text about breaking with the
pro-Israel cause comes at 3:54 PM, a Zoom call with Israeli officials comes at 6:00 PM,
and a warning to his own security detail comes at 6:48 PM. The sequence is the point.
Under the wrong date that sequence is invisible.

## What this page does not claim

The timeline is a **citizen-assembled graphic**, not a court exhibit or an official
record. Individual entries on it are sourced to public commentary — several to
[Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet), one to Andrew Wilson, one to Erika Kirk's own
account of her afternoon. Nothing here establishes that any named person did anything
wrong, and the participants on the 6:00 PM call are identified only as they were
described publicly.

The correction on this page is narrow and specific: **the date label is wrong, and the
correct date is September 9, 2025.** That is a fact about the calendar, not an
allegation about anyone.
