## What is claimed

The only entry in the index that **names Israel in the first person**. KM-02 says Kirk *thinks* Israel will kill him but is a third-party relay; KM-03 and KM-04 are about "they" in a pro-Israel context. KM-06 is rendered as Kirk himself naming a state actor — which is precisely why its thin sourcing matters most here.

## How it entered the record

1. Appears in Israel-motive compilations, often attached to the Iran-war disagreement period; see [Iran War: Charlie vs Israel](/Israel_Main_Suspect/iran-war-charlie-vs-israel).
2. Also placed "one day before" in Tier 2 quote listings, which puts it adjacent to but distinct from the Sept 9 cluster.
3. Frequently displayed next to the KM-07 all-caps line as if the two were the same message.

## Verification status

- **No named first-hand recipient** has come forward with this exact sentence.
- **The date is unsettled** — "one day before" and "Iran-war period" are months apart.
- Because it names a state, this line carries the highest re-publication risk in the set and is presented here strictly as an **attributed statement**.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** treat this as a first-person confirmation of KM-02. The two have different speakers, different forms, and different evidentiary weight.
