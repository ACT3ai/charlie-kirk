## What is claimed

The **most verifiable artifact** in this index and the most misread. What is documented is a **public post**: [Harrison Smith](/People/harrison-smith) wrote on **August 13, 2025** that someone close to [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had told him Kirk believed Israel would kill him if he turned against them. The post is real and archived. The **underlying statement** is third-hand and its original speaker has never been named.

## How it entered the record

1. Harrison Smith posts the claim on X on August 13, 2025, explicitly declining to name his source.
2. The post accumulates very high engagement and is screenshotted into dozens of later compilations.
3. Smith publicly reaffirms it — reportedly with a one-word "yes" — on the day of the shooting.

## Verification status

- **The post is confirmed.** Anyone can read it; this site treats the post as an artifact, not as proof of what Kirk said.
- **The quote inside it is unverified.** It is a relay of a relay: Smith → unnamed intermediary → Kirk.
- This is a **paraphrase in the third person**, not a first-person Kirk quote. Compilations that render it as Kirk speaking are re-writing it.

## Do not confuse it with

This is **not** KM-03 or KM-04. Those are first-person quotes attributed directly to Kirk. KM-02 is a third party reporting what an unnamed fourth party said. Many viral graphics collapse all three into one line.
