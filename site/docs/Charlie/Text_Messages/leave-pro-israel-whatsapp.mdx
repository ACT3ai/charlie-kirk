# Leave Pro-Israel WhatsApp Chat


## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: authenticated donor WhatsApp about leaving the pro-Israel cause falls in the **~48 hours before** UVU, with packaging on **SEPT 8** and later **SEPT 9** re-dating claims. Not a kill-me string.

**Date range (working model):** SEPT 7–10 · ~48h WhatsApp · SEPT 8 vs SEPT 9 re-dating

This is the **best-authenticated** private-message set in the Charlie Kirk pre-death record. A **WhatsApp group chat** (often described as roughly nine participants) from about **September 8, 2025** contains lines in which Kirk vents about donor pressure and says he is being pushed toward leaving the pro-Israel cause. [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) spokesman [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet) has **publicly confirmed** the authenticity of this chat in commentary widely clipped on X.

## Claimed content (as reported)

Circulating wording, aggregated from Owens releases and secondary reporting, includes variants of:

- Losing a **huge / ~$2 million** donor for refusing to cancel [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson)
- **"Jewish donors play into all the stereotypes. I cannot and will not be bullied like this."**
- **"Leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause"** (or near-identical phrasing)

Exact punctuation differs across screenshots and recaps. Treat line-level wording as **reported**, not as a court-stipulated transcript.

## Why investigators treat this as the anchor

On X, posts after Owens published the material argue that the chat stopped being dismissible as a "hoax" once Kolvet confirmed it. Commentators including [Clint Russell](https://x.com/LibertyLockPod) have claimed Kolvet not only confirmed authenticity but also **shared screenshots with the FBI** as potential motive context — a claim about Kolvet's conduct, not a finding that any donor committed a crime.

This page does **not** assert that any donor, lobby, or foreign government ordered the assassination. The chat is evidence of **political and financial pressure** in the 48–72 hours before UVU, full stop.

## Relationship to "kill me" texts

The WhatsApp donor chat is **not** the same artifact as the September 9 **"they are going to kill me"** ([KM-07](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)) cluster. Master-file research and Owens's own retellings generally place:

| Set | Approx. date | Content type | Public confirmation |
|-----|--------------|--------------|---------------------|
| Donor WhatsApp | ~Sept 8 | Leave pro-Israel / donor bullying | Kolvet / TPUSA — **yes** |
| Kill-me warnings | ~Sept 9 | Fear of being killed | **No** full public screenshot dump; see [They Are Going to Kill Me](./they-are-going-to-kill-me) |

Conflating the two is a common error in viral posts.

## Investigator discourse on X

- Amplifiers treat the confirmed chat as motive-adjacent: break with donors → assassination ~48 hours later.
- Critics argue venting about donors is not a death threat or a foreign plot, and that selective screenshots omit pro-Israel context from Kirk's broader record.
- Grok and citizen threads note that **extreme meme versions** ("I've told all the Jews recently to go to hell") are **not** the same as the Kolvet-confirmed chat and should be filed under [Authenticity, Fakes & Memes](./authenticity-and-fakes).
