# KM-13 — Donor Told: Executed / Killed Tomorrow

> Charlie messaged **a donor** (plus TPUSA and security) that he was going to be **executed / killed tomorrow**.

**Instance 13** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

## Best understanding of when he said this

Best understanding: Owens multi-recipient packaging puts the **donor** (with TPUSA person + Flood) on the **night before** death — **SEPT 9**. Josh Hammer later re-dated the *leave pro-Israel cause* WhatsApp to SEPT 9 not SEPT 8; that is a related but different string. Kill-tomorrow to a donor remains night-before in the kill-me packaging.

**Date range (working model):** Peak: night SEPT 9 · Do not merge with ~48h leave-pro-Israel WhatsApp (SEPT 8/9 separate artifact)

## What is claimed

Candace Owens and secondary X accounts describe a **three-lane recipient structure** the night before the assassination:

1. A **donor**
2. Somebody at **TPUSA**
3. Head of security **[Dan Flood](/Security_Team/dan-flood)**

Charlie is said to have told them he would be **executed tomorrow** or that **they** were going to **kill him tomorrow**. Flood's lane is tracked in depth as [KM-10](./kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow) (and the competing "Left" wording as [KM-11](./kill-me-11-the-left-will-kill-me)). The TPUSA / Kolvet lanes are [KM-07](./kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)–[KM-09](./kill-me-09-three-people-warned) and [KM-08](./kill-me-08-kolvet-they-will-kill-me). **KM-13 isolates the donor recipient** so it is never silently dropped or merged into the authenticated ~48-hour WhatsApp about leaving the pro-Israel cause.

## How it entered the record

1. Candace Owens packaging: donor + TPUSA + Flood received night-before execute/kill-tomorrow type messages.
2. High-reach X posts (examples captured 2026 research): [@itsmorganariel](https://x.com/itsmorganariel/status/1998174175395991976), [@aldamu_jo](https://x.com/aldamu_jo/status/1999402991842132410) (~2M views on the latter), [@GoUncensored](https://x.com/GoUncensored/status/1998204184743837905).
3. Master investigation file section **"They were going to kill him TOMORROW"** records the multi-recipient claim alongside Flood-specific detail.

## Verification status

- **Donor is unnamed** in public packaging reviewed for this page.
- **No public screenshot** of a donor-bound kill-me / execute-tomorrow text is established here.
- **Do not confuse** with the [Kolvet-authenticated WhatsApp](./leave-pro-israel-whatsapp) (~48 hours before) about Jewish donors, bullying, and leaving the pro-Israel cause. That document is a **different artifact** with a **different evidence status**. It does not contain a court-authenticated "kill me tomorrow" string as its confirmed core.
- Living persons named only as alleged recipients or amplifiers are **not** accused by this site of planning or carrying out the assassination.

## Full claimed multi-recipient packaging (preserve)

Circulating formula (paraphrase of Owens ecosystem posts):

> Charlie Kirk messaged a donor, somebody at TPUSA, and the security guard standing to his right when he was executed — Dan Flood — telling them he was going to be executed **"tomorrow."** Charlie was killed the following day at UVU.
