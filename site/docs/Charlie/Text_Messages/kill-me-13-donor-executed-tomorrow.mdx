## What is claimed

The **third recipient lane** of the night-before warning. Retellings of the September 9 messages name three kinds of recipient: TPUSA staff, head of security, and a **donor**. The donor lane is tracked separately here so it does not get quietly absorbed into the **authenticated** leave-the-pro-Israel-cause WhatsApp — which involves donors but contains **no threat of violence at all**.

## How it entered the record

1. Owens-sourced retellings describe warnings going to donors, TPUSA members, and head of security together.
2. A donor is sometimes described as a "white knight" figure in those retellings, without ever being named.
3. Because the confirmed donor chat also exists, this lane is the easiest one for compilations to blur.

## Verification status

- **The donor has never been identified**, and no donor has come forward with a message.
- **No screenshot** exists for this lane any more than for KM-07 through KM-12.
- It is a **recipient claim**, not an independent message: the same alleged warning, described as reaching one more person.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** merge with the [leave-pro-Israel donor WhatsApp](./leave-pro-israel-whatsapp). That chat is Kolvet-authenticated and is about money and platform decisions — there is **no threat of violence in it**. KM-13 is the unverified claim that a donor was also warned of a killing.
