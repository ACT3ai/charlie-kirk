# Candace Owens Private Texts

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) is both a **primary publisher** of Charlie-attributed texts and a **named correspondent** in private threads. Friendship and AmFest context also live at [Candace Owens Friendship](/Charlie/Candace_Owens_Friendship); this page is the **message inventory** view.

## Revolution / legacy text (claim)

Owens has publicly shared a private text in which Charlie allegedly wrote that he was **not sure he would live to see the end of this revolution**, and that God meant him to meet Candace so she could finish the fight. Treat as **alleged** until device-authenticated. It frames martyrdom language, not a named assassin.

## September 9 AmFest invitation

Investigation notes and Owens commentary highlight that on **September 9, 2025** Charlie reportedly texted that he would **invite her to speak at AmFest** — read by investigators as "taking his platform back" after donor pressure to distance from Israel-critical voices including Owens and [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson).

## 2023 defense texts (2026 release wave)

In **July 2026** episodes (widely clipped on X), Owens released additional messages she dates to around **November 15, 2023**, in which Charlie allegedly:

- Urged her not to resign during a coordinated hit  
- Said the pressure was **not Ben acting alone** and that others "workshopped" it  
- Described certain figures as **"part of the tribe"** and told her not to listen  

Podcast recap accounts on X treat these as proof of a multi-year friendship and shared enemies list. **Authenticity disputes continue** in TPUSA-adjacent commentary; some posts claim Kolvet questioned older screenshots while still affirming the 2025 donor WhatsApp. This site does **not** assert criminal conduct by [Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro) or any named person.

## Extreme circulating graphics

Separate viral images attribute lines such as **"I told all the jews recently to go to HELL"** to Charlie→Candace threads. Grok and careful investigators note these often **do not match** the Kolvet-confirmed WhatsApp set and may be exaggerated or fabricated. File those under [Authenticity, Fakes & Memes](./authenticity-and-fakes).

## Defamation note

We do **not** claim Candace Owens knew of the assassination in advance, participated in any illegal act, or that named media figures murdered Charlie Kirk. Documentation of **published private-message claims** is not an accusation of murder.
