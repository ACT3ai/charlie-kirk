## What is claimed

A **two-sided exchange** rather than a single outbound warning, and the only entry that shows a response to a question. A friend reportedly wrote that they hoped Kirk had improved his security; Kirk answered that he had, and added that he knew they wanted him dead. The reply structure is what makes it distinct — it implies the fear was already known to the person asking.

## How it entered the record

1. Recorded in the Sept 9 meeting chronology alongside the other day-before items.
2. Sourcing is described as second-hand in the material where it appears.
3. Its "want me dead" phrasing matches KM-05, which is why the two are so often merged.

## Verification status

- **Second-hand.** No named participant has published the thread.
- The security half of the exchange has never been reconciled with what the security detail actually did or did not change before September 10.
- Its date is inherited from the surrounding Sept 9 chronology, not from a timestamp.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** merge with KM-05. Turek describes **weeks before** and a prayer request; KM-12 is a day-before exchange with a different structure and a different (unnamed) counterparty.


## Full claimed wording (expanded)

Two-sided exchange form:

> **Other party:** "I hope you improved your security." / "I hope you've increased your security detail."  
> **CK:** "I did." / "I have."  
> **CK:** "I know they want me dead."

On X this exchange is frequently presented as the **Frank Turek** thread (see high-engagement posts under [KM-05](./kill-me-05-turek-want-me-dead)). Site index also carries it as a day-before friend exchange with second-hand sourcing. Both framings are recorded; native metadata would settle counterparty and date.

## Research notes (2026-08-01)

- Structure is unique in the index: it is the only item that includes a **response to a security question**, implying the fear was already known to the asker.
- Counter-claim from [@nosoup4knowles](https://x.com/nosoup4knowles/status/1999926989982163056): Turek clip may stitch texts **months apart**; "they want me dead" allegedly about violence in the news, not a Sept 9 plot. Preserve that counter-claim next to the exchange.
- Security half of the exchange has never been reconciled with what the security detail actually changed before September 10 — see [Security Team](/Security_Team/overview) and [Dan Flood texts](./dan-flood-security-texts).

## Separation from KM-05

- **KM-05:** weeks-before prayer request / "under the gun" (Turek on record).  
- **KM-12:** day-before (or near-event) security exchange structure.  
Do not merge without a native thread proving one continuous conversation.
