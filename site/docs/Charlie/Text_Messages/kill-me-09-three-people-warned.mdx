# KM-09 — I Think They're Going to Kill Me (Three People)

> "I think they're going to kill me."

**Instance 09** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

## What is claimed

The **multi-witness claim**: that on September 9, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) told **three separate people** words to the effect that he thought they were going to kill him, and that all three could confirm it. This is the load-bearing item in the Israel-motive argument — and the point at which, on this site's own assessment, the documentary evidence stops.

## How it entered the record

1. Described by [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) across podcast appearances and her own program.
2. Repeated in aggregator posts claiming three separate people can confirm, with identification still pending.
3. Used by Israel-motive accounts as the escalation that converts the confirmed donor chat into a threat narrative.

## Verification status

- **None of the three has been publicly identified**, and none has produced a message.
- Owens has declined to name at least one recipient, described as a TPUSA staffer.
- The site's stated position: without this escalation, the confirmed donor material shows pressure and a business dispute — not a threat.

## Do not confuse it with

Three retellings of one claim are **not** three witnesses. Until the three are named or produce messages, this remains a single-source account describing multiple sources.

No living person named on this page is accused by this site of a crime or of wrongdoing.
Recipients, relayers, and commentators are listed because they appear in the sourcing chain.
