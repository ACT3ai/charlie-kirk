## What is claimed

The **only entry in the September 9 cluster with a named recipient**. Where KM-07 says Kirk texted "others," this one specifies [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet), reportedly right after an emergency Zoom involving Kirk, a rabbi, and Josh Hammer. That specificity makes it the most testable item in the cluster — and it has not been tested.

## How it entered the record

1. Reported in timeline reconstructions as the message sent immediately after the Sept 9 emergency Zoom.
2. Carried by large X accounts as "Kirk then texted Kolvet: they are going to kill me."
3. Kolvet is separately on record authenticating the **donor WhatsApp** — a different, confirmed document, which is why his name gives this line borrowed credibility.

## Verification status

- **The named recipient has not published a native export** of this message in the sources reviewed here.
- Wording varies between retellings ("They will kill me" / "they are going to kill me"), which is a sign of paraphrase, not of forgery.
- **Kolvet is not accused of any wrongdoing** by this site; he is listed only as a reported recipient.

## Do not confuse it with

Kolvet's confirmation of the **donor WhatsApp** is not a confirmation of **this** message. Compilations routinely borrow the credibility of the first to prop up the second.
