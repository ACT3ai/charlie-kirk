# KM-10 — Executed Tomorrow (Security Text)

> "…executed tomorrow. / "They" are going to kill me tomorrow."

**Instance 10** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

## Best understanding of when he said this

Best understanding: **night of SEPT 9, 2025**, after a donor phone call, Charlie texted security (Dan Flood) that they would kill/execute him **tomorrow** (SEPT 10). Packaging is consistent across Owens amplifiers; no native timestamp published.

**Date range (working model):** Peak: night of SEPT 9 after donor call · “Tomorrow” = SEPT 10

## What is claimed

The **most operationally specific** entry: not a general fear but a claim that Kirk told his **head of security**, the night before, that he expected to be killed **tomorrow** — reportedly after a donor phone call. If authenticated, this is the item with real legal consequence, because it would put a dated warning in the hands of the person responsible for protecting him.

## How it entered the record

1. Reported by [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), who is described as having displayed texts on air.
2. Repeated across security-analysis pages and in the [Security Team](/Security_Team/overview) section.
3. Named as a discovery target in the proposed [Fix Laws](/Fix/overview), including whether the phrase was used in connection with a specific call.

## Verification status

- **No public screenshot** of a Flood-bound message is established in the materials reviewed for this page.
- **Flood has not released a native export** confirming the wording in the sources cited here.
- **Dan Flood has not been charged with any crime.** Proximity on the day and prior employment history are context for open questions, not findings.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** read the wording variance ("executed tomorrow" vs "they will kill me tomorrow") as two separate messages. They are competing renderings of the same claimed text. The rival ["The Left will kill me"](./kill-me-11-the-left-will-kill-me) wording is tracked as KM-11, and the claim that an unnamed **donor** was warned the same night is tracked as [KM-13](./kill-me-13-donor-executed-tomorrow).
