# Charlie's Text Messages

Citizen investigators and mainstream commentators treat **Charlie Kirk's pre-death texts** as a central evidence lane: donor-pressure chats, fear-of-death warnings, and private friendship messages. This section separates those threads by **what was claimed, who confirmed it, and what remains unverified**. Nothing here is a court finding that any living person planned or carried out his death.

## Why Investigators Keep Returning Here

X threads after September 10, 2025 repeatedly argue that **private written messages** — not just public speeches — show Kirk under political and personal pressure in his final 72 hours. The strongest public anchor is a **WhatsApp donor chat** (about September 8) that [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) spokesman [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet) has **publicly confirmed as authentic**, including lines about losing a large donor over [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson) and feeling forced to leave the "pro-Israel cause." That confirmation is what later made broader "kill me" claims hard for audiences to dismiss out of hand.

The **kill-me cluster** is different. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek), Kolvet (as paraphrased in commentary), and others describe September 9 wording such as "they are going to kill me" ([KM-07](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)) or "I know they want me dead" ([KM-05](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-05-turek-want-me-dead)) — often said to travel over **Signal or Telegram**, not iMessage. No complete court-authenticated dump of those warning texts is public. [Erika Kirk](/Messages/erika-kirk-denial) has stated on Glenn Beck's show that she holds Charlie's phone and that those day-before messages **did not happen**. Investigators treat that contradiction as a preservation and credibility issue, not as proof of any crime by the widow.
