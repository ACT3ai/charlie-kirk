# Frank Turek Fear Texts

[Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek) — long described as a mentor and Christian-apologetics partner to [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) — is one of the **named on-record sources** for pre-death fear messaging. Investigator threads on X treat Turek as a **Tier-1 witness** because he has spoken in interviews rather than only through anonymous leaks. Dated-hub page: [Frank Turek — Want Me Dead](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/Other_Fear_Channels/frank-turek-want-me-dead) under [Fear & Kill Warnings](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/overview).

**Numbered claims:** **[Claim KM-05](./kill-me-05-turek-want-me-dead)** · **[Claim KM-12](./kill-me-12-sept-9-security-exchange)**. Full index: [They Are Going to Kill Me](./they-are-going-to-kill-me).

## Claimed text content

Public compilations attribute to Turek:

- A text **weeks before** the assassination: roughly **"I know they want me dead. I'm under the gun right now. Please pray for me."** — **[Claim KM-05](./kill-me-05-turek-want-me-dead)**
- Day-before / near-event warnings that **"they're going to take me out"** (some retellings blend text and spoken conversation) — **[Claim KM-12](./kill-me-12-sept-9-security-exchange)**
- Full exchange form on X: prayer / under the gun → security hope → "I know they want me dead" (see KM-05 / KM-12 for carriers and months-apart edit counter-claim)

Exact punctuation varies by clip. No full native export is public on this site.

## Why it matters for the text-message map

Turek's account is independent of Owens's TPUSA-staffer narrative and of the **Kolvet-confirmed donor WhatsApp**. If authentic, it shows fear language circulating on a **mentor channel**, not only among political staff. It also collides with [Erika Kirk's denial](./erika-kirk-denial) that day-before kill-me messaging occurred.

## Investigator use on X

- Compilations list Turek next to Kolvet, Flood, Harrison Smith, and Candace as "multiple people close to Charlie."  
- Critics note memory and interview paraphrase risks; they want the original thread.  
- No court has ruled Turek's recollection false or true.
