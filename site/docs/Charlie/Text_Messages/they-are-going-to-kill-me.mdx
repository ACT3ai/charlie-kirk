# They Are Going to Kill Me Texts


## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: the all-caps / day-before kill-me cluster concentrates on **SEPT 9**. See Claim KM-07 deep page for full sourcing.

**Date range (working model):** SEPT 8–10 · Peak SEPT 9 (reuses km_07 chart)

The chart below is **not** a government finding. Bar height is **relative likelihood** from public claims. Domain is trimmed to non-trivial likelihood.

The single most-cited **unauthenticated** text claim in the case: that on **September 9, 2025** — during or after a combative meeting — [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) messaged associates wording such as **"THEY ARE GOING TO KILL ME"** or **"I think they're going to kill me."** Timeline depth lives at [Timeline: They Are Going to Kill Me Text](/Timeline/they-are-going-to-kill-me-text). This page is the **message-set** view for the Charlie text-messages section. For the **September 9 cluster inside the dated fear hierarchy**, see [Sept 9 Kill-Me Cluster](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/Other_Fear_Channels/sept-9-kill-me-cluster). Do **not** merge this with the mid-August Israel slogans ([Aug 13](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/August_13_Break_Away/overview) / [Aug 16](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/August_16_Go_Against_Israel/overview)) or the [~48-hour donor WhatsApp](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/Sept_48_Hours_Donor_Texts/overview).

## The numbered index — every alleged kill-me statement

Every distinct statement in which [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) is alleged to have said he
would be killed carries a permanent two-digit ID (**KM-01** through **KM-13**). The ID never
changes and is never reused. Wherever one of these statements appears anywhere on this site, it
links back to its own Level 4 page, so a reader can always check what the actual sourcing is before
accepting the line. Call them **Claim KM-01**, **Claim KM-02**, … when reading other pages — the
hyperlink is the source of truth for that claim number.

Every entry below is **alleged**. None has been authenticated by a published native export,
carrier record, or law-enforcement filing except where the row says otherwise.

## Channel claims

Compiled notes state some threat messages used **Signal and Telegram**, not iMessage — important for forensic recovery if devices are examined. See [Signal & Telegram Channels](./signal-telegram-channels).

## Denial

[Erika Kirk](/Messages/erika-kirk-denial) told Glenn Beck that Charlie was **not** messaging people the day before saying they were going to kill him, and that she has his cell phone. Full treatment: [Erika Kirk Phone Denial](./erika-kirk-denial).

## X investigator posture

- Owens ecosystem and amplifiers treat multi-witness consistency (Kolvet, Turek, Flood narrative, Candace display of *some* texts) as mutually reinforcing.  
- Skeptics note: **confirmed** material is the donor WhatsApp; kill-me lines remain description-without-screenshot.  
- Israel-motive accounts on X often splice kill-me language with the donor chat as if they were one document — they are not.
