## What is claimed

The **anchor line of the entire case narrative** and the most-cited unauthenticated text in it. Rendered in all caps, reportedly sent **during or just after** the September 9 combative meeting, and used by nearly every compilation as the hinge between donor pressure and the assassination the following day. It is also the line with the widest gap between how confidently it is repeated and what has actually been produced.

## How it entered the record

1. Enters circulation through commentary describing the September 9 meeting; see [the Sept 9 high-pressure meeting](/Timeline/high-pressure-meeting-sept-9).
2. Amplified into a 72-hour Sept 8–10 template by large accounts including [@AdameMedia](/Influencers/x/adamemedia).
3. Picked up by mainstream outlets quoting the Owens account, which is how the line reached general audiences.
4. Referenced by name in the proposed [Fix Laws](/Fix/overview) as a discovery target — the records that would authenticate or disprove it.

## Verification status

- **No law-enforcement filing published to date** has authenticated this string as a Kirk SMS, Signal, or Telegram export.
- **No screenshot or carrier record** has been published.
- It is contradicted by the phone denial — see KM-11 context and [Erika Kirk Phone Denial](./erika-kirk-denial).

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** collapse this with the **Kolvet-authenticated** donor WhatsApp about leaving the pro-Israel cause. Those are different claim clusters with different evidence status. The donor chat is confirmed; KM-07 is not.
