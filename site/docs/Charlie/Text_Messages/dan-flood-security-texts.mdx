# Dan Flood Security Texts


## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: Flood execute-tomorrow packaging is **night of SEPT 9**. Reuses km_10 chart.

**Date range (working model):** SEPT 8–10 · Peak SEPT 9 night (reuses km_10)

The chart below is **not** a government finding. Bar height is **relative likelihood** from public claims. Domain is trimmed to non-trivial likelihood.

[Dan Flood](/Security_Team/dan-flood) is repeatedly named as [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s **head of security** and as a **recipient** of night-before fear texts. Candace Owens and secondary X accounts claim Charlie told Flood he thought he would be **"executed tomorrow"** ([KM-10](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)) or that **"they" were going to kill him** ([KM-07](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)) after a donor call. **Flood has not been charged with any crime** related to the assassination; this page documents **claims about messages**, not guilt.

**Numbered claims:** **[Claim KM-10](./kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)** · **[Claim KM-11](./kill-me-11-the-left-will-kill-me)** (rival "The Left" wording) · **[Claim KM-13](./kill-me-13-donor-executed-tomorrow)** (donor multi-recipient lane). Full index: [They Are Going to Kill Me](./they-are-going-to-kill-me).

## Claimed sequence (commentary)

1. Charlie is on a tense call with a donor / pressure meeting context on September 9.  
2. He texts security (**"Dan" / Flood**) that they will kill him **tomorrow** — **[KM-10](./kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)**; rival wording **"The Left will kill me"** — **[KM-11](./kill-me-11-the-left-will-kill-me)**.  
3. Same packaging often says a **donor** and a **TPUSA** person also received execute/kill-tomorrow messaging — donor lane **[KM-13](./kill-me-13-donor-executed-tomorrow)**.  
4. Flood is later described as standing near Charlie when the shot is fired; some video analysts claim he stepped aside — a separate, contested visual claim filed under security analysis, not text authentication.

## Verification status

- **No public screenshot** of a Flood-bound kill-me text is established in the materials reviewed for this page.  
- Flood has not, in sources cited here, released a native export confirming the wording.  
- Commentary also notes Flood's **prior association with Benjamin Netanyahu's security team** (as reported by commentators). That résumé fact is used online to imply conflict of interest; it is **not** proof of involvement in the killing.

## Investigator questions on X

- Why has Flood not publicly produced the thread if it exists?  
- Was the message Signal, SMS, or verbal only?  
- How does this interact with missing [911 / SUV audio](/Charlie/Phone_Calls/suv-911-audio) claims?

## Defamation note

We do **not** assert that Dan Flood planned, facilitated, or failed to prevent the assassination as a criminal act. Proximity and prior employment are **context for open questions**, not findings.
