# KM-11 — The Left Will Kill Me

> "The Left will kill me."

**Instance 11** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

## What is claimed

A **rival rendering of the same message as KM-10**, and the single most consequential word swap in the entire index. If the night-before text named *"they"* in a donor-pressure context, it feeds the Israel-split narrative. If it named *the Left*, it points the other direction entirely and fits the lone-actor framing. The investigation notes that carry this version record it **with an explicit uncertainty marker**, and this site keeps it for exactly that reason.

## How it entered the record

1. Recorded in investigation notes as an Erika-attributed characterization of what the Flood message said, flagged as uncertain in the note itself.
2. Rarely carried by the compilations, because it cuts against the dominant narrative.
3. Sits directly alongside the broader phone denial — see [Erika Kirk Phone Denial](./erika-kirk-denial).

## Verification status

- **Lowest-confidence entry in the index.** It is preserved as a recorded variant, not asserted as the true wording.
- It is **not corroborated** by any published screenshot, and neither is KM-10.
- This site does **not** call any party to this dispute a liar. Two accounts of one unseen message are in conflict, and that is the finding.

## Do not confuse it with

This is not a separate message. Treating KM-10 and KM-11 as two texts would double-count a single unverified claim — the exact failure mode this numbered index exists to prevent.
