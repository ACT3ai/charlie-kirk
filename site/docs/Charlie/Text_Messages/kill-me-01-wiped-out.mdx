# KM-01 — I Might Get Wiped Out at Any Time

> "Since the beginning of TPUSA I knew in my gut that I might get wiped out at any time."

**Instance 01** in the numbered index of alleged fear-of-death statements attributed to
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). The full index, with every instance side by side, is on
[They Are Going to Kill Me Texts](./they-are-going-to-kill-me). Every statement on this page is
**alleged** and is presented as a **reported claim**, not as an established fact.

Best understanding: long-running premonition language; strongest public dating puts related Candace texts around **2018**, with the wording claiming a gut feeling since TPUSA founding (~2012). Not a SEPT 9 item.

## What is claimed

The **earliest-in-tone** item in the numbered set. Rather than naming a threat actor or a date, the message is a standing premonition: that from the founding of [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) onward [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) believed he could be "wiped out at any time," and that he dreamed about it repeatedly. It is the only entry in this index that is **not** tied to the Israel-split storyline.

## How it entered the record

1. Screenshot captures of a private text thread surfaced during the Candace Owens release wave and were re-posted widely.
2. The companion capture in the same set adds revolution/legacy language ("I will live to see the end of this revolution").
3. No named witness has narrated this one on camera the way [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek) narrated KM-05; it travels as an image.

## Verification status

- **Image exists** — unlike most of this index, there is a circulating screenshot rather than a description only.
- **Native export not published.** Device, thread, and timestamp metadata have not been released.
- Because it is undated, it cannot be used to date a threat — only to show a long-standing state of mind.

## Do not confuse it with

Do **not** date this to September 2025 or fold it into the Sept 9 cluster (KM-07 through KM-09, KM-12). It is a general premonition, not a warning about a specific person or week.


## X / Twitter posts on when this was likely said

Exact public claims and implications about **timing** (not a complete archive — these are the dated anchors used for the likelihood chart above):

- **[@grok](https://x.com/grok/status/2075315410077958396)**: "2018 texts to Candace Owens: Kirk wrote he had a gut feeling since TPUSA started that he “might get wiped out at any time” and wasn’t sure he’d “live to see the end of this revolution.”"
- **Master investigation notes**: "“Since the beginning of TPUSA, I knew in my gut that I might get wiped out at any time... I cannot explain it... But I dream about it all the time... Like all the time...” – Charlie Kirk. Screenshot. Probably to Candace."

More raw date research is kept in the investigation master file and the on-page X quotes above.
