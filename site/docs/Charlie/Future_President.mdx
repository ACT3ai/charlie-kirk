# Future President

A large body of public commentary — especially on **X/Twitter after September 10, 2025**, and in quieter form before — treated [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) as someone with a **realistic long-run path to the presidency of the United States**. People said he was **"on track"** for a **2028 or 2032** run, that he would have been **"presidential material,"** that **"they killed a future President,"** or that he could have been a **VP pick** in a post-Trump cycle. Others rooted the same idea in **institutions**: the [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Turning Point Action machine, Trump-world access, Gen Z name recognition, fundraising capacity, and the [Arizona governor](./Future_Governor) conversation as a springboard.

:::note What this page does and does not claim
This page **collects attributed claims and structural path arguments** so readers can evaluate how widely the "future president" idea circulated. It does **not** assert that Kirk would have won a nomination or general election, that he had secretly decided to run, or that any person or government killed him because of a presidential future. Motive theories that use the "future president" frame are documented as **discourse**, not as findings.
:::

## Why the claim felt plausible to so many people

Supporters of the "very good chance" view usually stacked several facts and inferences:

1. **National youth infrastructure** — TPUSA and Turning Point Action organized campuses, voter registration, ballot chasing, and precinct work at a scale allies called unmatched on the right for Gen Z.
2. **2024 outcome proximity** — After Trump's reelection, many X posts credited Kirk's GOTV operation as among the most important non-candidate forces in the win.
3. **Pressure power inside the GOP** — Kirk did not need a Senate seat to threaten senators; he could threaten **primaries** with a ready field organization.
4. **Name ID + money** — The same local-NBC argument used for a possible Arizona governor run (name recognition and fundraising) scales upward in presidential speculation.
5. **Age window** — Born **October 14, 1993**, he would turn **35 on October 14, 2028** — constitutionally old enough for the presidency in the **2028** cycle (and a more conventional age by **2032**).
6. **State stepping-stone** — Pollsters and Phoenix media had already **floated him for Arizona governor** in 2024–2025 (see [Future Governor](./Future_Governor)); American political culture often treats swing-state governorship as a White House ramp.

None of those points alone proves a future presidency. Together they explain why **credible-sounding path talk** and **high-emotion post-death claims** both spread.

## Constitutional age: the 2028 talking point

U.S. Const. art. II requires a president to be at least **thirty-five years of age**. Kirk's date of birth is widely reported as **October 14, 1993**.

| Cycle | Approximate age | Online claim pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2028** | Turns 35 on Oct 14, 2028 (before Jan 2029 inauguration) | "Old enough in 2028"; "interesting timing"; first fully eligible cycle |
| **2032** | ~38–39 | More common first presidential run age in modern practice |
| **4–8 years from 2025** | Mid-to-late 30s | "President or VP-pick in 4–8 years" formulation |

X examples of the age frame (post-assassination and 2026 recirculation):

* Users stating **"Charlie Kirk would have been 35 years old in 2028, OLD ENOUGH TO BECOME PRESIDENT"** (e.g. replies in Candace Owens–adjacent threads, Jul 2026).
* Accounts citing **@BasedSamParker** and others: the age eligibility point was among the first things some investigators/commentators "realized" when reconstructing path and motive — **that is their claim**, not a court finding.

Pre-death, Kirk did **not** publicly file a presidential campaign. The age math is **eligibility**, not a declared candidacy.

## Post–September 10, 2025: the "future president" wave on X

Within hours and days of the assassination, "future president" language went from occasional fan wishcasting to a **dominant grief-and-politics meme**. Below are **specific formulations** captured from X (engagement numbers are as returned by search tools at collection time — approximate).

### "They killed a future President"

Accounts recirculated video of **Vice President JD Vance** with Kirk's casket / **Air Force Two** with captions such as:

> "They killed a future President… Vice President JD Vance helped load Charlie Kirk's casket onto Air Force Two."

Example amplifier: **@defense_civil25** (US Homeland Security News branding) and many copy accounts. The **video** of the VP role in the departure is documentary; the **"future President"** label is **interpretation layered on top**.

### "On-track… president or VP-Pick in 4–8 years"

**@FromKulak** (writer associated with *Anarchonomicon*), Sep 11, 2025 (~2.3k likes, ~30k views on the sampled post):

> "someone pointed this out... Charlie Kirk was actually on-track that he could have been president or VP-Pick in 4-8 years.
>
> It could be an extended play to try to decapitate post-Trump MAGA before it can form and actual young non-corrupted people could get in."

This post is useful because it states the **succession theory** explicitly: Kirk as a **post-Trump MAGA generational option**, not only a campus activist.

### "Being considered as a Presidential Candidate possibly for 2028"

**@Christo21700560** (Christopher Robert Pearson), Sep 10–11, 2025 (higher-engagement version ~3.8k likes, ~112k views):

> "Charlie Kirk was being considered as a Presidential Candidate possibly for 2028. We are dealing with an assassination by design. We want to know who the assassin is and who the assassin is working for."

Here **"being considered"** is asserted without a named campaign committee or Trump endorsement in the post itself. Treat as **user claim**, high emotional reach.

### "Could have easily run… And he'd have probably won"

**@ATEOutdoors** (Aaron Blount), Sep 10, 2025:

> "From the few snippets of videos I've seen from Charlie Kirk, he could have easily run for President eventually. And he'd have probably won."

Plain **electability optimism** from a user reacting to clips — representative of thousands of similar replies.

### "We feel like we lost a vice president"

**@DavidRosler4**, Sep 10, 2025:

> "God bless you, young Charlie. We feel like we lost a vice president. But you are with God."

VP framing is a **softened** version of the same elevation: national executive tier, not merely influencer tier.

### Sustained 2026 recirculation

Months later, the same phrases still appear:

* "Charlie Kirk was going to be the future president" (repeated posts, often tied to disputed foreign-policy motive theories).
* "who would've made for a great future president."
* "He had my vote if he ever ran for President."
* "I use to joke with my fiancé that @charliekirk11 would be President someday!!"
* "They took a future president off the table."
* Pairing claims: Kirk president / **Candace Owens** vice — speculative ticket fanfiction, not a campaign.

Credibility rule for readers: **volume ≠ verification**. The volume shows the **idea was mainstream inside large online communities**; it does not by itself prove electoral inevitability.

## Institutional path arguments (higher-credibility building blocks)

These items are better sourced than anonymous "he would have won" posts. They support the **path**, not a guaranteed finish line.

### A. Turning Point as a national political machine

Allies described TPUSA/TPAction as:

* Campus chapters at scale
* Professional media (The Charlie Kirk Show)
* Electoral operations (registration, chase, precinct recruitment)
* Willingness to **primary** sitting Republicans who blocked Trump's cabinet or agenda

Example of leverage talk that went mega-viral: Nov 2024 clips of Kirk warning senators to **"give Trump his cabinet"** or face Turning Point activation even in deep-red states (widely amplified, e.g. large BehizyTweets-style posts with multi-million view counts). That is the behavior of a **factional power broker** — the same skill set people map onto presidential politics.

X grief posts after the assassination repeatedly said he was **"instrumental"** or even **"THE MOST influential"** non-candidate force in the 2024 victory. Those are **supporter attributions**.

### B. Trump-world access without being the nominee

Kirk spent years as a **surrogate, organizer, and youth face** of Trumpism. That is not the same as Trump anointing him for 2028. What it did provide was:

* National airtime and donor networks
* Practice message-testing with MAGA primary voters
* A reason pollsters and TV producers already treated him as a **serious Republican player**

### C. Arizona governor as documented intermediate step

The most **poll-and-TV-grounded** "office path" is state-level:

* **Nov 25, 2024 — KPNX 12News (Phoenix NBC):** rumors Kirk wanted to run for **Arizona governor**; segment language stressed money and name ID (amplified by Nick Sortor, Eric Daugherty, Mario Nawfal, Leading Report, etc.).
* **Feb 2025 NPI poll (KTAR):** in a hypothetical GOP primary field, Kirk **tied Andy Biggs at 14%**.
* **May 2025 NPI / Newsweek:** Kirk still polled around **17%**; Newsweek wrote he had been **"floated as a potential gubernatorial candidate"**; he then **endorsed Biggs** rather than running; Newsweek noted he **"never made public comments indicating he planned to"** run for governor.

Full detail: **[Future Governor](./Future_Governor)**.

Presidential-path claims often sound like: **governor of Arizona (2026/2030) → national ticket (2028/2032/2036)**. When Kirk chose **Biggs** publicly, he demonstrated **kingmaker** mode at the state level even while others still imagined **candidate** mode for him nationally.

### D. Debate-stage celebrity

Years of campus events, viral Q&A clips, and conflict with both left activists and right-flank critics ([Groyper Wars](./Groyper_Wars)) built a recognition profile closer to a national politician than a typical nonprofit CEO. Supporters treat that as **campaign infrastructure already half-built**; critics treat it as **media celebrity without governing experience**. Both readings appear in the discourse.

Honesty about those gaps **increases** credibility of the page: the strong claim is **"people believed he had a very good chance and built that belief on real institutional assets,"** not **"the nomination was locked."**

## Dual tracks people confuse

After September 10, online conversation mixed four different futures:

1. **Kirk as future president** (this page).
2. **Kirk as future Arizona governor** ([Future Governor](./Future_Governor)).
3. **Kirk as permanent kingmaker** who elects others (Biggs Project 2026, Senate primaries).
4. **Successors** — Erika Kirk at TPUSA, Vance as 2028 favorite in other threads, Biggs as Arizona vehicle.

Only (1) is the subject here. (2) and (3) are **supporting evidence for capacity**. (4) is what happened when (1) became impossible.

## Why this page is under Charlie (investigation context)

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) is the victim of the UVU assassination. Documenting the **"future president"** discourse matters because:

* It shows how **large** parts of the public ranked his political ceiling — far above "campus speaker."
* It intersects [motive](/Motive/overview) **speculation** (people claim he was killed *because* of that ceiling). Those claims must be **separated from evidence**.
* It sits next to the more evidence-dense [Future Governor](./Future_Governor) path and the [pre-event pressure](./Pre_Event_Timeline) record.

Readers should keep three layers distinct:

1. **What institutions and media actually did** (polls, TV rumors, org power).
2. **What Twitter said in grief and theory** ("future president").
3. **What can be proven about the assassination** (elsewhere on this site — ballistics, cameras, court, cover-up claims).

Layer (2) is loud. Layer (1) is real. Layer (3) is not settled by (2).
