# Bonhoeffer and Martyr Rhetoric

The Lance Wallnau interview and reaction ecosystem (including "raise up a thousand martyrs" / Bonhoeffer framing) is heavily clipped under [Charlie on His Own Death videos](/Videos/Vid_Charlie_Kirk/Vid_Charlie_His_Own/overview). After September 10, viewers re-hear those lines as **foresight**.
The rhetoric only became notorious because Charlie Kirk was in fact assassinated weeks later at Utah Valley University, and the [cause-of-death record](/Cause_of_Death/overview) is still contested.

## Competing readings

1. **Foreshadowing** — he knew a hit was plausible; private kill-me texts match.  
2. **Genre** — Christian activist speech often invokes martyrdom without specific threat intel.  
3. **Selective editing** — reaction channels over-weight seconds of a long interview.

Pair with the private [revolution text to Candace](/Charlie/Text_Messages/candace-owens-private-texts) ("not sure I will live to see the end of this revolution") without treating either as a suicide note or a confession by third parties.
