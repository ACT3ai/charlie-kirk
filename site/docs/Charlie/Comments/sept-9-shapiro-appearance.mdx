# September 9 Shapiro Appearance

Unlike private texts, Charlie's **September 9, 2025** appearance on **[Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro)'s** program is a **public tape**. Timeline pages note he voiced **sharper criticism of Israeli policy** to Shapiro directly the day before UVU.
It is the last major broadcast made before Charlie Kirk was killed the following afternoon, which is why it is read so closely alongside the [cover-up record](/CoverUp/overview).

## Why investigators prioritize it

- No screenshot authentication fight — the show exists.  
- Sits on the same calendar day as alleged [kill-me texts](/Charlie/Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me) and high-pressure meetings.  
- Feeds later narratives about Shapiro-org security [phone claims](/Charlie/Phone_Calls/shapiro-security-call) — those are separate and must not be smuggled in as proven by the interview alone.

## Defamation note

Appearing as a guest who disagrees on policy is **not** evidence Shapiro harmed Charlie. Keep political conflict and criminal accusation strictly separate.
