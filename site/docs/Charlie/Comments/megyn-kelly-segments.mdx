# Megyn Kelly Segments

Megyn Kelly interviews and joint stages are a major **clip source** for investigator montages. Master-file references include a [zebboykin compilation](https://x.com/zebboykin/status/1986806479718871495) and segments where Charlie discusses donor pressure and larger system questions.

## Common clip uses

- Donor / stereotype language paralleling WhatsApp  
- Post-Hamptons venting (as described in commentary)  
- Intelligence-involvement hypotheticals in short excerpts (see site video pages under Influencers clips)

## Method

Always open the **full segment** before concluding Charlie "admitted" a conspiracy. Interview banter and clipped "gotcha" frames diverge. Prefer primary YouTube/SiriusXM releases over X text cards.
