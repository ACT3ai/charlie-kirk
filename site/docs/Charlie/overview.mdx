# Charlie Kirk

Charlie Kirk was the **central victim** of the September 10, 2025 assassination at
[UVU](/UVU/overview). This section documents what he was **reportedly doing and saying** in his
final weeks — donor friction, Epstein advocacy, fear-of-death texts, and day-of security — not
his biography ([People](/People/charlie-kirk)) or autopsy ([Medical](/Medical/Autopsy/overview)).
Five communication hubs organize the primary material: [Text Messages](./Text_Messages/overview),
[Fear & Kill Warnings](./Fear_Kill_Warnings/overview) (dated Aug 13 / Aug 16 / ~48-hour incidents),
[Discord Messages](./Discord_Messages/overview), [Phone Calls](./Phone_Calls/overview), and
[Comments](./Comments/overview). Start with the [investigation index](./Charlie_Investigation_Index).

If [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) did not act alone — or did not fire the killing shot — motive analysis starts
with **who Charlie was becoming** in mid-2025. Compiled research describes a nationally visible
conservative leader who was **breaking with pro-Israel donor orthodoxy**, pushing **Epstein files**
release, platforming [Tucker Carlson](/Israel/Tucker_Carlson_Israel), and privately texting that
**["they will kill me"](./Text_Messages/kill-me-08-kolvet-they-will-kill-me)** ([Claim KM-07](./Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)–[KM-13](./Text_Messages/kill-me-13-donor-executed-tomorrow); full [KM index](./Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me)). Those claims are contested; this section organizes them for evaluation
alongside forensic and institutional evidence elsewhere on the site.

The substance is not biographical but **pressure-shaped**: a July 12 reported **Trump reprimand**
over Epstein coverage, a **Hamptons intervention**, alleged **phone surveillance**, a **DOGE audit**
order one week before the event, and a burst of **September 9** warnings and texts. Day-of context
includes a **lighter security detail**, **denied counter-drone** requests while a **media drone**
flew, and [UVU institutional gaps](/UVU/Campus_Security_Gaps) that left six campus officers
covering thousands of attendees.
