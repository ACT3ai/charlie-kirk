# TPUSA and Campus Discord Claims

Beyond the killer's gaming servers, some citizen threads discuss **Discord as youth infrastructure**: campus clubs, TPUSA chapters, and informal political servers. Hard primary sourcing is thin; this page records **what is claimed** so it can be confirmed or retired.

## Claim themes on X

- TPUSA or adjacent organizers used Discord for event logistics and student outreach.  
- Hostile actors monitored or infiltrated those spaces before September 10.  
- UVU-area student Discords carried threat chatter that was not escalated — overlapping [GRAMA / pre-event warning](/CoverUp/UVU_GRAMA_Records) themes more than Discord exports.

None of these themes, as of public materials reviewed here, produce a **court exhibit of Charlie-authored Discord posts** comparable to the WhatsApp donor chat.

## How to investigate productively

1. Ask for **server IDs, invite links, and date-bounded exports** — not vibes.  
2. Separate **chapter-run servers** from **Robinson gaming servers**.  
3. Cross-check any "threat chat" claim against UVU police GRAMA releases.  

## Defamation note

No living TPUSA staffer is accused here of running an illegal Discord operation or of facilitating murder. Organizational chat tools are normal; the open question is **whether relevant logs exist and were preserved**.
