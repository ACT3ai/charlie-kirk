Sam Parker ([@BasedSamParker](https://x.com/BasedSamParker)) published an August 6, 2026 post arguing
that Charlie Kirk's own communications are the strongest untapped evidence in the case — that
"Charlie Kirk is his own star witness." The claims below are Parker's assertions, reproduced
nearly verbatim and attributed to him; this website does not assert them as proven fact.

## The Post, Near-Verbatim

According to @BasedSamParker:

> Charlie Kirk is his own star witness
>
> FACT: Last August, Charlie Kirk was on private record actively telling people that the blue&white country would off him if he ever turned on it.
>
> FACT: About a week later, also last August, Charlie Kirk was on public record declaring that agents of the pro-blue&white country were bullying him & trying to morally blackmail him.
>
> FACT: Roughly a couple weeks after that, the day before he was offed, Charlie Kirk explicitly declared--to direct-connect agents of the blue&white country--that he was leaving the pro-blue&white cause.
>
> FACT: That same day, these blue&white agents then held a Zoom call with Charlie.
>
> FACT: That evening, Charlie messaged multiple people "They're going to k*ll me."
>
> FACT: Charlie Kirk had trouble sleeping that night. (I wonder why?)
>
> FACT: For weeks, those same blue&white agents obscured & omitted (some would say "LIED") about Charlie saying he was leaving the pro-blue&white cause, and reinforced the idea that he was NOT leaving the pro-blue&white cause.
>
> FACT: Before then, and ever since, the same people from the blue&white country have also relentlessly attacked Tucker Carlson and Candace Owens--two people whom Charlie was already deliberately giving his platform to (Tucker) or saying he was going to re-platform (Candace).
>
> Charlie's communications would shed light and context on every one of these facts. Journal entries. Text messages. DMs. Telegrams. Voice notes & messages. Emails. Memos, etc. Why isn't anyone producing a single receipt or message with respect to these facts? The universal, monolithic lack of curiosity from those with access isn't natural.

## How This Maps to Documented Claims on This Site

Parker's "blue&white" framing refers, in his wording, to a foreign country. The individual claims
he lists correspond to material already catalogued here:

* The private "they will kill me if I turn on it" statements → [Fear & Kill Warnings](./Fear_Kill_Warnings/overview) and the numbered [Claim KM index](./Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me) ([KM-02](./Text_Messages/kill-me-02-harrison-smith-report), [KM-03](./Text_Messages/kill-me-03-break-away-pro-israel), [KM-04](./Text_Messages/kill-me-04-go-against-israel)).
* The Sept 9 "THEY ARE GOING TO KILL ME" messages → [Claim KM-07](./Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps) and the [Sept 9 high-pressure meeting](/Timeline/high-pressure-meeting-sept-9).
* Reported bullying and moral-blackmail pressure → [Pre-Event Political Pressure](/Timeline/pre-event-political-pressure) and [Israel Events & Pressure Timeline](/Timeline/israel-events-pressure-timeline).
* Platforming Tucker Carlson and plans to re-platform Candace Owens → [Tucker Carlson & Israel](/Israel/Tucker_Carlson_Israel) and [Candace Owens Friendship](./Candace_Owens_Friendship).

Parker's closing point is a discovery argument: journals, texts, DMs, voice notes, and emails in
Charlie's accounts and devices would corroborate or refute each claim, yet none have been produced
publicly. That is the same disclosure gap the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) aim
to close.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person or government committed a crime or had
advance knowledge of the assassination. The claims above are attributed public commentary.
