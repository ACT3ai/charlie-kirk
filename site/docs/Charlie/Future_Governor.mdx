# Future Governor

For years before September 10, 2025, and especially after the 2024 election, people on X/Twitter, local Arizona television, and national political media **floated Charlie Kirk as a possible future governor of Arizona**. Some treated it as idle speculation. Some treated it as a serious 2026 path. Pollsters **included his name in hypothetical Republican primary fields**. Kirk himself **never publicly announced a candidacy** and, in May 2025, **endorsed U.S. Rep. Andy Biggs** instead — a choice that became [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Turning Point Action's **"Project 2026"** lane for the Arizona governor's mansion.

:::note Scope of this page
This page documents **claims, rumors, polls, and public statements** about Kirk as a potential Arizona governor. It does **not** assert that he would have won, that he had decided to run, or that any living person acted on a "future governor" theory in connection with his death. Where quotes or poll numbers appear, they are attributed to the outlet or account that published them.
:::

## Why Arizona entered the conversation

Kirk lived and worked substantially out of **Arizona**. Turning Point USA and Turning Point Action were headquartered in the Phoenix area; Kirk and his family were repeatedly described in Arizona coverage as Arizona residents. Arizona is a **swing state** where Republicans hoped to unseat Democratic Gov. **Katie Hobbs** after Hobbs narrowly defeated **Kari Lake** in 2022, and where Trump's 2024 margin was among his strongest in competitive states. Kirk's organization spent years on Arizona elections, ballot chasing, and candidate amplification — so "Charlie Kirk as governor" was not a random fantasy; it was an **extension of an existing power base** that media and pollsters found plausible enough to test.

Two separate claim-lines later get mixed together on social media:

1. **Kirk himself** would run for / become governor.
2. Kirk would **make someone else** (especially **Andy Biggs**) governor through TPAction.

This page keeps those distinct.

## November 25, 2024 — KPNX / local NBC "rumors he wants to run"

The highest-visibility **media seed** for the "Kirk for governor" boom on X was a **KPNX 12News (Phoenix NBC)** political segment after the 2024 election. Accounts across the right-wing news ecosystem clipped and restated the same lines within minutes.

### What the segment reportedly said

As restated on X by amplifiers quoting the broadcast:

* Kirk and Turning Point USA **"played a big role in Republican victories this year."**
* Kirk had **"emerged a significant player in Republican politics."**
* **"There are rumors he wants to run for governor."**
* His potential as a candidate should not be underestimated because he can **"raise money"** and **"a lot of people know who he is."**

That is **local-TV rumor reporting**, not a Kirk announcement. It is still the most-cited "credible" source in the November 2024 viral wave.

### High-reach X amplification (same night)

| Account | Role / credibility notes | Claim as posted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **@LeadingReport** | High-reach breaking-news aggregator | "BREAKING: Charlie Kirk pitched to run for Governor of Arizona by local NBC station." (~417k views reported on the post) |
| **@nicksortor** (Nick Sortor) | On-scene conservative reporter; frequent cable news guest | "JUST IN: Charlie Kirk could be running for Governor of Arizona… EPIC! I'd be the first one to move there. LET'S GO @charliekirk11!" |
| **@EricLDaugh** (Eric Daugherty) | Right-wing news editor / alerts account | "#BREAKING: Charlie Kirk may seek to run for governor of Arizona - local NBC station" + broadcast quote |
| **@MarioNawfal** | Large X live-news personality | "CHARLIE KIRK FOR ARIZONA GOVERNOR?" restating money + name-ID argument from NBC |
| **@alx** | Large political X account | "KPNX-12News: There are rumors that Charlie Kirk could run for Governor of Arizona" |
| **@MAGAVoice** | Large MAGA amplifier | "There are rumors that Charlie Kirk will run for Arizona's next Governor… I STRONGLY SUPPORT THIS / DO YOU?" |

None of those posts, by themselves, prove intent. They prove that **a local NBC rumor frame went national on X** and that **high-follower conservative media accounts treated a Kirk candidacy as desirable and plausible**.

## February–May 2025 — Polls that treated Kirk as a real hypothetical

### February 2025 — Noble Predictive Insights (NPI), covered by KTAR

Phoenix-area KTAR News reported NPI polling of **registered Republicans** (Feb. 11–13, 2025; **n = 374**) that included Kirk in a **hypothetical six-person GOP primary field**:

* Declared / known names: **Andy Biggs**, **Karrin Taylor Robson**
* Speculative names: **Charlie Kirk**, Treasurer **Kimberly Yee**, state Sen. **Jake Hoffman**, **Jack McCain**

**Reported primary shares:**

| Name | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Charlie Kirk | **14%** (tie for first) |
| Andy Biggs | **14%** (tie for first) |
| Karrin Taylor Robson | 11% |
| Jack McCain | 8% |
| Kimberly Yee | 5% |
| Jake Hoffman | 1% |

KTAR's framing was explicit: **"If he runs for governor, Charlie Kirk would have as good a chance at winning the Republican nomination as any of the current candidates."** That is a **pollster-and-local-news claim about electability if he ran**, not a prediction that he would run.

### May 2025 — Newsweek / second NPI wave

*Newsweek* (Andrew Stanton, late May 2025) reported a later NPI survey (May 12–16, 2025; **1,026 registered voters**, MoE **±4.76 pp**). In that snapshot:

* **Karrin Taylor Robson** 24%
* **Andy Biggs** 17%
* **Charlie Kirk** 17%
* **Kimberly Yee** 6%
* Unsure 37%

Key Newsweek statements (high-value, attributed):

* Kirk **"had been floated as a potential gubernatorial candidate for Republicans."**
* He **"endorsed Biggs earlier in May rather than running for the office himself."**
* **"Although it was rumored that Kirk could run, and he was included in early polls of the race, Kirk never made public comments indicating he planned to do so."**
* Kirk and Yee had **"both passed on running"** after the poll window.

**David Byler**, NPI Chief of Research, on the endorsement's effect:

> "The moment Kirk stepped out and backed Biggs, the math changed. Biggs now has the opportunity to inherit a young, digital-savvy base that Kirk built, which could be decisive."

Byler also described an **"army"** of potential support from Kirk's Turning Point network. The report noted **overlapping ideological profile and favorability** between Biggs and Kirk — which is why pollsters and Twitter users sometimes treated "Kirk voters" and "Biggs voters" as transferable.

**Mike Noble** (NPI founder/CEO) framed the general-election environment as competitive against Hobbs ("foundation, but not a fortress") with a still-strong Trump factor — context for why any high-profile MAGA-aligned figure (Kirk or Biggs) was taken seriously.

## What Kirk actually did publicly: endorse Andy Biggs

The documented public record of Kirk's own posts and appearances in **May–July 2025** is **not** "I am running." It is **"Andy Biggs is Arizona's next governor"** and **Turning Point Action will deliver the mansion**.

### Documented Kirk statements and events (as posted on X)

* **May 29–31, 2025** — Kirk promoted and headlined **Turning Point Action's Biggs for Governor rally** in Phoenix. Guest speakers promoted in his posts included **Matt Gaetz**, **Eli Crane**, **Paul Gosar**, **Mark Lamb**, **Byron Donalds**, **Burgess Owens**, **Lauren Boebert**, and others. Kirk posted that the event was **"the first day of the future of Arizona."**
* **May 31, 2025** — Kirk amplified Gaetz: **"You delivered the White House to Donald Trump, and you're going to deliver the Governor's Mansion to Andy Biggs."**
* **June 1, 2025** — Kirk posted video of his rally speech: to secure Trump's 2028 legacy, lock down Arizona by defeating Katie Hobbs; **"nobody is better-equipped to do that than Rep. Andy Biggs."**
* **June 7, 2025** — Kirk post: **"Andy Biggs, your next governor of Arizona."**
* **July 13, 2025** — Kirk post: **"Arizona's next governor Andy Biggs"** introducing Biggs on Gen Z / Reagan "freedom must be taught" themes.

A Kirk quote recirculated heavily after his death (in Biggs-primary content) runs roughly:

> "On election night in 2026, which feels like a LONG time away, but boy, it's going to be here just like this, we are going to be celebrating that my friend Andy Biggs is the next governor."

That quote is **Kirk predicting Biggs**, not Kirk predicting himself. Social media often blurs the two after September 10.

### "Project 2026"

Turning Point Action later described Kirk's Arizona governor project as **Project 2026** aimed at electing Biggs. After Kirk's death, **@TPAction** posted (Jan. 2026):

> "Charlie Kirk's Project 2026 was getting @andybiggs4az elected as Governor of Arizona. We're going to see it though."

When Biggs later won a Republican primary (July 2026 period coverage on X), amplifiers framed the win as fulfilling Kirk's plan — **"Charlie is smiling up in Heaven"** posts, Steve Bannon War Room–branded accounts noting Kirk's backing, etc. Those are **legacy-fulfillment claims**, still distinct from "Kirk would have been governor."

## Inventory of claim types (who said what kind of thing)

### A. "Rumors he will run" (media / amplifiers)

* **KPNX 12News** (Nov 2024): rumors he wants to run; money + name ID.
* **Nick Sortor, Eric Daugherty, Mario Nawfal, Leading Report, ALX, MAGA Voice**, and many smaller accounts: positive amplification; "EPIC," "HUGE," "strongly support."

### B. "If he runs, he is competitive" (pollsters / local news)

* **KTAR + NPI (Feb 2025):** tied for first with Biggs at 14% among Republicans in a speculative field; "as good a chance… as any of the current candidates."
* **NPI (May 2025) via Newsweek:** Kirk still at 17% alongside Biggs even as Robson led; then he declined to run.

### C. "Floated as a potential candidate" (national press)

* **Newsweek:** explicit language that Kirk had been **floated** as a potential Republican gubernatorial candidate; included in early polls; **no public comments from Kirk that he planned to run**.

### D. "He should run / I want him to run" (fan and influencer wishcasting)

* Dominant tone of Nov 2024 viral posts: support and excitement, not inside reporting from the Kirk household or TPUSA legal filings.
* Recurring theme: only a high-name, high-fundraiser MAGA figure can retake the Arizona governor's mansion after 2022.

### E. "He would have been / if he were still alive" (post–Sept 10 speculation)

* Example: Jul 28, 2026 X post asking whether Kirk would have run for the GOP nomination if still alive.
* These are **counterfactuals**. They are part of public discourse but are **not evidence** of a firm pre-death decision.

### F. Conflation errors common on X

* Treating Biggs primary wins as proof Kirk "was going to be governor."
* Treating poll inclusion as a campaign launch.
* Treating "rumors" on local TV as equivalent to an FEC/state filing.
* Treating TPAction "Project 2026" as Kirk's personal ballot line.

### G. Claims **not** found as solid primary hits in this research pass

* A clear **Donald Trump** public endorsement of **"Charlie Kirk for Arizona governor"** (Trump did dual-endorse **Biggs and Robson** when Biggs entered, per Newsweek).
* A **Kirk** tweet or speech saying **"I am running for governor of Arizona in 2026."**
* A formal campaign committee under Kirk's name for AZ governor.

Absence of those items matters: the "future governor" story is real as **rumor + poll + desire**, weaker as **declared candidacy**.

## Why this page sits under Charlie (investigation angle)

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) is documented here as victim and **motive-pressure subject**, not as a living candidate. The "future governor of Arizona" thread matters for three non-assassinating reasons:

1. **Power trajectory** — Poll inclusion and NBC rumor coverage show how far institutional MAGA and Arizona Republican politics treated him as more than a campus speaker.
2. **Choice of path** — He publicly chose **kingmaker** (Biggs / Project 2026) over a personal 2026 filing, which is the last clear signal on the record.
3. **Discourse after death** — Counterfactuals ("he would have been governor") and fulfillment narratives ("Biggs is carrying his plan") are part of how the public reconstructs who he was becoming in 2024–2025. Those reconstructions are **political interpretation**, not forensic findings.

Nothing on this page should be read as implying that ambition for the Arizona governorship caused his death, or that any named living person acted on that ambition against him.
