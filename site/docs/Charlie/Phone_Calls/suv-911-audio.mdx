# SUV 911 and "Get That Off of Him"

Mid-2026 Candace Owens–adjacent tips and X clips describe audio tied to the **security vehicle** transporting Charlie after he was shot. Circulating lines include:

- **"We got him. We're taking him to the hospital."**  
- **"Get that off of him."**

Investigators ask: get **what** off of him — clothing, medical gear, or a **microphone** (linking exploding-mic theories on [CoverUp](/CoverUp/Mic_Explosive))?

That question is the whole reason this audio is chased: if a device on his body is what killed Charlie Kirk,
the line is a witness to it, and the majority citizen reading of the evidence favors
[the exploding-mic explanation](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie) over the state's
rifle account.

## Status

These lines enter the record as **audio tips and commentary clips**, not as a published full 911 CAD package with authenticated chain of custody on this site. Competing posts claim Owens mischaracterized 911 material. Readers should demand the **raw file + metadata**.

## Why it sits under Phone Calls

Whether the path is cellular 911, radio, or an open line to another security team, the public fight is about **voice traffic in the critical minutes** after 12:23 PM MDT. Cross-link [Missing 911 / Harpole](./missing-911-harpole) and [Shapiro Security On the Line](./shapiro-security-call).
