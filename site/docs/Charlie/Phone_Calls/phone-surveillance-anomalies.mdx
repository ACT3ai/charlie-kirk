# Phone Surveillance Anomalies

Call quality and device behavior claims sit beside call **content** claims. Compiled narratives say Charlie described:

- Phone **overheating** and rapid drain  
- **Weird feedback** on calls  
- A **second SIM** in a backup phone he never installed  
- A mega-donor warning roughly: **back off or you won't make it to Christmas**  
- A subsequent **Hamptons intervention**

Full write-up: [Phone Surveillance Claims](/Charlie/Phone_Surveillance_Claims). This page exists so the Phone Calls hub TOC captures the surveillance lane without duplicating the entire essay.

## Investigative value

If true, surveillance would explain schedule leakage and psychological pressure. Symptoms alone are **not** forensic proof of state hacking. Need carrier logs, baseband analysis, and lawful imaging.
