# Charlie's Phone Calls

Alongside texts, investigators track **voice calls** — before the shooting, in the SUV after the shot, and in the hours when custody and press narratives formed. Call content is almost never in a public authenticated transcript; it arrives through **Candace Owens segments, security interviews, X audio tips, and secondhand paraphrases**. Treat every line as a **claim** until carrier records or sworn testimony land. Almost none of this voice
traffic has been entered into the prosecution of [Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview), who is charged
but not convicted, so the public record here remains built from tips rather than from filings.

## Threads That Dominate X

Three call stories dominate mid-2026 investigator posts. First: Owens and amplifiers claim Kirk's **final high-pressure call** involved **Benjamin Netanyahu** (or an Israel-linked intervention) pressing for **Iran war** alignment, after which TPUSA messaging allegedly snapped pro-war — a motive narrative, not a proven assassination order. Second: alleged **SUV / 911 audio** from the hospital run, with lines such as **"We got him"** and **"Get that off of him"** (some interpreters ask whether "that" means a microphone). Third: claims that **Ben Shapiro's** security (described in commentary as IDF-linked) was **on the phone with Kirk's team** while Charlie was dying in the vehicle.

Separately, [Shawn Ryan](/Influencers/podcasts-shawn-ryan)-adjacent coverage and [Baron Coleman](/Influencers/podcasts-baron) threads press a **missing 911** problem: security contractor accounts of calling 911 versus governments that have not produced a matching public call. [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said the last thing Charlie told him in person was to **stop a war with Iran** — not a phone log, but treated as the same "final voice" evidence class. Phone-surveillance symptoms (overheating, second SIM, feedback) are summarized here and detailed on [Phone Surveillance Claims](/Charlie/Phone_Surveillance_Claims).

## How To Read These Pages

Each page names the **alleged parties**, the **source path** (podcast, X clip, interview), the **counter-claim** where it exists, and what **records** (911 CAD, carrier CDRs, bodycam audio) would resolve it. This site does **not** assert that Netanyahu, Shapiro, security staff, or any living person ordered or carried out the killing.
