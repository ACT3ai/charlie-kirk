# Erika — "How Much Do You Know" Call

Master-file commentary records a claim that **a few hours after** Charlie was shot, [Erika Kirk](/Messages/erika-kirk-denial) received a phone call from an **"important individual"** who asked **"How much do you know?"** and that she answered **"Everything."**

## Status

- **Unverified** in public primary audio or official logs on this site.  
- Circulates as secondhand investigative lore, sometimes tied to [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet) retellings in online notes.  
- Does **not** establish the caller's identity, the call's reality, or any crime.

## Why keep the page

If authenticated later, the call becomes a major **day-of communications** node. Until then it is a **research flag**: identity of caller, carrier metadata, and Erika's own account under oath would be required. This site does **not** treat the anecdote as proof Erika participated in wrongdoing.
