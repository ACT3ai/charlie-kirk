# Netanyahu / Iran Intervention Call

One of the most shared **phone-call** claims on X: that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s **final high-pressure call** was a massive intervention by **Benjamin Netanyahu** (or Netanyahu-aligned pressure) pushing for **U.S. war footing on Iran**, and that Charlie refused — after which he was killed and [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) messaging allegedly snapped pro-war.

## Source path

The claim is associated with **[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)** show segments and large-amplifier reposts (e.g. high-engagement video posts restating "final phone call was a massive intervention by Benjamin Netanyahu"). It is **not** a published carrier CDR or official transcript on this site.

## How investigators use it

- Links private call pressure to public [anti-Iran-war comments](/Charlie/Comments/iran-war-opposition) and [Joe Kent's last conversation](./joe-kent-last-conversation).  
- Treats post-death organizational messaging shift as circumstantial.  
- **Does not**, by itself, prove state involvement in the shooting.

## Counter-points that belong on the record

- Heads of government routinely call U.S. political figures; a call is not a hit order.  
- Exact participants, time, and wording remain **unverified** in public primary sources.  
- Netanyahu and Israeli officials have publicly denied involvement in Kirk's death (as reported in contemporaneous coverage). Denial is not proof of innocence or guilt; it is part of the public record.

## Defamation note

This site does **not** assert that Benjamin Netanyahu, the State of Israel, or any named official ordered or carried out Charlie Kirk's assassination. The page records **attributed call claims** circulating among investigators.
