# Joe Kent — Last Conversation

Former NCTC-linked figure [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said on record (widely clipped from a [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson) appearance) that the last thing Charlie told him was effectively: **stop us from getting into a war with Iran.** Whether that exchange was a phone call or in person, investigators file it with **final-voice evidence** of Charlie's anti-escalation priority.

## Why it matters here

- Corroborates the public [Iran opposition comments](/Charlie/Comments/iran-war-opposition) lane.  
- Sits next to the contested [Netanyahu intervention call](./netanyahu-iran-call) narrative as independent pressure context.  
- Kent has also discussed online pre-knowledge posts and foreign-lead blocks — see [FBI Foreign Leads](/FBI/Foreign_Leads) and [Joe Kent Halt](/CoverUp/Joe_Kent_Halt).

## Status

Attributed personal recollection. Not a CDR. Still one of the highest-signal **named-witness** final-words claims in the file.
