# Missing 911 / Harpole Claims

Investigators including commentary amplified by **Baron Coleman** argue that Charlie's **security contractor** (named in public discussion as [Brian Harpole](/Security_Team/brian-harpole) in related threads) told [Shawn Ryan](/Influencers/podcasts-shawn-ryan) he **called 911**, yet government production of that call is missing or incomplete. The binary framed online: either the contractor misstated, or records are withheld/deleted — "both terrible options."

## Status

This is a **records-dispute** claim. Resolution requires CAD logs, ANI/ALI data, and any bodycam/radio sync — materials the [Fix Laws](/Fix/overview) agenda aims to force. This site does **not** assert Harpole committed a crime or that any agency destroyed evidence as proven fact.
