# "Jewish Donors Play Into All the Stereotypes"

## Alleged wording (KB157 partial)

> “Jewish donors play into all the stereotypes. I cannot and will not be bullied like this.”

Often concatenated with [“Leaving me no choice but to leave the Pro Israel cause”](./leave-pro-israel-cause-line) as one continuous vent. Master-file sources list X posts such as [@Jeannin55385803](https://x.com/Jeannin55385803/status/1983227989308649546), [@Rahul_EIWF](https://x.com/Rahul_EIWF/status/1984001761104081260), [@JustTheTweets17](https://x.com/JustTheTweets17/status/1983947359186038839), and [@ImBreckWorsham](https://x.com/ImBreckWorsham/status/1976263670721048925).

## Context claimed by publishers

- Written after pressure over booking / not canceling [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson) and friction with Israel-critical voices including [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)  
- Addressed into a **small WhatsApp group**, not as a public press release  
- Coupled with losing a large donor (figures like “~$2 million” appear in commentary)

Exact punctuation varies across screenshots. Treat line-level text as **reported**, even where Kolvet has confirmed the **chat set**.

## Defamation and reading rules

- Living donors and organizations are **not** accused here of murder or illegal threats unless a court has found that.  
- Private anger about fundraising is **not** proof of a kill order.  
- Reproducing Charlie’s alleged words is documentation of **speech claims**, not endorsement of ethnic generalizations.
