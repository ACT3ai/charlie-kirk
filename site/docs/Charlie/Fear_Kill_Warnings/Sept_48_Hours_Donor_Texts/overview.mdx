# ~48 Hours Before — Donor Bullying Texts

Investigation beat **KB157** captures the **highest-authentication** written cluster: roughly **48 hours before** September 10, 2025, Charlie is alleged to have written (in a multi-person chat) that **Jewish donors play into stereotypes**, that he **will not be bullied**, and that pressure left him **no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause**. This is **not** the same artifact as August “they will kill me” slogans — it is **donor / bullying / leave-cause** language.

## Why this cluster sits under Fear & Kill Warnings

The chat does **not** always say “they will kill me.” Investigators still place it here because X stack posts **pair** it with August Israel-kill fear language as a single narrative arc: **pressure → leave-cause text → death**. Separating the **words that are about bullying and donors** from **words that are about being killed** is the discipline this section enforces.
