# ~48 Hours Before — Donor Bullying Texts


## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: authenticated donor WhatsApp about leaving the pro-Israel cause falls in the **~48 hours before** UVU, with packaging on **SEPT 8** and later **SEPT 9** re-dating claims. Not a kill-me string.

**Date range (working model):** SEPT 7–10 · ~48h WhatsApp · SEPT 8 vs SEPT 9 re-dating

The chart below is **not** a government finding. Bar height is **relative likelihood** from public claims. Domain is trimmed to non-trivial likelihood.

Investigation beat **KB157** captures the **highest-authentication** written cluster: roughly **48 hours before** September 10, 2025, Charlie is alleged to have written (in a multi-person chat) that **Jewish donors play into stereotypes**, that he **will not be bullied**, and that pressure left him **no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause**. This is **not** the same artifact as August “they will kill me” ([KM index](/Charlie/Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me)) slogans — it is **donor / bullying / leave-cause** language.

## Why this cluster sits under Fear & Kill Warnings

The chat does **not** always say “they will kill me.” ([KM-08](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-08-kolvet-they-will-kill-me)) Investigators still place it here because X stack posts **pair** it with August Israel-kill fear language as a single narrative arc: **pressure → leave-cause text → death**. Separating the **words that are about bullying and donors** from **words that are about being killed** is the discipline this section enforces.
