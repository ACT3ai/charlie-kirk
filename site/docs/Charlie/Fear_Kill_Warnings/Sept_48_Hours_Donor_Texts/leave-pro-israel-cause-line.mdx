## Alleged wording (KB157 partial)

Core reported forms:

- “Leaving me no choice but to leave the Pro Israel cause”  
- “You’ve left me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause” ([@Wades2nd](https://x.com/Wades2nd/status/1983654950598332758))  
- “I’m going to have to stop supporting Israel…” ([@MaxtheCrowhouse](https://x.com/MaxtheCrowhouse/status/1983641464253903025))

## Who was in the “group”

Public retellings describe roughly **nine closest contacts**, sometimes including a **pastor**. If true, multiple independent recipients should eventually be able to confirm under oath. Absence of a public chorus of named recipients (beyond Candace-centered disclosure + Kolvet authentication of the chat’s reality) remains a **gap**.
