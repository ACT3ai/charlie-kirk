# Candace Owens Release & Kolvet Authentication

Authentication matters here for a practical reason: material confirmed as genuine can be put in front of a court, and none of this donor correspondence has surfaced in the [prosecution of Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview).

## Release path

1. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) publicly discusses and displays **private messages** attributed to Charlie regarding donors and the pro-Israel cause.  
2. Screenshots and show clips circulate on X; secondary accounts re-export the text.  
3. [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet), TPUSA spokesman, is widely reported / clipped as **confirming the authenticity** of the **donor WhatsApp chat**.  
4. Commentators (e.g. claims involving [Clint Russell](https://x.com/LibertyLockPod)) assert Kolvet also shared material with the FBI as motive context — a **claim about Kolvet’s conduct**, not a finding of guilt against any donor.

Deep friendship and other Candace threads: [Candace Owens Private Texts](/Charlie/Text_Messages/candace-owens-private-texts) and [Candace Owens Friendship](/Charlie/Candace_Owens_Friendship).
