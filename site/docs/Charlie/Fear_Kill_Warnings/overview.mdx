# Fear & Kill Warnings

In the month before September 10, 2025, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was **alleged** to have told different people — by text, group chat, and in-person conversation — that he feared being killed if he broke with pro-Israel politics, and that donor bullying was forcing him off the “pro-Israel cause.” This hub **separates those incidents by date, channel, and words**, so August 13 is not mixed with August 16, and neither is mixed with the ~48-hour donor WhatsApp. None of this material has surfaced in the [court case](/court/overview) the state has built around a single accused shooter.

## Why separate incidents matter

Citizen investigators and movie-beat indexes treat three high-traffic quote rows as distinct evidence items: **(1)** *“They will kill me if I break away from being pro-Israel”* ([KM-03](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-03-break-away-pro-israel)) (often dated **August 13**), **(2)** *“Hey, if I go against Israel, I think they will kill me”* ([KM-04](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-04-go-against-israel)) (some indexes list **August 16**; many public retellings still say August 13), and **(3)** the **~48 hours before death** WhatsApp lines about Jewish donors, bullying, and leaving the pro-Israel cause. Conflating them into one “Charlie said Israel would kill him” ([KM index](/Charlie/Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me)) meme erases who heard what, on which channel, and which podcaster first amplified it.

## What the public record actually supports

The **strongest authenticated written set** is still the **September ~8 donor WhatsApp** released via [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and publicly treated as real by TPUSA spokesman [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet) — donor pressure, Tucker non-cancellation, and “leave the pro-Israel cause” language. The **strongest contemporaneous pre-assassination public post** is [Harrison Smith](/People/harrison-smith)’s **August 13, 2025** X post: someone close to Charlie said Charlie thinks **Israel will kill him if he turns against them** ([KM-02](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-02-harrison-smith-report)) — later reconfirmed “yes” on the day of the shooting. The **Israel-specific first-person quotes** (*go against* / *break away*) remain **second-hand**: Instagram reels, X carriers ([@hodgetwins](https://x.com/hodgetwins/status/1975564275457589371), [@FactsVsZee](https://x.com/FactsVsZee), [@BalochRamzan2](https://x.com/BalochRamzan2/status/1965901874974044386), and others), and no court-authenticated Kirk-authored export of those exact strings.
