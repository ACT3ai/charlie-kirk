## Packaging patterns

1. **Premonition montage** — soft music + August date + UVU stills  
2. **Three-line stack** — August fear + 48-hour leave-cause + “and people get mad”  
3. **Smith screenshot** — crop of the August 13 post with “he told us” captions  
4. **Candace clip** — September releases used as *validation* of earlier August memes  

## How to cite these without laundering

- Always mark **alleged**.  
- Prefer linking the **original status URL**.  
- Note when a reel **misdates** or merges Sept 9 with August.  
- Do not treat view counts as corroboration of the original speech act.
