## Why a separate August 16 page exists

Different investigation products (site pages, master-file quote blocks, and spreadsheet beats) **disagree on the calendar day** of the *go against Israel* sentence. The master investigation file’s compiled quotes block frequently attaches **August 13** and “friend instructed to post online.” Spreadsheet **KB155** assigns **August 16**. Splitting pages forces every later writer to **cite which dating convention** they mean instead of silently averaging the dates.

## What is shared vs distinct

Shared with August 13: Israel-as-threat subject, secondhand chain of custody, no authenticated Kirk audio of the exact slogan. Distinct: **fuller first-person “hey…” framing**, friend + “post it online” instruction motif, and the **KB155 date stamp**. Treat them as **related claim-rows**, not proven separate speeches, until a recipient names a day under oath.
