## Why these matter for the three KB rows

KB155–KB157 are the **Israel / donor** spine. Other channels show whether Charlie also used **generic kill language** on September 9, warned a **mentor** weeks earlier, or texted **security** the night before. If multiple independent people describe fear language, that is a **pattern claim**; if all paths lead to one podcast, that is **amplification**.
