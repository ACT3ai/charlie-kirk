# Verbal vs Text vs Discord Delivery

Citizen discourse often says “Charlie texted that they would kill him” for **every** fear claim. Delivery mode changes **forensic path**. This page is the cross-cutting taxonomy for the whole Fear & Kill Warnings section.

Delivery mode also determines what could ever be introduced at a trial: a verbal recollection is a witness problem, while a carrier record is a subpoena problem, and neither has so far surfaced in the [state's case against Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview).

## Delivery taxonomy

| Mode | Example claim | Forensic notes |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| **In-person verbal** | Friend conversation / “hey if I go against Israel…” | Needs human witness; no metadata |
| **Public X post (third party)** | Harrison Smith Aug 13 | Timestamped, but **not** Charlie’s phone |
| **WhatsApp group** | ~48-hour donor leave-cause | Screenshots + Kolvet auth path |
| **Signal / Telegram** | Sept 9 kill-me to staffer | Disappearing messages risk |
| **SMS / iMessage** | Sometimes assumed; often **incorrect** per notes | Carrier subpoenas possible |
| **Discord** | Campus / TPUSA Discord lore | Separate section: [Discord Messages](/Charlie/Discord_Messages/overview) |
| **Podcast paraphrase** | Owens, Turek interviews | Medium is speech about a message |

## Podcaster vs original words

- **Originating words** — what Charlie is alleged to have said/typed  
- **Backing words** — what Owens, Smith, Hodgetwins, National File, etc. said *about* those words  

Always separate them in notes and on pages.
