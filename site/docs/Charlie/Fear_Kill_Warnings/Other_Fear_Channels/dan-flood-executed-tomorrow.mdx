## Alleged content

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and secondary X accounts claim Charlie messaged head of security **[Dan Flood](/People/dan-flood)** the night before death after a donor call, with language such as **“they” were going to kill him tomorrow** ([KM-10](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)) / **executed tomorrow**. ([KM-10](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)) See [Dan Flood Security Texts](/Charlie/Text_Messages/dan-flood-security-texts) and [Messages: Dan Flood](/Messages/message-to-dan-flood).

The claim carries weight only because of what followed roughly eighteen hours later: Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025, and the [manner of his death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) remains contested.
