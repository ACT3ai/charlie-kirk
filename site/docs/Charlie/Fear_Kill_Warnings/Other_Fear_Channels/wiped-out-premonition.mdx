# Wiped-Out Premonition Texts

## Alleged content

Screenshots and Candace-related releases attribute longer premonition language to Charlie, including variants of:

> “Since the beginning of TPUSA, I knew in my gut that I might get wiped out at any time ([KM-01](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-01-wiped-out))… I cannot explain it… But I dream about it all the time…”

Related: the **“end of this revolution”** private text to Candace — see [Candace Owens Private Texts](/Charlie/Text_Messages/candace-owens-private-texts) and [Messages: Wiped Out](/Messages/wiped-out-premonition).

Premonition language describes a diffuse sense of threat rather than a named actor, which is why it does not map cleanly onto the lone-gunman theory the state advanced when it charged Tyler Robinson — see [the case against that theory](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview).

## Why it is a different bucket

| Premonition language | August/Sept Israel-kill language |
|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| General fate / “wiped out” | Named **Israel** or pro-Israel break |
| Multi-year TPUSA self-image | Mid-2025 political pivot pressure |
| Martyr / calling framing | Donor bullying + leave-cause |

Both get used on X as “he knew.” They answer different questions.

|-------|-------|

## Status

Authenticity disputes apply, especially to extreme 2026 graphic packs. Prefer Kolvet-confirmed material for courtroom-grade claims; file outliers under [Authenticity & Fakes](/Charlie/Text_Messages/authenticity-and-fakes).

## Best understanding of when he said this

Best understanding: long-running premonition language; strongest public dating puts related Candace texts around **2018**, with the wording claiming a gut feeling since TPUSA founding (~2012). Not a SEPT 9 item.

**Date range (working model):** Domain: 2012–2025 · Peak claim year ~2018 (Candace text dating) · multi-year premonition
