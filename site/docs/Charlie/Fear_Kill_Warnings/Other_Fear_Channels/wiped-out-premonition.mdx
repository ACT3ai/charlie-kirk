# Wiped-Out Premonition Texts

## Alleged content

Screenshots and Candace-related releases attribute longer premonition language to Charlie, including variants of:

> “Since the beginning of TPUSA, I knew in my gut that I might get wiped out at any time ([KM-01](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-01-wiped-out))… I cannot explain it… But I dream about it all the time…”

Related: the **“end of this revolution”** private text to Candace — see [Candace Owens Private Texts](/Charlie/Text_Messages/candace-owens-private-texts) and [Messages: Wiped Out](/Messages/wiped-out-premonition).

## Why it is a different bucket

| Premonition language | August/Sept Israel-kill language |
|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| General fate / “wiped out” | Named **Israel** or pro-Israel break |
| Multi-year TPUSA self-image | Mid-2025 political pivot pressure |
| Martyr / calling framing | Donor bullying + leave-cause |

Both get used on X as “he knew.” They answer different questions.
