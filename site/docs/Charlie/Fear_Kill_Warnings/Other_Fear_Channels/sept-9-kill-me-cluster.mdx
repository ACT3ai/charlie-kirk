## Claimed setting

During or after a **combative meeting** on **September 9, 2025** — the day before UVU — Charlie allegedly messaged confidants. See [Timeline: high-pressure Sept 9](/Timeline/high-pressure-meeting-sept-9) and [Text Messages: They Are Going to Kill Me](/Charlie/Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me).

- [Dan Flood](/People/dan-flood) (security)  
- A donor “white knight” in some Owens retellings  
- [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek) (may be separate weeks-before thread)

## Channel

Often said to travel on **Signal or Telegram**, not iMessage — see [Signal & Telegram](/Charlie/Text_Messages/signal-telegram-channels). That matters for forensics if disappearing messages were enabled.

## Denial

[Erika Kirk](/Charlie/Text_Messages/erika-kirk-denial) has stated on Glenn Beck’s show that Charlie was **not** messaging people the day before saying they were going to kill him, ([KM-07](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)) and that she has his phone. Multi-source commentary (Owens, Turek, Kolvet narrative, Smith) **contradicts** that denial. Document the conflict; do not invent a crime by the widow.

This cluster is **later** and **more generic** (“they”) than the August Israel-specific slogans. X users often merge them; this site does not.
