## How the wording is used

On X and Instagram, this sentence is treated as a **paraphrase** of Charlie’s fear that leaving pro-Israel politics would be lethal. It is **shorter and more slogan-like** than the fuller *“Hey, if I go against Israel, I think they will kill me”* ([KM-04](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-04-go-against-israel)) line tracked under [August 16](../August_16_Go_Against_Israel/go-against-israel-quote). Compilations often list both as if they were one utterance; they may be **retellings of one conversation** or **two different carriers**. This page keeps the **break-away** string alone so readers can track its sources.
