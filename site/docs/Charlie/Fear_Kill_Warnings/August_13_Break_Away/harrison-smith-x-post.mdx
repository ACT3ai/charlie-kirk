# Harrison Smith August 13 X Post

## The post (public record)

On **August 13, 2025**, Infowars / War Room host **[Harrison H. Smith](/People/harrison-smith)** ([@HarrisonHSmith](https://x.com/HarrisonHSmith)) posted (status **1955705962964111425**):

> “I’m not gonna name names, but I was told by someone close to Charlie Kirk that Charlie thinks **Israel will kill him if he turns against them**.”

Public metrics captured after the fact include **tens of thousands of likes**, **~10K+ reposts**, and **multi-million views** (order-of-magnitude engagement that made this post a primary viral seed).

## Why investigators treat it as special

1. **Timestamped before UVU** — cannot be invented after the assassination.  
2. **Named speaker** under his own handle — stronger than anonymous meme accounts.  
3. **September 10 confirmation** — after the shooting, accounts such as **@ViceLitty** asked Smith for a yes/no; Smith replied **“yes.”** That thread is also documented on influencer and photo pages ([Harrison Smith X page](/Influencers/x/harrisonhsmith), [Israel photo f18540](/Photos/Israel/Img_Photo_f18540)).

## What Smith did *not* provide

- No name for the “someone close to Charlie”  
- No screenshot of a Kirk text  
- No claim that he heard Charlie say the exact *break-away* or *go against* slogan on camera  
- No accusation that Israel fired the UVU shot — the post reports **Charlie’s alleged belief**

Grok and other automated replies on X later stressed the secondhand nature of the claim; critics call it rumor; amplifiers call it the earliest public “he feared Israel” marker.

## Relationship to KB155 / KB156 wording

Smith’s wording is closest to **“Israel will kill him if he turns against them.”** The investigation’s **KB156** break-away sentence and **KB155** go-against sentence are **more specific first-person scripts** that circulate in reels and compilations. They may share a root conversation with Smith’s source — or they may be later polish. Keep them **linked but not merged**.
