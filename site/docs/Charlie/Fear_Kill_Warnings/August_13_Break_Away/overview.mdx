## What this date owns

August 13 is the **anchor day** for contemporaneous public discussion of Charlie’s alleged fear of Israel — not for the authenticated donor WhatsApp (that is ~September 8). Smith’s post is timestamped **before** the assassination, so it cannot be a pure post-hoc invention invented after UVU. The *break-away* sentence itself is still a **circulating attribution**, not a published native text export from Kirk’s phone.
