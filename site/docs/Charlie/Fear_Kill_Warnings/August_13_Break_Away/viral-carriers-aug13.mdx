# Viral Carriers of the August Fear Quotes

The August fear language did not stay inside one Infowars post. After September 10, 2025 (and in some threads before), a **carrier network** restacked Harrison Smith’s report with donor texts and September 9 kill-me claims. This page maps **who amplified what**, not who committed any crime.

## High-engagement stack posts

### Hodgetwins — three-line stack

[@hodgetwins](https://x.com/hodgetwins/status/1975564275457589371) (and reposts such as [@aldamu_jo](https://x.com/aldamu_jo/status/1994064816432951397)) pushed a template still dominant in late 2025–2026:

1. Charlie told people he thought **Israel would kill him** ([KM index](/Charlie/Text_Messages/they-are-going-to-kill-me)) if he turned against them.  
2. **~48 hours** before he was shot he texted a group that he was **done supporting Israel** / leaving the pro-Israel cause.  
3. “And people get mad at us for noticing that?”

This stack **glues** the August fear cluster to the [~48-hour donor chat](../Sept_48_Hours_Donor_Texts/overview). See also [Hodgetwins podcast page](/Influencers/podcasts-hodgetwins).

### National File — calendar stack

[@NationalFile](https://x.com/NationalFile/status/2035274236344865167) and similar accounts use a **Sept 8 / Sept 9 / Sept 10** calendar:

- Sept 8: donor stereotypes / leave pro-Israel cause  
- Sept 9: “They’re going to kill me tomorrow” ([KM-10](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow))  
- Sept 10: execution  

That is a **different dating scheme** from August 13/16; readers should not assume National File’s Sept 9 line is the same utterance as Smith’s August post.

## Instagram reels

Multiple Instagram reels (IDs archived in the master investigation file under Charlie quotes) date *“if I go against Israel I think they will kill me”* ([KM-04](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-04-go-against-israel)) to **August 13** and ask “when did he say this?” Those reels are **secondary packaging**, not primary recordings of Charlie speaking those words on camera.

## Investigator discipline

- Count **independent origins**, not repost volume.  
- Separate **carrier** from **source**.  
- Prefer posts with **timestamps and screenshots** over pure text memes.  
- File extreme lines (“I told all the Jews to go to hell”) under [Authenticity & Fakes](/Charlie/Text_Messages/authenticity-and-fakes) unless they match Kolvet-confirmed material.
