The **first 24 hours** set the pattern: false UVU **"suspect in custody"** alerts, the [Sept 11 surrender chain](./Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody) with **6:25 PM Miranda** vs **8:02 PM** [Brooksby](/People/nate-brooksby) call disputes, and Discord messages timestamped **~7:57 PM**. July 2026 prelim notes (Officer **Davis** / SBI multi-agency room; formal detain **Sept 12 4:00 AM**) keep the custody chain live. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not convicted** — custody timing is evidence-process questions, not guilt findings.

**Days to months** brought [courtyard paving](./Site_Changes_And_Crime_Scene_Handling) within days — with Lead Investigator **Hull** reportedly testifying he was **not consulted** on paving — [Judge Tony Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf)'s **Dec 16, 2025** gag order, a **Dec 29, 2025** redacted-transcript release, and **July 2026** media exhibit sanctions during a five-day prelim. Brooksby and UVU President **Tuminez** resigned in **2026** ([institutional exits](./Institutional_Resignations); *Guardian* confirmed Tuminez's May step-down). Citizen investigators parallel mainstream coverage with [censorship threads](./Media_Narratives_And_Censorship_After) — Ryne Simmons' 4K video, platform suspensions, sealed discovery, and ballistics culture-war posts.

**Political aftermath** runs through memorial and TPUSA succession (Erika Kirk, [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) Aspen/FlightRadar succession challenges, donor threads, national honors) and [visa enforcement](./Visa-Revocation-Celebrations) against celebration posts — policy debates that must stay **separate** from ballistics and rooftop evidence.

The residential block **north of campus** has become its own after-the-shot thread. This section's
[house pages](./house/overview) document **691 W 925 S** — the home
[UVU bought in 2019](./house/ownership) for $900,000 — and the citizen allegation, **unproven and
denied by no official document because none names it**, that the block was used to plan or stage the
attack. Real court media from the Robinson prelim does place a grey Dodge Challenger on **Ring video
at 680 W 925 S** around 00:30 on September 11 ([W 925 S block](./house/w-925-south-block)).

On **August 7, 2026**, [@NihiloX](https://x.com/NihiloX/status/2085788561123549371) added a foreign
angle: a Google Trends chart for the term **"645 W 925 S"** filtered to the region **Israel**, flat
across five years except for a **single spike in April 2022**. Two limits apply immediately —
**645 is a different house from 691**, a neighbor on the same block, and Google Trends plots
**normalized relative interest, not counts**, so the "100" may rest on a handful of searches and
names no searcher. It is logged as a lead, not a finding, on
[Israel Search: 645 W 925 S](/Israel/israel-google-trends-645-w-925-s).
