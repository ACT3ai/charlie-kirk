## Title-transfer instruments that matter

These are the **actual deeds** that moved fee title. Entry numbers are Utah County recorder entries so anyone can re-pull them.

### 1. Broderick → Markides (1993)

| Entry | Date | Type | Grantor → Grantee |
|-------|------|------|-------------------|
| **17408-1993** | 03/25/1993 (rec. 03/26) | QCD | BRODERICK, DE RAE & DERAE TEE (et al.) → BRODERICK, PAULINE S |
| **17409-1993** | 03/24/1993 (rec. 03/26) | **WD** | BRODERICK, PAULINE S → **MARKIDES, KOSTAS & KARIN E** ($10 nominal) |
| **17410-1993** | 03/25/1993 (rec. 03/26) | TR D | MARKIDES, KOSTAS & KARIN E → BRODERICK, PAULINE S ($266,650 trust deed — seller financing) |

The house sits on **College Heights** land the Broderick family developed; DeRae Broderick's construction firm had used this address as a registered business address in the early 1990s (see [Prior Owners](./prior-owners)).

### 2. Markides → Theobald (1998)

| Entry | Date | Type | Grantor → Grantee |
|-------|------|------|-------------------|
| **125453-1998** | 12/03/1998 (rec. 12/04) | **WD** | **MARKIDES, KOSTAS** → **THEOBALD, PAUL H & DOROTHY H** ($10 nominal) |
| **125454-1998** | 12/03/1998 | D TR | THEOBALD → BANK OF AMERICAN FORK ($452,600) |
| **133017-1998** | 12/14/1998 | D TR | THEOBALD → HEADLANDS MORTGAGE COMPANY ($450,000) |

Paul and Dorothy Theobald bought the home in **December 1998** and held it for roughly **20 years**.

### 3. Theobald → Utah Valley University (2019) — the key transfer

| Entry | Date | Type | Grantor → Grantee |
|-------|------|------|-------------------|
| **21832-2019** | 03/01/2019 (rec. 03/18) | REC | ZIONS BANCORPORATION TEE → THEOBALD, PAUL H & DOROTHY H (reconveyance clearing a prior trust deed) |
| **46392-2019** | **05/24/2019** (rec. same day, 2:21 pm) | **WD** | **THEOBALD, PAUL H TEE (et al.)** → **UTAH VALLEY UNIVERSITY** |
| **79336-2019** | 08/15/2019 (rec. 08/19) | RSUBTEE | MERS et al. → THEOBALD (reconveyance of remaining Security Home Mortgage trust deed) |

The **warranty deed of May 24, 2019** is the instrument that put UVU on title. The board minutes say the university accepted the Theobalds' contract at the **appraised value of $900,000** as an extension of the campus Master Plan — ratified by the full Board of Trustees on June 18, 2019 after the Executive Committee had already approved it in May.

Sources: [Abstract instruments](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/abstract.asp?av_serial=364430006); [UVU Board of Trustees minutes, June 18, 2019](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/minutes/6-18-2019_trustee_minutes_final.pdf) ("purchase of a house located at 691 West 925 South, Orem… contract… accepted by the Theobalds… appraised value of $900,000"); [utah.gov PMN meeting record](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/504485.pdf).

## How to re-pull every instrument

1. Open [Utah County Property serial 36:443:0006](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/Property.asp?av_serial=364430006010).
2. Click **Documents** or **Abstract**.
3. Open any entry number (e.g. `46392-2019`) for the scanned instrument.
4. For the obsolete early serial still floating online: [36:214:0001](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/32916/Property.asp?av_serial=362140001002) — do **not** treat it as current title.

## What this chain does and does not prove

- **Does prove:** continuous, ordinary residential ownership (developer → private owners → long-term family → university campus-expansion purchase), with instrument numbers.
- **Does not prove:** anything about who occupied the house in September 2025, whether trucks barricaded a driveway, or the [staging allegation](./staging-allegation). Title and occupancy are different questions — see [Rental & Listing History](./rental-listings) and [Records Requests](./records-requests).

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

A complete public deed chain still cannot show key cards, visitor logs, or short-term use on a university-owned house. That is what [GRAMA requests](./records-requests) and the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) are for.
