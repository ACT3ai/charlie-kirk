# West 925 South Block — Neighbors, Ring Video & Court Record

:::caution Neighbors are witnesses, not suspects
Addresses on this block appear in **news coverage and court media** because cameras or geography put them near the escape / vehicle path. **No resident named here is accused of any crime.** The Noble household at 680 W 925 S is documented as a **cooperating witness** source of Ring/Nest video.
:::

The [alleged staging house at 691 W 925 S](./overview) does not sit alone. The same short stretch of **West 925 South** — the residential edge north of UVU that locals and investigators call part of the **"horseshoe"** — produced some of the most concrete vehicle and camera evidence in the public case file. This page maps the block so the house lead is not read in isolation.

## Block map (conceptual)

Grid context: UVU's Computer Science building is at **601 W 1000 S** — one block south. Campus Drive and the Losee Center sit on the campus side of this residential strip. See [Location & the Property](./location-and-property).

## 680 W 925 S — Ring/Nest video in court (confirmed public record)

This is the strongest **on-block** vehicle fact in the open record, and it is **not** the same claim as the citizen "command center" nighttime Challenger at 691.

From the **State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson** preliminary hearing (case no. **251403576**), agent testimony and exhibit logs publicly summarized online include:

- Investigators collected **Ring doorbell / neighborhood camera** footage from the surrounding area.
- Video from a residence at **680 West 925 South, Orem** — identified with the **Noble** household (Mrs. Noble Rule 1102 statement admitted) — showed a vehicle consistent with the **grey Dodge Challenger** associated with the suspect **parking across the street** at approximately **00:30:08** (about **12:30 a.m.**) on the morning of **September 11, 2025**.
- The location is described in testimony as the **"Horseshoe" area**, close to campus.
- Distinctive wheels helped investigators identify the vehicle in nighttime footage.
- Some citizen timelines write **12:47 a.m.** / **00:47** for the same corridor event; treat minor timestamp variance as a **document-reconciliation** issue, not as two different cars, until the raw exhibit is public.

Sources: [Grabien preliminary hearing archive summary](https://news.grabien.com/robinson-prelim-archive/); [Rev.com Day 1 transcript excerpt](https://www.rev.com/transcripts/ut-v-tyler-robinson---day-1); secondary write-ups of Hull testimony (e.g. [charliekirk.co.za summary of David Hull testimony](https://www.charliekirk.co.za/video/david-hull-testifies-in-preliminary-hearing-for-tyler-robinson-suspect-in-charlie-kirk-shooting-at-utah-valley-university/15281/)); Tumblr case-log notes citing search-warrant Nest/Ring language for **680 West 925 South**.

**Why this matters to 691.** 680 and 691 are on the **same street segment** north of campus. Court media puts the Challenger on this block **hours after** the shooting and after reported campus LE contact — which is exactly the geography the [command-center video](./overview) walks. That does **not** prove anyone staged from 691; it does prove the **block** is already inside the official evidence set.

## 683 W 925 S — the Palantir confusion (debunked by records)

Citizen posts and Instagram reels circulated the claim that the murder weapon or stash point sat on land tied to **Palantir** via **683 W 925 S**. Open property and corporate checks reported publicly (including Grok/X fact-check threads) say:

- **683 W 925 S** is owned by **683 Properties LLC** (from about 2021 in aggregator ownership histories).
- That LLC is **not** the same entity as **683 Capital Management**, a separate firm that has held **Palantir stock** as an investment — stock ownership ≠ owning an Orem house.
- No charging document reviewed for this page names Palantir as owner of the rifle-recovery ground.

Sources: public X threads summarizing parcel ownership (e.g. [property-records summary posts](https://x.com/grok/status/1971622977277223331)); [Zillow / Homes.com listings for 683 W 925 S](https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/683-W-925-S-Orem-UT-84058/11914810_zpid/). Historically the address also appeared as an office for small local brokerages (AB Realty / Absolute Realty roster entries) — ordinary commercial use of a large home, not a spy HQ.

Keep **683** separate from **691**. Conflating them muddies both the Palantir rumor and the UVU-owned staging lead.

## 691 W 925 S — UVU house on the same street

Covered in depth on [Ownership](./ownership) and the [deed chain](./deed-chain). For block context only:

- Same street as the court-cited Challenger camera.
- Citizen posts (Aug 2026) explicitly argue the university owns it and that satellite imagery of the driveway on the day of the death should be checked ([@xcryptoace33](https://x.com/xcryptoace33/status/2086445602938720676)).
- A separate citizen theory thread ([@RBBL_ROUZR](https://x.com/RBBL_ROUZR/status/2086444418828013683)) claimed excavation near the house could relate to underground access — **entirely unverified** and recorded only as a social-media claim.

## Citizen timelines that name West 925 South

X and case-log discussions also assert:

- The grey Challenger was spotted on **West 925 South** around **00:30 on 9/11/2025** — matching the court Ring address corridor.
- Google Trends claims (citizen graphics) that searches from **Israel** for **"W. 925 S Street Orem, UT"** spiked on **Sept 7, 2025** as an alleged "rifle stash point" address — **third-hand trend screenshots**, not authenticated Google Trends exports; treat as **unverified citizen analysis** unless raw Trends data is published.
- One TikTok ([@jessicadabit](https://www.tiktok.com/@jessicadabit/video/7551818222098500894), Sept 2025) titled the house a **"vacant property linked to Charlie Kirk shooter"** — citizen framing; **no charging document** reviewed for this page adopts that label.

## Manhunt context for the neighborhood

Mainstream coverage of Sept 10–11 described a **shelter-in-place**, **SWAT** presence at neighborhood entrances, helicopters, and door-to-door camera collection on the residential streets the suspect fled into after leaving the roof and wooded edge. That is the same Orem residential band this block sits in. Sources: [Marietta Times / AP neighborhood manhunt piece](https://www.mariettatimes.com/news/2025/09/the-bubble-burst-utah-residents-recount-manhunt-for-charlie-kirks-killer-in-their-neighborhood/); [Fox News on home-camera collection](https://www.foxnews.com/us/charlie-kirk-assassination-authorities-collect-home-surveillance-video-may-show-killer-fleeing-scene); [Fox 13 ATF door-to-door](https://www.fox13now.com/news/local-news/northern-utah/atf-police-go-door-to-door-in-orem-neighborhood-searching-for-surveillance-footage).
