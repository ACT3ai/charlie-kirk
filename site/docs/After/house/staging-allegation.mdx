# The Staging Allegation — 691 W 925 S

:::caution Unverified — this is a lead, not a finding
What follows is an **unverified, unattributed allegation.** No court filing, charging document, or law-enforcement statement reviewed for this page names this address as a planning or staging site. **[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted.** No living person — including the property's [owner of record](./ownership) — is accused here of any crime. This page exists to state the claim precisely and to define what would be needed to prove or disprove it.
:::

## The claim, exactly as recorded

The allegation appears in the investigation's master file under a heading about a possible staging house, and reads in full:

> "It is alleged that the attackers planned from this house / building: 691 W 925 S St, Orem, UT 84058"

That is the entire claim. It carries **no named source, no date, no described evidence, and no chain of custody.** It asserts a possibility — that the people responsible for the [UVU shooting](/UVU/overview) used this address as a **planning or staging location** — and nothing more.

## What the claim does and does not say

- It says the house may have been used to **plan or stage** the operation. It does **not** describe it as a firing position. The [property sits only about a quarter-mile from the north edge of the UVU campus](./location-and-property) — walking distance from the shooting site — but authorities placed the reported firing position on the Losee Center roof, not at this house.
- It does **not** name who allegedly used the house, when, or how.
- It does **not** connect the [owner of record](./ownership) to any wrongdoing, and neither does this site.
- It is **not** corroborated by any public record found for this page, including [rental and listing history](./rental-listings).

## What would confirm — or kill — it

Treat this as a checklist for any investigator, journalist, or citizen researcher:

1. **A named source.** Who first alleged this, and on what basis? An anonymous line in a file is not evidence.
2. **A rental or occupancy record.** A lease, a short-term booking, or utility/occupancy data placing non-owners in the house around September 2025 — see [Rental & Listing History](./rental-listings).
3. **Device or vehicle data.** Phone-location, ALPR, or vehicle records tying anyone connected to the attack to this address.
4. **A law-enforcement action.** Any search warrant, evidence log, or filing referencing 691 W 925 S. None is known.
5. **Physical or photographic evidence.** Anything tying the house to the operation beyond assertion.

Until at least one of these exists, the honest label is **unconfirmed rumor recorded as an open lead** — worth chasing, not worth repeating as fact.

## Why it is worth documenting anyway

Off-site planning and staging locations are a real feature of coordinated attacks, and "was an off-campus property used?" is a legitimate investigative question the public record has not answered. Documenting the lead — with the property facts, the ownership record, and the listing history laid alongside it — makes the claim **checkable** instead of leaving it as a floating rumor. If the lead is false, the surrounding facts help show that; if it is true, they are where the confirmation will surface. Either way, the responsible move is to mark it unverified and ask for a real investigation.

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

An anonymous claim about a staging house can only be resolved by the records a mandated, transparent investigation can compel — leases, device data, warrants, and evidence logs. That is the purpose of the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview).
