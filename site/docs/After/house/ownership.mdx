# 691 W 925 S — Ownership on Record

:::caution No owner is accused of anything
This page reports **public property records and public university board minutes.** Naming the university, the prior owner, or the seller here is a matter of record only. Nothing on this page alleges that **[Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview)**, the prior owner, or the seller knew of, participated in, planned, or is in any way connected to the [September 10, 2025 shooting](/UVU/overview) or the [staging allegation](./staging-allegation). No record reviewed for this page links any of them to wrongdoing. An innocent explanation — a university buying an adjacent home to expand its footprint years before the event — fits every document below.
:::

## The headline record: UVU bought this house in 2019

The single most important ownership fact is documented in **Utah Valley University's own governing-board records**: the university **purchased 691 West 925 South** several years before the assassination.

| Field | Value (from UVU / Regents board records) |
|-------|------------------------------------------|
| Property | 691 West 925 South, Orem |
| Buyer | **Utah Valley University** (a public institution) |
| Seller | the **Theobald family** (the "Paul Theobald house") |
| Price | **$900,000**, the appraised value, paid with institutional funds |
| Lot | 1.239 acres |
| Home | 7,486 square feet, described as "in good condition" |
| Relationship to campus | **"contiguous to campus"** — the purchase was framed as a way to **expand the campus footprint to the north** |
| Stated use | the home "could be used for hosting events or housing campus visitors" |
| Approvals | UVU Board of Trustees and the Utah Board of Regents, 2019 |

Sources: the [UVU Board of Trustees agenda, January 17, 2019](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/agendas/01-17-19_trustee_agenda.pdf); the [UVU Board of Trustees meeting record, June 18, 2019](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/504485.pdf); and the [Utah Board of Regents agenda, May 17, 2019](https://ushe.edu/wp-content/uploads/pdf/agendas/20190517/TABS_2019-05-17.pdf).

Two consequences follow, and both matter to every other page in this section:

- **The current owner is a public university, not a private family.** That is unusual for a "staging house" theory and cuts in more than one direction — a university-owned property has institutional keys, staff access, and event use, but it is also subject to open-records law.
- **The property is next to campus, not miles away.** UVU's board called it "contiguous to campus." That directly contradicts the real-estate-aggregator description of a home in "Sunset Heights" several miles off, and it lines up with the citizen-investigation video that places the building [directly across from the Losee Center](./location-and-property). See the [location page](./location-and-property) for the geography.

## Board minutes — exact ratification language

The June 18, 2019 UVU Board of Trustees minutes state that the Executive Committee had already voted in May to approve the purchase "as an extension of the Master Plan," that a contract pending Trustee and Regents approval "was accepted by the Theobalds (owners) for the appraised value of $900,000," and that the full board ratified the purchase of **691 West 925 South, Orem** without opposition (motion by Trustee Rick Nielsen; second by Trustee Taylor).

Local circulation of the board action (preserved in community Facebook posts quoting the cover sheet) added: the house has **"excellent access to the University from Campus Drive"** and **"could be used for hosting events or housing campus visitors."** Homes for sale along the south side of 925 South were framed as a chance to **"expand the campus footprint to the north in a logical manner."**

Sources: [6-18-2019 trustee minutes (PDF)](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/minutes/6-18-2019_trustee_minutes_final.pdf); [June 18 2019 PMN packet](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/504485.pdf); [Jan 17 2019 trustee agenda](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/agendas/01-17-19_trustee_agenda.pdf); [June 18 2019 public agenda extract](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/agendas/06-18-19_trustees_public_meeting_agenda.pdf).

## The deed chain (summary)

Full entry numbers live on [Deed Chain](./deed-chain). Sequence:

1. **Broderick** (DeRae / Pauline) — College Heights developers; sold **1993**.
2. **Kostas & Karin Markides** — **1993–1998**.
3. **Paul H. & Dorothy H. Theobald** — **Dec 1998 – May 2019** ("Paul Theobald house").
4. **Utah Valley University** — **May 24, 2019** → present.

Paul Theobald died **April 1, 2021**; Dorothy died **August 21, 2012**. Neither is accused of anything; they sold a campus-edge home to the university years before the 2025 event. See [Prior Owners](./prior-owners).

## How to read this record

- **Ownership is public; involvement is not implied.** That a public university owns a house says nothing about what any third party may have done in or near it. See the [staging allegation](./staging-allegation) page for why the two questions are kept strictly separate.
- **A stale serial is not the current title.** The Markidas entry is an artifact of re-serialization, not evidence of present ownership.
- **A university purchase for "events / visitor housing" is an ordinary, documented reason** — recorded here so readers weigh the innocent explanation alongside the lead.
