# Prior Owners and Connected People

:::caution No living person is accused of wrongdoing
Every name below appears because it is on a **deed, tax roll, business registration, or published obituary**. Ownership of a house that later became the subject of a citizen-investigation lead **does not imply** knowledge of, participation in, or connection to the [September 10, 2025 shooting](/UVU/overview). Where status is known, it is listed. Living persons (if any) are described only with attributed public records.
:::

The [deed chain](./deed-chain) runs: **Broderick → Markides → Theobald → Utah Valley University**. This page is the people-facing companion to that instrument log.

---

## 1. DeRae and Pauline Broderick — developers / early title

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Names | **DeRae (De Rae) Broderick** and **Pauline Sharp Broderick** |
| Role | Early fee owners / College Heights subdivision developers; construction-company registration at this address |
| Status | **Deceased** — DeRae **June 4, 2019** (Orem); Pauline **June 5, 2021** (Orem) |
| Connection to case | **None found** beyond historical title and subdivision of the land |

**Public records.** Utah County shows Pauline S. Broderick as an early owner of record on serial **36:443:0006**, with a 1993 quitclaim from DeRae Broderick (as trustee) to Pauline, then a warranty deed the same week to the Markides family. A Florida foreign-corporation filing for **Broderick & Howell Construction Company** listed **691 W. 925 South, Orem, UT** as an address, with **Howell, Leland** also named — consistent with citizen-investigator notes that the firm was registered there in **1991**. DeRae's obituary (Sundberg-Olpin Mortuary) places him as a longtime Orem resident who died days after the house closed to UVU in May 2019 — chronological coincidence only; no case connection is claimed or found.

Sources: [deed chain entry 17408–17409-1993](./deed-chain); [DeRae Broderick obituary](https://www.sundbergolpinmortuary.com/obituaries/derae-broderick); [Pauline Broderick obituary](https://www.sundbergolpinmortuary.com/obituaries/pauline-broderick); [Sunbiz entity detail for Broderick & Howell Construction](https://search.sunbiz.org/Inquiry/corporationsearch/SearchResultDetail?inquirytype=EntityName&directionType=ForwardList&searchNameOrder=BRODERICKHOWELLCONSTRUCTION%20P291640&aggregateId=forp-p29164-ba21c0e1-73f9-4608-a2e4-80553e9fe5ea&searchTerm=BRODEMAN%20LLC&listNameOrder=BRODERICKCONSTRUCTION%20P050001132310); X discussion of the construction registration by [@artisbrutal2021](https://x.com/artisbrutal2021/status/1968839633799635254).

## 2. Kostas and Karin E. Markides — mid-1990s owners

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Names | **Kostas Markides** (also spelled **Markidas** on the obsolete serial 36:214:0001) and **Karin E. / Karine E. Markides** |
| Role | Owners **1993–1998** per warranty deeds |
| Status | **Unknown** (no reliable public obituary located in this research pass) |
| Connection to case | **None found** |

**Public records.** They took title from Pauline Broderick in March 1993 (WD 17409-1993) and sold to Paul and Dorothy Theobald in December 1998 (WD 125453-1998). A 1996 quitclaim put sole title in Kostas. The older county mobile-view page still shows "Markidas, Kostas" with mailing address at the house — that is a **stale serial**, not current ownership. No document reviewed for this page ties either person to the 2025 events.

Sources: [deed chain](./deed-chain); [old serial 36:214:0001](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/32916/Property.asp?av_serial=362140001002).

## 3. Paul H. and Dorothy H. Theobald — long-term owners who sold to UVU

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Names | **Paul Homer Theobald** and **Dorothy Carol Henderson Theobald** |
| Role | Owners roughly **December 1998 – May 2019**; sellers to UVU |
| Status | **Deceased** — Dorothy **August 21, 2012** (at home in Orem); Paul **April 1, 2021** (St. George, age 93) |
| Connection to case | **None found.** They sold the house to the university **six years before** the shooting; Paul died in 2021 |

**Public records and family context.** Deseret News and Daily Herald obituaries document an ordinary Utah family life: marriage December 15, 1947; Dorothy died at home in Orem in 2012; Paul later married Beulah Barney (November 23, 2012) and died in St. George in 2021. Board minutes and Facebook-circulated local-news extracts call the property the **"Paul Theobald house."** A **2002 Deseret News** missionary-reunion notice lists the reunion location as **691 W. 925 South, Orem**, contact **Jae Theobald** — evidence the family was using the house as a private home well into the 2000s. People-search aggregators still list **Jae Theobald** among historical residents and name relatives (Paul, Beulah, Brent, Elizabeth, Grace, and others); those are **directory echoes**, not accusations.

**Neighbor property note (not this parcel).** Utah County still shows **Theobald** and **Theobald Northeast LLC** connected to **665 W 925 S** and related parcels on the same block — the family kept nearby real estate after selling 691. That is ordinary estate/property management, recorded here only so researchers do not confuse **665** with **691**.

Sources: [Dorothy Theobald obituary (Deseret)](https://www.deseret.com/2012/8/23/20712981/obituary-theobald-dorothy/); [Paul H. Theobald obituary (Herald Extra)](https://www.heraldextra.com/lifestyles/2021/apr/05/paul-h-theobald/); [2002 missionary reunion notice](https://www.deseret.com/2002/10/4/19680529/missionary-reunions-abound-in-s-l-area/); [UVU June 18 2019 minutes](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/minutes/6-18-2019_trustee_minutes_final.pdf); [deed 46392-2019](./deed-chain); [FastPeopleSearch address page](https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/address/691-w-925-s_orem-ut-84058) (historical resident list — use with caution).

## Cross-check against the investigation file

For this research pass, names **Broderick, Markides/Markidas, Theobald, Jae Theobald** were checked against the public site structure and open web results for any pre-existing case connection beyond property ownership. **No charging document, court filing, or law-enforcement statement reviewed here names any of them as suspects or witnesses in the Kirk case.** The only living-person caution on the block that *does* appear in court media is the **Noble** household at **680 W 925 S** — cooperating Ring-camera witnesses, not suspects — covered on [W 925 S Block & Court Video](./w-925-south-block).
