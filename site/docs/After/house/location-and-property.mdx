# 691 W 925 S — Location and the Property

This page collects the plain physical facts about the address at the center of the [staging allegation](./staging-allegation). None of it, on its own, implies wrongdoing — it is the baseline a reader needs before weighing any claim about the house.

## The address

- **Street address:** 691 W 925 S, Orem, UT 84058
- **County:** Utah County, Utah
- **Neighborhood:** Sunset Heights, Orem (just north of the UVU campus), per real-estate aggregators
- **ZIP+4 on county mailing record:** 84058-6784

## What the property is

Public real-estate databases describe the home as follows. Figures differ slightly between sources, so both are shown rather than a single "clean" number:

| Attribute | Value (as listed publicly) |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| Property type | Single-family home |
| Bedrooms | 6 |
| Bathrooms | 6 |
| Living area | ~7,486 sq ft |
| Year built | 1980 |
| Lot size | ~0.82 acre (older county record) / **1.239 acre (UVU board record, Zillow, Movoto)** |
| Neighborhood | Real-estate sites tag it "Sunset Heights," but UVU's board calls it **"contiguous to campus"** (see below) |

The lot-size discrepancy (county acreage vs. aggregator acreage) is typical of these databases and is noted here rather than resolved — the county figure is generally the one to trust for the taxed parcel. Sources: the [Trulia listing](https://www.trulia.com/p/ut/orem/691-w-925-s-orem-ut-84058--2075264899), the [Movoto listing](https://www.movoto.com/orem-ut/691-w-925-s-orem-ut-84058/pid_xoiz29qcoh/), and the [Utah County land record](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/32916/Property.asp?av_serial=362140001002).

## Distance and relationship to the shooting site

**The house is close to campus — this is the single most important geographic fact, and an earlier draft of this page had it wrong.** By the Orem street grid and by geocoding, **691 W 925 S sits only about a quarter-mile from the northern edge of the [Utah Valley University campus](/UVU/overview)** — a few minutes' walk, not the "roughly three miles" a previous version stated.

The strongest proof is UVU's own paperwork. When the university [bought this house in 2019](./ownership), its Board of Trustees described the property as **"contiguous to campus"** and framed the purchase as a way to **"expand the campus footprint to the north"** — an institution does not call a home three miles away "contiguous." UVU's [Computer Science Building sits at 601 West 1000 South](https://ushpo.utah.gov/venue/utah-valley-university-computer-science-building-room-cs404/); the house at 691 West 925 South is one block north of it on the same grid. The board record and the building grid agree: this is a campus-edge property, which is why the aggregators' "Sunset Heights, several miles off" tag is simply a geocoding error.

The grid makes it concrete. UVU's north-campus building, the Utah County Academy of Sciences, carries the address **940 W 800 S**; the house is at **691 W 925 S** — about **125 address-units south and 250 units east**, which on Orem's grid is roughly **0.2 mile**. Geocoding puts the house near **40.2801, −111.7106** and the UVU campus point near **40.2778, −111.7139**, a straight-line separation of **about 0.25 mile**. The exact door-to-door distance to the [Losee Center courtyard](/UVU/overview) where Charlie Kirk was struck should still be measured on a current map, but it is well **under a mile** — walking distance.

| Reference point | Grid address | Approx. from the house |
|-----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| The house | 691 W 925 S | — |
| UVU north-campus (UCAS) | 940 W 800 S | ~0.2 mi |
| UVU campus point (geocode) | ~40.2778, −111.7139 | ~0.25 mi |

So the property is **not** a distant address in "south Orem" — it is an ordinary residential home **immediately north of the UVU campus**. That proximity does not by itself prove anything: thousands of homes sit near a university, and closeness is exactly what an innocent explanation predicts too. But it does mean the [staging allegation](./staging-allegation) describes a house **within easy walking distance of the shooting site**, which is why the lead is worth checking rather than dismissing on distance grounds. Note also that the house is a residence near — not a firing position on — the campus; the shooting position reported by authorities was the Losee Center roof, not this address. Sources: [UVU campus address / UCAS grid address](https://www.uvu.edu/maps/), and geocoding of the property grid address.
