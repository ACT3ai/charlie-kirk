# 691 W 925 S — Web Search Log

This page is the open notebook for the [house investigation](./overview): the actual web searches run on the address, what each one turned up, and what came up empty. It exists so the research is **reproducible** — you can run the same queries, check the same records, and pick up where the last pass left off instead of starting cold. Every claim on the other house pages traces back to a source found through one of the searches below. The reason a single Orem address warrants this much record-pulling is the event it sits next to: Charlie Kirk was assassinated a short distance away on September 10, 2025, and the [broader cover-up questions](/CoverUp/overview) mean ordinary public records are worth capturing before they change.

:::note How to use this page
Copy a query into Google (or any search engine) and you should surface the same records. Where a link is a **primary record** — a county land record, a university board minute — trust it over a real-estate aggregator. Aggregator distances and "neighborhood" tags for this address have proven **unreliable**; the government records are the ground truth.
:::

## Searches that paid off

**Property basics** — search: `691 W 925 S Orem UT 84058 property`
Surfaced the core record set and the physical specs (6 bed / 6 bath, ~7,486 sq ft, built 1980).
Best hits: the [Utah County land record](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/32916/Property.asp?av_serial=362140001002), the [Trulia listing](https://www.trulia.com/p/ut/orem/691-w-925-s-orem-ut-84058--2075264899), and the [Movoto listing](https://www.movoto.com/orem-ut/691-w-925-s-orem-ut-84058/pid_xoiz29qcoh/).

**The county owner of record** — the [Utah County land record page](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/32916/Property.asp?av_serial=362140001002)
Returns owner **Markidas, Kostas**, parcel serial **36:214:0001**, acreage 0.82, and a serial life of 1983–1993 — an **older snapshot**, not the live title. See [Ownership on Record](./ownership).

**The breakthrough** — search: `"691 W 925 S" Orem sold history owner Markidas`
Pairing the address with "sold history" and the owner surname surfaced a **Utah System of Higher Education Board of Regents agenda** revealing that **Utah Valley University bought this house in 2019 for $900,000.** This single result reshaped the entire section.
Hit: the [USHE Board of Regents agenda, May 17, 2019](https://ushe.edu/wp-content/uploads/pdf/agendas/20190517/TABS_2019-05-17.pdf).

**Confirming the UVU purchase** — search: `"Utah Valley University" 925 South residential property purchase board trustees 2019 appraised campus`
Confirmed the **Theobald family** as seller, **1.239 acres / 7,486 sq ft**, the property called **"contiguous to campus,"** and the purchase framed as expanding the footprint north.
Hits: the [UVU Board of Trustees meeting, June 18, 2019](https://www.utah.gov/pmn/files/504485.pdf) and the [UVU Board of Trustees agenda, January 17, 2019](https://www.uvu.edu/president/docs/trustees/agendas/01-17-19_trustee_agenda.pdf).

**Campus geography** — search: `UVU Losee Center Computer Science building 925 South Orem campus map`
Established that UVU's **Computer Science Building sits at 601 West 1000 South** — one block south of the house, proving the campus-edge location.
Hit: the [Utah SHPO venue record for the UVU CS Building](https://ushpo.utah.gov/venue/utah-valley-university-computer-science-building-room-cs404/).

**Neighborhood evidence** — search: `Charlie Kirk shooting house across from UVU campus vehicles barricade grey Challenger Ring camera`
Surfaced the post-shooting camera canvass and the residential-camera reporting behind [Neighborhood & Witnesses](./neighborhood-reports).
Hits: [Fox 13 — ATF/police door-to-door canvass](https://www.fox13now.com/news/local-news/northern-utah/atf-police-go-door-to-door-in-orem-neighborhood-searching-for-surveillance-footage); [ABC4 — homeowner whose camera caught the suspect](https://www.abc4.com/news/charlie-kirk-shooting/homeowner-alleged-charlie-kirk-shooter-surveillance-camera/); [ABC News visual timeline (grey Challenger, 8:29 a.m.)](https://abcnews.com/US/visual-timeline-charlie-kirk-shooting-unfolded/story?id=125478526).

## Searches that came up empty

- **Direct Airbnb / Vrbo search for the exact address** — no listing found. Short-term-rental platforms hide addresses until booking, so a null result here is expected and proves nothing; the deeper test is listing-ID and STR-analytics archaeology, still to do.
- **Real-estate aggregator "neighborhood" tags** — sites label the address "Sunset Heights" and imply it is miles from campus. This is a **geocoding error**, contradicted by UVU's own "contiguous to campus" board language. Do not rely on aggregator location framing for this parcel.
- **Fetching aggregator pages and large PDFs directly** — Trulia/Movoto/Homes.com block automated fetches, and the largest university PDFs are scanned images with no searchable text. The findings above came from search-result extracts and the smaller government meeting records instead.

### Reusable technique + the 691-vs-683 two-parcel distinction

**Technique that works every time:** the Utah County **`AbstractReverse.asp?av_serial=<serial>`** endpoint returns a full grantor/grantee document history as clean text (it fetches where aggregators 403). Pull it for any serial on the block.

Using it, the **house next door was separated out** so the two are never conflated again:

- **691 W 925 S** (old serial 36:214:0001 → re-platted 1993 as College Heights **Plat J**, live serial **36:443:0006**) — chain **Broderick → Markides → Theobald → UVU**; current owner **Utah Valley University**. [Abstract, old serial](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/AbstractReverse.asp?av_serial=362140001).
- **683 W 925 S** (serial **36:214:0002**) — chain **Broderick → Howell → Steel → Anderson → Vandagriff → 683 Properties LLC**; current owner **683 Properties LLC** (2020–2021), **not** UVU. [Abstract](https://www.utahcounty.gov/landrecords/AbstractReverse.asp?av_serial=362140002).

**Palantir debunk** — search: `683 W 925 S Orem "683 Properties LLC" OR "683 Capital" Palantir` → **683 Properties LLC** (owns 683) is **not** **683 Capital Management** (a hedge fund holding Palantir stock). Similar number, unrelated company; neither owns the house. [Grok correction #1](https://x.com/grok/status/1971547175177134231), [#2](https://x.com/grok/status/1971622977277223331).
