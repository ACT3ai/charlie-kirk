# The House at 691 W 925 S, Orem

:::caution Unverified allegation — read this first
The claim that attackers planned or staged the assassination from this house is an **unverified citizen-investigation allegation**. No charging document, court filing, or law-enforcement statement reviewed for this page names this address. **[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted.** The person listed as the property's owner in public land records is **not accused here of any crime**, and nothing on these pages should be read as claiming the owner knew of, participated in, or is connected to any wrongdoing. These pages document a lead and ask for a proper investigation — they do not assert facts about any living person.
:::

A citizen-investigation lead holds that the people behind the [September 10, 2025 shooting](/UVU/overview) at Utah Valley University may have **planned or staged** the operation from a single-family house **just north of the UVU campus** — **691 W 925 S, Orem, UT 84058**. This section gathers what is publicly knowable about that specific property: what it physically is, who holds title to it in county records, whether it has appeared as a rental, and exactly where the staging claim comes from and how strong it is.

**What this address is.** 691 W 925 S is a large single-family home on the **College Heights** plats just north of UVU — and, contrary to the "several miles away" impression some real-estate aggregators give, it sits only about **a quarter-mile from the north edge of campus**, walking distance from where Charlie Kirk was shot (see [Location & the Property](./location-and-property)). Public records describe a **7,486-square-foot, six-bedroom house built in 1980** on **1.239 acres** (Lot 2, Plat J). The **live** Utah County serial is **36:443:0006**; the older **36:214:0001** "Markidas" page is a dead serial. The [full deed chain](./deed-chain) now runs instrument-by-instrument: **Broderick → Markides → Theobald → UVU**. The headline fact remains the university's own board minutes: **[Utah Valley University bought this house in 2019](./ownership)** for **$900,000** from **Paul and Dorothy Theobald**, calling it *"contiguous to campus"* with *"excellent access… from Campus Drive"* and a stated use of hosting events or visitor housing. Tax notices now mail to **800 W University Parkway** — [assessed values and the post-purchase tax silence](./tax-and-values) match institutional ownership. Profiles of every named prior owner (all historical title figures; **none accused**) are on [Prior Owners & People](./prior-owners).

**Where the claim comes from.** The staging allegation in the master file is still a bare line: attackers allegedly planned from this house. Citizen coverage has since piled on — the [@DiligentDenizen "command center" video](./citizen-investigation-coverage), a TikTok calling it a vacant property "linked" to the shooter, and X threads noting UVU ownership. Separately, **court media from the Robinson preliminary hearing** puts a grey Dodge Challenger on **Ring video at 680 W 925 S** (~00:30 on Sept 11) on the **same street** — documented on the [W 925 S block page](./w-925-south-block). That is real official evidence about the **block**, not yet proof about **691**. The [Staging Allegation](./staging-allegation) page keeps the claim unmarked as fact and lists what would confirm or kill it. The same account has since posted a short clip arguing that a telescoping antenna at the construction site matches a **tactical communications mast** sold for police and military vehicles — logged, unverified, on [The 5G Comms Mast](./comms-antenna-mast).

**How to read these pages.** Treat everything here as a **research file, not a conclusion.** Property and deed facts are checkable; staging is not yet. Start with [Rental & Listing History](./rental-listings) for occupancy trails, then [neighborhood canvass and camera reporting](./neighborhood-reports). Because the house is public property, the [GRAMA templates](./records-requests) still matter most — UVU use logs and the police canvass file. The [web search log](./web-search-log) records every query used so you can extend the trail.

On August 9, 2026, citizen journalist [@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2086519443971318155) posted a 7-minute-48-second video calling the property the assassination's **"command center."** The post reads, near verbatim:

> ‼️🇺🇸: Charlie Kirk Black Ops Assassination "Command Center" 👀
>
> "They" created a barricade using vehicles to block access to a building directly across from Losee Center @ UVU 9/10 morning Charlie Kirk was assassinated.
>
> This is the SAME property the "shooter" used to escape after the hit—per eye witnesses.
>
> This is the SAME place where the grey Challenger parked later that night—per Ring camera.
>
> The building has a clear view of the Losee building & ALL UVU roofs south of the Computer Science building.

Note the geography: the video places the property **directly across from the Losee Center**, on elevated ground overlooking the [UVU campus](/UVU/overview). Independent grid and geocoding checks for 691 W 925 S **support the close placement, not the "several miles away" impression** — the address is roughly a quarter-mile from the north edge of campus, so the aggregators' distant framing appears to be the error (see [Location & the Property](./location-and-property)). What still needs on-the-ground confirmation is the **exact building** in the video and whether its rooftop line of sight to the Losee Center is real: the narrator's "clear view of ALL UVU roofs" is a strong claim that should be checked against a site visit or a sightline analysis before it is treated as fact.

## Key Statements from the Video

From the video narration by @DiligentDenizen, Aug 9, 2026 (full transcript on file; all claims are the narrator's allegations, not established fact):

* **The barricade.** "They used the box truck and the delivery truck to ensure that nobody could get in… they've got the dumpster pushed all the way back up against the house… the only entrance is through this door, and then they use the delivery truck to block the remainder, up on the curb, over the landscaping and the bushes… they have created a barrier to block entry inside this building."
* **The truck over the landscaping.** "They pulled the truck up over this bush, touching the box truck to block this driveway and access into the building… They could have just pulled the truck up normal right here, but if they did that, it would leave the sidewalk open."
* **The staging claim.** "I would say this is where they're staging. If this is an operation, this is their command center. This is where they're staging everything offsite, far enough away so that there's not 3,000 eyes on them, but close enough where they can radio, coordinate, see what's going on, have eyes above, planes. Remember the [planes](/Planes/overview) that were dipping down low?"
* **The sightline.** "This is up on a hill… You can see the entire roof of the Losee Center from that house… You can see all of the roofs of UVU from this house. It's the perfect location. If you were in here and you looked over there, you would see him running across that roof."
* **The conclusion (narrator's own).** "Nothing about it's normal. This is their command center, 100%."

The video's sightline claim ties directly to the rooftop questions in [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview): a watcher at this elevation could allegedly have kept "eyes on the shooter the entire time he was there." None of this has been confirmed by any official source, and no one — including the property's owner of record — is accused of any crime.

On **August 12, 2026**, citizen journalist [@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2087393163778347089) posted dated aerial imagery alongside a claim tying a **white Kia Soul** to two separate locations. The post reads, near verbatim:

> ‼️🇺🇸: Morning of 9/10/25 before Charlie Kirk's assassination, a WHITE KIA SOUL ODDLY turned around EXACTLY WHERE THE MVRD3R weapon was found.
>
> On 11/4/25 I went to Lance TWIGGS' HOUSE to try and interview them and there was a WHITE KIA SOUL parked there.
>
> Hat tip [@baroncoleman](https://x.com/baroncoleman) 🫡

The account credits **@baroncoleman** for the aerial-imagery observation. The imagery carries a **"Wed Sep 10 2025"** date stamp — the morning of the shooting — and covers the same residential block and wooded strip north of campus documented on [Location & the Property](./location-and-property) and the [W 925 S block page](./w-925-south-block).

**How to weigh this.** This is an **unverified citizen-investigation claim**, and every part of it needs independent checking before it means anything:

* A white Kia Soul is an extremely common vehicle. Two sightings of the same **make and color** at two locations is not evidence they are the **same car** — that requires a plate, a VIN, or distinguishing marks, none of which are offered.
* The claim that the vehicle "turned around exactly where the weapon was found" depends on a precise weapon-recovery location and a precise reading of a single aerial frame. Neither has been confirmed against any charging document or law-enforcement statement reviewed for this page.
* **Lance Twiggs is not charged with any crime** in connection with the assassination and is not accused of anything here. Reporting has described Twiggs as an associate of [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson), who is **charged, not convicted**. A vehicle parked at a family residence is not evidence of wrongdoing by anyone living there.
* No official source has connected any Kia Soul to the case.

The Kia Soul post quotes the account's own **November 4, 2025** clip from the doorstep visit. The narration in that clip is brief and states only that no one answered:

> "I got no answer at the home of Lance Twigg's family, and his car does not appear to be in the driveway."

The written post accompanying that November clip made a separate claim — near verbatim — that Twiggs' own vehicle, described as an **Infiniti X35**, was **no longer** at the family residence despite earlier reports that it had been parked there for weeks, and that Twiggs had reportedly not been seen publicly for roughly six weeks as of late October 2025. Those are the account's characterizations of other outlets' reporting, not verified facts, and the residents were not reached for comment. Nothing in the clip shows a Kia Soul; the vehicle claim rests on the still imagery and the account's later recollection.
