# Citizen Investigation Coverage

:::note Reported claims, not proven facts
Everything on this page is **attributed citizen or social-media content** unless a court or government source is cited. Citizen investigators are often first to point at an address; they are also often wrong. This log preserves the claims so they can be checked — it does not adopt them as findings.
:::

## @DiligentDenizen — "Command Center" video (Aug 9, 2026)

The most detailed citizen package aimed at this property is the **7:48 walkthrough** posted by [@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2086519443971318155), archived on IPFS for this site (see [overview](./overview)).

**Claims in the post / narration (abridged):**

- Vehicles (box truck + delivery truck + dumpster) formed a **barricade** blocking access the morning of **9/10**.
- The property is the same place the "shooter" used to escape, per "eye witnesses" (unnamed).
- Same place a **grey Challenger** parked later that night, per Ring camera.
- Elevated ground with a **clear view of the Losee Center roof** and other UVU roofs.
- Narrator's conclusion: staging / "command center."

**Independent geography check:** the house **is** campus-edge (~0.25 mi), so the "across from Losee / up on a hill" framing is **plausible**, not absurd — but the video's exact building still needs Street-View / site match. Replies on the thread itself debate whether two houses in the video are the same structure.

**Skepticism on X (same day):** users mocked the idea that a "black ops" team would need a residential command center that close to campus when satellite and remote comms exist — e.g. [@chrisG7676](https://x.com/chrisG7676/status/2086526974428909709). Record both the claim and the pushback.

## University ownership noticed on X

Within hours of the command-center video circulating, posters independently noted UVU ownership and urged satellite checks:

- [@xcryptoace33](https://x.com/xcryptoace33/status/2086445602938720676) (Aug 9, 2026): *"691 W 925 S St, Orem, UT 84058 - I'm pretty sure - the University Charlie was sacrificed - at owns it - check out satellite image of driveway on day if his death."*
- [@RBBL_ROUZR](https://x.com/RBBL_ROUZR/status/2086444418828013683): speculative underground / excavation / trap-door theory tied to the house — **no evidence presented**; logged only as a theory circulating in the same address thread.

County records **confirm** UVU ownership (see [deed chain](./deed-chain)); they do **not** confirm any black-ops use.

## TikTok "vacant property linked to shooter" (Sept 2025)

[@jessicadabit](https://www.tiktok.com/@jessicadabit/video/7551818222098500894) (about Sept 19, 2025) titled a video **"Investigating Vacant Property Linked to Charlie Kirk Shooter"** and named **691 W 925 S, Orem**, describing it as vacant and a short distance from where the shooter descended from the UVU roof. That is **citizen framing**. No charging document reviewed for this page "links" the property to the charged suspect as a staging house. Vacancy itself, if true, would be a UVU-use fact worth confirming by GRAMA — not proof of guilt.

## West 925 South in broader case discourse

Citizen case archives and X threads repeatedly name the **street**, not only 691:

- **Grey Challenger on W 925 S ~00:30 on 9/11** — now aligned with **court-cited Ring at 680 W 925 S** (see [block page](./w-925-south-block)).
- Timing debates about whether Robinson could have driven from southern Utah in time for that sighting (travel-time arithmetic on X; not settled here).
- **Google Trends / "Israel searched W. 925 S Street"** graphics posted by citizen accounts (e.g. threads amplified by @MadeYouThink12 / @baroncoleman materials) — treat as **unauthenticated screenshots** until raw Trends data is published.
- **683 W 925 S / Palantir** confusion — largely **debunked** by LLC vs. hedge-fund distinction ([block page](./w-925-south-block)).
- Construction-history posts noting **Broderick & Howell** registration at 691 in 1991 ([@artisbrutal2021](https://x.com/artisbrutal2021/status/1968839633799635254)) — true as historical business-address color; not a 2025 operational link.
