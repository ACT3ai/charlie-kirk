Most of the argument over [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s September 10, 2025 is fought over
documents — the [Cowboy's Smokehouse receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt), the
[Discord timestamps](/Tyler_Robinson/Discord_Messages), the
[custody clock](./Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody). This page is about something duller and harder to
argue with: **how long the driving actually takes**. On August 31, 2026 the account
[**@AdmetosL**](https://x.com/AdmetosL) posted a two-minute screen-recorded map walkthrough that puts
St. George, [UVU](/UVU/overview), the Dairy Queen, and Panguitch on one map and measures the legs
between them, then asks which arrangement of the day a person could physically perform.

Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, and is presumed innocent. Nothing on this page is a court
finding. The drive times below are the video author's own readings off a mapping tool, and the
Panguitch identification they are being tested against is itself
[claimed by restaurant staff and contested](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline).

## The legs, as measured in the video

According to @AdmetosL, reading distances off the map on screen:

| Leg | Drive time given |
|-----|------------------|
| St. George → UVU (Orem) | **3 hr 44 min** |
| UVU (Orem) → Cowboy's Smokehouse, Panguitch | **3 hr 03 min** |
| Panguitch → St. George | **1 hr 46 min – 2 hr 11 min**, depending on route |

The single number the whole page turns on is the last one. Panguitch is **not** a wild detour from a
UVU-to-St. George drive — it is off the direct path, but only by so much. In the author's phrasing,
routing through Panguitch **"would add over an hour of travel time to the trip between UVU and his
home."** That is the finding, and it cuts in an awkward direction for both sides.

## Two reconstructions of the same day

The video sets two versions of September 10 side by side and asks which one a person could actually
execute.

**The official-narrative version**, as the author summarizes it: Robinson leaves St. George in the
middle of the night, drives 3 hours 44 minutes north, is at UVU on the morning of the 10th, the
shooting happens around midday, he remains in the Orem/Provo area until **at least 6:35 PM** — the
reported [Dairy Queen](/Tyler_Robinson/Tyler_9_10_2025) stop — stays in the area until around
midnight, and only then drives home, arriving around **5 AM**. The author's objection is not about
evidence, it is about biology: that day requires either sleeping somewhere in the Provo area while
being, in his words, "America's Most Wanted," or staying awake close to **24 hours**. Another
commentator ran the same arithmetic further and reached
[a 25-hour day with about 13 hours 20 minutes behind the wheel](/Tyler_Robinson/twenty-five-hour-driving-timeline).

**The alternative version**, which the author offers as the logistically easier fit: Robinson arrives
at UVU in the morning or around midday, goes to the Dairy Queen **early** in the afternoon — "like 2
or 3" — then drives south, reaching Panguitch **before 9 PM**, eats at Cowboy's Smokehouse, and
continues home to St. George arriving **before midnight**. On this reading nobody has to nap in a
manhunt or stay up for a full day; the clock simply closes.

## The problem the author raises against his own case

The post does not stop at the version that is easier to drive. Its most useful line is the one that
argues **against** the alternative:

> "If it turns out the smokehouse receipt is his, then it would make less sense that he would go out
> far from path to home to go eat there and then drive back to UVU to retrieve his rifle."

That is the tension the receipt creates and it has not been resolved by anyone. The
[Cowboy's Smokehouse receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt) (Check #211, ordered 8:55
PM, paid 9:47 PM) places someone in Panguitch that evening. If that someone is Robinson, then the
state's account of the rest of the night — the post-11 PM Orem texts and sightings recorded on
[the surrender page](./Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody) — cannot also be him, because the driving does
not fit. But it equally undercuts the idea of a Panguitch stop **on the way to** anything at UVU. The
author's own concession is that the geometry only works one way at a time.

He offers one reading that would reconcile it — that Robinson may have wanted to be a few hours from
both home and UVU that evening — and then immediately undercuts it: *"he had to known his wanted stars
wouldn't just disappear the next day."*

## Where the home address claim comes from

The video states that Robinson and **Lance Twiggs** shared a rented three-bedroom townhouse in the
**Fossil Hills** complex in St. George. The author is explicit about his sourcing: he asked **Grok**
and read back the answer on camera. That is an **AI-generated claim, not a record**, and it is flagged
here as such — the drive-time arithmetic above depends on the St. George end point, so the provenance
of that end point matters.

## What this does and does not establish

**It does** put a number on the objection people have been making qualitatively — that the state's
September 10 is an unusually long day for one person, and that Panguitch is close enough to the route
home that a stop there is not absurd on its face.

**It does not** place any person anywhere. Drive times are a constraint, not a location. The video
measures what is possible; it does not show who was in the car, and the receipt that anchors the
Panguitch end has never been publicly matched to a card. See
[Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline)
for what restaurant staff say, and [Tyler Robinson — Travel](/Tyler_Robinson/Travel) for the wider
movement record.
