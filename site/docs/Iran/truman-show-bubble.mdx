**[Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent)** — a retired Green Beret and the former Director of the
**National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC)** — is the same official who says the FBI stopped his
foreign-nexus review of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s killing once a suspect was in
custody. In a separate March 2026 interview with **Harrison Smith**, Kent describes, in his own
words, how he believes Iran policy was actually set: an information bubble around
**[President Trump](/People/donald-trump)** that Kent calls "the echo chamber," and that his
interviewer calls **"Truman Show–esque."**

That matters to this section for one reason. Charlie Kirk was reportedly trying to get *inside*
that circle to argue against the [Iran war](./charlie-anti-iran-war). Kent is describing the
pressure system Charlie was reportedly pushing against — from the vantage point of a senior
official who was in the room. Everything below is **attributed public commentary**. Kent alleges
no crime, names no plot, and does not connect any of it to Charlie Kirk's death.

## What Kent actually said

Asked how a "false reality" could be maintained around a sitting president when the Vice
President and Trump's own sons know better, Kent reportedly answered:

> "From my vantage point, that is basically what happened. And the Truman Show is a good analogy
> to use. I just call it the echo chamber. But the Israelis have great access to senior decision
> makers within our government, and within Trump's inner circle and President Trump himself.
>
> "And so they'll use those relationships basically to kind of circumvent, like, the traditional
> channels of even having to go through the intelligence community or diplomats. So kind of what
> we were saying in the IC wasn't necessarily listened to all the time, and it wasn't the best
> venue for getting things in front of the president."

The claim is specific and institutional: Kent says the analysis coming out of the intelligence
community — his own community — was not reliably the thing reaching the president.

## The mechanism he describes: repetition, not coercion

Kent's account is not about threats. It is about **saturation** — the same argument arriving
through official channels and media channels at the same time:

> "And then you'd have, at the same time, the media talking head saying almost the exact same
> thing. So there was definitely a good deal of coordination, with the media talking heads and
> with what the Israelis were trying to inject into official channels, creating that media echo
> chamber in particular, it moved President Trump's own red line."

## The red line that reportedly moved

The most checkable claim in the clip is about a specific policy shift — from *no nuclear weapon*
to *no enrichment*:

> "I mean, Trump said, Iran can't have a nuclear weapon. The former Supreme Leader said, yeah, we
> agree. We have a fatwa that's been in effect since '04 that we won't have a nuclear weapon. And
> so that was a direct threat to the Israelis. The Israelis sought to move the red line to no
> enrichment. And they basically did that by just repetition, by coming in and saying, no, no,
> America's policy is what Mike Pompeo said in 2018 when he was your Secretary of State, and he
> said no enrichment.
>
> "And then they said that night after night on Mark Levin, the Foundation for Defensive
> Democracies, putting out think pieces on this getting it ran in the New York Post and the New
> York Times, until it kind of just became a reality."

Kent names the vectors he believes carried the repetition: a nightly cable program, a Washington
think tank, and op-ed placement in two major newspapers. **These are descriptions of advocacy and
opinion journalism — lawful activity.** Kent does not allege that any named outlet, organization,
or person did anything illegal, and neither does this page.

## "They created a very small circle"

The part of the clip that touches this investigation most directly is what Kent says happened
*after* the June 2025 fighting and
[Operation Midnight Hammer](./midnight-hammer-12-day-war):

> "And there was a lot of dissent. There was a lot of people putting out alternative ways that we
> could get out of this... even if we are successful in that bombing campaign, the Israelis are
> going to come right back to us and demand regime change.
>
> "Because, again, the Israelis were very clear about what they wanted. And so I think post, post
> June, they just, they created a very small circle around President Trump, and he didn't hear any
> alternatives."

Read alongside this section's other pages, that sentence is the connective tissue. Charlie Kirk
reportedly went to the White House on **June 18, 2025** to argue against regime change — that is,
in the exact window Kent describes as the moment the circle closed. Whether Charlie was one of the
"alternatives" the president stopped hearing is an open question this site does not claim to
answer. See [Charlie's Stand Against the Iran War](./charlie-anti-iran-war) and
[How the Iran War Raised His Risk](./iran-war-motive).

## The poster's objection: "Trump was tricked" is an excuse

Importantly, **[@SenseReceptor](https://x.com/SenseReceptor), who posted this clip, was
criticizing Kent, not amplifying him.** The post is headed *"Massive Joe Kent Red Flags"* and
argues:

> "Here Kent agrees Trump's kept in a 'Truman Show-esque' bubble run by Israelis, and he's simply
> been tricked into the Iran war. This is the slop we were sold re: Covid. Fauci just tricked the
> big orange baby! Trump didn't know any better."

That objection is worth keeping on the page. The "the president was deceived" frame removes
agency from the decision-maker and is, by construction, hard to falsify. A reader can accept
Kent's factual description of access and repetition while rejecting the conclusion that the
president was a passive victim of it.

## The counterpoint

Several ordinary explanations compete with the echo-chamber reading:

- **Allied lobbying is lawful and routine.** Every close U.S. ally cultivates access to senior
  decision makers. Access is not evidence of manipulation, and it is certainly not evidence of
  violence.
- **Presidents overrule their intelligence agencies all the time,** for reasons that have nothing
  to do with foreign influence. Kent's complaint that "what we were saying in the IC wasn't
  necessarily listened to" is a description familiar from every administration.
- **Kent is an interested party.** He says he was sidelined; that is a reason to weigh his account
  carefully in both directions, not to accept or dismiss it outright.
- **Nothing in this clip mentions Charlie Kirk, UVU, or September 10, 2025.** The link to this
  investigation is contextual — it comes from Kent's *other* statements about the halted NCTC
  review, documented at
  [Joe Kent — Foreign Inquiry Shutdown](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown).
