## Who He Is

PB posts as [@PBOnPoint](https://x.com/PBOnPoint) on X, where his profile describes his
work as "Independent Perspective | Predictive Analysis" and points to longer analysis and
live shows on YouTube. At the time this video was posted the account showed 7,808
followers and roughly 3,600 posts. He is an independent commentator, not a journalist at
a news organisation, and not a participant in the case.

He says in this video that he has been covering the Charlie Kirk assassination for
"essentially a year and 9 days" — that is, since shortly after
[September 10, 2025](/Before/overview).

## What He Argues

**Three actors.** PB says there are "three elements at play here," which the transcript
renders as "the USA, Israel and TP Israel." The third is his pejorative coinage for
[TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) — he uses the same term throughout when predicting the
organisation's collapse. He says each of the three was, in his view, "involved in the
assassination and the cover-up of it, from top to bottom." No court has found any of
this, and TPUSA has not been charged with anything in connection with the case.

**His sanction premise.** The load-bearing step in his argument is a general claim: that
"there is no assassination of any kind that can happen on anyone's soil without the
express sanctioning of that government," because otherwise it would constitute an act of
war. From that premise he reasons that a killing on U.S. soil implies U.S. sanction. This
is an assertion of how he believes the world works, not evidence about this case, and he
offers no source for it.

**On the defendant.** PB says he does not believe
[Tyler Robinson](/Killer/overview) "had anything to do with the actual act itself," and
goes further: "I'm not convinced he was even there." He suggests Robinson's life was
"infiltrated" beginning around the end of 2023, as part of an operation he estimates was
18 months in preparation. He allows he may be wrong, and frames a possible unwitting
role: "I don't think he understood what he was there for... perhaps there's something in
his background where he had no choice, perhaps he was threatened, his family was
threatened." He names Mossad, the CIA and the FBI as the kinds of organisation he
believes do this. He presents no documentary evidence for any of it and describes it as
"my own personal opinion."

**His prediction about the trial.** This is the claim the post is built around. PB says
Robinson "is not getting out innocent, or he's not getting out with his life," that
"there will be no trial that goes the distance and that will be fair where he has a
chance at innocence," and that any exculpatory evidence would not be ruled admissible.
His reasoning is that "there must be a fall guy for this. It cannot be completed unless
there is one."

**On Charlie Kirk's motive for being targeted.** He argues Kirk was "a direct threat"
because he opposed war with Iran, wanted the Epstein files released, and wanted
transparency — and stresses these were Kirk's own stated positions: "It's that man's
words, Charlie Kirk, not mine, not any of ours."

**Two outside statements he leans on.** He says Benjamin Netanyahu had said over the
preceding weekend that convincing the United States and Trump to join strikes on Iran
took on the order of a thousand hours. And he paraphrases — his word — Secretary of State
Marco Rubio as having said after the war began that "we did this for Israel. There are no
nuclear weapons." *Editorial note: both are PB's characterisations of what those men
said, given from memory in an unscripted video, and neither is quoted from a verified
transcript. Treat them as his recollection, not as established quotations.*

**Other assertions made in passing.** He states as his belief that Trump was threatened,
that there is "a connection between Butler and Utah," and that the golden pager given to
Trump "was not by accident." These are asserted without supporting material.

**What he predicts next.** He expects the case to go quiet for 12 to 24 months before
trial, and says the public "will have no choice" but to move on. He predicts TPUSA will
be "finished" and unable to operate within 18 months, once parents "catch the drift"; he
predicts Israel will continue to be "outed on a day-to-day basis" in a way that moves
younger American voters; and he predicts the affair will be "the final straw for voters."
His broader political conclusion is that the two-party frame is empty — "there is no
right, there is no left... Republicans and Democrats are in all of it" — and that
elections and midterms are "a scorecard for the people to vote on something where the
game has already been decided."

**His closing theory of the organisation.** He argues TPUSA was "deemed as an
instrument... a tool" — that its reach with young voters made it worth infiltrating and
taking over "to reprogram the young minds for future elections," and that this is "the
endgame." Again, his framing, and unevidenced in the video.

## What The Footage Shows

A single fixed vertical phone shot of one man speaking to camera for the full run time.
No B-roll, no screenshots, no documents held up, no citations on screen. Everything in
the video is spoken assertion. That matters for weighing it: nothing here is a document
the reader can check independently — the value of the page is the record of the position,
not corroboration of it.

## Where This Sits Against The Rest Of The Site

PB's conclusion that Robinson did not fire the shot runs alongside the material collected
under [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview), though he arrives at it by assertion
rather than by the physical and timeline arguments assembled there. His fall-guy framing
overlaps with [Cover Up](/CoverUp/overview). His account of a motive rooted in Kirk's
Iran and Epstein-files positions overlaps with [Motive](/Motive/overview) and the
[Israel](/Israel/overview) section.

He does not address the ballistics question at all, and does not engage with the
explosive-device analysis in [Gun & Bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview). His argument is
political and structural rather than physical.
