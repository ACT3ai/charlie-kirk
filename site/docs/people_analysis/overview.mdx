**Every page in this section is one person's opinion.** These are only people's
opinions. We do want them to be able to give their opinions. This website does not take
any stand behind them being true — but we do want people to challenge them and think
about whether they are true or not.

Nothing in this section is a finding of this site. Read them the way you would listen to
someone argue a case: work out which parts rest on a document you can check, which parts
rest on a premise you are being asked to accept, and which parts are prediction.

The people indexed here are commentators on the case, not participants in it. People who
are *subjects* of the investigation have pages under [People](/People/overview) instead;
podcasters and researchers whose work produced original findings are indexed under
[Influencers](/Influencers/overview). What separates this section is that the unit is
**the argument someone is making**, recorded whole, rather than a piece of evidence.

The reason to host opinions at all is that the official account of this case has been
contested from the first week, and the contest has happened almost entirely in video, on
platforms that delete things. Archiving what someone actually said — with the video
pinned and the transcript saved — means the argument can still be examined later, by
people who want to check it rather than agree with it. That cuts both ways: a claim that
does not survive scrutiny is easier to retire when there is an exact record of it.

Read each page against the evidence sections. Where a speaker's argument rests on
ballistics, check it against [Gun & Bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview); where it rests on the
timeline, check [Before](/Before/overview) and [After](/After/overview); where it rests
on the case against the defendant, check
[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview). Disagreeing with someone on this page is
the intended use of it.

### [PB — @PBOnPoint](./pb-onpoint)

*9 min 53 sec · posted September 1, 2026 · [original post](https://x.com/PBOnPoint/status/2094878764521955626)*

Recorded on a phone the afternoon of the preliminary hearing ruling, because his studio
internet was down. PB argues that Tyler Robinson is a fall guy who will not receive a
fair trial — "he's not getting out innocent, or he's not getting out with his life" — and
says he is not convinced Robinson was even present. He names three actors he holds
responsible, and rests the argument on a general premise of his own: that no
assassination happens on a country's soil without that government's sanction. He closes
with predictions about the next 12 to 24 months.

Everything in it is spoken assertion — no documents, no screenshots, no citations on
screen. **[Read the full analysis and transcript →](./pb-onpoint)**
