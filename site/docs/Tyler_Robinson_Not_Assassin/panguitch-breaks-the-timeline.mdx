# Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline

The state's night-of timeline needs Tyler Robinson in the Orem area all evening, but restaurant
staff 209 miles south in Panguitch say he was sitting at their counter — and nobody drives that
road in the time the timeline leaves.

**Panguitch** is a small town in Garfield County, southern Utah. It is also the name that
citizen investigators say the government's night-of timeline cannot survive. Staff at
**Cowboy's Smokehouse** — also listed as **Cowboy Smokehouse Cafe-Steakhouse** — in Panguitch
have said, on camera and to the [FBI](/FBI/overview), that the quiet man who ate alone at their
counter on the evening of **September 10, 2025** was [**Tyler Robinson**](/People/tyler-robinson).
A photographed receipt puts that meal at roughly **8:55 PM to 9:47 PM**, with the customer
leaving around closing, near **10:00 PM**. [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had been shot
that same day at approximately **12:23 PM** on the [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview)
campus in Orem — **209 road miles / roughly 3 hours 10 minutes to 3 hours 40 minutes north** of
that counter.

The claim runs through figures amplified by [**Candace Owens**](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens),
the citizen journalist [**@DiligentDenizen**](/Influencers/x/diligent-denizen-2) ("Diligent
Denizen"), and related X accounts. **Robinson is charged, not convicted, and is presumed
innocent.** Every claim on this page is attributed to the person or outlet that made it. The
receipt has not been authenticated by any court, the customer's identity has not been officially
confirmed, and the restaurant has since issued a statement of its own — covered in full below.

## Where Panguitch actually is

The distance is the whole argument, so it has to be stated precisely.

| From | To | Road distance | Typical drive time |
|------|-----|---------------|--------------------|
| UVU / Orem | Panguitch | ~200–209 miles | 3 h 09 min – 3 h 40 min |
| Orem | St. George / Washington (Robinson's home area) | — | ~3.5 – 4 hours |

Three things about that table matter:

* **Straight-line distance is irrelevant.** The only number that counts is road miles on
  I-15 south and the connecting routes east and southeast. That is where the 200–209 mile
  figure and the 3-plus-hour drive time come from.
* **Panguitch is not on the way.** It does not sit on the direct, continuous I-15 run from
  Orem down to Cedar City and St. George. Reaching it requires a deliberate exit and detour
  that costs significant additional time. A man simply fleeing south to his home area does
  not pass through Panguitch by accident.
* **It is an intermediate town, not a destination.** Robinson's own home area of
  St. George/Washington is farther south still. So the Panguitch stop, if real, is a chosen
  waypoint — someone sat down and ordered a steak.

## What the Panguitch witnesses say

The claim surfaced first in early-2026 reporting — **Fox News Digital** covered a tip about it
— and was then pursued hard in **July 2026** through on-location interviews and video published
by **@DiligentDenizen** and discussed by **Candace Owens**. The full 34-minute visit recording
and the receipt itself are preserved on their own pages:
[Cowboy's Smokehouse Full Visit Video](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-full-visit) and the
[Cowboy's Smokehouse Receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt).

What the staff describe, in their own words as recorded:

* A **quiet, shy, solo male customer** matching the photos of Robinson released by the FBI ate
  at the counter on the evening of September 10, 2025.
* He ordered a **medium-rare sirloin steak with vegetables and a baked potato** (mashed
  potatoes in some retellings). Accounts differ on drinks — the receipt shows soft drinks;
  the on-camera witness recalled a couple of beers. **No alcohol** is specified in some
  versions of the account.
* He **sat alone, texted on his phone the entire time**, paid by card, and left near closing.
* Staff **recognized him from the FBI photos the next day**, tipped the FBI themselves,
  provided the **last four digits of the card**, and were interviewed.
* **Cameras were inoperable or nonexistent** in the relevant area — one account describes a
  hole in the ceiling where a camera had been removed. This connects to the broader
  [camera questions](/cameras/overview) that recur across this case.
* A **receipt** has been referenced and shown in citizen reporting, with the **last four digits
  visible** and **no customer name** printed — the card was run as a debit transaction, which
  prints no name.

@DiligentDenizen published raw interview video with a server who **"100%" identified** the
customer as Robinson for the **8:55–9:47 PM window**, and said the restaurant's owner
facilitated access to both the receipt and the witness.

**Panguitch** is the seat of **Garfield County** — a small high-desert town on the southern
Utah tourist corridor, better known as a stop near Bryce Canyon than as anything connected to a
national murder case. **Cowboy's Smokehouse** is a family-run main-street barbecue and steak
house there. Nothing about either the town or the restaurant would be part of this
investigation at all except for one claim: that on the night of the assassination, the man the
state says was the shooter sat at that counter and ate dinner.

What the recorded visit establishes about the restaurant itself matters for how much weight the
identification can carry, in both directions:

* **It closes at 10:00 PM.** The employee gives that closing time on camera, and it is what
  makes a **9:47 PM** checkout a near-closing checkout rather than a mid-evening one.
* **The seating is a counter/bar.** The customer is described as sitting at the bar, "close to
  the end, second seat in" — a spot where staff pass repeatedly, which is the basis for the
  claim of multiple interactions rather than one glance across a dining room.
* **The card was run as a debit transaction, so the receipt prints no name.** That single
  technical detail is why this has never been settled: the paper carries a time but not an
  identity, and only the merchant processor can join the two.
* **There was no working camera.** On the recorded visit the owner says the old system "has
  never worked," that he removed the dining-room camera when repainting in the winter of 2023,
  and that the only camera on the property then was a consumer unit watching firewood on the
  patio — later moved inside "after the whole Tyler Robinson thing." A new security system was
  being installed at the time of the recording. See the broader
  [camera questions](/cameras/overview) that recur throughout this case.
* **It is a small-town business that did not ask for this.** The owner describes harassing
  phone calls since the story spread, including one in which a caller screamed obscenities at a
  **14-year-old girl** who answered the phone. He says he reported a number to police.

### Nobody came back to him

The part of the interview investigators treat as most significant is not the identification at
all — it is what happened after it. In his account:

* He told his boss; the boss **contacted the FBI**.
* **"They got our contact information, but we never heard anything from him."** Asked directly
  whether the FBI ever came back after the report: **"Never heard anything."**
* Asked whether the card details were ever checked: **"Not once. I don't know if they checked
  into that. I know they got that information from my boss."**
* Asked whether the **prosecution** ever reached out to have him testify — and then whether the
  **defense** did — his answer covers both: **"No one's reached out regarding anything to do
  with Tyler Robinson."**

He says that silence is what pushed him to send the tip to
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)' team himself: *"that's kind of what prompted me to send
it in. It just didn't sit right with me."* These are his claims. Neither the
[FBI](/FBI/overview) nor either party in the case has publicly responded to them.

## The government's timeline needs him in Orem that night

The prosecution's case keeps Robinson in the **Orem/UVU vicinity for hours after the 12:23 PM
shooting**, because that is what makes the text messages work as evidence. Those texts, with
roommate and partner [**Lance Twiggs**](/People/lance-twiggs), reportedly carry:

* The instruction to look **under the keyboard** for a handwritten note — the note reading
  *"I had the opportunity to take out Charlie Kirk and I'm going to take it."*
* An explicit admission — *"I am, I'm sorry."*
* **Motive** — *"I had enough of his hatred. Some hate can't be negotiated out."*
* **Planning duration** — that he had planned it for a bit over a week.
* **Rifle logistics** — the weapon wrapped in a **towel**, fingerprint worry, and the need to
  go back and **retrieve** it.
* **Real-time surveillance of the manhunt** — police activity in the area, dogs (*"I hope that
  pooch has a bad sniffer"* or similar), waiting for an officer to move so he could act.
* An instruction to Twiggs to **delete the messages**.

Alongside those texts, the official record and hearing testimony reference activity consistent
with him still being near campus or in the Orem area late — a **midnight-or-later officer
encounter**, and vehicle and doorbell-camera sightings **after midnight**. The next morning,
September 11, there is a claimed **gas-station stop farther south** in the
[Cedar City](/Locations/Cedar_City_Maverik) area. He surrendered the evening of September 11 in
Washington County after family involvement.

The rest of the state's evidence — campus surveillance of a figure with a distinctive stiff gait
that speeds up after the shot, DNA said to be consistent with him on the recovered rifle,
trigger, towel and screwdriver, and the handwritten note — is argued elsewhere on this site.
This page is only about **where his body was that evening**, because that is what the texts
require.

## Problem one: the return leg is impossible

If Robinson was physically at the Panguitch counter until **9:47–10:00 PM**, the later
Orem-area texts and sightings cannot be his.

* Leaving Panguitch at **~10:00 PM** puts the earliest realistic arrival back in Orem at
  **1:00–1:40 AM** — and that is with no traffic, no stops, and no slowdowns.
* He therefore **cannot** be sending real-time *"I'm watching the cops / the dogs / waiting for
  them to leave so I can grab the rifle"* updates from the Orem area **after 11 PM** while he is
  still hours to the south.
* The round trip is absurd on its face: shoot at **12:23 PM**, leave the scene, drive three-plus
  hours south for a calm sit-down steak dinner ending at **10:00 PM**, drive three-plus hours
  back north for post-midnight activity near the crime scene, and then eventually turn around
  and head south again toward home. That is six-plus hours of hard driving in both directions,
  on the day of a high-profile assassination, in the middle of an active manhunt.

## Problem two: the southbound leg is impossible too

A second, independent claimed sighting closes the trap from the other end.

Candace Owens and others circulated a photo said to show Robinson at a **Dairy Queen in Orem at
6:38 PM** on September 10 — maroon shirt, roughly **15 minutes from campus**. That location and
time are also part of the affidavit timeline discussed on the
[Dairy Queen Orem](/Locations/Dairy_Queen_Orem) page.

Now do the arithmetic:

| | |
|---|---|
| Depart Orem | 6:38 PM |
| Arrive Panguitch | ~8:45 PM (seated before the 8:55 PM order) |
| Elapsed | **~2 hours 7 minutes** |
| Distance | **209 miles** |
| Required average speed | **~98–99 mph, the entire way** |

That average requires **zero stops, no slowdowns, perfect conditions, and sustained illegal
speeding** for the whole run. Normal driving time is over three hours. Even the most optimistic
nonstop estimates do not get under roughly **2 hours 50 minutes to 3 hours**.

Citizen investigators draw the conclusion bluntly: **either the Dairy Queen photo or the
Panguitch sighting is wrong — or both are — or the person in the two places was not the same
individual**, because no one covers that ground at that speed.

## What this breaks in the government's narrative

The texts are not a supporting detail in the state's account. They are the spine. They supply
the **confession language**, the **motive**, the **planning duration**, the **instructions to
hide and delete evidence**, and the **post-crime actions** including the attempted rifle
recovery.

If Robinson was verifiably in Panguitch at the claimed dinner time, then those messages cannot
be genuine real-time communications from a man still near UVU. They would have to be
**fabricated, pre-written, sent by someone else, or the product of an entirely different
timeline**. That collapses the coherent post-shooting sequence — the lone suspect who lingers
near the scene, generates an electronic trail, and only then flees south. The
[custody-timeline argument](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession) about who typed
the later messages runs on a parallel track and reaches the same place.

It also raises a procedural question that investigators consider the most damning part: **why
did neither the prosecution nor, apparently, the defense formally subpoena the restaurant
records, the full card data, or the staff** in a way that resolved the conflict on the record
during the preliminary hearing process? The on-camera witness says the FBI took the staff's
contact information and **never followed up**, and that as of the recording neither party in the
case had contacted him to testify — despite his claimed direct, hand-to-hand contact with the
customer.

Taken together — **Panguitch eyewitness identifications + the receipt claim + drive-time
impossibility + the Dairy Queen timing conflict** — citizen investigators treat this as proof
that the official "Robinson did it, lingered, then fled" sequence cannot hold. Either he was not
the shooter in the manner described, or critical pieces of the government's evidence, the texts
above all, are unreliable.

## The contested side of the record

This claim is **not established in court** and it is contested. The following belongs on the
record alongside everything above:

* In **July 2026**, the restaurant issued a statement that, **based on the information available
  to them, they have no evidence establishing whether Robinson was there around September 10**.
* The owner — named as **Ralph Perkins** in some accounts — has been reported by some callers as
  later saying it was **not him** following law-enforcement verification, and that the receipts
  circulating publicly were **not the relevant one**. On the recorded visit, the owner had
  already said he was not present that night, that his staff believed it was Robinson, that
  "as far as I know, that wasn't him… it would make sense that it could be," and that
  authorities "never told us it was or wasn't."
* The restaurant has reported **harassing phone calls** since the story spread.
* A single staff identification from a brief interaction, plus an unauthenticated debit receipt
  carrying no printed name, **prove nothing on their own**.

Citizen investigators' answer to all of that is the same in each case: the **initial staff
identifications**, the **tip the restaurant itself made to the FBI**, and the **timing
arithmetic** are what matter, and the fastest way to settle any of it — pulling the merchant
processor records for Check #211 and matching the last four digits — is the step no official
body appears to have taken publicly.

## Why this one is a problem for narrative control

Citizen investigators argue that the reason Panguitch draws such heavy pushback is not that it is
the wildest theory in the case — it is that it is the **most checkable** one. A receipt is a
document. A card has a cardholder. Drive time is arithmetic. Unlike most of the disputed material
in this investigation, this thread could be closed in an afternoon by anyone with subpoena power,
and it has not been.

In the reading circulated by these accounts, there is a recognisable set of six subjects that
attract immediate and disproportionate effort to shut them down. Each is an **unproven theory in
public circulation**, and each is listed here as a topic that gets contested, not as a finding of
this site:

* **That [Israel](/Israel_Main_Suspect/overview) might be involved.** A foreign-nexus thread that
  former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) says his center's parallel review was
  ordered to stop pursuing once Robinson was in custody.
* **That [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) might be involved.** Robinson's roommate, whose
  phone supplied the text messages the state's night-of timeline depends on. **Twiggs has not been
  charged with any crime**, and no evidence establishing involvement has been presented in court.
* **That [Erika Kirk](/People/erika-kirk) might be involved.** **No accusation against her has

  speculation.
* **That [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) might be a patsy.** The framing that follows if

The observation those accounts keep returning to is about the **shape** of the response rather
than its content. Enormous energy goes into closing each of those six doors. Comparatively little
goes into the one thing that would make all six unnecessary:

> **Affirmatively and independently proving that Tyler Robinson did it.**

That asymmetry is the argument. If the physical case were airtight, the off-ramps would not need
policing — the evidence would close them on its own. Citizen investigators read the reverse
priority as a sign that narrative maintenance is doing work the evidence cannot, and they treat
the fear the Panguitch story provokes as a measure of how little it would take to break the
official timeline. **This is their inference about editorial behaviour, not an established fact
about anyone's motives**, and none of the six theories above is established either.

## What would settle it

1. **Subpoena the merchant processor.** Check #211, paid 9:47 PM on 9/10/25. The cardholder
   behind those last four digits is a matter of record somewhere.
2. **Release the FBI's disposition of the tip.** The restaurant says it called the Bureau and
   handed over "everything." What happened to that lead?
3. **Reconcile the two sightings on the record.** The 6:38 PM Dairy Queen entry sits in the
   affidavit timeline. If the state stands by it, the state also has to explain the 209 miles.
4. **Depose the staff.** A witness who says he personally took the customer's card and is
   "100%" certain has never, by his own account, been asked to testify by either side.

Until those things happen, Panguitch remains what investigators call it: a **209-mile hole in
the middle of the government's night-of timeline** that no one with subpoena power has publicly
tried to close.
