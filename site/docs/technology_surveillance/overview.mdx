# Technology & Surveillance

## Overview

This section collects **technical and surveillance claims** around the September 10, 2025 killing of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) at UVU: drones and UAS footage, camera systems (campus, TPUSA event, municipal Flock), digital forensics (phones, Discord, SD cards), and contested "exotic weapon" theories (IR laser designation, mic-as-device). Official proceedings emphasize a **rooftop rifle** narrative — the government's account; citizen investigators document other technical models. The majority citizen finding is that Charlie Kirk was killed by an [explosive device at the microphone](/Mic/overview), a technical model that has never been tested in the [criminal proceedings against Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview), who stands accused rather than convicted.

## Key Areas

- Drone / UAS sightings and foreign-system analogies ([Drones](/Drones/overview))
- Aircraft and ISR-adjacent flight context ([Planes](/Planes/overview), [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview))
- Campus and event camera chains; Flock / ALPR coverage gaps
- Digital forensics: Discord, phones, search history, wiped servers (as alleged)
- Technical models: the exploding mic (the majority conclusion)

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

*(Unverified technical claims from public commentary — not court findings.)*

- **Flock / municipal cameras:** **[@realstewpeters](/Influencers/podcasts-stew-peters)** (July 2026) asked why a dense **Flock** camera environment around Provo/Orem produced, in the public case narrative, mainly **grainy stills** and few plate hits. That is a **records-demand question**, not proof of spoliation by itself.
- **Event-camera seizure timing:** Separate but related tech-chain claims (see [Social Media Analysis](/social_media_analysis/overview)) allege **SD cards pulled within minutes** by event AV staff — a chain-of-custody issue that belongs in both tech and legal tracks.
- **Exploding mic:** The majority of citizen investigators conclude a **modified wireless microphone** or body-adjacent explosive device, not a .30-06, caused the fatal injury. See [Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview) and [Cover-Up — Mic Explosive](/CoverUp/Mic_Explosive). The government's narrative remains the rifle; the autopsy that would test it has not been released.

## How to Read Competing Models

| Model | Primary claimed sensor / tool | What would falsify it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lone rooftop rifle (government narrative) | Mauser .30-06, rooftop pads, DNA/prints | Independent ballistics + multi-angle video contradicting roof origin |
| Mic / close device (investigators' majority conclusion) | Wound geometry, mic removal video, burnt transmitter, near-field audio | Released residue tests on the soil, clothing, and SUV — the evidence that was removed |

Use [Drones](/Drones/overview), [cameras](/cameras/overview), [Gun_Bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview), and [US Intelligence](/US_Intelligence/overview) for depth.
