# Consciousness Control

This section maps the technologies and reported programs that touch human perception, hearing, and
behavior directly — and asks whether any of them bear on the September 10, 2025 assassination of
[Charlie Kirk](/Charlie/overview) or on the state's account of [Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview).
**Nothing here is offered as court-proven proof that any such technology was used at UVU.** Robinson is
charged, not convicted. The purpose of these pages is different: to show readers what intelligence services
have studied, fielded, or been *accused* of holding — including capabilities that can make a political
shooter look like a lone “crazy person.”

The reason this area exists is narrow and specific. A recurring question in the Kirk investigation is whether
the accused acted on his own initiative or was, in some manner, guided. Most answers are mundane — online
radicalization, personal grievance, ordinary human influence. A harder set of claims — discussed constantly
on X, and only partially admitted in formal government channels — points at hardware: beamed audio (“Voice of
God” / voice-to-skull), pulsed radio-frequency injury (the Havana Syndrome stack), neural interfaces, and
alleged brain-to-brain channels. Those claims deserve to be stated plainly, sourced where sources exist, and
separated from what is *formally* acknowledged. Formal admission and operational reality are not the same
thing; public discourse is often years ahead of declassification.

What *is* established is more limited than online absolutists claim, and more than official “nothing to see”
briefings allow. The microwave auditory effect — sound perceived inside the head from pulsed RF — is real and
was published by Allan Frey in 1961–62; it is the physics behind “Voice of God” and V2K discussions (see also
[Frey Effect / V2K](/Gov_Mind_Control/frey-effect-v2k)). A 2020 National Academies report concluded that
directed pulsed RF energy is a plausible mechanism for a subset of Havana Syndrome injuries. Congress passed
the HAVANA Act to compensate brain-injured personnel. A 2023 Intelligence Community assessment still called
foreign-adversary attribution “very unlikely.” In January 2026, CBS reported the Pentagon testing a pulsed-RF
device believed capable of reproducing AHI-type effects. Laboratory brain-to-brain interfaces exist at low
bandwidth. Whistleblowers on X and on shows such as Redacted allege far more — remote synthetic telepathy,
implants, operational B2B — while agencies fight that narrative. **The cover value of the whole stack is
obvious:** if a person can be spoken to, injured, or steered through a channel no jury can see, the public
default is psychiatric — “he was crazy” — not “he was run.”

Read [Havana Syndrome](./havana-syndrome) first — it is the best-documented fight between injured personnel,
scientific mechanism, and institutional denial. Then read [Directed Energy
Weapons](./directed-energy-weapons) for acknowledged hardware, and [CIA, DARPA and DEW Mind
Control](./cia-darpa-havana-mind-control) for the paper trail itself — the 1976 remote-brainwave patent, the
1994 Air Force *New World Vistas* passage on transmitting suggestion, and DARPA's N3 program, each separated
from what commentators assert on top of it. Pair this section with
[Frey Effect / V2K](/Gov_Mind_Control/frey-effect-v2k) for the Voice-of-God audio channel. Use [Covert
Communications](./covert-communications) for ordinary handler tradecraft before exotic physics. The pages on
[Communication by Consciousness](./communication-by-consciousness) and [Brain-to-Brain
Communications](./brain-to-brain-communications) separate pure telepathy claims from lab BBIs and from the
operational systems people discuss when government has not formally announced them. For how this maps onto
“programmed shooter” theories about this case, see also [Making a Shooter
Playbook](/Gov_Mind_Control/making-a-shooter-playbook) and [Hypnosis / Programmed
Patsy](/Gov_Mind_Control/hypnosis-programmed-patsy).
