# Consciousness Control

This section collects the technologies and reported programs that touch human perception, hearing, and
behavior directly — and asks, carefully, whether any of them bear on the September 10, 2025 assassination
of [Charlie Kirk](/Charlie/overview) or on the state's account of [Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview).
**Nothing here is offered as proof that any such technology was used at UVU.** Robinson is charged, not
convicted, and no evidence in the public record establishes a consciousness-control component to this case.

The reason this area exists is narrow and specific. A recurring question in the Kirk investigation is whether
the accused acted on his own initiative or was, in some manner, guided. Most answers to that question are
mundane — online radicalization, personal grievance, ordinary human influence. But a smaller set of claims
circulating among citizen investigators points at hardware: beamed audio, pulsed radio-frequency energy,
neural interfaces, and covert signalling channels. Those claims deserve to be stated plainly, sourced where
sources exist, and separated from what is actually established.

What *is* established is more limited than the online discourse suggests, and more than skeptics allow. The
microwave auditory effect — sound perceived inside the head from pulsed RF — is real and was published by
Allan Frey in 1962. U.S. government panels, including a 2020 National Academies report, concluded that
directed pulsed RF energy is a plausible mechanism for the Havana Syndrome injuries reported by American
diplomats and intelligence officers. A 2023 Intelligence Community assessment reached the opposite conclusion
on attribution, finding it "very unlikely" a foreign adversary was responsible. Both findings are on the
record and both are contested. Brain-to-brain interfaces exist in laboratories at low bandwidth. None of that
adds up to steering a person to commit a homicide, and this site does not claim it does.

Read [Havana Syndrome](./havana-syndrome) first — it is the best-documented case where a government
investigated whether people were injured at a distance, and the resulting fight over attribution shows how
hard these questions are to close. Then read [Directed Energy Weapons](./directed-energy-weapons) for the
underlying hardware and [Covert Communications](./covert-communications) for the mundane, well-attested
channels that explain far more real-world cases than exotic ones do. The two speculative pages —
[Communication by Consciousness](./communication-by-consciousness) and
[Brain-to-Brain Communications](./brain-to-brain-communications) — are labeled as such throughout and exist
so that claims made online can be examined against the actual research rather than repeated unexamined.
