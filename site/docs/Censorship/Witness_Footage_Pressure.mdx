# Witness Footage Pressure

Beyond platforms and gag orders, **individual witnesses** report pressure on their recordings.
Those recordings are the primary independent record of how Charlie Kirk was assassinated, and their disappearance narrows what anyone outside the state can check against the [official cause-of-death account](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Missing or altered phone video (claims)

[Media Censorship](/Media/Censorship) summarizes attendee accounts that **phone clips recorded at UVU** were **missing or appeared edited** by the time they got home — **anecdotal**, not verified here.

## Skyler Baird (claim)

Commentary in the master file alleges witness **Skyler Baird** was **asked to delete phone footage**. **Unverified** — listed because influencers cite it as part of a pattern.

## Ryne Simmons — FBI deletion

The best-documented case: [Ryne Simmons — FBI Video](./Ryne_Simmons_FBI_Video) (4K close-up, federal delete request).

## FBI "protect the investigation" vs selective release

Commentary contrasts:

- [FBI](/FBI/overview) allegedly **warned against footage release** to "protect the investigation"
- Later **selective 4K clips** dropped (e.g. birthday tribute framing) while **red-zone cams, roof angles, security logs** remain withheld

That **selectivity** argument is commentary — not proof insiders were protected.

## SD card / CCTV threads

- **[Terryl Farnsworth](/People/terryl-farnsworth)** allegedly removed **SD cards** before police secured area — evidence-handling thread crossing [Tent](/Tent/overview) and [cameras](/cameras/overview)
- **The executive said to have removed an SD card** had reportedly never attended another university event — per CK_FILE research note. Neither the removal nor the attendance point is confirmed.

## N1098L / signal jam theory (contested)

Some posts theorize **N1098L** or ISR aircraft could **jam livestreams** or **alter phone uploads** — highly contested. See [Planes/N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview). Documented as **speculation**, not fact.

### Cross-cutting X signals that touch this topic

- **Pre-event donor pressure (as reported):** [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) publicized private messages (authenticity discussed with TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet** in mainstream commentary) in which Charlie reportedly wrote ~**48 hours** before Sept 10 that donor pressure was "leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause," and that he had lost a major donor after refusing to cancel **Tucker Carlson**. These are **private-text claims**, not a formal public policy renunciation, and they do **not** establish who fired any shot.
- **July 2026 preliminary hearing:** Citizen live-notes and press describe defense challenges to **FBI DNA** methods and **ATF ballistics** (including **inconclusive** comparisons widely discussed online), plus multi-agency testimony on the [Sept 11 surrender chain](/After/Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody).
- **Information control:** Threads continue on [gag orders](/After/Legal_Process_Gag_Orders_And_Secrecy), [Ryne Simmons video](/Censorship/Ryne_Simmons_FBI_Video), platform deboosting, and rapid [crime-scene paving](/After/Site_Changes_And_Crime_Scene_Handling) (Lead Investigator **Hull** reportedly learned of paving via news, per circulating hearing notes).
- **Counter-claims:** Other accounts insist surveillance, DNA, texts, and admissions support a **lone-actor** charging narrative and treat foreign-intel theories as unsubstantiated. Present both sides; [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not convicted**.
