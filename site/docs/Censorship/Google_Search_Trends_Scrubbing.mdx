# Google Search & Trends Scrubbing

Search visibility shapes which investigators new audiences discover.

## Post-event Trends deletion (claims)

Investigation-file notes allege **post-event deletion** of **search-interest evidence** for:

- **UVU Rooftop / Gun / Parking / CCTV / Layout / Losee Center** (May–July 2025 scout spikes)
- Names tied to the case in **Google Trends** exports

Researchers preserve **Internet Archive** and **Trends screenshots** because live results shift — see [Google Searches](/GoogleSearches/overview).

## Israeli Trends spikes

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) threads cite **Israeli Google search spikes** before the event as part of broader foreknowledge claims. Trends data is **volatile** and **interpretation-contested**; we document because influencers cite it as censorship-adjacent **record loss** when graphs disappear.

## This site's visibility

[CoverUp — Google Censorship](/CoverUp/Google_Censorship) documents readers who say **whoassassinatedcharliekirk.com** is hard to find via Google — with both **suppression** and **algorithmic** explanations presented.

## Judge IP search allegation (cross-link)

Commentary ties **Israeli IP Google searches** on Utah judges (**Lunnen**, **Graf**) before the assassination to search-record integrity themes — see [Court Gag Orders](./Court_Gag_Orders). **Unverified.**

Google Trends deletion / UVU search scrubbing claims.

### Cross-cutting X signals that touch this topic

- **Pre-event donor pressure (as reported):** Candace Owens publicized private messages (authenticity discussed with TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet** in mainstream commentary) in which Charlie reportedly wrote ~**48 hours** before Sept 10 that donor pressure was "leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause," and that he had lost a major donor after refusing to cancel **Tucker Carlson**. These are **private-text claims**, not a formal public policy renunciation, and they do **not** establish who fired any shot.
- **July 2026 preliminary hearing:** Citizen live-notes and press describe defense challenges to **FBI DNA** methods and **ATF ballistics** (including **inconclusive** comparisons widely discussed online), plus multi-agency testimony on the [Sept 11 surrender chain](/After/Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody).
- **Information control:** Threads continue on [gag orders](/After/Legal_Process_Gag_Orders_And_Secrecy), [Ryne Simmons video](/Censorship/Ryne_Simmons_FBI_Video), platform deboosting, and rapid [crime-scene paving](/After/Site_Changes_And_Crime_Scene_Handling) (Lead Investigator **Hull** reportedly learned of paving via news, per circulating hearing notes).
- **Counter-claims:** Other accounts insist surveillance, DNA, texts, and admissions support a **lone-actor** charging narrative and treat foreign-intel theories as unsubstantiated. Present both sides; [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not convicted**.

### Open questions raised by investigators

1. What primary documents (GRAMA, work orders, unredacted orders, bodycam) would resolve disputes on this page's core claims?
2. Which circulating posts have independent corroboration vs single-source amplification?
3. How do July 2026 hearing exhibits (when unsealed or accurately transcribed) change the weight of earlier X threads?
