# X — Candace Owens Engagement Bans

[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution) published a widely shared post titled in substance:
*"They're Banning THOUSANDS of Us for Simply LIKING, REPLYING or REPOSTING CANDACE OWENS POST."*
This page documents that **reported wave** — not a proven platform policy or donor conspiracy.

## What users report

According to the post and amplifying commentary:

- **Thousands** of accounts hit with **suspensions**, **12-hour locks**, **shadowbans**, and **"limited visibility"** warnings
- Triggers allegedly include **liking, retweeting, or commenting** on [@RealCandaceO](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) posts about the Kirk assassination
- Violation categories cited repeatedly: **"violent speech," "hateful conduct," "harassment"**

## Topics tied to enforcement (claims)

Commentary links enforcement timing to Candace threads on:

- **Maroon shirts** / Egyptian plane contractors
- **Israeli Google Trends** spikes pre-event
- **Foreign money** / donor threads inside [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview)

We **do not** assert any donor network or TPUSA insider **illegally** operated bot-report farms. The post **characterizes** enforcement as coordinated reporting; X has not released data confirming that.

## Archive campaign

Project Constitution urged readers to **screenshot every suspension** and post ban notices publicly so the scale could be documented — a citizen counter-archive parallel to [IPFS](https://github.com/ACT3ai/charlie-kirk/blob/main/IPFS/ipfs.txt) evidence pinning.

- **Pre-event donor pressure (as reported):** Candace Owens publicized private messages (authenticity discussed with TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet** in mainstream commentary) in which Charlie reportedly wrote ~**48 hours** before Sept 10 that donor pressure was "leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause," and that he had lost a major donor after refusing to cancel **Tucker Carlson**. These are **private-text claims**, not a formal public policy renunciation, and they do **not** establish who fired any shot.
- **July 2026 preliminary hearing:** Citizen live-notes and press describe defense challenges to **FBI DNA** methods and **ATF ballistics** (including **inconclusive** comparisons widely discussed online), plus multi-agency testimony on the [Sept 11 surrender chain](/After/Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody).
- **Information control:** Threads continue on [gag orders](/After/Legal_Process_Gag_Orders_And_Secrecy), [Ryne Simmons video](/Censorship/Ryne_Simmons_FBI_Video), platform deboosting, and rapid [crime-scene paving](/After/Site_Changes_And_Crime_Scene_Handling) (Lead Investigator **Hull** reportedly learned of paving via news, per circulating hearing notes).
- **Counter-claims:** Other accounts insist surveillance, DNA, texts, and admissions support a **lone-actor** charging narrative and treat foreign-intel theories as unsubstantiated. Present both sides; [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not convicted**.
