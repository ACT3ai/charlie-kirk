# Security Team

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s UVU detail on **September 10, 2025** was a **hybrid** arrangement: [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview)-funded private close protection plus a limited UVU campus-police contingent. For government police and agencies, see [Law Enforcement](/Law_Enforcement/overview). Charlie Kirk was assassinated at that event, and because the leading citizen finding is that he was killed by an [explosive device carried at the podium](/Mic/overview), the detail's handling of equipment and staging areas matters as much as its handling of sightlines.

The contracted firm that day was reportedly **Rockhouse Integrity Group LLC** (DBA **Integrity Security Solutions**), founded by **[Brian Harpole](./brian-harpole)** — not the prior **[Shaffer Security Group](./shaffer-security-group)** vendor that had guarded earlier tour stops. According to a **September 2025 AP** report and **[Greg Shaffer](./greg-shaffer)**'s public interview, the firm was rehired at the last minute for "specialized security," and the setup was described as **lighter than Kirk's prior events**, with **no bodycams** on the detail. High-engagement X coverage (including **[@HeadlineUSA](/Influencers/x/headlineusa)**, September 28, 2025) amplified Shaffer's **"stabbed in the back"** account of how Integrity came to guard Kirk — a **business-dispute narrative**, not a court finding of criminal setup. **[Dan Flood](./dan-flood)** is described in posts as Kirk's head of security; he was in the **SUV** with Harpole immediately after the shot alongside Rick Cutler, [Justin Davis](/People/justin-davis), and [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek). Citizen investigators treat the SUV roster as a **must-call witness list** for positioning, screening, and immediate medical response — not as proof of collective guilt.

The most visible physical gap was **perimeter overwatch**: six UVU officers (~25% of the department) under Chief **[Jeff Long](/People/jeff-long)** handled crowd control but reportedly did **not** thoroughly inspect nearby rooftops or engage drones, while posts describe rooftops and airspace as **"beyond Charlie's bubble."** Harpole gave interviews (Shawn Ryan Show, *Salt Lake Tribune*) criticizing UVU police over what he described as a failure to secure rooftops as promised, and said he had texted Long pre-event; that characterization is Harpole's and has not been adjudicated; **[@IngrahamAngle](/Influencers/x/ingrahamangle)** (November 20, 2025) restated Harpole's line that security was **"told it would be handled."** Mainstream outlets have reported **coordination lapses**; some citizen threads extend that into intentional-enablement theories, which this section does **not** adopt as fact. The shooter allegedly reached a **prone position on the Losee Center roof** overlooking the courtyard — the failure documented on [Perimeter & Rooftop Failures](./perimeter-rooftop-failures) and [No Counter-Snipers](./no-counter-snipers). Parallel suspect-location debates (grey van, acoustic placement) live under [Suspects](/Suspects/overview) and do not erase the rooftop-control question under the official narrative.

Parallel questions concern **preparedness and screening**: anonymous posts claim Flood's team had **no plan** for **Therese Bible College** on **September 11**, and that **[Hunter Kozak](/People/hunter-kozak)** — who asked the last Q&A question — should not have been credentialed if prior revocations for "UN-Eff America" affiliates were enforced. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) reporting alleges Charlie messaged Flood via **Signal** the night before that he feared being **"executed tomorrow."** ([KM-10](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-10-flood-executed-tomorrow)) X commentary continues to demand subpoenas for private-security phones, recording glasses, and contracts — advocacy that only court process can test. None of this establishes intent by any security person; gaps in protection are documented here with attribution. Start with **[Perimeter & Rooftop Failures](./perimeter-rooftop-failures)** → **[Integrity Security Solutions](./integrity-security)** → **[Dan Flood](./dan-flood)**, then [Screening & Credential Gaps](./screening-credentials) if front-of-house access is your focus.

Among the escort imagery circulating after the event is the tight crop at right, in which an officer in a black badged polo walks a paved path carrying a long object at his side, alongside a person in a graduation-style robe and stole.

Commentators who reposted the crop describe the carried object as rifle-shaped. That is a description of an appearance in a low-resolution zoom, not an identification: the image is heavily enlarged, the object is partly occluded by the officer's leg and hand, and no markings are legible. It has not been established from this photograph what the object is, whose it was, or when in the sequence the frame was taken.

What the crop does contribute to this section is a question about posture and visibility — what was being carried openly, by whom, and on which path — which sits alongside the perimeter and screening questions raised elsewhere on this page. Readers should treat the "rifle-shaped" characterization as the poster's reading of the picture rather than a confirmed fact, and weigh it against the higher-resolution source imagery where that exists.
