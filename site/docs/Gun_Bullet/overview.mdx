# Gun & Bullet Analysis

The government's narrative requires a **scoped Mauser .30-06** fired from the **Losee rooftop** to have killed
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). This section tests that chain link by link. Most citizen investigators on X
have concluded the chain does not hold, and that Charlie was killed by an **explosive at his microphone** —
see the [Exploding Mic](/Mic/overview) section. Start with the
[investigation index](./Gun_Bullet_Investigation_Index).

The **strongest forensic gap** in public filings is the **September 17, 2025 ATF report**: autopsy
**Exhibit 6A** (bullet jacket fragment) **cannot be matched** to seized **Exhibit 1** (the Mauser) —
rifling fits **numerous makes and models**. At the **July 2026 preliminary hearing** the same ATF examiner
testified that **dust and debris** were observed inside the seized rifle's barrel and that the fragment
measured **.286 to .301 inches** — testimony citizen investigators read two opposite ways
([Barrel Dust & Bullet Diameter](./Barrel_Dust_And_Diameter)). Commentary alleges the FBI may follow with **VCM** and
revived **CBLA** lead analysis the Bureau **discontinued in 2005**. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not
convicted** — see [ATF Fragment Inconclusive](./ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive) and
[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/ATF_Inconclusive_Ballistics).

**Physics and wound geometry** challenge the .30-06 story even before alternative weapons enter:
.30-06 carries **~3,950 Joules** muzzle energy vs **~482 J** for 9mm — over **8×** —
([.30-06 Energy Mismatch](./Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch)). A Losee shot at **5–9° downward** would likely
strike **face/jaw** first, not the reported entry **between larynx and trachea** left of the spine
([Wound Trajectory & Angle](./Wound_Trajectory_Angle)). [@RealCandaceO](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) reporting
adds **multiple fingerprints** on the gun, a rooftop filmmaker saying Tyler's **gun and outfit don't match**,
and **no video** of Tyler firing.

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena) and the [.30-06 Energy Mismatch](./Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.
* **The rifle does not tie in** — the ATF could not match the autopsy fragment to the seized Mauser ([ATF Fragment Inconclusive](./ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive)).

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## What Citizen Investigators Emphasize on X

Gun & Bullet is where many readers abandon the government's lone-.30-06 narrative: **ATF inconclusive identification** to the specific rifle, energy/wound mismatch charts, trajectory geometry, and missing shoot-video. Defenders of the official account argue that fragmentation explains the non-match; investigators answer that an unmatched fragment, no exit wound, and a wound pattern that fits a [blast at the microphone](/Mic/overview) point away from the rifle altogether. Charging documents are not a completed trial record.

The **recovery site itself** is now being argued too. In August 2026 the citizen-journalist account **@DiligentDenizen** published dated aerial imagery circling the wooded lot he says the rifle was pulled from, and claimed a **white Kia Soul** turned around at that exact spot on the morning of September 10, 2025 — hours before the shooting. The vehicle identification is unverified and a white Kia Soul is a common car, so the claim is recorded as a claim: [Rifle Site — Kia Soul Turnaround](./Rifle_Site_Kia_Soul_Turnaround).

## Rifle-Concealment Satire Clip (Counterpoint)

A widely shared satire video, made in defense of the government's narrative, pushes back on one of the
recurring investigator arguments — that a full-length **.30-06 bolt-action rifle** could not
realistically have been concealed and walked off campus. In the clip, two men
wearing "Candace is a demon" shirts each slide a rifle down one leg of
slim-fit jeans and walk on camera, with the narration: *"we're going to prove
to these people that a 30-06 easily fits inside slim-fitting jeans... see, we
told you."* It is comedy, not a controlled test — no timing, clothing, or
gait comparison to the campus footage is offered — but it is recorded here
because the concealment question is argued on both sides across this section,
and the counter-argument belongs in the record alongside the
[backpack and rifle concealment analysis](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Backpack_Rifle_Impossibility).

## Scope Zero — The Gun Owners' Counter-Counterpoint

The reply to that satire clip is that **fitting is not the hard part — holding a zero is.** On August 2,
2026, comedian **Owen Benjamin** shared a 1:56 video by an unidentified gun owner who spends the first half
sliding a replica **.30-06** down loose-fitting jeans while narrating in open sarcasm — *"so obviously, this
is incredibly inconspicuous... you could go upstairs, walk across campus comfortably"* — and then turns to
the point: *"I wouldn't even trust that gun to be shot at a deer, let alone the most life-changing shot of my
life... I think the scope might be off already, just from trying to do that... You have to sight these things
in."* Benjamin's framing was blunter still: *"Sighting a scope takes a while and requires you shoot live ammo
while you do it. Just putting the gun in your pants let alone disassembling it would make the scope
useless."* The argument cuts at the **disassembly** version of the concealment theory in particular: taking
the optic off and remounting it reliably destroys a zero, so a theory that smuggles the rifle in pieces buys
concealment at the cost of accuracy. It is a **demonstration, not a controlled test** — no group is fired
before or after, and the rifle is a replica, not the seized Exhibit 1. Full transcript, Benjamin's post, and
the limits of the claim: [Scope Zero & Pants-Leg Concealment](./Scope_Zero_And_Concealment).
