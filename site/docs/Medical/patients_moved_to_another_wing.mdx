# Patients Moved to Another Wing (Claims)

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
This page documents an **anonymous, uncorroborated claim**. Nothing here asserts that
**Timpanogos Regional Hospital**, any administrator, medical professional, or other living
person withheld care, falsified records, knew of a crime in advance, or acted illegally.
The account is **unverified** and may simply be **mistaken**.
:::

On July 30–31, 2026, X discussion of the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) case
focused on a [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) segment: an unnamed
source allegedly told her that, in the **10 o'clock hour on September 10, 2025**, staff at
[Timpanogos Regional Hospital](./Timpanogos_Facility) directed **everyone in the emergency
room into a different wing** of the same building. The shot at Utah Valley University was
about **12:24 PM**; transport to the hospital is commonly estimated near **12:50 PM**. If
the wing move happened as described, it began **more than two hours before** any hospital
could have been reacting to the shooting.

This page isolates that **first step** — the **intra-hospital** relocation. The later
claim that patients were then sent to another facility is covered on
[Patients Moved to Another Hospital](./patients_moved_to_another_hospital). The umbrella
write-up is [ER Cleared Before Shooting](./timpanogos_er_cleared_before_shooting).

## What Was Said

Owens prefaced the account with **"I am told"** — she is relaying a tip, not a document.
In the clip widely reposted by **[@danksterintel](https://x.com/danksterintel/status/2082975397311033354)**
and **[@MarioNawfal](https://x.com/MarioNawfal/status/2083095317038215206)**:

> …at some point in the 10 o'clock AM hour, on September 10th… over 2 hours before Charlie
> was shot. The hospital staff directed everyone who was in the emergency room into a
> different wing of the hospital. People were not sure what was going on.

Reposts on July 31 compressed the point: the ER was "cleared" first inside Timpanogos,
**then** patients and files left the campus. Commentators such as
**[@RichiOfficially](https://x.com/RichiOfficially/status/2083046133610287204)** stressed
the sequence: wing first, other hospital second.

## Timeline as Asserted

| Time (local, Sept 10, 2025) | Asserted event |
|---|---|
| ~10:00–10:59 AM | ER patients directed to a different wing of Timpanogos |
| ~11:30 AM | Patients told of transfer to Intermountain / Utah Valley (see other pages) |
| ~12:24 PM | Charlie Kirk shot at UVU |
| ~12:50 PM | Approximate arrival at Timpanogos (investigative estimate) |

## Why This Element Matters Separately

A same-building wing move is different from a full diversion:

* It can look, to patients, like temporary crowding control or a drill — which is why
  ordinary explanations matter.
* It would still empty the primary ER bay if true, matching later claims that the ER
  looked unusually empty when Kirk arrived (see [Events at Hospital](./events_at_hospital)).
* It is easier for a facility to order than chartering shuttles — and harder for outsiders
  to prove without **census logs** or **staff assignment sheets**.

None of that makes the claim true. It only explains why investigators treat the **wing**
step as its own records question.

## Ordinary Explanations

Hospitals relocate ER patients inside a building for routine reasons the clip does not
rule out:

* Emergency-preparedness **drills** and accreditation exercises
* **HVAC**, water, power, contamination, or construction in the ER bay
* **Staffing / census** rebalancing within the facility
* Advance posture for a large nearby public event (capacity planning, not proof of crime)

Skeptical replies on X the same day argued the logistics do not match standard ER
practice. **[@TheMatrixOfNat](https://x.com/TheMatrixOfNat/status/2082999899738771846)**
wrote that random mass moves "make absolutely no sense" without a stated medical reason
and that liability would block improvised relocations. Counter-posts claiming "official
timelines" showed normal operations have **not** been tied here to a named hospital press
release.

## Verification Status

As of this writing:

* **Source:** anonymous tip via Candace Owens
* **No public log, incident report, or named staff witness** has been produced for a
  10am wing move
* **Grok replies** on the viral threads consistently note the absence of independent
  confirmation (hospital statements, local news, investigation findings)

Timpanogos Regional Hospital and its staff are **not accused** of wrongdoing.

## Records That Would Settle the Wing Claim

1. Timpanogos **ER census by bay / wing** for the morning of September 10, 2025  
2. Any **internal incident, drill, or facilities ticket** authorizing relocation  
3. **Staff assignment** sheets for the ER and the destination wing that hour  
4. Badge / access logs if the facility uses electronic doors between wings
