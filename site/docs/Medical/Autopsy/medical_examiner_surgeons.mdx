# Medical Examiner & Surgeons

This page documents claims about the Utah state medical examiner and hospital surgeons in the context of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s death, with particular focus on staffing changes and reported pre-event search activity from foreign IP addresses.

## Dr. Deirdre Amaro — New Chief Medical Examiner

According to compiled research, **Dr. Deirdre Amaro** began her role as Utah's full-time chief medical examiner on **July 1, 2025** — approximately two and a half months before Charlie's death on September 10.

Citizen investigators have flagged this timing as noteworthy because:

- She was reportedly **searched from Israeli IP addresses** in the same timeframe that other key figures and locations were being searched
- Her appointment meant she would be the ME responsible for any high-profile death in Utah during this period
- The investigation notes describe her as having been "put in place 2 weeks before July Google searches" began targeting Utah-area medical and legal personnel

Whether her appointment has any connection to subsequent events is speculative. New appointments to state ME positions are routine. However, the reported foreign search activity targeting her name has drawn attention. She is reportedly **quadruple board-certified** (anatomic, clinical, forensic, and neuropathology) and previously served as a chief medical examiner in Missouri. Her full profile is at [Dr. Deirdre Amaro](/People/deirdre-amaro).

A separate, **unconfirmed** claim circulating in citizen-investigator notes asserts that a **newly appointed examiner named [Angela Hammond](/People/angela-hammond)** entered the picture in September 2025. No official records confirm this appointment, and it should be treated as an open, unverified data point until primary documentation surfaces.

## The "Signed Off" Allegation (Unverified)

A secondhand claim on X (user **[@lindseey_loo](/Influencers/x/lindseey-loo)**, **July 3–4, 2026**) alleges that a "**young woman**" the medical examiner "had never met before" — described as "new, young, blonde and clearly unqualified" — "**signed off**" on Charlie's autopsy report before the examiner finished, with the poster noting a report is "a draft until it is signed" and that "changes can be made" in that window. The poster **speculated**, without confirming, whether the woman was Dr. Amaro. This is an **allegation**, not a finding; it is unverified and the "unqualified" description is inconsistent with Dr. Amaro's board-certification. The dedicated treatment is on the [Mystery Woman Who Signed Off (Allegation)](/People/mystery-woman-signed-off) page. Against this rumor, DPS agent **David Hull** testified (~July 6–7, 2026) that the **Utah OME conducted the autopsy**, ruled **homicide by gunshot wound to the neck**, and **recovered a bullet fragment** — while the specific performing examiner stayed unnamed in court.

## Seven Surgeons Searched from Israeli IPs (Claims)

One of the most cited data points in the investigation is the claim that **seven surgeons** at the hospital where Charlie was taken were searched from Israeli IP addresses around **July 14-30, 2025** — approximately seven weeks before the shooting. According to the compiled research:

| Surgeon | Reported Search Date |
|---------|---------------------|
| **[Richard Rasmussen](/People/richard-rasmussen)** | July 14 |
| **Steven Neilman** | July 18 |
| **[Lee Patterson](/People/lee-patterson)** | July 19 |
| **[Brian Gill](/People/brian-gill)** | July 22 |
| **[Robert Patterson](/People/robert-patterson) MD** | July 23-24 |
| **[Lee Trotter](/People/lee-trotter)** | (date not specified) |
| **[William Pugh](/People/william-pugh)** | July 30 |

Investigators characterize these searches as "scouting all emergency surgeons at Kirk's hospital" and describe them as part of a broader pattern of pre-event reconnaissance. The same IP addresses are alleged to have searched for the hospital itself, the medical examiner, local judges, and other figures connected to the case.

## Andrew Zenger — Hospital CEO (Not a Surgeon)

**Andrew Zenger** was appointed **CEO of Timpanogos Regional Hospital** on **August 19, 2025**, with a start date of **August 25**. He is a hospital **administrator** with a background in operations and management — **not a physician or surgeon**.

A claim that circulated on social media — that Zenger was "the doctor that treated Charlie" and that he "joined the surgical team on August 28" — is **false**. The CEO and the treating surgeon were two different people. Zenger has no medical degree or surgical training and did not perform any operation on Charlie Kirk; the physician who actually treated Charlie at the hospital remains **publicly unnamed**. The only items citizen investigators have actually flagged about Zenger are the timing of his CEO appointment and a reported (unverified) Israeli-IP search of his name on **August 27**. See [Hospital Leader](/Medical/hospital_leader) for the full treatment.

## Connection to Hospital Choice

The fact that these surgeons and administrators were at **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** — the facility Charlie was taken to despite it being farther away and a lower trauma level than the alternative — connects this page to the [Hospital Choice & Transport](/Medical/Hospital/hospital_choice_transport) analysis. If the hospital was pre-selected, investigators argue, the surgical staff would need to be known in advance.

## Important Caveats

- The "Israeli IP" search claims come from citizen-investigator analysis of Google Trends and related data. The methodology and reliability of these attributions have not been independently verified in court proceedings.
- Surgeons being searchable online is normal. The claim's significance rests on the **pattern** — multiple surgeons at the same hospital, searched from the same foreign IP addresses, in a concentrated timeframe weeks before the event.
- None of the named surgeons or Dr. Amaro are accused of wrongdoing. The claims center on who was searching for them and why.

Foreign-nexus questions like these are exactly the category of lead that former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) says his team was in the early phases of examining when it was reportedly ordered to stand down after Tyler Robinson's arrest.
