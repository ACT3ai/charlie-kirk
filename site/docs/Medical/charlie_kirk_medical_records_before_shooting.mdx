# Charlie Kirk's ER File Sent to the Wrong Hospital (Claims)

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
This page documents **unverified** social-media claims about electronic or paper medical
charts. **No hospital, nurse, administrator, or living person** is accused of creating a
false record, knowing of a crime in advance, or breaking the law. Charts can be created
for ordinary reasons (pre-registration, wrong facility, name collision). Nothing here is
a finding of fact.
:::

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was shot at about **12:24 PM** on September 10, 2025
and was transported to **[Timpanogos Regional Hospital](./Timpanogos_Facility)** in Orem
(arrival often estimated near **12:50 PM**). He was **not** treated at **Utah Valley
Hospital** in Provo — the hospital the transferred patients were reportedly sent to.

After [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) aired an anonymous tip that
Timpanogos moved ER [patients](./patients_moved_to_another_hospital) and
[files](./patient_medical_records_transferred) to Utah Valley around **11:30 AM**, X
users pushed a sharper claim: that a **file for Charlie himself** was already in the
Utah Valley ER system — **before the shot**.

## The Claim in One Sentence

Stated plainly, the community claim is this: **Charlie Kirk's ER file was swept into the
mass transfer out of the Timpanogos emergency room and ended up at Utah Valley Hospital's
ER — a hospital he was never taken to — and it got there before he was shot.**

Two separate oddities are stacked in that sentence, and they are worth pulling apart
because they fail or survive independently:

| Oddity | What would be strange about it | Innocent version |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrong place** — a Kirk chart at Utah Valley, where he never went | He was treated at Timpanogos in Orem; a chart at the Provo facility has no obvious clinical reason to exist | A chart swept along in a batch export; a chart opened for an expected arrival that never came; a wrong-facility entry later corrected |
| **Wrong time** — that chart existing before 12:24 PM | A record predating the injury implies someone expected the patient | Clock error in a secondhand retelling; post-shot chart misread as pre-shot; pre-registration for a large nearby event |

The **wrong place** element is the weaker of the two on its own — records land in the wrong
facility through ordinary error all the time. The **wrong time** element is what would
matter, and it is precisely the element that rests entirely on secondhand recollection
rather than on a timestamp anyone has published.

## Which Hospital Is Which — Naming Correction

The X threads on this claim almost universally call the receiving facility **"UVU ER."**
That name does not exist, and readers should not carry it forward:

| Name in circulation | What it actually refers to |
|---|---|
| **UVU** — Utah Valley **University** | The **shooting site**. A university campus in Orem. It has **no emergency room** and no hospital. |
| **UVH** — **Utah Valley Hospital** | The **receiving hospital** in this claim. 1034 N 500 W, **Provo**. Operated by **Intermountain Health**. Level II trauma center. This is where the shuttles allegedly went and where the files allegedly landed. |
| **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** | Where **Charlie Kirk was actually taken**. Orem. Operated by **MountainStar Healthcare**, a division of **HCA Healthcare**. Level IV trauma center. |

Two further points of ordinary confusion worth settling here, because they get swapped in
comment threads:

* **The two hospitals are run by different companies.** Timpanogos is for-profit HCA /
  MountainStar. Utah Valley Hospital is nonprofit Intermountain Health. They are **not**
  the same system, which is part of why a records transfer between them would leave an
  interface trail rather than a silent internal move.
* **The church-founded hospital is the destination, not the one Charlie went to.** Utah
  Valley Hospital was owned by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints from 1953
  until 1975, when the church divested its fifteen hospitals and the nonprofit that became
  Intermountain Health was created to run them. Intermountain has been an independent
  secular nonprofit ever since. Timpanogos, where Kirk was treated, has no church history —
  it opened in 1998 under for-profit ownership.

Where quotes below say "UVU ER," that is the **source's wording**, preserved as spoken.
Read it as **Utah Valley Hospital**.

## The Two-Clip Amplification

**[@NikkiRosier4](https://x.com/NikkiRosier4/status/2082993198163714167)** (July 31, 2026;
high engagement) posted two clips from Owens's discussion with this framing:

1. **All** Timpanogos ER patient files transferred to "UVU ER" (**Utah Valley Hospital**, Provo) at ~**11:30 AM**  
2. A **"UVU ER" nurse** — i.e. a **Utah Valley Hospital** nurse — allegedly "provided proof that a file for **CHARLIE** was in their system"  
3. Inference: **CK's ER file was transferred at 11:30 AM** — about **54 minutes before** the shot  

A follow-up

before arrival**. **[@OneLongLay](https://x.com/OneLongLay/status/2083114261643981262)**
wrote that CK's records in the batch meant Timpanogos "had them already, as if CK were a
patient in the ER there already," with transfer "an hour before CK was even shot."
**[@YRP237](https://x.com/YRP237/status/2083009287282913625)** called pre-shot record
creation a "smoking gun." **[@Virgil_Est70BC](https://x.com/Virgil_Est70BC/status/2083062334738567605)**

These are **citizen interpretations** of a podcast tip plus an alleged nurse proof. This
site has **not** independently authenticated a chart screenshot, audit log, or named nurse.

## Ordinary Explanations That Do Not Require a Plot

Even if some chart entry exists (unproven), benign mechanisms include:

* **Event pre-registration** — hospitals sometimes open charts for high-profile nearby
  events without knowing a specific injury will occur  
* **Wrong destination** — media and families were unsure which hospital received him;
  staff at UVH might open a chart for an expected arrival that never came  
* **Transfer-then-cancel** — a provisional UVH chart if someone planned to move him from
  Timpanogos (raised in earlier threads; not proven)  
* **Name / system error** — wrong patient merge, test chart, or misread screen  
* **Misremembered tip** — secondhand "nurse proof" without a retained document  

None of these are asserted as true. They are the **null hypotheses** any serious records
request must still falsify.

inference cannot replace that timestamp.

1. EMR **create time, create user, and facility** for any Charlie Kirk (or close match)
   chart at Timpanogos and at Utah Valley / Intermountain on September 10, 2025  
2. Whether the encounter type was **pre-reg**, **ED arrival**, **transfer-in**, or test  
3. **HL7 / interface** messages between the two facilities that morning  
4. Any **screenshot or export** Owens or a nurse is alleged to have seen — if it exists,
   it should be authenticated, not described secondhand
