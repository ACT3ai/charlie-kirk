# Lapel Mic and Audio Theories

From a **medical forensics** angle, the exploding-mic thesis asks whether [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s fatal
injury matches **internal detonation** at the lavalier pack rather than external rifle energy.
Full mic-section depth lives at [Mic — Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview); this page bridges
medical and audio evidence. On the citizen-investigation side of X the weight of opinion is that Charlie Kirk was killed by [a charge detonating at the microphone itself](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie) rather than by a round fired from a rooftop.

## Core medical claim

Charlie_Kirk.txt [Mossad](/intelligence/Mossad)-theory point **#3**:

> His lapel mic may have also had an explosive in it… We are waiting for the autopsies to confirm.
> This blast is what cut his necklace and shot it upward.

If confirmed, cause of death shifts from **penetrating ballistics** to **blast/fragmentation** —
with implications for [Tyler Robinson exoneration](/Tyler_Robinson/Weapon_Rifle) on ballistics grounds.

## Audio layer

- [Canon XA55](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio): **+321 ms stage detonation** loudest peak ~46 m at tent, separate from **+202 ms** muzzle blast ~120 m
- Speaker **hum** before final question — possible RF or power anomaly (unverified)
- "~36 ft" proximity claims from sound analysis (citizen, not peer-reviewed)
- Pre-mixed **gunshot over PA** hypothesis — would affect witness "everyone heard a close shot" without physical shooter nearby

## Hardware and placement

[Phillip Goldsberry Jr.](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup) reportedly routed [RØDE Wireless PRO](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic)
**under shirt** — atypical vs prior [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) outdoor events. [AES DoD contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract) for
miniaturized anti-personnel charges sits on procurement timeline.

## Competing internal device

[Crucifix pendant shaped charge](/Mic/exploding-pendant-shaped-charge) — USMC breacher argues
**necklace anchor** more stable than unanchored lapel pack. See [Mic vs Pendant](/Mic/Mic_vs_Pendant).

## Evidence to collect

- Autopsy: explosive residue, fragment metallurgy, wound track vs blast center
- Forensic exam of mic TX/RX, IEM, clothing
- Uncompressed event mixer multitracks
- High-FPS sync of audio impulses to video frames
