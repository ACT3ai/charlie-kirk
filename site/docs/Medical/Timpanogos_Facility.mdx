# Timpanogos Regional Hospital

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was transported to **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** in Orem — not to the higher-level
**Utah Valley Hospital** (Intermountain) in Provo. Investigators treat that choice as a central
medical-process question. **We do not claim** any hospital employee caused harm or knew of the attack.

## Facility basics

| Item | Reported detail |
|------|-----------------|
| Name | Timpanogos Regional Hospital (Orem, Utah) |
| Operator | **MountainStar Healthcare**, a division of **HCA Healthcare** (for-profit); opened 1998 |
| Trauma designation | **Level IV** (lower capability than Level II centers) |
| Distance from UVU | ~**2.6 miles** per CK_FILE distance notes |
| Alternative | Utah Valley Hospital (Provo) ~**3.4–12.3 miles** depending on routing source — **Level II**, **Intermountain Health**, not used |

The two hospitals belong to **different companies**: Timpanogos to for-profit HCA /
MountainStar, Utah Valley Hospital to nonprofit Intermountain Health — the system created in
1975 when the LDS Church divested its fifteen hospitals. The church-founded facility is
therefore the **alternative** hospital, not this one. Note also that **"UVU"** in citizen
posts means Utah Valley **University**, the shooting site; the hospital is **UVH**, Utah
Valley Hospital.

Standard serious-trauma protocol favors the **highest-level center reachable**. A gunshot to the
neck would normally trigger Level I/II preference if available within reasonable time.

## Leadership timing — Andrew Zenger

Reported appointments before September 10:

- **August 19, 2025** — Andrew James Zenger appointed **CEO**
- **August 25, 2025** — start date
- **August 28, 2025** — joined hospital **surgical team**
- Zenger also described as the **treating surgeon** for Kirk — dual CEO/surgeon role disputed

See [Hospital Leader](./hospital_leader). August **27** Israeli-IP search spike for Zenger name
per [Israel Google Searches](./israel_google_searches_medical_people) — correlation only.

## Cleared ER and bomb-scare theories

Commentary describes the emergency department as **unusually empty** on September 10. A July 2026
[Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) tip claims patients were
[moved to another wing](./patients_moved_to_another_wing) around 10am, then
[to Intermountain / Utah Valley](./patients_moved_to_another_hospital) by
[shuttle](./buses_to_move_patients) with [files](./patient_medical_records_transferred) — and
community posts claim a [Charlie Kirk chart](./charlie_kirk_medical_records_before_shooting)
appeared at the other hospital **before** the shot. All of that remains **unverified**; see
[ER Cleared Before Shooting](./timpanogos_er_cleared_before_shooting). A separate CK_FILE thread
claims a **bomb scare** at the closer Intermountain facility may have influenced routing —
unverified. [Hospital Scene & EMS](/Medical/Hospital/hospital_scene_ems) covers EMS access and
FBI footage claims.

## Surgeon search-pattern cluster

Charlie_Kirk.txt documents **seven Timpanogos surgeons** allegedly searched from Israeli IPs in
a July 2025 window — Neilman, Patterson, Trotter, Gill, Rasmussen, Pugh, and others. Listed on
[Doctors](./doctors) and [Medical Staff Changes](./medical_staff_changes). **No accusation** —
pattern documentation only.

## Romney / Bilt / ER scribe thread (reported)

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) coverage (summarized in Charlie_Kirk.txt) tied **Timpanogos** to:

- **Bilt Inc.** June 2025 ~$8M DOD contracts (3D immersive intelligence)
- **[Connor Romney Henderson](/People/connor-henderson)** — ER scribe at Timpanogos summer 2025
- **[Zachariah Qureshi](/distraction_people/Zachariah_Qureshi)** manhunt detainee — father linked to Bilt co-founder

These are **reported coincidences** requiring independent verification; see [FBI](/FBI/overview)
Qureshi threads. We do not claim any named person participated in wrongdoing.

## Medical examiner linkage

Utah chief medical examiner **Dr. [Deirdre Amaro](/People/deirdre-amaro)** (started **July 1, 2025** full-time) reportedly
handled autopsy work — see [Medical Examiner](./medical_examiner). Autopsy transparency fights
[Autopsy Report Not Public](./autopsy_report_not_public).
