## The Post

On July 30, 2026, the X account **@danksterintel (Danks)** posted a clip of
**[Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens)** with the caption:

> Candace Owens INSIDE INFORMATION regarding Timpanogos Hospital.
>
> Allegedly, at roughly 10am local time, staff were moved to a different wing of the hospital while
> around that time patients were said to be relocated to a new hospital.
>
> This is concerning

The post is a repost of a podcast segment. It adds no independent verification of its own.

## What Candace Owens Says in the Clip

Speaking on her program, Owens prefaces the account with **"I am told"** — she is relaying
information from a source who contacted the show, not reporting a document she holds. Her statement,
transcribed from the video:

> "I am told that at some point in the 10 o'clock AM hour, on September 10th, so to be clear, and
> this is crucial, over 2 hours before Charlie was shot. The [Timpanogos] hospital staff directed
> everyone who was in the emergency room into a different wing of the hospital. People were not sure
> what was going on. The staff was directed to move everyone in the emergency room to a different
> wing of the hospital.
>
> Then after some time at approximately 11:30 AM, patients were informed that they would be
> transferred aboard shuttles to Intermountain hospital. That's Utah Valley Hospital. And that's what
> happened. All of the emergency room patients and all of their files were then transferred to
> Intermountain. So there's effectively no trail outside of hospital cameras, which have been
> conveniently seized by [Kash] Patel's office of these patients ever having been at Timpanogos
> hospital.
>
> Now, there are a few things I'd like to say here. First, there's always a trail. There's always a
> trail. Somebody somewhere drove that shuttle bus, booked the shuttle bus, saw the shuttle bus
> arriving at Intermountain hospital, was working at Intermountain hospital, when a shuttle bus
> stopped and people emerged and files mysteriously, then were transferred. And if you are that
> somebody. That one body. Please email us."

Owens closes with an appeal for anyone involved in the transport — a driver, a booker, an
Intermountain staff member — to contact her program. She is arguing that an operation of this size
cannot be trace-free, not that she has already found the trace.

### The two hospitals, named

Two different buildings carry this whole story, and X posts routinely blur them:

* **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** — Orem. **MountainStar Healthcare**, a division of
  **HCA Healthcare**. **Level IV** trauma center. The ER allegedly cleared that morning, and
  the hospital [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was actually taken to after 12:24 PM.
* **Utah Valley Hospital** (UVH) — 1034 N 500 W, **Provo**. **Intermountain Health**.
  **Level II** trauma center. The destination the shuttles and files allegedly went to.

**"UVU ER" does not exist.** UVU is Utah Valley **University**, the campus where the
shooting happened; it has no emergency room. Posts using that phrase mean **Utah Valley
Hospital**. Separately — because the two get swapped in comments — the **church-founded**
institution is the destination, not Timpanogos: the LDS Church owned Utah Valley Hospital
from 1953 until 1975, when it divested its fifteen hospitals to the new nonprofit that
became Intermountain Health. Timpanogos opened in 1998 under for-profit ownership.

## Why This Is Not Established

Every load-bearing element of the account is unverified:

* **The source is anonymous.** Owens says "I am told." No name, role, or department is given, and no
  way to assess whether the person was positioned to know what they describe.
* **Nothing was produced.** No hospital log, diversion notice, patient transfer record, shuttle
  contract, internal email, or staff statement accompanies the claim.
* **It is secondhand at minimum.** The account passes from an unnamed person, to a podcast, to a
  repost. Details such as exact times are the most fragile part of any recollection told months
  after the fact.
* **No agency, hospital, or named witness has corroborated it.** As of this writing there is no
  public record confirming that an ER relocation or patient shuttle occurred at Timpanogos on
  September 10, 2025.

## Ordinary Explanations the Clip Does Not Address

Hospitals move patients for routine reasons, and none of them are discussed in the segment:

* **Scheduled drills and exercises.** Emergency preparedness drills, including full ER relocations,
  are a normal accreditation requirement.
* **Facility problems.** HVAC failures, water or power issues, contamination, a fire-alarm event, or
  construction work can force a wing to be vacated on short notice.
* **Census and staffing.** Diverting patients to a larger sister facility when a smaller hospital is
  short-staffed or over capacity is an ordinary operational decision.
* **Advance posture for a large public event.** A hospital anticipating surge demand from a large
  nearby gathering may reposition capacity in advance — which would explain timing that precedes the
  event rather than following it.
* **Same-system transfer is routine.** Timpanogos and Utah Valley Hospital sit inside the same
  regional health-care landscape. Transferring patients along with their files between facilities is
  a standard, documented act, not by itself evidence of anything.

The clip also does not establish that anyone at the hospital had knowledge of any planned crime, and
this page makes no such claim.

## The Camera Element

Owens links the alleged patient transfer to a separate reported thread — that hospital security
footage was seized by the office of FBI Director [Kash Patel](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings). That
claim is documented on its own page and carries its own caveats; see
[Cameras Removed / FBI](./hospital_security_cameras_removed). Treating the two as one story is an
interpretation offered in the clip, not a demonstrated connection.

## Records That Would Settle It

This is a claim that documents can resolve quickly, which is why it belongs on a forced-disclosure
list rather than in open speculation:

1. **Timpanogos ER census and diversion logs** for September 10, 2025.
2. **The internal incident, drill, or facility notification** that authorized any relocation.
3. **The shuttle or transport contract**, dispatch record, and driver manifest.
4. **Intermountain / Utah Valley Hospital intake records** for any patients received that morning.
5. **Staff scheduling records** showing which wing personnel were assigned to that day.

Any one of these would either substantiate the account or dispose of it. See
[Fix — forced disclosure laws](/Fix/overview) for the legal mechanism this site proposes.
