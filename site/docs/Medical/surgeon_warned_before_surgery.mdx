## The Claim

In a livestream clip posted by **@billiewebbb** on July 31, 2026 — described by her as "a clip
from a livestream that @brandisiciliani and I did on December 11th" (2025) — Webb relays a
secondhand story from a coworker. According to the account, the coworker lives next door to a
surgeon who works at [Timpanogos Regional Hospital](./Timpanogos_Facility) — explicitly **not**
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s surgeon — who reportedly had a surgery scheduled for
**September 10, 2025**, the day of the shooting. Per the story, he received a phone call telling
him: *"if you come into the hospital today to do this surgery, you're not leaving for a long time."*

The clip was quoted on August 4, 2026 by **@LionelMedia**, who wrote: "I applaud the open and
critical examination of every aspect of what amounts to the murder investigation of a lifetime.
This is where journalism is. Not where it's heading. But where it is. Brava!"

## Why the Timing Matters

The speakers themselves stress that everything turns on **what time the call was made**. Charlie
Kirk was shot at approximately **12:23 PM**. As discussed in the clip:

* If the surgeon's surgery was scheduled for the **morning** (8–9 AM) and the warning call came
  before it — say 7 AM — the call would predate the shooting and would suggest foreknowledge
  that the hospital was about to be locked down.
* If the surgery was scheduled for the **afternoon** (e.g., 2 PM), the call could have come
  after the shooting, in which case it would be an ordinary heads-up about a hospital already
  handling a high-profile emergency.

Webb's guest also raises the counter-question the story does not answer: even with a VIP patient
in the building, **why would an unrelated surgeon be "trapped" there at all?** One speculation
offered in the clip is that staff on site may have been held for questioning or kept from seeing
something. The clip ends with the practical suggestion: *"You should try to find out what time
they called."*

But I'm just saying if you think about it, if they told your coworker's neighbor, if you come in today, you're going to be here for hours, had to have been before Charlie was shot, right? Because Charlie was shot at 12:23 PM. It just depends on what time his surgery was scheduled for. If he had a surgery scheduled for 2 p.m. that day, Charlie would have already been shot. They could have called him at one PM and been like, hey, don't come in or you're going to be trapped here. But my question is, why would he have been trapped there? Yeah. Like why? If the surgery was scheduled for 8 or 9 AM that day, and they called him at 7 AM and said, don't come in, that is why. You should try to find out what time they called.

## Caveats

This is a **thirdhand, anonymous account**: a livestream speaker relaying what a coworker said
her surgeon neighbor told her. The surgeon is unnamed, the time of the call is unknown, and no
call log, surgery schedule, or hospital record has been produced. The speakers themselves flag
the ambiguity and disagree in real time about what the story proves. Timpanogos Regional Hospital
and its staff are **not accused of anything**; ordinary explanations — a post-shooting lockdown
call, a rescheduled elective surgery — have not been ruled out. This page records the claim
alongside the other unverified hospital-routing claims: [ER Cleared Before
Shooting](./timpanogos_er_cleared_before_shooting), [Patients Moved to Another
Wing](./patients_moved_to_another_wing), and [Patients to Another
Hospital](./patients_moved_to_another_hospital).

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime, did
anything wrong or illegal, or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination.
