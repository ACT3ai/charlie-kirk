# Buses to Move Patients (Claims)

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
This page documents an **unverified** transport claim. No bus company, driver, hospital,
or official is accused of wrongdoing. The shuttle story may be **false or incomplete**.
:::

In the same July 30, 2026 segment that alleged a morning clear-out of the
[Timpanogos Regional Hospital](./Timpanogos_Facility) ER,
[Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) said patients were told around
**11:30 AM** they would leave **aboard shuttles** for Intermountain / Utah Valley Hospital.
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was shot about **12:24 PM**. This page focuses only on
the **vehicle mechanism** — buses / shuttles — not the full medical narrative.

**Where the buses went:** the destination is **Utah Valley Hospital** (UVH) at 1034 N 500 W
in **Provo**, operated by **Intermountain Health**. It is a **Level II** trauma center,
about four miles from Timpanogos Regional Hospital in Orem, which is run by a different
company — **MountainStar Healthcare**, a division of **HCA Healthcare**. Posts that say the
buses went to "UVU" or "UVU ER" mean this hospital: **UVU is Utah Valley University**, the
campus where the shooting happened, and it has no emergency room. Getting the destination
name right matters here more than on most pages, because a vendor trip sheet or a booking
record is searched by the **address it delivered to**.

For the destination claim see [Patients Moved to Another Hospital](./patients_moved_to_another_hospital).
For the earlier same-building move see [Patients Moved to Another Wing](./patients_moved_to_another_wing).

## What Was Said

Owens (as clipped and reposted on X):

> …patients were informed that they would be transferred **aboard shuttles** to
> Intermountain hospital… Somebody somewhere **drove that shuttle bus, booked the
> shuttle bus, saw the shuttle bus** arriving at Intermountain hospital…

She framed the buses as the **hard-to-hide** part of the story: a booking, a driver, a
receiving dock, witnesses. The appeal was for those people to contact her show — not a
claim that she already held a contract or photo.

Viral posts on July 30–31 used "shuttles," "shuttle bus," and "buses" interchangeably
([@danksterintel](https://x.com/danksterintel/status/2082975397311033354),
[@MarioNawfal](https://x.com/MarioNawfal/status/2083095317038215206)).

## Why Buses Are the Most Documentable Piece

If a multi-patient ER transfer by bus happened, ordinary commercial and clinical systems
usually leave traces:

1. **Vendor purchase order** or hospital transport contract  
2. **Dispatch / GPS** for the vehicles  
3. **Driver timesheet** and passenger manifest  
4. **Parking / curb camera** at Timpanogos and at Utah Valley  
5. **Receiving-dock** notes when patients arrived  

Owens's "always a trail" point is about that paper trail. It is **not** evidence that
any of those items exist for this morning — only that investigators should demand them.

## Protocol Pushback Circulating on X

Replies the same day challenged the logistics:

* **[@TheMatrixOfNat](https://x.com/TheMatrixOfNat/status/2082999899738771846)** argued
  you "can't just have a random shuttle bus come and pick people up" given ER liability.  
* **[@grok](https://x.com/grok)** replies on related threads noted that **standard
  ED-to-ED transfer** outside mass-casualty events typically uses **individual ambulances**,
  not a shared shuttle, and that **no hospital statement or local news** had confirmed
  a pre-shooting bus relocation.

Those are practice norms and absence-of-reporting arguments — useful context, not a
substitute for released logs.

## What Has Not Appeared (as of this writing)

Citizen discussion has **not** produced, on the public posts reviewed for this page:

* A named **bus or shuttle company**  
* **License plates**, fleet numbers, or photographs of vehicles at Timpanogos that morning  
* A named **driver** or dispatcher  
* A published **contract** or invoice  

Until one of those appears (or a hospital confirms diversion), the bus claim remains
**assertion without exhibit**.

## Camera Dependency

Owens links the "no trail" worry to reported seizure of hospital cameras (see
[Hospital Security Cameras Removed](./hospital_security_cameras_removed) and
[Cover Up — Hospital Footage](/CoverUp/Hospital_Footage_And_NDAs)). Even if exterior
lot footage is held, **vendor and payroll records** sit outside the hospital NVR and
would still settle whether buses were booked.

## Records That Would Settle the Bus Claim

1. Timpanogos / Intermountain **transport vendor contracts** for September 10, 2025  
2. **Dispatch tickets** and GPS for any multi-patient shuttle that morning  
3. **Driver manifests** (de-identified patients OK)  
4. Lot and dock **video** if retained by hospital, city, or federal agencies
