# Patients Moved to Another Hospital (Claims)

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
This page documents an **anonymous, uncorroborated claim**. **Timpanogos Regional
Hospital**, **Intermountain Health**, **Utah Valley Hospital**, their staff, and any
named public official are **not accused** of crime, advance knowledge of the shooting, or
illegal record handling. The account may be **mistaken**.
:::

After the alleged [wing relocation](./patients_moved_to_another_wing) inside
[Timpanogos Regional Hospital](./Timpanogos_Facility), [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens)
said patients were told — at approximately **11:30 AM** on September 10, 2025 — that they
would be transferred to **Intermountain hospital, Utah Valley Hospital**. [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)
was shot about **12:24 PM** and was taken to **Timpanogos**, not Utah Valley. If the
transfer happened as described, the destination hospital received Timpanogos ER patients
**before** the shooting and **before** Kirk's arrival (~**12:50 PM** estimate).

### The Trail She Says Would Exist

The longer 2:32 cut of the segment (embedded above, transcribed for this site) sets out the specific
records and people Owens believes a shuttle transfer of this size would necessarily leave behind:

> "Somebody somewhere drove that shuttle bus, booked the shuttle bus, saw the shuttle bus arriving at
> Intermountain hospital, was working at Intermountain hospital, when a shuttle bus stopped and people
> emerged and files mysteriously, then were transferred. And if you are that somebody, that one body,
> please email us."

She goes on to suggest checking **Google satellite imagery** for that day and **looking up local
shuttle companies** for an unusual booking, and adds that anyone who was a transferred patient
"probably should also contact a lawyer." She closes with a conditional, not a conclusion: *"if we are
able to prove that this took place beyond a shadow of a doubt with paperwork, then I would say
foreknowledge of Charlie's assassination can no longer be disputed."*

No such paperwork has been produced. The vendor and booking records she describes are exactly the
[records that would settle it](#records-that-would-settle-it) — and are the same trail the
[buses / shuttles page](./buses_to_move_patients) tracks.

## The Receiving Hospital, Named

The destination in this claim is one specific building, and the X threads name it three
different ways — including one name that does not exist. For the record:

| | Receiving hospital | Hospital Charlie Kirk went to |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | **Utah Valley Hospital** (UVH) | **Timpanogos Regional Hospital** |
| **City** | **Provo** — 1034 N 500 W | **Orem** |
| **Operator** | **Intermountain Health** (nonprofit) | **MountainStar Healthcare**, a division of **HCA Healthcare** (for-profit) |
| **Trauma level** | **Level II** | **Level IV** |
| **Role in this claim** | Where the shuttles and the [patient files](./patient_medical_records_transferred) allegedly went, ~11:30 AM | Where the ER was allegedly cleared, and where Kirk was actually treated after 12:24 PM |

**"UVU ER" is a misnomer.** UVU is Utah Valley **University** — the campus where the
shooting happened. It has no emergency room and no hospital. Every X post referring to a
"UVU ER" nurse, a "UVU ER" file transfer, or patients arriving at "UVU" means **Utah Valley
Hospital in Provo**. The two are about four miles apart and are unrelated institutions.

Also worth separating, since it recurs in comment threads: **Utah Valley Hospital is the
one with church origins, not Timpanogos.** The LDS Church owned Utah Valley Hospital from
1953 until 1975, when it divested its fifteen hospitals and the nonprofit that became
Intermountain Health was created to operate them; Intermountain has been an independent
secular nonprofit since. Timpanogos opened in 1998 under for-profit ownership and has no
church history. Neither fact is evidence of anything on its own — they are here so the
institutions are not mixed up.

That the two hospitals sit in **different corporate systems** is relevant to verification:
a transfer between them is an inter-system event that would generate contracts, interface
messages, and intake records on both sides, rather than a silent move inside one network.

## Two Hospitals — Do Not Collapse the Stories

Utah Valley Hospital appears in **several different** citizen threads. Keep them separate:

| Thread | Rough time | Claim type |
|---|---|---|
| **Owens ER transfer** | Morning, **before** shot | Timpanogos ER patients → UVH |
| **Hospital choice** | After shot | Why Kirk went to Level IV Timpanogos instead of closer Level II UVH — see [Hospital Choice & Transport](./Hospital/hospital_choice_transport) |
| **Bomb-scare tip** | Around event time | Unverified claim UVH had a bomb scare / NDAs ([@TheLFLounge](https://x.com/TheLFLounge/status/2025003999624057135); [Project Constitution](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2025029870686777657) skeptical of dispatch audio) |
| **Elizabeth Lane lockdown** | **After** shot | Reporter describes heavy police / no visitors at UVH while Kirk was at Timpanogos ([@imelizabethlane](https://x.com/imelizabethlane/status/2037178355812859913)) |

A lockdown **after** the shooting does not prove a patient dump **before** the shooting.
The Owens transfer claim stands or falls on **morning transfer logs**, not on afternoon
crowd control.

## Ordinary Explanations

Inter-facility ER diversion is common when:

* A smaller hospital is **over capacity** or short-staffed
* A **sister or regional** facility has open beds
* A **drill** or facilities failure forces a temporary diversion
* Planners anticipate **surge** from a large public event nearby

None of those require advance knowledge of a crime. Same-region transfers with
[files](./patient_medical_records_transferred) are ordinary paperwork, not proof of
conspiracy — **if** they occurred for ordinary reasons.

## Verification Status

* **Primary source:** anonymous tip via Candace Owens ("I am told")
* **No public** Timpanogos diversion notice, UVH intake batch for that morning, or
  hospital statement confirming a pre-shooting mass transfer has been produced here
* Skeptical posts argued mass shuttle diversion without a declared mass-casualty event
  is operationally implausible; that is opinion, not a released audit

## Records That Would Settle It

1. Timpanogos **diversion / transfer authorization** for September 10, 2025  
2. **Utah Valley Hospital / Intermountain intake** list for ER patients received that morning  
3. Reason codes (drill, capacity, facilities, diversion status)  
4. Patient de-identified counts (how many, which acuity levels)  

[Buses / shuttles](./buses_to_move_patients) are the physical mechanism claimed for the
same transfer. [Charlie Kirk's chart at UVH](./charlie_kirk_medical_records_before_shooting)
is a **separate** community claim layered on top of the group transfer.
