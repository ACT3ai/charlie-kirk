# Surveillance Cameras

The **highest-stakes camera gap** investigators cite is the contrast between **low-quality public suspect clips** and the **HD institutional and production systems** that existed on campus — plus witness **Ryne Simmons'** reported **4K close-up** that the FBI allegedly asked him to delete ([Censorship](/Censorship/Ryne_Simmons_FBI_Video)). The morning Tyler timeline is a **chain of captioned sightings** on Ring, [WYZE](./WYZE), and [UVU surveillance](./UVU_Surveillance), not eyewitness IDs; the [Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Broadcast) broadcast track adds a separate **four-channel acoustic** record ~46 m from the tent that citizen analysts use in bullet-versus-device debates. On X, **[@Villgecrazylady](/Influencers/x/villgecrazylady)**'s map of that morning chain remains a common reference point; competing threads push **Wordle 11:28 vs Ring 11:44** timing notes and **courtyard-vs-rooftop 12:23** dual-location claims.

The **substance** is timestamps, missing files, and contested enhancement. Doorbell and WYZE cameras place a figure in maroon and light shorts between **8:07 and 9:57 a.m.**; UVU CCTV picks up a stiff-gait figure in a black flag shirt from **~11:50 a.m.** through the **12:23 p.m.** shot. Evening [Dairy Queen CCTV](./Merchant_And_Travel_CCTV) and missing **Washington County lobby** footage test whether the same person fits the full day. Crime-scene camera actions — alleged SD card removal, alleged camera takedown — ask whether rear tent angles ever entered evidence; July 2026 X commentary revived **Terryl Farnsworth / flash-drive vs original SD card** chain-of-custody questions. TPUSA production birthday clips released **selective 4K** while red-zone and roof angles stay withheld; the same commentary cites a cease-and-desist **media-drone B-roll admission** while security was denied drones. Preliminary-hearing coverage describes UVU video of **rooftop positioning and flight**, not a verified public trigger-pull frame, and defense-aligned posts claim Agent **Hull** acknowledged under cross-examination that the roof figure lacks **identifiable features**.

Readers tracing the **official narrative** should start with [UVU Campus Surveillance](./UVU_Surveillance) and [Tyler on Cameras](./Tyler_On_Cameras). Readers testing **identification** should read [Tyler as Placeholder](./Tyler_Placeholder) and [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview). Readers focused on **what the tent cameras show** should pair [Eyewitness Mobile Video](./Eyewitness_Mobile_Video) with [Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Broadcast) and [Videos](/Videos/overview) — without duplicating the full censorship deep dive in [Censorship](/Censorship/overview).

## "Where are all the videos that day?"

Fixed cameras are only part of the recording picture. A courtyard full of attendees meant hundreds of phones were filming, and the striking thing is how little of that footage is publicly available at high quality.

One witness account speaks directly to why. A man who filmed the event says he sent his footage to the [FBI](/FBI/overview) as a tip, was escalated from a local field office to a contact he describes as national, and was advised by that contact to erase the video from his phone because it would only give him PTSD. He says a follow-up call asked him to confirm he had. He did not — and says that call is what made him publish the footage and copy it across many drives in many places. The clip circulated most widely under exactly that question as its caption: *where are all the videos that day?*

The relevance to this section is concrete. Where fixed-camera coverage is disputed, withheld, or missing, eyewitness phone video is the fallback record — and it is the least durable one, held on private devices with no chain of custody and no preservation obligation. See [Eyewitness mobile video](/cameras/Eyewitness_Mobile_Video) for how that material is handled here. The FBI has not publicly addressed the account, ordinary welfare and evidence-handling explanations fit the same described facts, and no wrongdoing by any individual is asserted.
