**Two black SUVs** drive into a UVU parking lot right behind the **gray Dodge Challenger** that
the state says belonged to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson), on the morning of
**September 10, 2025**. Both SUVs run the same loop past the Challenger, nine seconds apart.
Citizen investigators describe this as the SUVs **following** the car. This page has the two
videos, what each SUV most likely is, and the best-guess license plates. It names no driver, and
it accuses no one.

## Candace Owens called this important

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) aired this footage on her podcast
([episode coverage](https://sageofquay.substack.com/p/candace-owens-another-dodge-challenger))
and treated it as important. She opened with it: *"Take a look at this never before seen
footage from September 10th. There is our Dodge Challenger pulling in to the lot. And here is
our Black SUV behind it."* She identified the SUV as a **Chevy Tahoe**. Her own post introduced it
as *"exclusive footage which throws a wrench in the Dodge Challenger 9/10 story."*

What she pointed out in the segment (our machine transcript of the clip):

* The Challenger and the black SUV **pull in together at about 8 a.m.** She shows a fuller video
  of **both drivers getting out at the same time**, *"so people can say I'm lying or that I
  chopped things up."*
* She describes **both drivers as tall**. The Challenger driver appears to wear **jeans, a
  backpack and a hat**. The SUV driver wears **shorts and a short-sleeved, light-gray T-shirt**,
  with a stockier build.
* She anticipates the innocent answer and answers it: *"Maybe that SUV in the Challenger just
  happened to pull in at the same time... certainly possible, but you should also know that
  they also left at the exact same time within a minute of one another."*
* They leave at **about 9:50 a.m.**, so both had been on campus for a little under two hours.
* The Challenger *"gets especially interesting when you plot it into our official timeline...
  I'll tell you what becomes problematic."*
* She asked anyone who recognizes themselves in the footage, including people walking to the
  motorcycle parking, to contact her.

Her episode also raises a question this page does not settle: **was this Challenger Tyler
Robinson's at all?** The official account puts his gray Challenger on campus at
[8:29 a.m.](./Tyler_9_10_2025). Candace's description of the driver (jeans and a backpack) does
not match the reported arrival clothing (maroon T-shirt, light shorts, no backpack).

## Who pulled in, and who got closest

A citizen compiler, **@MQniverse**, put Candace's overhead footage on top of a motorcycle
helmet-cam clip and marked the two SUVs with arrows. We pulled every frame of that
compilation (1,096 frames, 30 per second) and traced both SUVs through the overhead view:

* **SUV 1** (red arrow, the Chevy) pulled in **first**.
* **SUV 2** (yellow arrow, the RAV4) came in **9 seconds later** on the exact same loop: in at
  the same lower-left spot, down the aisle, and back up the Challenger's lane.
* SUV 1 came **closest**. It rolled right alongside the Challenger, about **4 feet** away.
* SUV 2 stopped about **5 feet** off the Challenger's right-rear corner, beside the scooter rider.
* **Neither one parks** in this 36-second clip. Both come up to the car and the scooter, then move
  on. Candace's longer footage shows the SUV parking further up the lot.

A light-colored **scooter** rolls in with the Challenger and stays at its right rear, about 5
feet away, for the whole clip. The rider never appears to get off. Both SUVs drive right up to
that scooter. Who the rider is remains unknown.

## Make, model and year (best guess)

* **SUV 1:** black **Chevrolet Tahoe, 2015-2020 (50%)**. Chevrolet Suburban is next at 30%. A
  Chevrolet bowtie is visible on the liftgate, so a GMC Yukon is unlikely. Candace's team also
  settled on a Tahoe.
* **SUV 2:** black **Toyota RAV4, 2019-2025 (84%)**. A Toyota emblem is visible on the tailgate.

## The license plates: best guesses, not facts

We ran AI over every frame where a plate showed. Twelve agents each read a slice of the frames,
and every read was stacked into a histogram for each character. The plates were read from the
**helmet-cam** half, where both SUVs are parked. Every string below is a **probability**. The
image beside this section is a mock-up of the top guesses, not a photo of the real plates.

**SUV 1, the Tahoe.** This is the most important vehicle. Light plate, heavy motion blur. Best on
top:

1. **LD2 6JW**
2. LB2 6JM
3. CD2 6JW
4. CB2 6JM
5. LUZ 6JM
6. LIZ 61D
7. LDZ 6JW

**SUV 2, the RAV4.** Dark plate, light characters, five characters. This is the stronger read:

| Plate | Likelihood |
|-------|-----------|
| **GMILY** | 19% |
| GNILY | 8% |
| GWILY | 5% |
| GM1LY | 4% |
| GHILY | 2% |
| OMILY | 2% |

The last three characters, **ILY**, are 77-88% certain each. The second character (M, N or W)
is the open question. Five letters and no numbers looks like a personalized plate. The RAV4 does
**not** match either rental RAV4 plate in the [Egyptian crew rental thread](/Planes/Egyptian-Crew-Hotel)
(Utah T092ZS and Utah T912KM).

Anyone with access to Utah DMV records should be able to find both vehicles from the vehicle
type plus these candidate plates. A plate on this list may belong to someone with no connection
to the case. Do not contact or confront anyone based on this page.

## What the frames cannot tell us

* **The two halves were not filmed at the same time.** The overhead view has long, low morning
  shadows, which fits the 8:29 a.m. arrival. The helmet-cam has midday sun and a clock reading
  **11:23**. The compiler's arrows are what tie the parked SUVs to the ones in the overhead view.
  If that clock was left on Arizona time, 11:23 is 12:23 p.m. in Utah, about the moment of the
  shooting. That is unverified.
* **A witness placed an Arizona rental tag, CWN9872, on a black Tahoe** near University Parkway
  that morning ([Egyptian crew rental thread](/Planes/Egyptian-Crew-Hotel)). SUV 1 is a black
  Chevy, most likely a Tahoe. These frames are too blurry to confirm or rule out that plate.
* **Presence is not purpose.** Two vehicles on the same path and the same schedule is a pattern
  worth answering. It is not proof that anyone in them did anything wrong. Nothing on this page
  says either driver, or the scooter rider, committed any crime. We are asking who they are.

## What would settle it

The campus holds the full-resolution lot footage, and the helmet-cam rider holds the original
file. Either one, before re-compression, would likely read the Tahoe's plate outright. A plate
lookup on GMILY, GNILY, GWILY and GM1LY for a black 2019+ RAV4 would take minutes. The
[Charlie Kirk Disclosure Laws](/Fix/overview) would put trusted investigators on exactly these
records: the registrations, any rental agreements, the full-resolution footage, and the
drivers' own accounts. If you have a sharper copy or you recognize these vehicles, that is the
most useful thing you can bring.
