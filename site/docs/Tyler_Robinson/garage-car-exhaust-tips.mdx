The state says Tyler Robinson drove his grey [2013 Dodge Challenger](./tyler-dodge-challenger)
onto the UVU campus at 8:29 a.m. on September 10, 2025. Citizen investigators have now compared the
**back of that car**, as the campus parking-garage video shows it, with the back of the car police
impounded from his parents' home. **The tailpipes are different.**

## What the post shows

According to [@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2103169123647893795), a citizen
journalist with more than 215,000 followers, posting on September 24, 2026:

> Left - Stock "vacuum style" Dodge Challenger exhaust tip that Tyler Robinson left on his car we see
> when it gets impounded
>
> Right - Rectangular custom Dodge Challenger quad exhaust tip we see on the car in UVU Parking Garage
> on 9/10/25 🫡👇🏻

The difference is not subtle:

* **His car, as impounded:** one exhaust tip per side. A single rectangular chrome surround with
  **one round pipe** set inside it. That is the factory tip.
* **The garage car on September 10:** **two separate rectangular tips side by side**, a quad
  set-up. The post calls them custom, meaning aftermarket.

Exhaust tips are bolted on. A car does not grow a second pair of tips overnight, and someone swapping
custom quad tips back to stock between September 10 and the impound would be a story of its own.

This is the photograph that settles the comparison. The top frame is **his actual car**, on a tow
truck at impound. The bottom frame is **the car from the campus parking garage**. According to
[@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2103154327451816154), in a post seen by
nearly 30,000 people on September 24, 2026:

> UNDER OATH, a Utah SBI officer told you these were both the same vehicle 👀
>
> It is clearly NOT THE SAME vehicle.
> Tyler Robinson's car has had the same dual (not quad) exhaust since his family helped purchase it
> years ago.
>
> They degraded video quality in courtroom evidence to lie.

Look at the red circles. His impounded car has **one tip at the left rear corner and one at the right**,
spread across the bumper. The garage car has **both tips together under one corner**. That is a
different exhaust layout, not a different angle.

### What the agent actually said

The officer is SBI Agent [David Hull](/People/david-hull), who walked the court through the
campus footage at the [preliminary hearing, Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2). His words, from
the hearing record:

> **Agent Hull:** That's the vehicle that is believed to belong to Mr. Robinson arriving in the parking
> garage on campus. [...] It's approximately 8:30 in the morning on September 10th, 2025.
>
> **Mr. Sturgill:** And what leads you to believe that this particular vehicle belongs to Mr. Robinson?
>
> **Agent Hull:** Information that was provided regards to driver's license and Department of Motor
> Vehicle records, the vehicle fits the description of a vehicle owned by Mr. Robinson.
>
> **Mr. Sturgill:** Is there anything unique about that vehicle that stands out to you?
>
> **Agent Hull:** The shape of the vehicle is very distinct, but predominantly the wheels were very
> distinctive on this version of the vehicle.

Read that closely. The identification rests on the car **"fitting the description"** of a DMV record
and on its **shape and wheels**. No plate read. No VIN. No telematics. The one feature that visibly
differs, the exhaust, was never mentioned. Earlier in the same testimony Hull said the exhibit was
"true and accurate" and that "the only changes are some zooms and red highlights." The post's charge
is that the courtroom video was **degraded** so the exhaust could not be compared. Only the original,
full-resolution camera export can answer that.

Agent Hull is a living person and has not been charged with anything. Nothing here says he knowingly
testified falsely. What the images show is that **the state's identification of this car appears to
be wrong**, and that it was presented to the court under oath as a match.

## Why it matters: the government said it was his car

This is the point that matters most. The **charging affidavit**, sworn to a judge, states that
investigators "identified Robinson arriving on UVU campus in a grey Dodge Challenger at approximately
08:29." Prosecutors and the national press repeated it (BBC and ABC News timelines among them): *his car,
on campus, that morning.*

If the exhaust tips are what the post shows, **the car on the campus video was not Tyler Robinson's
car**, and the government's statement that it was is false. Citizen investigators say plainly that the
government lied about it. The identification was never proven by a plate read, a VIN, a telematics
download, or the car's own dash camera. It was asserted, and a grey coupe on a grainy camera was
enough for the whole country to accept it.

The car is also the bridge the state needs. Without it, nothing on the morning footage ties the man
walking from that car to Tyler Robinson except the text-message confession, which investigators
already say was [fabricated](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/faked-confession).

## The record already had cracks

This is not the only thing wrong with the car in the official paperwork:

* **Two different plates.** Warrant 3213501 lists plate **T44CE**. Warrant 3214771 lists
  **T455CE** and a VIN, and says it was re-filed "for clarifying and relevancy purposes."
  ([details](./tyler-dodge-challenger#what-the-record-says-about-the-car))
* **Two different towns.** The first warrant puts the car in St. George, the second in Orem.
* **The data was seized and never shown.** The state took the car's infotainment/telematics system
  and its **VANTRUE dash camera**. Either would show where the car was on the morning of
  September 10. Neither has been produced
  ([aircraft and ground vehicles](/court/ai_attorney/aircraft-and-ground-vehicles)).
* **More than one grey Challenger.** Grey Challenger sightings on campus, on West 925 South at
  00:30, and near the alleged staging house keep being merged into one car
  ([neighborhood reports](/After/house/neighborhood-reports)).

## What we cannot yet confirm

* **The first post's left image is a reference picture of the tip style**, from an image search. It
  is not a photograph of Robinson's car. The second post's top frame **is** his car at impound, and
  it shows one tip at each corner.
* **Both garage frames are low resolution.** They are copies of copies. The comparison is clear on
  layout (one tip per corner vs two tips together) but the original export would remove any doubt.
* **We do not yet hold the full, uncropped garage frame** with its camera timestamp. That frame is
  the one to preserve.
* **Owners change parts.** The simplest innocent explanation is that the tips were changed. That
  would have to have happened in the day and a half between the shooting and the impound, and it
  would be on the state to show it.

## What would settle it

The originals. The impound photo above already shows his car's exhaust. What is missing is the
**full-resolution, unaltered UVU parking-garage export** from 9/10/25, and the state's own rear
photographs of the impounded car taken under both vehicle warrants. Put those side by side. If the tips match, this claim is finished. If they do not, the
state's identification of the car falls, and the question becomes **whose Challenger was in the UVU
garage.** The [Charlie Kirk Disclosure Laws](/Fix/overview) would force exactly that record out.
Robinson is **charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent**.
