:::tip Start here — the one page that puts him 209 miles away
Of everything in this section, **Panguitch is the thread that would end the case fastest if it holds**.
Staff at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah — **209 road miles and roughly three and a half hours south
of Orem** — say the man who ate alone at their counter from **8:55 to 9:47 PM on September 10, 2025** was
Tyler Robinson, and the server who says he personally ran the card puts his certainty at **"100%."** If
that is right, the Orem-area manhunt texts the state's night-of case depends on cannot be his, and the
6:38 PM Orem [Dairy Queen](/Locations/Dairy_Queen_Orem) sighting would have required **98–99 mph the whole
way** south. It is the most interesting item in this section because it is the **most checkable one** — a
receipt, a card, and arithmetic — and by every account given on camera, no official body has publicly moved
to close it. The identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**; Robinson is **charged,
not convicted, and presumed innocent**.
**[Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline →](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline)**
:::

The Tyler Robinson section matters because the entire federal and media frame rests on one
proposition: a lone 22-year-old fired a .30-06 from the Losee Center roof and was captured
after a dramatic manhunt. Project materials and X investigators converge on a different
picture — **clothing and backpack mismatches** between campus cameras and the stairwell figure,
an **ATF inconclusive** bullet-to-rifle link, a **6:25 PM Miranda** that predates the
**7:57 PM Discord confession**, and wound trajectories that argue against a distant rooftop
shot. None of that proves alternate shooters in court; it documents why the official story
has not closed the case for this audience. The government's own review was cut short too:
former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said on the record that his
center's parallel check for foreign ties was ordered to stop once Robinson was in custody —
before those leads were exhausted.

The substance clusters into four lines of inquiry readers should not skip. **Identity:**
[Stairs Guy](./Stairs_Guy) and the Black Clothing Suspect
do not match Tyler's morning appearance or weapon-carry feasibility. **Timeline:** the
[25-second egress](./Tyler_9_10_2025), [Dairy Queen sighting](./Travel), and
[Surrender](./Surrender) sequence collide with custody records. **Forensics:** the
[Mauser rifle](./Weapon_Rifle) is class-linked at best; [shaped-charge](/Gun_Bullet/overview)
and [mic](/Mic/overview) theories offer a non-bullet cause of death. **Legal process:**
[gag orders](./Trial/overview), jailhouse informant claims, and withheld discovery appear
alongside messages the state may not introduce forensically.

Prioritize [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index) for a memo-style map, then
[Stairs Guy](./Stairs_Guy) and [Weapon & Rifle](./Weapon_Rifle) if you doubt he shot Charlie,
[Surrender](./Surrender) and [Discord Messages](./Discord_Messages) if you doubt the confession
timeline, and [Trial](./Trial/overview) plus [Mirandizing](/Timeline/Tyler_Robinson/Mirandizing)
for court-record conflicts. Cross-read [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Real Killer](/Killer/overview) when you are ready to move beyond the named defendant.

## The Mirandize Timeline — Why It's Pivotal

When Tyler Robinson was first taken into custody, and when his phone was seized, may be the single
most important unresolved fact about him. Defense filings place a Miranda reading at **6:25 PM on
September 11** — about 90 minutes before the **~7:57 PM** Discord messages attributed to him —
raising the question of who actually typed that confession. Start with the
[Mirandize evidence hub](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview) and the
[6:25 PM custody timeline](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession).

## Rifle-Concealment Satire Clip (Counterpoint)

A widely shared satire video pushes back on one of the recurring skeptic
arguments — that a full-length **.30-06 bolt-action rifle** could not
realistically have been concealed and walked off campus. In the clip, two men
wearing "Candace is a demon" shirts each slide a rifle down one leg of
slim-fit jeans and walk on camera, with the narration: *"we're going to prove
to these people that a 30-06 easily fits inside slim-fitting jeans... see, we
told you."* It is comedy, not a controlled test — no timing, clothing, or
gait comparison to the campus footage is offered — but it is recorded here
because the concealment question is argued on both sides across this section,
and the counter-argument belongs in the record alongside the
[backpack and rifle concealment analysis](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Backpack_Rifle_Impossibility).
