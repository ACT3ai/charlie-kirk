## What the Screenshots Show

Both captures reproduce **page 5, paragraph 3** of the same report. Read off the text:

> "On 9/12/2025, at approximately 0100 hours, FBI Special Agent Lange conducted an interview with
> the boyfriend of Tyler Robinson at the Washington County Sheriff's Office. The boyfriend was
> identified as Lance Twiggs. During the interview, **Lance showed investigators text messages**
> between Tyler Robinson and himself."

Then a transcribed exchange, quoted here as the document renders it:

> Tyler: "I am still ok my love, but am stuck in orem for a little while longer yet"
>
> Tyler: "shouldn't be long until I can come home, but I gotta grab my rifle still. To be honest
> had hoped to keep this secret till I died of old age. I am sorry to involve you"
>
> Lance: "you weren't the one who did it right????"
>
> Tyler: "I am, I'm sorry"
>
> Lance: "I thought they caught the person?"
>
> Tyler: "no, they grabbed some crazy old dude, then interrogated someone in similar clothing"

And in the second block on the same page:

> "The text messages were **photographed by investigators as Lance showed them the text
> messages**."

The substance of this exchange, its provenance, and the disputes over it are covered in the
Discord section &mdash; see
[Warrant: the Twiggs Texts, Page 5](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/warrant-twiggs-texts-page5) and
[Twiggs Immunity and Statements](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/twiggs-immunity-and-statements). We do not
assert the captures are authenticated, and the contents are **allegations recorded in a filing**,
not established fact. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is **charged, not convicted, and
presumed innocent.**

## Why It Matters — for the Steam side

Look at the mechanism, not the content.

Within roughly **thirty-nine hours** of the shooting, the FBI had a full message record from this
circle. Not by subpoena, not by forensic extraction, not by court order. **One person handed over
a phone and let agents photograph the screen.**

That is the fast path, and it worked. It is also the *only* path that produced a message record in
this case, and it produced exactly one: the SMS thread on one roommate's handset.

Now list what it did not touch:

| Produced | Not produced |
|---|---|
| SMS between two people on one phone | The **Steam friend list** |
| Photographed on the spot, night of 9/11&ndash;9/12 | **Private Steam messages** |
| Volunteered by the holder | **Voice chat sessions** &mdash; who, how often, how long |
| One platform, one device | **Login IP history** for the profile and every friend on it |
| | Whether any of those accounts was **outside the United States** |

The right-hand column is not on anybody's phone. It is on **Valve's servers**, and getting it
requires compelling a private company &mdash;
[The Friend List and What Valve Holds](./steam-friend-list-and-what-valve-holds). Nothing in the
public record indicates that return has been obtained, and prosecutors have said they
[do not intend to offer the forensic extractions](./device-record-not-being-offered) that would be
the device-side mirror of it.

So the case has a message record from the person who volunteered one, and no record at all from
the platform where the friendships lived. That asymmetry is the argument.

**A Steam friend list is chosen one person at a time.** If anyone online knew him well enough to
influence him, they are on that list. The names visible in the public captures are collected on
[The Steam Names Roster](./steam-friends-and-handles-roster), and **none of this is saying that any
of those people did anything illegal or immoral.**

## What Would Settle It

* **Valve's subpoena return**: friend list with account identifiers, private message record, and
  full login IP history.
* **Session-IP correlation to the September 10&ndash;12 window** &mdash; the same window this
  interview covers.
* Whether the FBI ever served Valve at all, and if so, what came back.
* Whether any account in the circle was operated from **outside the United States**, and from which
  country.

## The Fix

* **Law 1 &mdash; DoJ / FBI Forced Disclosure** forces out whatever the Bureau already pulled from
  Valve and from Discord.
* **Law 3 &mdash; Require to Investigate** makes the friend-list and IP inquiry mandatory instead of
  optional.
* **Law 4 &mdash; Trusted Investigations** is the one that reaches a private company directly, and
  lets trusted investigators take testimony from people in that circle under threat of perjury &mdash;
  the same voluntary-cooperation mechanism that produced this interview, except that it does not
  depend on someone volunteering.

[Read the four laws &rarr;](https://whoassassinatedcharliekirk.com/Fix/overview) &middot;
[The explainer video &rarr;](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1RbDMiEReys&t=133s)
