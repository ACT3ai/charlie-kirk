## The running joke this wall is made of

Three of the eight comments on this capture &mdash; this one, the
[Sep 10 comment](./account-thank-you), and the [Aug 29 comment](./account-miss-your-son-is-clicker-trained)
&mdash; are the same running gag, carried across months by different accounts. The
*"#letshumpitup"* hashtag and the *"hump nation"* framing are in-group nonsense of the kind that
accumulates in any friend group's comment wall over years.

The display name itself &mdash; a name in the *Xx_..._xX* format &mdash; is a deliberate joke about
an early-2010s gamertag convention. Nothing about it is a disguise or an operational alias. It is a
person being funny on the internet.

We list it because it is a name readable off the capture, and because a roster with gaps in it is
not a roster. **The presence of this name on this page implies nothing about the person behind
it.**

## The friend list is the thing that would matter

A Discord server can hold hundreds of people who have never spoken to each other. **A Steam friend
list is chosen one person at a time**, over years of voice chat. If anyone in Robinson's online
life knew him well enough to matter, they are likelier to be on that list than on a server roster.

Whether this account was ever **on** that friend list is not publicly known. Commenting on a public
profile does not require being a friend of it. Valve knows which it was, and Valve has not been
asked in public.

See [The Friend List and What Valve Holds](./steam-friend-list-and-what-valve-holds).

## Why this account has a page at all

Because the alternative list does not exist.

Every username in the [Discord](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/overview) group chat was **redacted** in
the search warrant except the one attributed to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) &mdash;
see [Every Other Username Was Redacted](./warrant-redacted-every-other-username). The state has
also said it **does not intend to offer** material obtained through forensic extractions of the
seized electronics &mdash; see [The Device Record Nobody Is Offering](./device-record-not-being-offered).

So the roster around this case is not in the public record, and on current course it is not going
to be. The one place names entered public view is the **Steam comment wall**, where visitors typed
their own names and handles in the open, and the **Steam profiles** in the same circle, archived
before they were scrubbed.

That is a thin basis, and we say so. It is also the only basis there is &mdash; and an
investigation that can obtain **login records** could rule this account **in or out in an
afternoon**.

## What Would Settle It

* **Valve's login-IP history** for this account, which would place it in a country.
* Whether it was ever **on the friend list** rather than only a wall commenter.
* Whether the FBI's twenty-plus interviews touched it &mdash; and if not, **saying so**.

## The Fix

None of the records above is available to the public, and nothing in the current process compels
their release. The [Charlie Kirk disclosure laws](https://whoassassinatedcharliekirk.com/Fix/overview)
are the mechanism that does.

* **Law 1 &mdash; FBI and DOJ Disclosure.** The federal file on this circle, including whatever
  the twenty-plus interviews produced, is held and not published.
* **Law 2 &mdash; US Intelligence Disclosure.** If any account in this circle was operated from
  outside the United States, US intelligence already knows or can find out. Law 2 forces that
  answer into the open &mdash; and a clean answer **clears** everyone here by name.
* **Law 4 &mdash; Trusted Investigations.** The strongest of the four, because it removes the
  Department of Justice as gatekeeper. It lets trusted investigators compel records from a
  **private company** &mdash; Discord, Valve &mdash; and take testimony from people who were
  actually in that circle under threat of perjury.

## The Location Field Is Typed In. The IP Address Is Not.

On Discord, on Steam, and on every platform in this section, the country shown on a profile is
**a text box the user filled in**. Anyone can type Utah, or Egypt, or nothing at all, and change
it again in seconds. Whether it is shown publicly is a setting. It is not evidence of where a
person was sitting when they typed a message.

What is **not** typed in is the **IP address**. Every session, every login, every message send is
written to the platform's own logs with the address it arrived from, alongside device
fingerprints and session timing. Discord holds that record for the servers named in the Utah State
Bureau of Investigation's warrant &mdash; **Craftopia**, **Gamin** and **The Corruption** &mdash;
and for the account that posted the confession. Valve holds the equivalent record for every Steam
account in the same circle.

That is the question this section keeps returning to: **is it known where the people in these
servers actually were?** The FBI reportedly examined twenty or more people in Robinson's Discord
circle and charged none of them, and nothing in the public record states where any of them were.
To be explicit, because it matters: **being in another country is not wrongdoing**, no one in
those servers has been accused by any authority of anything, and nothing on this site suggests
any of them did anything improper. The point is narrower &mdash; that a location nobody has
verified should not be treated as if it were verified, in either direction.

The answer exists. It is in Discord's subpoena return and in the login-IP history for
September 10&ndash;12 &mdash; records that investigators can obtain through lawful process and
the public cannot. **What we are asking for is that the record for the account that posted the
confession be established through that process and, so far as the law allows, made public.**
Until it is, every claim of foreign involvement in these chats &mdash; and every denial &mdash;
rests on a field somebody typed.

We are **not** asking anyone to identify, locate, contact or expose any private individual, and
readers should not attempt it. We name no one and we accuse no one. This is a request for a
record, not an allegation against a person.
