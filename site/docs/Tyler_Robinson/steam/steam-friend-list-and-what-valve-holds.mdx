This is the thesis of the whole Steam section, stated once, in one place.

## A friend list is not a member list

A **Discord server** can hold hundreds of people who have never spoken to each other. Joining one
proves close to nothing &mdash; you click an invite link and you are in a room with strangers.
Discord itself has said it found [no evidence of planning](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/discord-says-no-plotting)
on its platform.

A **Steam friend list is chosen one person at a time.** Each name on it is a deliberate act: a
request sent, a request accepted. Steam is a PC gaming platform with voice chat built into the
games, and the people on a friend list are the people you spend hundreds of hours talking to,
night after night, sometimes for years.

That difference is the entire reason this section exists. **If anyone in
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s online life knew him well enough to influence him, they
are far more likely to be on the Steam friend list than in a Discord server roster.**

Robinson is **charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent**, and the attribution of the archived
Steam profile to him is the poster's claim rather than a documented court finding &mdash; see
[the profile thread](./x-thread-craftin247-steam) and
[Steam Profile and Comments](/Tyler_Robinson/Messages/steam-messages) for the evidentiary standing.

## What has never been published

Every archived capture in this section shows the **outside** of the account: an avatar, a bio, a
badge count, a public comment wall. Not one of them shows the thing that would matter.

| Published, and circulating widely | Never published by anyone |
|---|---|
| Profile page, avatar, display name | The **friend list** with account identifiers |
| Self-entered country field | **Login IP history** per session |
| Public comment wall | **Private** Steam messages and voice chat |
| Item and inventory counts | Device fingerprints, session timing |
| Screenshots taken by strangers after Sept 10 | Whether and when the pages were **scrubbed** |

The right-hand column is not missing because it does not exist. It exists, in one place, in
Valve's own operational logs. It is missing because no public process has compelled it out.

## Why Valve's copy is the good copy

The account holder controls the left column. They typed the display name. They typed the country.
They can delete a comment, rename a profile, or set it private &mdash; and the archived captures
show a profile that was **renamed** (craftin247 &rarr; milk) and an Item Showcase marked *"This
profile is private."*

The account holder controls **none** of the right column. Every session, every login, every message
is written to Valve's own servers with the address it arrived from. It is not a claim anyone
makes; it is a byproduct of the connection existing at all. That is what makes it worth having,
and it is the subject of
[Typed-In Country vs. Logged IP](./self-reported-location-vs-login-ip).

## The open questions

Nothing in the public record answers any of these:

* **Were one or more of the people in this circle outside the United States?**
* If so, **which country** was each of them in?
* **What did the messages actually say** &mdash; the voice chat, the private Steam messages, the
  parts of the comment history since removed?
* Was any of it a **foreign intelligence** contact rather than a gaming friendship?

The names we can read off the public captures are collected on
[The Steam Names Roster](./steam-friends-and-handles-roster). That roster is a list of people to
**clear**, not a list of suspects. **None of this is saying that any of these people did anything
illegal or immoral.**

## Why the ordinary process will not produce it

Two things in the case record close this door rather than open it.

* Every username in the Discord group except Robinson's was **redacted** out of the search
  warrant. [Every Other Username Was Redacted](./warrant-redacted-every-other-username).
* Prosecutors have stated they **do not intend to offer** evidence obtained through forensic
  extractions of the seized electronics. [The Device Record Nobody Is Offering](./device-record-not-being-offered).

A record that is not offered is not tested, not disclosed, and not published. The friend list sits
behind both of those doors at once: it is on a private company's servers, and the device that
would mirror it is not being put in front of anyone.

## What Would Settle It

* **Valve's subpoena return** for the profile: account creation date, complete login IP history,
  and the friend list resolved to account identifiers rather than display names.
* The same return for **every account on that friend list** and for the accounts that posted on the
  public comment wall.
* **Correlation of session IPs to the September 10&ndash;12 window.**
* The private Steam message and voice-session record for the weeks before September 10.
* Whether, when and by whom the public pages were scrubbed.

## The Fix

Valve is a private company in Washington State. No amount of public pressure produces its server
logs, and the current process is not asking for them. The
[Charlie Kirk disclosure laws](https://whoassassinatedcharliekirk.com/Fix/overview) are built for
exactly this gap.

* **Law 4 &mdash; Trusted Investigations** is the one that reaches Valve. It removes the Department
  of Justice as gatekeeper and lets a trusted investigation team compel records from a **private
  company** and take testimony from individuals under threat of perjury.
* **Law 2 &mdash; US Intelligence Disclosure** answers the foreign question. If any account in this
  circle was operated from outside the country, US intelligence can establish that, and Law 2
  forces the answer out.
* **Law 1** forces out what the FBI already pulled; **Law 3** makes the inquiry mandatory rather
  than optional.
