The [Discord section](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/overview) covers where the confession appeared.
This section covers where the friendships actually lived.

A Discord server can hold hundreds of people who have never spoken to each other. A **Steam
friend list is chosen one person at a time**, and the people on it are the people you spend
hundreds of hours in voice chat with, night after night, for years. If anyone in
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s online life knew him well enough to influence him,
they are more likely to be on that list than in a server member roster.

## The Location Field Is Typed In. The IP Address Is Not.

On Steam, exactly as on Discord, the country shown on a profile is **a text box the user filled
in**. Anyone can type Utah, or Egypt, or nothing at all, and change it in seconds. It is not
evidence of where a person was sitting.

What is not typed in is the **IP address**. Every session, every login, every message is written
to the platform's own logs with the address it arrived from, along with device fingerprints and
session timing. Valve holds that record for this profile and for every account on its friend list.

That is the open question: **is it known where the people in this circle actually were?**
Nothing in the public record answers it, and self-reported locations are not evidence either way.
**Being in another country is not wrongdoing**, nobody in this circle has been accused by any
authority of anything, and nothing here suggests any of them did anything improper. The record
that would answer it &mdash; subpoena returns and login-IP history &mdash; is obtainable by
investigators through lawful process and not by the public. **What we ask is that it be
established through that process and, so far as the law allows, made public.**

We are **not** asking anyone to identify, locate, contact or expose any private individual, and
readers should not attempt it. Commenting on a gaming profile or listing a handle in a bio is not
evidence of anything at all. We name no one and we accuse no one.

## What Would Settle It

* Valve's subpoena return for the profile: account creation, login IP history, and the friend
  list with account identifiers.
* The same for the accounts that posted Discord handles on the comment wall.
* Correlation of any session IP to the September 10&ndash;12 window.
* Whether, when and by whom the public pages were scrubbed after the event.
