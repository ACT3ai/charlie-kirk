# Tyler Robinson's Car — The 2013 Dodge Challenger

[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) owned a **grey 2013 Dodge Challenger**. It is often
mis-called a "Charger" online; the Charger is Dodge's four-door sedan, and every court record calls
his car a **Challenger**, the two-door coupe. This page sets out what the state has put on the record
about that car, and why it matters so much.

:::danger The state's case runs through one car
The charging affidavit says investigators "identified Robinson arriving on UVU campus in a grey
Dodge Challenger at approximately 08:29" on September 10, 2025. That sentence is how the state ties
the man on the campus cameras to Tyler Robinson. **Citizen investigators now say the Challenger on
the UVU parking-garage video is a different car** — its exhaust tips do not match his. If they are
right, the government told the court and the public that a car was his when it was not.
See **[The Garage Car Was Not His — The Exhaust Tips](./garage-car-exhaust-tips)**.
:::

## What the record says about the car

* **Make and year.** A 2013 Dodge Challenger, grey. Sworn in the charging
  [probable cause affidavit](/court/overview) (September 12 and 16, 2025).
* **Plate and VIN.** Search warrant **3214771** (September 14, 2025) lists Utah plate **T455CE**,
  VIN **2C3CDYAG0DH675106**.
  [PDF](/court/filings/warrants/2025-09-14_SW3214771_2013_Dodge_Challenger_Utah_Lic_T455CE_VIN_2C3CDYAG0DH675106_editorial.pdf).
* **A second plate number.** The earlier warrant, **3213501** (September 12, 2025, Washington
  County, St. George), describes "a 2013 Dodge Challenger with a Utah License Plate **T44CE**."
  [PDF](/court/filings/warrants/2025-09-12_SW3213501_2013_Dodge_Challenger_Utah_Lic_T44CE_Returned_9_18_25.pdf).
  The later warrant says outright that it covers the same car as 3213501 and was re-filed "for
  clarifying and relevancy purposes." It does not say what was being clarified.
* **Two different towns.** Warrant 3213501 places the car in **St. George**. Warrant 3214771, two
  days later, places it in **Orem**.
* **Where it was collected.** Crime-scene Sergeant [Jennifer Faumuina](/People/jennifer-faumuina)
  testified at the preliminary hearing (Day 4, July 9, 2026, transcript page 122) that the car
  "was collected at the Robinsons' parents' home" and that it "is a gray colored Dodge Challenger."
* **The family statement.** The affidavit says a family member "confirmed Robinson had a grey Dodge
  Challenger." That confirms he **owned** such a car. It does not show that the car on the campus
  video **was** that car.
* **The engine.** Under standard Chrysler VIN decoding, the eighth character **G** is the 3.6-litre
  V6 used in the SXT trim, the base model. The citizen claim on the next page is that the garage car
  wears custom quad exhaust tips, which a stock SXT does not have.

## What the car should be able to prove, and has not

The state asked for everything this car could tell it and has not shown any of it:

1. **The infotainment and telematics download.** Warrant 3214771 expressly sought "the
   Infotainment/Telematic Systems and related digital storage media." That data would show where
   the car was on September 10. No extraction has been produced in public.
   ([Aircraft and ground vehicles](/court/ai_attorney/aircraft-and-ground-vehicles))
2. **The VANTRUE dash camera.** Warrant 3215391 seized a dash camera and memory card from the car.
   A dash camera records the road it drives. Its footage of the morning of September 10 would show
   whether this car went to UVU at all.
3. **Photographs of the car as impounded.** Both warrants authorize photographs of the exterior. A
   single rear photo of the impounded car settles the exhaust-tip question.

Any one of those three would confirm or destroy the 8:29 a.m. identification. None has been made
public, a year after the arrest.

SBI Agent [David Hull](/People/david-hull) told the court how the car was tied to Robinson.

**[Day 1](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_1):** the tip file included "a DMV record with an
associated vehicle." Asked the make and model: "The Dodge Challenger." Then: "It was **silver** in
color, and I believe there were **two registered owners** of the vehicle... Mr. Robinson and then his
mother." The affidavit calls the car **grey**.

**[Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2):** over the garage video, "That's the vehicle that is
**believed to belong** to Mr. Robinson arriving in the parking garage on campus." Asked why: "the
vehicle **fits the description** of a vehicle owned by Mr. Robinson," and "predominantly the
**wheels** were very distinctive."

So the match presented to the court is a DMV description, a body shape and a set of wheels. No plate
and no VIN were read off the garage footage. According to
[@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2103154327451816154): "UNDER OATH, a Utah SBI
officer told you these were both the same vehicle 👀 It is clearly NOT THE SAME vehicle. Tyler
Robinson's car has had the same dual (not quad) exhaust since his family helped purchase it years
ago. They degraded video quality in courtroom evidence to lie." The side-by-side is on
**[The Garage Car Was Not His](./garage-car-exhaust-tips)**. Agent Hull is a living person; nothing
here says he knowingly testified falsely. It says the identification appears to be wrong.

## Why this matters

The prosecution's account is a **single-actor, single-car case**
([warrant gap analysis](/court/ai_attorney/crime-scene-warrant-gap-suppression)). The Challenger
arrives on campus at 8:29, the man on the cameras walks from it, and the Challenger drives him
south afterwards on the [25-hour driving timeline](./twenty-five-hour-driving-timeline). If the car on
the garage video is not his car, the link between the campus footage and Tyler Robinson rests on
nothing but the confession — the confession investigators say was
[faked](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/faked-confession).

Several grey Challengers appear in reporting about that week — on campus, on West 925 South at
00:30, and near the alleged staging house. They are kept apart on
[the neighborhood reports page](/After/house/neighborhood-reports). Robinson is **charged, not
convicted, and presumed innocent**.
