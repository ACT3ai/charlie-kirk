# The Goforth Midnight Stop

About twelve hours after [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed at
[Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview), a gray **Dodge Challenger** reportedly pulled up to the
corner of **Campus Drive and 800 South**, right on the edge of the campus. A police officer on
security detail spoke with the driver. The prosecution now uses that brief contact to put
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) **back in Orem after midnight**. The problem is timing.
Restaurant staff **209 road miles south**, in Panguitch, say he was paying for a steak dinner at
**9:47 PM** that same night. Both accounts cannot be true on an ordinary drive.

Robinson is **charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent**. Nothing on this page is a court
finding. The officer's account reaches us **second-hand**, through another investigator's testimony,
and the Panguitch identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**.

## What the court heard, word for word

The stop entered the record on **Day 2 of the preliminary hearing** (July 7, 2026). It came during
the prosecution's direct examination of Utah investigator [David Hull](/People/david-hull) by
prosecutor [David Sturgill](/People/david-sturgill). **Officer Goforth did not testify.** Everything
the court heard about the stop is Hull's account of what Goforth told him. The full hearing record is
on the [Day 2 page](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2).

State's video compilation **Exhibit 12** was described as showing Robinson *"arriving and leaving in
a vehicle, arriving and leaving on foot, and then returning and leaving, and then attempting to gain
access again in a vehicle."* That final clip is this stop. The exchange, from the Rev transcript at
**00:57:15–00:59:41**:

> **Hull:** This is the intersection of Campus Drive and 800 South. There's a vehicle attempting to
> turn right onto Campus Drive. That's the vehicle that's driven by Mr. Robinson. …
> That vehicle's now pulling away and heading east on 800 South.
>
> **Sturgill:** And why do you believe that that vehicle was Mr. Robinson's vehicle?
>
> **Hull:** When that vehicle was stopped, it actually made contact with an officer who was doing
> security detail. … Officer Goforth. … Spanish Fork Police. … It was approximately **12:30 AM**
> … On September 11th, 2025.
>
> **Hull:** It was an interaction that, **I guess cop intuition**, but there was something about the
> interaction that made him make a note of the license plate of the vehicle. And then subsequently,
> when information was available that that vehicle **or a vehicle matching the description** of that
> was involved in the incident, he ran the information … Yes, he ran a **partial plate**.
>
> **Hull:** That there were two registered owners of the vehicle, **Tyler Robinson and an Amber
> Robinson**. … It was a male that he **believed** to be Tyler Robinson.

That is the whole of it on the record. Hull gave no bodycam, no written stop report, no citation, and
no field-interview card. There is no statement about what the driver said, and no explanation of what
made the contact memorable. Hearing coverage describes the contact as roughly **20 seconds** long.

## Why the state needs this stop

The stop does a specific job in the prosecution's timeline. Nothing else in the public record puts the
Challenger at UVU after dark with a named officer attached.

* **It supports the rifle-retrieval texts.** The messages reportedly recovered from
  [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs)' phone describe waiting for police to leave the
  [wooded area](/Locations/Wooded_Area_Gun_Recovery) so the rifle could be retrieved. A car trying
  to get back onto Campus Drive at 12:30 AM is presented as that attempt.
* **It ties plate to person.** Campus cameras show a car. The stop is offered as the moment an
  officer put a face and a registration together.
* **It came before the surrender.** Prosecution-side summaries of Day 2 call it an independent thread
  that **predates** the surrender. Otherwise, the identification rests largely on the family bringing
  him in on the evening of September 11
  ([Surrender](./Surrender); [Washington County Sheriff](/Locations/Washington_County_Sheriff)).

## Six problems citizen investigators raise

The following are questions raised on X and by hearing-watchers. They are **not** accusations against
Officer Goforth or Agent Hull. Each is a gap that the public record has not filled.

1. **Cop intuition, not cause.** Nothing on the record says what prompted the note. Hull's account
   implies the car had not yet been linked to the case when the note was made, which is why
   "intuition" is the stated reason. But intuition is
   also the one reason that cannot be tested on cross-examination when the officer is not on the stand.
2. **A partial plate, run later.** Goforth wrote down part of a plate and ran it only after a vehicle
   *"matching the description"* was linked to the case. A partial plate returns a **list** of vehicles.
   How the list was narrowed to one Challenger has not been explained in open court.
3. **"Believed to be."** The identification is Hull relaying Goforth's belief. It is not a sworn
   identification by the officer. The officer who says he saw the driver's face has not been
   cross-examined by the defense.
4. **Where is the camera?** Officers on UVU security detail that night were working a homicide scene.
   Questions posted on X focus on why a stop important enough to be the compilation's final clip
   produced only a noted partial plate, and no bodycam clip of a face.
5. **The other Challenger.** On cross-examination, defense attorney [Kathryn Nester](/People/kathryn-nester)
   had Hull confirm that the **Nobles**, who supplied the Ring doorbell video of the car, told
   investigators the driver was **bald** and that **three other people** were in the car. Hull
   agreed he *"did not"* mention that on direct (Day 2, **02:06:46–02:07:08**). Robinson is not bald,
   and the state's account has him alone. Citizen timeline researchers, including
   [@HadynWood](https://x.com/HadynWood), list this as one of several conflicts in the vehicle
   evidence. They also cite search-warrant language describing the car as parked near that home for
   a long stretch around the same hour. If that holds, the same car cannot be both parked for an
   hour and making a 20-second contact at an intersection.
6. **The order of the compilation.** Exhibit 12 plays the stop as the final clip, right after the
   **12:24:42 PM** clip of the figure crossing Campus Drive. Hull dated the stop to **12:30 AM** the
   next day from his conversation with Goforth, not from a timestamp read aloud off the video. The
   burned-in timestamp on that clip has not been published.

## The collision with Panguitch

The staff at **Cowboy's Smokehouse** in Panguitch say a lone, quiet man at their counter ordered
at **8:55 PM** and paid **$38.51** by Visa debit at **9:47 PM** on September 10, 2025. They say they
recognized him from the released photos as Robinson. Citizen journalist
[Diligent Denizen](/Influencers/x/diligent-denizen) interviewed the staff and published the receipt.
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and [@VLuvMully](/Influencers/x/vluvmully) amplified it. The
full receipt and visit are on [Cowboy's Smokehouse Receipt](./cowboys-smokehouse-receipt) and
[Cowboy's Smokehouse Full Visit](./cowboys-smokehouse-full-visit). The restaurant has
since said it has no evidence establishing whether he was there
([Chain: Panguitch Breaks the Evening](/Narrative/chains/panguitch-breaks-the-evening)). Weigh the
identification with that in mind.

Put the two anchors side by side:

| | Panguitch counter | Goforth stop |
|---|---|---|
| Place | Cowboy's Smokehouse, Panguitch | Campus Drive & 800 South, Orem |
| Time | Paid **9:47 PM**, Sept 10 | **~12:30 AM**, Sept 11 |
| Source | Receipt + staff identification | Hull relaying Goforth's belief |
| Road distance between them | **~209 miles** | |
| Normal drive time | **3 h 09 min – 3 h 40 min** | |
| Time actually available | **~2 h 43 min** | |
| Average speed required | **~77 mph, nonstop**, from the moment the card cleared | |

A normal drive leaving the moment the card was approved arrives **between about 12:56 and 1:27 AM**.
That is too late for a 12:30 AM contact. Hitting 12:30 AM means beating the mapping estimate by
roughly half an hour with **zero stops**, on a route that starts on two-lane US-89, during an active
statewide manhunt. That is not strictly impossible. It is simply not the evening the state
describes. The southbound leg is worse: from the
[Orem Dairy Queen sighting](/Locations/Dairy_Queen_Orem) at 6:38 PM to an 8:55 PM order in Panguitch
would take **~98–99 mph the entire way**
([Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline)).

The direction is also odd. Robinson's home is in the St. George / Washington area, **south** of
Panguitch. The state's story has him eat 3 hours south of the scene, drive 3 hours **back north** to
the scene, then turn around and drive south again. That last leg is reportedly captured at the
[Cedar City Maverik](/Locations/Cedar_City_Maverik) around **7:15 AM** on September 11. One commentator
totals that as roughly **13 hours 20 minutes** at the wheel in a single day
([The 25-Hour Driving Day](./twenty-five-hour-driving-timeline)).

## Three ways the two stories can be reconciled

Readers should hold all three open. The record cannot yet pick between them.

* **The Panguitch customer was not Robinson.** Then the staff identification is mistaken, and the
  state's night-of account survives. The last four digits of the card, which staff say they gave to
  investigators, would settle it in minutes. Nobody has publicly said whose card it was, and staff say
  they were [told not to talk](/CoverUp/Panguitch_Told_Not_To_Talk).
* **The Goforth driver was not Robinson.** Then the stop is a different car, a different driver, or a
  partial plate that matched to the wrong registration. The bald driver and the three passengers the
  Nobles described point this way. So does the second-hand, belief-level identification.
* **The midnight story was assembled after the fact.** Some citizen investigators read the stop, the
  Ring video and the rifle-retrieval texts as **one package** that only works together. In that
  reading, the Panguitch receipt pulls on all three at once. This is the framing theory set out under
  [Patsy Framework](/Gov_Mind_Control/tyler-robinson-patsy-framework). It is an argument about the
  evidence, **not** an accusation that any named officer invented anything.

## What would settle it

These are records that exist or should exist. Each one is a discovery request the defense can make:

* **Officer Goforth's own report**, notes and bodycam for the contact, and his sworn testimony.
* **The partial plate as written**, and the full list of vehicles it returned.
* **The native video file for the final Exhibit 12 clip**, with its embedded timestamp.
* **The Noble interview report** (defense poll 48) and the full Ring/Nest export with metadata.
* **Bank records for the Visa debit card** that paid Check #211 at Cowboy's Smokehouse, plus the
  restaurant's POS log. Diligent Denizen has said packets were sent to both the prosecution and the defense.
* **Phone location data** for the evening, which would place the handset whichever way it points.

Until those surface, the stop remains what it is on the record: **one officer's intuition, one partial
plate, and one belief, relayed by a second investigator**. The whole post-midnight half of the state's
timeline rests on it. For how that timeline was built around the
[33-hour manhunt](/Narrative/fed_narrative/thirty-three-hour-manhunt), and how each piece scores, see the
[Fed Narrative Scorecard](/Narrative/score_card/fed-narrative-scorecard).

**Status of people named:** Tyler Robinson, alive, charged, not convicted. Officer Goforth, David Hull,
Lance Twiggs, alive. Not accused of wrongdoing on this page.
