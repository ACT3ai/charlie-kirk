Photographs circulating on X show many pages of frenzied ink drawings and scribbles —
on lined notebook paper and on yellow sticky-note pads — reportedly recovered from the
home shared by [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) and his roommate
[Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs). The images appear as a phone photo-gallery,
each page held up bare-handed for the camera. Who actually drew them, and who
photographed them, has not been established in court; attribution to Twiggs is a
claim made by X commentators, not a proven fact.

## What the Drawings Show

Across the photographed pages, the drawings are dense, layered scribbles mixed with
small sketches and fragments of handwriting. Legible fragments include:

* "Calm Down" and "stabilize"
* "good call?"
* "do anything for me"
* "patience virtue"
* "then re-educate ex/let them spell it"
* A word beginning "Expl—" (partially obscured by scribbling)

One gallery screenshot ends on a Discord message dated 09/11/2025 at 9:57 PM from a
user "zealous_monkey_5…": "Hey guys, I have bad news for you all. it was me at UVU
yesterday. im sorry for all" — the confession message discussed on the
[Discord Messages](./Discord_Messages) page.

## The Evidence-Handling Question

According to [@CrystalSmallen](https://x.com/CrystalSmallen/status/2085530517286600707),
the photographs raise a chain-of-custody question:

> "Also I want to know what kind of a police officer or anyone in law enforcement
> that would go into somebody's home take pictures of these crude writings and not
> wear gloves unless they already knew what the addendum was."

The hands holding the pages in the photos are bare. If the photographs were taken by
law enforcement during evidence collection, handling original documents without gloves
would be a departure from standard forensic practice. It is also possible the photos
were taken by a resident of the home before or apart from any police search — the
origin of the photo set is unresolved.

In her quoted post, the same author argues the drawings bear on the question of who
in the household fit the profile being attached to Tyler Robinson: "If we're going to
talk about twigs we're going to talk about the fact that he's far more favorable to a
psychopath then Tyler Robinson." That is her characterization, not a clinical or court
finding, and this site records it as an attributed claim only. Lance Twiggs is a living
person; no court has found him responsible for any wrongdoing in this case.

## Open Questions

1. Who created the drawings — and can handwriting comparison establish it?
2. Who took the photographs, when, and were they part of the official evidence set?
3. If law enforcement took them, why bare-handed handling of original documents?
4. Do the drawings appear anywhere in court discovery, such as the materials on
   [Twiggs' phone](/court/Twiggs_Phone)?
