The thread that put the **craftin247** alias into circulation, and the handle that connects Robinson's
gaming identity across four platforms.

## The post

> "**STEAM SECRETS UNLOCKED:** Tyler James Robinson's **'Milk'** Profile &mdash; A Creepy Dive into
> Gaming Gore, Bizarre Aliases & Ominous Captions. Thread: 1 of 3
>
> We've cracked open Tyler Robinson's (aka **craftin247 &rarr; milk**) **11-year Steam vault**.
> This isn't your average gamer setup. From..."

The capture shows an **Item Showcase** marked *"This profile is private,"* and beneath it the same
comment wall reproduced on
[Discord Handles Left in the Steam Comments](./steam-comments-discord-handles) &mdash; including
*luvr*'s **"Add me on discord! Mine is fentanylsandwich1312"** and *cento*'s September 2 roleplay
bulletin.

## The alias that actually matters

**craftin247** is the thread's real contribution, because the same handle turns up elsewhere. The
[Discord archive leak](./discord_friends) reports accounts under the same or near-identical handle
on:

* **DeviantArt** &mdash; created roughly two years before the shooting, listing pronouns as they/them
* **FurAffinity** &mdash; the same handle, effectively no activity
* **Reddit** &mdash; two accounts, *craftin247* and *craftin248*, one mundane post each, both years old

That cross-platform consistency is the strongest available link between the Steam identity and the
person, and it predates the shooting by years, which is why it cannot have been manufactured
afterwards.

## Two things worth separating

**The framing.** "Creepy," "gaming gore," "ominous captions," "cracked open the vault" &mdash; the
thread is written to alarm. What it actually documents is a private profile, a comment wall of
crude friend-group jokes, and an eleven-year account history. Crude gaming humour among young men
is not evidence of anything, and the thread does not distinguish between the two.

**The data.** The aliases, the account ages, and the cross-platform matches are checkable and
useful. We keep those and set the adjectives aside.

## On the Steam profile settings

The leaker's account notes that Robinson had **most Steam settings set to private** and was level
50 on the platform, while [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) had essentially everything public,
with a last-played game **two days after** September 10 and no activity since. The contrast is
consistent with every other description of the two men's online presence.
