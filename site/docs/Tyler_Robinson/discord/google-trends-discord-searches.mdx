This one belongs in the Discord section for exactly one line inside it, and the rest is a useful
lesson in what a claim needs before it can be used.

## The Discord-relevant line

> "**Tyler's Digital Footprint:** 'Tyler Robinson **Discord**/Reddit/FBI/LDS/TPUSA/Israel' &mdash;
> from **June '24 to Nov '24**. They tracked his EVERY MOVE online. Why? To recruit or silence?"

## The wider claim

The post asserts that IP addresses in **Israel and Washington DC** were searching terms connected
to Charlie Kirk's circle for months beforehand, with clusters around **July 20, 2025** &mdash;
which it notes was the same week Kirk platformed Tucker Carlson at AmFest. Terms it lists include
Lance Twiggs' townhouse address, TPUSA staff names, UVU campus and rooftop terms, and Utah
political figures.

## Why the Discord line cannot bear the weight put on it

Google Trends **does not expose IP addresses**. It reports relative search interest by region over
time, aggregated and normalised. There is no mechanism in it by which a member of the public can
see that a specific country's IPs searched a specific phrase.

That is not a small quibble &mdash; it removes the entire evidentiary basis. Regional interest in
"Tyler Robinson Discord" spiking is expected: it is what a story about a Discord confession
produces. And a dated range of *June to November 2024* for searches naming Robinson raises its own
problem, since Robinson's name was not publicly connected to anything until September 2025.

## What would make this checkable

* The raw Trends exports, with the exact query strings and date windows.
* The region-selection settings used, since Trends normalises within a chosen region.
* Any source at all for the IP-level attribution, which Trends cannot provide.

None of that has been published.

## Why it is here

Because the post circulated widely and readers deserve to find it addressed rather than ignored,
and because it names living people &mdash; TPUSA staff, Utah officials &mdash; in a framing that
implies they were surveilled or targeted. We name no one as a suspect on this basis and neither
should the post.

The [Israel](/Israel/overview) and [Planes](/Planes/overview) sections carry the versions of the
foreign-involvement question that rest on documents rather than on search-interest graphs.
