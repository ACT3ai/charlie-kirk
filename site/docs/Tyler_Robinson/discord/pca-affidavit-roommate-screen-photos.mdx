Everything in the message case starts here, in the affidavit's own words. It is worth reading
slowly, because the document describes its own evidentiary weakness without appearing to notice.

## The passage

> "Investigators identified an individual as **the roommate** of Robinson. Investigators
> interviewed the roommate, who stated that his roommate (referring to Robinson) **made a joke on
> discord**. Investigators asked if he would show them the messages on discord, **he opened it and
> showed several messages to investigators and allowed investigators to take photos of the screen
> as each message was shown by Robinson's roommate**. These photos consisted of various messages,
> including content of messages between the phone contact name **'Tyler' with an emoji icon**, and
> Robinson's roommate's device."

The affidavit then lists what those messages referenced: needing to **retrieve a rifle from a drop
point**, leaving the rifle **in a bush**, and having **changed outfits**.

## Four problems visible on the face of the document

**1. The evidence is a photograph of somebody else's screen.** Not a forensic image, not an export.
A camera pointed at a phone held by a witness, who controlled which messages appeared and in what
order &mdash; *"as each message was shown by Robinson's roommate."*

**2. The platform label is wrong, or Discord is wrong.** The affidavit says Discord. Discord says
the planning content
[was not on Discord](./discord-denies-planning-messages). The affidavit's own description &mdash;
messages between *"the phone contact name 'Tyler' with an emoji icon"* and the roommate's device
&mdash; describes a **phone contact**, which is SMS language, not Discord language. Discord does
not have phone contacts.

**3. The attribution is a contact name.** "Tyler" with an emoji is a label the roommate's phone
applied. It establishes what the device called the sender. It does not establish who held the
sending device.

**4. "Made a joke on discord."** That is how the roommate first characterised it. The document
moves from *joke* to *confession* without recording what changed.

## The surrounding narrative in the same affidavit

The page also records the tip route &mdash; on the evening of September 11 a **family member**
reached out to a **family friend**, who contacted the **Washington County Sheriff's Office**,
relayed to Utah County and the FBI &mdash; and the UVU surveillance description of a grey Dodge
Challenger arriving at approximately **08:29** on September 10.

Booking data at the foot of the page: **Arrest Date/Time 09/12/2025 04:00**, PC entered
**09/12/2025 07:18**, OTN **70090584**, sworn by **Officer Brian Davis**.

That 04:00 arrest stamp sits against the Bates-stamped video showing Robinson **Mirandized at
6:25 PM on September 11** &mdash; roughly ten hours earlier. See
[Bates 003996-R2](./bates-003996-miranda-vs-discord).

## Research needs

* The photographs themselves, in sequence, with EXIF.
* Whether the roommate's device was ever forensically imaged &mdash; and if so, why the state has
  said it [will not offer forensic extractions](./state-wont-offer-forensic-extractions).
* Bodycam from the interview, showing which messages were shown and which were scrolled past.
