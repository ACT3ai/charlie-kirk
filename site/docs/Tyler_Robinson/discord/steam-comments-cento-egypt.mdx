## The comment count

> "Comments &mdash; **View all 1,116 comments**"

The most recent entry is stamped *"43 minutes ago."* A friend-group profile that had a handful of
comments in April was carrying over eleven hundred by the time this capture was taken. Everything
posted after September 11 is strangers, not associates, and none of it is evidence of anything
except public attention.

## The September 2 comment

The one pre-shooting entry visible in full is a piece of gaming roleplay by a user named **cento**,
dated **Sep 2 @ 7:50am**:

> "Breaking News: '[Cute Lamb Needs Attention] **FOUND SHOT IN THE HEAD IN EGYPT.** Authorities say
> the culprit was an **expert gunman from the ghettos of Egypt**. The culprit is named [Milsnss]
> and he has been dealt with by our star officer [cento] who also would like to share a message
> "Yoooo hev mb gangie". Authorities have also interviewed an eye witness named [silly goose] 'Bro
> that guy got yeww. funniest thing I've seen all week honestly.' Eye witness named [silly goose]
> has been sent to a mental institution for his remarks. We'd like to share our condolences to
> [Cute Lamb Needs Attention] and hope for their well being and fortune'"

## Handling this responsibly

Read in hindsight, eight days before a head shot, a joke about someone being "found shot in the
head" by an "expert gunman" is jarring. It has circulated on X as though it were foreshadowing.

It is not. This is a recognisable genre: gaming friend groups write mock news bulletins about each
other constantly, and the bracketed names are the participants' own screen names, killed and
avenged in a game. The bulletin format, the "our star officer" framing, and the "mental institution"
punchline are all standard for it.

We include it because it is in the record and because readers will encounter the X posts about it.
It is a coincidence of language in a gaming joke, and we do not present it as anything more.

## The Egypt thread

"Egypt" in this comment is the joke's setting. Separately, a Steam group member's profile lists
Egypt as their location &mdash; see
[The Ankha Profile and the Egypt Claim](./steam-ankha-egypt-profile) &mdash; and X commentators
have tried to connect the two. There is no established link between a roleplay comment's setting
and another user's profile field.
