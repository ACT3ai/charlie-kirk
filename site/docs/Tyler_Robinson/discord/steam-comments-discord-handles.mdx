The warrant redacted every username in the Discord group **except** Robinson's. But Discord handles
also leaked out sideways &mdash; onto a public Steam comment wall, posted voluntarily by the
commenters themselves.

## What the capture shows

| **luvr** | Apr 26 @ 2:33pm | "I agree! **Add me on discord! Mine is fentanylsandwich1312**" |
| **Alive but Gay** | Apr 26 @ 2:20pm | "Humpers, I think it's time we get to know each other! **Add me on discord 'generic8076'**" |
| Thank You | Apr 23 @ 8:22pm | "always perma all the time 24/7" |

## Two Discord handles, in public, unredacted

* **fentanylsandwich1312** &mdash; posted by *luvr*
* **generic8076** &mdash; posted by *Alive but Gay*

These are handles the commenters published themselves on a public profile. We record them because
they exist in the public record and because the warrant's redactions make the group roster
otherwise unreconstructable &mdash; see
[Tyler Robinson's Discord Friends and Handles](./discord_friends).

We make **no claim whatsoever** that either handle belongs to anyone connected to the shooting, or
that either person knew anything about it. They commented on a gaming profile. That is all this
shows.

## The date that matters

The top comment is stamped **Sep 10 @ 11:01am** &mdash; the morning of the shooting, hours before
it happened, and it is about nothing at all. The wall is ordinary gaming-community joking, right
up to the day.

That ordinariness is itself the evidentiary point. The public trail of the account in the days
before September 10 shows no build-up, no politics, no target. Compare Discord's own statement that
it found [no evidence of planning](./discord-says-no-plotting) on its platform.

## Note on the "1312" suffix

*1312* is a widely used numeric substitution for "ACAB." It is common across a great many online
handles and carries no case significance on its own. We note it only because it will be obvious to
readers and is better addressed than left hanging.
