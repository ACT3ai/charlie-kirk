This is the sharper of the company's two statements, and it is the one that creates a direct
conflict with a sworn document.

## The quote

> "Messages referenced in recent reporting about planning details **do not appear to be Discord
> messages**," a company spokesperson told TMZ. Instead, those words came from **a note Robinson
> left behind** and **conversations his roommate later relayed to a friend**.

The excerpt also records that Discord *"dug into Robinson's activity after his arrest,"* that
*"investigators did find an account linked to him,"* but that the company *"insists it found no
evidence that Robinson plotted violence or shared anything related to the killing on its
servers."*

## The conflict this creates

The probable-cause affidavit describes the roommate saying Robinson **"made a joke on discord,"**
and investigators then **photographing Discord messages off the roommate's screen** &mdash; see
[The Affidavit: Photographing a Roommate's Screen](./pca-affidavit-roommate-screen-photos). The
affidavit attributes that content to Discord.

Discord says the planning content was not on Discord.

One of two things follows. Either the affidavit mislabeled the platform &mdash; the messages were
SMS, which is what the
[search warrant text-message exhibits](./warrant-twiggs-texts-page5) actually show &mdash; or
Discord's server-side review missed material sitting in its own systems. Analyst
**Chris Friel** has argued the mislabeling is the more likely of the two, and
that it matters because it means the "Discord planning" story was never sourced to Discord at all.

## Why the distinction is load-bearing

| If the planning content is | Then authentication depends on |
|---|---|
| Discord | Server-side logs, message IDs, account tokens &mdash; verifiable by the company |
| SMS on a roommate's phone | A forensic extraction of that phone &mdash; and the state has said it does not intend to offer forensic extractions |

That second row is not hypothetical. See
[The State Says It Will Not Offer Forensic Extractions](./state-wont-offer-forensic-extractions).

Discord's statement pushes the entire evidentiary weight from a company that keeps logs onto a
private phone whose extraction the prosecution has said it will not put in.
