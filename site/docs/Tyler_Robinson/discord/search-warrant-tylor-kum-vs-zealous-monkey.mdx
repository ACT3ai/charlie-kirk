This capture is the single most important document image in the Discord story, because it puts
**both** conflicting attributions on the same warrant, four pages apart.

## Page 5 &mdash; the prose

> "On 9/12/2025, at approximately **0100 hours**, FBI Special Agent **Lange** conducted an
> interview with the boyfriend of Tyler Robinson at the **Washington County Sheriff's Office**."
>
> "Lance showed FBI Special Agent Lange additional messages on his cellphone from a social media
> app called **'Discord'**. ... In one of the messages that Lance showed to investigators, the user
> **'tylor/kum'** posted the following message in a group chat: 'Hey guys, I have bad news for you
> all. It was me at UVU yesterday. Im sorry for all of this. Im surrendenng through a sheriff
> friend in a few moments. Thanks for all the good times and laughs, you've all been so amazing.
> Thank you all for everything'."

Note the capitalisation: **"Im sorry," "Im surrendering," "Thanks"** &mdash; all capitalised in the
warrant's retyping, all lowercase in the screenshot. That is the transcription difference analysed
on [Two Different Surrender Messages](./two-surrender-messages).

## Page 7 &mdash; the exhibit

Four pages later the same warrant reproduces the screenshot:

> September 11, 2025 &middot; **zealous_monkey_55095** &middot; 9/11/25, 8:57 PM
>
> "Hey guys, I have bad news for you all. it was me at UVU yesterday. im sorry for all of this. im
> surrendering through a sheriff friend in a few moments. thanks for all the good times and laughs,
> you've all been so amazing. thank you all for everything"

With the source line beneath it:

> `https://www.kenklippenstein.com/p/exclusive-leaked-messages-from-charlie`
>
> "Screenshot of leaked Discord message from Tyler Robinson (username: **zealous_monkey_55095**,
> **other usernames redacted**)"

## Three findings from one page

**1. The warrant's exhibit is a journalist's publication.** The sworn affidavit does not attach a
Discord export or a law-enforcement capture. It attaches, and cites by URL, **Ken Klippenstein's
Substack post**. The state's document is sourced to the press, and the press was sourced to an
anonymous member of the group. See
[How the Confession Screenshot Reached the Public](./confession-sourcing-klippenstein).

**2. The two usernames sit four pages apart, unexplained.** *tylor/kum* in the prose,
*zealous_monkey_55095* in the exhibit, no reconciliation anywhere in the document. See
[Two Usernames for One Post](./zealous-monkey-vs-tylor-ikum).

**3. "Other usernames redacted."** Every other participant in that group chat has been removed
from the public record. That redaction is why the roster of who was actually in the server has to
be reconstructed from a leaker's archive rather than read off the warrant &mdash; see
[Tyler Robinson's Discord Friends and Handles](./discord_friends).

## The timestamp, again

The exhibit reads **8:57 PM**. Charging documents and reporting say **7:57 PM MDT**. The warrant
reproduces the 8:57 image without remarking on the difference.
