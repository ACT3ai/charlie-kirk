This is the longest published version, and the one that carries the material everything else quotes
from: the motive line, the planning window, and the engraved bullets.

## The framing line

> "After reading the note, the roommate responded, **'What?????????????? You're joking, right????'**"

## The transcript

**Robinson:** "I am still ok my love, but am stuck in orem for a little while longer yet.
Shouldn't be long until I can come home, but I gotta grab my rifle still. To be honest I had hoped
to keep this secret till I died of old age. I am sorry to involve you."

**Roommate:** "you weren't the one who did it right????"

**Robinson:** "I am, I'm sorry"

**Roommate:** "I thought they caught the person?"

**Robinson:** "no, they grabbed some crazy old dude, then interrogated someone in similar clothing.
I had planned to grab my rifle from my drop point shortly after, but most of that side of town got
locked down. Its quiet, almost enough to get out, but theres one vehicle lingering."

**Roommate:** "Why?" &mdash; **Robinson:** "Why did I do it?" &mdash; **Roommate:** "Yeah"

**Robinson:** "**I had enough of his hatred. Some hate can't be negotiated out.** If I am able to
grab my rifle unseen, I will have left no evidence. Going to attempt to retrieve it again,
hopefully they have moved on. I haven't seen anything about them finding it."

**Roommate:** "How long have you been planning this?"

**Robinson:** "**a bit over a week I believe.** I can get close to it but there is a squad car
parked right by it. I think they already swept that spot, but I don't wanna chance it"

**Robinson:** "I'm wishing I had circled back and grabbed it as soon as I got to my vehicle. ...
I'm worried what my old man would do if I didn't bring back **grandpas rifle** ... idek if it had a
serial number, but it wouldn't trace to me. I worry about prints I had to leave it in a bush where
I changed outfits ... I might have to abandon it and hope they don't find prints. how the fuck will
I explain losing it to my old man..."

**Robinson:** "only thing I left was the rifle **wrapped in a towel**...."

**Robinson:** "remember how I was **engraving bullets**? The fuckin messages are mostly a big meme,
if I see 'notices bulge uwu' on fox new I might have a stroke"

**Robinson:** "alright im gonna have to leave it, that really fucking sucks. ..."

## The four things this exchange is quoted for

**The motive.** *"I had enough of his hatred. Some hate can't be negotiated out."* This single
sentence is the entire stated motive in the public record.

**The planning window.** *"a bit over a week."* Set that against Discord's statement that it found
[no evidence of planning on its platform](./discord-says-no-plotting) &mdash; a week of planning
left no trace where the planning supposedly happened.

**The engraved bullets.** *"The fuckin messages are mostly a big meme."* Note that the roommate
has to be **reminded** of the engravings &mdash; *"remember how I was engraving bullets?"* &mdash;
which is one of the oddities **Chris Friel** builds on: a partner who forgot
something that distinctive, days later. See
[The Twiggs Text Exchange and the Fabrication Argument](./friel-twiggs-text-exchange).

**The double astonishment.** The roommate reads a note confessing to the killing, replies
*"You're joking, right????"*, and then still asks *"you weren't the one who did it right????"*
Two separate expressions of disbelief about a fact he has just read.

## What is not here

No plan, no target research, no reconnaissance, no date selection &mdash; nothing from the week
that was allegedly spent planning. The exchange begins after the act and is entirely about
retrieving the rifle.

## The Location Field Is Typed In. The IP Address Is Not.

On Discord, on Steam, and on every platform in this section, the country shown on a profile is
**a text box the user filled in**. Anyone can type Utah, or Egypt, or nothing at all, and change
it again in seconds. Whether it is shown publicly is a setting. It is not evidence of where a
person was sitting when they typed a message.

What is **not** typed in is the **IP address**. Every session, every login, every message send is
written to the platform's own logs with the address it arrived from, alongside device
fingerprints and session timing. Discord holds that record for the servers named in the Utah State
Bureau of Investigation's warrant &mdash; **Craftopia**, **Gamin** and **The Corruption** &mdash;
and for the account that posted the confession.

That is the question this section keeps returning to: **were one or more of the people in these
servers outside the United States?** The FBI reportedly examined twenty or more people in
Robinson's Discord circle and charged none of them, and nothing in the public record states where
any of them actually were. Self-reported locations circulated widely &mdash; see [the Egypt claim on a Steam profile](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/steam-ankha-egypt-profile) &mdash; and self-reported locations are worthless.

The answer exists. It is in Discord's subpoena return, in the login-IP history for
September 10&ndash;12, and in the traffic records that US intelligence and federal investigators
can obtain and the public cannot. **We want that answer found and published.** Until it is, every
claim of foreign involvement in these chats &mdash; and every denial &mdash; rests on a field
somebody typed.

We name no one and we accuse no one. We are asking for the record that would settle it.

## The Steam Friend List May Matter More

Discord is where the confession appeared. **Steam is where the friendships actually lived.**
A server can hold hundreds of people who have never spoken to each other; a **friend list is
chosen one person at a time**, and the people on it are the ones you spend hundreds of hours in
voice chat with, night after night, for years. If anyone in Robinson's online life knew him well
enough to matter, they are likelier to be on that list than on a server roster.

The two circles overlap directly: visitors to the Steam profile posted their **Discord handles**
on its public comment wall. And Steam stores exactly what Discord stores &mdash; **the IP address
each session came from** &mdash; no matter what country a profile claims.
