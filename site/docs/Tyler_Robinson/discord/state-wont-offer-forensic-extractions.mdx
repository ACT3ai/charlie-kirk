One sentence in a filing about scheduling, and it changes what the message evidence can be.

## The quote

> "With the exception of a photograph of a burnt note, doorbell surveillance from a single
> property, a DNA report regarding items found in (Robinson's) apartment, and a toolmarks report,
> each of the witnesses and exhibits has been known to the defense for at least six months before
> the preliminary hearing," prosecutors argue. "Most of the evidence to be presented at the
> preliminary hearing, including the DNA report regarding the rifle found at the scene, was
> provided to the defense in October of 2025. **The state does not intend to offer evidence
> obtained through forensic extractions of electronics seized in this case**."

## Why that last sentence matters

A forensic extraction &mdash; a Cellebrite-style image of a phone &mdash; is the only thing that
can turn a **photograph of a screen** into **authenticated data**. It produces message IDs,
database timestamps, deletion records, and a hash that proves the file was not altered afterwards.

Without it, the message evidence at the preliminary hearing rests on:

* photographs taken of [a roommate's screen](./pca-affidavit-roommate-screen-photos),
* a screenshot [republished from a Substack post](./confession-sourcing-klippenstein), and
* [Lance Twiggs' testimony](./twiggs-immunity-and-statements) about what he was shown.

That is witness recollection and pictures, not device data.

## Read it alongside Discord's position

Discord says the planning messages
[were not Discord messages](./discord-denies-planning-messages) &mdash; which pushes
authentication onto the phones. The state then says it will not offer the phone extractions.

The two statements together close both doors:

| Authentication route | Status |
|---|---|
| Discord server-side logs | Company says the content was not on its platform; account removed |
| Forensic phone extraction | State says it does not intend to offer it |
| Photograph of a witness's screen | This is what remains |

## The careful caveat

This is a filing about a **preliminary hearing**, which has a low evidentiary bar and is not the
trial. The state is entitled to hold evidence back at that stage and produce it later. Nothing
here proves extractions do not exist.

But the preliminary hearing is where probable cause is tested, and the state has said the device
data will not be there. Readers should verify the current docket, since positions in an active
case change.
