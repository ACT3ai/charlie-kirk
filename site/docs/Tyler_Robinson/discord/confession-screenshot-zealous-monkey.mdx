This is the image the entire Discord story rests on. It is a dark-mode chat capture covering two
days in one friend-group server. Nothing in it is a native export &mdash; it is a picture of a
screen, and the red rectangle around the timestamp was drawn on afterwards by whoever circulated
it. We reproduce the text exactly as it reads.

## What the screenshot says, line by line

**September 10, 2025**

| Timestamp on the image | Message |
|---|---|
| 9/10/25, 3:17 PM | Charlie Kirk got shot |
| 9/10/25, 3:44 PM | Dead |
| 9/10/25, 4:40 PM | I just saw the video holy shit |
| 9/10/25, 4:40 PM | Rip I guess |
| 9/10/25, 4:40 PM | Bro didn't deserve to go out like that sad |

Those five lines carry **no username** in the crop. They read as an ordinary group chat reacting
to breaking news, the same way millions of chats did that afternoon.

**September 11, 2025**

Below a second date divider the crop shows a blue Discord avatar, the username
**zealous_monkey_55095** in purple, and the timestamp **9/11/25, 8:57 PM** boxed in red. The
message:

> Hey guys, I have bad news for you all. it was me at UVU yesterday. im sorry for all of this. im
> surrendering through a sheriff friend in a few moments. thanks for all the good times and laughs,
> you've all been so amazing. thank you all for everything

## The timestamp is the whole argument

The image reads **8:57 PM**. Court filings and nearly all reporting cite the message at
**7:57 PM MDT**. That is a one-hour offset, which is what a screenshot taken on a device in a
different timezone would produce &mdash; but it also means the picture and the paperwork do not
agree on their face, and neither figure has been reconciled in open court.

Either figure is a problem for the official account, because Bates-stamped discovery places
Robinson **Mirandized at 6:25 PM on September 11**. See
[Bates 003996-R2 &mdash; Miranda at 6:25 vs. the Discord clock](./bates-003996-miranda-vs-discord).

## Five words that read oddly

The message says "all" **five times** in three sentences &mdash; *bad news for you all*, *sorry for
all of this*, *thanks for all the good times*, *you've all been so amazing*, *thank you all for
everything*. Analyst **Chris Friel** has flagged this as a post written to an
audience rather than to friends. That is a stylistic observation, not proof of anything.

It also says **"surrendering,"** while the charging document quotes Robinson as saying he was
**"gonna turn myself in willingly."** Those are two different sentences describing the same act.
See [Two Different Surrender Messages](./two-surrender-messages).

## What the screenshot cannot tell you

* Which server it was posted in, and who else was in it.
* Whether the account was in Robinson's hands at the moment of posting.
* Whether the five earlier messages were sent by the same person as the confession.
* What was above, below, or between the messages &mdash; the crop hides all of it.

Discord itself has since said the messages that reporting described as planning material
**were not Discord messages at all**. See
[Discord Says It Found No Plotting](./discord-says-no-plotting) and
[Discord Denies the Planning Messages Came From Its Platform](./discord-denies-planning-messages).

We do not claim the screenshot was forged. We document that it is a photograph of a screen whose
clock, authorship, and context are all still open.
