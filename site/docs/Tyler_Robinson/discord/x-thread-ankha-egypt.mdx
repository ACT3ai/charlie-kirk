This is the X post as it circulated, rather than the raw profile. It matters as a record of **how
a gaming profile field became a foreign-intelligence claim in one step**.

## The post

> "ALERT: Somebody Named **Ankha** Is Also In Tyler Robinson's Steam Group. Notably They Claim To
> be From Egypt. Hmm...
>
> Where else have we heared Egypt before? Oh Yeah That's right, @RealCandaceO Exposed There Were
> **Egyptian Government Planes and Egyptian Military Contractor's In Provo, UT On Sept 10th**. Same
> Day Charlie Kirk was assassinated.
>
> @FBIDirectorKash Are you paying attention yet?"

Embedded beneath is the Steam profile itself, showing **Egypt**, *"Left 4 Dead 2 Death Runner AKA
Sniper GOD"*, and **"My Discord: Ankha_Kitty."**

## The reasoning chain, and where it breaks

1. A member of the Steam group lists **Egypt**. &mdash; True, as a self-entered field.
2. Reporting raised questions about **Egyptian aircraft in Provo on September 10**. &mdash; A
   separate claim, attributed to [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens); see
   [Planes](/Planes/overview).
3. Therefore the two are connected. &mdash; **This does not follow.**

Step 3 has no supporting evidence. A country field on a gaming profile is unverifiable, commonly
fictional, and here almost certainly a reference to the *Animal Crossing* character Ankha, who is
Egyptian-themed. See [The Ankha Profile and the Egypt Claim](./steam-ankha-egypt-profile) for the
raw profile and the ordinary reading.

## Why we publish it anyway

Two reasons.

**It is part of the public record of this investigation.** Thousands of people saw it. Documenting
it with the counter-explanation attached is more useful than pretending it did not circulate.

**It illustrates a real failure mode.** After September 10 the entire Steam and Discord circle
around Robinson was scraped by strangers, and ordinary gaming detritus &mdash; a meme name, a
country field, a joke about a sniper &mdash; was read as tradecraft. The people in that circle were
mostly just gamers; the leaker interviewed in the
[Discord archive leak](./discord_friends) said as much, describing their connection as tenuous and
complaining about harassment from journalists and random people online.

We name no living person as a suspect on the basis of a Steam field, and neither should anyone else.
