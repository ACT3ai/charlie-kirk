A single line, dropped almost as an aside in a TMZ item, and it is one of the more consequential
facts in the whole Discord story.

## The line

> "BTW ... Discord says it has removed Robinson's account."

The same capture notes, in the surrounding copy, that *"Utah authorities have said that as of this
point, they believe Robinson acted alone, but the investigation is ongoing."*

## Why an aside matters

Every open question about the confession runs through one place: **the account itself**.

* Was the account logged in at 7:57 PM &mdash; or 8:57 PM &mdash; on September 11?
* From what IP, and on what device?
* Was the message edited after posting? Discord keeps an edit flag.
* What message ID did it carry, and what sat immediately before and after it?

All of that is server-side metadata. None of it is in a photograph of somebody else's screen. And
an account that has been removed cannot be inspected by a defense expert, a journalist, or the
public.

## What removal does and does not mean

Removal is normal platform practice after a violent crime, and it is not evidence of bad faith.
Discord may well retain the underlying records internally under legal hold even after the account
is no longer visible.

But **retention is not disclosure**. Nothing in the public record shows those server-side records
being produced, and Discord's own public position is that it
[found no planning content](./discord-says-no-plotting) and that the messages in question
[were not Discord messages](./discord-denies-planning-messages).

So the platform reviewed the account, reported a negative finding, removed the account, and the
only artifact left in public is a cropped photograph of a screen.

## Research needs

* The preservation letter and its date &mdash; was the account preserved before removal?
* Any subpoena return from Discord, and whether it was produced to the defense.
* Login IP records for the account across September 10&ndash;12, 2025.
