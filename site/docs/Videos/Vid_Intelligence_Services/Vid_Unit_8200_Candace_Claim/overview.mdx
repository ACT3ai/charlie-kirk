## About This Cluster

{/* CK_PROSE_START */}

This cluster holds footage in which an **anonymous speaker names a foreign intelligence
service** in connection with a claimed plot against [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens),
the most prominent independent voice still pressing questions about Charlie Kirk's death.
It currently holds a single clip.

The organization named in that clip is **[Unit 8200](/Israel/unit-8200)**, the signals
intelligence and cyber unit of the Israel Defense Forces. The claim is that an 8200 liaison
commissioned the attack and that a French service was tasked to carry it out. That claim is
**anonymous and uncorroborated**, and the clip's own page sets out in detail why the
investigation does not find it credible — starting with the fact that 8200 is a SIGINT
organization and not a direct-action one.

Clips are grouped here by concept rather than by date or by who posted them. Claims spoken
inside the videos are the speakers' claims, reported with attribution rather than adopted
as conclusions. This cluster in particular exists to hold a claim **at arm's length** —
recording that it circulated, and recording what is wrong with it, is the point of filing
it rather than ignoring it.
