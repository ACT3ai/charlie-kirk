## What This Video Is

A **2 minute 56 second** audio-led recording posted to X on **August 20, 2026** by
[@AMERICANFIRSTQ_](https://x.com/AMERICANFIRSTQ_/status/2090466320869458085), an account
with roughly 5,000 followers. The posting caption reads:

> "Alleged French whistleblower released a video regarding Candace Owen's' paid
> assassination. They are desperate to shut her up. Pray."

The account describes the speaker as an "alleged French whistleblower" and does not
identify him, does not say how the recording reached the account, and does not link to any
original source for it. The post carried 38 likes, 20 reposts and 869 views at the time it
was archived here.

## What The Speaker Claims

The speaker introduces himself as a **colonel in the action division of the DGSE** —
France's foreign intelligence service — with twenty-one years running what he calls black
contracts in Africa and the Middle East, "and yes, sometimes on your soil." He says he is
breaking every oath he took.

His central allegation is a tasking cable:

> "Two days ago, a priority cable landed on my desk... Target Candace Owens. Client, unit
> 8200 liaison embedded inside the Élysée, payment routed to a share in Cyprus we have
> used since the rainbow royal days."

**This is the 8200 reference.** [Unit 8200](/Israel/unit-8200) is the signals intelligence
and cyber unit of the Israel Defense Forces — Israel's counterpart to the NSA. The speaker
alleges that a liaison from that unit, working inside the French presidential palace, is
the paying client.

He then alleges:

* A directive to kill [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) **during a live podcast**, quoting it as "eliminate the subject during live podcast, maximum visceral impact."
* That the point is not the target but the audience — to "break the Americans who still speak against Israel" and make others "too afraid to open their mouths."
* That the attack would be **framed as a jihadist attack** to advance a geopolitical agenda.
* That a team is already in the country, including blade specialists from Corsica, a woman he links to "the job on that Saudi journalist in Istanbul," and a spotter he says "was in the crowd in Utah."
* That the team holds a studio floor plan and the security shift change, and that two people in her own circle "accepted crypto for information two weeks ago."
* That she should trust no American agency offering protective custody because "more than half of them are penetrated," and that the only safe move is to disappear for ninety days.

The reference to a spotter "in the crowd in Utah" is the only line that touches the
Charlie Kirk case directly. It is asserted in passing, with no detail, no name, and no
supporting material.

## Assessment

The site records this because it was posted and because it names a specific service. It
does not find it credible, and the reasons are worth stating plainly rather than leaving
to the reader:

**The organization named does not match the act alleged.** Unit 8200 is a SIGINT and cyber
organization. Direct action abroad, when Israel is alleged to conduct it, is attributed to
Mossad. Assigning a contract killing to 8200 is a category error of the kind someone
writing from headlines makes and someone inside a service does not.

**The operational security is incoherent.** A serving colonel in an action division would
be describing, on an unencrypted recording routed to an anonymous account, a live tasking
that would identify him within hours. He says as much himself — "when they find me" — which
functions as narrative justification rather than as tradecraft.

**The transcript degrades under examination.** Proper names do not resolve. Phrases such as
"rainbow royal days," "Opel a Champoignard," and "for populators" are not recognizable
French service terminology, and several sentences lose grammatical coherence in ways that
are more consistent with synthetic generation or a scripted performance than with a
practitioner speaking about his own work. The transcription below is machine-produced, so
some of that is transcription error — but the structural incoherence survives it.

**The content is optimized for virality, not for disclosure.** The specific detail is
concentrated in the parts that produce dread — the on-air method, the intestines, the chat
scrolling — and thins out precisely where a real disclosure would be checkable: no cable
reference, no date on the Cyprus account, no name for the liaison, no unit within 8200.

**Nothing here is corroborated.** No document, no named source, no second account, no
reporting. Synthetic-voice fabrication is inexpensive in 2026 and manufactured "insider"
testimony now circulates within hours of any high-attention story.

What the video **does** establish is that a claim of this shape is circulating, attached to
Candace Owens, and reaching real audiences. That is a fact about the information
environment around this investigation, and it is the fact this page records.
