## What This Video Is

A 2:51 statement delivered straight to camera by [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent), the former director of the National Counterterrorism Center. It was published on his own X account on August 19, 2026 and circulated the same day by [@Trillion0x](https://x.com/Trillion0x/status/2089892369709846599) under the headline "Joe Kent explains the 'Foreign Nexus' involvement in the Charlie Kirk assassination."

This is not commentary, and it is not a clip pulled from a longer interview. It is a named former official, on the record, deliberately describing what his own agency was doing on this case and what happened when it tried to keep doing it. That makes it a primary source rather than analysis of one.

It should be read alongside the earlier [Julian Dorey podcast clip](/Videos/Vid_Intelligence_Services/Vid_Joe_Kent_Interview/Vid_ArtifexMemor_Video_Juliandorey_Julian_2079560), where Kent first said he had been "a little bit stonewalled." This statement is more specific: it names the mechanism, the access that was withdrawn, and the handover that followed.

## What Is Said

The transcript below is taken from the audio of this clip.

**What NCTC is and why it was there.** Kent opens by describing the standing arrangement rather than anything special to this case:

> "NCTC analysts are a part of every joint terrorism task force throughout the entire country. The JTTFs are typically led by the FBI. NCTC's role within the JTTF is to determine if there is any foreign nexus. So that would be a foreign country, a foreign government, a foreign individual, a foreign company."

**Who asked for the work.** He is explicit that NCTC was not freelancing:

> "NCTC analysts were operating at the request of the FBI to determine if there was any kind of foreign connection or foreign nexus to the assassination of Charlie Kirk."

**Why NCTC in particular.** He explains the agency's unusual visibility, and ties it back to why it exists at all:

> "It can see all the operational traffic, all the raw reporting coming from the forward facing intelligence community, the CIA, the DIA, the NSA, et cetera, but it can also see the raw operational reporting coming from the FBI and other federal law enforcement agencies... It has this authority in order to connect the dots. NCTC was formed after 9/11 because of the failures of the CIA and FBI and their inability to communicate prior to 9/11."

**The shutdown.** This is the core of the clip, and it goes further than the Dorey account did — it describes a withdrawal of access, not just an instruction to stop:

> "In very short order, we were told that our services were no longer required, that we no longer had access to the repository, the FBI had, where all the information on the investigation was being kept, and then the FBI said shortly thereafter, they were going to hand over the investigation to the Utah law enforcement."

**His objection.** Kent states plainly that he pushed back and that his position has not changed:

> "I protested. I disagreed. I thought that we still had a lot more work to do. I still believe that to this day that we had more leads to run down to determine if there was any kind of foreign connection."

**The hedge he insists on.** He is careful, and the care is part of why the statement carries weight:

> "I'm not saying there definitively was. I am only saying that we still had more work to do to ensure that we actually did our due diligence to ensure there was no foreign nexus to Charlie Kirk's assassination."

**His stated reason for doubting the lone-gunman account.** Kent points to material posted before the killing:

> "There was people that were posting online ahead of Charlie's assassination, that he was going to be killed that day in Utah. So that strongly indicates that there were more people involved in the plot to kill Charlie Kirk."

That is Kent's characterisation of online posts and his inference from them. This site does not identify any person as having had advance knowledge of anything, and nothing on this page should be read as doing so.

**The close.** He ends on Charlie Kirk's own words:

> "I think it's critical that we continue to ask who killed Charlie Kirk, how and why. And I'll just close with a Charlie Kirk quote that it is critical that we continue to ask questions because it is the only thing that keeps us free."

## What The Footage Shows

A single fixed vertical shot of Kent speaking to camera, unedited, with no graphics, no B-roll and no inserted footage. There is nothing to analyse in the pictures. The entire value of the clip is in the audio and in the fact that a named former director chose to put it on the record himself.

## What It Does And Does Not Establish

It establishes three specific, checkable claims about process: that NCTC was working the foreign-nexus question **at the FBI's own request**; that NCTC's access to the FBI's investigation repository was **withdrawn**; and that the investigation was then **handed to Utah law enforcement** over Kent's stated objection. Each of those is answerable by records — who made the decision, on what date, and under what authority.

It does not establish foreign involvement, and Kent goes out of his way to say so twice. What he describes is an **investigative** gap: leads that existed and were not run. That distinction is the whole of the disclosure argument built on it, and it is why this clip sits with the [halt-of-review](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC) material rather than with the theories about who was behind the killing.

The reason it matters more than an ordinary "questions remain" statement is the source. Kent is not a commentator reading the case from outside. He is describing a decision he was personally subject to, in an agency created specifically so that this kind of dot-connecting would not be missed again.
