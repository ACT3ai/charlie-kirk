## What This Video Shows

A 38-second slow-motion analysis clip of the moment Charlie Kirk collapses at UVU. In the slowed footage you can see small dark plastic shards falling from underneath Charlie's shirt as he collapses — the debris the poster identifies as shrapnel from the explosion of the [Rode wireless microphone transmitter](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) he wore on his belt on his left side. The fragments drop from his left side, near the belt line, in line with the neck wound.

The poster, @ProjectConstitu, argues frame by frame that this shows "an explosive force radiating upward from the device, not an external projectile entering from a rooftop" — in their words, "THIS WAS NOT A GUNSHOT" but "a PETN explosion — a controlled detonation of an explosive device disguised as audio equipment." They conclude the rooftop shooter was a distraction and that the real weapon was under Charlie's shirt, planted by someone with close access. These are the poster's claims, reported here with attribution; the official account remains a rifle shot from a rooftop.

The falling black shards seen here match the physical debris documented elsewhere in the investigation — see [Explosive Residue](/Mic/explosive-residue) and the wider [mic explosion video analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis).
