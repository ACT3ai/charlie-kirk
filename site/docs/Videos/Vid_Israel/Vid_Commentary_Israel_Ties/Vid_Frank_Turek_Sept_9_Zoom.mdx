## What This Video Is

A 53-second podcast excerpt. [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek) — Christian apologist, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) mentor, and one of the men in the SUV that carried Kirk to the hospital on September 10, 2025 — describes a meeting he says he personally attended the previous day.

He is speaking to a host he addresses only as "Jack." This site has not established which Jack, and does not name one.

It is a first-person eyewitness account, which is rare in this section. Most Israel-related video here is commentary about other people's claims. This is a man saying *I was in the room.*

## What Is Said

Transcribed in full from the clip:

> "We're out on a walk next day, September 9th. Charlie calls a meeting, a scheduled Zoom meeting, with two officials in Israel, one Jewish Defender of Israel in the United States. I'm in the room, with Charlie, Blake's in the room, Mikey's in and out. What do you think we're talking about, Jack? I know. We are talking about how the propaganda war against Israel is gaining ground, even though it's all lies and deception, and Charlie is asking these guys, how can I better defend the truth regarding Israel? Regarding this? You guys help me with that. That's right. I was in this meeting. It lasted 50 minutes. This idea that Charlie was giving up on Israel is false."

## What The Clip Establishes

Four checkable specifics, all stated by Turek as things he witnessed rather than heard about:

* **The date** — September 9, 2025, the day before the assassination.
* **The duration** — 50 minutes.
* **Who was on the call** — two officials *in Israel*, plus one person he describes only as "a Jewish Defender of Israel in the United States."
* **Who was in the room** — Turek, Kirk, "Blake" ([Blake Neff](/People/blake-neff)) and "Mikey" ([Mikey McCoy](/People/mikey-mccoy)) in and out.

What it does **not** establish is any name for the two Israeli officials, any ministry or office, or who arranged the call. Turek supplies a participant count, not a participant list.

## Why It Is Contested

The closing sentence is the argument: *"This idea that Charlie was giving up on Israel is false."*

That is a direct rebuttal to [the September 8 donor group chat](/Messages/sept-8-donor-group-chat) — the best-authenticated message set in the entire investigation, published as screenshots and confirmed genuine by TPUSA spokesman [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet) — in which Kirk wrote in his own words that donor pressure was "leaving me no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause."

Turek is describing a meeting; he is not producing a document. No recording, calendar entry, or Zoom log for the September 9 call has been published. The clip is one witness's characterization of a private meeting's purpose, offered inside a live dispute about what Kirk had decided. It is presented here as one side of that dispute, and it is a well-sourced side: Turek is the same witness who says Kirk texted him ["I know they want me dead"](/Messages/frank-turek-messages).

Nothing in this clip establishes wrongdoing by any person, organization, or state.
