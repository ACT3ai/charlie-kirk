## About This Cluster

{/* CK_PROSE_START */}

{/* CK_PROSE_BASELINE */}

This section collects the video evidence the investigation has filed under **Spy Plane Footage** — 5 clips, all of them about one airframe: **[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)**, a Bombardier Global 6500 flying as AXLE10, and what it did over Orem and Provo on September 10, 2025. Every entry links to its own page, where the footage plays next to a write-up of what it shows, the source it came from, and what it does and does not establish.

Read in order, the clips build outward from the raw record. Two of them are the same screen recording held twice — the **[method walkthrough](/Videos/Vid_Aircraft_Flight_Evidence/Vid_Spy_Plane_Footage/Vid_Spy_Plane_Analysis_oW3EvAi)** and **[Blake Bednarz's copy of it](/Videos/Vid_Aircraft_Flight_Evidence/Vid_Spy_Plane_Footage/Vid_Blake_Bednarz_Original_Post_1978553)** — and they matter because they teach the reader to pull the archived Flightradar24 track themselves, export it to Google Earth, and convert the UTC timestamps without taking anyone's word for the arithmetic. The **[full HADES walkthrough](/Videos/Vid_Aircraft_Flight_Evidence/Vid_Spy_Plane_Footage/Vid_Hades_Spy_Plane_86EdBzo)** is the longest piece here and lays out both low passes end to end: the outbound leg roughly three hours before the shooting, a stated 600 feet above ground at 118 knots over the lake beside UVU, and a second pass at 12:48 Mountain. **[Oliver Janich](/Videos/Vid_Aircraft_Flight_Evidence/Vid_Spy_Plane_Footage/Vid_Oliver_Janich_Mysterious_Spy_1969702)** leaves the flight track altogether and follows the registration to LASAI Aviation II LLC and to Leidos, a corporate chain that is a matter of public record.

The fifth clip runs the other way. **[The chatbot reply](/Videos/Vid_Aircraft_Flight_Evidence/Vid_Spy_Plane_Footage/Vid_Charo_BlakeBednarz_Grok_Attached_1978846)** is an exculpatory answer asserting the low passes were routine training, filed here because a reader should see the shape of that argument and the fact that nothing behind it resolves to a document.

What holds this cluster together is the ADS-B and Flightradar24 record, which anyone can re-pull. What none of these clips shows is a payload, a tasking order, or a person aboard. Claims spoken inside the videos are the speakers' claims, reported with attribution rather than adopted as conclusions.
