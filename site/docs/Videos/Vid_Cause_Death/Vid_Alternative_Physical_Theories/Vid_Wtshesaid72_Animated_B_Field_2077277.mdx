## What This Video Is

The overlays in the video itself do not use that language; they describe pressure, compression and expansion - a blast-wave reading of the same frames. The observations the overlays point to are real things visible in the frames: hair moves, the shirt moves, the head goes back. The question is what accounts for them.

The government's .30-06 account would attribute these movements to a passing round's pressure field. Citizen investigators read them instead as the push of a blast at the chest and neck: the shirt expands outward, spherically, before any wound appears, which is what the [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) and the [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena) set out in detail. At this frame rate and compression the intermediate stages are not fully resolved, so the labelled phases are the poster's annotation of the motion rather than a measurement of it.

The subject of the footage is Charlie Kirk at the moment he was fatally wounded, slowed and looped. It is included in this archive as evidence and it is handled here as such. The two audience members partly visible behind him are members of the public and nothing is asserted about them.
