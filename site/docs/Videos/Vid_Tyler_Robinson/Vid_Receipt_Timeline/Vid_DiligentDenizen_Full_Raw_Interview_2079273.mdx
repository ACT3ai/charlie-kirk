## What This Video Shows

A 3 minute 46 second on-location interview recorded inside Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah, in which the citizen journalist questions a restaurant employee about the customer he served on the night of September 10, 2025. The employee's face was cropped out and his name withheld at his request, so the clip is carried by the audio. He states he has worked there about a year and a half, that the customer sat at the bar close to the end and ordered a steak and a couple of beers, that he was not the waiter taking the order but had several interactions helping out, and that he personally ran the customer's card at the 9:47 PM checkout near the 10 PM closing. Asked how certain he is that the customer was Tyler Robinson, he answers 100 percent, saying the voice was unforgettable and that he recognized it the next day when a video of Robinson receiving his college acceptance letter was released. He says his boss reported the sighting to the FBI, that agents took the staff's contact information and never followed up, and that neither the prosecution nor the defense has ever contacted him. This description is drawn from the recorded audio and the poster's stated framing; no independent visual analysis of the footage was performed.

## The Part That Is Not About The Identification

The last third of the clip is what citizen investigators weigh most heavily, and it makes no claim

He says his boss contacted the [FBI](/FBI/overview), that agents **"got our contact information,

once."** Asked whether the prosecution ever approached him to testify, and then whether the defense
did, his answer covers both: **"No one's reached out regarding anything to do with Tyler
Robinson."** He says that silence is why he sent the tip to
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)' team himself - *"It just didn't sit right with me."*

These are one witness's claims, recorded by a citizen journalist, and neither the FBI nor either
party in the case has publicly responded to them. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted, and
is presumed innocent.** A single staff identification from a brief interaction is not proof of
anything on its own - which is precisely why the un-pulled merchant record is the part that
matters.
