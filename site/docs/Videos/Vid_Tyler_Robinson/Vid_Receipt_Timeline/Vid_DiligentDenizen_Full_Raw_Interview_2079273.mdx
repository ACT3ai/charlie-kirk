A 3 minute 46 second on-location interview recorded inside Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah, in which the citizen journalist questions a restaurant employee about the customer he served on the night of September 10, 2025. The employee's face was cropped out and his name withheld at his request, so the clip is carried by the audio. He states he has worked there about a year and a half, that the customer sat at the bar close to the end and ordered a steak and a couple of beers, that he was not the waiter taking the order but had several interactions helping out, and that he personally ran the customer's card at the 9:47 PM checkout near the 10 PM closing.
