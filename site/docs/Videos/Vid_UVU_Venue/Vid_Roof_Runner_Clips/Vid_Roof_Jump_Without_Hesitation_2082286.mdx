Two of the claims inside that passage are checkable against the record rather than against the video, and
they are the load-bearing ones:

* **That the roof is 16–20 feet.** The height is asserted, not measured, in the post. The
  [roof jumping timeline](/Timeline/roof-jumping-timeline) and the [UVU venue](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU)
  page carry what is documented about the Losee Center elevation.
* **That the prosecution never indicated the accused had scoped the corner or evaluated it as an egress
  point.** This is an argument from *absence in the preliminary hearing record* — see
  [court and legal proceedings](/court/overview). An absence in one hearing is not proof no such evidence
  exists; discovery in this case is contested and incomplete.

The rest is inference from movement quality: no pause, no hitch, clean weapon transfer, clean foot placement.
Reasonable people read body language in footage differently, and adrenaline produces committed movement in
untrained people too. The observation is worth recording; it is not self-proving.
