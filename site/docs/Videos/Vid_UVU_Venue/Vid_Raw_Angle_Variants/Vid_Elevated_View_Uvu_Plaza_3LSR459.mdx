## What This Video Is

Thirty-seven seconds of handheld phone video looking down across the outdoor plaza at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview) on the day Charlie Kirk was shot. It was posted to X on 30 July 2026 by the account [@CharlotteLee_88](https://x.com/CharlotteLee_88/status/2082652969468588522), which says it found the clip on Instagram, credits it to an account under the name Sierra Henderson, and states that it was taken at 11:24 a.m. on 10 September 2025 and uploaded on 11 September with very few views.

Two things about that framing should be held loosely. The 11:24 a.m. capture time is the finder's claim, repeated from the Instagram post; nothing visible in the frames independently fixes the clock, and this site has not verified it. The attribution to Sierra Henderson is likewise the finder's, not something established here. What can be stated plainly is that the footage is of the [UVU courtyard](/UVU/Courtyward) with the crowd assembled, and that if the stated time is right, it was filmed roughly an hour before the 12:23 p.m. shot.

## What The Footage Shows

The camera is above the crowd, not in it. In the opening seconds a hand and forearm cut across the right of frame, close to the lens — the phone is being held out over a railing, ledge or open window rather than raised overhead from ground level. That elevated vantage is the single most useful thing about the clip.

* **00:00 - 00:08** — A wide view down onto the plaza. The long reflecting pool runs diagonally through the shot. The crowd is packed on both sides of it and continues up the lawn and steps beyond, several hundred people at least, most apparently of student age.
