## What Is Said

The account, cleaned only of transcription noise and kept in his own sequence:

On why he made contact: *"I sent this video into the FBI … For tips. I thought I was doing a service and, you know, to Charlie and helping out."*

On how it escalated: *"So it started off where I got in contact, I was talking to someone at the local field office, there in my local state, and then it jumped up a few levels. Then I was talking to someone on like the national level."*

On the request: *"That person had called me back and they had said — because this was your friend — I think it's best that you erased the video from your phone, because it's just gonna give you PTSD."*

On the follow-up call: *"and then called at another time to ask if I had erased it … so I got a call back and was asked to confirm it was gone."*

On what he did instead: *"That was the first time that I felt like it was even right to post these videos … that's when I started sharing, posting and saving on much, many different hard drives, of many different places."*

And on the cost of it: *"It's been — it's been crazy. It's been rough."*

## How To Weigh It

This is one man's account of two telephone calls, given on camera. It is his word, and it is the only direct evidence of those calls that the investigation has seen. The FBI has not publicly confirmed, denied, or otherwise addressed it, and no call record, case number, or agent name has been produced on either side.

Ordinary explanations exist and should be stated plainly. A field contact suggesting that a witness need not keep distressing footage on a personal phone — particularly footage of someone the witness describes as a friend — is consistent with a welfare conversation, and a follow-up call is consistent with routine case note-keeping. Nothing on this page asserts that any individual acted unlawfully, improperly, or in bad faith, and no such finding has been made anywhere.

What is documented is narrower and is not in dispute: a witness has said this on camera, has repeated it, and describes responding by copying the footage widely rather than deleting it. Whether the advice was welfare or something else is exactly what a disclosure of the tip-line record would settle, and that is why this account keeps being raised.
