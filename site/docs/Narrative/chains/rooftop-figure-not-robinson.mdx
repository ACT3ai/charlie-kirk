# Chain 6: The Rooftop Figure Is Not Identified as Robinson

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**. Identification arguments here are made by named independent analysts from public imagery. Amateur photo-comparison is wrong often enough that this page states that first. No court has adopted these exclusions.
:::

## The conclusion this chain reaches

> **The FBI's 7:55 PM rooftop-and-stairwell release does not identify Tyler Robinson.** Clothing conflicts, a backpack that does not fit the claimed rifle, no visible firing event, uncalled roof witnesses, and a BOLO issued after the custody time at issue are five separate problems. Any one of them can be explained. The official identification has to survive all five.

The long-form objections sit at [Fed Claim 6](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson), [stairs guy identity gap](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap), and [roof figure not Tyler](/Roof/roof-figure-not-tyler).

## Link 1 — The imagery was released as a wanted poster after the custody time

The FBI put rooftop and stairwell stills out at **7:55 PM on September 11** as a person-of-interest BOLO. If [Chain 4](./miranda-discord-impossible) is dated correctly, Robinson had already invoked counsel. A BOLO is for a fugitive. See [enhanced suspect photos](/CoverUp/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos) and [rooftop video trimmed](/CoverUp/Rooftop_Video_Trimmed).

**Strength: this link is only as strong as the Miranda date.** If the tape is September 12, the BOLO is ordinary. If it is September 11, the public was shown a manhunt for a man already sitting down.

## Link 2 — Clothing does not match the documented Robinson

Analysts comparing the rooftop figure frame by frame against what Robinson is documented wearing report conflicts — [black clothing versus jeans](/Roof/black-clothing-vs-jeans), [Robinson clothing changes](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/robinson-clothing-changes), [Tyler clothing conflicts](/Tyler_Robinson/clothing-change-agent-david-hull). Multiple witnesses described a man in **all black**, which is not the state's documented outfit — [all-black construction worker](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/all-black-construction-worker), [eyewitness vs. the text confession](/Tyler_Robinson/eyewitness-black-clad-fugitive), [black-clad suspect dropped](/CoverUp/Black_Clad_Suspect_Dropped).

**Strength: weak individually** (compression and lighting fake clothing colour constantly) **and interesting in aggregate** (the all-black description kept recurring, and those witnesses have not been called).

## Link 3 — The backpack does not fit a 44-inch Mauser 98

A Mauser 98 is a full-length military bolt-action, roughly **44 inches**. The concealment argument is [backpack rifle impossibility](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Backpack_Rifle_Impossibility) and [stairs guy and backpack](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/stairs-guy-backpack). A satire clip of two men sliding rifles down slim-fit jeans is recorded on the [Narrative overview](/Narrative/overview) because the counter-argument belongs in the record; comedy is not a controlled test.

On X from late September 2025 onward, this was one of the first "the official story is physically wrong" lines — *"It wasn't broken down and put in a bag. Impossible"* ([@dean38499124245, Sept 27, 2025](https://x.com/dean38499124245/status/1971849005404770445)).

**Strength: moderate.** People have walked rifles off campuses before. The specific backpack in the stills is the question, and native-resolution footage would settle the volume argument.

## Link 4 — Nothing on the roof looks like a shot being fired

Reviewers of the available footage report they cannot identify a **muzzle flash** or a **recoil event** at the moment of the shot — [no muzzle flash or recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil). Some go further: [no shot from the roof at all](/Roof/decoy-no-shot-from-roof). [Chain 1](./not-a-thirty-06) uses this as a mechanism argument. This chain uses it as an *identification* argument: a figure on a roof who does not fire is not the shooter even if he is Robinson.

**Strength: weak on its own.** Daylight muzzle flash is frequently invisible; shouldered recoil is a small motion at that distance and resolution.

## Link 5 — The movement profile is being read as trained, not panicked

[Based Sam Parker](/Influencers/sam-parker) and others argue the roof sequence shows a **lateral military crawl**, a drop from height without hesitation, and a controlled sprint — [military lateral crawl](/Roof/military-lateral-crawl), [trained roof drop](/Roof/trained-roof-drop), [roof-run limp that disappears](/Suspicious/Five_Minutes/roof-run-limp-disappears). A first-time shooter fleeing in panic is a different vocabulary.

**Strength: contested.** Adrenaline makes untrained people look athletic. Movement-profile claims from compressed video are a known overfit.

## Link 6 — Roof eyewitnesses have not been called

The identification the state wants is a video identification. The identification that would actually close it is a **person who saw the figure's face**. Investigators have catalogued [an uncalled roof eyewitness](/Suspicious/Trial_Legal/uncalled-roof-eyewitness) and [multiple roofs, multiple figures](/Roof/multiple-roofs-figures). A preliminary hearing that does not call the people who were looking at the roof is a hearing that is not using the strongest available ID evidence.

**Strength: moderate as a process fact.** Preliminary hearings are not trials. The absence will matter more if it repeats at trial.

## Where the chain lands

The official identification is a release of selected, later-enhanced stills of a distant figure, issued as a BOLO, matched to a charged man whose clothing, pack, and firing event are all in dispute, while the humans who saw the roof have not been put on a stand. Citizen investigators have been saying "that is not him" since the week of the killing ([@CAMsRemixes, October 12, 2025](https://x.com/CAMsRemixes/status/1977505568916148568): *"Tyler Robinson was NOT the shooter"*). The state's public ID has not gotten sharper in the eleven months since. The footage that would settle it is [UVU HD withheld](/CoverUp/UVU_HD_Footage_Withheld).

This chain does **not** identify the rooftop figure. It says the state's identification has not been made.

## The strongest objection to this whole chain

**Low-resolution surveillance is a terrible medium, and that cuts both ways.** It undermines the FBI's confident release and it equally undermines confident amateur exclusions. Clothing colour shifts. People move well under stress. Muzzle flash hides in daylight. A household rifle can be carried in pieces. A BOLO issued after an arrest is sometimes just a slow press shop. Pixel-level exclusion is the weakest genre of internet investigation, and this chain contains some of it on purpose — labelled weak — because it is what people actually argue.

The links that survive that haircut are **release timing**, **the uncalled eyewitness**, and **the backpack-versus-rifle geometry**. Those three do not need a pause button.

## What would settle it

1. **Native-resolution, untrimmed UVU and campus camera footage.**
2. **The FBI image-enhancement work product** — what was enhanced, when, by whom.
3. **The timestamped BOLO release log against the custody record.**
4. **The roof eyewitnesses, under oath.**
