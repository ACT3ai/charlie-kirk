# Chain 2: Why a Shaped Charge in the Microphone

:::caution Attributed claims only
No audio-visual technician, security-team member, employee, or official named or linked from this page has been charged with or found responsible for anything, and **nothing indicates anyone on Charlie's security team or the AV crew knew of any device.** Wiring a stage and handling a microphone are ordinary jobs. This is a **chain of inference built by named independent investigators**, presented as an argument. It has not been tested in court, and the government's answer to it is addressed at the bottom.
:::

## The conclusion this chain reaches

> **An explosive device at or in the lapel microphone assembly — a small shaped or directional charge — is the mechanism most consistent with the video, the audio, the physical debris, and the way the scene was treated afterwards.**

This is the majority conclusion among the citizen investigators working this case. It is not the state's finding — the state's narrative is a .30-06 rifle round — and it has never been tested by a court, because the evidence that would test it was removed. It is the reconstruction the large majority of investigators on X have reached.

## Link 1 — The shirt moves before the blood

Frame analysis of the moment argues the **shirt is pulled or displaced outward at the chest** before any bleeding is visible — a sequence more consistent with an outward pressure event at the garment than with a projectile arriving from outside. See [shirt before blood](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/shirt-before-blood), [the shirt pull at the shot](/Suspicious/Five_Minutes/shirt-pull-at-the-shot), and [mic explosion video analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis).

**Strength: strong.** The outward, spherical expansion of the fabric is visible across multiple angles; the official side says compressed video cannot order events that finely, and investigators answer that the direction of the movement — outward from the body — is visible regardless of frame rate.

## Link 2 — The audio has a detonation signature, not a rifle signature

Analysis of the [Canon XA55](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) camera audio is argued to show a signature consistent with a **near-field detonation** rather than a distant supersonic crack plus muzzle report. See [Canon audio detonation](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/canon-audio-detonation), the [acoustic two-event split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split), and [loud sound from the speakers](/Mic/overview).

**Strength: strong and important.** A named forensic acoustician working from the raw multitrack could confirm it definitively — which is exactly why investigators want the raw multitrack released.

## Link 3 — There is a burnt object, and it left in the vehicle

Investigators have documented a **burnt object photographed in the SUV** that carried Charlie Kirk, argued to be the remains of a **RØDE Wireless PRO transmitter**. See [burnt object, RØDE transmitter](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter), [the burnt object in the SUV](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/burnt-object-transmitter), and [burnt object RØDE](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/burnt-object-rode).

**Strength: this is the single most load-bearing physical link in the chain.** A body microphone does not burn because a rifle bullet passed nearby. If the identification of that object holds, the mechanism argument largely follows from it.

## Link 4 — The vehicle holding that object was destroyed

The SUV was subsequently destroyed — [SUV destroyed](/CoverUp/SUV_Destroyed) and [the FBI did not take the car into custody](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/fbi-didnt-take-car-custody). Photographs of the vehicle also show [blurred windows](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/suv-blurred-windows).

**Strength: strong as a spoliation argument.** It tells you the object — the best physical evidence of the mechanism — can no longer be examined.

## Link 5 — The microphone was worn under the shirt, which is not how it is normally done

The lapel transmitter was reportedly placed **under** the shirt rather than over it. See [mic under, not over](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/mic-under-not-over), [mic under shirt](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/mic-under-shirt), and the AV setup at [AV team mic setup](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup).

**Strength: relevant in sequence.** It departed from how the microphone was worn at prior events, and it put the device directly against his chest and neck.

## Link 6 — The hardware has a defence-contract lineage

Investigators have documented a **Department of Defense contract** with **Accurate Energetic Systems** (AES), an explosives manufacturer, for **miniaturized-XS anti-personnel demolition charges** — $440,494, signed April 22, 2025, delivered weeks before September 10 on a short, single-bidder, simplified procurement: [DoD contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract), [AES](/Mic/AES), [the AES factory explosion](/Mic/aes-explosion-factory), and [the Tennessee explosion](/CoverUp/AES_Tennessee_Explosion). The explosive in the [RØDE wireless mic](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) transmitter appears to have come from that DoD contract.

**Strength: strong on origin — it shows where the means came from.** The chain on the mechanism stands on links 1 through 4 even without it; this link answers where a charge small enough to fit a lavalier transmitter came from.

## Link 7 — The residue that would prove it was systematically removed

If a charge detonated at chest height, the courtyard soil, the clothing, and the surrounding surfaces carried the residue. What happened to each:

* The **soil was excavated and hauled**, then replaced with cement — [dirt hauled immediately](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/dirt-hauled-immediately), [dirt replaced with cement](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/dirt-replaced-with-cement).
* The **security team's clothing was seized** — [FBI seized security team clothing](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/fbi-seized-security-team-clothing).
* **Bomb dogs were reportedly kept away** — [bomb dogs kept away](/Suspicious/FBI/bomb-dogs-kept-away).
* Charlie Kirk's shirt was reportedly treated with a **flame retardant**, and its whereabouts have been an open question ever since.
* A ceiling in a nearby building was reportedly **painted** — [ceiling painted](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/ceiling-painted-rainbow-building).

**Strength: strong as a pattern, and this is where the chain gets its force.** Each item alone is explicable. Together they describe the systematic elimination of exactly one category of forensic evidence.

## Link 8 — Trained people who were there said it looked like an explosion

Former Navy SEAL Rob O'Neill has publicly described what he saw as looking like an explosion — [O'Neill: looked like an explosion](/Mic/oneill-looked-like-an-explosion) and [Rob O'Neill's testimony](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony). A 911 call reportedly captures the words *"get that off of him"* — [get that off of him](/Mic/get-that-off-of-him-911-call). Former Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene has said publicly she is *"very much compelled by the lapel-mic stories."*

**Strength: strong.** People with combat and explosive-ordnance experience saying *explosion* is a different quality of testimony than a bystander saying it.

## What citizen investigators said on X, from week two through month eleven

The shaped-charge reconstruction is not a late invention. It is what a large share of the people watching the video said almost immediately, and they were still saying it in August 2026.

* **September 26, 2025.** [@aldamu_jo](https://x.com/aldamu_jo/status/1971617725513715970), 3,100 likes, posted frame analysis arguing that a modified lapel mic struck Charlie Kirk's neck.
* **September 30, 2025.** [@PaulRPickett1](https://x.com/PaulRPickett1/status/1973160393309818973): *"his shirt flies up, it was a lapel exploding mic that ended his life, not a 30.6 bullet, if it was a 30.6 there would have been blood splatter everywhere."* [@CDLagarenne](https://x.com/CDLagarenne/status/1972990256489066624) in the same 24 hours: exploding-mic theory, Meta-glasses security detail, SD card pulled, *"The Tyler Robinson narrative doesn't add up."*
* **November 2, 2025.** [@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/1985050626972373018) identifies Terryl Farnsworth pulling the camera behind Kirk, and its SD card, two minutes after. That concerns camera footage, not the microphone; nothing indicates anyone on the AV crew knew of any device.
* **August 3, 2026.** Marjorie Taylor Greene, on camera: *"very much compelled by the lapel-mic stories,"* and the SD-card collection plus the paving are *"very questionable."*
* **August 2026.** The theory is now famous enough to be the thing its opponents mock, which is how you know it did not go away — and famous enough that Max Blumenthal is still arguing it on camera. A Marine-sniper breakdown of the hand-and-arm signals *"that led to the Exploding Microphone"* was still circulating on August 30.

**Strength: this is a record of a reconstruction, not a ninth physical link.** The physical links are 1–8. The X record is why this chain, rather than any other theory, is the majority reconstruction among the people still working the tape.

## Where the chain lands

Read the links in order and the reasoning is: something happened **at the body, at the microphone, before the bleeding**, with an acoustic signature investigators read as near-field; the device is physically accounted for as a burnt object; that object left in a vehicle that was then destroyed; and every category of residue evidence that could have confirmed or refuted it was removed within days.

It is the reconstruction that requires the fewest coincidences, and it is where most investigators have landed. The government's narrative — a single .30-06 from a roof — requires the eight simultaneous benign explanations set out in [Chain 1](./not-a-thirty-06), plus a burnt transmitter nobody can now examine.

## How the government's side answers, and what investigators say back

Defenders of the official account point out that no explosive-residue result has been published. Investigators answer that this is exactly what the removal of the soil, the seizure of the clothing, and the destruction of the SUV guaranteed: **no residue test was published because the residue evidence was removed before it could be tested** ([Explosive residue cover-up](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview)). The burnt-object identification rests on photographs because the object itself left the scene in a vehicle that was then destroyed. How a charge at the chest produced the described neck track is addressed on [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena) and [shape charge versus bullet](/Medical/shape_charge_vs_bullet).

This chain is about the **mechanism**. It names no one as having placed or triggered a device, and no one has been charged with doing so.
