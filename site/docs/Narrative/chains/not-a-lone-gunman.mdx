# Chain 9: The Lone-Gunman Finding Cannot Absorb the Intelligence Indicators

:::caution Attributed claims only
No agency or official named here has been found to have concealed a conspiracy. The claim that an inquiry was halted is **attributed to former NCTC director Joe Kent**, speaking about his own experience. Flying an aircraft, parking it at a public airport, and operating a lawful surveillance programme are all lawful acts. Nothing below asserts that any person or government committed a crime.
:::

## The conclusion this chain reaches

> **"He acted alone" is the posture the public was given within days. It is not a shown result.** It has to absorb a halted foreign-ties review, an Army ISR aircraft over the corridor that day, foreign-registered jets on the Provo ramp, the FBI director's own accomplice language, and a scene-control pattern that does not look like a 22-year-old with a grandfather's rifle. It has not absorbed them. It has stopped mentioning them.

This chain is not [Chain 3](./following-planes-and-israel). Chain 3 is the aircraft. This chain is what those aircraft — and the halted review, and Patel's own words — do to the **lone-actor finding**. The catalogue is [Fed Claim 9](/Narrative/fed_narrative/lone-gunman) and [Proof Intel Services](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview).

## Link 1 — Patel said the FBI was investigating 20-plus people and possible accomplices

On **September 16, 2025**, FBI Director Kash Patel told the Senate Judiciary Committee the Bureau was investigating *"anyone and everyone involved in that Discord chat"* — more than twenty users — and the **possibility of accomplices**. Those are not lone-gunman words. They are week-one words of an agency that thinks the shooter may not have been alone.

The intervening step — the work in which those twenty-plus people were investigated and cleared — has not been shown. Citizen investigators have been asking for the dispositions ever since.

**Strength: strong as a contemporaneous statement. Unresolved as to what happened next.**

## Link 2 — A named former NCTC director says the foreign-ties review was ordered stopped

[Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent), former director of the National Counterterrorism Center, has said on the record that his centre was examining foreign-nexus leads — including Charlie Kirk's disputes with pro-Israel donors — and that the review was **ordered to stop**. See [Joe Kent foreign inquiry shutdown](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown), [Kent NCTC probe halted](/Suspicious/US_Intel/kent-nctc-probe-halted), [Patel shut down the Kent probe](/Suspicious/FBI/patel-shut-down-kent-probe), [White House halt to the IC](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC).

On X this is the sentence that will not die. [@RealTheForce, August 31, 2026](https://x.com/RealTheForce/status/2094532739622134212): *"Why did Kash Patel shut down Tulsi and Joe Kent from investigating foreign involvement in Charlie Kirk's murder?"* Tucker Carlson has quoted Kent on air. An investigation that is stopped has not concluded there was nothing there. It has concluded nothing at all.

Former Green Beret and CIA contractor **Mike Glover** has separately said on *Mike Drop* that investigators who flew into Utah were *"denied access and told to turn around and fly away."*

**Strength: strong as attribution to named officials; the underlying directive has not been produced.**

## Link 3 — An acknowledged US Army intelligence aircraft flew the corridor that day

**N1098L** is a Bombardier Global 6500 operated for the Army's **HADES** programme — High Accuracy Detection and Exploitation System. On September 10 it operated near the Orem corridor under callsign **AXEL10 / AXLE10**. See [US Army HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES), [N1098L HADES over UVU](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU). This is not a screenshot of a tracking site. Identity of the airframe is not in dispute. Purpose is.

Citizen investigators have treated the low, slow profile as drone-deployment capable since October 2025 ([@ProjectConstitu, October 14](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/1977966438414512291)). John Cullen has carried the HADES question into August 2026. A lone 22-year-old with a Mauser does not book an Army ISR sortie.

**Strength: strong on identity, unresolved on purpose.** Training, transit, and a scheduled sortie are all live explanations. An acknowledged collection aircraft over the site on the day is still a fact a lone-gunman finding has to *name*.

## Link 4 — Two Egyptian-registered jets were on the ground five miles away

[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), on-ground flag set, ~1.2 km from Provo Municipal, about five miles from Orem, on **10 September 2025**. SU-BTT since September 4; SU-BND for 113 days. Primary ADS-B. Full chain: [Chain 3](./following-planes-and-israel). This link only needs the *presence*. A lone-gunman finding that does not mention two foreign-state tails on the local ramp that day is a finding that has not looked up.

**Strength: strong on presence. Zero on occupancy — ADS-B locates an airframe, not a person.**

## Link 5 — A survey contractor photographed the campus that morning

Piper **N59906**, operated by MARC Inc., made multiple passes over UVU the morning of September 10 (investigators' times: 11:01, 11:09, 11:18, 11:35). Equipped for large-format photography and LiDAR. [@ProjectConstitu, November 7, 2025](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/1986848303871328357), updated [August 31, 2026](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2094377089679995086): the certificate later issued to **Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC**. [@HolonCitizen, August 31, 2026](https://x.com/HolonCitizen/status/2094458461367205995) records the passes at ~19,000 ft MSL as a standard mapping band, publishes the correction, and names the remaining question: **who was the client.** Charlie Kirk Law 1, Item #44 is written to force that contract out.

**Strength: strong on the aircraft being there. Purpose unknown. A mapping grid is lawful ordinary work.**

## Link 6 — Professional-hit indicators and scene control do not look like a first-time shooter

[State actors and the professional-hit argument](/Proof_Intel_Services/State_Actors_Professional_Hit), [FBI intel-cover signatures](/Proof_Intel_Services/FBI_Intel_Cover_Signatures), [UVU Sept 9 foreknowledge](/Proof_Intel_Services/UVU_Sept_9_Foreknowledge), [cameras empty / SD cards pulled / courtyard paved](./scene-was-destroyed). A 22-year-old acting alone does not typically produce a residue-removal pattern, a halted NCTC review, and an Army ISR overflight on the same day. That is not a proof. It is a **fit** problem.

On X the fit problem is what people mean by "this was a military hit" ([@bradleybaird4, July 6, 2026](https://x.com/bradleybaird4/status/2074196163620929970): *"The Intel Cartel JFKd Charlie Kirk... Robinson was already in custody when the discord admission chats were made"*). This page does not adopt that sentence. It adopts the list of things the lone-gunman finding has to explain and has not.

**Strength: moderate as a pattern, and this is where overclaim happens.** Each item has a mundane reading. The chain's job is to refuse to let the official account skip the list.

## Where the chain lands

Strip it to what survives:

1. The FBI director said, in week one, that accomplices were on the table. The clearing work has not been shown.
2. A named former NCTC director says the foreign-nexus review was ordered stopped.
3. An Army HADES aircraft flew the corridor that day.
4. Two Egyptian-registered jets were on the Provo ramp.
5. A survey contractor photographed the campus that morning, and the client has not been named.
6. The scene-control pattern is the pattern of [Chain 8](./scene-was-destroyed).

That is a case for **compelled disclosure**, including restoration of the NCTC review. It is not a case that names a service. Chain 3 already stops before the country-attribution step; this chain stops before the "therefore a professional hit" step. Both stops are load-bearing.

## The strongest objection to this whole chain

**Investigators say "we are looking at accomplices" in the first week of every high-profile case.** Ruling it out later is the normal outcome. Lone-actor political violence is the US base rate. Twenty Discord users interviewed and none charged is what a negative result looks like. HADES flies training sorties. Business jets sit. Survey companies map I-15. Kent's account is one man's, and it has not been tested under oath.

The honest criticism is not "the lone-gunman finding is false." It is **"the work supporting it has not been shown, and a named former official says a specific line of inquiry was closed by order rather than by evidence."** Those are process complaints. Process complaints are the ones that are actually fixable.

## What would settle it

1. **Publish the disposition of all 20-plus Discord users.**
2. **Produce the NCTC direction that ended the foreign-ties review, and the work product to that date.**
3. **Take Kent's and Glover's accounts under oath.**
4. **Disclose every federal aircraft, drone, and collection asset** over Utah County on 10 September 2025, and the HADES mission tasking.
5. **Name the MARC Inc / N59906 client** for that morning's flight.
