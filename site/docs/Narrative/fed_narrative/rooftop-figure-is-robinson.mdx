# Fed Claim 6: "That Is Him on the Roof"

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**. Identification arguments here are made by named independent analysts from public imagery; none has been tested by a court, and photo-comparison arguments made by amateurs are wrong often enough to require exactly this caveat.
:::

## Claim snapshot

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| The official claim | Video and stills released September 11, 2025 at 7:55 PM show the shooter crossing the Losee Center roof and walking off campus; that figure is Tyler Robinson |
| Who said it | The FBI, in the "person of interest" release; incorporated into the state's case |
| What is disputed | Clothing that does not match, a movement profile investigators read as trained, no visible muzzle flash or recoil, and a backpack too small for the claimed weapon |
| Status | **DISPUTED — AND THE IMAGERY WAS RELEASED AFTER THE CUSTODY TIME AT ISSUE** |

## The problem that comes before identification

Before asking whether the figure is Robinson, note **when the imagery was released**: 7:55 PM on September 11. If the defense filing quoting Bates 003996-R2 is dated September 11, Robinson had already been read his rights and invoked counsel **ninety minutes earlier**. A BOLO for a man already in an interview room is not an identification problem; it is a conduct problem. Investigators have separately documented [enhanced suspect photos released after he was in custody](/CoverUp/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos) and [the rooftop video being trimmed](/CoverUp/Rooftop_Video_Trimmed).

## The identification objections

**Clothing.** The figure's clothing has been compared frame by frame against what Robinson is documented wearing that day, and analysts report conflicts — [black clothing versus jeans](/Roof/black-clothing-vs-jeans), [Robinson clothing changes](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/robinson-clothing-changes), and [Tyler clothing conflicts](/Tyler_Robinson/clothing-change-agent-david-hull). Separately, multiple witnesses described a man in **all black**, which is not what the state's suspect is documented in — see [the all-black construction worker witness](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/all-black-construction-worker).

**Movement.** Analysts including [Based Sam Parker](/Influencers/sam-parker) argue the roof sequence shows a **lateral military crawl**, a drop from height taken without hesitation, and a controlled sprint — a movement vocabulary they associate with training rather than with a first-time shooter fleeing in panic. See [military lateral crawl](/Roof/military-lateral-crawl), [trained roof drop](/Roof/trained-roof-drop), and [the roof run limp that disappears](/Suspicious/Five_Minutes/roof-run-limp-disappears).

**No muzzle flash, no recoil.** Reviewers of the available footage report they cannot identify a muzzle flash or a recoil event at the moment of the shot from the claimed position — [no muzzle flash or recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil), and the harder claim, [no shot from the roof at all](/Roof/decoy-no-shot-from-roof).

**The backpack.** A Mauser 98 is a full-length military bolt-action, roughly 44 inches. The concealment argument is worked through at [backpack rifle impossibility](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Backpack_Rifle_Impossibility) and [stairs guy and backpack](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/stairs-guy-backpack). A widely shared satire video pushes back on that argument by sliding rifles down slim-fit jeans, and it is recorded on the [Narrative overview](/Narrative/overview) precisely because the counter-argument belongs in the record.

**The identity gap itself.** The comparison between the stairwell figure and Robinson is set out at [stairs guy identity gap](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap) and [roof figure not Tyler](/Roof/roof-figure-not-tyler).

## The counterarguments, stated fairly

Low-resolution surveillance video is a terrible medium for identification, and that cuts **both ways** — it undermines the FBI's confident release and it equally undermines confident amateur exclusions. Clothing appears to change colour under different lighting and compression. People who have never trained move athletically under adrenaline all the time. Muzzle flash is frequently invisible in daylight, especially in a long barrel and off-axis to the camera, and recoil on a shouldered rifle is a small motion at that distance.

Investigators pressing the identification objections are on their strongest ground with the **release timing** and the **uncalled roof eyewitness** — see [uncalled roof eyewitness](/Suspicious/Trial_Legal/uncalled-roof-eyewitness) — and on their weakest with pixel-level clothing comparison.

## What would settle it

1. **Release the full, untrimmed UVU and campus camera footage** at native resolution rather than selected clips — see [UVU HD footage withheld](/CoverUp/UVU_HD_Footage_Withheld).
2. **Produce the FBI's own image-enhancement work product**, including what was enhanced, when, and by whom.
3. **Publish the timestamped release log** for the 7:55 PM BOLO against the custody record.
4. **Call the eyewitnesses who reported seeing figures on roofs** and put their accounts on the record under oath.
