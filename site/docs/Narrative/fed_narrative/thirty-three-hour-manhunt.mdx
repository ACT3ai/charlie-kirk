# Fed Claim 8: "A 33-Hour Manhunt"

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**. The Panguitch identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**; it has not been established in court. Officials named here are accused of nothing.
:::

## Claim snapshot

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| The official claim | 33 hours elapsed between the 12:23 PM shot on September 10 and the suspect being in custody late on September 11 — a nationwide manhunt with a fugitive at large the entire time |
| Who said it | The published AP/NPR timeline, repeated in every major outlet |
| What is disputed | The end of the window (custody documents point earlier) and the middle of it (a restaurant 209 miles south says he was eating dinner there) |

Thirty-three hours, cleanly told. The number itself became a headline.

## Squeezing the window from the back

The custody argument compresses the end of the manhunt. A defense filing quoting Bates video **003996-R2** logs a Miranda warning at **6:25 PM** on what investigators argue was September 11, with Robinson immediately invoking counsel. If that date holds, the manhunt ended at least **three and a half hours earlier** than published, and the FBI's 7:55 PM BOLO went out for a man already in a sheriff's interview room. That argument is worked through on [Fed Claim 1](./turned-himself-in) and [Fed Claim 2](./discord-confession).

## Squeezing the window from the middle

The other pressure comes from **Panguitch, Utah** — a town 209 road miles south of Orem. Staff at **Cowboy's Smokehouse** say they served Tyler Robinson dinner between roughly **8:55 and 9:47 PM on September 10, 2025**, the evening of the shooting.

What makes this different from every other dispute on this site is that it is **not a contest over description**. Chest or neck, one shot or two, wrong guy or right guy — those are arguments about wording, and wording bends under retelling. Geography does not. If Robinson was eating in Panguitch that evening, he was not in the Orem area, and the real-time manhunt communications that anchor the public story become impossible for him to have sent. A receipt and a card transaction would settle it in an afternoon.

By the accounts given on camera, nobody with subpoena power has publicly moved to settle it. Staff have separately said they were [told not to talk](/CoverUp/Panguitch_Told_Not_To_Talk). The full treatment is [Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline), with the visit and receipt detail at [Cowboy's Smokehouse full visit](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-full-visit) and [the receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt).

## The driving problem

Investigators have also mapped the driving the official timeline requires across September 10 to 12 — see [the twenty-five-hour driving timeline](/Tyler_Robinson/twenty-five-hour-driving-timeline) and [the impossible timeline pages](/Tyler_Robinson/Travel). The round trip between Utah County and Washington County alone is over seven hours. Layering a Panguitch dinner, a return, an evening surrender, and an SBI officer's own 3.5-hour drive into the same window is where the arithmetic starts to fail.

## The counterarguments, stated fairly

Eyewitness identification by restaurant staff, made after a face has saturated every screen in the country, is **among the least reliable forms of evidence that exists**. Cross-racial and cross-context misidentification is routine; so is honest, confident, sincere error. That is not a slight against the staff — it is the reason lineups have rules. The Panguitch account needs the **transaction record**, not more interviews, and until that record is produced it remains a claim.

The 33-hour figure itself is also just journalism doing arithmetic on official statements. It was never an independent finding, and nobody has claimed it was.

## What would settle it

1. **Subpoena the Cowboy's Smokehouse card-transaction and POS records** for the evening of September 10, 2025.
2. **Produce the cell-site and device-location record** for Robinson's phone across September 10–11 — see [cell tower and phone data timeline](/Timeline/cell-tower-phone-data-timeline).
3. **Date the Bates 003996 video** and publish the custody log against it.
4. **Release automated licence-plate-reader hits** along the I-15 and US-89 corridors for the vehicle in question.
