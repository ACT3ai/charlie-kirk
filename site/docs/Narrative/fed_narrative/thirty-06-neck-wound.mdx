# Fed Claim 3: "A Single .30-06 Round to the Neck"

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**. The medical examiner and the testifying agents are living professionals accused of nothing. What follows sets the official cause of death beside the physical objections raised by named independent investigators. Those objections are **arguments**, and no court has ruled on them.
:::

## Claim snapshot

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| The official claim | Manner of death **homicide**; immediate cause a **gunshot wound of the neck** from a .30-caliber rifle projectile fired from the Losee Center roof |
| How it entered the record | The medical examiner **did not testify** at the July 6–10, 2026 preliminary hearing. Utah SBI Agent **David Hull** relayed the report's conclusions. The autopsy itself was not published in open court |
| What is disputed | Energy and wound geometry, the acoustic record, the absence of a recovered intact bullet, and the trajectory the state's own brief describes |
| Status | **THE CENTRAL DISPUTE OF THE CASE** |

## What the government said

The described wound track is a complex penetrating path: entrance on the **anterior left side of the neck**, with the projectile or its fragments perforating the left strap muscles, the left common carotid artery, and the left internal and external jugular veins. The claimed weapon is a **Mauser Model 98** bolt-action in **.30-06 Springfield**, scoped, recovered wrapped in a towel, and said to have belonged to Robinson's grandfather.

Two things about how that reached the public record matter. The medical examiner did not take the stand at the preliminary hearing; an investigating agent summarized the findings. And the report was not published in open court, on the stated grounds of Utah law restricting release of autopsy material. The most contested document in the case has therefore never been read in full by the people arguing about it.

## Why independent investigators reject it

**Energy.** A .30-06 Springfield leaves the muzzle at roughly 2,700–2,900 feet per second carrying something on the order of 2,800 foot-pounds. At the short range in question it arrives with most of that intact. Investigators argue the observed damage does not match what that energy does to a human neck — the analysis lives on [Thirty-06 energy mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch).

**No intact projectile, and an inconclusive fragment.** The ATF examination of the recovered fragment reportedly returned a **class-level, inconclusive** result rather than an individual match to the rifle — see [ATF fragment inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive). Separately, the FBI's reported willingness to lean on **comparative bullet-lead analysis**, a technique the Bureau abandoned in 2005 as scientifically unsupportable, is itself a finding: see [the CBLA question](/court/ballistics-atf-cbla).

**Trajectory.** The defense has pressed a contradiction between the state's described trajectory and the roof position — [state brief trajectory contradiction](/Medical/state_brief_trajectory_contradiction) and [wound trajectory angle](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle).

**Acoustics.** Audio analysis of the [Canon XA55](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) camera feed and other recordings is argued to show a **two-event signature** and a direction of arrival inconsistent with the claimed firing position — [acoustic two-event split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split) and [acoustics from the south](/Suspicious/UVU_9_10/acoustics-from-south).

**The witnesses said chest.** A striking number of the earliest on-camera accounts described a **chest or heart** wound, not a neck wound, several of them on national television within the hour. That catalogue is [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart), with the video compilations at [Shot to the Heart](/Witnesses/shot-to-the-heart).

**And the injuries listed exceed one bullet.** A July 2026 court filing quoting the medical examiner's report lists injuries well beyond a single neck track — see [Charlie's injuries](/Medical/charlies_injuries).

## Where the independent investigation has landed

Taken together, the majority position among the citizen investigators working this case is that Charlie Kirk was killed by an **explosive device**, most plausibly a shaped charge at or in the lapel microphone assembly, and not by a rifle round fired from a roof. That conclusion is argued from the physical record on [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview), [shape charge versus bullet](/Medical/shape_charge_vs_bullet), and the [Mic section](/Mic/overview) — and it is set out as a chain of reasoning on [Why It Was Not a .30-06](/Narrative/chains/not-a-thirty-06) and [Why a Shaped Charge in the Microphone](/Narrative/chains/shaped-charge-microphone).

## The counterarguments, stated fairly

A .30-06 through the neck is not a strange finding. High-velocity rifle rounds fragment, deflect off bone, and produce wound tracks that surprise laypeople; "the damage looks wrong" is the single most common lay error in gunshot analysis. Eyewitnesses under acute stress routinely misplace a wound by a foot or more, and a neck wound bleeding heavily reads as a chest wound to someone twenty feet away. Echo off hard campus architecture produces double reports from one shot. An inconclusive fragment comparison means **inconclusive** — it is not affirmative evidence of a different mechanism.

Those are real and they should be weighed. What they do not do is explain the burnt object, the residue questions, the microphone's behaviour on video, or why the courtyard was excavated and paved within days. Those belong to the [chain of reasoning](/Narrative/chains/shaped-charge-microphone), not to this page.

## What would settle it

1. **Publish the complete autopsy report**, with imaging, under whatever protective order Utah law requires.
2. **Put the medical examiner on the stand** rather than an agent summarizing the report.
3. **Independent re-examination** of the recovered fragment by a lab with no stake in the outcome.
4. **Release the raw multi-camera audio** for acoustic analysis by named experts on both sides.
