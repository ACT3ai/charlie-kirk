The framings cataloged in this section are all contests over **description** — chest or neck, one shot or
two, wrong guy or right guy. **Panguitch is different: it is a contest over geography**, and geography does
not bend to retelling. Staff at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah — **209 road miles south of Orem** —
say they served [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) dinner from **8:55 to 9:47 PM on September 10,
2025**, which would make the real-time Orem-area manhunt texts that anchor the public story impossible for
him to have sent. That is why researchers find it the most interesting story in this section: it is the one
claim that **cannot be settled by wording**, only by a receipt and a card, and by every account given on
camera nobody with subpoena power has publicly moved to settle it. The identification is **claimed by
restaurant staff and contested**; Robinson is **charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent**.

## What this area covers

This area is about *framing*, not about any single piece of physical evidence. In a fast-moving, heavily filmed public event, the words used to describe what happened — where the wound was, who was arrested, whether there was a confession, who was to blame — spread through television and social media long before any official finding. This section collects the pages where the investigation documents those early descriptions as-given and asks a narrow, recurring question: which framings appeared, how widely they were amplified, and which ones later receded once raw footage and official records circulated.

The clearest worked example is the "shot to the heart" description. Across the earliest on-camera interviews, multiple witnesses — several of them featured in national television segments — described Charlie Kirk as struck **directly in the heart**. Skeptics note that no imagery shows a chest entry wound or chest blood, that a high ratio of the earliest confident statements landed on TV news, and that the framing faded as videos of a **neck** wound dominated timelines. The dedicated catalog of X posts is [Shot in the Heart](./Shot_in_the_heart); full TV compilations and the shaped-charge question live on [Shot to the Heart](/Witnesses/shot-to-the-heart). The same pattern-question runs through the [confession-fabrication](/CoverUp/Confession_Fabrication) and custody-timeline disputes and the broader [narrative-control](/CoverUp/Narrative_Psyop) claims.

Start with [Shot in the Heart](./Shot_in_the_heart) for the complete clickable X archive of heart/chest claims, then [Shot to the Heart](/Witnesses/shot-to-the-heart) for embedded witness video. Read [Wrong Witnesses](/Witnesses/wrong_witnesses) and [Shot in Chest, Then Neck](/Witnesses/Shot_in_Chest_Reversal) for the witness-account layer. For the online and media dimension — how framings were amplified or suppressed — see [Online Narrative-Control Operations](/CoverUp/Narrative_Psyop), [Media Framing & Coverage](/Media/overview), and [Censorship](/Censorship/overview). Throughout, every framing is recorded as a **question**, weighed against the ordinary explanations (genuine virality, lay shorthand, and coverage naturally tracking official findings), and never asserted as a coordinated operation.

## Rifle-Concealment Satire Clip (Counterpoint)

A widely shared satire video pushes back on one of the recurring skeptic
arguments — that a full-length **.30-06 bolt-action rifle** could not
realistically have been concealed and walked off campus. In the clip, two men
wearing "Candace is a demon" shirts each slide a rifle down one leg of
slim-fit jeans and walk on camera, with the narration: *"we're going to prove
to these people that a 30-06 easily fits inside slim-fitting jeans... see, we
told you."* It is comedy, not a controlled test — no timing, clothing, or
gait comparison to the campus footage is offered — but it is recorded here
because the concealment question is argued on both sides across this section,
and the counter-argument belongs in the record alongside the
[backpack and rifle concealment analysis](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Backpack_Rifle_Impossibility).
