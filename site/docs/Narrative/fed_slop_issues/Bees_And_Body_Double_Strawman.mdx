# 51. The "bee / LDS / body-double hospital" stack as the whole opposing case

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims as fact that any commentator, official, or organization acted in bad faith,
or that any person committed a crime. This site does not adopt the viral clips discussed here.
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted, has not entered a guilty plea, and is
presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Every big case attracts strange theories. This one has a set of viral clips that get passed around
together, mocked under shorthand like "bees," theories about the LDS church, and the claim that a body
double was used at the hospital. They are easy to laugh at, and they are often the first thing a
newcomer sees when the case is discussed on the official side.

A "strawman" is when you argue against the weakest version of the other side instead of its strongest.
The question here is whether the official side's habit of satirizing these clips is a fair rebuttal of
the doubters, or a way to avoid the four specific, documented gaps that citizen investigators actually
rest their case on.

## The fed slop side, in full

These clips are real, they are widely shared, and some of them have huge audiences. If the movement
doubting the official account spreads them, it owns them. On this view, pointing out how absurd they
are is fair: it shows the quality of thinking behind the doubt, and it warns ordinary people away from
a rabbit hole. A movement that cannot police its own worst claims does not deserve to be taken
seriously on its best ones.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the loudest clips are not their case, and that satire of them answers nothing. They
list four concrete gaps, each documented on this site:

* **The steakhouse last-four.** Staff at [Cowboy's Smokehouse](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt)
in Panguitch, about 209 miles south of Orem, say they served the accused dinner from 8:55 to 9:47 PM on
September 10 and gave the FBI the last four digits of the card. Nobody has published a comparison with
Robinson's card.
* **The 6:25 custody.** A defense filing quotes a video showing Robinson read his Miranda rights at
[6:25 PM on September 11](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession) and invoking counsel a
minute later, which investigators set against the later Discord "confession" traffic.
* **The 911 kit.** Police dispatch described the roof figure as wearing all black, a tactical helmet, a
mask, and possibly a tactical vest, and the man who says he [filmed the roof figure](/Witnesses/911-calls-rooftop-witness)
describes the same kind of gear, not a t-shirt and jeans.
* **The fragment mismatch.** The ATF compared the autopsy bullet jacket fragment with the seized rifle
and returned [inconclusive](/court/ballistics-atf-cbla).

Investigators say a rebuttal of the case has to answer those four, and that a joke about bees does not.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Each of the four gaps points away from Robinson on its own: a dinner 209 miles away, a clock problem
with the confession, a clothing description that does not match, and a fragment that was not matched
to his rifle. If public debate is steered to the weakest clips, these never get answered in public,
and the presumption of innocence becomes a punchline.

## What would settle it

* The FBI's comparison of the Panguitch card digits with Robinson's accounts, disclosed in full.
* The complete September 11 custody video, with its timestamps, and the phone seizure record.
* The unedited dispatch audio and the rooftop witness's statement to police.
* The full ATF report and bench notes on the jacket fragment.
