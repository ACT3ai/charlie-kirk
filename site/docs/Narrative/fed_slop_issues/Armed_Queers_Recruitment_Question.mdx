# 46. Armed Queers SLC: recruitment, or just the milieu?

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes no claim that any member or organizer of Armed Queers SLC, or any similar group, knew
of or took part in any crime. No individual member is named here. This site also makes no claim that
[Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) knew about or had any role in Charlie Kirk's death. Nothing here
treats anyone's identity or community as evidence of anything. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)
is charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

In the days after the arrest, news coverage and online posts described Tyler Robinson's social world:
his partner and roommate, [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs), who is transgender, and online
associations with activist circles in Utah. One name that appeared in early media was **Armed Queers
SLC**, a Salt Lake City group. The site's [organizations overview](/organizations_groups/overview)
records that name under activist networks, with online-association scrutiny and no court finding of any
group plot.

Two very different stories can be built from the same facts. In one, this world simply explains a
motive: a young man steeped in a community hostile to Charlie Kirk. In the other, the question is
whether someone inside or around that network steered a vulnerable young man into a role, which is what
people mean by a patsy.

## The fed slop side, in full

The State's motive story is about ideas, not organizations. Prosecutors point to texts and statements
between Robinson and his roommate, and to his views about Charlie Kirk. On this view, the activist
groups he may have followed online are the backdrop for those views. Plenty of people join or follow
such groups and never hurt anyone. Calling a community group a cell because of one member's alleged
act smears everyone in it, and there is no evidence the group planned anything.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators agree that membership proves nothing. Their question is narrower: if Tyler Robinson was
set up, how would a setup reach him? A network that knew him, trusted him, and could shape what he
did and where he went is one obvious path. They want his contacts inside any such network examined
the way any investigator would examine a suspect's contacts: who introduced whom, who suggested what,
and who knew his schedule.

They tie this to other recruitment questions the site catalogs on its [Tyler Robinson Recruited](/Tyler_Robinson/Recruited)
page, including claimed search interest in "Tyler Robinson Discord/Reddit/FBI/LDS/TPUSA/Israel"
running months before the shooting. Investigators also note the State's own motive evidence runs
through the roommate relationship, so the people closest to Robinson are already at the center of the
case. They say that fact cuts both ways: the same closeness that makes a motive story also makes a
steering story possible. Asking the question, they stress, is not an accusation against any member.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If the milieu is only motive, it helps the State. If someone in or around it guided Robinson, it is
the path by which an innocent or unwitting young man could be placed where the State now says he was.
Either way, the contacts need to be examined as facts, not treated as a caricature that ends the
inquiry.

## What would settle it

* Robinson's complete message history with any group contacts, as extracted from his devices.
* Whether the FBI interviewed group organizers and members, and what their 302 reports say.
* Any informant or undercover presence in the network, disclosed under Brady if it exists.
* A timeline of who first suggested the UVU event, and when, in Robinson's communications.
