# 11. A second shooter, an exploding microphone, or another mechanism instead of the roof rifle

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The
rooftop .30-06 is **the government's narrative**. The exploding microphone is the **citizen investigators'
conclusion**. This site makes no claim that anyone on Charlie Kirk's security team, the AV team, or TPUSA
staff knew of, placed, triggered, or removed any device. Nothing indicates any of them did.
:::

## The issue in plain words

There are two very different stories about **how** Charlie Kirk died at 12:23 PM on September 10,
2025.

**The government's narrative** is that a single .30-06 rifle round, fired from the Losee Center roof about 150
yards away, struck him in the neck.

**Most citizen investigators conclude** that the fatal event was a small **explosive device at the lapel
microphone** clipped to his shirt, a RØDE Wireless PRO transmitter worn against his chest. This issue lays out
both, so a newcomer can see why so many independent researchers left the rooftop story behind.

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says **none of that** happened. The medical examiner ruled it a **homicide by gunshot
wound**. There was **no explosive residue** reported. Microphone theories are slop. There was one person who
jumped from the roof and one bullet.

The strongest form of this position is that a gunshot is the ordinary explanation for a sudden fatal neck
wound at a public event, that echoes off campus buildings can make one shot sound like two, and that the
burden is on anyone claiming something more exotic.

## What citizen investigators say

Most citizen investigators conclude the fatal event was **an explosive device at the microphone**, not
the rooftop rifle. The chain they build, set out on the [Exploding Mic hub](/Mic/overview), runs like this:

* **The shirt moves outward before any blood appears**, in frame analysis from several angles.
* **The camera audio** is argued to carry a near-field detonation signature, with the louder event at the tent,
  where the microphone was, not at the roof.
* **A burnt object**, argued to be the remains of the RØDE transmitter, was photographed in the SUV that carried
  him. That vehicle was later destroyed.
* **The earliest witnesses** described a **chest or heart** wound, not a neck wound.
* **The residue that would prove it** was removed: courtyard soil excavated and paved within days.
* A former Navy SEAL at the scene publicly said what he saw **looked like an explosion**.

The wound, the 911 audio, and the missing firing tape also leave **a second origin** open in some analyses.
Claims of **electrocution** circulate too, but they are **uncorroborated and are not the investigators'
conclusion**.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If Charlie Kirk was killed by a device at his microphone, then the rooftop rifle is not the murder
weapon, and a man accused of firing it from 150 yards away could not have caused the death in the way he is
charged. That is the deepest reason investigators believe Tyler Robinson is being framed: the case against him
is built around a mechanism they conclude did not kill Charlie.

## What would settle it

* The full, unredacted autopsy report and the medical examiner's testimony.
* The raw multitrack audio from every recording device at the event, released to independent acousticians.
* Chemical testing of Charlie Kirk's shirt and the burnt object from the SUV, if they still exist.
* The chain of custody for the microphone transmitter and its receiver.
* The records of what was removed from the courtyard, and when, before it was paved.
