# 19. Panguitch geography versus the Orem phone story

:::caution Attributed claims only
The Panguitch identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**; it has not been authenticated in court, and the restaurant later said it has no evidence either way. Nothing on this page says any officer, investigator, or other living person invented evidence, lied, or had foreknowledge of the killing. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. Lance Twiggs has been charged with nothing.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk was shot at **12:23 PM** on September 10, 2025, at Utah Valley University in **Orem**. That evening, staff at **Cowboy's Smokehouse** in **Panguitch**, a small town about **209 road miles** south, say a quiet young man matching the FBI photos ate a steak dinner alone at the counter. A photographed ticket, **Check #211**, shows an order around **8:55 PM** paid at **9:47 PM** on a Visa debit card.

The State's account of that same night needs Robinson back in the Orem area: texts exchanged with his roommate, and a car stopped by an officer near campus at about **12:30 AM**. This issue asks whether both stories can fit on one map.

|---|---|

| **Citizen Investigators** | Panguitch is ~3 hours south. A ticket, a PIN card, and a server ID wreck the Orem-at-9:00 p.m. texts, the midnight stop, and the 12:38 / 1:33 a.m. sightings, unless the phone was not on his body. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: a staff identification of a stranger, made from FBI photos the next day, is a well-known source of error. A card can be carried by someone other than its owner. Against that, the State has physical and digital anchors. Agent Brian Davis testified that a **Cellebrite** extraction of the roommate's phone shows texts with Robinson beginning at **11 PM** on September 10 ([Twiggs' phone](/court/Twiggs_Phone)). And Agent David Hull testified that a Spanish Fork officer, **Goforth**, spoke with the driver of a car registered to Robinson at **Campus Drive and 800 South** at about **12:30 AM** on September 11. On this view, Panguitch is a mistaken sighting.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with arithmetic. From a 9:47 PM card approval, a normal drive back to Orem arrives between about **12:56 and 1:27 AM**. Reaching the campus by 12:30 AM requires about **77 mph with no stops**, starting on two-lane US-89 during a statewide manhunt ([Goforth midnight stop](/Tyler_Robinson/goforth-midnight-stop)). The southbound leg is worse: from a claimed Orem Dairy Queen sighting at 6:38 PM to an 8:55 PM order in Panguitch takes **98–99 mph** the whole way.

Second, they look at the quality of the State's anchor. Officer Goforth did not testify. The court heard Hull's second-hand account of a contact hearing coverage describes as about **20 seconds**, a **partial plate**, and a driver the officer *"believed"* to be Robinson. No bodycam or written stop report was presented.

Third, the texts show where a phone was, not where a man was. If the diner was Robinson, investigators say, the Orem-area texts either came from a phone that was not on his body or were sent by someone else. Staff say they gave the FBI the **last four digits of the card**, and the server says nobody came back ([the receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt)).

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Panguitch is a contest over place, not description. If an authenticated card record puts Robinson at that counter at 9:47 PM, the State's night-of story — the texts, the rifle-retrieval attempt, the midnight stop — cannot all be true. A record that would settle it in minutes has not been shown in public.

## What would settle it

* The bank authorization for Check #211: cardholder, last four, time, and terminal ID.
* The restaurant's POS log and any counter camera, with its retention record.
* Cell-site and location data for Robinson's phone on the evening of September 10.
* Officer Goforth's own report, notes, bodycam, and sworn testimony.
* The partial plate as written and every vehicle it returned.
