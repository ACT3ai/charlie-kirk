# 64. A security-team member ("Rick") as the shooter

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes no claim that Rick, or any member of the security team, the AV team or the TPUSA staff, fired, had any device, triggered anything, knew Charlie would be killed, or caused the death. Nothing on this page says any living person committed a crime or had foreknowledge of the killing. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk had a [security detail](/Security_Team/overview) around him at Utah Valley University on **September 10, 2025**. After the killing, some posts online singled out one member of that detail, referred to as "Rick", and suggested he might have been the shooter.

This page exists so readers who meet that claim can see where this site stands. The only observation this site repeats about him is that it appears he pulled on his shirt sleeve, which looks interesting. Nothing more is claimed.

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: the security team's job in that moment was to get Charlie Kirk off the stage and to a vehicle, and that is what they did. They were standing near him because protecting him was their job. Singling out an individual on the detail, with no evidence, exposes a private person to harassment and threats, and it distracts from the actual case.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the right way to handle every angle, including this one, is with measurements rather than names. They ask for the trajectories: the entry point, the angle and the path of the wound, set against the positions of everyone near Charlie Kirk at the moment of the event. That data would close this question and every other one like it.

The only observation this site repeats about Rick is that it appears he pulled on his shirt sleeve, and that this looks interesting. This site makes no claim that he fired, had any device, triggered anything, knew Charlie would be killed, or caused the death.

The investigators' conclusion on the cause of death is an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview), and this site notes that nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Alternate-shooter claims that name individuals without evidence tend to make every question about the case easier to dismiss. The questions that could actually clear Robinson are about the wound path, the fragment and the cause of death. Investigators say those should be answered with the trajectory and forensic data, which would settle this row and the charge against Robinson at the same time.

## What would settle it

* The full autopsy, with entry location, angle and wound path.
* A published scene reconstruction placing every person near Charlie Kirk at the moment of the event.
* The complete ATF fragment report.
* Independent residue testing of the clothing and the vehicle.
