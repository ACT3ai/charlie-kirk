# 64. A security-team member ("Rick") as the shooter

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes no claim that Rick, or any member of the security team, the AV team or the TPUSA staff, fired, had any device, triggered anything, knew Charlie would be killed, or caused the death. Nothing on this page says any living person committed a crime or had foreknowledge of the killing. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk had a [security detail](/Security_Team/overview) around him at Utah Valley University on **September 10, 2025**. After the killing, some posts online singled out one member of that detail, referred to as "Rick", and suggested he might have been the shooter.

This page exists so readers who meet that claim can see where this site stands. The only observation this site repeats about him is that it appears he pulled on his shirt sleeve, which looks interesting. Nothing more is claimed. This site does not identify a legal last name, does not publish a home address, and does not treat a still frame as a shooting.

The investigators' conclusion on the cause of death is an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview). That conclusion is about a device. This site notes that nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device.

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: the security team's job in that moment was to get Charlie Kirk off the stage and to a vehicle, and that is what they did. They were standing near him because protecting him was their job. A black-SUV extraction in under a minute is what a protective detail is trained to do after a casualty.

Singling out an individual on the detail, with no charging document and no recovered weapon from that person, exposes a private person to harassment and threats. Official-side accounts treat naming staff as an attack on the people who were there for Charlie, and as a distraction from the man the state has charged. [Erika Kirk](/People/erika-kirk) and the family's later notice of claim against UVU frames a rooftop assassin the campus failed to stop. On that telling, the detail is a victim of the same security gap, not a suspect class.

No public video shows anyone on the team firing. That, for the official side, is the end of the row.

The harassment point is real as far as it goes. Private security staff are not public officials. Posting a name, a face crop, and a theory that the person fired is the kind of pile-on this site will not join. The official side then uses that restraint as a shield for the whole tent: if you may not name Rick as a shooter, you may not ask where everyone was standing. Investigators reject the second half. Positions, trajectories, and residue tests are not a name.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the right way to handle every angle, including this one, is with measurements rather than names. They ask for the trajectories: the entry point, the angle and the path of the wound, set against the positions of everyone near Charlie Kirk at the moment of the event. That data would close this question and every other one like it. See [trajectory and wound mismatch](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch) and the [alternative-mechanisms routing page](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).

The only observation this site repeats about Rick is that it appears he pulled on his shirt sleeve, and that this looks interesting. This site makes no claim that he fired, had any device, triggered anything, knew Charlie would be killed, or caused the death. A still frame cannot show what anyone was thinking. Online threads that go further than that are not this site's line.

Citizen investigators do ask process questions about the [security arrangements](/Security_Team/overview) that day: reported gaps include no counter-snipers, no medical standby, and an unwatched rooftop line of sight. Those questions are about planning. They are not claims about any individual's intent. The [cover-up file](/CoverUp/overview) is the place for withheld scene records, not for a named shooter.

The investigators' conclusion on the cause of death is the [shaped-charge microphone](/Narrative/chains/shaped-charge-microphone). The [full autopsy](/Medical/autopsy_report_not_public) and the [ATF fragment report](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive) are the documents that would close every named-person variant at once, including this one. The [FBI](/FBI/overview) holds scene video that would place every person near the tent. [Fix](/Fix/overview) is how those records get forced out.

This site makes no claim that Erika Kirk, TPUSA, or any named member of the detail had any role in or knowledge of the killing.

The sleeve-pull is recorded because readers will see the clip and will look for a page that says what this investigation does with it. The answer is: it looks interesting, and that is the entire published observation. Anything past that — a device in a sleeve, a trigger, a signal, a shot — is other people's claim, and this site does not repeat it as a finding.

## What is actually in the public record

* Charlie Kirk had a private protective detail at UVU on September 10, 2025. They extracted him from the tent toward a vehicle after the event.
* Online threads named a team member called Rick as a possible shooter. This site does not adopt that claim.
* The only observation this site repeats about him: it appears he pulled on his shirt sleeve, which looks interesting.
* No public video shows anyone on the detail firing. No charging document names him. Nothing on this site says he had a device, knew Charlie would be killed, or caused the death.
* Investigators ask for trajectories and a published reconstruction of everyone near the tent. Those records have not been released.

## When this fight ran

* **September 10–16, 2025.** Immediate frame-by-frame watching of stage video. Named-staff threads appear in the same window as the lone-wolf lock-in. Official-side: security did its job.
* **July 2026.** Hearing week. Official coverage of extraction and the rooftop "sniper pad" is used to keep the origin on Losee, away from the tent.
* **September 16, 2026.** Family notice of claim against UVU over rooftop security. Official-side uses that filing to talk about campus failure, not about the detail as a suspect class.
* **Ongoing.** Investigator accounts that stay on this site's line ask for trajectories. Accounts that name a shooter are not the investigators' main line and are not adopted here.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Alternate-shooter claims that name individuals without evidence tend to make every question about the case easier to dismiss. The questions that could actually clear [Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) are about the wound path, the fragment and the cause of death. Investigators say those should be answered with the trajectory and forensic data, which would settle this row and the charge against Robinson at the same time. See [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and the [real-killer map](/Killer/overview).

A weak named theory is how a strong mechanism question gets thrown out. That is why this page is short on names and long on what would close the angle.

Readers who want to know what the detail did that day should read the [security-team overview](/Security_Team/overview) as process: who was contracted, what the plan was, what failed. That is a different question from "who fired." Mixing them is how a private person gets hunted and how a residue test never gets run.

## What would settle it

* The full autopsy, with entry location, angle and wound path.
* A published scene reconstruction placing every person near Charlie Kirk at the moment of the event.
* The complete ATF fragment report.
* Independent residue testing of the clothing and the vehicle.
* Native-resolution stage video of the five seconds around 12:23 p.m., from every camera that had the tent in frame.
