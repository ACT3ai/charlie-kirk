# 47. Joe Kent, the NCTC, Arctic Frost, and Kirk-as-informant

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims as fact that the FBI, Kash Patel, Joe Kent, any agency, or any official
committed a crime, obstructed anything, or had any knowledge of or role in Charlie Kirk's death. This
site does not claim Charlie Kirk was an informant or asset of any agency; that is a hypothesis raised by
investigators. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Three separate threads meet on this page, and they all sit on top of the same fact: [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)
was killed on September 10, 2025 at Utah Valley University, and the federal government closed the public
file on a lone Utah defendant.

First, [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent), who led the [National Counterterrorism Center](/US_Intelligence/nctc)
(NCTC), has said on the record that his center opened an inquiry into possible foreign involvement in
the death, and that the [FBI](/FBI/overview) stopped it before the foreign leads were exhausted. This is
a first-person statement from a serving official at the time, not anonymous speculation.

Second, **Arctic Frost** was an FBI investigation later tied to Jack Smith's election case. At a Senate
Judiciary release on September 16, 2025 — six days after the killing — files showed the probe's scope
had grown to at least 92 Republican-linked targets, and that one of Charlie Kirk's groups, Turning
Point USA, was on that list.

Third, some investigators go further and ask whether Kirk had once been close enough to government
work to be an asset, and whether he stopped being useful. That is a hypothesis. It is not a finding.

## The fed slop side, in full

The State has a defendant, a rifle, DNA, and texts. Once a case is that concrete, talk of foreign
services and informant files pulls attention away from the evidence and toward things nobody can
check. Agencies disagree about turf all the time, and the FBI leading a domestic murder case over the
NCTC is how the system is built. Arctic Frost was about the 2020 election, years earlier. On this view,
stringing these together is exactly how a conspiracy theory grows: every unrelated federal story
becomes a clue, and Robinson fades from view.

A *New York Times* report on October 28, 2025 put the official objection in plain language: [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel)
and other senior officials believed Kent was overstepping, treading on FBI responsibilities, and
potentially interfering with the prosecution of Tyler Robinson. Officials involved in the arrest said
they believed Robinson [acted alone](/Narrative/fed_narrative/lone-gunman). A later law-enforcement
source, quoted after Kent's March 2026 interviews, said Kent lacked authority to access the files and
rejected claims of obstruction. Official-side commentators treat Kent's later media tour as a mutation
away from the charging package: a way to keep a foreign-hit story alive after a court had already
seen DNA, texts, and a bind-over.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with the part that is on the record. Kent's own account, summarized on this site's
[Joe Kent page](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown), is that the foreign-nexus
work was halted. In March 2026, after resigning while citing opposition to a U.S. war with Iran, he
told Tucker Carlson: *"We were then told that, 'Hey, you guys need to stop. You can't work on this
anymore.'"* He told Megyn Kelly that NCTC's role *"was to look to see if there was any foreign
involvement — now that could be government or that could just be foreigners themselves,"* and that
*"there were additional leads that we needed to run down and fully investigate, and that simply was
not done."* He has said he is not claiming a smoking-gun of government involvement. He is claiming the
leads were not finished.

Reporting cited on the [White House halt](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC) page says administration
officials worried the inquiry could give Robinson's defense grounds to argue more than one suspect.
Investigators say a foreign lead that was stopped, rather than finished and ruled out, is an open
door. A sitting intelligence official saying the FBI kicked NCTC off the file is, they argue, the
opposite of independent review. See [Kash Patel's briefings](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings) for the federal
seal that then went on the lone-actor package.

On Arctic Frost, they point out that the FBI had TPUSA under an investigative scope before September
2025, according to files Chairman Chuck Grassley released. That means federal files on Kirk's
organization existed. Investigators want to know what those files held, and whether any of that
collection was sitting in a drawer the week Kirk was killed.

The informant idea is the most speculative part, and investigators present it as a question: if Kirk
was ever close to government, and was moving away from positions his backers expected in his final
weeks, would that create a motive the roommate texts do not? Kent has said his last conversation with
Kirk, in June 2025, included Kirk telling him to [stop a war with Iran](/Charlie/Phone_Calls/joe-kent-last-conversation).
That is a policy fight, not a personnel file. Investigators still ask the asset-to-liability question
because the State's motive story was settled within days, before the foreign inquiry was allowed to
finish. The [Israel motive timeline](/Proof_Intel_Services/Israel_Motive_Timeline) and the
[Israeli-phones / Bumblehive](/Proof_Intel_Services/Israeli_Phones_Bumblehive) claims are the adjacent
files they say NCTC should have been allowed to run.

In March 2026, journalist Michael Shellenberger reported that Kent said he would testify under oath
at Robinson's trial about the foreign nexus. Project Constitution and Ryan Matta amplified that
willingness as the discovery fight the official story cannot survive. Investigators treat that offer
as the test: put the halted inquiry in front of a jury, or admit the [lone-gunman finding](/Narrative/chains/not-a-lone-gunman)
was a posture.

## When this fight ran

* **September 16–17, 2025.** Arctic Frost files land in public, six days after the killing. TPUSA is
  on a 92-entity Republican target list. The same week, the FBI Director is putting a federal seal on
  DNA and a lone actor.
* **October 28, 2025.** The *New York Times* reports Kent examined FBI files on whether Robinson had
  support from a foreign power or another entity, and that Patel was alarmed. A White House meeting
  follows. This is the first mainstream confirmation that a foreign-nexus look existed inside the
  building.
* **March 19–24, 2026.** Kent resigns, citing the Iran war. He goes on Tucker Carlson and Megyn Kelly.
  *"You guys need to stop"* becomes the quote. Shellenberger reports he would testify. Official-side
  commentators and TPUSA-adjacent hosts frame the testimony offer as a threat to the jury pool.
* **July 2026.** A later clip, circulated by Julian Dorey, has Kent saying multiple people had
  pre-knowledge and that the FBI shut the investigation down before all leads were run. The July
  hearing does not call him.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

A defendant can point to an alternative explanation only if the evidence for one is gathered. If the
only federal inquiry into foreign involvement was stopped midway, the leads it would have produced
never reach the defense or the jury. That gap favors a one-man story whether or not it is true. The
theoretical [NCTC subpoena](/court/ai_attorney/nctc-halted-inquiry-subpoena) exists because a capital
case that hides a halted foreign-nexus file is, investigators say, the opposite of Brady.

## What would settle it

* NCTC's records of the Kirk inquiry: what it opened, what leads it held, and the written order ending it.
* Any FBI memo explaining why the inquiry was halted, including the "defense could call you" rationale Kent described.
* The Arctic Frost records on TPUSA, including what was collected and when that work stopped.
* A sworn answer from the FBI on whether Kirk or TPUSA staff ever had any source or informant relationship with any agency.
* Kent's testimony, under oath, in the Utah case.
