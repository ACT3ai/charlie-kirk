# 26. The Discord "confession" clock versus the surrender clock

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any officer, agent, sheriff, or other living person fabricated a message, lied, or had foreknowledge of the killing. This site makes no claim that anyone aboard or tasking the aircraft named here did anything wrong. Timing conflicts are documented as conflicts, not as proof of forgery. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

On the evening of **September 11, 2025**, a message appeared on Discord from the account **zealous_monkey_55095**, which the State attributes to Tyler Robinson. It began *"Hey guys, I have bad news for you all"* and was widely reported as his confession.

The question is **when**. Court filings and most reporting date it **7:57 PM**. The screenshot's own clock reads **8:57 PM**. Meanwhile a defense filing quotes discovery showing Robinson was **read his Miranda rights at 6:25 PM** that evening at the Washington County Sheriff's Office, and asked for a lawyer at **6:26 PM**. If he was in custody, with his phone seized, who was typing?

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | The Discord appearance and the ~9 p.m. surrender are the same evening package. The timing is coincidence, or the suspect talking after he walked in. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | The 8:57-9:00 p.m. window is the one that also sits next to HADES at St. George. Move custody earlier and, investigators say, the Discord post looks timed to the aircraft. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: the federal account described a manhunt of about **33 hours**, which, counted from the shooting, ends near **9:23 PM** on September 11. Sheriff Nate Brooksby cited surrender-arranging calls at **8:02 PM and 8:04 PM**. On that clock, Robinson messaged friends on Discord and then surrendered, all in one evening. Timestamp differences can come from time zones, screenshot settings, or reporting errors. On this view, a one-hour discrepancy is noise, and any aircraft nearby is coincidence.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the court record moved the clock. Page 70 of a defense filing quotes discovery **Bates 003996-R2**, showing Miranda at **06:25 PM** and counsel invoked at **06:26 PM** ([6:25 PM custody](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession)). If county protocol seized his phone on Miranda, a message at 7:57 PM — or 8:57 PM — was not posted by him from that phone, unless the logs are wrong or another device or login was used ([Discord and custody timeline](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Discord_Custody_Timeline)).

Then the aircraft. Two independent archives show HADES-program jet **AXEL21** flying an approach to **St. George Regional Airport** at **8:57–9:00 PM**, leveling about **1,100 feet** above the runway, and going around at **9:00:45 PM** without landing ([AXEL21 over St. George](/Planes/AXEL21-St-George-Sept-11)). That window matches the screenshot's **8:57 PM**. Investigators say that once custody is moved to 6:25 PM, the confession no longer looks like a suspect talking before he walked in. It looks, to them, like a message whose timing lines up with the aircraft.

This site does not say who typed the message, and it does not say the aircraft had any role. It records that two clocks in the official story disagree with the court file, and that one of them sits beside a military flight.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The Discord message is one of the three legs of the identity case. If Robinson was already in custody when it was posted, it cannot be his confession from his phone, and the question becomes who had the login. That turns the most-quoted piece of evidence against him into an open question about someone else.

## What would settle it

* Discord's server-side login, IP, and device records for the account on September 11.
* The native Bates 003996-R1 and R2 video from WCSO, with its embedded timestamps.
* The property log showing when Robinson's phone was seized and where it was held.
* An explanation of the 7:57 PM versus 8:57 PM difference from whoever produced each.
* The tasking record for AXEL21 on the night of September 11.
