# 26. The Discord "confession" clock versus the surrender clock

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any officer, agent, sheriff, or other living person fabricated a message, lied, or had
foreknowledge of the killing. This site makes no claim that anyone aboard or tasking the aircraft named here did
anything wrong. Timing conflicts are documented as **conflicts**, not as proof of forgery. Tyler Robinson is
**charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

On the evening of **September 11, 2025**, a message appeared on Discord from the account **zealous_monkey_55095**,
which the State attributes to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). It began *"Hey guys, I have bad news for
you all"* and continued, in the screenshot later published, *"it was me at UVU yesterday. im sorry for all of this.
im surrendering through a sheriff friend in a few moments."*

The question is **when**. Court filings and most reporting date it **7:57 PM**. The screenshot's own clock reads
**8:57 PM**. A defense filing quotes discovery showing Robinson was **read his Miranda rights at 6:25 PM** that
evening at the Washington County Sheriff's Office, and asked for a lawyer at **6:26 PM**. If he was in custody,
with his phone seized, who was typing?

This is a hinge of the whole case. The [Discord confession](/Narrative/fed_narrative/discord-confession) is one of
the three legs of the identity package sold to the public. Move custody earlier and the post no longer sits next
to a walk-in. It sits next to a booking room.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | The Discord appearance and the ~9 p.m. surrender are the same evening package. The timing is coincidence, or the suspect talking after he walked in. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | The 8:57-9:00 p.m. window is the one that also sits next to HADES at St. George. Move custody earlier and, investigators say, the Discord post looks timed to the aircraft. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated at full strength: the federal account described a manhunt of about **33 hours**, which, counted from the
12:23 PM shot on September 10, ends near **9:23 PM** on September 11. That is the story of the
[33-hour manhunt](/Narrative/fed_narrative/thirty-three-hour-manhunt). Sheriff Nate Brooksby cited
surrender-arranging calls at **8:02 PM and 8:04 PM**. On that clock, Robinson messaged friends on Discord and then
surrendered, all in one evening.

The Washington Post, quoted widely on **September 15–16, 2025**, put the admission at **approximately 7:57 PM
local time**, "roughly two hours ahead of Robinson's detention." Newsweek and other outlets ran the same package:
the post is a confession, the family walked him in, the case is closed. Discord itself told reporters it had
identified an account associated with the suspect, found no evidence the incident was planned on the platform,
and later removed the account.

Timestamp differences, on this view, come from time zones, screenshot settings, or reporting errors. A one-hour
gap between 7:57 and 8:57 is noise. Any aircraft nearby is coincidence. An investigative hold can delay Miranda.
The calendar date is not printed on every excerpt of Bates 003996-R2 as circulated, so the warning could belong
to September 12 after transport. Official-side commentators treat the Discord post as the voice of a guilty man
talking to his friends before he walked in.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the court record moved the clock. Page 70 of a defense filing quotes discovery **Bates 003996-R2**,
showing Miranda at **06:25 PM** and counsel invoked at **06:26 PM**. The discovery index lists exhibit
**Bates 3996-R1** as "Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025." That is the [6:25 PM custody record](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession).
If county protocol seized his phone on Miranda, a message at 7:57 PM — or 8:57 PM — was not posted by him from
that phone, unless the logs are wrong or another device or login was used. The [Discord and custody timeline](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Discord_Custody_Timeline)
is the page that lays the two clocks next to each other.

Attorney and commentator Baron Coleman said in April 2026 that he had confirmed Robinson was in the Washington
County Sheriff's Office by **6:25 PM** (Coleman has also used **6:50 PM** in a shorter public post). Candace Owens
wrote on X that her team had reported custody before 7 PM on a **September 19, 2025** episode, and that the later
court filing confirmed it. Ryan Matta posted the same week that the Discord confession went out after custody,
and asked why the surrender video was missing. Project Constitution published the Bates pages next to the Utah
County booking sheet that lists arrest on **09/11/2025**. None of that is a finding of forgery. It is a
**conflict**: the public confession clock and the Miranda clock do not sit on the same evening in the same way.

The screenshot itself is the other clock. Ken Klippenstein published it on Substack on **September 16, 2025**.
The visible time on the confession line is **8:57 PM, September 11**. Filings and hearing testimony still say
**7:57 PM**. The [confession screenshot](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/confession-screenshot-zealous-monkey) and the
[Bates 003996-R2 page](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/bates-003996-miranda-vs-discord) keep both numbers on the table.
Investigators also note two usernames on one warrant: **zealous_monkey_55095** on the screenshot and **tylor/Ikum**
in the warrant prose. Discord's own statement that the "planning details" in early reporting were not Discord
messages at all sits in [Discord says no plotting](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/discord-says-no-plotting).

Then the aircraft. Two independent archives show HADES-program jet **AXEL21** flying an approach to
**St. George Regional Airport** at **8:57–9:00 PM**, leveling about **1,100 feet** above the runway, and going
around at **9:00:45 PM** without landing. That window matches the screenshot's **8:57 PM**. See
[AXEL21 over St. George](/Planes/AXEL21-St-George-Sept-11). Investigators say that once custody is moved to
6:25 PM, the confession no longer looks like a suspect talking before he walked in. It looks, to them, like a
message whose timing lines up with the aircraft. This site does not say who typed the message, and it does not
say the aircraft had any role. It records that two clocks in the official story disagree with the court file,
and that one of them sits beside a military flight. The step-by-step version is
[Chain: Miranda makes the Discord post impossible](/Narrative/chains/miranda-discord-impossible).

The [investigative-hold counterpoint](/court/mirandize/investigative-hold-counterpoint) is the official side's
best answer and belongs in the same reading. A delayed Miranda warning is not, by itself, a rights violation.
The question that still has to be answered is the phone: when was it seized, and from which device did the
post leave.

## When this fight ran

* **10–16 September 2025.** First framing. Official-side commentators and national outlets ran the Discord post
  as a confession two hours before surrender. Kash Patel, as FBI Director, told Congress more than 20 other
  users were in Discord chats with the alleged assassin. Discord's own "no planning on our platform" statement
  went out on September 12.
* **19 September 2025.** Candace Owens' team put custody before 7 PM on a public timeline.
* **April 8–16, 2026.** Baron Coleman published the Bates-stamped Miranda time. Owens amplified it. Project
  Constitution posted the booking sheet next to the filing.
* **11–12 May 2026.** Ryan Matta and Project Constitution pushed the "already in custody" receipts hard. Times
  of India carried Coleman's claim to a general audience.
* **July 7–8, 2026.** Preliminary hearing. The State still walked the court through a 7:57 PM Discord exhibit
  and a September 11 turn-in. Prosecutors also played booking-room video of Robinson in a maroon shirt at
  Washington County Jail. See [Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2) and
  [September 11 turn-in testimony](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/september-11-turn-in-testimony).

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The Discord message is one of the three legs of the identity case against a man who has entered **no guilty
plea**. If Robinson was already in custody when it was posted, it cannot be his confession from his phone, and
the question becomes who had the login. That turns the most-quoted piece of evidence against him into an open
question about someone else. The [faked-confession file](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/faked-confession) is the
investigators' longer write-up of that implication. This page stops at the clocks. A clock conflict is not a
forgery finding. It is a reason the [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) hub treats the confession as
unsettled.

## What would settle it

* Discord's server-side login, IP, and device records for the account on September 11.
* The native Bates 003996-R1 and R2 video from WCSO, with its embedded timestamps.
* The property log showing when Robinson's phone was seized and where it was held.
* An explanation of the 7:57 PM versus 8:57 PM difference from whoever produced each.
* The tasking record for AXEL21 on the night of September 11.
* The lobby and booking CCTV Washington County has so far declined to release. See
  [Bodycam GRAMA: no footage](/court/mirandize/bodycam-grama-no-footage).
