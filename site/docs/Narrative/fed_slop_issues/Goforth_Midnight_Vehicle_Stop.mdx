# 27. Officer Goforth's midnight vehicle stop

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that Officer Goforth, Agent David Hull, or any other officer invented,
altered, or misstated anything. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not
convicted, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The questions below are about gaps in
the public record, not accusations against any person.
:::

## The issue in plain words

About twelve hours after Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University, a gray Dodge Challenger
reportedly pulled up to the corner of Campus Drive and 800 South, on the edge of campus. A Spanish Fork
police officer named Goforth, working security that night, spoke with the driver for a short time.
Hearing coverage describes the contact as roughly 20 seconds.

The prosecution uses that contact, placed at about 12:30 AM on September 11, 2025, to put Tyler Robinson
back in Orem after midnight. It is the only item in the public record that puts his car at UVU after dark
with a named officer attached.

The trouble is timing. Staff at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, about 209 road miles south, say a man
they recognized as Robinson paid for dinner at 9:47 PM the same night. Both accounts cannot comfortably
be true on an ordinary drive.

## The fed slop side, in full

The official-side argument is simple. An officer saw a car trying to get back onto campus after
midnight, felt something was off, and wrote down part of the plate. When the case broke, he ran it, and
the registration came back to Tyler Robinson and a relative. That is independent of the family bringing
Robinson in, and it came before the surrender. It also fits the messages the state says were recovered
about retrieving the rifle from the wooded area once police left. On this view the Panguitch story is a
mistaken identification, and the stop is one more pin in a timeline that already holds.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators point first to how thin the record is. Officer Goforth did not testify at the July 2026
preliminary hearing. Everything the court heard came from Agent David Hull relaying what Goforth told
him, on Day 2 of the hearing. Hull described the reason for the note as, in his words, "cop intuition."
He said Goforth ran a "partial plate" only after a vehicle "or a vehicle matching the description" was
linked to the case, and that the driver was a male Goforth "believed" to be Robinson. No bodycam clip,
written stop report, citation, or field-interview card was shown.

On cross-examination, defense attorney Kathryn Nester had Hull confirm that the Noble household, which
supplied Ring doorbell video of the car, told investigators the driver was bald and that three other
people were in the car. Robinson is not bald, and the state's account has him alone.

Then there is the drive. From a 9:47 PM card approval in Panguitch, a normal drive to Orem takes about
3 hours 9 minutes to 3 hours 40 minutes, arriving roughly 12:56 to 1:27 AM. Reaching a 12:30 AM contact
needs about 77 mph with no stops, starting on two-lane US-89 during a statewide manhunt. Investigators say
that is not strictly impossible, but it is not the evening the state describes. Either the Panguitch
customer was someone else, the Goforth driver was someone else, or the night-of timeline has a hole.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The stop carries the whole post-midnight half of the state's timeline, including the rifle-retrieval
story. If the Panguitch receipt is his, the stop cannot be him on any normal drive, and a key
independent identification becomes a second-hand belief about a partial plate. If a central
identification can be wrong here, readers are entitled to ask what else in the package rests on
the same kind of footing.

## What would settle it

* Officer Goforth's own report, notes, and bodycam for the contact, and his sworn testimony.
* The partial plate exactly as written, and the full list of vehicles it returned.
* The native video file for the final clip of State's Exhibit 12, with its embedded timestamp.
* The Noble interview report and the full Ring export with metadata.
* Bank records for the Visa debit card that paid the Panguitch check, plus phone location data for the evening.
