# 17. Body doubles and the 2022 bodycam gait

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any officer, agent, or other living person fabricated video, planted anyone, or had foreknowledge of the killing. A body camera that stopped recording is described as an undocumented gap, not as proof of anything. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

The State says campus cameras followed one man from a grassy area near **800 West** at about **11:50 AM** on September 10, 2025, to the Losee Center roof. The pictures are not sharp enough to show a face clearly. So the identification leans on other things — clothing, a backpack, and above all the man's **walk**.

The probable-cause affidavit says the figure *"walks with a distinctive gait,"* with *"a stiff, right leg"* and a *"restricted"* ability to bend it. Supporters of the official account say that same walk appears in a **2022 traffic-stop bodycam** of [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). Citizen investigators ask why the walk became the identifier, and whether lookalikes on campus were ever ruled out. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) has said the campus maroon-shirt clips are decoys. She has also said, on the record, that she does **not** treat the Washington County surrender footage as a body double.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | The same gait as the 2022 traffic-stop bodycam proves the campus walker is Robinson. Body-double talk is panic after Owens' own clips showed him on campus. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Maroon-shirt lookalikes, bad stills, and sudden high-quality 2022 bodycam next to missing station and roof tape are the tell. The official side moved to gait when 4K roof ID failed. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: gait is a real forensic signal. A stiff right leg is unusual, and it is hard to fake for a whole walk across campus. If older footage of Robinson shows the same movement, that is independent support for the identification.

At the July 2026 preliminary hearing, Agent David Hull testified that the figure returning to campus around **12:15 PM** wore different clothes but the same shoes, and was *"walking with a gait or a limp."* Hearing coverage and official-side commentators treated that limp as a rifle concealed in a pant leg — a "reasonable inference," in the later defense-brief paraphrase that those same commentators then quoted back as if it were a confession. A TPUSA-adjacent spokesman posted Discord lines attributed to Robinson telling friends the surveillance stills were him, plus a co-worker telling the *Washington Post* that Robinson often walked with his fists locked.

On this view, [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)' own campus clips were said to show him that day, so body-double theories are what people reach for once they have to admit he was there. The 2022 fender-bender bodycam, they say, is simply older video of the same walk. The [fed claim that the rooftop figure is Robinson](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson) treats the walk, the clothes sequence, and the Discord "it was me" lines as a closed identification.

Hearing-week coverage filled in the State's movement story around that walk. Hull described a first arrival around **8:30 AM**, trips off campus, a Chick-fil-A stop, a return after **11 AM** in different clothes, and the parking-structure stairs where the limp is most visible. A local-TV reporter in the room wrote that even zoomed in, the video quality was "surprisingly high," and that a later clip of the figure in jeans showed "that very distinct limp with the rifle likely in the leg of his pants." That last clause is a reporter's inference from a non-public exhibit. Official-side commentators ran it as fact. The [UVU surveillance page](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance) is where this site keeps what is actually public.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators first point to the State's own testimony. At the hearing, Hull described a *"noticeable gait or limp"* on the walk in. Asked about the figure on the roof after it stood up, he said: *"there is no longer a visible limp or gait"* ([Day 2 transcript](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2)). Fox News' own hearing write-up recorded the same point: investigators noted the limp was no longer visible after the shooting. A walk offered as a signature disappears in the minute that matters most. The [chain that the rooftop figure is not identified as Robinson](/Narrative/chains/rooftop-figure-not-robinson) starts there.

Second, the clothing does not stay still. Earlier that day the figure attributed to Robinson wears a **maroon T-shirt**; the affidavit's campus and rooftop description is a **long-sleeve black shirt with an American flag and eagle**. Early witness accounts describe **black tactical clothing** ([stairs figure identity gap](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap); [black-clad eyewitness](/Tyler_Robinson/eyewitness-black-clad-fugitive); [FBI black-clothing photo discrepancy](/FBI/Black_Clothing_Photo)). A maroon shirt is also common enough that other young men on campus fit it.

Owens said so in public, twice, during the hearing week. On **8 July 2026** she posted that *"MANY young men"* in maroon shirts and light shorts were on campus that day, that she had archived a folder of them, and that she had identified two. On **9 July 2026** she corrected a Charlie Kirk Show producer who claimed she thought the [surrender footage](/Tyler_Robinson/Surrender) was a body double: she does not. Her claim is that the *campus* maroon clips are decoys, and that the blur is the point. On **11 August 2026** she posted uncompressed parking-garage stills and said the crash-out over those frames was a concession that the figure does not look like Robinson.

Third, the footage that would settle identity is the footage that is missing. The officer who reached the roof testified his **body camera had stopped recording** ([Bagley bodycam gap](/CoverUp/Bagley_Bodycam_Gap); [officer-Bagley page](/Law_Enforcement/officer-bagley-bodycam)). Owens, on **16 July 2026**, said officers from other departments called an Axon Body Cam 4 dying at that exact moment implausible. The surrender video from the Washington County station was later described as deleted under a retention policy ([sheriff video withheld](/CoverUp/Sheriff_Video_Withheld)). Investigators say a clear 2022 clip surfacing while those recordings stay absent is the wrong way round. Owens returned to that 2022 fender-bender bodycam on **16 September 2026**, saying it "deserves a second glance" and was "suddenly REALLY bothering me." This site has not verified the 2022 bodycam comparison itself.

Owens' further claim, stated as hers, is that some of the maroon-shirt men may have been using a military "color of the day" to pass as civilians. That is a hypothesis, not a finding of this site, and this page does not name any such unit or person. What the page does repeat is the observation she put on camera: multiple young men, same color, same day, some holding maroon shirts as if exchanging them, and stills too poor for a capital identification.

The [UVU campus surveillance](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance) compilation is stills and cuts, not a continuous identified body. The [enhanced suspect photos](/FBI/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos) and the [evidence-footage](/FBI/Evidence_Footage) fights are the same problem at a different zoom: identity asserted, native files not released. [Witness-footage pressure](/Censorship/Witness_Footage_Pressure) is how independent copies of the walk get taken down. Official-side commentators moved to gait after the "4K roof" claim failed to convince people who were in the same room. Investigators say that move is the tell.

The identification stack, in the order the State used it:

* **Walk.** Affidavit: stiff right leg, restricted bend. Hull at the hearing: limp on the way in; no limp once the rooftop figure stands up.
* **Clothes.** Maroon T-shirt and shorts in the morning scout clips. Long-sleeve black shirt with flag and eagle, plus jeans, in the later campus and roof description. 911 callers: all-black tactical. Same shoes, Hull said, across the clothes change.
* **Older tape.** A 2022 traffic-stop / fender-bender bodycam of Robinson, offered as the same walk, and put back into circulation in September 2026.
* **Missing tape.** Bagley's rooftop bodycam stops. Washington County surrender video described as deleted. Native campus files not released.

## When this fight ran

* **12–18 September 2025, then 18 October 2025.** Official-side accounts posted campus limp clips as the shooter walking in. The gait was already being sold as identity.
* **7–10 July 2026, preliminary hearing.** Hull's limp testimony, the rifle-in-the-pant-leg inference, and Owens' maroon-decoy posts ran in the same 72 hours. Official-side commentators treated the hearing video as the close.
* **11–17 August 2026.** Uncompressed maroon stills versus official-side quotes from the defense brief about a gait "impaired by something long in his pant leg."
* **16–17 September 2026.** Owens put the 2022 bodycam back on the table. Independent posters asked whether campus "imposters" had been walked against that older tape.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The identity case, by the defense's own account, rests on the roommate texts, the Discord messages, and a surveillance identification. If the surveillance leg depends on a walk that changes on the roof and a shirt that changes on the video, it is weaker than it is presented, and an innocent lookalike becomes a real possibility. [Proof it was not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) is built on that gap, not on a cartoon of hospital doubles.

## What would settle it

* The full, native-resolution campus video, continuous rather than cut to stills.
* A published gait analysis by a named examiner, with its method and error rate.
* The 2022 bodycam, its source, and when it entered the case file.
* The officer's rooftop body-camera device logs and the WCSO arrival video.
* A public accounting of every maroon-shirt figure Owens and others archived that day.
