# 4. The names of the crew aboard N1098L on September 10, 2025

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site **does not publish any N1098L crew names** and makes **no claim that any person was aboard
N1098L on September 10, 2025, or did anything**. This site makes no claim that any crew member, operator,
contractor, or agency committed a crime or had any role in or knowledge of Charlie Kirk's death. Tyler
Robinson is charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

**N1098L** is a **Bombardier Global 6500** jet operated by **LASAI Aviation II LLC**, a US Army
intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) contractor. It is publicly documented as part of the
Army's **HADES** program. On September 10, 2025 it flew two low passes into the Utah Valley, the second one
about 25 minutes after Charlie Kirk was shot.

People naturally asked: **who was flying it, and who was aboard?** Lists of names began to circulate online.
This issue is about those lists. Are they real, or are they made up? And what should a careful reader do
with them?

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Crew-name lists circulating online are AI-fabricated or unverified fan fiction. No public crew field exists, so stop naming people. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Published lists should be checked against manifests and FOIA, not memory-holed as "AI." Until records are produced the crew question stays open. This site does not republish the names or claim any listed person was aboard or did anything. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says the crew-name lists are **AI-fabricated or unverified fan fiction**. There is no
public crew field for this aircraft, so anyone naming people is guessing, and naming private people in
connection with a killing can do real harm. The advice is simple: stop naming people.

That position has a solid core. Flight trackers such as FlightAware and Flightradar24 **do not publish
personnel** for private special-mission jets. Crew identities for this sortie have **not been publicly
confirmed** by anyone with records.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators agree the names are **unverified**, and this site agrees too. That is why no
names appear here. Where they part ways with the fed-slop side is the next step. Investigators say a
published list should be **checked against manifests and FOIA records**, not simply waved away as "AI." An
unverified list is an open question, not a closed one.

The records that would answer it exist somewhere: a **crew manifest**, a **tasking order**, a **sensor log**.
Because LASAI is a defense contractor, those records sit behind doors the public cannot open. Until they are
produced, the crew question stays open. That is not an accusation against anyone. It is the ordinary
demand that a question be answered with documents.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

No ADS-B track, tasking order, or sensor product from N1098L has appeared in the prosecution of
Tyler Robinson, even though the flight overlapped the shooting window. If the aircraft carried sensors over
the area, the government may hold airborne records of the scene. Whoever flew it could say what was
recorded. Investigators say a case that never asks is a case that does not want the answer, and that the
crew question belongs to the evidence, not to gossip.

## What would settle it

* The FAA and operator crew manifest for the September 10, 2025 sorties.
* The HADES mission tasking order for that day, and who authorized it.
* Any sensor or collection logs covering Utah County that day, and whether they were preserved.
* Written confirmation, under oath, of who was aboard, taken by a body with subpoena power.
