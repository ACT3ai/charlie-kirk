# 55. Kash Patel and the federal seal on DNA and a lone actor

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says Kash Patel, the FBI, or any living person committed a crime, lied, or had
foreknowledge of the killing. Changes in how evidence was described are treated as open discrepancies,
not as findings of fabrication. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty
plea, and is presumed innocent. His roommate, Lance Twiggs, has been charged with nothing, and this
site makes no claim that Lance Twiggs did anything wrong.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk was killed on **September 10, 2025**. Within days, FBI Director [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel)
spoke publicly about the physical evidence. On **September 15, 2025**, on Fox & Friends, he described a
screwdriver as recovered **on the roof** with DNA tied to it. DNA on the towel wrapped around the rifle
was also described publicly. His words, as reported: the DNA hits from the towel and the screwdriver
were "positively processed for the suspect in custody." The *New York Times* carried the same briefing
that day.

For many people, the head of the FBI saying this was the final word. This issue asks whether a federal
official repeating a charging theory is the same thing as an independent check of it. The [FBI
hub](/FBI/overview) and the [Kash Patel briefings](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings) page are the place to
read the statements in order.

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: the FBI runs one of the best forensic labs in the world. When its Director says DNA
links the defendant to the towel and the screwdriver, he is summarizing lab work, not guessing. Two
separate agencies, state and federal, looking at the same evidence and reaching the same conclusion is
exactly what a careful investigation should produce. Continuing to doubt it means doubting the
country's top law-enforcement lab.

At the July 2026 preliminary hearing, official-side commentators circulated numbers they said came
from courtroom DNA testimony: on the towel, roughly 95 percent Robinson and 5 percent
[Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs); on the screwdriver, roughly 89 percent Robinson and 11 percent
Twiggs. Hearing coverage also said DNA from the rifle, cartridges, and an engraving tool matched
Robinson. On this view, Patel's September 15 statement was simply the first public cut of a lab result
the courtroom later filled in.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with the screwdriver, because its description moved. According to the
[court testimony page](/court/testimony), CNN on **September 11** placed it **near the rifle** in the
woods. Patel on **September 15** placed it **on the roof** with DNA. The **September 16** charging
documents reportedly **omitted** it entirely. The
[screwdriver page](/Gun_Bullet/Screwdriver_Near_Rifle_To_Roof) explains why the location matters: in
the woods it is a disposal tool, on the roof it becomes proof of presence.

Second, DNA shows contact, not who fired. Hearing coverage summarized on the
[more reasons page](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons) reports forensic testimony that both
Robinson and his roommate left DNA on the rifle and the screwdriver. The two shared a home. See
[DNA does not prove a shot](/Narrative/chains/dna-does-not-prove-a-shot). A household rifle with two
household profiles on it is not a muzzle flash. See also [DNA and the rifle](/Narrative/fed_narrative/dna-and-the-rifle).

Third, the lone-actor framing sits against Patel's own words. On **September 16, 2025**, he told the
Senate Judiciary Committee the FBI was investigating more than twenty Discord users and the
possibility of accomplices ([lone gunman](/Narrative/fed_narrative/lone-gunman)). And former NCTC
director Joe Kent has said his center's review of foreign-nexus leads was **ordered to stop**
([Joe Kent](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown)). Investigators argue that once
the federal side endorsed the Utah theory, leads such as the [aircraft over the Provo corridor](/Planes/overview)
and the [Panguitch receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt) were never publicly run down.
See the [not-a-lone-gunman chain](/Narrative/chains/not-a-lone-gunman) and the
[manhunt timeline](/Timeline/kash-patel-manhunt-timeline).

The [ATF fragment](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive) is the other federal lab in this story. It
did not tool-mark the metal in Kirk to the court rifle. A federal seal on DNA-on-a-towel does not
close that gap.

## When this fight ran

* **15 September 2025.** Patel on Fox & Friends: DNA on the towel and on a rooftop screwdriver,
"positively processed for the suspect in custody." The rifle, he said, was still at ATF in Maryland.
* **16 September 2025.** Charging documents. Senate testimony on Discord users and possible
accomplices. The screwdriver, investigators say, drops out of the charging paper.
* **21 September 2025.** Patel said the Bureau was examining theories including accomplices, Discord,
shot angle, and a plane. The public posture still read as a closed lone-actor case.
* **7–11 July 2026.** Hearing week. DNA percentages on towel and screwdriver were circulated as the
federal period at the end of the sentence.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

A federal endorsement makes the case look doubly confirmed. But if the FBI is repeating the state's
theory rather than testing it, there is only one check, not two. For a defendant who may be framed,
independent review is what matters, and investigators say that review has not happened in public.

The [gun-and-bullet hub](/Gun_Bullet/overview) and the [proof-intel-services hub](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview)
are where a reader can still ask what a federal lab actually produced.

## What would settle it

* The FBI lab reports on the towel, rifle and screwdriver, including every DNA profile found, not only matches.
* Body-camera or photo documentation of where and when the screwdriver was found.
* An explanation for why the screwdriver was left out of the charging documents.
* A public statement on whether the Discord accomplice inquiry was closed, and on what basis.
* Any record that the Panguitch receipt or the aircraft leads were assigned to agents.
