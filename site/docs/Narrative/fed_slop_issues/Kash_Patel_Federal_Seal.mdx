# 55. Kash Patel and the federal seal on DNA and a lone actor

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says Kash Patel, the FBI, or any living person committed a crime, lied, or had foreknowledge of the killing. Changes in how evidence was described are treated as open discrepancies, not as findings of fabrication. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. His roommate, Lance Twiggs, has been charged with nothing, and this site makes no claim that Lance Twiggs did anything wrong.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk was killed on **September 10, 2025**. Within days, FBI Director [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel) spoke publicly about the physical evidence. On **September 15, 2025**, on Fox News, he described a screwdriver as recovered **on the roof** with DNA tied to it. DNA on the towel wrapped around the rifle was also described publicly.

For many people, the head of the FBI saying this was the final word. This issue asks whether a federal official repeating a charging theory is the same thing as an independent check of it.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | The FBI Director confirming DNA on the towel and screwdriver is the federal period at the end of the sentence. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | A federal seal on a Utah charging theory is the opposite of independent review. It is why the plane and the receipt never got a real tasking. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: the FBI runs one of the best forensic labs in the world. When its Director says DNA links the defendant to the towel and the screwdriver, he is summarizing lab work, not guessing. Two separate agencies, state and federal, looking at the same evidence and reaching the same conclusion is exactly what a careful investigation should produce. Continuing to doubt it means doubting the country's top law-enforcement lab.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with the screwdriver, because its description moved. According to the [court testimony page](/court/testimony), CNN on **September 11** placed it **near the rifle** in the woods. Patel on **September 15** placed it **on the roof** with DNA. The **September 16** charging documents reportedly **omitted** it entirely. The [screwdriver page](/Gun_Bullet/Screwdriver_Near_Rifle_To_Roof) explains why the location matters: in the woods it is a disposal tool, on the roof it becomes proof of presence.

Second, DNA shows contact, not who fired. Hearing coverage summarized on the [more reasons page](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons) reports forensic testimony that both Robinson and his roommate left DNA on the rifle and the screwdriver. The two shared a home. See [DNA does not prove a shot](/Narrative/chains/dna-does-not-prove-a-shot).

Third, the lone-actor framing sits against Patel's own words. On **September 16, 2025**, he told the Senate Judiciary Committee the FBI was investigating more than twenty Discord users and the possibility of accomplices ([lone gunman](/Narrative/fed_narrative/lone-gunman)). And former NCTC director Joe Kent has said his center's review of foreign-nexus leads was **ordered to stop** ([Joe Kent](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown)). Investigators argue that once the federal side endorsed the Utah theory, leads such as the aircraft over the Provo corridor and the Panguitch receipt were never publicly run down.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

A federal endorsement makes the case look doubly confirmed. But if the FBI is repeating the state's theory rather than testing it, there is only one check, not two. For a defendant who may be framed, independent review is what matters, and investigators say that review has not happened in public.

## What would settle it

* The FBI lab reports on the towel, rifle and screwdriver, including every DNA profile found, not only matches.
* Body-camera or photo documentation of where and when the screwdriver was found.
* An explanation for why the screwdriver was left out of the charging documents.
* A public statement on whether the Discord accomplice inquiry was closed, and on what basis.
* Any record that the Panguitch receipt or the aircraft leads were assigned to agents.
