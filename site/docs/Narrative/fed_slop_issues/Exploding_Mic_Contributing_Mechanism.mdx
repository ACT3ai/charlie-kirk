# 34. An exploding microphone, or electroshock, as a contributing mechanism

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that any living person or organization placed, supplied, triggered, or
knew of any device. This site makes no claim that anyone on the AV team, the security team, or TPUSA
staff knew of, placed, triggered, or removed any device. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he
is charged, not convicted, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was wearing a small [RØDE wireless microphone](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) clipped to his shirt when he was struck at
12:23 PM on September 10, 2025. The government's narrative is that a single .30-06 rifle round fired
from the Losee Center roof killed him.

Most citizen investigators have reached a different conclusion. After months of frame-by-frame video
study, audio analysis, and review of the injuries quoted in court filings, they conclude he was killed
by an explosive device at the microphone. That is the investigators' conclusion, and the evidence for it
is collected on this site's [Exploding Mic hub](/Mic/overview) and the [shaped-charge chain](/Narrative/chains/shaped-charge-microphone).

A separate claim, that electricity or electroshock caused or contributed to the death, also circulates.
It is uncorroborated. It is not the investigators' finding, and this page does not present it as one. See [Electrocution as Trigger](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/electrocution-trigger).

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says the medical examiner ruled the death a homicide by gunshot wound of the neck.
Fragments consistent with a .30-caliber bullet jacket were recovered in the autopsy. A rifle was found in
the woods, and a spent casing is tied to it. A campus officer testified he recognized gunfire and found a prone pad on the roof.

Fact-checkers labeled the microphone theory false. A September 8, 2026 fact-check cycle said Utah's medical examiner certified a gunshot to the neck, that examiners recovered a fragment, and that they found no explosive residue or shrapnel from a detonated device. Official-side commentators after the July 6, 2026 hearing used "GSW neck, fragments, not a freaking exploding microphone" as a one-line close. Earlier, in June 2026, similar fact-checks said investigators had found no evidence an exploding lavalier caused the death.

On this view there is no need for a device of any kind. Consumer wireless packs cannot function as shaped charges; they lack the explosive compound, liner, and geometry. Shirt "puff" is hydrostatic cavitation from a bullet. Two bangs are echoes off campus buildings. The burnt object in the SUV is debris, not a transmitter. The theory exists to muddy a clear case against [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). Electrocution and directed-energy talk, they say, is the same decay one step further out.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the official "no explosive signature" means no residue test was ever made public, not
that one came back clean. The courtyard soil was dug out and paved within days — [10 inches of dirt removed](/CoverUp/Dirt_Removed) and [Removing Explosive Residue](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview). The transport SUV was
reportedly destroyed, as on [SUV Destroyed](/CoverUp/SUV_Destroyed). The clothing was taken, with no published residue test on any of them. [Cover-up: the exploding microphone theory](/CoverUp/Mic_Explosive) collects those gaps as claims, not as a finding that any named person destroyed evidence.

Their positive case rests on several separate lines:

* **Video.** Frame analysis shows the shirt pushing outward before the neck wound appears. That work is on [Video Frame Analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) and [Six Forensic Physics Phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **Audio.** Analysis of the [Canon XA55](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) production audio is argued to show two events, with the louder one at the tent, where the microphone was, not at the roof. The same split is on [Acoustic Two-Event Split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split). Project Constitution spent April 2026 amplifying Jon Bray's uncompressed-audio timestamps: a distant muzzle at ~120 meters and a louder detonation ~46 meters from the camera, at the tent.
* **Witnesses.** Early witnesses, including combat veterans, described what they saw as looking like an explosion. Rob O'Neill's NEWSMAX cut is on ["That Looked Like an Explosion"](/Mic/oneill-looked-like-an-explosion). The earliest on-camera accounts said chest or heart, collected at [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart).
* **Hardware.** Photos of the SUV interior show a burnt object some believe is part of the microphone transmitter — [Burnt Object — RØDE Transmitter?](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter). Stew Peters and Project Constitution pointed to glass or plastic shards in the same vehicle. A DoD-contract thread on miniature anti-personnel charges at Accurate Energetic Systems is on [The DoD Contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract).
* **The wound.** Contained, no clean exit, "miracle" stop reached the public only second-hand. Investigators say that fits a small charge at the collar better than a high-energy rifle round. See [why it was not a .30-06](/Narrative/chains/not-a-thirty-06).

They also point to what is missing. No public video shows a muzzle flash or recoil on the roof at the
moment of the shot. Candace Owens and Project Constitution kept this file open through 2026; Jimmy Dore summarized the same stack in June 2026 (shirt puff, PETN, paved courtyard, cleaned SUV). [Explosive in Mic Likely Killed Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie) is the pillar page for why that conclusion clears Robinson.

The hardware argument is specific. Charlie was wearing a [RØDE Wireless PRO](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) under the shirt, against the chest — placement an AV staffer later described as ordinary, and the placement every contact-device theory needs. Investigators do not say a consumer battery "became a bomb by itself." They say a small directional charge can be built into or next to a pack that size, and they point to a [DoD miniaturized-charge contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract) and the later [AES factory explosion](/CoverUp/AES_Tennessee_Explosion) as the procurement file that should be forced open. Until residue is tested, "no explosive signature" is an absence of a published test.

Electrocution stays in a different box. Some threads pair a shock with a charge, or substitute electricity for a rifle. No agency has reported electrical injury. No published pathology describes it. Investigators do not treat it as their finding.

## When this fight ran

* **10–16 September 2025.** Shirt-bulge and "inside job" posts. Official line: gunshot. Early fact-checks treat a "blood bag" / fake-death variant as false and note the bulge is a microphone.
* **Late September–October 2025.** Veteran "looked like an explosion" clips. Ryan Matta and others push the unsourced miracle-bullet hospital line.
* **January–April 2026.** SUV-shard and Canon XA55 audio work. Project Constitution and Stew Peters on glass, AES, and two-event sound. Official-side "mic bomb is garbage" replies harden.
* **June–July 2026.** Fact-checks label the theory false. July 6 hearing: ME homicide by GSW, fragments recovered, used as a microphone-theory killer.
* **August 2026.** Physics-phenomena thread; debate circuit treats "firearms, video, DNA" as having won in public.
* **8 September 2026.** Fact-check cycle again: ME certified GSW, no explosive residue. Investigators answer that residue was never published because the test surfaces were removed.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If an explosive at the microphone killed Charlie Kirk, then whatever happened on the roof did not cause
his death. Robinson is charged with aggravated murder for a rifle shot. A different mechanism would mean
the charge rests on the wrong cause of death, and that the person responsible has not been identified. That is the core of [Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview) and [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview).

## What would settle it

* Independent residue testing of the clothing, the microphone parts, and any SUV debris still held.
* The full autopsy report and wound photographs, reviewed by independent forensic pathologists and blast experts.
* The raw multitrack audio from every recording device at the event.
* The recovered microphone and transmitter, or a record of what happened to them.
