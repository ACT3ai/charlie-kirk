# 37. Blake Neff's hospital and aftermath role

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes no claim that Blake Neff did anything wrong, had any foreknowledge of the killing, or
played any role in it. This site makes no claim that TPUSA, Erika Kirk, or any staff member knew of or
had any role in the killing. Timing differences in the worst minutes of people's lives are ordinary.
Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not convicted, has not entered a guilty plea, and is
presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Blake Neff is a producer of The Charlie Kirk Show and a TPUSA team member. He was at Utah Valley
University on September 10, 2025, and has since given public accounts of the minutes after the shot.
Because he was there, and because he speaks publicly, his account is one of the pieces people use to
rebuild the timeline.

Two kinds of questions have been raised. The first is about timing: where he was in the first minutes,
and when he reached Timpanogos Regional Hospital, where Charlie was taken. The second is about his
later public stance toward independent investigators.

Neither question is an accusation. They are requests for a clearer record.

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says Blake Neff was a colleague and friend who lived through a horror. People in shock
do not keep perfect clocks, and small differences between their memories and a video frame prove nothing.
He and others have spoken publicly to defend Charlie's family and organization from what they see as
false and cruel accusations. On this view, turning his grief and his public statements into a timeline
puzzle is harassment of the people who were closest to Charlie that day.

## What citizen investigators say

The best-documented question is about one frame. Neff has publicly said he witnessed Mikey McCoy's calls
to Erika Kirk and then to Rob McCoy. A second-by-second reconstruction of the video, starting at the
shot at about 12:23:30 PM, has the SUV loaded at about 12:24:01. At about 12:24:02 McCoy is seen well
past the SUV, and Neff is not in the frame. Investigators ask how, precisely, he witnessed both calls. In
late October 2025, Neff and Andrew Kolvet publicly described McCoy reacting in shock and moving to call
Erika Kirk and then Rob McCoy.

Investigators also ask when Neff reached the hospital. The SUV is estimated to have arrived there
between about 12:29 and 12:35 PM. No public record gives Neff's arrival time or how he got there.

On his public posture, commentary notes that he has taken part in livestreams and threads addressing
what TPUSA described as false accusations, and has publicly responded to independent investigations
such as Candace Owens'. Investigators say public criticism of their work is his right, and that it is also a
reason to put his own timeline on the record.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The first minutes after the shot are when the evidence about what happened at the tent was freshest. A
clear, documented account from the people standing there helps every side, including a defendant who
says he did not do this. Unresolved gaps in those minutes leave room for doubt about the official
account.

## What would settle it

* Phone records and call logs for the calls he says he witnessed, with times.
* Full native video of the courtyard and SUV area for the first two minutes.
* Timpanogos Regional Hospital visitor or security records, and his own stated arrival time.
* A sworn statement from him on his position and movements from 12:23 to 12:40 PM.
