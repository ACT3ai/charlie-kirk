# 29. Identity of the campus "scout" walker

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that any investigator, agency, or commentator misrepresented evidence or
acted improperly. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not convicted, has not entered
a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The question here is what the public footage can and cannot show.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Before the shooting, campus cameras recorded a man walking around [UVU](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU).
The state calls this the scouting trip. Per Agent David Hull's hearing testimony, this man wore a maroon
or red T-shirt, gray or olive shorts, and Converse shoes. He visited the courtyard, bought food, went into
the wooded area across Campus Drive, and went to the railing that gives access to the Losee Center roof,
all before walking off campus just before **11:00 AM**.

If that walker is [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson), it shows planning, and it links him to the
exact roof and the exact woods. If it is not him, or cannot be shown to be him, the case loses its best
morning evidence.

How was the walker identified? Hull told the court he recognized the person from video. Official-side
commentators went further and compared the walker's gait, build, and clothing with a 2022 police
body-camera clip of Robinson. The public campus cameras are catalogued at
[UVU campus surveillance](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance).

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says identification does not need a perfect face. Investigators combine many clues:
the car with its distinctive wheels, the route, the shoes, the build, and how the man moves. The 2022
bodycam gives a clean reference for how Robinson walks and carries himself, and the campus walker
matches it. The walker then goes to the very railing and the very woods that feature in the shooting.

Hull's Day 2 testimony is the state's best version. The driver exits the garage around 8:30 AM in a
red T-shirt, shorts, and shoes. Hull: he believes that is Tyler Robinson. The same clothing, plus a
blue backpack, returns just after 10:00 AM from the neighborhood to the northeast. Hull describes
"a maroon colored t-shirt, gray or olive colored shorts, and then Converse shoes." The man eats at
Chick-fil-A, dumps the backpack in the woods, checks the roof railing, and leaves before 11:00 AM.

On this view, demanding a studio-quality face is a way of refusing any evidence at all. Commentators
who attended the July 2026 hearing said they saw enhanced HD, some of it in 4K, with a crystal-clear
face, plate, clothes, and rooftop actions. Fox and other official-side outlets treated "maroon shirt
lookalikes" as a conspiracy theory the compilation had already killed. The maroon shirt at surrender,
they say, is the same maroon shirt on campus. Identity is closed. See
[Fed claim: the rooftop figure is Robinson](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson).

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with the footage the public has seen. It is distant, compressed campus video. Hull
himself told the court that in the parking-garage footage, **"some individuals" had their faces blurred**.
They say a lookalike in a common outfit, a maroon T-shirt and shorts, at that resolution, is not the
kind of identification that should carry a death-penalty case. The mismatch table is
[Stairs Guy identity gap](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap) and
[Stairs Guy vs Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/Stairs_Guy).

They also point to the order of the arguments. After the July 2026 hearing, some commentators who
attended said they had seen enhanced video, some of it in 4K, with a clear face. Other same-day attendees
disputed that, saying most clips were already public and none showed a face-clear shot. No 4K face
footage has been released. Investigators note that the public argument then shifted to gait and build,
which they see as a sign the face claim could not be backed up. Kash Patel, as FBI Director, has been
quoted acknowledging that stairwell images were "enhanced." That fight lives on
[Enhanced suspect photos](/FBI/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos).

Gait cuts both ways. Hull testified the afternoon figure walked with a noticeable limp on the way in,
and ran without one on the roof. Investigators ask which gait the 2022 bodycam is supposed to match.
A morning scout in shorts, an afternoon figure in a dark long sleeve and jeans with a limp, and a roof
runner with no limp, is three movement profiles. Official-side commentators collapsed them into one
body. Citizen investigators treat that collapse as the splice. The chain is
[Chain: the rooftop figure is not identified as Robinson](/Narrative/chains/rooftop-figure-not-robinson).

Project Constitution's reply to a hearing attendee who claimed crystal-clear 4K was blunt: the video
was blurry, distant, and half the time the head was down or turned away. Candace Owens and other
investigators have also noted that the scout kit — T-shirt, shorts, Converse — is the wrong kit for
the 911 tactical description. That clothing fight is the next issue. None of this states that a
second living person committed a crime. It states that the public footage does not close identity.

## When this fight ran

* **10–16 September 2025.** First stills of a campus walker in a maroon shirt. Official-side accounts
  named Robinson immediately.
* **July 7–11, 2026.** Preliminary hearing. Hull IDs the walker from video. Official-side commentators
  in the room claimed 4K. Other attendees said no. Fox ran a "maroon shirts" debunk.
* **After the 4K claim stalled.** Gait and the 2022 bodycam became the public substitute for a face.
  That is the same window as [Fed Slop Issue 17](/Narrative/fed_slop_issues/Body_Doubles_And_Bodycam_Gait)
  and [Fed Slop Issue 59](/Narrative/fed_slop_issues/Campus_Video_Face_ID_Quality).

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The scout walk is how the state ties Robinson to the roof and the woods before anything happened. If the
walker is identified only by outfit and resemblance, a second person in similar clothes fits the same
footage. Investigators say that is exactly the gap a frame would need. He is charged, not convicted.
The [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) hub and the
[Tyler Robinson not assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview) overview both start from identity
that has not been shown at face resolution.

## What would settle it

* Release of the native, full-resolution campus footage behind State's Exhibit 12, with metadata.
* Any enhanced or 4K frames the state relies on, shown in open court or to independent experts.
* A qualified forensic gait comparison, disclosed with its method and error rate, covering morning
  scout, afternoon limp, roof run, and the 2022 bodycam.
* Purchase records from the Chick-fil-A visit, which would name the card used.
* Unblurred garage frames, or a statement of why faces in that clip were blurred.
