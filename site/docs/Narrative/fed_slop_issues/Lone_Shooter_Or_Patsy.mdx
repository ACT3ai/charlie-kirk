# 6. Is Tyler Robinson the lone shooter, or a patsy?

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **claimed to be the shooter**. He is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty
plea, and is presumed innocent. No agency or official named here has been found to have concealed
anything. This site makes no claim that any living person or organization committed a crime, recruited
anyone, or had foreknowledge of Charlie Kirk's death.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Within days of Charlie Kirk's death on September 10, 2025, the public was given a finished story:
one radicalized 22-year-old, one rifle, one rooftop, no one else. That is the **lone gunman** account.

Many citizen investigators think the story is too neat. They use the word **patsy**: a person set up to take
the blame for something bigger. This issue sets the two views side by side. It is the big question that most
of the other issues on this list feed into.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Open-and-shut lone wolf. DNA on rifle, trigger, casings, towel and screwdriver; a multi-camera campus track; roommate texts and the keyboard note; engraved casings; grandpa's Mauser. Acted alone. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Patsy or an incomplete package. No aiming or firing video, a tactical 911 description that does not match the T-shirt scout, a possible second origin of the wound, and a possible recruitment or informant overlay. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says this is **open and shut**. FBI Director Kash Patel said DNA consistent with
Robinson was found on the **trigger** and other parts of the rifle, on the **fired casing**, on **two of three
unfired cartridges**, on the **towel** the rifle was wrapped in, and on a **screwdriver** from the roof. Campus
cameras tracked a figure across UVU. Texts to his roommate and a note **under the keyboard** read like
admissions. Casings were reportedly **engraved** with slogans. The rifle was **his grandfather's Mauser**.

This is the state's strongest ground, and it should be stated at full strength. DNA on a fired casing is
harder to explain away than DNA on a household gun. Lone-actor political violence is also common in the
United States, and investigators say "we are looking at accomplices" in the first week of almost every big
case.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say the package is **incomplete**, and some say it describes a patsy. Their main
points, each covered in its own issue on this list:

* **No video of anyone aiming or firing.** The public footage shows a distant figure, not a shot.
* **The clothing does not match.** 911 callers and dispatch described **all-black tactical** gear, a helmet,
  and a mask. The campus scout videos show **a T-shirt and jeans or shorts**.
* **The wound story does not close.** The state's own ATF lab could not tie the recovered fragment to the
  rifle, and most citizen investigators conclude the fatal event was an
  [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview), not a rooftop rifle round.
* **Accomplices were on the table.** On **September 16, 2025**, Patel told the Senate the FBI was looking at
  more than **20 Discord users** and the possibility of accomplices. The work clearing them has not been shown.

Investigators also raise a possible **recruitment or informant overlay**, meaning the question of whether
anyone steered Robinson. That remains a question, not a finding.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

This is the whole case in one line. DNA on a family rifle proves contact with a family rifle, not
that he fired it at a person on September 10. If the shot, the clothing, and the mechanism of death do not
fit him, then the rest of the package points somewhere else, and a young man could be carrying the weight
of an event he did not carry out.

## What would settle it

* Full DNA lab reports: mixture interpretation, number of contributors, and statistical weights for every item.
* Latent fingerprint and gunshot-residue results, including any that were never attempted.
* Native-resolution campus and rooftop video, untrimmed.
* The disposition of all 20-plus Discord users the FBI said it was investigating.
* The raw audio and the medical examiner's full report, tested by independent experts.
