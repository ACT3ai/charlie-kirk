# 1. Did Tyler Robinson eat at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch on the night of September 10, 2025?

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The
Panguitch identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**. It has not been authenticated in
court. Nothing on this page says any restaurant employee, owner, agent, or officer said anything untrue.
This site makes no claim that any living person or organization committed a crime or had foreknowledge of
Charlie Kirk's death.
:::

## The issue in plain words

[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed at Utah Valley University in Orem at **12:23 PM on
September 10, 2025**. Citizen investigators conclude the fatal event was an
[explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview). The government's charging story is a rooftop .30-06 fired
by [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson), and it keeps him in the Orem area for the rest of that day
and night. The main proof of that evening is a set of text messages to his roommate, Lance Twiggs, that
reportedly describe police, dogs, and a rifle hidden in the woods near campus.

Then staff at **Cowboy's Smokehouse** in **Panguitch** came forward. Panguitch is a small town in Garfield
County, about **200 to 209 road miles** south of Orem, roughly **3 hours 10 minutes to 3 hours 40 minutes**
of driving. It is **not on the way** from Orem to St. George, Robinson's home area. A stop there is a
detour. Servers say a quiet young man matching the FBI photos ate a steak alone at the counter that night.
A photographed ticket, **Check #211**, shows an order at **8:55 PM** and a Visa Debit payment at
**9:47 PM** on 9/10/25.

If the man at that counter was Tyler Robinson, he could not also have been in Orem that evening sending
the texts the state relies on. Geography does not bend.

## The fed slop side, in full

The strongest version of the official-narrative case is **mistaken identity**. A small-town staff sees
FBI photos the next day, remembers a quiet young man, and becomes sure. That is how false identifications
are born. The receipt names no diner. The card number is blurred. A last-four match or an e-signature
does not prove who held the card. Someone else could have sat at the counter. Someone else could have
held the plastic.

In **July 2026** the restaurant said publicly that, on the information available to it, it had **no
evidence establishing whether Robinson was there**. Official-side commentators treat a later owner
interview as a recant: he was not on the floor that night, he cannot see a last name on a debit-card
ticket, and he wants the harassment to stop. Some callers report he later said it was not him.

On this view the story is a viral screenshot. It cannot outweigh a text timeline the state says comes
from a roommate's phone, or a [midnight vehicle stop](/Tyler_Robinson/goforth-midnight-stop) the state
places back in the north valley. The defense has not, as of this writing, filed Panguitch as an alibi,
and official-side commentators treat that silence as the answer. Outlets that ran the restaurant in
January 2026 framed it as the last meal of a guilty man on the drive home, color for the charging
package, not an alibi at all.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say the staff account is specific, not vague. According to the servers, the
customer sat quietly at the counter, ordered a **medium-rare sirloin**, and paid by card. A server has
said on camera that he took the card, that 9:47 "sounds about right," and that the identification is
"100%." Staff reportedly told the FBI the next day. The restaurant reportedly gave agents **the last
four digits of the card**, and the owner told Fox News Digital in **January 2026** that the guest
entered a PIN. That is a debit card. The receipt's printed fields — Check #211, Table 24, sirloin
$27.00, total **$38.51**, Visa Debit, approval code 010864 — are itemized on the
[Cowboy's Smokehouse Receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt) page.

On a recorded **July 11, 2026** visit, published in full by citizen journalist Diligent Denizen, the
owner handed the printed ticket over and said it **is the ticket in question**. He also said he was not
there that night, that his staff believe it was Robinson, and that authorities **never told the
restaurant it was or was not him**. That is the honest shape of it: staff who say they saw him, an
owner who did not, and no official body that has resolved it. The
[full visit video](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-full-visit) is on this site.

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) went back to sources after the first owner interview. She has
said on her show that she moved from about **60 percent** certainty to **95 percent** that it was
Robinson's debit card swiped that night. Project Constitution put the same point in public: the owner
told Fox a quiet lone diner matching Robinson ate the sirloin; later, under heat, he called the whole
thing a conspiracy. What changed, they argue, is the pressure on a small alcohol-serving restaurant,
not the ticket.

Then the arithmetic, worked through on
[Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline)
and in the [chain of reasoning](/Narrative/chains/panguitch-breaks-the-evening). Leaving Panguitch
around 10:00 PM puts the earliest arrival back in Orem around **1:00 to 1:40 AM**. Going the other way,
a claimed **6:38 PM** [Dairy Queen photo in Orem](/Locations/Dairy_Queen_Orem) would need about
**98 to 99 mph** nonstop to reach the counter before 8:55 PM. Investigators say either the Panguitch
diner, the Dairy Queen photo, or the Orem-area evening texts cannot be Tyler Robinson. They cannot all
be true. A Cedar City [Maverik pump stop](/Locations/Cedar_City_Maverik) logged the following morning
is the next pin on that southbound road.

## When this fight ran

* **January 17, 2026.** Fox News Digital and other outlets ran the restaurant. Official-side coverage
  treated a possible steak dinner hours after the shooting as color of a guilty man heading south, not
  as a clock problem for the Orem texts.
* **July 1 and July 11, 2026.** Diligent Denizen interviewed the owner, then returned for the
  34-minute visit in which the ticket changed hands and a server said he was certain.
* **July 18, 2026.** The restaurant posted that it had no evidence establishing whether Robinson was
  there, and would not comment further because the matter was active.
* **August 14–31, 2026.** The photographed receipt circulated widely. Official-side accounts called
  the alibi "debunked" off the owner's later interviews. Investigators answered that the servers, not
  the back-office owner, are the witnesses, and that a CVS scribble is not a bank match.
* **September 8–10, 2026.** On the eve of the one-year mark, Candace Owens said she was 95 percent
  certain the card was Robinson's. Official-side commentators spent that week repeating that the
  steakhouse had been closed.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The Twiggs texts are the spine of the state's case. They carry the confession language, the motive,
the hidden rifle, and the watching of police near the woods. If Robinson was eating in Panguitch at
9:47 PM, those real-time Orem-area messages cannot be his, which means someone else wrote them or the
timeline is wrong. That is exactly what a framing would look like.

It also takes down the rest of the night. The [Goforth midnight stop](/Tyler_Robinson/goforth-midnight-stop)
and the 12:38 / 1:33 a.m. sightings in the north valley cannot share a body with a 9:47 PM Panguitch
checkout unless the phone and the car were somewhere he was not. The
[33-hour manhunt](/Narrative/fed_narrative/thirty-three-hour-manhunt) is the official story of that
window. [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview) collect the rest of why
investigators say the charging package does not hold.

A death-penalty case that will not compare four card digits in public is, investigators say, a case
that has chosen what not to look at. [Justice](/Fix/overview) here means producing the processor
record, not closing the file on a recant.

## What would settle it

* The full card authorization for Check #211: name, last four, time, and terminal ID, from the payment processor.
* Tyler Robinson's own debit-card last four, published by the family or by the FBI, next to those digits.
* Tyler Robinson's phone location, tower, and geofence records for the evening of September 10.
* The FBI's own interview notes with the Panguitch staff, and what follow-up was done.
* Any counter or parking-lot camera footage, with its retention log.
* Sworn testimony from the server who took the card.
