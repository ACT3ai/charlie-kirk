# 1. Did Tyler Robinson eat at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch on the night of September 10, 2025?

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The
Panguitch identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**. It has not been authenticated in
court. Nothing on this page says any restaurant employee, owner, agent, or officer said anything untrue.
This site makes no claim that any living person or organization committed a crime or had foreknowledge of
Charlie Kirk's death.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University (UVU) in Orem at **12:23 PM on September 10, 2025**.
The state's account keeps [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) in the Orem area for the rest of that
day and night. The main proof of that is a set of text messages to his roommate, Lance Twiggs, that
reportedly describe police, dogs, and a rifle hidden in the woods near campus.

Then staff at **Cowboy's Smokehouse** in **Panguitch** came forward. Panguitch is a small town in Garfield
County, about **200 to 209 road miles** south of Orem, roughly **3 hours 10 minutes to 3 hours 40 minutes**
of driving. Servers say a quiet young man matching the FBI photos ate a steak alone at the counter that
night. A photographed ticket, **Check #211**, shows an order at **8:55 PM** and a Visa Debit payment at
**9:47 PM** on 9/10/25.

The question is simple to state. If the man at that counter was Tyler Robinson, he could not also have been
in Orem that evening sending the texts the state relies on. Geography does not bend.

## The fed slop side, in full

The strongest version of the fed-slop side is **mistaken identity**. A small-town staff sees FBI photos
the next day, remembers a quiet young man, and becomes sure. That is how false identifications are born.
The receipt names no diner, the card number is blurred, and a last-four match or an e-signature does not
prove who held the card. In **July 2026** the restaurant said publicly that, on the information available
to it, it had **no evidence establishing whether Robinson was there**. Some callers report the owner later
said it was not him.

On this view the story is a viral screenshot, and it cannot outweigh a text timeline that the state says
comes from a roommate's phone. The defense has not, as of this writing, filed Panguitch as an alibi, and
fed-slop commentators treat that silence as the answer.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say the staff account is specific, not vague. According to the servers, the
customer sat quietly at the counter, ordered a **medium-rare sirloin**, and paid by card. A server has said
on camera that he took the card, that 9:47 "sounds about right," and that the identification is "100%."
Staff reportedly told the FBI the next day, and the restaurant reportedly gave agents **the last four
digits of the card**. The receipt's printed fields are itemized on the
[Cowboy's Smokehouse Receipt](/Tyler_Robinson/cowboys-smokehouse-receipt) page.

On a recorded July 2026 visit, the owner handed the printed ticket to a citizen journalist and said it
**is the ticket in question**. He also said he was not there that night, that his staff believe it was
Robinson, and that authorities **never told the restaurant it was or was not him**. That is the honest shape
of it: staff who say they saw him, an owner who did not, and no official body that has resolved it.

Then the arithmetic. Leaving Panguitch around 10:00 PM puts the earliest arrival back in Orem around
**1:00 to 1:40 AM**. Going the other way, a claimed **6:38 PM** Dairy Queen photo in Orem would need about
**98 to 99 mph** nonstop to reach the counter before 8:55 PM. Investigators say either the Panguitch diner,
the Dairy Queen photo, or the Orem-area evening texts cannot be Tyler Robinson. They cannot all be true.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The Twiggs texts are the spine of the state's case. They carry the confession language, the motive,
the hidden rifle, and the watching of police near the woods. If Robinson was eating in Panguitch at 9:47 PM,
those real-time Orem-area messages cannot be his, which means someone else wrote them or the timeline is
wrong. That is exactly what a framing would look like, and it is why this one restaurant ticket matters so
much.

## What would settle it

* The full card authorization for Check #211: name, last four, time, and terminal ID, from the payment processor.
* Tyler Robinson's phone location, tower, and geofence records for the evening of September 10.
* The FBI's own interview notes with the Panguitch staff, and what follow-up was done.
* Any counter or parking-lot camera footage, with its retention log.
* Sworn testimony from the server who took the card.
