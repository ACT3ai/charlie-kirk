# 5. What the Army ISR jet N1098L was doing over the Provo and UVU corridor

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes **no claim that N1098L, its crew, LASAI Aviation, Leidos, or the US Army had any role
in or knowledge of Charlie Kirk's death**. Flying an aircraft and running a lawful surveillance program are
lawful acts. A flight trace proves presence, never purpose and never occupancy. Tyler Robinson is charged,
not convicted, and presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

On September 10, 2025, a US Army intelligence jet, **N1098L**, flew from
**[Biggs Army Airfield](/Planes/Airport-Biggs-Army-Airfield)** in El Paso and came down into the Utah
Valley **twice**. Two free volunteer flight archives hold the full day, with **2,872** and **4,439**
position points, and they agree. The aircraft is a Bombardier Global 6500 operated by LASAI Aviation
II LLC under the Army's **[HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES)** program, callsign AXLE10 (also
rendered AXEL10). The full record is [N1098L Spy Plane](/Planes/N1098L/overview).

The first pass ran **9:04 to 9:28 AM** local time. The second ran **12:38 to 1:00 PM**, closest to the
UVU campus at **12:48 PM**, about **25 minutes after** [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed
at 12:23. Both times the jet flew the same inbound track, slowed to about **120 knots**, dropped to
roughly **200 feet above [Provo Municipal Airport](/Planes/Airports/KPVU)'s field elevation**, then
went to full power and climbed out over the runway. Its closest point to UVU was **4.16 km, about 2.6
miles**, on both passes.

The question: was this routine flying, or something that should have been investigated? Citizen
investigators conclude Kirk was killed by an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview). The
government's story is a rooftop .30-06 fired by [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). An Army
collection jet over the valley the same day is either a coincidence or a record the charging package
never asked for.

## The fed slop side, in full

The official-narrative side says this is a **LASAI and Leidos Global 6500 Army trainer** flying
**routine missed approaches** at Provo Municipal, the kind of thing these aircraft do hundreds of
times at more than 100 airports. It was **not drone-launch capable** and was **not flying operational
HADES ISR**. It has no link to the shooting. The FAA and FBI, on this telling, found no aircraft
link. The El Paso-to-Kalispell route is a standard training line. The Provo dips are currency work.

This site's own reading of the data leans the same way on the geometry. A steady track, down to about
200 feet above the field, then power and a climb over the runway, is the shape of **a straight-in
approach followed by a go-around**. It was flown at the airport, not over the campus. Closest
approach of **4.16 km** is what the Provo Municipal approach path measures from UVU. Sister ship
[N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview) landing at Provo on 1 July 2025 passed **4.17 km** from the same
point. The site also says plainly that the aircraft **very likely did not launch drones** and was
very likely not built out to do so. That is the honest ordinary reading, and it belongs on the
table at full strength.

Official-side commentators treat any remaining interest as conspiracy decay: if it looks like an
instrument approach, it is an instrument approach, and asking for tasking orders is a way to smear
the Army. On that view, [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is the case, the jet is a hobby, and
the [September 10 flight stack](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline) is weather.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say the anomaly is the **timing and the repeat**. A HADES-program aircraft flew
the **same low, slow double pass on the same day** a political leader was killed nearby, and one of
those passes came **after the shot**. They argue a drone drop-and-recovery profile is a live
hypothesis worth testing, and they note that a **drone-retrieval training** explanation is itself one
of the benign accounts offered for the passes. The drone question is collected on
[N1098L and the Drone Question Over UVU](/Drones/N1098L_Air_Launched_Drones) and the
[Drones hub](/Drones/overview).

John Cullen and other trackers have put the same two Provo dips next to a third clock:
[AXEL21 over St. George on September 11](/Planes/AXEL21-St-George-Sept-11), near the sheriff's
station, around 9 PM — Discord-post time. Three HADES-family appearances in 36 hours is, they argue,
the pattern a lone-gunman file cannot absorb. That is the
[chain that it was not a lone gunman](/Narrative/chains/not-a-lone-gunman).

This site's position is more careful. The plane very likely did not launch drones. It may have been a
training plane, and it may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people
who planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had
foreknowledge. **The site does not claim that is true.** It claims the question is important. The
[N1098L HADES Over UVU](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU) page and the
[Proof Intel Services hub](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview) are where that argument is kept in bounds.

What investigators agree on is the next step: **the tasking order and sensor logs** are the records
that would answer it, and they should be forced out rather than assumed. The
[Airborne Surveillance Records](/CoverUp/Airborne_Surveillance_Records) page is the CoverUp file for
products that may exist and have not been produced. [Who was aboard](/Narrative/fed_slop_issues/N1098L_Crew_Names)
is the companion personnel question; this site does not republish crew names.

## When this fight ran

* **September 14–20, 2025.** Citizen OSINT first posted the tail and the two low passes. Altitude
  figures in early threads did not agree with each other (600 ft vs 203 ft; 12:24 vs 12:48). The
  recovered ADS-B later settled the clocks at **9:16 AM** and **12:48 PM** bottom-outs. Official-side
  accounts mostly did not engage.
* **Late September – October 2025.** Trainer-versus-ISR became the split. One camp said missed
  approaches at 100+ airports. The other camp said a HADES double pass including after the shot is
  the anomaly even if the geometry is an approach. FAA/FBI "no aircraft link" language circulated as
  a close.
* **2026.** Recovery of both volunteer archives (2,872 and 4,439 points agreeing to two decimals)
  hardened the geometry. A widely watched August debate on whether Robinson acted alone did not
  mention the jet. Trackers were still posting the Provo dips in September 2026.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

No ADS-B track, tasking order, or sensor product from N1098L has appeared in the case against Tyler
Robinson. If an Army collection aircraft was over the valley that day with sensors running, the
government may hold airborne records of the scene that could show where the fatal event came from.
A lone-gunman case that never mentions such an aircraft, investigators say, is a case that has
chosen what not to look at.

[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview) put the missing airborne product
next to the missing firing tape and the ATF fragment. The
[low-pass timing page](/Suspicious/Five_Minutes/n1098l-low-pass-timing) is the place that records how
early claims of a 12:24 pass conflicted with the later 12:48 figure. [The LASAI fleet](/Planes/LASAI-Fleet/overview)
shows this was not a one-off airframe. [Fix the laws](/Fix/overview) is how a reader forces the
tasking out.

A trace proves presence. It does not prove purpose. Investigators still want the tasking, because
presence of a HADES jet 25 minutes after the shot is the kind of fact a charging package that
closes on a 22-year-old apprentice has to explain or disclose.

## What would settle it

* The HADES mission tasking order for September 10, 2025, and who planned the Provo approaches.
* The aircraft's configuration that day: what sensors were fitted and whether any were recording.
* Any sensor or imagery product covering the Orem and Provo area, and its preservation status.
* Provo Municipal tower records and radio logs for both passes.
* A control comparison: how often this tail flew the same missed-approach pair at PVU in 2024–2025,
  on days with no sourced Kirk event.
