# 5. What the Army ISR jet N1098L was doing over the Provo and UVU corridor

:::caution Attributed claims only
This site makes **no claim that N1098L, its crew, LASAI Aviation, Leidos, or the US Army had any role
in or knowledge of Charlie Kirk's death**. Flying an aircraft and running a lawful surveillance program are
lawful acts. A flight trace proves presence, never purpose and never occupancy. Tyler Robinson is charged,
not convicted, and presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

On September 10, 2025, a US Army intelligence jet, **N1098L**, flew from **Biggs Army Airfield** in
El Paso and came down into the Utah Valley **twice**. Two free volunteer flight archives hold the full day,
with **2,872** and **4,439** position points, and they agree.

The first pass ran **9:04 to 9:28 AM** local time. The second ran **12:38 to 1:00 PM**, closest to the UVU
campus at **12:48 PM**, about **25 minutes after** Charlie Kirk was shot at 12:23. Both times the jet flew
the same inbound track, slowed to about **120 knots**, dropped to roughly **200 feet above Provo Municipal
Airport's field elevation**, then went to full power and climbed out over the runway. Its closest point to
UVU was **4.16 km, about 2.6 miles**, on both passes.

The question: was this routine flying, or something that should have been investigated?

## Side by side

| | |

| **Citizen Investigators** | A HADES/AXEL low, slow double pass the same day, including after the shot, is the anomaly. A drone drop/recovery profile is the live hypothesis. Tasking and sensor logs are the records that should be forced out. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says this is a **LASAI and Leidos Global 6500 Army trainer** flying **routine missed
approaches** at Provo Municipal, the kind of thing these aircraft do hundreds of times at more than 100
airports. It was **not drone-launch capable** and was **not flying operational HADES ISR**. It has no link to
the shooting.

This site's own reading of the data leans the same way on the geometry. A steady track, down to about 200
feet above the field, then power and a climb over the runway, is the shape of **a straight-in approach
followed by a go-around**. It was flown at the airport, not over the campus. The site also says plainly
that the aircraft **very likely did not launch drones** and was very likely not built out to do so.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say the anomaly is the **timing and the repeat**. A HADES-program aircraft flew
the **same low, slow double pass on the same day** a political leader was killed nearby, and one of those
passes came **after the shot**. They argue a drone drop-and-recovery profile is a live hypothesis worth
testing, and they note that a **drone-retrieval training** explanation is itself one of the benign accounts
offered for the passes.

This site's position is more careful. The plane very likely did not launch drones. It may have been a
training plane, and it may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people who
planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had foreknowledge.
**The site does not claim that is true.** It claims the question is important.

What investigators agree on is the next step: **the tasking order and sensor logs** are the records that
would answer it, and they should be forced out rather than assumed.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

No ADS-B track, tasking order, or sensor product from N1098L has appeared in the case against Tyler
Robinson. If an Army collection aircraft was over the valley that day with sensors running, the government
may hold airborne records of the scene that could show where the fatal event came from. A lone-gunman case
that never mentions such an aircraft, investigators say, is a case that has chosen what not to look at.

## What would settle it

* The HADES mission tasking order for September 10, 2025, and who planned the Provo approaches.
* The aircraft's configuration that day: what sensors were fitted and whether any were recording.
* Any sensor or imagery product covering the Orem and Provo area, and its preservation status.
* Provo Municipal tower records and radio logs for both passes.
