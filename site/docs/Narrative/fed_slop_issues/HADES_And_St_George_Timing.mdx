# 16. HADES military aircraft and the St. George timing

:::caution Attributed claims only
Position data shows where an aircraft was. It never shows why, or who knew what. This site makes no claim that anyone aboard, operating, or tasking these aircraft committed a crime, had foreknowledge of the killing, or did anything wrong, and it does not name any crew. Nothing here says any sheriff's officer did anything wrong. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

**HADES** is a U.S. Army surveillance-aircraft program flown on converted Bombardier business jets. Two jets from the same contractor fleet matter here. On **September 10, 2025**, the day Charlie Kirk was killed, [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) flew two low passes into the Utah Valley. On the night of **September 11**, its fleet-mate [N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview), callsign **AXEL21**, flew an approach over **St. George** — near the sheriff's office where Tyler Robinson was in custody.

Supporters of the official account call all of it routine flying. Citizen investigators say two appearances on the two key days, at the two key places, deserve an answer from whoever scheduled them.

## The fed slop side, in full

This side has real data behind it, and this site says so. N1098L departed **Biggs Army Airfield in El Paso** around **7:48 AM MDT** bound for **Kalispell, Montana**. Both of its Utah Valley passes flew a steady **146°** track, bottomed out about **200 feet above Provo's field elevation**, and climbed away **over the runway at Provo Municipal Airport** — the shape of a straight-in approach and go-around, a routine training manoeuvre. Its closest approach to UVU was **4.16 km**. The transponder was on the whole time, visible to every hobby tracker, which is not how covert flights are flown. No aircraft record has been introduced in the State's case.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators answer that the timing is the point, not the altitude. The first pass ran **9:04–9:28 AM**. The second ran **12:38–1:00 PM**, closest to campus at **12:48** — about **25 minutes after the shooting** ([N1098L over UVU](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU)). No ADS-B track, tasking order, or sensor product from the aircraft has appeared in the prosecution.

Then St. George. According to two independent archives that agree to the second, AXEL21 landed at Ontario, California at **6:39 PM** on September 11, departed at **8:00 PM**, flew northeast into Utah, and at **8:59–9:00 PM** was level at about **1,100 feet** above St. George Regional Airport on the runway 19 centreline. At **9:00:45 PM** it went around without landing and left toward Arizona ([AXEL21 over St. George](/Planes/AXEL21-St-George-Sept-11)).

That window sits beside the Discord "confession." Filings date it **7:57 PM**, but the screenshot's own clock reads **8:57 PM** ([confession screenshot](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/confession-screenshot-zealous-monkey)). The court record also places Robinson's Miranda warning at **6:25 PM**, hours earlier. Investigators do not claim the plane did anything. They ask who planned for it to be there, and why that is not in the record.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If military surveillance aircraft were in the air near both the shooting and the custody building, their sensor data could confirm or rule out large parts of the official timeline. A case built on a lone young man has no place for those flights, which is why investigators say their records should be produced rather than waved away.

## What would settle it

* The tasking orders and flight plans for N1098L on September 10 and N2100L on September 11, 2025.
* Any sensor product or collection log from either flight over Utah.
* A statement from the FAA or FBI, with records, on what was checked about these aircraft.
* Discord's server-side login and IP records for the confession account that evening.
