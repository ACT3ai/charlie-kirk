# 8. Was anyone filmed taking the shot?

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The
rooftop witness described here gave an **interview, not sworn testimony**, and this site does not name him.
This site makes no claim that any prosecutor, agent, or officer said anything untrue or committed a crime.
:::

## The issue in plain words

The state says the shot that killed Charlie Kirk at 12:23 PM on September 10, 2025 came from the
roof of the **Losee Center**, about 150 yards from the tent where he was speaking. Campus cameras, the state
says, tracked Robinson around UVU that day.

But there is a difference between video of a person **on campus** and video of a person **firing a rifle**.
This issue asks: is there any footage, public or shown in court, of anyone aiming or firing?

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Prosecutors told the court surveillance shows him on campus taking the shot. Rooftop prone time plus egress is the shooter. Identity is closed even without a public muzzle flash. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Still no video of anyone aiming or firing. The roof witness stopped watching ~5 seconds before the shot and described different kit and a different gun. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says prosecutors told the court that surveillance shows Robinson on campus **taking
the shot**. A figure goes onto the roof, lies prone for a time, and then leaves in a hurry. On this view,
rooftop prone time plus that exit is the shooter, and identity is settled **even without a public muzzle
flash**.

There is a real point here. A muzzle flash in bright daylight can be invisible on video, and distant
surveillance cameras are not built to capture recoil. The absence of a visible flash does not, by itself,
prove no shot was fired.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say there is **still no video of anyone aiming or firing**. Reviewers of the
public footage report no visible muzzle flash and no recoil at the moment of the shot. The state's hearing
compilation, Exhibit 12.1, was described by the FBI agent who authenticated it as campus video of movements,
with zooms, blurring, and red circles added. It was not described as footage of a shot.

The closest thing to an eyewitness on the roof is a man who says he filmed the figure. In his own words, he
recorded **about 20 seconds**, and the shot came **about five seconds after he stopped recording**. He
described **all-black tactical clothing, a face mask, and a long gun**, and said the figure then "absolutely
sprinted off the roof." According to Candace Owens, he says the rifle he saw **does not match** the one
presented in court and that the person was not dressed like Robinson. His video and his campus report have
not been released by any agency, and he has not testified.

Most citizen investigators go further. They conclude the fatal event was an
[explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview), which would explain why no one on the roof is ever seen
firing.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

A murder case with no footage of the act leans harder on everything else: the DNA, the texts, and
the identification of a distant figure. If the only person who watched the roof says the figure was dressed
differently and carried a different gun, and stopped filming five seconds before the shot, then the gap in
the video is not a small one. Investigators say that is the gap a frame-up would sit in.

## What would settle it

* Release of the rooftop witness's video and his written campus report from September 10.
* Native-resolution footage from every camera with a view of the Losee Center roof at 12:23 PM.
* The raw multitrack audio from the event, analyzed for the direction and number of sound events.
* Sworn testimony from every person who watched the roof.
