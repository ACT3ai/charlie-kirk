# 50. France or another foreign-service theory

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims as fact that France, its president, the French Foreign Legion, any French
official, or any other government or service ordered, took part in, or knew of Charlie Kirk's death.
The French-source claims are Candace Owens' own public account from an anonymous source and are
uncorroborated. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted, and presumed
innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

In 2026, [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) said a person she described as a high-ranking French
government employee contacted her. According to Owens, that source said a plot against her own life
had been authorized, and that Charlie Kirk's killer trained with a French Foreign Legion brigade. She
has said she published this in case something happens to her. The source is anonymous, and no document
has been released.

Other foreign-service threads exist too. The site tracks an Egyptian-registered jet,
[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), reported flying into Provo from France on September 4, 2025 and
leaving the day of the killing, and a halted U.S. inquiry into foreign involvement. The fed-slop side
uses the France claim in particular to dismiss the whole independent investigation.

## The fed slop side, in full

Naming a foreign government as behind a killing is a grave claim, and it needs evidence: documents,
witnesses, money, orders. Owens offered an unnamed source. Critics note the cast of the story has
changed over time, from Israel to Egypt to France, and they ask for the versions to be reconciled. On
this view, a claim that keeps changing its suspects is a sign of content built for an audience, and it
discredits the people repeating it.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators largely agree that the France claim is not proven, and this site says so plainly on its
[Candace Owens threat claims](/Messages/candace-owens-threat-claims) page: no named source, no
document, no independent confirmation. Their point is that the case against the official account does
not rest on France, or on any foreign theory at all.

It rests, they say, on physical and timing gaps any reader can check. The receipt: restaurant staff in
Panguitch, about 209 miles from Orem, say they served the accused dinner that night. The clock: a
defense filing cites a video showing Robinson read his Miranda rights at 6:25 PM on September 11, while
the Discord "confession" traffic comes later. The missing shot tape: no public video shows anyone
firing from the roof. Investigators say mocking a country name answers none of those. Foreign-service
ideas stay on the list as hypotheses to test, below the evidence.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If the loudest foreign claim is used to wave away every question, the concrete facts that point away
from Robinson get thrown out with it. Keeping the foreign theories in their place, as open hypotheses,
protects the physical gaps from being dismissed by association.

## What would settle it

* Any document supporting the French-source account: a record, a payment, a communication.
* A formal French government response to the specific claims, published in full.
* Flight manifests and ground-handling records for SU-BTT's Provo stay.
* The halted NCTC records, which would show whether any foreign lead, French or otherwise, was examined.
