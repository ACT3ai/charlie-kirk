# 63. Electroshock or directed energy instead of, or with, a rifle

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any living person, agency or organization used, built or knew of any weapon or device, or had foreknowledge of the killing. The electrocution posts archived on this site are uncorroborated and are recorded only for completeness. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

Some posts online have claimed that Charlie Kirk was killed, or partly killed, by an electrical shock or a directed-energy weapon, either instead of a rifle or together with one. The most detailed version on this site is a July 2026 post describing a claimed electromagnetic mechanism, archived on the [Electrocution page](/Electrocution/overview).

This page needs to be clear about where the citizen investigators stand. **Electrocution is not their conclusion.** Nothing has corroborated it: no burn, arc mark or current path has been reported. This site treats it as a fed-slop narrative. The investigators' conclusion is an [explosive device at the microphone](/Mic/overview). The rooftop .30-06 is the government's narrative.

## The fed slop side, in full

Supporters of the government's narrative argue that every non-rifle theory is the same mistake. The medical finding presented at the preliminary hearing was homicide by gunshot. No energy weapon has ever been documented at the venue. Once people start reaching for exotic mechanisms, the argument goes, they have left evidence behind, and the microphone claims belong in the same bin as the energy-weapon claims.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators agree with part of that and reject the rest. They agree there is no corroborated evidence of electroshock or directed energy. The archived post rests on author-drawn diagrams and an animated video rather than measurements, documents or testimony, and pointing to military electromagnetic expertise is an assertion of capability, not evidence of involvement. Some of these posts, this site notes, carry the hallmarks of fed-slop accounts.

What they reject is the bundling. The microphone conclusion rests on physical and recorded evidence of its own: [video](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) of the shirt expanding outward before the wound, [audio](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) separating a louder event at the tent from a distant report, a [burnt object](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) in the SUV, and a [neck fragment](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic) that may be charred plastic. Lumping that with uncorroborated energy-weapon posts, they say, is how a strong case gets dismissed alongside a weak one.

The one thing this row shares with their case is the gap in the rifle account. The ATF fragment comparison is [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive), a .30-06 at that range [should not produce](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) the reported wound picture, and there is no public ballistic match.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Uncorroborated theories hurt the defense of an accused man, because they make every question about the cause of death look like fantasy. If the rifle account is unproven, Robinson's case needs that shown with evidence, and investigators say the evidence points to the microphone, not to energy weapons. Keeping the two apart protects the strongest argument that he did not fire the fatal shot.

## What would settle it

* The full autopsy report, which would show any burn, arc mark or current-path injury, or their absence.
* Independent residue testing of the shirt, the microphone parts and the SUV.
* The complete ATF fragment report.
* A documented inventory of electrical and electronic equipment at the tent.
