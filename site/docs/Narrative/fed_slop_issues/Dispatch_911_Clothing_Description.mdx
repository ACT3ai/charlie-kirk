# 30. The 911 and dispatch clothing description

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that any dispatcher, officer, agency, or security staff member acted
improperly or knew anything in advance. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not
convicted, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The rooftop witness gave an interview, not
sworn testimony, and this site does not name him.
:::

## The issue in plain words

In the first minutes after the shot at **12:23 PM on September 10, 2025**, people called 911 and police
radio started broadcasting a lookout description. Those first descriptions are the least rehearsed
record of the afternoon. They were made before any press conference and before any charging document.
The event is the [UVU public speaking event](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU).

As rendered in a compilation of the audio, one transmission describes a suspect **"all black, black long
gun, black tactical helmet, a black mask, possibly a tactical vest."** Another unit places the person
**"on top of the building on the far north side, just east of the library,"** wearing **"jeans, black shirt,
black mask, long rifle."** Fox News posted the first of those lines on **September 11, 2025**, quoting a
female dispatcher: *"He's going to be wearing all black, black long gun, black tactical helmet, a black
mask, possibly wearing a tactical vest and jeans."* PEOPLE magazine and Daily Mail ran matching
transmissions that also mentioned aviator glasses, a black bag, and longer hair.

The state's figure is different. At the hearing, the morning walker the state calls
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) wore a maroon T-shirt, shorts and Converse. The state says he
changed before the roof, but no one has described a helmet, a mask or a vest in the state's account.
The long page is [911 calls and the rooftop witness](/Witnesses/911-calls-rooftop-witness). The radio
minute by minute is [Dispatch and radio scanner timeline](/Timeline/dispatch-radio-scanner-timeline).

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says first-minute descriptions are famously unreliable. People are frightened, radio
audio is compressed, and dispatchers repeat what callers guess. On a day with armed security around the
event, a caller could easily mistake a security officer for the shooter. Even the radio traffic is not
consistent: one transmission says "jeans," which fits the state's later account of a dark long-sleeve
and jeans on the return. Campus video is a steadier record than a few seconds of shouted description,
and the video is what the court relied on.

On this view the 911 kit is a first-hour mess that the investigation cleaned up. The court rifle is a
towel-wrapped Mauser, not a black long gun in tactical hands. The morning scout in a maroon T-shirt is
the same man after a clothes change. Official-side commentators treat the dispatch tape as color, not
as identity evidence, and they treat anyone still quoting "tactical helmet" as ignoring the compilation
the judge saw. See [Fed claim: the rooftop figure is Robinson](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson)
and [Gun and bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview).

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the description is not one garbled call. It recurs across dispatch traffic, and it
matches a separate witness who saw the figure before the shot. That man says he filmed the person on the
Losee Center roof from about **175 yards** away. In his account the person moved tactically, dropping,
looking and running, and was in **"completely all black,"** with a face mask and all skin covered. He
says he first assumed it was event security, until the long gun and the movement struck him as strange.
He says he stopped recording about **five seconds** before the shot.

According to [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), the same witness told her he does not think the
person was Tyler Robinson, and that the rifle he saw does not match the one presented in court. He has
not testified, and his video and campus report have not been released by any agency. That is
[The uncalled roof eyewitness](/Suspicious/Trial_Legal/uncalled-roof-eyewitness). A construction-crew
witness separately described a man in a black trench coat, black mask and black cargo pants near campus
after the shooting.

The clothing fight on the roof itself is [Black clothing vs jeans](/Roof/black-clothing-vs-jeans).
Investigators also track an early all-black-clad suspect that dropped out of the narrative once federal
agents took over: [Black-clad rooftop suspect dropped](/CoverUp/Black_Clad_Suspect_Dropped). A related
photo discrepancy — what sheriffs showed construction workers versus what the FBI released — is
[FBI black clothing photo](/FBI/Black_Clothing_Photo).

Investigators also note that local officers were reportedly told over the radio not to interview
witnesses until federal agents arrived, which is part of why so few first-hour statements exist. That
is [Fed Slop Issue 39](/Narrative/fed_slop_issues/FBI_Hold_On_Witness_Interviews). Project Constitution
posted a compilation of 911, dispatch, and the rooftop-witness interview on **September 13, 2026**,
putting the tactical kit next to the T-shirt figure the court was shown. The cheapest instrument that
would settle the radio half is a Rule 14 subpoena for the CAD log:
[Subpoena the dispatch and CAD record](/court/ai_attorney/dispatch-and-cad-records).

Citizen investigators conclude the fatal event was an exploding or rigged microphone, which would make
the rooftop figure a distraction even if the kit matched. That is [Mic](/Mic/overview). Electrocution
claims are uncorroborated and are not their finding. The identity question on this page is narrower:
the kit the first callers described is not the kit the charging package sold.

## When this fight ran

* **10–11 September 2025.** Live radio. Fox News posted the dispatcher line the next afternoon. PEOPLE
  and Daily Mail repeated "all black, tactical helmet, mask, vest." This is the first public description
  of the person on the roof.
* **12–16 September 2025.** Official-side accounts shifted to a maroon T-shirt scout and a later clothes
  change. The tactical kit receded from press conferences.
* **July 2026 hearing.** The court was shown a T-shirt-and-shorts morning walker and a dark-clothes
  roof figure. No helmet, mask, or vest was produced. The roof witness was not called.
* **13 September 2026.** Project Constitution's 911 compilation put the original radio next to the
  hearing figure and reopened the kit fight.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If the person on the roof was wearing a helmet, mask and vest, the state needs to show where that gear
came from and where it went. None of it has been produced. A figure the witnesses saw and a figure the
court was shown that do not match is a reason to ask whether the man charged is the man on the roof.
He has entered no guilty plea. The [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) hub treats the 911 kit
as a live identity objection, not as a radio glitch.

## What would settle it

* The full 911 recordings and call-taker notes from UVU and Orem.
* The complete CAD log and all radio channels used that day, with timestamps.
* The rooftop witness's own video and his campus report, and his sworn testimony.
* Any recovered helmet, mask or vest, or a clear statement that none was found.
* Side-by-side stills of the roof figure and the morning scout, at native resolution, with clothing
  labeled by the state.
