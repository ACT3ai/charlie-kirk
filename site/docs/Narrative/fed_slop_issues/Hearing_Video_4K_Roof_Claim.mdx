# 3. Does court or hearing video show Tyler Robinson on the roof in 4K?

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. This
page does not say any commentator, agent, or prosecutor said anything untrue. It compares public
descriptions of hearing video with the hearing transcript and with the footage that is public. This site
makes no claim that any living person or organization committed a crime or had foreknowledge.
:::

## The issue in plain words

The state says a figure seen on the roof of the **Losee Center** at UVU on September 10, 2025 was
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson), and that a [rifle shot](/Gun_Bullet/overview) came from there.
The government's narrative is a rooftop .30-06. Citizen investigators conclude
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed by an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview). The
public has only seen grainy, distant clips of that roof figure.

During and after the **July 2026 preliminary hearing** at the
[Fourth District Court in Provo](/Locations/Fourth_District_Court_Provo), a claim spread: that the
courtroom had been shown a far better copy, enhanced HD and some 4K, with a clear face, a clear plate,
clear clothing, and rooftop actions timed to the shot. If that were true, the blur in public clips
would not matter.

So this issue asks one factual question. **What did the hearing video actually show, and did any of it
show a clear face firing a rifle?**

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Commentators who attended the hearing (Benny Johnson among them) say they saw enhanced HD and some 4K: a crystal-clear face, plate, clothes, and rooftop actions timed to the shot. The public blur does not matter because the courtroom saw the good copy. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Other same-day attendees disputed it: most clips were already public and none was face-clear 4K of a shot. The circulating roof footage is grainy, with no gun, no recoil, and no firing. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The official-narrative side says commentators who attended the hearing, Benny Johnson among them,
described seeing enhanced HD and some 4K footage: a crystal-clear face, a license plate, clothes, and
rooftop actions timed to the shot. On this view, the public blur is irrelevant because the court saw
the good copy, and people who argue from the blurry clips are arguing from the wrong evidence.

Johnson's July 10, 2026 post, from outside the courthouse, is the canonical version. He wrote that
Judge Tony Graf had cameras turned off, that Erika Kirk had asked them to be there, and that the
enhanced set showed "every movement" that day, "zoomed in," with "some footage in 4K." He listed a
crystal-clear face, a plate number, clothing details, and rooftop actions timed to the minute
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed. He said the courthouse gasped. He said the footage
would eventually be released. Fox News ran him the next night calling it an "evidentiary bombshell."
Official-side commentators treated that as the close of the identity question.

In fairness, the state did play an enhanced compilation at the hearing, and a prosecutor argued that
the enhanced version makes it easier to "truly see what's going on." That part is real. Agent
testimony on [Day 1](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_1) and [Day 2](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_2)
walked the courtroom through campus cameras, a Dodge Challenger, and a figure on the Losee roof. The
fed claim that [the rooftop figure is Robinson](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson)
is built on that compilation.

## What citizen investigators say

Other attendees on the same day disputed the description. Brandi Siciliani wrote from inside the
room: there is no high-quality, zoomed-in footage showing anyone taking a shot; the video shown is
the same distance as the public clip of a figure jumping off the roof; people who know the tape will
not be released are misrepresenting what was shown. Project Constitution named three same-day
attendees — Billie Webb, Elizabeth Lane, and Siciliani — as confirming that what was described as
crystal-clear 4K of a face on the roof did not happen. Their account is that most clips shown were
**already public**, that only a few new clips were added, and that **none was face-clear 4K of a
shot being fired**.

The hearing transcript supports a narrower picture than "crystal-clear 4K." FBI Agent David Hull
described **State's Exhibit 12.1** as a **compilation** of UVU camera video, prepared by the Utah
County Attorney's Office, showing the movements of the person he believes is Robinson around campus
from September 10 into the morning of September 11. He testified that it has **zooms that were not a
function of the cameras**, that some people were **blurred**, and that **red circles** were added.
That is an edited compilation of campus surveillance, not a new high-resolution camera. The
[preliminary hearing overview](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/overview) collects all five days.

The rooftop footage that circulates is grainy. Reviewers report **no visible rifle, no recoil, and no
muzzle flash** at the moment of the shot. That is the
[No Muzzle Flash or Recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil) page. The
[roof hub](/Roof/overview) and [roof-figure identity](/Roof/roof-figure-not-tyler) pages collect the
clothing, gait, and face-ID problems. Investigators say that if a clear 4K recording of the figure
firing existed, it would be the single most powerful exhibit in the case, and it would not need to
be described secondhand.

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) later posted stills drawn from the set the influencers were
shown, and said there is evidence the person in those stills might not be Robinson at all. That is
the opposite of a closed identification. The [enhanced stills released on September 11](/FBI/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos)
and the [CoverUp page on those stills](/CoverUp/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos) already showed the state
working from processed images. [Rooftop Video Trimmed](/CoverUp/Rooftop_Video_Trimmed) is the
companion question about the public clip's start time.

## When this fight ran

* **July 10–11, 2026.** The hearing-room claim. Official-side commentators who had been invited in
  described 4K. Same-day attendees who had camped for a seat described the public blur plus red
  circles. Fox ran the "bombshell" language the next night.
* **July 12–29, 2026.** The claim was clipped, quoted, and treated as a substitute for a public
  exhibit. Investigators answered with attendee notes and with Hull's own words about zooms and
  circles.
* **August 10, 2026.** Candace Owens advertised exclusive stills from the "4K footage" the
  influencers were shown, and said the person might not be Robinson. The fight moved from "the court
  saw it" to "show the stills."

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

Identification is the heart of the case against Robinson. If the only rooftop images are distant,
compressed, and enhanced after the fact, the identification rests on interpretation, not on a clear
picture. Claims that "the courtroom saw 4K" can close public debate without anyone ever seeing that
footage. Investigators say a framing case would need exactly that: confidence about images the
public is not allowed to check.

The [chain that the rooftop figure is not identified as Robinson](/Narrative/chains/rooftop-figure-not-robinson)
walks five problems with the identification. [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview) put the missing firing tape next
to the ATF fragment and the custody clock. Citizen investigators conclude the kill mechanism was at
the microphone. A guest describing 4K of a sniper is how the rooftop .30-06 was re-sold in July 2026
without a public muzzle flash.

## What would settle it

* Public release of State's Exhibits 12.1 and 12.4, with the native-resolution source files behind them.
* The camera model, resolution, and frame rate of each UVU camera used in the compilation.
* The enhancement work product: what was zoomed or altered, by whom, and when.
* Any footage, from any camera, that shows the figure aiming or firing.
* Sworn descriptions from more than one uninvited attendee of what the enhanced set actually contained.
