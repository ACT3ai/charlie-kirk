# 62. An exploding microphone as the neck event

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any living person or organization placed, built, triggered or knew of any device, or had foreknowledge of the killing. Nothing indicates that anyone on the security team, the AV team or the TPUSA staff knew of any device. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

At about 12:23 p.m. on **September 10, 2025**, Charlie Kirk was speaking into a wireless microphone under a tent at Utah Valley University when he suffered a sudden, fatal injury to the neck. The government's narrative is that a single .30-06 round fired from the Losee Center roof, roughly 142 to 175 yards away, caused it. That is the [fed claim of a .30-06 neck wound](/Narrative/fed_narrative/thirty-06-neck-wound).

The large majority of citizen investigators have reached a different conclusion: the fatal event was an **explosive device at the microphone** — a small shaped or directional charge at the lapel assembly. This is the central cause-of-death question in the whole case. The full evidence is gathered on the [Microphone section](/Mic/overview). The step-by-step reasoning is the [shaped-charge chain](/Narrative/chains/shaped-charge-microphone).

Electrocution and directed-energy claims are a **separate, uncorroborated** row. They are not this conclusion. See [Issue 63](/Narrative/fed_slop_issues/Electroshock_Or_Directed_Energy).

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Homicide by gunshot per the medical examiner. No explosive residue. Mic-bomb claims are fact-checked garbage. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | The investigators' conclusion. Sudden neck trauma, no muzzle flash on any public video, and the "miracle" stop fit an exploding or rigged microphone. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Supporters of the government's narrative put it this way. At the July 2026 preliminary hearing, DPS agent David Hull testified that the manner of death was homicide by a gunshot to the neck and that a bullet fragment was recovered ([autopsy sealed](/CoverUp/Autopsy_Sealed_Examiner)). A nine-page medical-examiner report was admitted as an exhibit and not published. The only findings read aloud were manner: homicide, and immediate cause: gunshot wound of the neck. A rifle was found. Most witnesses heard one shot.

Mainstream fact-checks, including an anniversary piece on **September 8, 2026**, have dismissed the microphone claims as false: the medical examiner certified a gunshot, recovered a fragment, and (as those write-ups put it) found no explosive residue or shrapnel from a detonated device. A debate-circuit commentator in **August 2026** made the smoke point: a real explosion, he said, would leave visible smoke, so the mic theory fails physics. Official-side hearing coverage treated Hull's testimony as having "ruled out" an exploding microphone.

On this view, a consumer wireless transmitter cannot be a shaped charge, the wound is a gunshot, and the rest is content.

The official side also treats "no smoke, no flash, no shock wave reported by people next to the stage" as a physics veto. If a demolition charge had gone off at the tent, they say, the crowd would have known. Investigators' answer is in the hardware, not in a quarry demo: a small directional charge at the lapel is built to dump energy into tissue, not into the courtyard. Using the absence of a smoke cloud as the close is, they say, a category error that fact-checks have repeated for a year.

## What citizen investigators say

This is their conclusion, not a punchline. They answer each official point with a physical file.

On residue, they say nothing was tested in public at all: the soil, the pavers, the clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [reportedly hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no released residue result. The absence of a result is not a negative result, and residue on fabric is [recoverable with standard lab methods](/Mic/explosive-residue). The [autopsy report is not public](/Medical/autopsy_report_not_public). "No residue" in a fact-check is, they say, a claim about a report the public cannot read.

On the video, [frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears. The [shirt-before-blood sequence](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/shirt-before-blood) is the same observation in another file. Veterans Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt said on air that it looked like an explosion.

On the audio, the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly separates a louder detonation at the tent, about 46 meters from the camera, from a distant muzzle report about 120 meters away. That is the [acoustic two-event split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split): two events, two distances.

On smoke, the [shaped-charge demo page](/Mic/Demo_of_shape_charge) sets out why a large demolition charge is the wrong comparison for a small directional device. A tiny liner charge is built to cut in one direction. A quarry blast is not the model.

On the wound, a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and cause massive tissue damage. That is the [.30-06 energy mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) and the [why it was not a .30-06](/Narrative/chains/not-a-thirty-06) chain. Instead, a relayed account from TPUSA-affiliated figures described the surgeon calling it an "absolute miracle" that the bullet did not exit ([hospital claims](/Medical/hospital_people_and_bullet_claims)). Investigators say that "miracle" is exactly what a device at the neck would look like.

The microphone was also [routed under the shirt](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), unlike prior outdoor events. The ATF fragment comparison is [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive). A citizen analysis of one recovered fragment argues [charred black plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic) rather than lead. Project Constitution has argued a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) is consistent with a destroyed RØDE transmitter. None of that is a court finding. All of it is why investigators will not put this row back in the "garbage" bin.

The [cause-of-death hub](/Cause_of_Death/overview) is the summary of that conclusion.

Project Constitution and other named investigators have kept the 911 tape and the shirt sequence in circulation specifically because the official close ("Hull said gunshot") does not address those two records. A medical-examiner sentence read into a probable-cause hearing, with the report sealed, is not independent residue testing and is not a published wound path. Investigators will not retire a majority conclusion on that basis.

## What is actually in the public record

* Charlie Kirk suffered a sudden fatal neck injury at about 12:23 p.m. while speaking into a wireless microphone under a tent.
* The state's public finding, read at the preliminary hearing, is homicide by gunshot wound of the neck. The autopsy exhibit was not published.
* No public video shows a muzzle flash or recoil at the shot. Frame analysis circulated by investigators shows the shirt expanding outward before the wound.
* Canon XA55 audio is argued to split a louder event at the tent (~46 m) from a distant report (~120 m).
* Residue testing of the shirt, mic parts, SUV and soil has not been released. The ATF fragment comparison is inconclusive. Investigators conclude a shaped charge at the microphone.

## When this fight ran

* **September 10–22, 2025.** Immediate "blood bag" and shirt-bulge talk. Fact-checks correctly note Kirk was wearing a microphone. That correction is later reused as if it also disproved a device.
* **March 31, 2026.** After a court filing on the ATF fragment mismatch, investigator posts revive the explosive-mic pack as the mechanism that fits a non-match.
* **July 6–11, 2026.** Hearing week. Hull's "homicide by gunshot" line is sold as a mic-theory funeral. The autopsy exhibit is not published.
* **August 2026.** Debate-circuit "no smoke" close. Investigator replies: wrong charge size, wrong comparison.
* **September 8, 2026.** Anniversary fact-checks list "exploding microphone" as a prominent false theory. Investigator accounts keep posting the shirt sequence, the XA55 split, and the residue gap.

The investigator conclusion has been majority on X for most of the year after the event. The official side's counter has been the medical-examiner sentence and the fact-check.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

This is the issue that decides the others. If Charlie Kirk was killed by a device at the microphone, then whoever was on the Losee Center roof did not cause the death, and the rooftop case against Robinson becomes beside the point. Investigators say that is why the residue evidence matters so much, and why its handling is part of the reason they believe he is being framed. See [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and [Tyler Robinson not assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview).

A probable-cause sentence of "gunshot" with a sealed report cannot retire a majority investigator conclusion that has video, audio, energy, and residue-gap legs. The public is being asked to take the close on trust. That is the opposite of what a capital file requires.

## What would settle it

* Independent residue testing of the shirt, the microphone and transmitter parts, the SUV and the soil.
* The full autopsy report, including whether charred plastic or device material was found in the wound.
* The complete ATF fragment report and the fragment itself, for independent examination.
* The original, unedited Canon XA55 audio and every other recording of the moment.
* Chain-of-custody records for the microphone and transmitter after the event.
