# 62. An exploding microphone as the neck event

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any living person or organization placed, built, triggered or knew of any device, or had foreknowledge of the killing. Nothing indicates that anyone on the security team, the AV team or the TPUSA staff knew of any device. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

At about 12:23 p.m. on **September 10, 2025**, Charlie Kirk was speaking into a wireless microphone under a tent at Utah Valley University when he suffered a sudden, fatal injury to the neck. The government's narrative is that a single .30-06 round fired from the Losee Center roof, roughly 142 to 175 yards away, caused it.

The large majority of citizen investigators have reached a different conclusion: the fatal event was an **explosive device at the microphone**. This is the central cause-of-death question in the whole case, and the full evidence is gathered on the [Microphone section](/Mic/overview).

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Homicide by gunshot per the medical examiner. No explosive residue. Mic-bomb claims are fact-checked garbage. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | The investigators' conclusion. Sudden neck trauma, no muzzle flash on any public video, and the "miracle" stop fit an exploding or rigged microphone. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Supporters of the government's narrative put it this way. At the July 2026 preliminary hearing, DPS agent David Hull testified that the manner of death was homicide by a gunshot to the neck and that a bullet fragment was recovered ([autopsy sealed](/CoverUp/Autopsy_Sealed_Examiner)). A rifle was found. Most witnesses heard one shot. They say no explosive residue has been reported, and that an explosion would leave visible smoke, a point debater Andrew Wilson made in August 2026. Mainstream fact-checks have dismissed the microphone claims.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators answer each point. On residue, they say nothing was tested in public at all: the soil, the pavers, the clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [reportedly hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no released residue result. The absence of a result is not a negative result, and residue on fabric is [recoverable with standard lab methods](/Mic/explosive-residue).

On the video and audio, [frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears. The [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly separates a louder detonation at the tent, about 46 meters from the camera, from a distant muzzle report about 120 meters away. Veterans Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt said on air that it looked like an explosion. On smoke, the [shaped-charge demo page](/Mic/Demo_of_shape_charge) sets out why a large demolition charge is the wrong comparison for a small device.

On the wound, a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and cause massive tissue damage. Instead, a relayed account from TPUSA-affiliated figures described the surgeon calling it an "absolute miracle" that the bullet did not exit ([hospital claims](/Medical/hospital_people_and_bullet_claims)). Investigators say that "miracle" is exactly what a device at the neck, rather than a rifle round, would look like. The microphone was also [routed under the shirt](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), unlike prior outdoor events. The ATF fragment comparison is [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive).

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

This is the issue that decides the others. If Charlie Kirk was killed by a device at the microphone, then whoever was on the Losee Center roof did not cause the death, and the rooftop case against Robinson becomes beside the point. Investigators say that is why the residue evidence matters so much, and why its handling is part of the reason they believe he is being framed.

## What would settle it

* Independent residue testing of the shirt, the microphone and transmitter parts, the SUV and the soil.
* The full autopsy report, including whether charred plastic or device material was found in the wound.
* The complete ATF fragment report and the fragment itself, for independent examination.
* The original, unedited Canon XA55 audio and every other recording of the moment.
* Chain-of-custody records for the microphone and transmitter after the event.
