# 28. The official movement story: Chick-fil-A, clothes change, garage, roof, woods

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that any investigator, officer, or agency fabricated or altered video, or
acted improperly. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not convicted, has not entered
a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The TPUSA representatives mentioned in the testimony are not
accused of anything, and this site makes no claim that they knew of or had any role in the killing.
:::

## The issue in plain words

At the preliminary hearing in July 2026, Utah investigator Agent David Hull walked the court through a
video compilation, **State's Exhibit 12**, that the state says follows [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)
across the UVU campus on September 10, 2025. That compilation is the backbone of the identity case.
The event itself is the [UVU public speaking event](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU).

Per Hull's Day 2 testimony, a man in a maroon or red T-shirt, gray or olive shorts and Converse shoes
parks in a campus garage around **8:30 AM**, visits the courtyard and talks with TPUSA representatives,
and is back at the car about **9:25 AM**. He returns on foot just after **10:00 AM** with a blue backpack,
buys food at the Chick-fil-A in the Sorenson Center, crosses Campus Drive into a wooded area, and comes
back without the backpack. He goes to the railing that gives access to the Losee Center roof, comes back
down, and walks off campus just before **11:00 AM**.

Then a figure returns "in different clothing." At about **11:53 AM to 12:15 PM** a person climbs the
exterior stairs, rolls over the railing, runs to the southwest corner, and lies prone until the
**12:23:28** shot. Afterwards the person runs to the northeast corner, drops to the grass, and crosses
Campus Drive into the woods at **12:24:42**. The state's version of the rooftop figure is
[Fed claim: the rooftop figure is Robinson](/Narrative/fed_narrative/rooftop-figure-is-robinson).

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says this is what good investigative work looks like. Many cameras, one route, one
car with distinctive wheels, one set of shoes. The man arrives, scouts, leaves, changes, returns, climbs,
waits, and runs. Every stage is on video, the timestamps line up with the shot, and the path ends in
the woods where the rifle was later recovered.

CBS News put the package in one sentence on **July 7, 2026**: the man charged "strolled Utah Valley
University in shorts and a T-shirt, bought a meal at Chick-fil-A and made contact with Kirk's staff,
before returning in different clothes to shoot the conservative activist from a rooftop." CNN's
takeaways the same week counted **four** campus appearances: twice before the shot, at the shot, and
after midnight. Governor Spencer Cox had already described a maroon T-shirt, light shorts, and a later
change into dark clothing. Hull told the court the returning figure wore a dark long-sleeved shirt and
jeans, same Converse. Official-side commentators who attended the hearing said the compilation was
devastating, that the room gasped, and that identity was closed.

Nobody, on this view, needs a face in every frame when the route, the car, and the clothing sequence
all point the same way. The court rifle, the DNA, and the roommate texts do the rest. See
[Theory: Tyler Robinson — official suspect](/Killer/tyler-robinson).

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the track is a chain of separate clips, each identified by resemblance, and that the
weakest link sits exactly where it matters. The change of clothes itself is **not on camera**. When the
prosecutor asked Hull why he believed the returning figure in different clothes was Robinson, Hull
answered: **"The shoes are the same and the images that we have from my viewing and seeing Mr. Robinson
on the video, it's the same person."** That is the identification. It is in
[Clothing change and Agent David Hull](/Tyler_Robinson/clothing-change-agent-david-hull).

Hull also told the court the returning figure walked **"with a gait or a limp,"** noticeable on the
approach to the Losee building around 11:53 AM. Later the prosecutor asked about the roof run. Hull
said that after the figure went over the railing, **"there is no longer a visible limp or gait."**
Investigators ask how one body limps on the walk in and runs cleanly on the roof. Some see a second
person picked up after the change. The official side can answer that a limp can be faked or dropped
under stress, but no witness has said so on the record. The mismatch table is
[Stairs Guy vs Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/Stairs_Guy) and
[Stairs Guy identity gap](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap).

Public accounts of the clothing do not agree either. Governor Cox described a change on the roof and a
change back. Hull described a change before the return. A construction-crew witness and the rooftop
video witness describe black clothing and a mask. None of the changes is shown on video. What campus
cameras exist, and what has been released, is [UVU campus surveillance](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance).
Investigators' chain on the roof figure is
[Chain: the rooftop figure is not identified as Robinson](/Narrative/chains/rooftop-figure-not-robinson).

Candace Owens has walked the full day as one continuous clock: a 4 AM start in St. George, a maroon
shirt, Chick-fil-A, a clothes change, a Wordle score at 11:28 AM, the shot, a jump, a change back,
Dairy Queen, a three-hour drive to Panguitch, a return into a manhunt, the Goforth stop, and a maroon
shirt again at arrest. She calls that sequence absurd on its face. The driving-day page is
[The 25-hour driving day](/Tyler_Robinson/twenty-five-hour-driving-timeline). Citizen investigators
conclude the fatal event was an explosive at the microphone, which would make the roof track a
distraction even if every clip were the same body. That is [Mic](/Mic/overview) and
[Gun and bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview). Electrocution claims are uncorroborated and are not their
finding.

## When this fight ran

* **12–16 September 2025.** Governor Cox's press package: maroon shirt, clothes change, Dodge
  Challenger, campus track. Official-side accounts treated identity as closed from day one.
* **7–11 July 2026.** Day 2 of the preliminary hearing. Exhibit 12 is played. CBS, CNN, Court TV,
  KUER, Deseret News, and Fox ran the Chick-fil-A-to-roof compilation as the identity case.
  Official-side commentators said conspiracies were dismantled. Same-day attendees disputed that
  the public saw a face-clear 4K shot.
* **August 2026.** Owens and other investigators restated the 25-hour day against the hearing
  compilation. The splice at the clothes change stayed the live objection.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If the track is one continuously identified person, it is strong evidence. If it is a morning man, an
unfilmed change, and an afternoon figure matched by shoes and resemblance, then the roof figure is tied
to Robinson by inference, not by sight. That is the gap where investigators say a frame could be built.
He has entered no guilty plea. Probable cause at a bind-over is the lowest bar. The
[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) hub starts from that gap.

## What would settle it

* The full native campus footage, unedited, with metadata, rather than a prosecution compilation.
* Any footage of the clothing change, or proof that none exists for that window.
* A qualified gait comparison shared with the defense, covering both the limp and the roof run.
* The discarded clothing and backpack, with forensic results and where each was found.
* Chick-fil-A purchase records for that morning, which would name the card used.
* Any enhanced or 4K frames the state relies on, shown in open court. See
  [Enhanced suspect photos](/FBI/Enhanced_Suspect_Photos).
