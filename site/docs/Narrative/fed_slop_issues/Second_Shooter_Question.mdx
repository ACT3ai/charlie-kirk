# 61. Whether there was a second shooter

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any identified living person fired a weapon, took part in the killing, or had foreknowledge of it. Figures reported on other roofs may be workers, students, security or coincidence; this site makes no claim that anyone on the security, AV or TPUSA staff fired or knew of any plan. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

The government's narrative has a single shooter: one figure on the roof of the Losee Center, one bolt-action rifle, one shot at about 12:23 p.m. on **September 10, 2025**, fired from roughly 142 to 175 yards away. The rifle later recovered in the woods is the one in court. That is the [lone-gunman claim](/Narrative/fed_narrative/lone-gunman).

A "second shooter" means a second source of the fatal injury: another position, another weapon, or another person. This issue asks whether the evidence released so far actually rules that out.

The citizen investigators' own conclusion on the cause of death is an [explosive device at the microphone](/Mic/overview), not a rifle. The second-shooter question sits beside that conclusion: both start from the same gap, which is that the single rooftop shot has never been shown. This site does not name a second gunman, and it does not claim one. It records why the origin is still open.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | One jumper, one rifle, one bullet. The campus track plus the Mauser close the origin. A second shooter is slop. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | No public firing video, a 911 tactical kit that does not match the court rifle, and a wound path that may not fit a single roof line of fire. A second origin stays open. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: campus cameras track a person to the roof and away from it. A figure is seen dropping off the building after the shot. The rifle was found, wrapped in a towel, along the escape route, and DNA results were reported on it. One shot was heard by most witnesses.

At the July 2026 preliminary hearing, former UVU officer Christopher Bagley testified he found a "sniper pad" — elbow, knee and foot impressions in rooftop gravel — on the Losee Center with a line of sight to the tent. Former State Bureau of Investigation agent David Hull narrated surveillance that, prosecutors say, shows the charged man climbing a rail, going prone at 12:22 p.m., and moving after 12:23 p.m. Official-side accounts treat that compilation as the origin closing.

Every extra shooter theory, on this view, needs extra people, extra weapons and extra silence, and none of them has produced a second weapon, a second casing or a second person in court. Viral posts in **July 2026** about "another bullet on a different roof" were treated as rumor. The simplest account that fits the known facts, they say, is one shooter. The [official-suspect theory page](/Killer/tyler-robinson) is that package.

Official-side hearing coverage also leaned on "one shot heard." Most people under the tent heard a bang. One bang, on that telling, is one rifle. Investigators answer that a shaped charge at the microphone and a distant muzzle, or a muzzle with no hit, can occupy the same second in a crowd. Counting bangs from a courtyard is not a trajectory. It is a memory.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with what has not been shown. According to hearing summaries on the [more reasons page](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons), a witness asked in court whether there is any footage of the alleged shooting from the roof answered that there is not. Reviewers of the available footage report they cannot see a [muzzle flash or recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil) at the moment of the shot.

Next, the description. First-hour [911 and dispatch audio](/Witnesses/calls-rooftop-witness) describes all black, a black long gun, a black tactical helmet, a black mask and possibly a tactical vest. The man who says he filmed the roof figure before the shot describes tactical movement and a long gun, and says he stopped recording about five seconds before it fired. Project Constitution has kept that tape in circulation: the kit matches neither the jeans-and-T-shirt figure tied to Robinson nor the towel-wrapped Mauser in court. The [black-clad suspect](/CoverUp/Black_Clad_Suspect_Dropped) and the [black-clothing photo discrepancy](/FBI/Black_Clothing_Photo) are the same mismatch in two files.

Then the wound. Analysts compiled on the [trajectory page](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch) note that a shot from about 120 meters east, angled down roughly 5 to 9 degrees, would be expected to strike the face or jaw first, while the reported entry was lower on the neck and more straight-on. The ATF comparison of the recovered fragment to the rifle is reported as [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive). Citizen posts also report [figures on other roofs](/Roof/multiple-roofs-figures) that morning. Close-range and non-rifle theories are routed on the [alternative mechanisms page](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).

The [lone-gunman chain](/Narrative/chains/not-a-lone-gunman) is the wider argument: even if a figure was on Losee, the intelligence indicators, the 911 kit, and the wound path are more than one apprentice can absorb. The [gun-and-bullet hub](/Gun_Bullet/overview) is the physical file.

This site does not fill the open origin with a named second gunman. Issue 64 and Issue 65 exist to say that plainly. The honest residue of the gap is: the rooftop rifle as the fatal mechanism is unproven in public, the 911 kit does not match the court rifle, and the wound path may not match a single roof line. Those three facts keep a second origin on the board. They do not identify a person.

## What is actually in the public record

* The state charges one man, one Losee Center roof, one Mauser, one fatal shot at about 12:23 p.m.
* Hearing testimony described a "sniper pad" and a compilation of campus video. A witness asked whether any footage shows the shot being fired said there is not.
* First-hour 911 and dispatch audio describes all-black tactical kit, helmet, mask, black long gun. That is not the jeans-and-T-shirt figure tied to Robinson, and not the towel-wrapped court rifle as described.
* The ATF fragment-to-rifle comparison is reported as inconclusive. Trajectory analysts dispute a clean roof-to-neck path.
* Other roofs that morning have been claimed in citizen posts. Native footage of those roofs has not been published. This site names no second gunman.

## When this fight ran

* **September 10–16, 2025.** Day-of and charging week. "Acted alone" is the state's first lock. 911 tactical-kit audio is already on the tape and already being dropped from the public clothing story.
* **July 6–11, 2026.** Hearing week. Official-side: one jumper, one rifle, conspiracy theories "dismantled." Investigator-side: still no firing video; 911 kit still unmatched.
* **July 7, 2026.** A viral "second bullet on a different roof" post. Official coverage treated it as rumor. Investigators treated it as a reminder that other roofs were never published at native resolution.
* **September 1, 2026.** Bind-over. Probable cause for one defendant is sold as a closed origin.
* **September 16, 2026.** Kirk family notice of claim against UVU still frames a single rooftop assassin. That civil filing, investigators note, assumes the origin the criminal file has not shown.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The case against Robinson depends on the rooftop shot being the fatal one. If the fatal injury came from anywhere else — whether a device at the microphone or a second position — then his presence on or near a roof does not make him the killer. A second origin left unexamined is exactly how a [patsy](/Killer/Patsies_Distraction_Actors) ends up carrying the whole case. That is the core of [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and of the [real-killer map](/Killer/overview).

Keep the two questions separate. "Was there a second gunman?" is open because the first origin is unshown. "What killed Charlie?" is the question investigators answer with the microphone. Filling the gap with a named person is how the official side gets to call the whole file slop.

## What would settle it

* Any footage showing the shot being fired, or a formal statement that none exists.
* The full autopsy, including entry location, angle and whether there was an exit.
* The complete ATF fragment report and all projectile material recovered.
* The original 911 recordings and dispatch logs, with timestamps.
* Native-resolution footage of the other campus rooftops for the hour before the shot.
