# 61. Whether there was a second shooter

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page says any identified living person fired a weapon, took part in the killing, or had foreknowledge of it. Figures reported on other roofs may be workers, students, security or coincidence; this site makes no claim that anyone on the security, AV or TPUSA staff fired or knew of any plan. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent.
:::

## The issue in plain words

The government's narrative has a single shooter: one figure on the roof of the Losee Center, one bolt-action rifle, one shot at about 12:23 p.m. on **September 10, 2025**, fired from roughly 142 to 175 yards away. The rifle later recovered in the woods is the one in court.

A "second shooter" means a second source of the fatal injury: another position, another weapon, or another person. This issue asks whether the evidence released so far actually rules that out.

The citizen investigators' own conclusion on the cause of death is an [explosive device at the microphone](/Mic/overview), not a rifle. The second-shooter question sits beside that conclusion: both start from the same gap, which is that the single rooftop shot has never been shown.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | One jumper, one rifle, one bullet. The campus track plus the Mauser close the origin. A second shooter is slop. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | No public firing video, a 911 tactical kit that does not match the court rifle, and a wound path that may not fit a single roof line of fire. A second origin stays open. |

## The fed slop side, in full

Stated fairly: campus cameras track a person to the roof and away from it. A figure is seen dropping off the building after the shot. The rifle was found, wrapped in a towel, along the escape route, and DNA results were reported on it. One shot was heard by most witnesses. Every extra shooter theory needs extra people, extra weapons and extra silence, and none of them has produced a second weapon, a second casing or a second person. The simplest account that fits the known facts is one shooter.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators start with what has not been shown. According to hearing summaries on the [more reasons page](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons), a witness asked in court whether there is any footage of the alleged shooting from the roof answered that there is not. Reviewers of the available footage report they cannot see a [muzzle flash or recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil) at the moment of the shot.

Next, the description. First-hour [911 and dispatch audio](/Witnesses/911-calls-rooftop-witness) describes all black, a black long gun, a black tactical helmet, a black mask and possibly a tactical vest. The man who says he filmed the roof figure before the shot describes tactical movement and a long gun, and says he stopped recording about five seconds before it fired. Investigators say that kit matches neither the jeans-and-T-shirt figure tied to Robinson nor the towel-wrapped Mauser in court.

Then the wound. Analysts compiled on the [trajectory page](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch) note that a shot from about 120 meters east, angled down roughly 5 to 9 degrees, would be expected to strike the face or jaw first, while the reported entry was lower on the neck and more straight-on. The ATF comparison of the recovered fragment to the rifle is reported as [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive). Citizen posts also report [figures on other roofs](/Roof/multiple-roofs-figures) that morning, and close-range theories are routed on the [alternative mechanisms page](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The case against Robinson depends on the rooftop shot being the fatal one. If the fatal injury came from anywhere else, whether a device at the microphone or a second position, then his presence on or near a roof does not make him the killer. A second origin left unexamined is exactly how a patsy ends up carrying the whole case.

## What would settle it

* Any footage showing the shot being fired, or a formal statement that none exists.
* The full autopsy, including entry location, angle and whether there was an exit.
* The complete ATF fragment report and all projectile material recovered.
* The original 911 recordings and dispatch logs, with timestamps.
* Native-resolution footage of the other campus rooftops for the hour before the shot.
