# 32. Wound entry side and path

:::caution Attributed claims only
Nothing on this page claims that any medical examiner, doctor, prosecutor, or agent misstated evidence
or acted improperly. Tyler Robinson is claimed to be the shooter; he is charged, not convicted, has
not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. The rooftop .30-06 is the government's narrative. Most
citizen investigators conclude the cause of death was an [exploding microphone](/Mic/overview).
:::

## The issue in plain words

Every shot has a direction. If you know where a projectile went in and which way it traveled through
the body, you can draw a line back toward where it came from. That is why the wound path matters so much
in this case.

The government's narrative has [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) struck once in the neck by a rifle fired from the southwest
corner of the Losee Center roof, roughly 142 to 175 yards away and slightly above him, at 12:23 PM on September
10, 2025. The state's own probable-cause brief says the bullet traveled downward and quotes the medical
examiner describing a "complex entrance" wound and obliteration of the left side of the vertebrae from C7 up to C2.

Citizen investigators say that path does not draw a clean line back to that roof. Some point to video
stills that appear to show blood at the right ear and temple, and argue the injury entered on the right
side or from behind, the wrong direction for a clean shot from the front. The still behind that claim is
[Blood at the Right Ear](/Photos/Charlie_Kirk/After/Img_Blood_Right_Ear_7b969b_x). The geometry problem is walked on [Wound Trajectory and Angle](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle) and [Trajectory & Wound Mismatch](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch).

A third reading is the one most investigators hold. A charge at the microphone on his chest and collar
produces damage radiating from a point, with no entrance-to-exit track at all. That would explain why no
single line of fire fits.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | A single neck shot from the roof line of fire. Fragments inside. No second vector required. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Entry described under the right ear / right-rear, which is the wrong clock for a clean frontal roof shot. The path is the second-angle problem. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The official side says the medical examiner found a gunshot wound of the neck and ruled it a homicide.
Fragments consistent with a .30-caliber jacket were recovered. Officer Bagley testified at the July 2026 hearing that gravel on the southwest side of the Losee roof looked like a prone "sniper pad" with a clear line of sight to the tent. Bodies turn and heads move in the instant
before a shot, so a wound can look odd from a single camera angle. Stills of blood on skin do not show
an entry wound. Only a pathologist examining the wound margins can say which hole is which, and the
examiner did.

On this view there is no need for a second shooter or a second direction. Official-side commentators after the hearing treated "single gunshot wound to the neck with .30-06 bullet fragments" as enough. A later forensic-physics paper circulating in July 2026 argued a non-bonded .30-06 soft point at about 142 yards can fragment in the neck, produce no exit, and still match the official geometry. Commentators who attended the hearing said the roof figure and the wound are one event. Public stills, they say, are not the autopsy.

The government's answer to the right-ear stills is that blood runs. A wound on the left-front neck of a seated man can throw or drain blood onto the right ear in the seconds after impact. The front-neck hole is the entrance. The C2-to-C7 language is the track of a high-energy round that hit cervical bone and broke up. No second vector is required.

## What citizen investigators say

Investigators say the state's own brief pulls in two directions. That tension is the subject of [The State's Brief: Downward Trajectory vs C2–C7 Damage](/Medical/state_brief_trajectory_contradiction). A downward shot from the roof, they
argue, should reach the jaw or face first — a 5 to 9 degree descent from an elevated roof over ~150 yards — yet the track described runs down the left side of the neck
through the vertebrae from C2 to C7. Commentary summarized on this site places the observed entry between
the larynx and the trachea, left of the spine, and more straight-on than a steep rooftop descent. See also [line of sight behind the roof lip](/Roof/roof-line-of-sight).

A separate thread argues the origin was to the south, not the east where the roof sits. Another argues
from video that the crowd heard the sound and saw the impact at the same moment, which they say points
to a source far closer than 150 yards. [Possible shooter locations](/Suspects/Shooting_Locations) collects those alternatives without picking one as proven. The entrance-versus-exit dispute — is the front-neck hole the way in, or the way out? — is on [Is the Front Neck Wound an Entrance or an Exit?](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/entrance-or-exit).

A USMC-sniper analysis circulated on X in mid-September 2025 argued the shot came from slightly behind Charlie's right side, that viewers were looking at an exit, and that a .30-06 from the Losee roof does not produce that picture. Later citizen posts kept returning to the right-ear still. Official-side replies called the artifact debris or runoff. Investigators answer that the state's brief never published a wound diagram that would settle it.

The "complex entrance" phrase, as quoted from the examiner, is doing a lot of work. Investigators read it as the state's way of covering a wound that does not look like a clean distant rifle hole. A contact or near-contact event at the collar — a shaped charge in the microphone — would look complex because it is not a bullet track. That is why they keep asking for the photographs, not for another agent's summary of page one of [Exhibit 11](/Medical/charlie_kirk_medical_examiner_report).

[Charlie's Injuries](/Medical/charlies_injuries) then widens the problem. The July 2026 filing quotes damage in both sides of the chest, around the heart, in two brain regions, and through airway cartilages. [Ballistics and Wound Analysis](/Medical/ballistics_and_wound_analysis) sets that list next to a single roof line of fire. Investigators say a single vector from one roof does not buy all of that. Their conclusion is the [chain that it was not a .30-06](/Narrative/chains/not-a-thirty-06): the fatal event was a device at the microphone, which has no entrance-to-exit clock at all. [No muzzle flash or recoil](/Roof/no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil) on the roof at the moment of impact is the matching video gap. Early witnesses who said chest or heart, collected at [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart), fit a blast at the collar better than a left-neck entrance from the east.

## When this fight ran

* **10–16 September 2025.** First stills and slowed video. Right-ear / right-rear entry claims appear within days. Official line: one neck shot from the roof.
* **Late September 2025.** Sniper-angle and "exit on the left-front" videos circulate. Official-side accounts call them artifacts.
* **July 6–11, 2026.** Hearing: "complex entrance," C2–C7 language, Bagley's sniper-pad testimony. Official-side commentators treat trajectory as closed.
* **Late July 2026.** Injury-list filing screenshot spreads. Investigators argue the quoted damage is multi-system, not one track.
* **August–September 2026.** Bind-over and fact-check cycle repeats "single neck shot, fragments inside." The wound diagrams stay unpublished.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

The case against [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) places him on one roof in one direction. If the wound path cannot be drawn
back to that roof, then even a perfect identification of the roof figure does not show that figure
caused the death. The path is where the identity case and the cause-of-death case meet. That is why
[Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/thirty-06-did-not-kill-charlie) treats trajectory as a pillar, not a side debate.

## What would settle it

* The medical examiner's wound diagrams and photographs, showing entry and any exit.
* Sworn testimony from the examining doctor on direction, angle, and the C2 to C7 damage.
* A 3D reconstruction from the state's scan of the courtyard, tested against the roof position.
* Frame-accurate native video of the moment of impact from every camera angle.
