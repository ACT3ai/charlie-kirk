# 2. The exact time and date Tyler Robinson was taken into custody

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. Every
official and officer named here is a living person charged with nothing. This site makes no claim that any
officer, agent, sheriff, or prosecutor fabricated a record, lied, or acted improperly. The inferences below
are arguments made by named investigators from court filings and public records.
:::

## The issue in plain words

After Charlie Kirk was killed on September 10, 2025, the public was told a clear story. The next
evening, September 11, the FBI released photos of a person of interest at about **7:55 PM**. Around
**7:57 to 8:00 PM**, a Discord message from an account tied to Robinson said "It was me at UVU yesterday."
The Washington County sheriff says he got his first call naming Robinson at **8:02 PM**. Robinson then
arrived with his parents around **9 PM**, and the arrest was logged at **10:00 PM**.

That order of events matters. A man can only type a Discord confession if he is still free and still holds
his phone. So the question of this issue is simple: **when did Tyler Robinson actually go into custody?**

A defense filing quotes a Bates-stamped video, **003996-R2**, in which an officer reads Robinson his rights
at a logged **6:25 PM** and Robinson asks for a lawyer at **6:26 PM**. If that was September 11, the whole
public sequence runs backwards.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Evening of Sept 11 after 7:50 p.m., commonly the ~9:00 p.m. Washington County window, or loosely Sept 12. Matches the Discord post and the official surrender story. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Sept 11 itself, at 6:25 p.m. or earlier, not after 7:50 p.m. The later clock is the one that lines up with the aircraft and the Discord post. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The fed-slop side says custody came on the evening of September 11 **after 7:50 PM**, usually put in
the roughly **9:00 PM** Washington County window, or loosely on September 12. That matches the Discord post
and the family surrender story. The strongest point on this side is real: **the calendar date is not printed
on the excerpt** of Bates 003996-R2 as circulated. The state's reading is that the Miranda warning happened
on **September 12**, after transport, which would put it many hours after the Discord message.

Commentators on this side also offer a technical reading: that the "06:25 PM" is a body-camera transcription
clock misread, which would move the warning to after midnight. Investigative holds are lawful, booking-sheet
fields are often filled in later, and small agencies lose video. On this view, nothing is out of place.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say custody came on **September 11 itself, at 6:25 PM or earlier**. They point
to several records that line up on that evening. The discovery index lists exhibit **Bates 3996-R1** as
"Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025." On the tape, Robinson says his lawyer's office is "closed for the night."
September 11 was a **Thursday**. Attorney and commentator Baron Coleman argues that on a Friday a person
would say closed "for the weekend." Coleman has said the state's "entire narrative depends on him turning
himself in after nine o'clock p.m."

The Utah County booking sheet lists the arrest at **10:00 PM September 11** and intake at **1:58 AM
September 12**, with a State Bureau of Investigation agent as arresting officer. Investigators argue that an
agent starting from northern Utah had to leave around **6:30 PM** to be in Washington County by 10 PM, which
is before the sheriff's 8:02 PM first call. Washington County also told a public-records hearing it found
**no bodycam** of the intake.

The family account, as relayed by citizen journalists, is that his parents went with him **in the afternoon**
and that his phone was taken on arrival. Investigators say the later clock is simply the one the Discord
post needs.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If Robinson was Mirandized and without his phone at 6:25 PM, he did not type "It was me at UVU
yesterday" at about 7:57 PM. Take away that message and the public confession rests on third-party screens
and a roommate's narration. It also means the 7:55 PM wanted-person release went out for a man already in
an interview room. A custody clock that has to be moved to fit the confession is one of the clearest signs,
investigators say, that the story was built around a conclusion.

## What would settle it

* The date metadata on the Bates 003996-R1 and R2 video files.
* The property log showing when and by whom Robinson's phone was taken.
* The arresting agent's dispatch, departure time, and route for September 11.
* Discord's server-side record for the message: timestamp, IP, and device.
* The written reports Utah law requires when a deputy's body camera was not recording.
