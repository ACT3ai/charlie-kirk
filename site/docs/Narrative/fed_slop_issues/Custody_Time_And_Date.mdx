# 2. The exact time and date Tyler Robinson was taken into custody

:::caution Attributed claims only
Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**, has not entered a guilty plea, and is presumed innocent. Every
official and officer named here is a living person charged with nothing. This site makes no claim that any
officer, agent, sheriff, or prosecutor fabricated a record, lied, or acted improperly. The inferences below
are arguments made by named investigators from court filings and public records.
:::

## The issue in plain words

After [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed on September 10, 2025, the public was told a
clear story. The next evening, September 11, the FBI released photos of a person of interest at about
**7:55 PM**. Around **7:57 to 8:00 PM**, a Discord message from an account tied to
[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) said "It was me at UVU yesterday." The Washington County
sheriff says he got his first call naming Robinson at **8:02 PM**. Robinson then arrived with his
parents around **9 PM**, and the arrest was logged at **10:00 PM**. Kash Patel, as FBI Director, later
sold that window as a **33-hour manhunt**.

That order of events matters. A man can only type a Discord confession if he is still free and still
holds his phone. So the question of this issue is simple: **when did Tyler Robinson actually go into
custody?**

A defense filing in case 251403576 quotes a Bates-stamped video, **003996-R2**, in which an officer
reads Robinson his rights at a logged **6:25 PM** and Robinson asks for a lawyer at **6:26 PM**, naming
Utah attorney Doug Terry. If that was September 11, the whole public sequence runs backwards.

## Side by side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fed Slop Side** | Evening of Sept 11 after 7:50 p.m., commonly the ~9:00 p.m. Washington County window, or loosely Sept 12. Matches the Discord post and the official surrender story. |
| **Citizen Investigators** | Sept 11 itself, at 6:25 p.m. or earlier, not after 7:50 p.m. The later clock is the one that lines up with the aircraft and the Discord post. |

## The fed slop side, in full

The official-narrative side says custody came on the evening of September 11 **after 7:50 PM**, usually
put in the roughly **9:00 PM** Washington County window, or loosely on September 12. That matches the
Discord post and the family surrender story. Governor Spencer Cox announced the arrest the next
morning. Kash Patel confirmed it at a mid-morning press conference. Outlets repeated that Robinson
was arrested two days after the shooting, and that the Discord message went out "roughly two hours
before" he walked in.

The strongest point on this side is real: **the calendar date is not printed on the excerpt** of Bates
003996-R2 as circulated. A Washington County deputy attorney told *The American Prospect* in April
2026 that the "evening of Robinson's arrest" was **September 12**, because Robinson was never
actually arrested in Washington County but nearly four hours north in Provo, by which point it was
well past midnight. That reading would put the Miranda warning many hours after the Discord message.

Commentators on this side also offer a technical reading: that the "06:25 PM" is a body-camera
transcription clock misread, which would move the warning to after midnight. Investigative holds are
lawful. Booking-sheet fields are often filled in later. Small agencies lose video. On this view,
nothing is out of place. The [investigative-hold counterpoint](/court/mirandize/investigative-hold-counterpoint)
is the official side's best answer, and it is on this site so a reader can take it at full strength.

## What citizen investigators say

Citizen investigators say custody came on **September 11 itself, at 6:25 PM or earlier**. They point
to several records that line up on that evening. The discovery index lists exhibit **Bates 3996-R1**
as "Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025." On the tape, Robinson says his lawyer's office is "closed for the
night." September 11 was a **Thursday**. Attorney and commentator Baron Coleman argues that on a
Friday a person would say closed "for the weekend." Coleman has said the state's "entire narrative
depends on him turning himself in after nine o'clock p.m." His write-up is on
[Baron Coleman: Early Turn-In](/court/mirandize/baron-coleman-early-turn-in).

The Utah County booking sheet lists the arrest at **10:00 PM September 11** and intake at **1:58 AM
September 12**, with a State Bureau of Investigation agent as arresting officer. Investigators argue
that an agent starting from northern Utah had to leave around **6:30 PM** to be in Washington County
by 10 PM, which is before the sheriff's 8:02 PM first call. Washington County also told a
public-records hearing it found **[no bodycam](/court/mirandize/bodycam-grama-no-footage)** of the
intake.

The family account, as relayed by citizen journalists, is that his parents went with him **in the
afternoon** and that his phone was taken on arrival. Investigators say the later clock is simply the
one the Discord post needs. The hour-by-hour reconstruction is on
[6:25 PM Custody vs Discord](/Timeline/custody-vs-discord-confession-timeline) and
[Discord & Custody Timeline](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Discord_Custody_Timeline).

A second clock sits next to the 8:57–9:00 PM window: a HADES-program aircraft tracked over
[St. George on the night of the 11th](/Planes/AXEL21-St-George-Sept-11), near the sheriff's station,
at Discord-post time. Move custody earlier, investigators say, and the Discord dump looks timed to
the aircraft.

## When this fight ran

* **September 11–16, 2025.** The first framing. Official-side accounts and wire copy locked in a
  ~9:00 PM surrender, a Discord confession two hours earlier, and a 33-hour manhunt. Kash Patel's
  Senate remarks on September 16 treated the Discord chat as a live investigative target.
* **March 30 – April 15, 2026.** A defense motion put Bates 003996-R2 in public. *The American
  Prospect* ran the ninety-minute hole. Official-side commentators answered that the Miranda date was
  the 12th, or that a hold made the warning lawful even if it was early.
* **July 2026 preliminary hearing.** An agent testified Robinson arrived at Washington County around
  9:00 PM and that the Discord message was 7:57 PM on the 11th. The hearing did not produce the native
  video metadata.
* **September 1–2, 2026.** Bind-over and a not-guilty plea on all counts. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)
  told readers to go find every influencer who had said he turned himself in and admitted guilt.

## Why it matters for Tyler Robinson

If Robinson was Mirandized and without his phone at 6:25 PM, he did not type "It was me at UVU
yesterday" at about 7:57 PM. Take away that message and the public confession rests on third-party
screens and a roommate's narration. It also means the 7:55 PM wanted-person release went out for a
man already in an interview room. A custody clock that has to be moved to fit the confession is one
of the clearest signs, investigators say, that the story was built around a conclusion.

The [fed claim that he confessed on Discord](/Narrative/fed_narrative/discord-confession) and the
[fed claim that he turned himself in](/Narrative/fed_narrative/turned-himself-in) are two halves of
the same package. The [chain that he could not have sent it](/Narrative/chains/miranda-discord-impossible)
and the [chain that he did not turn himself in when they said](/Narrative/chains/did-not-turn-himself-in)
are the investigator answer. The [Utah County jail booking sheet](/court/utah-county-jail-custody) is
the paper that still lists 10:00 PM. The hub for every document is
[The Miranda-Timing Hub](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview).

[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) treats this clock as one of the reasons the charging
package does not prove a shot. Citizen investigators conclude Kirk was killed by an
[explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview). A Discord post that could not have been typed from the
interview room is how a lone-gunman story was sold to the public in the first 48 hours.

## What would settle it

* The date metadata on the Bates 003996-R1 and R2 video files.
* The property log showing when and by whom Robinson's phone was taken.
* The arresting agent's dispatch, departure time, and route for September 11.
* Discord's server-side record for the message: timestamp, IP, and device.
* The written reports Utah law requires when a deputy's body camera was not recording.
* A sworn statement from the Washington County sheriff of the first moment Robinson was not free to leave.
