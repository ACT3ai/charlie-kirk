# "Wiped Out At Any Time" — The Long-Running Premonition

Not every message in this section is about September 2025. One of them describes something Charlie Kirk reportedly felt for **years**.

## The message

A screenshot preserved in the investigation notes — believed to have been sent to [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), though the recipient is not confirmed — reads:

> "Since the beginning of TPUSA, I knew in my gut that I might get wiped out at any time... ([KM-01](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-01-wiped-out)) I cannot explain it... But I dream about it all the time... Like all the time..."

## Why it changes how the other pages read

This message cuts both ways, and both readings deserve to be on the page.

**It weakens the causal story.** If [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had a standing, unexplained sense of doom dating back to TPUSA's founding — one he says he *could not explain* and that predates the Israel dispute by a decade — then his 2025 statements are not necessarily a response to a specific new danger. They may be a long-standing anxiety expressed again under stress. That is the most obvious counterargument to the entire "he knew it was coming" thesis, and it comes from Kirk's own reported words.

**It strengthens the record.** At the same time, it establishes that expressing fear of being killed was **in character** for him. That matters against the suggestion that the 2025 messages were fabricated after the fact by people who wanted a conspiracy: if he talked this way habitually, messages in that register are more plausible, not less.

## What would distinguish the two readings

Escalation. A background fear is constant; a response to a real threat spikes. The question is whether the frequency and specificity of these statements **increased sharply** in August and September 2025 — specific dates, specific fears, specific people warned — compared to the preceding years. The [September 9 messages](./sept-9-they-are-going-to-kill-me), if they exist as described, are specific in a way this one is not: a date ("tomorrow"), an actor ("they"), and multiple recipients in a single day.

## Open questions

2. Is a full, unredacted screenshot available anywhere?
3. Do earlier examples exist — statements of this kind from 2015, 2018, or 2022?
4. Did anyone close to Kirk describe a change in tone during summer 2025?
