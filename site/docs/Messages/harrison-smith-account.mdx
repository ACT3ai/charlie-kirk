# Harrison Smith's Account

**[Harrison Smith](/People/harrison-smith)** is an Infowars host. He went public stating that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had told him, in the period before the assassination, that he feared being taken out over his shifting position on Israel.

## What is attributed to Smith

Investigation notes record Smith as a **named, on-the-record source** for the account that Kirk expressed fear of being killed. Compilations of the widely circulated **["If I go against Israel, I think they will kill me"](./if-i-go-against-israel)** quote frequently cite an **August 14, 2025 Harrison Smith report** as their origin, including posts such as [@Rahul_EIWF](https://x.com/Rahul_EIWF/status/1983573879650988408) and [@jakeshieldsajj](https://x.com/jakeshieldsajj/status/1975815285199544692).

## Why the origin question matters here

Smith's account is important less for what it adds than for what it **explains**. A very large number of the circulating quotes trace back to a small number of original reports. If the August 13–14 material and the September 9 material both flow through the same handful of tellers, then the apparent weight of "many independent sources" is partly an illusion of amplification.

This page therefore records Smith's report as **one origin point**, not as independent confirmation of [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek)'s, [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet)'s, or [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)' accounts. Whether those are genuinely independent is one of the open questions of the whole section.

## Weighing the source

Smith is a named person speaking under his own byline, which is stronger than anonymous sourcing. He also works for an outlet with a strongly stated editorial position, which is a reason for readers to look for corroboration rather than a reason to discount him. Both things are true at once. **Nothing here alleges wrongdoing by Smith or by anyone he names.**

## Open questions

1. What exactly did Smith say Kirk told him, in Smith's own words and on what date?
2. Was the communication in person, by phone, or in writing — and does any artifact survive?
3. Did Smith's report precede or follow the other August 2025 accounts?
4. Has Smith been contacted by investigators?
