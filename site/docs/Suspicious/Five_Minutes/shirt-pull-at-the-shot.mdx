# The Shirt Pulled at the Instant of the Shot (Claims)

These frames sit at the exact second Charlie Kirk was killed, which is why they are argued over so hard: they are the closest visual record anyone has of the [explosive-device explanation for his death](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie).

:::caution Attributed claims only
The frame readings below are investigators' interpretations of compressed social-media video; the original camera files have not been released. The government's narrative is a .30-06 rifle shot; citizen investigators have largely concluded an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview) killed him. Everyone named below is discussed as a claimant or a subject of claims, not as someone accused of a crime by this site. Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**.
:::

## What is alleged

[@matt82704417](/Influencers/x/matt82704417) (Matthew St Baker), crediting Jon Bray with the underlying work, posted what he called "**visual proof**" that the wound was caused by an internal explosion rather than a rifle round. His reading of the footage runs in five steps: the blast reportedly starts at the mic receiver pack on Kirk's left belt; the explosive force drives an object **up** under the shirt; the shirt is reportedly "pinched and yanked violently over his left shoulder" as the object travels toward the neck wound; the object then falls, tracking diagonally under the fabric; and a bulge is said to be visible as it comes to rest on the lower-right side of the shirt. He identifies the charge as PETN and writes: "It's over. The 30.06 gunshot narrative is dead."

The claim circulates alongside related exploding-mic material. Candace Owens has argued a rigged microphone explains a cluster of oddities at the scene, and has said her theory is that a shaped charge was intended for the chest and struck the neck instead. [@DiligentDenizen](/Influencers/x/diligent-denizen-2) amplified Owens photographs reported to show shattered tempered glass on the SUV floor, framed as debris consistent with a fragmented RØDE transmitter. [@realhonestash](/Influencers/x/realhonestash) amplified an Owens segment on early witnesses who described a chest wound.

The argument calls the object at Kirk's belt "**the modified battery pack**." Whether the pack was modified is exactly what a residue and teardown examination of the recovered RØDE hardware would show — and no such examination has been released.

## How investigators answer the objections

**The mic-versus-pendant question is about where the charge sat, not whether there was one.** The explosives authority investigators cite, [@cletus_jethro](/Influencers/x/cletus-jethro) — a former USMC explosive breacher — writes that "**if you're setting off a charge, you need to anchor it, so the theories about an explosive device in his lapel microphone on his shirt makes no sense, since if that happened, the mike would move left, not right**, from the force of the blast being initiated." He adds that "something unanchored like the lapel mic on his shirt would not have been very accurate," and that a chain-anchored pendant could aim a charge. His argument places the device at the pendant rather than the lapel pack — it is still an explosive at Kirk's chest and neck, not a rifle round. See [Mic vs Pendant](/Mic/Mic_vs_Pendant).

**The smoke-signature objection compares the wrong size of charge.** Defenders of the government's rifle narrative, including [@Trillion0x](/Influencers/x/troofevades) and [@troofevades](/Influencers/x/troofevades), circulated video of a full-size shaped charge producing a fireball and smoke cloud and argue nothing comparable appears at UVU — [@troofevades](/Influencers/x/troofevades) put it as "that's a lot of smoke that I don't see at the CK event." [@Trillion0x](/Influencers/x/troofevades) also notes that **Jon Bray has promised a simulated demonstration of his theory for over six months and has not produced one**, despite the test being, in his words, less than an afternoon's work. Investigators answer that the modeled device is a ~2 g micro-charge, not a breaching charge, and that an inward-firing charge sends its energy into the body rather than out as a fireball. The test that would settle it directly, residue chemistry on the recovered RØDE hardware, was always in the authorities' hands and has never been released.

**The frame order is the point.** Defenders of the rifle narrative say fabric on a body struck by a projectile moves unpredictably. Investigators answer that the shirt movement begins at the chest **before** the neck wound opens, which a bullet entering the neck does not explain — see [the shirt moves before blood appears](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/shirt-before-blood).

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## What would settle it

1. **Release the residue chemistry.** Testing of the recovered RØDE transmitter, battery and clasp for PETN or other explosive residue would settle the question directly.
2. Obtain the original, uncompressed camera file rather than a re-encoded social-media copy, and have a video forensics examiner analyze it.
3. Produce the RØDE transmitter and battery pack from evidence and have them examined for explosive residue and modification.
4. Establish from the original footage whether the charge sat in the lapel pack or the pendant — the remaining question among investigators.
