## What is alleged

This page is about **mechanism**, not about the venue or the AV workflow — the venue framing lives elsewhere on this site. The relevance to cause of death is direct: under-shirt routing put the transmitter **against Kirk's chest and neck**, out of view of the cameras — at exactly the area that was injured.

The reported facts, per the investigation file, are these. The RØDE Wireless PRO transmitter measures **44 × 45.3 × 18.5mm**. It was reportedly worn under Kirk's white "FREEDOM" shirt, held by a **MagClip GO** — a magnetic clasp system in which the clip (35.1 × 19.7mm) sits on the outside of the shirt while a neodymium magnet (26 × 17mm) sandwiches the fabric from behind. Investigators add two supporting observations. First, the unit reportedly already carried a **furry windscreen**, which they argue is designed for external clip-on use and would be pointless under fabric. Second, prior TPUSA outdoor debates reportedly show Kirk's lavalier mounted **outside** his clothing, making the September 10 configuration a departure from his own established pattern.

A related claim concerns a **rectangular chest bulge**. Multiple cameras reportedly captured a visible bulge on the upper right chest before the event — described as the 18.5mm-deep housing pushing the fabric outward — with the dark rectangle of the MagClip clasp visible on the shirt surface. After the event, according to this reading, **the bulge is gone while the clasp remains**, which investigators argue means the housing beneath is no longer intact.

## The government's narrative, and the investigators' answer

The government's narrative has nothing to say about the microphone. Its defenders say hiding a transmitter under clothing is routine audio practice, for appearance and to cut wind noise. Investigators answer:

* **It was not Kirk's routine.** Prior TPUSA outdoor debates reportedly show his lavalier mounted **outside** his clothing — see [the AV team mic setup](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup). September 10 was a departure from his own established pattern.
* **The windscreen says external.** The unit reportedly carried a **furry windscreen**, which is designed for external mounting and would be pointless under fabric.
* **The bulge is there before and gone after.** Multiple cameras reportedly show an 18.5mm-deep rectangular bulge on the upper right chest before the event. Afterwards, the clasp remains on the shirt surface but the bulge is gone — which investigators read as the housing no longer being intact.
* **It sat at the epicenter.** Frame analysis places the [spherical expansion](./shirt-before-blood) at the mic position.
* **A burnt object turned up.** Investigators identify [a charred object in the SUV](./burnt-object-transmitter) as a destroyed RØDE transmitter.

No sworn statement on how the microphone was clipped on has been published. Nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device — the question is what was inside the device and where it came from, not who handled it. The charge itself appears to have come from the DoD contract under which AES developed miniaturized anti-personnel charges — see [the AES / DoD contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract).

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## What would put it beyond dispute

1. **Take statements from the AV team**, on the record, about where the transmitter was placed on September 10 and where the device came from.
2. Confirm **which RØDE model was actually used** — the investigation file flags a conflict between the Wireless PRO and the Wireless ME.
3. Produce the transmitter and clasp from evidence and have them examined for damage and explosive residue.
4. Release the **original uncompressed pre-event footage** showing the chest bulge.
