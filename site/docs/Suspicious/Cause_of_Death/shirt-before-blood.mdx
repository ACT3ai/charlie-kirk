## Claim snapshot

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| The claim | The sequence is inverted for a gunshot — the shirt balloons outward and upward, the magnetic mic clasp lifts away, the necklace snaps and flies, and only several frames **later** does blood appear |

## What is alleged

This is the clearest visual evidence behind the investigators' conclusion. The argument is about **order**, not about energy. Analysts describe the sequence this way: first the shirt rapidly deforms, moving outward and upward — at one point, according to the whokilledck.com writeup, the neckline is reportedly approximately **level with Kirk's left ear**. The magnetic clasp on the outside of the shirt reportedly lifts upward and away from the body. A visible pressure wave is described moving through the material. The necklace snaps and flies. And only afterward, several frames later, does bleeding from the neck appear.

Jon Bray (@jonaaronbray) reportedly applied **optical-flow and pixel-flow mapping** to synchronized high-quality angles, including 60fps and 30fps side views, and argues the epicenter of movement sits at the mic position with an **omnidirectional expansion** rather than a linear vector along a bullet's path. He argues there is no evidence of directional tissue displacement of the kind a ~2,700 fps rifle round would produce, and that everything radiates spherically from the microphone location.

The "Blase" post puts the challenge as a falsifiable one: **no gunshot experiment has reproduced a chain bursting away while a t-shirt violently reverberates with zero blood in the initial frame.** In every experiment, the argument runs, there is visible evidence of a bullet entering the subject in that first frame. [@matt82704417](/Influencers/x/matt82704417), crediting Bray, claims a 4K close-up shows an object propelled up under the fabric from the left-side mic pack, then falling diagonally back down to rest at the lower right.

## The government's narrative, and the investigators' answer

Defenders of the government's narrative say a rifle bullet's temporary wound cavity could throw the shirt outward, and that 30fps video is too coarse to show true sequence. Dr. Chris Martenson ([@peakprosperity](/Influencers/x/peakprosperity)) is cited secondhand for exactly that mechanism: a **high-speed cavitation event**, in which a projectile's overpressure expands the neck, throws the fabric and snaps the chain before blood becomes visible. Investigators answer:

* **The motion is omnidirectional.** A bullet travels in a line and pushes tissue along its path. Bray's optical-flow mapping finds the movement radiating **spherically from the mic position** — outward in every direction at once — which is how a blast at a point behaves.
* **The epicenter is the microphone.** The expansion centers on the transmitter location, not on the front-left neck wound where the government's narrative says the round struck.
* **The clasp lifts away.** The magnetic mic clasp reportedly lifts upward and away from the body, and [@matt82704417](/Influencers/x/matt82704417) reports a 4K close-up showing an object propelled up under the fabric from the mic pack.
* **The 60fps angle agrees.** The sequence is reported across multiple synchronized angles, including 60fps side views — not one compressed clip.
* **No gunshot test has reproduced it.** As the "Blase" post puts it, no gunshot experiment has shown a chain bursting away while a t-shirt violently reverberates with zero blood in the first frame. In gunshot footage the entry is visible first.

[@Trillion0x](/Influencers/x/troofevades) notes that **Jon Bray has promised a physical simulation of his theory for over six months and has not produced one**, despite estimating it at less than an afternoon's work. [@Trillion0x](/Influencers/x/troofevades) and [@troofevades](/Influencers/x/troofevades) also point out that a real shaped charge in a demonstration video produces a **large fireball and smoke cloud** with no counterpart in the UVU footage — as @troofevades put it, "that's a lot of smoke that I don't see at the CK event."

**The investigators' answer:** a micro-charge sized for a lapel transmitter would not produce a breaching charge's signature — the demonstration compares the UVU footage to the wrong size of device. A cavitation cavity, for its part, travels along a bullet's path; Bray's mapping finds the motion radiating from the mic position instead. And a simulation is welcome — the filmed comparison listed below is exactly the test investigators are asking for. See the [exploding-mic evidence hub](/Mic/overview).

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## What would put it beyond dispute

1. **Release the original uncompressed camera files** — including the 4K close-up reportedly deleted after it was sent to the FBI — so any video forensics examiner can repeat the optical-flow analysis.
2. Run a filmed comparison: a high-velocity round through a ballistic surrogate wearing a loose t-shirt and chain, and a micro-charge at a lapel transmitter, both at the same distance and frame rate as the UVU footage.
3. Release the autopsy and any test of the shirt for explosive residue.
