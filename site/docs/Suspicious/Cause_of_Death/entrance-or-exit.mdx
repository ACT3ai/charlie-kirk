## What is alleged

Three separate lines of argument all reach the same place: **the front neck wound does not fit the government's rooftop shot.**

One position reasons from neurology. An unattributed note in the investigation file citing "medical experts" states that Kirk's reported **decorticate posturing** "can only be due to a MIDBRAIN injury," and concludes that "the activation of a weapon from high and behind him, creating the exit wound we see in his left neck, is the only explanation which makes any sense." Whatever the exact mechanism, a midbrain injury from a front-entering round at the neck is hard to square with the government's account.

[@stevendenoon](/Influencers/x/stevendenoon) and a collaborator argue that if the front neck wound is an exit, it "clears Tyler" entirely and points to a source **inside the tent**. They propose an Israeli-made **CornerShot** — a hinged weapon with a camera feed and IR laser scope — mounted with a 9mm Glock, arguing it ejects shells downward and would explain a cartridge reportedly seen rolling down the tarp. They also argue the injury does not fit a .30-06, which carries far more energy than the contained wound shows.

A third strand argues from trajectory that a .30-06 **from the front is impossible**, on the grounds that the required turn would demand roughly **12,000 lbs of force**.

Notice what these share. Each, by a different route, rules out the government's picture of a .30-06 entering the front of the neck from the Losee rooftop. The early inside-the-tent readings put the source at the tent; most investigators have since concluded that the source at the tent was the **microphone device itself**, worn under the shirt at the collar — which needs no hidden shooter and explains the shirt moving outward first.

## The government's narrative, and the investigators' answer

Defenders of the government's narrative say entrance and exit wounds cannot be read from video — that only a pathologist examining the wound margins can say. Investigators agree that the autopsy is decisive, and point out who is withholding it:

* **The State has the autopsy and will not release it.** If the wound margins showed a clean rifle entrance from above, publishing them would settle this in a day.
* **The State's own filing contradicts itself.** The brief describes a downward path while quoting [damage from C2 through C7](/Medical/state_brief_trajectory_contradiction), and a court filing lists [hemopericardium, bilateral hemothorax and subarachnoid hemorrhage](/Medical/charlies_injuries) — injuries spread well beyond one bullet track through the neck.
* **The geometry does not work.** [Trajectory analysis](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle) and the [five-inch roof lip](/Roof/roof-line-of-sight) both strain the government's line of fire from the rooftop.
* **A blast explains the wound without a path.** A charge at the collar produces damage radiating from a point, which is what investigators see in the video and in the listed injuries — no entrance-to-exit track is required.

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## What would put it beyond dispute

1. **Release the autopsy report**, including the wound margins, the wound track and any finding of burns, soot or blast injury.
2. Release the **death certificate and the medical examiner's file**, including who examined the body and on what authority.
3. Have an independent, named forensic pathologist review the autopsy against both the government's rooftop path and a charge at the collar.
