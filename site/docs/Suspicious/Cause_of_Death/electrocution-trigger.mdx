## What is alleged

Every explosive needs an initiator. PETN, the explosive most often proposed for a miniaturized charge, is normally set off by a detonator, and detonators are commonly fired electrically. So the question of *how* the charge at the microphone was fired is an ordinary engineering question, separate from the question of *whether* it was fired.

[@cletus_jethro](/Influencers/x/cletus-jethro) proposes one answer. He argues that making detonation depend on an electrical circuit completed through the target would give the operation built-in redundancy: "By making the explosive activation dependent on successful electrocution, any failure in the electrical component would prevent detonation altogether. In such a scenario, alternatives could be pursued seamlessly." He contrasts this with a remote detonation, which "risks the target surviving long enough to speak," and with relying on a shot, which introduces uncertainty — citing "the last-second movement seen in the Trump Pennsylvania attempt." His conclusion: the method "provides the ideal balance: risk mitigation, built-in redundancy, and synchronized execution."

In this proposal the electricity is only the **switch**. The harm is done by the explosive charge at the collar — the same mechanism described across this section.

## Other trigger possibilities

Investigators have discussed several ways a miniaturized charge in a wireless transmitter could be fired, and the evidence released so far does not choose between them:

* **Remote radio command** — a wireless transmitter already contains a radio and a battery, the two things a remote initiator needs.
* **A timed or cued initiator** — synchronized to a moment in the event.
* **An electrical initiator completed through contact** — @cletus_jethro's proposal above.

Which one was used is exactly the kind of detail that only the recovered device, the [burnt object in the SUV](./burnt-object-transmitter), or the withheld autopsy could answer. The uncertainty is about the switch, not about the explosion.

## What would put it beyond dispute

1. **Produce the RØDE transmitter, its battery, and the charred SUV object from evidence**, and have an independent examiner test them for explosive residue and for any added initiator circuitry.
2. **Release the autopsy**, including any finding at the collar consistent with a detonator.
3. Release the records of **who supplied, configured and handled the microphone kit** on September 10 — without any presumption that the AV staff knew what was inside it.
