# The Burnt Object in the SUV (Claims)

:::caution Attributed claims only
**Nothing in the SUV has been recovered, tested, or examined by any laboratory, and no chain of custody exists for any of it.** No person is accused of anything here. These are social-media interpretations of a compressed photograph, not authenticated forensic findings.
:::

## What is alleged

The post argues that while much of the burnt object is melted polyester from Kirk's shirt fused with charred skin, a thicker black section on its left side "appears to contain a melted female audio jack — the exact same type of jack found on the top of the RØDE Wireless ME transmitter that Charlie was wearing under his shirt, clipped to his belt." It further claims that a fragment in the center of the mass bears "visible writing and a circular symbol" said to match warning symbols from a modified battery pack used inside the transmitter. Separately, [@DiligentDenizen](/Influencers/x/diligent-denizen-2) amplified claims of shattered tempered glass "like from a RODE microphone" scattered across the SUV floorboard.

**Which RØDE model it was is an open records question, not a flaw in the finding.** The investigation file notes that this post says **RØDE Wireless ME**, while the site's own microphone page is built on the **Wireless PRO**. Either way the claim is the same: parts of the transmitter Kirk wore under his shirt appear in the charred mass. Only the AV team's equipment records can settle the model, and they have not been released.

Chain of custody is unestablished. The vehicle was later photographed **at an auto auction**, which is covered at [the SUV's blurred windows](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/suv-blurred-windows).

## Why investigators read it as blast debris

The government's rifle account treats anything in the SUV as ordinary first-aid debris, or as a transmitter damaged mechanically by a round passing through the neck. Investigators point out what that explanation has to ignore: the mass is **charred and melted**, not cut or crushed, and a rifle round does not burn plastic. Melted transmitter parts fused with shirt fabric and skin is what a small charge at the microphone would be expected to leave — which is why this object sits among the [main reasons investigators conclude it was the exploding mic](/Mic/overview).

The image is a **screenshot of a video frame of the original photo**, and the post's own annotation marks the jack with a question mark — a reason to demand the original photograph and the object itself, not a reason to dismiss what can be seen. Commentators such as [@troofevades](/Influencers/x/troofevades), quoting [@Trillion0x](/Influencers/x/troofevades), note that a real shaped charge produces a large fireball and smoke cloud — "that's a lot of smoke that I dont see at the CK event" — and that **Jon Bray has promised a simulated test of the theory for over six months without delivering one**. Investigators answer that a miniaturized charge of the kind in the [AES anti-personnel contract](/Mic/DoD_Contract) is designed to produce no comparable signature.

The decisive fact is that **no one has tested the object**. Burnt material from the vehicle that carried Kirk to the hospital was never publicly examined for explosive residue, and the vehicle itself was [reportedly scrapped](/CoverUp/SUV_Destroyed).

## What would settle it

1. Obtain the original uncompressed [@michael_vina27](/Influencers/x/michael-vina27) photograph at full resolution, not a screenshot of a video frame, with a scale reference in frame.
2. Physically recover the object and submit it for laboratory examination and explosive-residue testing.
3. Establish which RØDE model Kirk actually wore — **ME or PRO** — because both theories on this site depend on the answer and they give different ones.
4. Obtain the chain of custody for the SUV from the hospital to the auto auction, and establish who released it.
5. Have Jon Bray or anyone else actually run the promised simulated shaped-charge test and publish the result either way.
