# Cause of Death

**Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his RØDE
lavalier microphone — [the Exploding Mic](/Mic/overview) — not by a .30-06 rifle round.** The .30-06 / Mauser
account is the government's narrative. The large majority view among the investigators working this case on X
is an explosive, and this page sets out why. Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## Cause of death: the Exploding Mic

1. **[Exploding Mic](/Mic/overview)** — the conclusion of the large majority of citizen investigators: a small
   explosive in or at the microphone assembly, not a rifle round, killed Charlie Kirk. See
   [Explosive in Mic Killed Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie), the
   [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis), and the
   [chest-shot witnesses](/Mic/Chest_Shot_Witnesses).

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

Citizen investigators have largely concluded that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his
microphone, not by the government's .30-06 rifle narrative. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

## The government's narrative: the .30-06

The government's narrative holds that a single **.30-06** round fired from a **Mauser Model 98** on the Losee
Center rooftop was the cause. Investigators have taken that account apart point by point:

* [.30-06 Bullet Did Not Kill Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/thirty-06-did-not-kill-charlie) — the
  central argument that the wound, trajectory, and energy do not fit a distant high-power rifle kill.
* [.30-06 Energy Mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) — why the energy a .30-06 delivers at that
  range is inconsistent with what was observed.
* [Wound Trajectory & Angle](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle) — the geometry problem between the claimed
  firing position and the reported wound.
* [ATF Fragment Inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive) — the state's own ballistics work could
  not tie the recovered fragment to the recovered rifle.
* [Gun & Bullet (full section)](/Gun_Bullet/overview) — the complete ballistics record.

**Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted,** and is presumed innocent. See
[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview).

## Why this section exists

The cause of death is the hinge of the entire case. If a .30-06 round fired from a rooftop killed Charlie Kirk,
the official account would be coherent and the remaining questions would be about motive and process. Because
investigators conclude it did not, the charged suspect's alleged weapon did not do it, the rooftop firing
position does not explain the death, and every element built on that foundation — the confession, the
timeline, the evidence handling — needs to be re-examined from the beginning. That is why this question is
separated out and stated directly rather than left distributed across a dozen pages.

The evidence that led investigators to the explosive is convergent. The state's own ATF examination could not
match the recovered fragment to the recovered rifle. The reported wound geometry and the claimed firing position
cannot be reconciled. Witnesses close to the stage described a chest event. Video frame analysis shows an
outward rupture at the body rather than an incoming projectile. The mic was routed under the shirt, debris
consistent with a burnt transmitter surfaced, and the ground, clothing, and vehicle that could have carried
explosive residue were removed or cleaned without any public residue test. Together these are why the large
majority of investigators concluded it was the [Exploding Mic](/Mic/overview).

The **autopsy** has not been released. See [the autopsy report is not public](/Medical/autopsy_report_not_public)
and [the Autopsy section](/Medical/Autopsy/overview). Readers new to this question should start with the
[Mic hub](/Mic/overview), then [.30-06 Did Not Kill Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/thirty-06-did-not-kill-charlie).

## Scope of this page — no allegation against TPUSA, security, or the AV team

**We are not making the allegation that anybody at [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview), on the
[security team](/Security_Team/overview), or among the [audio and visual people](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup)
involved had any knowledge of, or committed, any unethical or criminal act.** No such claim is made or implied
anywhere on this page or on the pages it links to.

The people who built the stage, ran the sound, clipped on the microphone, and handled security that day were
doing their jobs. Nothing documented here suggests otherwise. Where these pages discuss the microphone, the
staging, the truss, or the site, they are discussing **equipment and physical evidence** — not the conduct of
the people who worked with that equipment. A theory about a device is not an accusation against the person who
supplied it.

This page exists to **document what the majority of citizen investigators were concluding on X as of July
2026** — nothing more. It is a record of the state of independent analysis at a point in time, not a finding of
fact, not a legal conclusion, and not an accusation against any person or organization. The conclusions
summarized here are those of third-party investigators and are reported as such. The central document — the
autopsy — remains sealed.

## Video: Navy SEAL Rob O'Neill — "The More I Look At It, The Less It Makes Sense"

This is the clip most directly on this section's subject. **Rob O'Neill**, former Navy SEAL, states
from combat experience that a **.30-06 round to the neck** *"would blow his head off"*, that he saw
**no exit wound**, and that the shirt movement suggested *"an impact nearby"*. He then notes that
the official explanation changed — first a ricochet off body armor, then no body armor — which
cannot both be true. His account is an experience claim, not a forensic finding, and it is no
substitute for the autopsy that has not been released. See [the Exploding Mic hub](/Mic/overview).
