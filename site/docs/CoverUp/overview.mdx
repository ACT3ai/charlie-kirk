**Narrative control** has a case study that lives outside this section, in the cause-of-death area: the [Shot-Through-the-Heart Narrative](/Suspicious/Cause_of_Death/shot-through-heart-narrative). Witnesses on live television described, in the first person, a shot "right directly to the heart" — a description Charlie Kirk's **white shirt** shows did not happen, since there is no chest entry and no blood from the sternum. That framing surfaced fast, reached TV news in several places, and then receded as coverage moved to the neck wound. Whether that is a **selection effect** of live booking (the ordinary explanation, argued in full on that page) or something installed is not settled by anything currently public. It is an **attributed thesis**, rated SPECULATIVE, and no witness is accused of lying or being coached.

## Removing Explosive Residue — The Cross-Cutting Thread

Several items above are usually read one at a time — [dirt removed](./Dirt_Removed), the
[transport SUV](./SUV_Destroyed), the [skipped forensic tests](./Forensic_Tests_Skipped), the
[mic explosive](./Mic_Explosive) theory. Collected on one page they form a narrower question: if a
charge detonated on Charlie Kirk's chest, **explosive residue would be on fabric, skin, soil and
upholstery**, and a single swab would confirm or kill the theory. Item by item — the necklace, ten
inches of soil, the pavers poured over it, the ceiling under the courtyard, the security detail's
clothing, the Yukon, the charred object in it — those surfaces were reportedly removed, sealed,
cleaned or resold, and **no public record shows any of them tested for explosives**. Each step has an
ordinary explanation on its own; no wrongdoing by any agency or person is established. The full
item-by-item hub is
**[Removing Explosive Residue Cover-Up](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview)**.

## Asking A Witness To Erase His Own Copy

Most of the threads on this page concern physical evidence at the scene. This one concerns evidence that never left a private phone.

A witness who filmed the event says he sent his footage to the [FBI](/FBI/overview) as a tip, was escalated from a local field office to a contact he describes as national, and was then advised by that contact to erase the video from his phone — presented to him as being for his own wellbeing, since the victim had been his friend. He says a later call asked him to confirm he had erased it. He had not, and says the confirmation call is what pushed him to publish the footage and copy it across many hard drives in many places.

Note carefully what this is and is not. It is not the claim, made separately elsewhere on this site, that footage vanished from people's phones on its own — see [Videos deleted remotely](/CoverUp/Videos_Deleted_Remotely) for that distinct allegation. This is an account of two telephone conversations, given on camera by the person who says he had them. The FBI has not addressed it publicly, ordinary welfare and evidence-handling explanations fit the same described facts, and nothing here asserts wrongdoing by any individual.

## Charlie Kirk: The Threat He Said Actually Got His Attention

Speaking on stage before September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk drew a line between two kinds of people who hated him. The stranger on a campus who wanted him dead, he said, did not bother him. The ones who did were the "close proximity" people — the ones he already knew, who had some sort of relationship with him, and who he says had betrayed or deceived him. Clip surfaced by [@NihiloX](https://x.com/NihiloX/status/2089076950174158960) on X, quoting [@unhealthytruth](https://x.com/unhealthytruth/status/2088853084998865178).
