# Rick Cutler — Camera Handling in Citizen Threads

**Rick Cutler** is a **named [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview)/event crew figure** whom post-shooting video analysis identifies as a
**brown-shirted figure** near [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) and alleges took
down a camera behind the stage. Both the label and the described actions are citizen-investigator
interpretations of footage, not confirmed by any official source. He is interesting to document, but
we make no claim that he knew about or was involved in the killing.

## What footage threads describe (not adjudicated)

CK_FILE and [Mic — SUV transport](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie) research note:

- **Brown-shirt figure** labeled Cutler in annotated stills — sunglasses in crowd clips
- Reportedly **pulled down or adjusted camera** behind Charlie before or around the shot window
- **SUV interior** seating chart includes Cutler among TPUSA staff transporting Kirk ([cropped public image with disclaimer on Mic page](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie))

## Background notes (public record / posts)

- CK mentions Cutler **sued [Tim Ballard](/People/tim-ballard)** (Operation Underground Railroad) in **2023** — civil docket reference in research file
- Appears in **Google Trends** political-search clusters (July 2025) alongside other Utah figures in CK notes — unverified significance

## Why he is in Distraction People (not People/Killer)

Cutler is listed here because his handling of cameras near the stage drew attention in citizen footage
threads. **No charge** ties him to the shooting.

## Status

**Status:** Alive (per public commentary).
