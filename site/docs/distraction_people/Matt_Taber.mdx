# Matt Taber

"**Matt Taber**" was an early name floated by online citizen researchers for the
[curly-haired man](./Curly_Hair_Man) seen in UVU crowd footage — the **same man**
also seen [chanting "USA"](./USA_Chant_Man) after the shot. Researchers later moved
away from it toward other names ([James Norman Rawlinson](./James_Norman_Rawlinson),
[David Sprague](./David_Sprague)). It is documented here only because the guess
circulated publicly.

:::caution One person, unverified identification — no accusation
This page describes the same unidentified man that several other pages on this
site also cover — the curly-haired figure in the UVU crowd who was also seen
chanting "USA." The names below (Matt Taber, James Norman Rawlinson, David
Sprague) and the handle [@RtothepowerofX](/Influencers/x/rtothepowerofx) all come from **anonymous crowd-sourced
facial matching and public-records guessing**. None has been confirmed by a
reliable, on-the-record source. Being present in a crowd — or reacting to a
shooting — is **not** evidence of involvement in any crime. Nothing on this page
is a finding of wrongdoing against any living person.
:::

## The same man — several labels

Online researchers use several different labels for what they say is one
individual. Each label has its own page on this site so search traffic lands
somewhere accurate and caveated:

* [Curly Hair Man](./Curly_Hair_Man) — the neutral description of the crowd figure
* [USA-Chant Man](./USA_Chant_Man) — the man seen chanting "USA" after the shot
* **Matt Taber** — an early name guess, later set aside (this page)
* [James Norman Rawlinson](./James_Norman_Rawlinson) — floated as a possible birth name
* [David Sprague](./David_Sprague) — a name he reportedly uses now (reportedly a
  mother's maiden name; also written "DavidJames Sprague")
* **[@RtothepowerofX](/Influencers/x/rtothepowerofx)** — an X handle cited online in connection with him

The links between these labels are **allegations by anonymous online accounts**,
not established fact. This site does not vouch for any of them.

## What the footage shows

The underlying observation is narrow and comes from short, isolated video loops:

* He was described as **turning toward the direction of the shot**.
* He was described as **standing up when others around him did not**.
* He **appeared to pump his fist in the air**.
* In separate news clips he is seen **chanting "USA"** in the seconds after the shot.

The video may look like he pumped his fist in the air. That is a description of a
gesture, not of a state of mind, and this site does not say he cheered. Readings
of a few frames of body language are **subjective readings of short clips**, not
findings — a startle-and-stand reaction, or a reflexive chant under shock, is
common and looks very different in isolation than in fully time-synced context.

## His own account — he says he did not cheer

He has **sent a message to this site's author** asking that people know **he did
not cheer**. That is his own first-person statement about his own conduct, and it
is recorded here at his request.

According to the same clips that circulated, he also said he chanted "USA"
**to create a distraction that might calm the panicking crowd and help save
lives**. His stated intent is a reported account, not an established fact, and it
cuts directly against the reading some posters attached to the footage.

## Public records cited online

Commentators point to ordinary, publicly-searchable records. None of these
records indicates any wrongdoing; they are listed only because they are
repeatedly cited online:

* A business reportedly operated as **Joseph Hasel LLC, DBA "Salon 21"**.
* That business reportedly received roughly **$115,200 in federal COVID-era
  loans** — a program used by hundreds of thousands of small businesses.
* Buzzfile reportedly estimates the business's revenue at about **$47,000/year**.
* A stylist profile for locs work at a **Provo, Utah salon ("Dreadknotilus")**
  under a similar name.

Running a salon, receiving a pandemic small-business loan, and cutting hair are
**normal, lawful activities**. They are noted here only to document what online
researchers have pointed to — not to imply anything improper about anyone.

## Open questions

1. Has any reliable, on-the-record source actually identified this man?
2. Do the circulated names refer to one real person, or to mismatched records?
3. Does complete, time-synced video place his movements and his chant in ordinary
   context rather than the short loops that circulate?
4. Was his reported explanation — chanting to calm the crowd — ever corroborated?

## Status

**Status:** Alive (private individual). This page names no confirmed identity and
accuses no one of any crime or of any role in the events of September 10, 2025.
