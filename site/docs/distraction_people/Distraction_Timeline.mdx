# Distraction Timeline — Sept 10–11

Distraction theories depend on **timing** — who pulled responders and cameras **before** the narrative
locked onto [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). This page orders **reported moments** from the master file and public
reporting. Times are approximate where sources disagree. Every entry below is anchored to one fixed
point: Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025, and the
[cause-of-death evidence](/Cause_of_Death/overview) still does not match the story told that afternoon.

## September 10, 2025 — day of shooting

| Time (MT, approx.) | Event | Distraction relevance |
|--------------------|-------|------------------------|
| **~12:23 PM** | [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) shot at UVU courtyard | — |
| **Minutes after** | [George Zinn](./George_Zinn) detained; "I shot him, now shoot me" per police | **First custody**; officers diverted |
| **~12:42 PM (+19 min)** | Campus alert: **"suspect in custody"** (GRAMA thread) | **False** — suspect not in custody; ~10k students stayed |
| **Same hour** | [Zachariah Qureshi](./Zachariah_Qureshi) reportedly detained near scene | Early **federal/local custody** confusion |
| **Same hour** | [Kash Patel](https://x.com) / FBI threads: announced shooter in custody → **release ~90 min later** | Possible **manhunt pause** (allegation) |
| **Afternoon** | [Pellet Gun Man](./Pellet_Gun_Man) on Provo scanner — bike + air rifle | Parallel **weapon scare** |
| **Afternoon** | Crowd footage: [Curly Hair Man](./Curly_Hair_Man), [USA-Chant Man](./USA_Chant_Man), [Black Clothing](./Black_Clothing_Figure) threads | Attention on crowd behavior |
| **Evening** | Manhunt narrative shifts toward Tyler Robinson | Distractor window allegedly closing |

## How to use this page

A **single** false arrest (Zinn) proves distraction **can happen** — police say he admitted it. Whether a **roster**
was coordinated requires **logs** (dispatch, booking, FBI 302s), not timeline inference alone.

## How X still uses the day-of distraction clock

Citizen posts in 2026 still compress the **immediate post-shot minutes** into a multi-actor story: Zinn's self-accusation, other detentions, black-clothing figure tracks, and security-camera removals. Use this timeline as a **checklist against primary video timestamps**, not as proof every listed figure coordinated. Cross-check [George Zinn](./George_Zinn), [Black Clothing Figure](./Black_Clothing_Figure), and [cameras](/cameras/overview).

*Commentator coverage:* Citizen journalist [Ian Carroll](/People/ian-carroll) has questioned the FBI's lone-gunman narrative and the day-of custody confusion in his posts, raising the possibility — as attributed commentary, not fact — that Tyler Robinson could be a patsy and that Discord-message timing deserves scrutiny against the custody clock mapped here.
