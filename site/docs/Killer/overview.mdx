Citizen investigators argue that the official narrative — that [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) acted alone — does not hold up against what they read in the ballistics record, the acoustic profile of the shot, questions raised about security posture on the day, and the movement of aircraft and drones around UVU on September 10. Those are contested readings of the public record, not court findings, and no named security officer or other living person is accused here of wrongdoing. The alternative theories documented on this site argue that the killing would have required coordination, foreknowledge, and operational support that a lone student could not plausibly provide. The question of who ordered and executed the assassination is the central unresolved question of the entire investigation.

Three categories of evidence bear on who was responsible: the physical evidence of how the shot was fired (ballistics, acoustics, wound profile, camera angle), the logistical evidence of what was arranged before the event (security posture, aviation assets, lane access), and the motive evidence of who stood to gain from Kirk's death politically and strategically. Each of the suspect categories scores differently on these dimensions — and each has a different level of evidentiary support and a different institutional relationship to the cover-up that investigators allege.

Start with the [Israel/Mossad page](./israel-mossad) for the strongest motive argument. Read the [US Intelligence page](./us-intelligence) for the operational capability and cover-up argument. Review [Tyler Robinson](./tyler-robinson) for the official case and the documented challenges to it. The [More Theories](./more-theories) page covers TPUSA internal conflict, other foreign actors, and combination theories that multiple investigators consider more likely than any single-actor explanation.

## Charlie Kirk: The Threat He Said Actually Got His Attention

Speaking on stage before September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk drew a line between two kinds of people who hated him. The stranger on a campus who wanted him dead, he said, did not bother him. The ones who did were the "close proximity" people — the ones he already knew, who had some sort of relationship with him, and who he says had betrayed or deceived him. Clip surfaced by [@NihiloX](https://x.com/NihiloX/status/2089076950174158960) on X, quoting [@unhealthytruth](https://x.com/unhealthytruth/status/2088853084998865178).
