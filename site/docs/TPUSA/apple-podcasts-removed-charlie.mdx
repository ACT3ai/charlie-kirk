# Apple Podcasts: Charlie's 752 Episodes Removed

This page is about **Apple Podcasts only**, and about **one set of recordings**: the episodes of *The Charlie Kirk Show* published in **August 2025 or earlier** — the shows Charlie recorded while he was alive.

**In August 2025 there were 752 of them on Apple Podcasts. Today there are none.**

:::info How and when these numbers were calculated

**Measured July 31, 2026, against the Wayback Machine's captures of the Apple Podcasts show page.** The live side is Apple's full catalog for *The Charlie Kirk Show*, pulled from Apple's own catalog API on **July 31, 2026** — 2,000 episodes. The archived side is **467 Wayback Machine captures** of that same Apple page, whose pre-assassination reference point is the **[August 25, 2025 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250825101452/https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818)** printing **Episodes: 752 · Years Active: 2019 – 2025**.

**The two numbers on this page measure different things.** *752* is the episode count Apple itself printed on the page in August 2025. *1,793* is the count of individual episodes the archive proves Apple once served and that Apple no longer serves — matched one at a time on Apple's own episode ID, across every capture from 2019 to 2026, not just the August 2025 one.

**Only pre-death episodes are counted.** All 1,793 were published before September 10, 2025. Nothing published after his death is counted anywhere on this page.
:::

Those 752 episodes were the pre-assassination catalog. The archived page is a dated, third-party record of exactly what Apple was carrying while Charlie was still alive.

## What Is On Apple Podcasts Now

**Episodes published on or before August 31, 2025, currently listed on Apple Podcasts under their original publication dates: zero.**

Apple Podcasts does not host the audio. It ingests the show's RSS feed. That feed — the [Omny Studio feed](https://www.omnycontent.com/d/playlist/5e27a451-e6e6-4c51-aa03-a7370003783c/c865b590-c84f-4f7e-a43e-ac64014b61d9/8978e846-cacd-4d65-b085-ac64014cd49f/podcast.rss) linked from the show — currently carries only the most recent block of episodes, roughly 30 to 50 items. **Every one of them has a `pubDate` in 2026. Not one item in the feed carries a publication date on or before August 31, 2025.**

What Apple lists instead of the originals are **"From the Archive"** entries: older speeches, Q&As, and segments — some recorded between 2018 and 2025 — **republished with fresh 2026 dates**. They are re-issues chosen and posted after his death. They are not the 752 episodes, and they do not appear where the originals appeared.

Apple's headline "2.2K / 2019–2026" figure is a cumulative catalog number carried on the show's directory record. It is not a list of what a listener can open. What a listener can open, dated August 2025 or earlier, is nothing.

## The Sequence

1. **Through August 2025** — Apple Podcasts lists 752 episodes of *The Charlie Kirk Show*, spanning 2019 to 2025. Captured by the Wayback Machine on **August 25, 2025**.
2. **September 10, 2025** — Charlie Kirk is [assassinated at Utah Valley University](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU).
3. **After the assassination** — the show continues under the same name and the same Apple listing, with new hosts and new leadership at [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview).
4. **By July 31, 2026** — none of the 752 remain on Apple Podcasts under their original dates. The feed Apple reads contains 2026 items only.

## Why These Specific Episodes Matter

The removed block is the tape of Charlie's own final years and final months — in his own voice, on the record, under a date stamp:

* What he said about **[Israel and the donor pressure on him](/Israel/overview)** in his last summer.
* What he said about the **[Iran war he opposed](/Iran/funding-rejection-claims)** — a position [the show reversed after his death](./tpusa-iran-bombing-support).
* What he said about the **Epstein files**.
* **Who he chose to platform**, including the bookings that reportedly cost TPUSA donors.
* The **September 9, 2025 episode** — recorded the day before he was killed.

Every one of those is a primary source. A quote from them is checkable against a dated, playable original. That checkability is what ended on Apple Podcasts.

## Who Controls The Feed

Apple did not write the feed. The show's operators did. A podcast directory lists what the publisher's RSS feed offers it; when items leave the feed, they leave the directory. The feed behind this listing is operated on the show's side, which since September 2025 has been run by post-Kirk [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) leadership. **The party that decides what is in that feed is the party the archive documents.**

There is no public change log. Apple publishes no diff of a show's catalog, and neither does the feed. The only reason the removal is measurable at all is that a **third party — the Wayback Machine — happened to capture the page on August 25, 2025**, with the number 752 printed on it.

## Verify It Yourself

1. Open the **[August 25, 2025 Wayback capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250825101452/https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818)**. Read the episode count and the years-active line.
2. Open the **[live Apple show page](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818)**, press **See All**, and scroll to the bottom. Note the **oldest publication date you can actually reach**.
3. Check each old-sounding title you find against its **listed date**. "From the Archive" reissues carry 2026 dates; that is how you tell a re-post from an original.
4. Download the **[Omny RSS feed](https://www.omnycontent.com/d/playlist/5e27a451-e6e6-4c51-aa03-a7370003783c/c865b590-c84f-4f7e-a43e-ac64014b61d9/8978e846-cacd-4d65-b085-ac64014cd49f/podcast.rss)** and search it for any `pubDate` in 2025 or earlier.
5. **Save a Wayback capture of the Apple page today.** The August 2025 snapshot is the only reason this page can be written. The next removal will be provable only if someone captures the current state now.

## Open Questions

1. **Where is the list of the 752?** No published index names the exact 752 that Apple counted on the page in August 2025. What can be named is the **[1,793 episodes the archive proves Apple once served and no longer serves](./apple-podcasts-removed-episode-list)** — reconstructed from Apple's own episode IDs across 467 Wayback captures. Apple has published no index of its own.
2. **When did they leave the Apple listing** — in one action, or over months? A Wayback timeline between September 2025 and July 2026 would date it.
3. **Who instructed the change to the feed**, and on what date?
4. **Which episodes were selected for "From the Archive" re-posting**, who chose them, and by what criteria?
5. **Does a complete master copy of the 752 exist** in TPUSA, estate, or distributor custody in a form legal discovery could reach?
