# Every Apple Podcasts Episode Removed: The Full List of 1,793

This is the list. Every row below is one episode of *The Charlie Kirk Show* that
**Wayback Machine captures of the Apple Podcasts show page prove was listed on Apple**,
and that **is not in the live Apple Podcasts catalog** today.

:::info How and when these numbers were calculated

**Measured July 31, 2026.** The live side is the full Apple Podcasts catalog for
*The Charlie Kirk Show* ([id1460600818](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818))
and its companion feed (id1564144037), pulled from Apple's own catalog API on
**July 31, 2026** — 2,000 episodes.

**The archived side is the Wayback Machine.** It is 467 separate archived captures of the
Apple Podcasts show page, and the reference point for the pre-assassination catalog is the
**[August 25, 2025 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20250825101452/https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818)**
— the one that printed **Episodes: 752 · Years Active: 2019 – 2025** while Charlie Kirk was
still alive. Across all captures the archive documents **2,915 episodes**.

**The list below is the difference between those two sets**, matched on Apple's own
episode ID. Nothing here is inferred from a missing title or a changed description — an
episode appears only when Apple itself once served an ID that Apple no longer serves.

**Only pre-death episodes are counted.** Every one of the 1,793 was published before
September 10, 2025. Nothing published after his death is on this page.
:::

## Was It All The Charlie Kirk Show?

**Yes. All 1,793.** Every removed episode carries Apple show ID **1460600818** —
*The Charlie Kirk Show*. Zero came from the companion feed (id1564144037), and zero came
from any other podcast. There is no second number to report here: the removal is confined
entirely to Charlie Kirk's own show, and entirely to the years he hosted it himself.

Every one is also `episode_type: full` — these are complete episodes, not trailers,
bonus clips, or promos.

The 2023 row is where the line falls. **Everything the archive documents with a publication
date before September 8, 2023 is gone — 1,792 out of 1,792.** The oldest episode a listener
can still open on Apple Podcasts is *"What is a Traitor? + A Surprising Contender for 2024"*,
published **September 8, 2023**. One later episode is also missing — *THOUGHTCRIME Ep. 15*,
September 30, 2023 — which brings the total to 1,793.

That shape matters. A catalog that decays at random loses episodes in scattered ones and
twos across every year. A catalog cut at a date loses everything on one side of a line and
nothing on the other. This one is cut at a date.

## What This Page Does Not Claim

This page reports a set difference between two public records: Apple's live catalog on
July 31, 2026, and the Wayback Machine's captures of Apple's own show page. It does not
allege that any named living person committed a crime.

* It does **not** establish **when** each episode left the listing. The archive proves an
  episode was there and the live catalog proves it is not there now; the date in between is
  not recoverable from this data.
* It does **not** establish **who** made the change, or whether any request was filed with
  Apple. Apple lists what a publisher's RSS feed offers it.
* It does **not** claim the audio was destroyed. An episode can be absent from Apple and
  still exist elsewhere.
* Some episodes may be absent for ordinary reasons — a rights expiry, a guest request, a
  feed migration. Those explanations are available for individual rows. They are harder to
  apply to a clean cut at a single date across 1,793 rows.

## Verify Any Single Row Yourself

1. Take the **Apple Episode ID** from the row you want to check.
2. Open `https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/x/id1460600818?i=` followed by that ID.
   A removed episode returns an error page instead of an episode.
3. Confirm it was once there: the archive URL for that episode is recorded in the
   downloadable CSV below, in the `wayback_snapshot_url` column.
4. Compare against the **[live show page](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818)** —
   press **See All**, scroll to the bottom, and read the oldest date you can reach.
5. **Save a Wayback capture of the Apple page today.** The only reason this list exists is
   that someone captured the page before the episodes went.
