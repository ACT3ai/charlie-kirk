# Were Charlie's Podcasts Deleted?

**Short answer:** Multiple independent listeners and public posts from **February 2026 onward** reported that the **pre-death back catalog** of *The Charlie Kirk Show* stopped showing on **Spotify** and **Apple Podcasts** (and that the **full** show was never the archive on **YouTube**). That observation was **widely corroborated**. The stronger claim — that Erika Kirk or [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) **ordered** a deliberate erasure to hide Charlie's words — is **alleged, not proven**. The show brand and **new post-death episodes** kept publishing on the same apps. As of **July 29, 2026**, the **master RSS / Omny feed still lists thousands of pre-death episodes with audio enclosures**, and episodes remain reachable on **Rumble**, **charliekirk.com**, and related hosts — so this looks more like **directory accessibility / feed configuration** than total destruction of the recordings.

**What this site treats as established from those claims:** people **looked** and found the pre-death catalog **hard or impossible to browse** on the major U.S. podcast apps around the February 2026 dispute window. **What is not established:** a signed takedown request from Erika Kirk, a TPUSA board order, or a platform admission that TPUSA demanded deletion.

### How to read the table

- **"Deleted from Spotify/Apple"** in public speech usually means: *the consumer app no longer made the pre-death catalog easy or possible to scroll and play* — not that every audio file was wiped from every server on earth.
- **RSS full + app empty/truncated** is exactly what a **feed truncation, re-ingest, or platform episode-cap** looks like. Spotify and Apple **do not host** most podcasts natively; they **read the RSS**. One upstream change can hit multiple apps at once without three companies colluding.
- **"From the Archive"** episodes on Spotify/Apple in 2026 are often **re-issued speeches** (new publish dates, old Charlie audio). They are **not** the same as having every daily 2019–2025 show still listed in order.

## What Is True, What Is Not, What Is Unknown

### Supported by multiple independent observers

1. **Pre-death episodes became hard or impossible to find on Spotify and Apple Podcasts** during the period people were checking in **February 2026** (and many later posts still describe the same problem).
2. **New episodes under the same show name kept appearing** on those platforms after Charlie's death — so this was not "the show was banned."
3. **YouTube was never a complete podcast archive** of full-length episodes (Owens' correction + staff reply). Treating YouTube as "they deleted the podcast" overstates what was ever there.
4. **Rumble and the official Charlie Kirk / TPUSA web presence continued to carry show material** when app directories looked empty.
5. **The master Omny RSS feed, as of July 29, 2026, still enumerates thousands of pre-death episodes** with audio links — which is strong evidence against "the tapes were destroyed."

### Alleged, not proven

1. That **Erika Kirk personally ordered** the removal as CEO.
2. That the motive was to **hide Charlie's positions** (Israel, Iran, donors, etc.).
3. That **Spotify, Apple, and YouTube** each executed a coordinated political purge rather than reflecting an **upstream feed / rights / cap** change.

### Ordinary explanations that remain live

* **Episode-count / feed limits** on hosts and directories (exactly what Neff's email gestured at — even if critics reject that framing).
* **Salem / Omny distribution and licensing** changes after a host's death.
* **Estate / rights** sorting while the show continues under new hosts.
* **UI caps**: a directory can report a high episode count while still being painful to search, or can re-ingest only part of a restored feed.

None of those alternatives are proven either. They are the bar that has to be cleared before "suppression order" is the best explanation.

## Why This Matters To The Investigation

Charlie's final months of on-air positions (Israel, Iran, Epstein files, who he would platform) are evidence for claims about **what he actually said** versus what successors attribute to him — see [Fourteen Ways TPUSA Went the Opposite Direction After Kirk](./TPUSA_Opposite_Charlie) and [TPUSA Replacement Timeline](./TPUSA_Replacement_Timeline).

If the easiest public apps no longer surface the pre-death catalog, **citizens lose the ability to check quotes against tape** even when the files still exist on RSS, Rumble, or a hard drive. That is a real record problem whether the cause was malice, negligence, or podcast plumbing — the same category of concern as [Archive.today](/CoverUp/Archive_Is_Purge) and [Videos Deleted Remotely](/CoverUp/Videos_Deleted_Remotely).

## How To Check This Yourself (July 2026 method)

1. **Apple Podcasts:** open [The Charlie Kirk Show](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818) → **See All** → page until you hit dates **before Sept 10, 2025**. Confirm the episode **plays**, not just that a title exists.
2. **Spotify:** same show URL → sort / scroll oldest-first if the client allows → look for full daily shows, not only "From the Archive" reissues.
3. **RSS (authoritative feed the apps read):** [Omny playlist RSS](https://www.omnycontent.com/d/playlist/5e27a451-e6e6-4c51-aa03-a7370003783c/c865b590-c84f-4f7e-a43e-ac64014b61d9/8978e846-cacd-4d65-b085-ac64014cd49f/podcast.rss) — search for a known pre-death title (e.g. a Sept 2025 guest episode) and open the enclosure URL.
4. **Rumble + charliekirk.com** — compare video and web pages for the same date range.
5. **Wayback Machine** — capture Apple/Spotify listing pages from **late 2025 vs Feb 2026** if you can; a dated capture of the transition is still the missing public proof of *when* the directories changed.

## Open Questions

1. **Exact date** the consumer catalogs truncated — still no widely published Wayback pair that pins the switch.
2. **Who initiated** the change: show producer, host platform (Omny), Salem contract, Apple/Spotify policy, or TPUSA leadership?
3. **Was Apple's ~2,200 count a restore**, a cap, or a mix of new episodes + reissued archive speeches?
4. **Does a complete master archive** exist under TPUSA / estate / Salem custody in a form FOIA or discovery could eventually force?
5. Are any **specific politically sensitive episodes** missing from Rumble/RSS while others remain (selective scrub), or is the pattern **bulk feed truncation** only?

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

Who controls the master archive, whether any takedown request was filed with Spotify or Apple, and the distribution contracts for the pre-death catalog are exactly the kind of facts the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) are meant to force into the open.

## What This Page Does Not Claim

This page does **not** claim that Erika Kirk, TPUSA, Blake Neff, Salem Media, Spotify, Apple, or YouTube committed a crime or destroyed Charlie Kirk's recordings. It records that:

* users **observed** the pre-death catalog become unavailable or severely limited on major podcast apps;
* Candace Owens **publicly attributed** that to new TPUSA leadership, then **narrowed** the YouTube half of the claim;
* **no verified primary document** proves who initiated the change;
* **ordinary technical and contractual explanations remain unexcluded**;
* as of **July 29, 2026**, the **RSS feed still lists a deep pre-death inventory**, and **Rumble / official web** still carry material.

No wrongdoing by any living person named here is alleged or established.
