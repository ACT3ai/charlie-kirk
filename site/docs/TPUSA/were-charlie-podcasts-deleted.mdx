# Were Charlie's Podcasts Deleted?

This page is about **one thing**: the episodes of *The Charlie Kirk Show* that **Charlie himself hosted, before he was killed on September 10, 2025**. Those are the recordings that matter to this investigation. They are the tape of what he actually said in his final months — on Israel, on Iran, on the Epstein files, on who he would and would not platform.

The question is simple. **Did those episodes disappear from Spotify and Apple Podcasts — yes or no?**

For **Apple Podcasts specifically**, that question now has a hard, dated answer: the platform listed **752 episodes** in August 2025 and lists **none of them today**. See [Apple Podcasts: Charlie's 752 Episodes Removed](./apple-podcasts-removed-charlie).

## The Answer, As Of July 29, 2026

**They were deleted, and then in April 2026 TPUSA restored them.** Charlie-hosted episodes are on both platforms right now, and they play — after a period in early 2026 when [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) reported they were gone and listeners could not reach them.

We checked directly rather than repeating claims. Verified today:

| Platform | Pre-death Charlie episodes present today? | Proof |
|----------|-------------------------------------------|-------|
| **Spotify** | **Yes** | [Ben Shapiro on George Floyd, Israel, and Gen Z](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2pWSuexoM8wZmhr29ajrhq) — **September 9, 2025**, the day before the assassination. Live under the official show, [The Charlie Kirk Show](https://open.spotify.com/show/0LLIIAYfTJ0axsrOcgsE0V), with playable audio. |
| **Apple Podcasts** | **Yes** | The [same September 9, 2025 episode](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ben-shapiro-on-george-floyd-israel-and-gen-z/id1460600818?i=1000725785245) is live on the official show page. So are older Charlie-hosted shows, including [Donald Trump's Great Realignment ft. Newt Gingrich](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/donald-trumps-great-realignment-ft-newt-gingrich-and/id1460600818?i=1000676283254) from **November 2024** and *Why America Needs a Right Wing Revolution* from **June 2024**. |
| **Master RSS feed** (the source both apps read) | **Yes — in bulk** | A direct parse of the [Omny/Salem feed](https://www.omnycontent.com/d/playlist/5e27a451-e6e6-4c51-aa03-a7370003783c/c865b590-c84f-4f7e-a43e-ac64014b61d9/8978e846-cacd-4d65-b085-ac64014cd49f/podcast.rss) returns **4,358 episodes**, of which **3,902 are dated before September 10, 2025**, going back to **June 10, 2020**. Audio files still resolve and download. |

So the strongest version of the claim — *"all of Charlie's episodes were removed from Spotify and Apple"* — **is not true today.** Anyone can open the two links above and hear Charlie's voice on an episode published the day before he died.

## But The Counts Do Not Match — And That Is The Real Story

Here is where it stops being clean.

The show's own master feed carries **3,902 episodes recorded before Charlie's death**.

Apple Podcasts' directory, checked today, reports **2,202 episodes in total** for the show — the page itself says *"See All (2.2K)"*, and Apple's catalog API returns a track count of **2,202**.

Even if **every single one** of Apple's 2,202 listed episodes were a Charlie-hosted pre-death show — which it is not, because hundreds of post-death episodes are also in that number — Apple would still be carrying **roughly 1,700 fewer** than the feed contains. The realistic gap is larger.

A third number disagrees again: Wikipedia's entry for the show lists **1,608 total episodes** as of 2026.

**Three sources, three different counts: 3,902 · 2,202 · 1,608.** Nobody has published a reconciliation of that gap. That is the honest state of the evidence — not "everything was deleted," and not "nothing happened."

## What This Gap Could Be

We do not know which of these is correct, and we do not claim to:

* **Directory episode caps.** Apple and Spotify do not host most podcasts. They read the RSS feed. Very-high-volume shows routinely hit ingestion limits, and a show publishing multiple episodes daily since 2019 is exactly that kind of show. This is the explanation the show's side gave publicly (see below).
* **A feed truncation that was later reversed.** If the feed was cut back and then restored, the apps would show a gap for a period and could re-ingest only part of it afterward. That would match both what listeners reported earlier in 2026 and what we measure today.
* **A deliberate reduction.** This is what critics allege. **It is not proven**, and no takedown request, platform statement, or internal document has been made public.

## What Was Claimed Earlier In 2026 — And What Happened Next

In **mid-February 2026**, [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) ([@RealCandaceO](https://x.com/RealCandaceO)) publicly reported that the *Charlie Kirk Show* episodes had been deleted. Her allegation was **total: that 100% of the episodes recorded while Charlie was alive were gone** — pulled from Spotify, Apple, and YouTube — and she attributed the decision to **new leadership at [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview)** in the weeks after the assassination. The post reached well over a million views.

**In April 2026, TPUSA restored them.** The pre-death catalog came back. That restoration is why the checks at the top of this page succeed today: the episodes anyone can play right now are episodes that spent part of 2026 unavailable and were then put back.

So the timeline is not "deleted" or "never deleted." It is:

| Date | State of the pre-death catalog |
|------|-------------------------------|
| Before **February 2026** | Present |
| **Mid-February 2026** | Owens reports deletion — alleging **100%** of Charlie-era episodes gone; many listeners independently report they cannot scroll back past roughly January 2026 |
| **April 2026** | **Restored by TPUSA** |
| **July 29, 2026** (this page's check) | Present and playable |

A restoration is an admission that something was removed. What has never been produced is **who ordered the removal, on what date, and why** — or any takedown request or platform statement covering the months in between.

Three further things about the February claim:

1. **She corrected the YouTube part the same day.** Full episodes were never posted to YouTube — that channel carried **clips and speeches**, never the podcast archive. Treating YouTube as part of a "deleted podcast" story overstates what was ever there.
2. **She published a reply she attributed to the show's staff**, which blamed the platforms and *"how they choose to handle shows like ours with a high number of episodes."* Owens rejected that explanation. It is, however, the same mechanism described in the bullet points above.
3. **The core observation was widely corroborated at the time.** Many independent listeners, across X and podcast-app reviews, reported the same thing that month: they could not scroll back past roughly **January 2026** on Apple or Spotify.

What was **never** produced — then or since — is a takedown request, a platform statement, or any primary document showing that a decision by TPUSA caused it. **That remains alleged, not proven.**

## Their Own Site Still Has Them — And That Is Not The Same Thing

The material is also reachable outside the big apps. **charliekirk.com** continues to run a podcasts hub, and **[Rumble](https://rumble.com/user/charliekirk)** is still the official video home for the show. Throughout the February dispute, that is where people were told to go when the app catalogs looked empty.

This is genuinely useful for investigators. It is also **not a substitute**, for a reason worth stating plainly:

**A site that controls its own archive can quietly remove an individual episode and nobody outside would know.** There is no public index of what used to be there, no notification, no diff. The same is true of the RSS feed — items can be dropped from it silently.

**We cannot police Apple and Spotify either.** Neither platform publishes a change log for a show's catalog. If specific politically sensitive episodes were pulled next month, there would be no announcement. The only way anyone would notice is if someone had already **captured a list of what existed** and re-checked it later.

That is the actual vulnerability here — not that the tapes were destroyed, but that **the record is entirely under the control of the parties it documents**, with no independent copy and no audit trail. This is the same category of problem as [Archive.today and the record-preservation fight](/CoverUp/Archive_Is_Purge) and [videos deleted remotely from phones](/CoverUp/Videos_Deleted_Remotely).

## Check It Yourself, And Save What You Find

Do not take this page's word for it. The whole point is that these checks are reproducible.

1. **Play a pre-death episode.** Open the [Spotify link](https://open.spotify.com/episode/2pWSuexoM8wZmhr29ajrhq) or the [Apple link](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ben-shapiro-on-george-floyd-israel-and-gen-z/id1460600818?i=1000725785245) above and confirm it plays. A title that exists but will not play is not an archive.
2. **Page back on Apple.** Open the [show page](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-charlie-kirk-show/id1460600818), hit **See All**, and scroll past September 10, 2025. Note the **oldest date you can actually reach**.
3. **Do the same on Spotify.** Sort oldest-first if your client allows it. Distinguish real daily shows from **"From the Archive"** reissues, which are old Charlie audio republished with new 2026 dates.
4. **Pull the master feed.** Download the [Omny RSS](https://www.omnycontent.com/d/playlist/5e27a451-e6e6-4c51-aa03-a7370003783c/c865b590-c84f-4f7e-a43e-ac64014b61d9/8978e846-cacd-4d65-b085-ac64014cd49f/podcast.rss), search for a known pre-death title, and open its audio URL directly. This is the authoritative source both apps read.
5. **Archive the list.** Save the feed file and a Wayback capture of the Apple and Spotify show pages **with today's date**. That snapshot is what makes any future removal detectable. Right now, almost nobody has one — which is why the February 2026 dispute could never be settled.

## Open Questions

1. **Why does the feed list 3,902 pre-death episodes while Apple's directory reports 2,202 total?** No party has explained the gap.
2. **What is the oldest pre-death episode actually reachable and playable** in each app's interface, as opposed to merely counted?
3. **Who removed the pre-death catalog before February 2026, and on whose instruction?** TPUSA restored it in April 2026, which settles that a removal occurred — but not who ordered it, when, or why.
4. **Are any specific episodes missing** while others from the same week remain — a selective gap rather than a bulk one? This is answerable only by diffing the feed against a dated snapshot.
5. **Does a complete master archive exist** in TPUSA, estate, or Salem Media custody in a form that legal discovery could reach?

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

Who controls the master archive, whether any removal request was ever filed with a platform, and what the distribution contracts say about the pre-death catalog are exactly the kind of facts the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) are meant to force into the open.

## What This Page Does Not Claim

This page does not claim that TPUSA, Salem Media, Spotify, Apple, or YouTube committed a crime or destroyed Charlie Kirk's recordings. It records measurements and attributed statements:

* Pre-death Charlie-hosted episodes **are live and playable** on Spotify and Apple Podcasts as of **July 29, 2026** — verified directly, with links above.
* The **episode counts across the master feed, Apple's directory, and Wikipedia do not agree**, and the discrepancy is unexplained.
* Many listeners **reported** a much shorter catalog on those apps earlier in 2026; in **mid-February 2026** Candace Owens reported the episodes deleted, alleged **100% of the Charlie-era catalog was gone**, and **publicly attributed** that to decisions by TPUSA — while **narrowing** her own claim on YouTube the same day.
* **TPUSA restored the pre-death catalog in April 2026.** Who removed it, when, and on whose instruction has not been documented publicly.
* **No verified primary document** shows who initiated any change.
* **Ordinary technical explanations — directory caps, feed re-ingestion — remain unexcluded.**

No wrongdoing by any living person named here is alleged or established.

## Footnote: Episodes Published After His Death

*The Charlie Kirk Show* continued publishing under the same name after September 10, 2025, with new hosts, and those episodes are on both platforms — the master feed carries **456** of them. This investigation does not treat that material as evidence of anything Charlie said or believed, and the availability of post-death episodes has no bearing on the questions above.
