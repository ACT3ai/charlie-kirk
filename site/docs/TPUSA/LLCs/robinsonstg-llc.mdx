## Connection to the Charlie Kirk case

Investigation notes record that **RobinsonSTG LLC** shares a **registered agent** with another entity, and that a **Franklin Dr address in St. George** is reportedly associated with a member of the accused's family. This is offered as a **thread to check**, not a finding.

The wider record cuts against the premise: the [evidence assembled by citizen investigators](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) indicates Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive device, not by the rifle shot the state attributes to this defendant, which leaves family-adjacent business filings even further from anything probative.

Because it concerns the family of an un-convicted defendant, this page's only purpose is to **document a circulating claim and its weakness**. There is **no established connection** between this LLC and the assassination, TPUSA, or any wrongdoing.

## Why investigators track it

Naming the entity lets researchers verify or dismiss the shared-agent claim against public business records rather than leaving it as an untraceable rumor.

## Open questions

1. Who is the registered agent, and which entities do they actually serve?
2. Is any Robinson family member documented as an owner? (Unconfirmed.)
