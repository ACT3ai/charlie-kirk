# Property & Locations

Every murder investigation eventually becomes a **map**. In the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) case, citizen threads on X converge on whether the **official geography** — rooftop shot, wooded gun drop, Dairy Queen sighting, I-15 escape, Hurricane surrender — holds together in time and space. Start with the [investigation index](./Investigation_Index). Charlie Kirk was assassinated on September 10, 2025 at Utah Valley University, and the majority citizen finding — that an [explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview) rather than a distant rifle shot did the killing — is what every location below is ultimately being tested against.

Those fixed coordinates let readers test drive-time math and custody transfers without accepting every theory.

Parallel **disputed-location** threads keep resurfacing: [785 College Dr construction](./Construction_Site_785_College) (all-black eyewitness vs affidavit clothing), [university / Palentire parcels](./University-House-Ownership) at the gun-drop edge, [Provo Airport](./Provo_Airport) badge and foreign-jet claims, and the [Valhalla / Goat Island](./Valhalla) coincidence after [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel)'s press-conference wording. Campus spatial detail (Losee rooftop, tent, pavers) lives in [UVU](/UVU/overview); flight numbers live in [Planes](/Planes/overview).

Prioritize **[Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index)** → your story (crime scene vs custody chain vs aviation) → cross-links into [Tyler Robinson — Travel](/Tyler_Robinson/Travel), [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview), and [Maps](/maps/overview).
