# About This Investigation

WhoAssassinatedCharlieKirk.com is a citizen-led, open investigation archive on the
assassination of Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025, at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah.
It gathers the evidence, court records, flight data, video, photographs, witness accounts and
open questions in one place, so that anyone can check the record for themselves.

## What this site is

* **An evidence archive.** Court filings from *State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson* (Case No.
  251403576), public flight-tracking records, event video, photographs, news coverage and the
  work of independent citizen investigators, organised by topic.
* **A place for open questions.** Many pages document questions that the official account has
  not answered. A question is not an accusation, and the site presents it as a question.
* **A push for disclosure.** The [four proposed federal laws](/Fix/overview) describe how the
  records needed to settle these questions could be forced into the open.

## How evidence is sourced

* Every factual claim on a topic page is attributed — to a court filing, an official
  statement, a news report, a public data source, or the named investigator who raised it.
* Primary records are preferred over commentary. Court documents are published in full under
  [Court & Trial](/court/overview) so readers can read the original filing, not a summary.
* Images and videos are kept as they were captured. Each photograph has its own page under
  [Photos](/Photos/overview) with a written description and links to the pages that use it;
  each video has its own page under [Videos](/Videos/overview).
* Flight data is checked against a control aircraft with no link to the case before any gap
  is described as a removal. A flight trace shows where an aircraft was; it does not show who
  was on board or why it flew.

## How claims are labelled

* **The official account** is stated as the official account — what prosecutors, the FBI,
  and state officials have said.
* **Citizen findings and claims** are attributed to the people who made them. Where the
  evidence is disputed, the page says so.
* **Open questions** are written as questions.
* Results that weaken a claim are published as prominently as results that support it.

## People named on this site

Everyone named on this site is presumed innocent unless and until convicted in a court of law.
The site does not state as fact that any living person committed a crime unless a court has
found so. Where a person has denied a claim, the denial is included. Some media is
deliberately never published: images or videos that identify private individuals from crowd
footage, and material that makes unproven accusations against living people, including
unsourced lists of names.

## Corrections

Pages are updated as new records and court filings appear; each page shows the date it was
last changed. When a published statement turns out to be wrong, the page is corrected or
removed, and a finding that is weakened by new evidence is reported, not quietly dropped.

To report an error, a broken link, or a missing source, use the **Edit this page** link at the
bottom of any page, or open an issue on the site's public source repository:
[github.com/ACT3ai/charlie-kirk](https://github.com/ACT3ai/charlie-kirk/issues). Please name the
page, the statement, and the source that shows it is wrong.
