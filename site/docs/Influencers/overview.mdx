Mainstream television either ignored the case or accepted the FBI lone-shooter narrative without
scrutiny. The combined reach of the programs and accounts listed here spans **hundreds of millions**
of listeners and viewers — yet their work is fragmented across platforms. This section indexes that
record so readers can find **GRAMA breakthroughs** ([danksterintel](./x/danksterintel)), **ballistics**
deep-dives ([Ian Carroll](./podcasts-ian-carroll)), **security interviews** ([Shawn Ryan](./podcasts-shawn-ryan)),
and **motive timelines** ([Candace Owens](./podcasts-candace-owens)) in one place.

Three hosts — [Tucker Carlson](./podcasts-tucker-carlson), [Dave Smith](./podcasts-dave-smith), and
[Candace Owens](./podcasts-candace-owens) — are discussed as **Designated Trusted Investigators** under
proposed [Law 4](/Fix/overview). That reflects independence from institutional control and willingness
to pursue intelligence-community angles co-opted media avoids. [Ian Carroll](./podcasts-ian-carroll) and
[Glenn Greenwald](./podcasts-glenn-greenwald) anchor the open-source research and press-freedom lanes;
[Project Constitution](./podcasts-project-constitution) and [X forensics](./x) supply frame-by-frame
video analysis the government has not released in full.

New readers: **[Investigation Index](./Influencers_Investigation_Index)** → **Candace Ep 287** →
**[danksterintel GRAMA](./x/danksterintel)** → **Ian Carroll Wrong Rifle**. If posts disappear, see
[Censorship of Investigators](./Censorship_of_Investigators). Related context on what these hosts are
trying to prove appears in the [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Proof Intel Services](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview) sections.

Post-assassination threats to investigators, withheld FBI 302s, sealed flight manifests, and platform
takedown records are among the truths the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) may force
into public view.

## What Citizen Investigators Emphasize on X

The Influencers section is the **distribution layer** of the investigation: Candace Owens and Ian Carroll drive skepticism of the official kill-chain; critics accuse them of overreach; Alex Jones, Baron Coleman, Benny Johnson, Dave Smith, Elijah Schaffer, Hodgetwins, Greenwald, and Whitlock each own a niche (ballistics guests, Trends, TPUSA proximity, libertarian free-speech, Hamptons pressure, Israel theories, intel critique, cultural motive). Censorship and trusted-investigator law concepts sit beside the profiles. This overview does not rank "correctness"—it maps who said what.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime, did anything wrong or illegal, or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. Names appear here only in the context of public discussion, and nothing here is a finding by this website.
