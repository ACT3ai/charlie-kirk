# Aggelos Syrmos — X Posts

[@aggelos_syr](https://x.com/aggelos_syr) (**Aggelos Syrmos**) appears in the site's court-scrutiny threads as a questioner in reply chains around the July 2026 hearing coverage — pressing on custody video and Miranda timing. The items below are **attributed questions and claims**, not findings.

## Claims from X posts

- Raised the question of why **station-entry footage** of Tyler Robinson's surrender was "lost" — part of the claim set that two separate video systems that should have captured the surrender reportedly produced nothing. See [missing surrender footage](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/missing-surrender-footage).
- In replies on the **Miranda defense-filing** thread (a Bates-stamped exhibit showing Robinson Mirandized at 6:25 PM and invoking counsel at 6:26 PM), raised the **"closed for the night"** and missing-station-footage questions. See [Miranda defense filing](/Suspicious/Defense_Attorneys/miranda-defense-filing).
- Asked why Washington County first refused a news organization's request for the surrender video, later said it was **deleted under a 30-day retention policy**, while pleadings suggest the footage exists in law-enforcement hands. See [missing surrender discovery](/Suspicious/Defense_Attorneys/missing-surrender-discovery).

## Investigative value

Syrmos is cited alongside attorney-commentator [Baron Coleman](/Influencers/podcasts-baron) and @VLuvMully in the surrender-footage claim cluster — rated MODERATE/EMERGING on the site's evidence scale because it rests partly on a docketed discovery addendum and a public-records appeal, not only on social posts. The unresolved question — where is the video of the most important surrender in the case — remains open.
