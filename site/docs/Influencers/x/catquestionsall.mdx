# CatQuestionsAll — X Posts

[@CatQuestionsAll](https://x.com/CatQuestionsAll) is one of the most-cited accounts in the site's
**U.S. intelligence** and **ballistics-gap** lanes. Their June 29, 2026 quote-tweet of the
**Benny Johnson "state actors" video** is the primary artifact behind several pages on the reported
halt of [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent)'s foreign-involvement probe. All items below are
attributed claims from their posts, not findings.

## Claims from X posts

- **June 29, 2026** ([post](https://x.com/CatQuestionsAll/status/2071736376215920842)) — Circulated
  video of commentator **Benny Johnson** stating there is *"some considerable evidence that there
  were state actors involved here. That's it. I don't want to go any further than that,"* with the
  caption *"Remember our government stopped Joe Kent from investigating foreign involvement."* The
  clip anchors [State Actors / Professional Hit](/Proof_Intel_Services/State_Actors_Professional_Hit),
  the [Joe Kent halt](/CoverUp/Joe_Kent_Halt), and the [NCTC shutdown](/Law_Enforcement/nctc-shutdown)
  pages. Johnson reportedly met FBI Director Kash Patel and AG Pam Bondi the next day over death
  threats — reported, not confirmed.
- **July 7, 2026** — Claimed a **"2nd bullet at the scene"** and **"5 unknown DNA profiles on the
  rifle"** — figures not independently verified; part of the DNA "mixture" discourse on
  [more reasons](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons).
- **July 12, 2026** — Compressed the defense-side list: no positive ID of the roof figure in the
  videos, **no ballistics match**, vehicle and metadata problems — *"uncontested unverified hearsay
  is a far cry from proof of guilt."* See
  [.30-06 did not kill Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/thirty-06-did-not-kill-charlie).
- Their Kent-halt framing recurs across [WhiteHouse Halt IC](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC),
  [FBI Foreign Leads](/FBI/Foreign_Leads), and
  [narrative control](/US_Intelligence_Assisted/narrative-control).
