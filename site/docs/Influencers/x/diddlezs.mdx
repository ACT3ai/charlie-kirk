# Fritz (diddlezs) — X Posts

[@diddlezs](https://x.com/diddlezs) (**Fritz**) is a flight-tracking contributor in the aircraft
lane of the investigation. The account works in **Flightradar24 playback** rather than commentary —
pulling up a tail number, stepping through the recorded track, and posting the capture — and it is
credited by name in the site's
[SU-BTT plane movements timeline](/Timeline/su-btt-plane-movements-timeline) for the
[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) ramp windows. All items below are attributed tracker-data claims
from X posts, not findings of this site.

## Claims from X posts

- **Sept 10, 2025 — SU-BND same-day window** — Flightradar24 playback posted by the account puts
  [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) — the "blue plane," serial **1332** — with its transponder coming
  online on the Provo tarmac about **21 minutes before** the shot, staying powered roughly **an hour
  after**, then going dark **without departing that day**. Aircraft presence is not involvement; the
  jet is separately described as leaving Utah on Sept 13.
- **Provo ramp playback generally** — the account's contribution across the aircraft threads is the
  raw playback capture rather than the reading of it: which tail, which minutes, powered or dark.
  That is why the [SU-BTT plane movements timeline](/Timeline/su-btt-plane-movements-timeline)
  credits it directly, and why the numbers can be re-checked against
  [other-planes activity](/Timeline/other-planes-activity-timeline) by anyone with the same tools.

## Investigative value

The value here is the layer beneath the argument. Transponder on/off windows posted by @diddlezs
are timestamps a reader can pull up again in the same tracker, which is more than most of the
aircraft lane offers. They are still **tracker reconstructions** — a transponder going quiet is not
an aircraft going still, and playback shows only what receivers heard. The ramp record that would
settle it — parking assignments, work orders, badge access at
[Provo Municipal Airport](/Locations/Provo_Airport) — is exactly what the
[Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) would compel into the open.
