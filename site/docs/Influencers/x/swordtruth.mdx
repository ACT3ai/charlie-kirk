# SwordTruth — X Posts

[@SwordTruth](https://x.com/SwordTruth) is the primary X source cited on this site for the **Operation Grim Beeper capability argument** — using the September 2024 pager attack as background for the [Israel / Mossad theory](/Killer/israel-mossad). The bullets below are **claims attributed to their X posts**, not confirmed findings, and no Israeli institution or named person is found by this site to have killed Charlie Kirk.

## Claims from X posts

- **July 10, 2026** — The 2024 pager operation shows [Mossad](/Israel/Mossad) "infiltrated the supply chain of Taiwanese manufacturer Gold Apollo," embedding **[PETN](/Israel/petn)** in device batteries — cited as a **reach-and-patience capability reference** ([Beepers](/Israel/Beepers), [Operation Grim Beeper](/Israel/Operation_Grim_Beeper)).  ([Source](https://x.com/SwordTruth/status/2075589097603891487))
- *"Maybe Charlie Kirk was killed by a bullet but it sure as hell wasn't a 30-06. One man killed by a bullet? That's child's play for Mossad."* — the **capability-not-evidence** quote this site repeatedly flags as rhetoric, not proof ([intelligence-service-behind](/Israel_Main_Suspect/intelligence-service-behind), [extreme motivation](/Israel_Main_Suspect/extreme-motivation)).
- The [Hezbollah](/Israel/Hezbollah) operation is invoked to illustrate the **scale and precision** Israeli intelligence reportedly achieved against a hard target — the implicit contrast being that a single individual would be far simpler.
- **January 20, 2026** — Alleged **mass staff firings** at TPUSA in a "mole hunt" for leaks to [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) about the assassination and finances — an **unverified organizational-drama claim** ([Candace Owens Israel Controversies](/Media/Candace-Owens-Israel-Controversies)).
- Circulated graphics including **"The Last 60 Days of Charlie Kirk"** infographic of reported donor and Israel friction — unverified, attributed to the graphic's anonymous author ([Grim Beeper photo cluster](/Photos/Israel/Israel_Operation_Grim_Beeper/overview)).

## Investigative value

@SwordTruth supplies the **capability half** of the motive-and-means pairing that runs through the [Israel](/Israel/overview) section. This site stresses on every page that capability of a foreign service in a different theater is **not evidence** it acted in Utah; prosecutors present a domestic lone-suspect case against Tyler Robinson (charged, not convicted).
