# Candice60896290 — X Posts

[@Candice60896290](https://x.com/Candice60896290) is one of the court-process trackers in the citizen
investigation, best known for **March 2026 threads** on the defense motion to **seal the ATF report**
and the **inconclusive** ballistics comparison in the [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) case.
Everything below is attributed claims from their X posts, not court findings.

## Claims from X posts

- **March 2026** — Reported a defense motion to **seal the ATF report**, with ballistics described as
  **INCONCLUSIVE** when comparing the autopsy bullet jacket to the **Mauser 98** — a press coalition
  (Deseret News, SL Tribune, AP, NYT, Fox, CBS) reportedly opposed the sealing. Covered on
  [Legal Process, Gag Orders and Secrecy](/After/Legal_Process_Gag_Orders_And_Secrecy).
- Alongside **@Sina1MAGA**, pushed the claim that FBI/ATF were **withholding DNA chain-of-custody**
  records from the defense — see [trial and autopsy report](/Medical/trial_and_autopsy_report).
- Their seal-motion threads are cited in the site's [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) trial-evidence
  summary and the [UVU Investigation Index](/UVU/UVU_Investigation_Index), where media-coalition
  opposition to sealing is logged against **Judge Tony Graf's** December 2025 gag order.
- Commentary framed the June 2026 **Christopher Ballard civil contempt** finding around the weakness
  of the bullet evidence — the contempt ruling is a documented court action; the interpretation of
  *why* is @Candice60896290's commentary. Ballard and Judge Graf are not accused of wrongdoing.

## Investigative value

The account is a recurring source for the **ATF-inconclusive / seal-motion lane** — one of the two
forensic pillars of [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview). Their threads are how the March
2026 seal fight entered the public record on this site. Tyler Robinson is presumed innocent; the
claims above are reporting and commentary about court filings, not verdicts.
