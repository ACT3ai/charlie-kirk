# Ana Escobar — X Posts

[@AnaEscobarShow](https://x.com/AnaEscobarShow) (**Ana Escobar**) is a flight-record researcher in the [Baron Coleman](/Influencers/podcasts-baron) / [John Cullen](/Influencers/john-cullen) tradition — she credits both, writing that she planned to go "John-Cullen-level" on the flight records. Her contributions run through the Planes section. All items below are **attributed OSINT questions and claims**, not established coordination between any aircraft or person.

## Claims from X posts

- **The Duncan Aviation link-up question** — "Why did Charlie's plane ([N102DZ](/Planes/N102DZ/overview)) land in Provo at 3:30pm, wait for 30 mins at the gate and THEN make its way to Duncan Aviation to link up with the Egyptian plane ([SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)) which was still there on and running? Was this the wait for Andrew?" — framed as a question about parking and timing; co-location at a busy maintenance ramp is not proof of coordination. See [Sept 10 Flight Timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline).
- **Egyptian armada transponder behavior** — claims about the fleet's transponders switching on and off around the shooting window, and questions about encrypted-comms capability and whether an aircraft was "waiting for someone." See Egyptian Armada and [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview).
- **June 7, 2026** — Claimed **~17 Google Trends spikes align with Miriam Adelson private-jet locations** (Israel/Cyprus on the same days as Trends geographies for Timpanogos hospital, Utah hotels, etc.) — a correlation claim only, with fragile and contested methodology; no crime asserted. Logged on the [censorship and information-control timeline](/Timeline/censorship-information-control-timeline).
- **[Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview) image-search chronology** — with the Stew Peters network, published a dated list of Fort Huachuca-geo image searches for figures including Johnnie Moore, [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet), [Brian Harpole](/Security_Team/brian-harpole), and DIA names, mid-July through Sept 9 — geo image-search data cannot identify searchers. See [Fort Huachuca image searches](/GoogleSearches/fort-huachuca-image-searches).
- Amplified the **[N59906](/Planes/N59906/overview)** thread alleging the "MARC INC" surveillance plane ties to a company where Kash Patel's girlfriend is registered agent — unproven and disputed in the same threads, including Grok rebuttals.

## Investigative value

Escobar's ramp-position and transponder questions are the kind that manifests and work orders could answer — which is why the Planes pages route them into the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) disclosure demands. Her Trends-correlation work is far weaker methodologically and is labeled as such wherever the site cites it.
