# danksterintel — X Posts

[@danksterintel](https://x.com/danksterintel) (**Danks**) thread-compiled **Daily Caller GRAMA**
releases (112 pages) into a viral breakdown of UVU institutional failures the night before the
shooting. Claims are from their X synthesis — verify against primary GRAMA PDFs when available.

## Claims from X posts

- **Sept 9, 10:35 PM** — Two unidentified men asked UVU police about **"a plan"**; cabinet notified;
  44 minutes later dismissed as **"GOP reps"** with no documentation of what was said.
- **Sept 9, 2:23 PM** — Email chain: someone wanted a **message delivered to [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** — delivery
  undocumented; names redacted.
- **Sept 3** — Orem PD sent ticket link; joint Public Order Unit **never activated**.
- **Post-shooting alerts** — false **"suspect in custody"** at ~19 min; **"Shooter in [INSERT location]"**
  placeholder at ~88 min.
- **Six campus police** for ~3,000 attendees; no ambulance; no active-shooter faculty training.
- UVU acknowledged **additional withheld** documents beyond the 112 pages.

([Primary thread](https://x.com/danksterintel/status/2044300845609824305))
