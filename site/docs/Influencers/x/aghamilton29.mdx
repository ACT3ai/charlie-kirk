# AG Hamilton — X Posts

[@AGHamilton29](https://x.com/AGHamilton29) (**AG Hamilton**) is one of the main **counterweight voices** in the site's record — an account that defends the official account of the case and criticizes the largest independent investigators. This site documents both directions of the argument; the items below are **attributed commentary**, not findings.

## Claims from X posts

- **April 18, 2026 (~1.5k likes)** — Argued the UVU camera set captures the accused making his way to the rooftop and called the cumulative evidence **overwhelming** against alternative-origin theories. Recorded as the counterweight entry on the [roof-jumping timeline](/Timeline/roof-jumping-timeline); neither side has been tested by a verdict.
- Criticized [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) over pre-death [Ian Carroll](/People/ian-carroll) proximity and what the account characterized as the speed of Israel-blame after the shooting.
- Accused Ian Carroll of **kitchen-sink conspiracy linkage** without proof — recorded on the [Ian Carroll coverage page](/Influencers/podcasts-ian-carroll) so readers see the criticism beside the claims.

## Investigative value

The site's assessment standard requires both readings on contested pages, and AG Hamilton supplies the strongest public statement of the "evidence is overwhelming" position outside official channels. Readers weighing the [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) stack should read this account's argument as the standing rebuttal.
