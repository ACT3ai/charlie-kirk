# misfitpatriot_ — X Posts

[@misfitpatriot_](https://x.com/misfitpatriot_) argues the **government's side** of the ballistics
debate. The site records these arguments across the [Gun & Bullet](/Gun_Bullet/Gun_Bullet_Investigation_Index)
cluster as attributed claims from X posts. Citizen investigators on X have, by a large majority, rejected
the .30-06 account and concluded Charlie Kirk was killed by [an explosive at the microphone](/Mic/overview).

## Claims from X posts

- Argues that **soft-point .30-06 ammunition *should* fragment** on impact, and that the "bullet
  doesn't match the rifle" reading of the ATF's inconclusive finding is **overclaimed**. The argument
  appears across
  [ATF Fragment Inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive),
  [Mauser .30-06 Rifle](/Gun_Bullet/Mauser_30_06_Rifle),
  [Rifle Fingerprints Mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Rifle_Fingerprints_Mismatch), and
  [Thirty-06 Energy Mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch).
- Their position pairs with **@OfVoice35353**'s citation of hearing testimony that the recovered
  fragments measure roughly **.286–.301 inches** and fit the .30-caliber family — the
  official-narrative-leaning side of the fragment debate.
- **July 2026** — Posted video (amplified by @JustJenRX, July 8, 2026) claiming a person **can bend
  a leg with a rifle down the pant leg**, answering the "physically impossible" logistics challenge,
  and adding the claim that Robinson allegedly **spoke with TPUSA staff before the stage** —
  prosecution-leaning premeditation framing. Recorded on
  [More Reasons](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/more-reasons) with the counters it answers.

## Investigative value

This account restates the government's account on fragments, energy and carry logistics. Nothing
here is a forensic finding; all of it is attributed commentary. Investigators answer that the state's
own ATF examiner [could neither identify nor exclude](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive) the seized
rifle, that "should fragment" does not explain the [missing exit wound or the energy mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch),
and that the shirt expands outward before the wound appears — the core of the
[exploding-mic conclusion](/Mic/overview).
