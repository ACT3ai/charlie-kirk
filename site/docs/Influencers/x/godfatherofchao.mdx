# godfatherofchao — X Posts

[@godfatherofchao](https://x.com/godfatherofchao) is cited in the investigation record within
the **memorial and succession** commentary lane — the dispute over how [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s
memorial and the TPUSA leadership transition were presented. Items below are attributed claims
from X posts, not findings of this site.

## Claims from X posts

- **July 6, 2026** — Circulated Candace Owens' argument that Erika Kirk's
  **eulogy** felt more like a **fundraising appeal** than a personal tribute.
  ([Primary post](https://x.com/godfatherofchao/status/2074164592109125991)) — recorded on
 Memorial and TPUSA Succession explicitly as an
  **opinion about tone, not a legal claim**.
- The same amplification appears on [the funeral and memorial timeline](/Timeline/funeral-memorial-timeline),
  where it sits beside a July 2026 poll summary claiming a majority sided with Erika's
  forgiveness posture — the public-opinion beat around the September 21, 2025 memorial.

## Investigative value

The account is a small but datable node in the **succession-skepticism** discourse: Owens'
questions about the Aspen retreat succession story, audio-without-video AmFest playback, and
tarmac-timeline discrepancies circulated through amplifiers like this one. The site treats all
of it as commentary; nothing here asserts wrongdoing by Erika Kirk or any TPUSA figure.
