# ginamilan_ — X Posts

[@ginamilan_](https://x.com/ginamilan_) is cited in the investigation record for July 2026
summaries of the leaked text material attributed to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)
and **Lance Twiggs**. Items below are attributed claims from X posts, not findings of this site.

## Claims from X posts

- **July 9, 2026** — Summarized the leaked-text claim that Robinson said he **dropped a rifle at
  a drop point** and could not retrieve it because **vehicles were still lingering**.
  ([Primary post](https://x.com/ginamilan_/status/2075281454817276120)) — logged on
  [Discord Messages](/Tyler_Robinson/Discord_Messages) alongside @REVWUTRUTH's multi-image
  confession-text posts and @dailymindvirus's claim that TPUSA spokesman **Andrew Kolvet**
  circulated the images.
- The same thread places Twiggs as the **recipient** of alleged confession messages and
  rifle-drop discussion — part of the leaked-text discourse tracked on
 the Twiggs / girlfriend overview.

## Investigative value

Defense-oriented commentators urge treating screenshot dumps as **chain-of-custody problems**
until authenticated in court; prosecution-oriented commentators treat them as corroboration of
the charging theory. The site records @ginamilan_'s summary as one node in that dispute. No
court has authenticated the leaked images as presented on X, and claims that Twiggs was a
co-conspirator are **unverified public commentary**.
