# JustTheTweets17 — X Posts

[@JustTheTweets17](https://x.com/JustTheTweets17) (**Trisha Hope — National Delegate-TX**) is a
high-engagement account whose anomaly lists and oversight questions appear across the site's FBI,
motive, and censorship records. Every item below is an attributed public allegation or question —
not a finding that any living person committed a crime.

## Claims from X posts

- **June 2026** — Posted a widely shared **FBI anomaly list**: weekend crime-scene excavation and
  paving reportedly demanded by state and federal leadership, removal of personal effects, absence
  of **bomb dogs and roof protection**, and K9 non-alerts during the gun search
  ([FBI Investigation](/FBI/FBI_Investigation), [Event Security fix](/Fix/Event_Security)).
- Tied **Kash Patel** to weekend crime-scene paving demands with Governor Cox — no public order
  document produced; attributed allegation ([Kash Patel Briefings](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings)).
- **Oct 25–26, 2025** — Echoed the witness clip in which the FBI reportedly asked a witness to
  delete video, calling it "not normal, this is a coverup!"
  ([FBI Asked to Delete Video](/CoverUp/FBI_Asked_Delete_Video)).
- Circulated screenshots of the alleged early-September **donor texts** ("just lost another huge
  Jewish donor," "leave the pro-Israel cause") — unauthenticated extracts
 (Donors, Israel and Foreign Policy,
  [Charlie's Reported Quotes on Israel](/Israel/Charlie_Quotes_Israel)).
- **November 2025** — Asked why, if Erika Kirk leads TPUSA, the org would ignore a reported
  **September 2, 2025 DOGE-type audit memo** and accept an **award from Israel** roughly 48 hours
  after Charlie's reported leave-the-cause texts — conditional opinion needing primary documents
  ([Control over TPUSA](/Israel_Main_Suspect/control-over-tpusa),
  [Control of the Podcast](/Israel_Main_Suspect/control-podcast)).
- Flagged the apparent disappearance of certain **[Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) podcasts from YouTube** — the
  screenshot preserved in the site's censorship-evidence photo cluster
  ([the screenshot](/Photos/Censorship_Evidence/Img_Photo_9f7fd1)).

## Investigative value

One of the accounts the [FBI overview](/FBI/overview) names as carrying the critical frame in
2025–2026: packaging separate anomalies into a single narrative list. The site routes each item to
a Level 3 page where it is individually sourced rather than adopting the packaged conclusion.
