# DataRepublican — X Posts

[@DataRepublican](https://x.com/DataRepublican) is documented on this site as a leading **rebuttal
voice** against the Owens-franchise theories — a data-research account that defends the state's
charged narrative and condemns attacks on **Erika Kirk**. The site preserves this side of the
argument alongside the skeptic threads; all items are public commentary, not findings.

The site preserves this rebuttal side while still holding that the majority citizen reading — that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive device rather than a rifle round — is set out at [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Claims from X posts

- **July 5–10, 2026** — Published a long rebuttal of Candace Owens-franchise theories, **condemned
  character attacks on Erika Kirk**, and cited **DNA and medical-examiner testimony under oath** at
  the preliminary hearing as supporting the state's charged narrative. Logged on the
  [Erika Kirk timeline](/Timeline/erika-kirk-timeline), which stresses these are commentary, not
  findings of wrongdoing by anyone.
- **Mid-July 2026** — Widely quoted **[Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens)'** statement that she felt "confident" saying
  Robinson *"did not kill, murder Charlie Kirk"* and her raising of blackmail as a possible
  explanation — circulating it into the debate documented on the
  [Tyler Robinson Not Assassin overview](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview), where
  counter-accounts reply that motive skepticism is not proof of innocence.
- Appears in the site's Owens-vs-rebuttal conflict table: Owens docuseries and succession/security
  questions on one side; @DataRepublican and TPUSA-aligned accounts citing sworn forensic testimony
  on the other.

## Investigative value

The site's quality bar requires both sides of every contested lane. @DataRepublican is the
strongest documented **pro-state, pro-Erika** data voice, making it the natural counterweight to
the Proof Not Tyler cluster. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) remains charged, not
convicted; Erika Kirk is not accused of wrongdoing by this site.
