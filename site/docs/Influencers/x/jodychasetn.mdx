# JodyChaseTN — X Posts

[@JodyChaseTN](https://x.com/JodyChaseTN) is one of the highest-volume amplifier accounts in the
site's **cover-up and scene-alteration** record, spanning the paving contractor story, the transport
SUV's fate, courtroom-video disputes, and resignation timing. All items are attributed claims and
questions, not findings of wrongdoing by any living person.

## Claims from X posts

- Amplified **Dan Merrell's Hardscape Utah** "paver emergency" interview clips (from ~May 2026,
  multi-million-view recirculation May 21–22, 2026 alongside @MJTruthUltra and @ShadowofEzra) —
  the account of a Sunday, Sept 14, 2025 call to pave the courtyard
  ([Dirt Removed](/CoverUp/Dirt_Removed), [Scene Changes & Paving Timeline](/Timeline/scene-changes-paving-timeline)).
- Shared **"10 months after it was paved over"** revisit footage keeping the physical alteration
  visible ([Signs of Cover-Up](/US_Intelligence_Assisted/signs-of-cover-up)).
- Posted "located" photo threads on the **black GMC Yukon Denali** that transported Kirk, claiming
  **rear seats removed** — one of several competing disposal narratives
  ([Rental Cars & Vehicle Movements](/Timeline/rental-cars-vehicle-movements-timeline)).
- **July 10, 2026** — Relayed courtroom-attendee commentary (Brandi Siciliani) that **no zoomed-in
  face ID** of [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) firing was shown in court, against competing
  claims that footage showed him taking the shot — both recorded as unverified spectator reports.
- **May 24, 2026** — Asked whether Sheriff **Brooksby** and UVU President **Tuminez** resigning the
  same year is coincidence — a question, not proof of coordination
  ([post](https://x.com/JodyChaseTN/status/2058364181317910586),
  [Institutional Resignations](/After/Institutional_Resignations)).
- Broader threads allege a post-assassination **"hijacking" of TPUSA leadership** — attributed
  commentary that names executives but establishes no wrongdoing by any board member
  ([Tom Sodeika](/People/tom-sodeika)).

## Investigative value

The account functions as a **recirculation engine** for the scene-alteration evidence chain —
paving, SUV disposal, resignations — keeping older physical-evidence questions in front of new
audiences during each news cycle.
