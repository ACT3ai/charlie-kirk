# aldamu_jo — X Posts

[@aldamu_jo](https://x.com/aldamu_jo) is one of the most prolific device-theory and cover-up-lane accounts in the site's record, best known for a high-engagement **January 2026 PETN-in-the-mic-wire video**. Everything below is **attributed social-media claims** — unproven, heavily contested, and not findings of this site; no living person is accused of a crime here.

## Claims from X posts

- **Jan 2026 (high engagement)** — Video claiming "**PETN explosive hidden in his mic wire**" with remote activation "by one of his bodyguards" — pairing neck-motion sequences with a modified-wireless-mic cause of death rather than a bullet. Documented with physics counterarguments at [RØDE mic](/US_Intelligence_Assisted/rode-mic).
- **Jan 2026 (with @realstewpeters)** — Emphasized **glass shards** visible in the SUV that transported Kirk to Timpanogos, linked to a tempered-glass lav mic — a mechanism claim disputed by rifle-shot proponents. See [SUV Transporting Charlie](/Mic/suv-transporting-charlie) and [Charlie's trip to the hospital](/Medical/charlie_trip_to_hospital).
- Pushed modified wireless-mic / shaped-charge claims cited across [the Tent page](/UVU/Tent), [the shooting overview](/Tyler_Robinson/Shooting/overview), and [shot-to-the-heart witness threads](/Witnesses/shot-to-the-heart) — labeled unproven throughout.
- **Nov 23, 2025** — Paired Kash Patel's "see you in **Valhalla**" tribute line with sharp criticism of Patel's record — part of the Valhalla/[Goat Island](/Locations/Goat_Island) commentary stack the site documents alongside debunk replies. See [Valhalla](/Locations/Valhalla).
- Echoed early "**no autopsy**" framing posts — later contradicted by documentary evidence an autopsy occurred; see [autopsy report not public](/Medical/autopsy_report_not_public).
- **Jan 21, 2026** — Tracked the **FedEx discovery list** delivered by citizen investigators to counsel in the case. See [gag orders and secrecy](/After/Legal_Process_Gag_Orders_And_Secrecy).
- Recirculated the three-line Israel stack (kill-threat warning + leaving-the-cause text + 48-hour timing) documented at [Charlie said Israel would kill him](/Israel_Main_Suspect/charlie-said-israel-would-kill-him).

## Investigative value

This account illustrates how the device-theory, autopsy-secrecy, and Israel-motive lanes braid together in citizen traffic. The site pairs each claim with its counterargument — physics rebuttals from [@alexboge](./alexboge), the autopsy evidence, and debunk replies — because the claims travel farther than the corrections.
