# sonnydayzzzz — X Posts

[@sonnydayzzzz](https://x.com/sonnydayzzzz) appears in the site's distraction-people record as one
of the accounts casting **George Zinn's** on-scene false confession as a designed element of a
coordinated operation. The bullets below are **claims attributed to their X posts** — poster
framing, not adjudicated findings.

## Claims from X posts

- Wrote that [George Zinn](/distraction_people/George_Zinn) "jumped up in the crowd declaring he
  shot [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) **to create a distraction**," concluding the killing
  was **"well planned"** — the "planned op" reading, versus the official treatment of Zinn's
  obstruction as real but uncoordinated.
- The handle is cited among the independent X/YouTube commentary sources behind
 The List of Distraction People (Claims), alongside @TheCKFiles,
  @DiligentDenizen, @RickyTheGuido, and others.

## Investigative value

Zinn's admitted obstruction is court record; whether it was coordinated is not. This account
documents the strongest form of the coordination reading, which the site keeps next to the official
position so readers can weigh both. Nothing here is a factual finding that any living, uncharged
individual participated in a crime.
