# robertcalliste1 — X Posts

[@robertcalliste1](https://x.com/robertcalliste1) is cited on the site's forensics pages as a
**July 2026** amplifier of trajectory analysis challenging the official single-rifle account. The
bullets below are **claims attributed to their X posts** — investigative commentary, not court
rulings.

## Claims from X posts

- Circulated **Valhalla VFT "autopsy trajectory"** material that, per the posts, further undercuts
  the rooftop-to-neck bullet path the state attributes to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson).
  See [Tyler Robinson Not Assassin](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/overview).
- Posted that the autopsy / trajectory material **"destroys" the FBI narrative** — attributed
  commentary pending full public autopsy disclosure, quoted on
  [.30-06 Did Not Kill Charlie](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/thirty-06-did-not-kill-charlie).
- The clips sit alongside the wider skeptic stack: the ATF fragment comparison reported as
  [inconclusive](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive), the
  [.30-06 energy mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) arguments, and the
  [trajectory-wound mismatch](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch) analyses.

## Investigative value

The account is part of the July 2026 wave that moved wound-geometry arguments from long-form video
into viral X clips. Its value is as a pointer to the Valhalla VFT material; the underlying autopsy
measurements are not public, so every claim remains unverifiable second-hand analysis. Robinson is
charged, not convicted, and is presumed innocent.
