# Furkan Gözükara — X Posts

[@FurkanGozukara](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara) (**Furkan Gözükara**) is a ~270K-follower account
focused on AI tutorials and tech that periodically amplifies Charlie Kirk case commentary. He is
one of the most-cited **amplifiers** in the site record — a distributor of third-party clips, not
a subject of the investigation. He has a profile at [Furkan Gozukara](/People/furkan-gozukara).
**Status: Alive.** The claims within amplified clips belong to their original speakers and are unverified.

## Claims from X posts

- **May 16, 2026** — Amplified a [Stew Peters](/Influencers/podcasts-stew-peters) segment alleging
  Charlie Kirk rejected an alleged **$1 billion Israeli offer** and that funds were reportedly
  accepted by his organization after his death (1,203 likes / 497 reposts).
  ([Post](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara/status/2055445490003489041)) — full treatment on
 the $1B offer claim page.
- **April 21, 2026** — Shared the **Buckley Carlson** clip criticizing FBI Director
  [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel)'s "I'll see you in **Valhalla**, Charlie" remark and the FBI
  press conference. ([Post](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara/status/2046380775579209982)) — see
  [Buckley Carlson on the Valhalla comment](/Videos/buckley-carlson-kash-patel-valhalla) and
  [Kash Patel briefings](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings).
- **June 17, 2026** — Amplified [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens)–linked commentary claiming bomb dogs were kept off
  the seating area to hide a **rigged explosive microphone**.
  ([Post](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara/status/2067070393743995252)) — tracked on
  [Mic Explosive](/CoverUp/Mic_Explosive) and [Bomb Dogs](/FBI/Bomb_Dogs).
- **June 24, 2026** — Circulated claims that enhanced photos show shattered glass from a rigged
  mic on an SUV floorboard ([post](https://x.com/FurkanGozukara/status/2069583851479875626)); the
  same account appears among the amplifiers of the Owens SUV-interior photo sets — see
  [SUV Destroyed](/CoverUp/SUV_Destroyed).
- High-engagement posts present **Joe Kent** saying multiple people had pre-knowledge and that the
  FBI stopped the foreign-leads inquiry before leads were exhausted — see
  [Foreign Leads](/FBI/Foreign_Leads) and the [legal investigation overview](/legal_investigation/overview),
  which logs his clips alleging discovery obstruction with JFK-files parallels.

## Investigative value

Gözükara's account is a case study in **cross-audience amplification**: a large tech audience
receiving assassination commentary packaged as "BOMBSHELL" framing. Every substantive claim
above originates with other speakers (Peters, Carlson, Owens-adjacent analysts, Kent) and
remains unverified; amplification does not make any of it a finding.
