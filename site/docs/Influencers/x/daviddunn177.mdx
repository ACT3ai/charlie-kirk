# daviddunn177 — X Posts

[@daviddunn177](https://x.com/daviddunn177) is part of the **March 31, 2026 Brooksby cluster** — the
high-engagement X wave that paired **Sheriff Nate Brooksby's resignation** with the reported **ATF
inability to match** the recovered bullet fragment to the Mauser rifle. Temporal clustering is not
proof of causation; these are attributed posts, not findings.

A fragment that reportedly will not match the rifle is central to the argument that Charlie Kirk was not killed by the weapon the state names; see [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Claims from X posts

- **March 31, 2026** — Posted **"We have questions,"** pairing the Brooksby resignation with the
  ATF non-match reporting, alongside @EvanAKilgore and @BowesChay. Logged on
  [official resignations](/Law_Enforcement/official-resignations) and
  [Washington County Sheriff](/Law_Enforcement/washington-county-sheriff).
- The same cluster feeds the [rifle chain-of-custody](/Law_Enforcement/rifle-chain-of-custody)
  page, where the non-match theme meets the July 2026 hearing summary that fragments from Kirk's
  body **"could not be identified or excluded"** from the recovered rifle.
- The site records the counter-narrative too: Police1-style reporting (via @brenda11831) quotes
  Brooksby crediting retired colleague **Mike Mitchell** with enabling a calm surrender — official
  framing that emphasizes de-escalation, not concealment. Brooksby's resignation is a public
  personnel fact; any suggestion he resigned to avoid scrutiny is **commentary**, and he is not
  accused of wrongdoing.

## Investigative value

A representative voice of the temporal-clustering school: the account's value is documenting how
the resignation and the ballistics report landed on the same day in public awareness — a
correlation the site flags explicitly as **not proof**.
