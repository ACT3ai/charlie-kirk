## This Site Is Dedicated to the Citizen Investigators

**WhoAssassinatedCharlieKirk.com is dedicated to all of the citizen investigators working to get truth
for Charlie Kirk.**

Almost nothing on this site originated here. The
[second-by-second evacuation clock](/Timeline/ten-minutes-after-shot), the
[GRAMA records fights](/CoverUp/UVU_GRAMA_Records), the frame-by-frame video comparisons, the
[ballistics arguments](/Gun_Bullet/Gun_Bullet_Investigation_Index), the flight-record digs, the
court-docket reading — that work was done by people with no budget, no press credential, and no
institutional protection, posting under handles on X and YouTube because no newsroom would run it. This
site's job is to **collect that work in one place, source it, and pin it to IPFS** so it survives
deletion.

Their names run through nearly every page here: [Sam Parker](./sam-parker),
[Ian Carroll](./podcasts-ian-carroll), [danksterintel](./x/danksterintel),
[Project Constitution](./x/project-constitution), and the hundreds of accounts indexed under
[X Coverage](./x) and [YouTube Coverage](./youtube). Many have paid for it in reach — see
[Censorship of Investigators](./Censorship_of_Investigators).

## What the Anthem Actually Says

The lyrics are worth reading, because they are a **standard of evidence**, not a slogan. From the
auto-transcript of the video above:

> "This one's for everyone still asking questions. No fear, no silence."

> "I'm not here for rumors, I'm here for the facts. Truth is different when you peel it all back.
> Everybody's talking, everybody's got a side."

> "Justice ain't justice if it changes the day. It should be the same no matter what they say. Turn the
> lights on, let the evidence breathe."

> "Tell me why the standards always seem to change, depending on the headline, depending on the name."

> "You can disagree — that's part of being free. But truth don't belong to you or me. Ask the hard
> questions, let the answer stand. Don't build a verdict with an empty hand."

> "Justice for Charlie — truth don't run when the pressure comes."

> "Truth lasts longer than the headlines."

The refrain — *don't build a verdict with an empty hand* — is the same rule this site tries to hold
itself to. Every claim here is attributed to whoever made it, counterarguments are carried on the same
page as the claim, and nothing is asserted as a finding of this website. That is why pages across this
site read "reportedly," "according to," and "attributed claim" so often. The
[Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and
[Proof Intel Services](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview) sections exist to lay out what the evidence
supports — and where it stops.

## What This Site Is Trying to Get

The end goal is not a theory. It is **records** — the FBI 302s, the complete ballistics workup, the
unredacted booking and arrest paperwork, the [UVU camera feeds](/cameras/overview), the 911 audio, and
the flight manifests. Those are the four
[Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview), and the citizen-investigator community is the reason
there is public pressure behind them at all. What a reader can personally do about it is listed at
[Your Actions Fix It](/Your_Actions_Fix_It/overview).

**Status:** Bryan Starbuck (@HolonCitizen) — Alive

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

The suppressed records this community has spent a year requesting, litigating, and being denied — GRAMA
denials, sealed manifests, withheld camera feeds, unreleased 302s — are precisely the documents the
[Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) are written to force into public view.

## What This Site Does and Does Not Say

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here or in the linked material
committed a crime, acted unlawfully, or had advance knowledge of the assassination. The video on this
page is a **song by a member of the public**, hosted here as a record of the citizen-investigator
movement — it is not evidence about the assassination and makes no factual claim this site adopts.
