## What This Image Shows

This is an **AI-generated explainer graphic**, not a photograph and not a screenshot of any Steam page. The figure on the left is a synthetic illustration standing in for a gamer at a monitor &mdash; it is not a photograph of [**Tyler Robinson**](/People/tyler-robinson) or of anyone else. Everything of substance in the graphic is the table on the right, which collects into one place the eleven handles that open-source researchers have reported as friends of, or commenters on, the Steam account attributed to him.

The handles as listed are **Ankha**, **Ankha_Kitty**, **cento**, **peace walker**, **Lucy**, **Thank You**, **Xx_chubby_ch43_xX**, **luvr**, **fentanylsandwich1312**, **Alive but Gay**, **generic8076** and **miss your son is clicker trained**. Every one of them is a pseudonym. None has been publicly tied to a real identity, and the roster itself is a researcher compilation drawn from screenshots of a comment wall and a friend list &mdash; it is not a Valve record, a subpoena return, or anything a filing has vouched for. The image reproduces that caution in its own footer: **most or all are almost certainly innocent gamers**.

The header line asks a narrow question &mdash; whether US intelligence should establish which, if any, of these accounts were operated by a foreign service. That is a request for an investigative step, not a finding, and nothing here alleges wrongdoing by any account holder. The reason the question is asked at all is that account identity in this case is genuinely unresolved: the Utah State Bureau of Investigation sought [Discord](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/overview) records for named usernames, prosecutors filed that they did not intend to offer material obtained through forensic extractions of the seized electronics, and the roster below has never been reconciled against a platform record. Handles are also trivially reused &mdash; **Ankha** is an Animal Crossing character, and the same name is on thousands of accounts.

Read this as an index card for the [Steam](/Tyler_Robinson/steam/overview) section rather than as evidence in itself. The underlying screenshots are the primary material: [the Ankha profile](/Photos/Tyler_Robinson/Steam_msgs/Img_Photo_75bf47), [the comment threads](/Photos/Tyler_Robinson/Steam_msgs/Img_Photo_013e49) and [the composite of usernames across documents](/Photos/Tyler_Robinson/Steam_msgs/Img_Photo_9eb218).
