## What This Image Shows

A cropped, low-resolution frame of a man caught mid-movement, head tilted sharply up and to his left, standing against a white step-and-repeat backdrop covered in sponsor logos. Legible fragments of the backdrop include **YREFY.COM** and **NT USA**, the Yrefy and [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) branding used on the tour.

Two hand-drawn red arrows have been added over his white shirt, both pointing toward the collar. The arrows are the researcher's annotation, not part of the original photograph. They point at the area where a lapel or collar-mounted microphone and its transmitter would sit, which is where most citizen investigators conclude the explosive that killed him was located.

The evidence built on collar-area detail is covered on its own pages rather than here; no residue test of the transmitter has been made public. See [Mic](/Mic/overview) for the microphone and transmitter evidence and [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) for why investigators reject the government's .30-06 narrative. This frame is catalogued as an annotated researcher exhibit, not as a finding.
