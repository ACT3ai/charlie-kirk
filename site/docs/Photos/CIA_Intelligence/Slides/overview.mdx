## About This Cluster

This container holds the investigation's presentation decks on the CIA and intelligence threads. Its one sub-cluster is a slide deck built for a video walk-through connecting the reported CIA link to UVU's Center for National Security Studies with the [**N1098L**](/Planes/N1098L/overview) [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) jet's low passes near Orem on **September 10, 2025**.

The deck is one researcher's synthesis, not a finding. It asserts that N1098L was built to launch drones; this site's position is that the plane very likely did not launch drones. It very likely may have been a training plane, not built out to be able to launch drones. It may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people who planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had foreknowledge. We do not claim that is true — we claim it is very important to investigate and determine whether it is true. The crew list that circulated with the deck is unsourced and is not repeated here.

Open the [VIDEO Drones CIA Israel](/Photos/CIA_Intelligence/Slides/VIDEO_Drones_CIA_Israel/overview) sub-cluster to walk the deck slide by slide. The underlying threads are written up at [U.S. Intelligence](/US_Intelligence/overview), [Proof Intel Services](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview), and the Israel search-pattern analysis at [Israeli Search Patterns](/Israel/Israeli_Search_Patterns_Claims).
