## About This Cluster

These images are frames from a single citizen-research slide deck built around the assassination of [**Charlie Kirk**](/People/charlie-kirk) at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview) on **September 10, 2025**. The deck tries to draw one map across a claimed CIA link to UVU's **Center for National Security Studies**, a set of **Google Trends** charts, and the aircraft [**N1098L**](/Planes/N1098L/overview) overhead — which is why it sits inside the [CIA and US Intelligence](/Photos/CIA_Intelligence/overview) cluster.

The flight data on the slides is the deck author's reading of public flight-tracking records: **N1098L**, associated with the callsign **AXEL10** and the Army's **[HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES)** program, is shown descending to roughly **203 feet at about 116 knots** one mile from UVU at **9:16 a.m. MT** on September 10 and passing again about **25 minutes after** the shooting. The deck also asserts that the aircraft was built to launch drones. This site's position is different. The plane very likely did not launch drones. It very likely may have been a training plane, not built out to be able to launch drones. It may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people who planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had foreknowledge. We do not claim that is true — we claim it is very important to investigate and determine whether it is true.

The crew list and name charts that circulated with this deck are unsourced — the crew of the flight is not public — and this site does not repeat them or attach any person to the aircraft. For the flight record itself see [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview), and for the wider thread the [U.S. Intelligence](/US_Intelligence/overview) section.
