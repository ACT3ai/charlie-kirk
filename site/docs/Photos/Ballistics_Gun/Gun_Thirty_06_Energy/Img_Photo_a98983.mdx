## What This Image Shows

A size comparison of three cartridges — a 9mm handgun round, a 5.56 NATO rifle round, and a **.30-06** — shown side by side so the difference in scale is immediate. The graphic is pinned on IPFS and is credited on the investigation page to [@HolonCitizen](/Influencers/x/holoncitizen), posted April 17, 2026.

It is used on [.30-06 Energy Mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) to establish the premise of that page before the numbers arrive: the round in the official account is not a handgun round and is larger than the standard-issue rifle round used by most modern military units.

The argument built on that premise is that a projectile of this size and energy behaves very differently on impact from what the described injury suggests. Commentators including Dr. [**Chris Martenson**](/People/chris-martenson) have argued a genuine .30-06 strike at that range would have produced far more violent movement and far more catastrophic tissue damage than the footage shows. The autopsy that could test the government's account has not been released, and the energy mismatch is one of the reasons investigators have concluded the fatal injury came from an explosive at the microphone rather than a .30-06 round — see [Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview). The numbers are laid out at [.30-06 Energy Mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch), with related material at [Alternative Calibers and Weapons](/Gun_Bullet/Alternative_Calibers_Weapons) and [Alternative Kill Mechanisms](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).
