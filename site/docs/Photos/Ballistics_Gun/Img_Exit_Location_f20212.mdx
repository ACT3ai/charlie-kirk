## What This Image Shows

A three-view infographic built on three-dimensional renders of a head and shoulders against black — front view, right profile, and left profile. Red circles mark the claimed entry wound, yellow circles the claimed exit, a yellow dotted line joins the two on the right profile, and green arrows indicate direction. A dreamstime watermark on the renders shows the base models are stock artwork, not case photographs.

The diagram is an online reconstruction, not a medical document, and no autopsy or medical examiner's finding has been released against which to test it. Its only real value is that it commits to something specific: a named entry point, a named exit point, and a direction between them. Most of the material in this cluster gestures at the problem in general terms; this one states a position precise enough to be checked whenever the medical record is released.

The competing reconstructions of the wound are collected at [Trajectory and Wound Mismatch](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch), [Wound Trajectory Angle](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle), and [Ballistics and Wound Analysis](/Medical/ballistics_and_wound_analysis). They do not agree with each other, and the arguments over what mechanism could produce the wound are set out at [Alternative Kill Mechanisms](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).
