## What This Image Shows

This is a composite of two numbered transcript excerpts from a preliminary hearing, presented as monospaced question-and-answer text with reader annotations. A black divider bar between the two panels reads “07.09.2026 PRELIMINARY HEARING TRANSCRIPT”, and a repeated faint watermark reads “@DaniHasReceipts”. In the upper excerpt (lines 17–25) counsel asks whether the witness inspected the interior of the barrel and whether it had any debris or dust; the witness answers that their notes state debris and dust was observed inside the barrel, and when asked to characterise it further says the notes say “dust.” The words “interior of this barrel”, “debris and dust” and “inside of the barrel” are picked out in yellow highlighter and the key answer is boxed in red. In the lower excerpt (lines 9–18) counsel asks whether the witness measured the diameter of the bullet and what the reading was; after asking to refer to notes, the witness answers that the range of the diameter for the 6A bullet jacket fragment was “.286 to .301 inches”, a line highlighted in yellow and boxed in red.
