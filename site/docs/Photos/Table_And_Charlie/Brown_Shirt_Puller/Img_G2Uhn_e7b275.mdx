## What This Image Shows

An indoor portrait of a smiling, tattooed middle-aged man in a grey sleeveless shirt and a black structured baseball cap, holding an infant with a pacifier on his lap. They are seated in a light grey upholstered armchair against a plain wall with vertical wood panelling.

The cap carries a white embroidered **HEX** logo with a stylized arrow for the X, and additional text fragments read **OPERATION UNDERGROUND** and a partial word. Operation Underground Railroad is a Utah-founded anti-trafficking organisation, and apparel associated with it is widely sold and worn.

A family photograph in which the only investigative content is a logo on a hat is very thin material, and the site says so plainly. It was gathered during an identification attempt and is retained for completeness of that record.
