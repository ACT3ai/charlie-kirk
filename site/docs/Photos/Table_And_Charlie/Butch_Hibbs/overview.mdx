## About This Cluster

Two images sit here. Researchers filing this material identify the older man with a full white beard, sunglasses and a cap, photographed standing beside [**Charlie Kirk**](/People/charlie-kirk) in the white FREEDOM T-shirt at the event, as [**Butch Hibbs**](/People/butch-hibbs); that identification comes from open-source comparison rather than from any released record. The object in his front shirt pocket looks about the size of a RODE wireless mic receiver. We do not claim that it is one. We do not claim that he knew anything ahead of time, and we do not claim that he was involved in any way. We say only that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated.

The second image is a close-up of a black **RODE Wireless GO II** receiver clipped to the chest pocket of a green, grey and white plaid button-up shirt, its LCD lit and showing active channel levels. Researchers say the shirt matches the one worn by the older man in the first photograph; that match is theirs. A receiver on a chest pocket is a completely normal way to run event audio. We assume that every TPUSA employee and every audio-visual contractor working at UVU that day had no knowledge of any explosive that may have been in the mic, even if they handled the mic.

Read [Butch Hibbs](/People/butch-hibbs) for the profile. For the equipment, go to [RODE Wireless Mic](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) and [AV Team and Mic Setup](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup).
