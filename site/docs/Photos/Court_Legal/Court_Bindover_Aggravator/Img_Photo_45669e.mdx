## What This Image Shows

This image is a digital photograph or scan of page 2 of a legal brief filed in the Utah criminal case State v. Tyler Robinson. The page is headed ARGUMENT in capital letters, followed by a first heading arguing that the State has not met its burden to show probable cause by evidence deemed reliable under Rule of Criminal Procedure 1102, in violation of the defendant's constitutional rights and separation of powers principles. The body text discusses hearsay, the identity evidence drawn from Discord communications and UVU surveillance footage, and a recorded interview with witness Lance Twiggs whom the defense was not allowed to subpoena from another state. A second roman-numeral heading argues the State cannot establish the aggravator that the shooting knowingly created a high probability of death to another individual, given a single shot from a high-powered scoped rifle from a rooftop vantage that hit the intended target, citing Utah Code section 76-5-202(2)(a)(ii). The page number 2 appears at the bottom.
