## About This Cluster

This cluster collects the recordings behind one of the earliest and most repeated claims about September 10, 2025: that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was hit **in the heart or the chest**. Several on-camera witnesses at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview) said so within minutes, on live television, before any footage was public and before any medical information had been released. The written record for the whole thread is [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart), and the witness-side analysis is at [Shot to the Heart](/Witnesses/shot-to-the-heart).

The items are kept together because they disagree with each other, not because they agree. Items 1, 2 and 4 are the heart-and-chest accounts. Item 3 is the contrasting account of blood from the **neck**, which is closer to what the released video is read as showing. Item 5 is a broadcast remark about a pulse. Items 6, 7 and 8 are posters advancing **their own mechanisms** — body armor, fragmentation, a ricochet off a vest — and are recorded here as those posters' arguments, not as findings. Item 9 is a widely circulated report about a request that he wear a vest.

Nothing on this page establishes a wound location or a cause of death. What the cluster documents is **what was said, by whom, and how early** — which is the part of the record that can still be checked. Every clip is pinned so it survives deletion of the original post, and each item page names its source. Living people appear in these recordings and none of them is accused of anything here. See also [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) and [Shot in the Chest, Then the Neck](/Witnesses/Shot_in_Chest_Reversal).
