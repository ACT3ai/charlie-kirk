## What This Image Shows

A stylized overhead site map, drawn in flat greys, black and white rather than taken from satellite imagery, with lettered badges marking buildings and zones - **PS**, **HF**, **EN**, **B**, **WB**, **FG**, **BA** - and coloured dots placed at specific positions.

The graphic carries a colour legend and the watermark **[@WeAreNotGoingToMars](/Influencers/x/wearenotgoingto)**. It is one researcher's annotated hypothesis about positions around the venue. It is unverified, no named individual is attached to any dot on this page, and nothing here should be read as an accusation against any person. For the venue geometry see [Angles](/Photos/Maps/Angles/overview) and [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center).
