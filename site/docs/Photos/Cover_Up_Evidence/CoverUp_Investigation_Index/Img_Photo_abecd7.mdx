## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Project Constitution - 🚨EXPOSED： FBI Ordered Local Police Not to INTERVIEW Witnesses...”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video clip compiles two security/phone footage segments overlaid with police dispatch audio during an incident at Utah Valley University (UVU). The first segment shows an exterior zoom-in on the Sparks Automotive building on campus. The second segment, filmed from inside a building overlooking a massive crowd, highlights a figure on a distant roof with a red circle. The dispatch audio details coordination with the FBI regarding a potential suspect.
