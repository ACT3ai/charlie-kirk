## What This Image Shows

A low-resolution capture of a phone screen — taken as a photograph of the display rather than as a screenshot, judging by the slight angle, soft focus and uneven lighting — showing the mobile Wikipedia article headed "Herzog family."

The legible text is the article's own summary: that the Herzog family is a Jewish family that includes the sixth and eleventh Presidents of Israel and a Chief Rabbi of Israel, with an information sidebar classifying it as a political family, listing the current region as Israel and the place of origin as Eastern Europe.

The image is a reference lookup, not proof. It carries no caption, no annotation and no stated connection to any person on the security detail or to the events of [**September 10, 2025**](/Timeline/Sept_10_2025), and this site asserts none. Someone collecting material for the security-team folder looked up a public encyclopaedia entry and saved the screen; what they intended to establish is not recorded on the image, and readers should not infer a link that the file itself does not make.

The site's sourced treatment of the Israel thread, including what is and is not established, is at [Israel](/Israel/overview) and [Israel and Mossad](/Killer/israel-mossad).
