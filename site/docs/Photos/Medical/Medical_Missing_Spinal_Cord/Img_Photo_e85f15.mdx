## What This Image Shows

This is a scan of a handwritten witness statement, headed in print “Voluntary Statement / Affidavit (continued)” with “Page 2 of 3” completed by hand at the top right. The body is a continuous first-person account in blue ballpoint on ruled lines, with several crossings-out. It describes Charlie walking out and throwing hats into the crowd for about five to ten minutes, then sitting down and addressing the crowd, saying something like “give me Utah's best liberals”; someone making the claim that “Mormonism has more historical claims for it than protestantism”, after which he spent about five minutes talking about what he loves about Mormons and went back and forth until the crowd began yelling “Next question”; and then a further question, “How many shooters have been Trans in the past 20 years”, to which he answered “too many”, with a follow-up exchange about “counting or not counting gang violence.” A long passage marked by the reader in yellow highlighter then records that the writer was turning back to look at the stage and heard a loud pop they thought was a balloon, saw Charlie's body jump from the impact, and could see the spot where it hit in his neck — adding that they had originally thought the bullet hit the other side but it was on his left side, where the writer was standing. The account continues that the impact was dime-sized and very dark red, that bright red blood sprayed forward and down about a foot and a half in front of him, that the crowd was in shock because of the amount of blood, and that he slumped back in the chair but stayed propped up. The writer then describes being pulled to the ground, praying and repenting, expecting further shots, and being stepped on and trampled, with one name blacked out by a redaction bar near the foot of the page. A red stamp at the lower right reads “PROTECTED INFORMATION — Further dissemination to any unauthorized person or agency may result in both civil and criminal liability.”
