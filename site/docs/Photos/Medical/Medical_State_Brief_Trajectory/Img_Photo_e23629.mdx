## What This Image Shows

This is a zoomed, annotated crop of page 2 of the same court filing, with orange hand-drawn underlining added throughout by a reader. The opening lines state that a gunshot rang out and a bullet struck the left side of Mr. Kirk's neck, killing him, and that the bullet's trajectory was “front to back, slightly left to right, and downward.” An italic section heading, underlined, reads “The investigation”. The following paragraph records that the medical examiner concluded the manner of death “to be homicide”, that an ATF forensic examination revealed the bullet was a .30 caliber class round, and that it created a “complex entrance gunshot wound” and fragmented in the neck. The final paragraph quotes that the bullet, or at least parts of it, “sequentially perforated the anterior left side of [Mr. Kirk's] neck, strap muscles of the left side of the neck, [and the] left common carotid and left internal and external jugular veins”, that it also “obliterate[ed] the left side of the C2-7 vertebrae and transect[ed] the cervical spinal cord”, and that the medical examiner observed that “[a] significant portion of [Mr. Kirk's] cervical spinal cord [was] traumatically” — the sentence continuing onto the next page. Exhibit citations appear throughout and the page number 2 sits at the foot. The underlining marks the trajectory and anatomical findings; the page records the prosecution's account.
