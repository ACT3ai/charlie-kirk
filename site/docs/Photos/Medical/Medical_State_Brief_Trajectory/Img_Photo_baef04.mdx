The visible text records that Mr. Kirk suffered other severe injuries including “bilateral apical and posterior intercostal hemorrhages, pulmonary apical hemorrhages/hematomas … and multiple disruptions of the thyroid, cricoid, and tracheal cartilages”, and that the medical examiner also observed hemorrhaging around his heart (“hemopericardium”), both of his lungs (“left and right hemothorax”), and in the subarachnoid space around “the cerebellar vermis and the parietal regions” of his brain.
