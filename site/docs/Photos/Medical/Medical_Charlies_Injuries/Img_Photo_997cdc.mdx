## What This Image Shows

This is a scan of page 3 of a court filing, set in justified serif type with legal citations, on white with a soft grey border. The first paragraph continues a quotation from the medical examiner's report, listing other severe injuries including “bilateral apical and posterior intercostal hemorrhages, pulmonary apical hemorrhages/hematomas … and multiple disruptions of the thyroid, cricoid, and tracheal cartilages”, and recording that the examiner also observed hemorrhaging around the heart (“hemopericardium”), both lungs (“left and right hemothorax”), and in the subarachnoid space around “the cerebellar vermis and the parietal regions” of the brain. The second paragraph states that when the bullet struck, Officer Christopher Bagley of the UVU police was stationed on the south end of the walkway roof of the Hall of Flags above the canopy, and that based on his experience with firearms Bagley believed the bullet came from a rifle fired from a position east of the canopy. The third describes Bagley reasoning that the Losee Building lay east of the amphitheater with a direct line of sight, going to that roof, and observing a screwdriver about 10 or 15 feet from the guardrail and disturbances in the gravel at the southwest edge consistent with a “sniper pad” — a place where a person was “laying in a prone position” with a “line of sight”. The fourth recounts Bagley reviewing surveillance video of the Losee Building rooftop showing a male running to the southwest edge, dropping down, crawling and getting prone, and that at approximately 12:23:28 p.m. — described as the moment of the gunshot — that individual stood, ran northeast across the roof carrying a long, skinny, black object, and dropped down from the northeast side of the roof to the ground. Transcript and exhibit citations appear throughout. The page records the prosecution's account.
