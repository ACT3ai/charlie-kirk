## About This Cluster

Jeremy Gallman is a camera operator and video editor who works for [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) out of Phoenix, Arizona. An image naming him circulated on X in April 2026, which is the only reason a photo cluster carries his name. Operating a camera is a job. Nothing here indicates that he had knowledge of, or involvement in, anything that happened on [September 10, 2025](/Timeline/Sept_10_2025), no such claim is made, and he has not been charged with or accused of anything by any authority.

The single image is the composite graphic that made the identification. It sets an Instagram-style group photograph taken in front of a Turning Point Action backdrop in Phoenix, Arizona, with one man boxed in green, beside a bright outdoor frame of a camera operator working an event, and adds a large golden arrow and red pointers to link the two. It was published by @MuppetMasher on X on April 20, 2026. The graphic's own claim is that the boxed man and the camera operator are the same person; nothing in it verifies that beyond visual resemblance.

Read it as what it is: an unverified visual match naming a working video professional. The career record — where he has worked and what he does — is set out on his profile at [Jeremy Gallman](/People/jeremy-gallman), which also distinguishes him from two unrelated men of the same name. A second copy of the same graphic sits under [TBD](/Photos/People/People_TBD/overview).
