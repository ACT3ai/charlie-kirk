## About This Cluster

Jeremy Gallman is a camera operator and video editor for [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) — a role he states himself on his own LinkedIn profile. He came to the organization as an independent contractor doing video work and, from January 2025, is listed as staff rather than contract. A graphic naming him circulated on X in April 2026, which is why a photo cluster carries his name.

**Operating a camera is a job.** Nothing here indicates that he had knowledge of, or involvement in, anything that happened on [September 10, 2025](/Timeline/Sept_10_2025), no such claim is made, and he has not been charged with or accused of anything by any authority. This site makes no claim about which events he was assigned to; Turning Point USA has never published a crew list.

The identifying image is the composite graphic that named him publicly. It sets an Instagram-style group photograph taken in front of a Turning Point Action backdrop, with one man boxed in green, beside a bright outdoor frame of a camera operator working an event, and adds a large golden arrow and red pointers to link the two. It was published by @MuppetMasher on X on April 20, 2026.

His career record is set out in full on his profile at [Jeremy Gallman](/People/jeremy-gallman), which also distinguishes him from the two unrelated men who share his name. A second copy of the same graphic sits under [TBD](/Photos/People/People_TBD/overview).
