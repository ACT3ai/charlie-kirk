## What This Image Shows

Black-and-white quote card circulated on X by critics of conservative commentator Andrew Wilson after his August 14 2026 PBD Podcast debate with Candace Owens about the Charlie Kirk assassination. The left half is a portrait photograph of Wilson, bearded with slicked-back hair, seated at a podcast microphone against a blurred crowd background. The right half is solid black carrying the line "The bullet could have broken apart in mid air for all we know!" in white serif type, credited beneath to Andrew Wilson. The attributed wording has not been located in any published transcript of the debate and is treated on the site as an unverified attribution rather than an established quotation.
