## About This Cluster

Chris Bagley is a former Utah Valley University campus police officer who, by the reported account, was the first officer to reach the roof of the [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) — the position the state's charging narrative identifies as the shooter's perch. He testified at the 2026 preliminary hearing in State of Utah v. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). He is a witness, not a suspect; he has not been charged with anything and is presumed innocent. The questions raised about his body-worn camera in public commentary are questions, not findings, and body-worn cameras genuinely do fail.

The single image is a screenshot of an anonymous email sent to the [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) tips line, with the sender redacted, describing the behaviour of an Axon Body Cam 4. It was shared by [@tsorama](/Influencers/x/tsorama) on X on July 17, 2026, quoting [@TheCKFiles1](/Influencers/x/theckfiles1). The email is anonymous, its author is unknown, and nothing in it has been verified. It is preserved because the technical claim it makes — about how a specific camera model records and when it stops — is checkable against the manufacturer's documentation and against the officer's reported recording length of roughly twenty-seven and a half minutes.

Read the email as an unverified tip and the recording question as unresolved. What is documented is the testimony itself: the reported roof arrival around 12:44 p.m., roughly twenty-one minutes after the shot, and the discovery of a screwdriver and disturbed gravel. The full profile is at [Chris Bagley](/People/chris-bagley), the roof location is at [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center), and the hearing record is at [Court](/court/overview).
