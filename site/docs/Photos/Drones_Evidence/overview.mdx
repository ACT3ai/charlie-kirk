## About This Cluster

Both images on this page are screenshots of public X posts by the account "Project Constitution" (@ProjectConstitu) asking the same question: were drones flying over the [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) event at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview) on the day [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was shot. The first argues the possibility should not be dismissed. The second circles what the poster calls a "mysterious man" holding a controller-like device in the crowd. Neither establishes that a drone was present, and the object in the second post is not identified. What they document is that the drone question became an active thread in the online discussion of the case within days of the shooting.

The stronger material sits in the sub-clusters below rather than on this page. [Crowd Drones](/Photos/Drones_Evidence/Drones_Butch_Hibbs/Img_Photo_acbbc0) covers what witnesses in the crowd reported. That sub-cluster's title names Butch Hibbs only because of discussion of an object in his front pocket about the size of a Rode wireless mic receiver; we do not claim that it is one, and we do not claim he knew anything ahead of time or was involved — only that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated. [Butler PA Drone Parallel](/Photos/Drones_Evidence/Drones_Butler_Parallel/overview) sets the UVU reports against the drone activity documented at the Butler, Pennsylvania rally.

Separately, and on a different kind of evidence, the ADS-B record shows the business jet [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) making low, slow passes near the Utah Valley that morning. That flight profile is in the public tracking data and stands on its own. It does not by itself say anything about drones, and no drone has been tied to it by any record this investigation has been able to obtain. The plane very likely did not launch drones. It very likely may have been a training plane, not built out to be able to launch drones. It may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people who planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had foreknowledge. We do not claim that is true — we claim it is very important to investigate and determine whether it is true.
