## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “10 Drones”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video is a compiled digital analysis of a livestream captured at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. The clip captures a gathering of students, brief interviews, the sudden sound of a gunshot, and the subsequent panic. The video is presented as a forensic investigation or video report analyzing several fast-moving unknown aerial objects (UFOs/UAPs) spotted in the sky shortly after the shooting.
