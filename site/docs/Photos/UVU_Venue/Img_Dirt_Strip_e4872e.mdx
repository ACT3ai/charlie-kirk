## What This Image Shows

A ground-level frame of the Sorensen Student Center courtyard at Utah Valley University, shot across the excavated zone toward the tiered amphitheater and the stacked-stone planter. A **wide strip of bare, dry soil** runs the full width of the foreground. Behind it the amphitheater lawn is still green and intact, and the two surfaces meet along a **clean, straight cut line** — the sod was cut and lifted along an edge rather than worn or scuffed away.

A **metal stake** stands upright in the dirt with a cable or hose running from its base. An **irrigation valve box** sits at right where the soil meets a poured concrete pad. Across the upper terrace, **yellow tape** is still strung between supports, and a green dumpster is parked under the building deck at left. The lower right corner carries an **SPN** (Stew Peters Network) watermark, so this is a frame circulated through that outlet rather than an original from the posting account.

## Why Researchers Point To It

This is the courtyard **between** the two phases described on [10 Inches of Dirt Removed Before the Pavers](/Tent/Dirt_Removed_Before_Pavers) — after the ground was opened, before the pavers went down. The detail researchers focus on is the **cut sod edge**: a deliberate excavation boundary, consistent with an intentional strip-out rather than incidental damage from foot traffic or equipment.

The yellow tape still strung across the terrace behind is the second point raised. Whether it is scene tape or ordinary construction barrier tape **cannot be determined from this resolution**, and the photograph carries no visible timestamp. Both readings circulate; neither is established here.

## What It Does Not Establish

The frame does **not** show a measurable depth, does not carry an intact timestamp, and does not identify who performed the work. Contractor [Daniel Merrell](/Tent/Daniel_Merrell_Hardscape_Utah) has described arriving on **Sunday, September 14, 2025** to find roughly ten inches already removed by a crew he did not name, and has said he photographed what he found. **This image has not been confirmed as one of his photographs.** Nothing here shows any person or agency destroying evidence, and no court has made a spoliation finding in this matter. See [State Workers & Groundskeepers](/Tent/State_Workers_Groundskeepers) for what the record does and does not say about the first crew, and [Site Alteration Evidence](/Tent/Site_Alteration_Evidence) for the full catalog of physical changes.
