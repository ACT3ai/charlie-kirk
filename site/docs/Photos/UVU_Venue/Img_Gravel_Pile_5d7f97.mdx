## What This Image Shows

A vertical frame taken from the upper plaza level looking out across campus. A **mound of loose aggregate** — coarse gravel or road base — sits on the concrete apron in the middle distance. Someone has **circled it by hand in blue marker**; the annotation is the poster's, not part of the original photograph.

To the right of the mound, a **white flatbed dump truck** is parked with its bed empty. In the foreground, **stacked metal crowd-control barricades** lie on their sides against a low retaining wall, with more construction material piled behind them at left. Beyond the mound the **campus fountain** is running, light poles and trees line the walkway, and the Wasatch range fills the horizon under a partly clouded sky. An **SPN** watermark sits at lower right.
