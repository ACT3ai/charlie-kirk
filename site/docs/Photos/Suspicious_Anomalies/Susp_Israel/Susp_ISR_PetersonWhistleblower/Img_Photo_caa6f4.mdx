## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “@ProjectConstitu - Joshua Peterson TPUSA whistleblower testimony”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video is a self-recorded vlog by a young man who identifies himself as Joshua Peterson, a former representative for Turning Point USA. He recounts a story from his training at Turning Point, describing how after several intense days of training, they were promised some group time. However, instead of hanging out with their leaders, they were paired up with former representatives from previous years. He explains that these former reps, whom he cannot name, rented two stripper vans to take them out to bars in Arizona, where they allegedly got them very drunk and then began questioning them individually. Peterson details his own encounter where he was questioned about his views on Israel and was told that the former reps were part of Israeli groups and forces, despite working for Turning Point for the past several years.
