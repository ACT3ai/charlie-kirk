This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Shot Angle”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This investigative video presents a conspiracy theory regarding a shooting incident at Utah Valley University's Losee Center. The second half consists of zoomed-in, slowed-down clips of a live public speaking event under an "American Comeback" tent that is interrupted by a gunshot.
