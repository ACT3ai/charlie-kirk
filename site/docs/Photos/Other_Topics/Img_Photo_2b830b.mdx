## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Sarah Fields”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video captures the immediate aftermath of the July 13, 2024, assassination attempt on former U.S. President Donald Trump at a rally in Butler, Pennsylvania. Shot on a mobile phone from within a crowd of attendees, it shows people huddled and ducking for safety inside an open-ended concrete tunnel structure. A black SUV, presumably part of the security detail, is parked nearby as attendees, some wearing "Make America Great Again" hats, react with confusion, fear, and panic. The purpose of the video is eyewitness citizen journalism, documenting the tense atmosphere immediately following the shooting.
