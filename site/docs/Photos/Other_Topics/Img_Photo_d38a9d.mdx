This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Rosy - @shaunmmaguire @elonmusk Suspect Spotted on Rooftop before Charlie Ki”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A short amateur video clip recorded on a mobile phone showing a zoomed-in view of a modern multi-story campus building. The video documents what appears to be a dark figure crouching or lying flat on the rooftop.
