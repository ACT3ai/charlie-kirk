## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Chopper 5 Over the Losee Center Roof, One Hour After”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: KSL Chopper 5 aerial footage of the Losee Center roof at Utah Valley University, filmed roughly an hour after the September 10, 2025 shooting. The camera holds a high oblique angle on the roof deck and the surrounding campus. The clip carries ambient rotor noise but no narration. It was posted to X to argue that the disturbance in the roof gravel sits significantly farther back from the edge than the state's account describes, and reviewers note that no crime-scene tape and no screwdriver are visible anywhere on the roof in this footage.
