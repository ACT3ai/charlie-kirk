## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Project Constitution - Black Shards Falling From Charlie Kirk's Shirt”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A 38-second slow-motion analysis clip of the moment Charlie Kirk collapses at UVU. The poster circles small dark fragments they say fall from under his shirt near his belt line and argues they are shrapnel from the Rode wireless mic transmitter — the poster's claim that an explosive device, not a gunshot, caused the wound. No meaningful speech; captioned slow-motion footage.
