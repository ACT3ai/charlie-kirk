## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Chris Goodwin - Here is the @RealCandaceO Video matched up with the footage from the preliminary hearing”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A 3:07 assembly posted to X. The first ~1:28 is the rooftop video circulated by Candace Owens; after 1:28 the clip switches to the version shown at the preliminary hearing, filmed off courtroom TV screens by media cameras, with courtroom audio of an investigator being walked through the footage by counsel.
