## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “6 Pellet Gun”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A handheld smartphone video captures a police arrest on a city sidewalk in Provo, Utah. Several Provo Police officers are seen securing a handcuffed suspect in the back of a police SUV, after which they search his green drawstring bag, emptying its contents onto the sidewalk.
