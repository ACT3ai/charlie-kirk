## What This Image Shows

This is a stitched screenshot of a long private message addressed to Charlie, shown as three consecutive grey message bubbles with a timestamp of 2:45 PM at the foot. The sender identifies themselves as an Evangelical Christian Zionist and “a sister in the faith”. The message asks why he would platform “anti-Semites like Candace and Tucker”, disputes his account of why people accuse him of antisemitism, and asks what exactly he disagrees with about the Israeli government. A passage underlined in red by the reader reads “Be very careful, Charlie.” It continues that he is in violation of the 9th Commandment by bearing false witness against “your brother, Judah”, asks when he last read his Bible, and cites Gen. 12:3 and Obadiah as prophets warning that those who curse Israel will be cursed. It criticises a TPUSA Tampa event as a disaster, says Michele Bachmann was booed and about ten percent walked out when she talked about standing with Israel, and contrasts his influence over young voters with his failure to make them “stand with our Jewish brethren”. The final bubble says the Jews are under attack, refers to God wiping out the descendants of Edom for standing by while their brother Jacob/Israel was held captive, and urges him not to be counted among the Edomites — closing with a sentence highlighted in orange: “It will be to your peril, as well as the unfinished work G-D was going to do through you.” The sender's name and handle are not visible in the crop.
