## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “@troofevades - Demonstration of a real shaped charge detonating”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video is a demonstration of a handmade shaped explosive charge. The creator showcases the construction of the charge and its primary elements, including a copper cone and a custom casing, before deploying it against a brass padlock. The device is detonated outdoors in a grassy area, successfully blasting a hole through the lock body.
