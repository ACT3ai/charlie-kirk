## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Video 3”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video captures a large outdoor crowd gathered on a university campus for an event featuring Charlie Kirk. From an elevated perspective, the camera pans across the courtyard when a sudden loud popping sound occurs. The crowd immediately panics, screams, and scatters in multiple directions as the narrator speculates about a gunshot.
