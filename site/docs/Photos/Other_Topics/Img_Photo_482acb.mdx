## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “13 Pellet Gun”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This citizen-shot video captures police officers arresting a suspect in Orem, Utah. Several officers from the Provo Police Department secure the handcuffed suspect in a police vehicle and search his green bag on the sidewalk. The footage serves as an eyewitness record of a law enforcement operation, with the cameraman narrating the discovery of a rifle.
