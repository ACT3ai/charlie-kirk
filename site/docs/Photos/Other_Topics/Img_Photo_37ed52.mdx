## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Witness Clip of the American Comeback Tent Setup at UVU”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A 12-second handheld vertical clip filmed from inside the crowd at Utah Valley University before the event began, looking toward the TPUSA American Comeback Tour tent with its PROVE ME WRONG banners. The camera zooms in past the packed crowd to show staff working under the tent — arranging the stage table stacked with red caps, moving equipment behind the backdrop, and a man in a light blue shirt carrying a dark case across the platform. The account that published it presents it as new witness footage of the set-up and claims two staffers named Chrissy and Angel were responsible for setting up the tent.
