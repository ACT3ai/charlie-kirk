## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Crowd Shot From the Side: Twenty Seconds Ending at the Sound”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A 20-second vertical phone capture filmed from an elevated position on the side of the Utah Valley University plaza on September 10, 2025, panning across a crowd of several thousand toward the AMERICAN COMEBACK event tent. The clip runs up to the sound and ends abruptly on a bystander reaction. Surfaced publicly on 2026-08-10.
