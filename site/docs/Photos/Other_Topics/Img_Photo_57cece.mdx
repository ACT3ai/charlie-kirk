## What This Image Shows

This is a still frame taken from a video in the investigation's video archive. A short podcast segment in which Frank Turek, speaking to a host he addresses as "Jack", describes a scheduled Zoom meeting Charlie Kirk convened on September 9, 2025 with two officials in Israel and one person Turek calls a Jewish Defender of Israel in the United States. Turek says he was physically in the room along with Blake Neff, with Mikey McCoy in and out, that the meeting ran 50 minutes, and that Kirk was asking the participants how he could better defend Israel publicly. He concludes that the idea Charlie was giving up on Israel is false.
