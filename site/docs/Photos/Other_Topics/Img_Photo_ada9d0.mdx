## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “9 Pellet Gun”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: This video is a split-screen citizen-journalism clip documenting a police arrest on a public city sidewalk in Provo, Utah. The left side of the frame shows a wide-angle view of multiple police officers searching a vehicle and securing a suspect. The right side features a continuous close-up of a handcuffed male suspect standing beside a police cruiser. The overall purpose is to capture and share a real-time law enforcement incident.
