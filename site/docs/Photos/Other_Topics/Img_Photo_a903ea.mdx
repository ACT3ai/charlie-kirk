## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Darth Powell - If you think Charlie Kirk was shot with a...”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A high-speed ballistic demonstration video showcasing the physical impact of a bullet on a human head model. The clip features two angles of the impact: a normal-speed shot from behind the model, and an extreme slow-motion shot from a side profile. The purpose of the video is ballistic testing, capturing the violent expansion and tearing of the gelatin head.
