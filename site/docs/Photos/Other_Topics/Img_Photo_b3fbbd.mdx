## What This Image Shows

This is a two-panel enlargement of low-resolution surveillance video, heavily blurred and pixelated, watermarked “CANDACE” at the lower right of the upper panel. The upper panel shows an oblique overhead view along a building roofline: a pale gravel roof fills the lower right, a dark parapet line runs diagonally across the frame, and beyond it are trees, parked cars and a teal-green field. A small dark form is visible on the pale surface near the centre. The lower panel is a much closer crop of that same area, so blurred that shapes are only approximate. Two annotations have been added: a thick red arrow at the right labelled “Roof edge” in bold white text, pointing left along the parapet line, and a cyan line at the centre labelled “Object is behind roof edge”, pointing to the small dark form. The annotations assert the spatial relationship between the object and the roof edge; the underlying footage is too degraded to resolve detail.
