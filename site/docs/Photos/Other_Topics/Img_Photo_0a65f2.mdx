## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Elevated View Across the UVU Plaza, 11:24 a.m.”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A 37-second handheld clip filmed from an elevated position looking down across the outdoor plaza at Utah Valley University, showing the packed crowd around the reflecting pool, the TPUSA event tent and American flags, while the crowd chants USA. The account that published it says the footage was taken at 11:24 a.m. on 10 September 2025 and was originally posted to Instagram the following day.
