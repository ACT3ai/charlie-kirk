## What This Image Shows

This is a two-panel composite of press photographs from the aftermath at the campus event. The upper panel, shot through a dark vertical post, shows uniformed officers working a cordon: two stand at the left near equipment cases, and one in the centre foreground walks toward the camera unspooling yellow crime-scene tape across a paved path. Behind them are metal barriers draped in “AMERICAN COMEBACK” banners, a stage backdrop reading “PROVE ME WRONG” with sponsor logos, a police vehicle with lights on, camera crews and scattered litter on the grass. A red circle has been drawn at the lower right around a small pink evidence flag and a green marker planted at the edge of the lawn. The lower panel is a wide view from behind a metal railing over the campus lawn and plaza, where a large crowd is dispersing — many sitting on a terrace at the left near a white canopy, others walking or running across the grass and paving beneath a “A PLACE FOR YOU” panel and a UVU banner. A red circle is drawn around a small area of paving in the middle distance, and a long black arrow points down and right toward a running figure in a dark cap, maroon shirt and dark shorts at the lower right. The annotations link a marked point on the plaza to that figure.
