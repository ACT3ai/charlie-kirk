## What This Image Shows

This is an annotated enlargement of blurred surveillance video, watermarked “CANDACE” twice at the lower right and overlaid with the handles “@BasedSamParker @SamParkerSenate”. The footage shows an oblique overhead view along a pale roof surface with a dark parapet, trees, parked vehicles and a teal field beyond; a small indistinct figure stands near the centre left. Four coloured straight lines — green, red, yellow and cyan — have been drawn across the frame at differing angles as measurement or sight guides. A black caption box at the centre right reads, in cyan text, “10-11 running steps between points”. A grey band beneath the image carries bold white text reading “Enters frame 1-3 steps past the alleged screwdriver drop point”. The annotations are the poster's own step-count and positional assertions, and the word “alleged” is theirs.
