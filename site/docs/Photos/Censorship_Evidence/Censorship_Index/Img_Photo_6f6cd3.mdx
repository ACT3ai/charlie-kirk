## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Ryne Simmons”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A TikTok vlog featuring a man explaining his experience recording the July 13, 2024, Donald Trump rally shooting in Butler, Pennsylvania. The speaker details how he submitted his footage to the FBI, only to be advised by officials to delete the file. The clip features raw, slow-motion footage of the rally stands during the shooting, interspersed with the speaker's commentary on digital censorship.
