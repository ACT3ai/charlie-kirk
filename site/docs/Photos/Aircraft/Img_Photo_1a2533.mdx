## What This Image Shows

A screenshot of an X post circulating an article from **The War Zone** (TWZ) in the days after the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) shooting. What matters in the capture is the embedded article card, not the poster's commentary.

The article is headlined "Army Wants To Test Air-Launched Drones From A Global Express Business Jet," filed under AIR / AIRBORNE SENSORS, with the standfirst "The planned trials tally with previous comments about launching the drones from the new Global 6500-based HADES surveillance jet." That is a defense-trade report about a US Army program, published independently of this case: the [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) aircraft is a Global 6500 airframe, the same model as [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview).

Note carefully what the article does and does not say. It reports **planned trials of a capability**. It does not say any such launch occurred, over Utah or anywhere else, and nothing on this site should be read as claiming it did.

The value of the screenshot is that it preserves the article and its URL while both were live. That URL later stopped resolving, which is the subject of the [Cover Up](/Photos/Aircraft/Cover_Up/overview) sub-cluster and its [Versions](/Photos/Aircraft/Cover_Up/Cover_Versions/overview) slides — a capture taken before a page disappears is the only thing that can show what the page said.

Read [N1098L Spy Plane](/Planes/N1098L/overview) for the aircraft's documented movements and [Cover Up](/Photos/Aircraft/Cover_Up/overview) for the disappearance of the source page.
