## What This Image Shows

A composite graphic. The upper left carries The War Zone headline "Army Wants To Test Air-Launched Drones From A Global Express Business Jet" with the standfirst about trials tallying "with previous comments about launching the drones from the" [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) jet, bylined **THOMAS NEWDICK**. The rest of the frame is a large photograph of a military-modified Bombardier Global Express in flight against an overcast sky, with a red circle drawn around a fairing on the underside of the rear fuselage.

The circle is the argument. Whoever made the graphic is pointing at a ventral modification and asking whether that is where drones would exit. The filename this image was filed under reads it as a drone exit hatch, which is the poster's interpretation rather than an identification from any published diagram.

What can be said carefully: aircraft in this program carry belly-mounted sensor fairings for the [surveillance mission](/Planes/ISR-Operations), and a fairing visible in a photograph does not establish a launch tube. **[@tjphager](/Influencers/x/tjphager)** has argued specifically that the airframe was never built out to launch drones, which is the countervailing view worth holding while looking at this image. The plane very likely did not launch drones. It very likely may have been a training plane, not built out to be able to launch drones. It may have been there to waste the time of citizen investigators. If the people who planned for it to be there knew the crime would happen, that may be a path to people who had foreknowledge. We do not claim that is true — we claim it is very important to investigate and determine whether it is true.
