## About This Cluster

Nobody has produced a verified crew list for [**N1098L**](/Planes/N1098L/overview) on **September 10, 2025**. Flight-tracking services publish route, altitude and timing but withhold personnel, and the operator, **LASAI Aviation II LLC**, holds Department of Defense work that keeps such details out of public reach. Name lists that circulated online to fill that gap carry no documentation, and this site does not repeat them: the crew of N1098L is not public, and nothing here identifies anyone who was aboard.

The images in this cluster are **Google Trends** screenshots that researchers ran against those circulating names. Google Trends normalizes to a relative index, so a spike from near-zero volume can represent a very small number of searches, common names attract unrelated traffic, and none of this establishes that any person was aboard the aircraft or did anything at all.

The aircraft analysis is on [N1098L Spy Plane](/Planes/N1098L/overview), with the operator and airframe documents in [Nwq](/Photos/Aircraft/Nwq/overview). For what the flight data itself shows, see [Sept 10 Flight Timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline) and [ISR Operations](/Planes/ISR-Operations). The honest summary is that the crew of N1098L remains unidentified, and a FOIA request to the FAA is the route by which that could change.
