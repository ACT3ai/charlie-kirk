## What This Image Shows

This is the poster frame — the single still image used as the thumbnail — for the video “Project Constitution - 🚨ALERT： BREAKING Down The MYSTERY FLIGHT BEFORE CHARLIE KIRK...”. It is one representative frame lifted from that video, not a separate photograph. The video it stands for is described as follows: A compilation video presenting evidence of an alleged aerial surveillance flight over Utah Valley University during an event featuring Charlie Kirk. The video begins with informational slides showing the website, flight paths, and sensor capabilities of a private contractor, MARC, Inc. The second half consists of high-altitude aerial surveillance footage of the campus, focusing on a large outdoor crowd gathered in an amphitheater.
