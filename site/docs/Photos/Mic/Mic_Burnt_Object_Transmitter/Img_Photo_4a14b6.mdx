## What This Image Shows

An annotated image labelled Symbol from label on modified battery from transmitter. A battery-pack label is shown at left with legible text including MK1SL, 1200mAh, 4Wh, a Sino-brand logo, and a row of standard warning and compliance symbols. A red line connects one circular symbol on that label to a circled small upright fragment within the burnt mass.

The claim is that a fragment sticking up from the charred material carries visible writing and a circular symbol matching the warning glyph from a modified battery pack used inside the transmitter. A clearer original at known scale would let the glyph be matched to the label directly; the circulated image shows a small upright fragment circled in the heat-damaged mass.

This is one of four images published by [@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution) on X on July 16, 2026, arguing that the burnt object left in the back of the SUV is not only melted shirt fabric and tissue but contains parts of the RODE wireless transmitter. The host page sets out the identification in detail, including why a clearer original photograph — rather than a phone screenshot of a video frame — would sharpen the component match. Independent of that detail, investigators ask why there is charred material in the vehicle at all, when a rifle round does not typically char tissue or fabric.

The claim is preserved as the poster made it, with attribution. No laboratory result on this object has been made public, and the SUV itself was reportedly disposed of.
