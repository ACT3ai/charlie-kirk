## About This Cluster

This sub-cluster documents the "pocket object" question: pre-event photos of a man photographed with **[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** before the event, whom researchers identify as **[Butch Hibbs](/People/butch-hibbs)**. That identification is theirs and is unconfirmed. The object in his front shirt pocket looks about the size of a RODE wireless mic receiver. We do not claim that it is one. We do not claim that he knew anything ahead of time, and we do not claim that he was involved in any way. We say only that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated. Carrying a common microphone accessory establishes nothing, and no wrongdoing by any person shown here has been alleged in court or established anywhere.

The two images are the visual record. One is a close-up of a black RØDE Wireless GO II receiver clipped to the breast pocket of a plaid shirt, its "RØDE WIRELESS GO II" label legible. The other shows a smiling white-bearded man in a plaid shirt and white cap standing beside a man in the white "FREEDOM" t-shirt — the shirt Kirk wore on stage that day. Researchers use the pairing to compare the size of the pocket object with the receiver; the original post making the comparison has reportedly been taken down.

For what the transmitter looked like on Kirk himself, see the [RØDE Wireless PRO page](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) and the parent cluster [The Microphone](/Photos/Mic/overview).
