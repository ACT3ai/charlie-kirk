## What This Image Shows

A product photograph of a RODE Wireless GO II wireless microphone transmitter resting in an open human hand, shown for scale — the matchbox-sized black body pack is roughly the width of three fingers, with the RODE wordmark and WIRELESS GO II printed in white on its face and a USB-C port on its lower edge. A black RODE lavalier microphone on a short coiled cable is plugged into the pack's 3.5mm input and lies across the palm to the right. The lower-right corner carries the caption 'shown with optional equipment', indicating the lavalier is an add-on to the base kit rather than the standard configuration. The image circulated on X as a visual reference for the class of wireless mic Charlie Kirk was wearing at Utah Valley University, accompanying a public request that Turning Point USA produce the actual device and its original audio recording.
