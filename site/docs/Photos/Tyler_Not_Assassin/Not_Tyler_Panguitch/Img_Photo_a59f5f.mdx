## What This Image Shows

A tall, narrow photograph of a printed restaurant customer receipt from Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah, posted by citizen journalist @DiligentDenizen on August 14, 2026 with the caption that it is the Tyler Robinson Panguitch receipt per the restaurant's servers. The ticket is the one circulated as covering the evening of September 10, 2025, the day Charlie Kirk was shot at Utah Valley University roughly 209 road miles to the north, and its printed order time of 8:55 PM and card checkout time of 9:47 PM are the fields citizen investigators use to argue the state's night-of text-message timeline is physically impossible. The card was run as a debit transaction so no customer name is printed, and the receipt has not been authenticated by any court.
