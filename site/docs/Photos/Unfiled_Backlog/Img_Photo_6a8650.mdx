Screen capture of page 2 of a six-page discovery exhibit index in State of Utah v. Tyler Robinson, viewed in a PDF reader at 175 percent. Row 15 reads 'Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025' with Bates number 3996-R1 and disclosure date 11/25/2025; the row is highlighted in blue and the Bates cell is outlined in red. Surrounding rows list item 13 doorbell camera surveillance from 9/10/2025, item 14 a written statement of Mike Mitchell, item 16 the recorded 1102 statement of Lance Twiggs, 16.1 text messages between TR and LT, 16.2 Discord chats, 16.3 a photograph of a note, 16.4 FBI press release photographs, and items 19 and 20 photos of rounds from the rifle.
