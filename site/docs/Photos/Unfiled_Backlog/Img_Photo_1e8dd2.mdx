## What This Image Shows

A photograph of a digital screen or monitor, identifiable as such by the faint horizontal scanning lines and an interface icon in the upper right. The screen shows a man in a white shirt and white baseball cap holding up a smartphone, standing behind a black PA speaker on a stand, flanked by security personnel and a videographer with a camera rig.

There is no readable on-screen text in the frame.

The recurring element worth noting is the PA speaker directly in front of the speaking position, which appears in frame after frame across this archive. Speaker placement matters to the acoustic analysis, since a loudspeaker between a microphone and a crowd affects both what was recorded and what witnesses heard.
