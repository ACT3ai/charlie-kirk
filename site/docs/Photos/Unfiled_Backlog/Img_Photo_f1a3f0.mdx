## What This Image Shows

An iPhone screenshot of an iMessage conversation with Ana Escobar. Three grey message bubbles read "Stew they wiped the Burton Hibbs search", "It happened while I was live on air" and "And I tried both with caps and without". Between the first and second is an embedded video frame showing a Google Trends Explore page for the search term "Burton Hibbs Calvalry church", region Tucson (Sierra Vista) AZ, date range 1/1/25 to 9/9/25, Image Search, in which the Interest over time panel has been replaced by a warning triangle and the message that the search does not have enough data to show. The broadcaster's own camera feed is visible in the lower left of that frame, watermarked @AnaEscobarShow.

What changed is the Trends result, not anything Butch Hibbs did. This site does not claim that he knew anything ahead of time or that he was involved in any way.
