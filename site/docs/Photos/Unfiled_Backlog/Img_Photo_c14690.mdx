## What This Image Shows

This is a two-panel before-and-after comparison graphic on a black background, built from Internet Archive Wayback Machine captures of the website unfuckamericatour.com. The left panel is headed “SEPTEMBER 9, 2025” with the time 21:41:25 in blue and shows the archived homepage of “THE UNF*CK AMERICA TOUR”: a red and navy logo, navigation links for ABOUT, TOUR, CAMPUS CHAPTERS and SHOP with a red DONATE button, a headline reading “TAKING THE FIGHT TO CHARLIE KIRK &amp; THE FAR RIGHT”, body copy about building a movement of students, a crowd photograph, and a sign-up form with Full Name, Email and Phone fields and a SUBMIT button. The right panel is headed “SEPTEMBER 10, 2025” with the time 19:55:06 in red and shows the same URL returning a plain “404: Not Found” page reading “The page you're looking for couldn't be found.” with a “Go back home.” link. Beneath each panel is a checklist: green ticks on the left for the site loading normally with full content, branding, form and navigation; red crosses on the right for a 404 error, no homepage content, inaccessible navigation and the site appearing down or removed. A caption bar at the foot reads: “Between September 9 at 21:41 and September 10 at 19:55, the website went from fully operational to returning a 404 error.” Both archive toolbars show “120 captures, 5 Apr 2025 – 25 Mar 2026”.
