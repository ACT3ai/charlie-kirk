## What This Image Shows

A screenshot of an Arizona corporate registry entry, headed COMPANY INFORMATION in a blue bar, with several fields picked out in yellow highlight.

The record reads: "Company Name: FIREBIRD ALARM SYSTEMS INC. Entity Type: ARIZONA DOMESTIC FOR-PROFIT (BUSINESS) CORPORATION. File Number: 08806415. Filing State: Arizona (AZ). Filing Status: Active. Filing Date: July 2, 1999. Company Age: 26 Years, 6 Months. Registered Agent: Duane M Burt, 943 E Lockwood St, Mesa, AZ 85203. Mailing Address: 943 E Lockwood St, Mesa, AZ 85203. Report Due Date: July 2, 2016. Products/Services: Construction Electrical Contractors and Other Wiring Installation Contractors. Business Category: Electricians. Governing Agency: Arizona Corporation Commission."

This is the companion filing to the Wyoming record held elsewhere in this cluster, and the two together are how corporate tracing actually works: same name family, different states, different statuses, and an overdue annual report as a marker of when a filer stopped maintaining an entity. What either company connects to is not established on this page. See [Companies and Organizations](/Companies_Organizations/overview).
