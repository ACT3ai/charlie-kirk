## What This Image Shows

This is a clean medical-illustration diagram titled “Visual Representation of Reported Injuries” with the italic subtitle “Illustrative anatomical locations only — no assumed cause.” Six anatomical renderings are arranged over a white background, each with numbered call-outs and red/orange shading marking reported injury sites. “FRONT (ANTERIOR) TORSO” and “BACK (POSTERIOR) TORSO” appear at the top: the front labels 2 pulmonary apical hemorrhages/hematomas, 7 left and right hemothorax and 6 hemopericardium; the back labels 1 bilateral apical and posterior intercostal hemorrhages. A boxed “NECK (CLOSE-UP ANTERIOR VIEW)” in the middle shows the larynx and trachea with call-outs 3 multiple disruptions of the thyroid cartilage, 4 multiple disruptions of the cricoid cartilage and 5 multiple disruptions of the tracheal cartilages. Below, “CHEST (INTERNAL ANTERIOR VIEW)” repeats call-outs 2, 7 and 6 on an internal view of lungs, heart and pericardium, and “BRAIN (VIEWS)” shows an inferior/posterior view labelled 8 subarachnoid hemorrhaging around the cerebellar vermis and a superior/top view labelled 9 subarachnoid hemorrhaging around the parietal regions. A key at the foot states “Red/orange shaded areas = reported injury locations”.
