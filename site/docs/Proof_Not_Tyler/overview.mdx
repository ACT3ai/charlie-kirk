This section compiles **reported reasons** that story may fail — ballistics, wounds, audio, cameras,
and custody timing. Start with the [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index). **Robinson is charged,
not convicted.**

:::tip The single most checkable item in this whole section
Every other gap on this page needs an expert — a ballistician, an acoustics analyst, a pathologist.
**Panguitch needs a calculator.** Staff at Cowboy's Smokehouse in Panguitch, Utah — **209 road miles south
of Orem** — say they served [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) dinner from **8:55 to 9:47 PM on
September 10, 2025**, which would put him three-plus hours away from the Orem-area texts that the state's
night-of case is built on, and would require **98–99 mph the entire way** from the 6:38 PM Orem
[Dairy Queen](/Locations/Dairy_Queen_Orem) sighting to get there. That is why it is the most interesting
thread here: a **printed receipt, a debit card with a cardholder of record, and simple drive-time
arithmetic** could settle it in an afternoon — and by every account given on camera, no official body has
publicly tried. The identification is **claimed by restaurant staff and contested**; Robinson is
**charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent**.
**[Panguitch — The Town That Breaks the Timeline →](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/panguitch-breaks-the-timeline)**
:::

**Proof Not Tyler** is not a verdict — it is a map of **gaps in the official kill chain**. The state's
own **September 17, 2025 ATF report** reportedly **cannot match** the autopsy bullet jacket fragment
to Robinson's alleged Mauser ([ATF page](./ATF_Inconclusive_Ballistics)). The same examiner testified at the
July 2026 hearing that the seized barrel held **dust and debris** and that the fragment measured **.286–.301
inches** ([barrel dust & diameter](/Gun_Bullet/Barrel_Dust_And_Diameter)). Independent analysts argue that
**trajectory** from the east rooftop does not align with reported **neck entry** ([trajectory](./Trajectory_Wound_Mismatch)).
**Canon XA55** audio is argued to separate a **tent/stage event** from a **distant rifle** ([acoustic split](./Acoustic_Two_Event_Split)).
Campus video is cited to raise the question **Stairs Guy ≠ Tyler** ([identity gap](./Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap)).

The institutional layer matters too: the rifle was reportedly found only after **failed searches** and
**federal direction** ([gun discovery](./Gun_Discovery_Sequence)); **Discord posts** are said to timestamp **after**
**Miranda custody** ([timeline](./Discord_Custody_Timeline)); witnesses describe **pops not .30-06**
([witness conflicts](./Witness_Audio_Conflicts)). If the rooftop rifle did not kill Kirk, [alternative
mechanisms](./Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms) route to tent, mic, and intel threads elsewhere on the site.
The custody-equals-case-closed logic also reached the intelligence community: former NCTC director
[Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said on the record that his center's parallel review of possible
foreign ties was ordered to stop once Robinson was in custody — before those leads were exhausted.

Prioritize [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index) → [ATF](./ATF_Inconclusive_Ballistics) +

## The Mirandize Timeline — Why It's Pivotal

If Tyler Robinson was mirandized and in custody at **6:25 PM on September 11** with his phone
seized, then he physically could not have typed the Discord "confession" posted around **7:57 PM**.
That single timing gap is one of the strongest exculpatory arguments in the entire case, because it
attacks the **authorship** of the confession, not merely its meaning. Read the
[Mirandize evidence hub](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview) and the
[custody timeline vs. the Discord confession](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession).

arguments — that a full-length **.30-06 bolt-action rifle** could not

slim-fit jeans and walk on camera, with the narration: *"we're going to prove
to these people that a 30-06 easily fits inside slim-fitting jeans... see, we
told you."* It is comedy, not a controlled test — no timing, clothing, or
gait comparison to the campus footage is offered — but it is recorded here
