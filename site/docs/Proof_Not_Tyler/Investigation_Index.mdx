# Proof Not Tyler — Investigation Index

The official narrative says [**Tyler Robinson**](/People/tyler-robinson) fired a **.30-06** from the [**Losee Center rooftop**](/UVU/Losee_Center)
and killed [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). This section compiles **reported challenges** to that story — forensic,
physical, witness, and timeline — without asserting Robinson's innocence as a legal conclusion.
**Robinson is charged, not convicted.**
