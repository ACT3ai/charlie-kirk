# Witness & Audio Conflicts

If [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) fired a **.30-06** from a rooftop **~120 m** away, witnesses near the tent
should predominantly report a **heavy rifle crack**. Citizen compilations instead highlight
**pistol/fireworks-class** sound descriptions, **false all-clear alerts**, and **eyewitness photo
mismatches** — all **reported conflicts**, not proof of another shooter.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime, did anything
wrong or illegal, or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. Names appear only in the
context of public discussion, and nothing here is a finding by this website.

## Witness Nick — not a large-calibre sound

[@MuppetMasher](https://x.com/MuppetMasher/status/2053805778952417528) interviewed [**Nick**](/Witnesses/Nick), a UVU
witness who says it is **hard to find anyone** describing a **large-calibre rifle**:

- Many heard **pops**, **fireworks**, **smaller calibre** or **pistol** sounds
- **None** in Nick's experience said **.30-06**
- Nick's **trajectory** description is flagged as **interesting** in the thread

Video preserved on IPFS: `QmNjgg3Trjfiq4YEK4XjDur9YyJF1xk8nhAkeCkXMoo41Y`.

## False "suspect in custody" alert

UVU GRAMA threads ([@danksterintel](https://x.com/danksterintel/status/2044300845609824305)) document
a campus alert **19 minutes** after the shooting stating **suspect in custody** when the suspect
**was not** — leaving **~10,000 students** on campus believing it was safe. Wrong early custody
claims undermine trust in same institutions' Tyler narrative.

## Construction crew vs FBI photo

Public commentary quotes **Mr. Hope**: officers showed construction workers a photo that
**did not** resemble the person they encountered post-shooting — while narrative converged on Robinson.
See [Gun Discovery](./Gun_Discovery_Sequence).

## Black-clothing descriptions

Witness descriptions characterize the shooter's appearance as **black sunglasses, C-mask, trench coat, cargo
pants, small backpack** — not maroon-shirt Tyler packaging. Overlaps [Stairs Guy Gap](./Stairs_Guy_Identity_Gap)
and Black Clothing Suspect.

## Zachariah Qureshi — announced then discarded?

According to public reporting, [**Zachariah Qureshi**](/distraction_people/Zachariah_Qureshi) was within **~25 feet** of Kirk; [**Kash Patel**](/FBI/Kash_Patel_Briefings) reportedly rushed to
announce him as shooter. Qureshi was questioned and released (with a later obstruction case noted).
Commentators ask if he was a **distraction patsy** before Robinson's selection. There is **no public finding**
that Qureshi shot anyone.

## Deleted eyewitness media

Witness accounts allege **cell footage deleted by the time people got home** — if true,
independent audio/video corroboration of .30-06 vs pops is **lost**. See [Censorship](/Media/Censorship).

## Professional audio split

[Acoustic Two-Event Split](./Acoustic_Two_Event_Split) — Canon XA55 separates tent event from distant
rifle — complements ear-witness "not .30-06" accounts.

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

*(Challenges to the lone-rooftop /.30-06 attribution — Robinson is charged and presumed innocent until proven guilty.)*

- July 2026 preliminary-hearing coverage on X was **split**: some accounts called the state’s DNA/video package “ironclad”; others (and defense reporting) stressed **ATF inconclusive** ballistics, DNA-method challenges, and “little mysteries” on scene gaps.
- Long-running citizen threads still highlight **acoustic two-event** claims, **backpack/rifle** concealment doubts, **stairs-guy** clothing mismatch, **Miranda vs Discord** timing, and **GSR/physical-test** gaps — see sibling pages under [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview).
- Page focus: **Witness & Audio Conflicts**. Competing official and citizen narratives both deserve documentation; neither substitutes for a completed trial record.
