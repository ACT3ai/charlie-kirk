# Foreign Weapons & Hardware

If intelligence services participated at UVU, some theorists expect **non-retail weapons** —
**CornerShot** mounts, **ARCAS**-class systems, **shaped charges**, or **UAS** — rather than a
grandfather's **Mauser .30-06**. This page routes **foreign-hardware claims**. **No public weapons
exam** confirms exotic gear at the scene.

## Israeli CornerShot / tent thread

[@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/1973304018572071043) (amplified by **[Steven DeNoon](/People/steven-denoon)**
— self-described ex-CIA analyst):

- **CornerShot** hinge system firing **9mm** around barriers inside **tent**
- **Shell casing** rolling down tarp; **IR laser** on **Dr. [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek)** phone screen (frame claim)
- **Dr. [Chris Martenson](/People/chris-martenson)** cavitation analysis — energy dump inconsistent with distant .30-06

Detail: [Tent — Shooter Theories](/Tent/Tent_Shooter_Theories), [Proof Not Tyler — Alternatives](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms).

## UAS / HADES linkage

The **[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)** low passes near UVU are in the **public ADS-B record** — an Army ISR
airframe over the campus before and after the shot. In the same week, **Counter-UAS** Egyptian contractors were
reportedly working at [Provo Airport](./Provo_Airport_Counter_UAS), ten minutes away. Together those put
**military and counter-drone activity in the same airspace** as the hit window. What the jet was tasked with, and
whether anything small was flying under it, remains unanswered — see
[N1098L and the Drone Question Over UVU](/Drones/N1098L_Air_Launched_Drones).
