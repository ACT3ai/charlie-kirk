# Proof Intel Services — Investigation Index

**Proof Intel Services** collects **indicators** — not verdicts — that U.S. or foreign intelligence
apparatus may have had **foreknowledge, presence, or cover role** around UVU on September 10, 2025,
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s death, or the [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) frame. Every item is **allegation or pattern analysis**
until records release.
