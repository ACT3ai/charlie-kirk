# N1098L HADES Over UVU


## Best understanding of when this happened

Best understanding: flight-tracking discussions focus on **SEPT 10 daytime** relative to the UVU window (not a multi-month axis).

Public **ADS-B** and citizen OSINT place a **U.S. Army [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES)** ISR jet — tail **N1098L**, callsign
**AXEL10** — making **extremely low, slow passes** near **UVU/Orem** on **September 10, 2025**,
including **before** and **after** the ~12:23 PM shooting. CK_FILE summarizes: *"It was ours. From
either the Pentagon, or Langley."* Full flight dossier: [Planes — N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview).

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime, did anything wrong or illegal, or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. Names appear only in the context of public discussion. Nothing here is a finding by this website.

## Documented pass pattern (citizen tracking)

| Window | Altitude / speed (reported) | Timing vs shot |
|--------|----------------------------|----------------|
| **Morning pass** | ~**600–650 ft** AGL, ~**118 knots** | ~**3 hours before** shooting (~9:16 AM MT cited) |
| **Afternoon pass** | ~**204–335 ft** AGL, ~**129 knots** | ~**20–24 min after** shot (~12:48 PM MT cited) |

Second pass described as over **Lake Utah** ~**2 miles** from UVU — unusually low for a Global 6500
in civilian airspace near a major campus event. The tighter published sequence runs: low pass **~9:16 AM MT**,
assassination **12:23 PM MT**, second low pass **~12:48 PM MT** — the afternoon pass again dropping to
**~203 ft** and slowing to **stall speed (~116 knots)** roughly **1 mile** from campus. These are altitude,
speed and clock figures off the flight data. They say where the aircraft was. They do not say what it was doing.

## Aircraft identity and operator

Per CK_FILE and ADS-B Exchange / Flightradar24 records:

- **Tail:** N1098L — **formerly N911PV** — **ICAO A0299E**, callsign **AXEL10**
- **Airframe:** Bombardier **Global 6500**, the platform the Army wants to test **air-launched drones** from
- **Operator:** **LASAI Aviation II LLC**, an ISR defense contractor (FAA address **10660 Aviation Lane, Manassas, VA 20110**); control **fell under Leidos in 2021**
- **Departure Sept 10:** **Biggs Army Airfield (KBIF, El Paso, TX)** ~**07:48 MDT** → Glacier Park Intl (KGPI, Kalispell, MT), with the Orem loops en route
- **HADES base:** the program is run out of **Biggs AFB** — where personnel reportedly went **after [Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview)** (see [Fort Huachuca](./Fort_Huachuca_Sept_9))
- **Current location:** as of ~**Oct 6, 2025**, N1098L was on the ground at **Corpus Christi Intl (CRP, TX)**

CK also notes the same LASAI/Leidos aircraft lineage was **found circling Washington, DC on and immediately
after Jan. 6, 2021** — cited by researchers as a pattern of ISR presence at politically charged events.

## What the airframe was and was not carrying

The obvious question about a HADES jet at two hundred feet is whether it was carrying anything small. The most
substantive answer on the record came from **[@TJPHager](/Influencers/x/tjphager)**, who examined the **ME-11B**
configuration and found **interior mission equipment only** — no external pylons, hardpoints, launch rails, or
recovery gear. That points away from this airframe as a drone platform, and it is stronger evidence than anything
offered on the other side.

Public defense-trade reporting does describe Army interest in **air-launched drones from Global Express–class
jets**, and small-UAS platforms in that weight class are real — see
[Switchblade & DARPA Claims](/Drones/Switchblade_DARPA_Claims). That establishes doctrine for the airframe
family, not for this flight. No recovered airframe, wreckage, radar return, or released record places any drone
under N1098L on September 10. The drone question is handled in full at
[N1098L and the Drone Question Over UVU](/Drones/N1098L_Air_Launched_Drones).

## Why investigators treat this as intel involvement

HADES (**High Accuracy Detection and Exploitation System**) is **military ISR** — signals, sensors,
and (in public debate) **drone coordination**. Presence at homicide window suggests:

- **Real-time surveillance** of the event (capability),
- **Sensor coverage** of the ground below during the homicide window (capability, unconfirmed use),
- **Fort Huachuca** / Army intelligence command relay (theory in X threads)

The Army has **not** published a mission explanation for Orem loops on Sept 10.

## Hansell and HADES command chain

CK ties **[Bradley D. Hansell](/People/bradley-hansell)** (Under Secretary of War for Intelligence) to **HADES** oversight and
the [Fort Huachuca Sept 9](./Fort_Huachuca_Sept_9) VIP flight — linking **cabinet-level DOD intel**
to the same week as N1098L behavior. Allegation only; not proof Hansell ordered UVU passes.

## LASAI Aviation / Ackman address rumor

CK notes **LASAI Aviation II LLC** operator and alleges shared **New York address** with **Bill Ackman's**
Pershing Square — used in commentary to question **private-capital adjacency** to a military ISR shell.
The corporate tie is **unverified**, and this website does not assert that Ackman operated the flight or had any involvement in it.

## Official vs routine training debate

Debunkers call passes **routine training**; proponents cite **timing, altitude, and dual passes**
on assassination day as beyond routine. Proof Intel Services holds **both** until mission logs release.
