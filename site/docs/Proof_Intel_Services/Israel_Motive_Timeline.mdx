# Israel Motive Timeline

**Proof Intel Services** includes **foreign-state motive** indicators — especially **Israel-aligned
pressure** on [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) in the **48–72 hours** before UVU. This is **context for intelligence theories**,
not proof any Israeli agency ordered the killing. See [Israel overview](/Israel/overview) for the
full Israel section.

## Late-summer shift (selected dates)

| Date | Event (reported) |
|------|------------------|
| **Aug ~2025** | **Hamptons "intervention"** — pro-Israel figures (Wolicki, Hammer, Dillon, Ackman, Hausdorff cited) |
| **Aug 4–6** | **Netanyahu** call; Kirk reportedly rejected **Israel trip** |
| **Sept 1–5** | Group chats: **"no choice but to leave pro Israel cause"** |
| **Sept 8** | Tweet about **leaving pro-Israel movement** |
| **Sept 9** | **[Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro)** show — Kirk criticizes Israel to Ben's face |
| **Sept 9** | Texts: **lost huge Jewish donor** over Tucker; **"stereotypes"** quote (CK item 23) |
| **Sept 10** | Assassination at UVU |

CK timeline item **23** frames donor break and **"48 hours later publicly executed"** — rhetorical
sequence, not legal causation.

## How motive links to intel services

Motive theories ask: **who had means + motive + cover capacity?** Israel-aligned **donor state**,
**Egyptian jets**, **NSA-visible phones**, and **halted NCTC foreign probe** are often stitched into
one narrative. Each link remains **independently disputed**.
