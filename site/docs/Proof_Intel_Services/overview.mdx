# Proof Intel Services

**Intelligence services** leave fingerprints — halted foreign inquiries, ISR aircraft, foreign phones,
VIP flights, and scene control. This section maps **indicators** that U.S. or foreign intel may connect
to UVU on **September 10, 2025**, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s death, or the **[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)** frame. Start with the
[Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index). Nothing here is adjudicated fact.

**foreign-nexus** inquiry and the **FBI halted** it while the administration reportedly feared defense
ammunition for Robinson. Parallel **signals** threads cite **Israeli and foreign handsets** at the courtyard
([Bumblehive](./Israeli_Phones_Bumblehive)) and **Army [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES)** jet **[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)** at **204–600 ft** over Orem
**before and after** the shot ([N1098L](./N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU)). **Foreign government** aircraft (**[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)**)
sat in **Provo Sept 4–10** with **Counter-UAS** contractors ([Egyptian jets](./Egyptian_Jets_Provo),
[Counter-UAS](./Provo_Airport_Counter_UAS)).

gun-discovery sequence, paving, video issues — while **[UVU Sept 9 warnings](./UVU_Sept_9_Foreknowledge)** suggest
institutional **foreknowledge**. **[Benny Johnson](./State_Actors_Professional_Hit)** publicly cites **"state actors"**
without naming them; **[Israel motive](./Israel_Motive_Timeline)** and **[foreign hardware](./Foreign_Weapons_And_Hardware)**
threads supply **why** and **how** intel services might operate. **[Fort Huachuca](./Fort_Huachuca_Sept_9)** ties
**cabinet DOD intel** to the **day before** the killing.

## The Mirandize Timeline — Why It's Pivotal

Investigators argue that if Robinson was already in custody by **6:25 PM** while BOLO photos, the
rooftop video, and the Discord confession were still being released to the public, the sequence
points to a coordinated, multi-agency effort to build a narrative around an already-detained
suspect. Attorney Baron Coleman frames the refused and allegedly deleted surrender video as the
piece that would make such a timeline "definitive." See the
[Mirandize evidence hub](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview) and
[Baron Coleman's analysis](/court/mirandize/baron-coleman-early-turn-in).
