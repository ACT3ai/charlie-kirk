## The Clip

A clip circulating on X — the streamer receiving the call goes by the handle **BeLo** — captures a phone call that reportedly came in just moments after [Charlie Kirk](/Charlie/overview) was shot at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. According to [@ChewingTheVoid](https://x.com/ChewingTheVoid/status/2084007515218493721), the caller was Zee Cohen, founder of the Unfuck America Tour, accidentally calling into a livestream connected to Destiny (streamer Steven Bonnell, a participant in the tour) — a call that "was never meant to be public."

The full post, near-verbatim:

> This clip has NEVER sat right with me.
>
> Just moments after Charlie Kirk's death, Zee Cohen—the founder of the Unfuck America Tour—accidentally calls into Destiny's livestream.
>
> The call was never meant to be public!
>
> "Dude, we're fucked. We are so fucked."
>
> Seconds later, they move the conversation to Discord and shortly afterward, the Unfuck America Tour website returns a 404/is wiped!
>
> How is almost nobody talking about this?

## Transcript of the Call

From the video clip (0:31, transcribed from the posted video):

> Wow, what's up? I'm streaming, by the way. This is, did you see what happened? Yeah, I think so. Yeah, like, did Charlie's going to be fucking dead? I'm pretty sure he's already dead. Yeah. Dude, we're fucked. We are we are so fucked. By we, I mean, we as a country, right now, the United States and the current. Like, yeah, like that, the party, but like mostly like, I think that the, I think that we're super fucked. I agree. Hold on, right. I'll text you on discord. I don't want to understand anything crazy. It's gonna ruin our shit.

Note that the caller clarifies on the call itself: "By we, I mean, we as a country" — before the conversation is moved off-stream to Discord with the closing line "It's gonna ruin our shit."

## The Website 404 the Same Day

The post includes a Wayback Machine comparison of unfuckamericatour.com. Per the archived captures shown in the image:

* **September 9, 2025** — the site loads normally: full homepage ("CHARLIE KIRK & THE FAR RIGHT — We're building a movement of students across America"), tour sign-up form, navigation, donate/shop links.
* **September 10, 2025 at 19:55:06** — the same URL returns "404: Not Found." Homepage content gone, navigation inaccessible, site down or removed.

That is the day of the assassination, roughly seven hours after the 12:23 PM shooting. The archive banner shows 120 captures spanning April 5, 2025 to March 25, 2026.

## What This Does and Does Not Show

The Unfuck America Tour was a publicly announced counter-programming effort that scheduled debate-style campus events in response to [TPUSA's](/TPUSA/overview) tour; Destiny was among its named influencer participants. Nothing in the clip or the archive captures is evidence that anyone connected to the tour had foreknowledge of, or any role in, the assassination — and no court or mainstream investigation has alleged that. Innocent readings are straightforward: shock and fear in the immediate aftermath ("we as a country"), and a website takedown as a safety measure by an organization whose entire brand referenced Charlie Kirk at the moment his killing set off a wave of threats and doxxing.

What citizen investigators find worth recording is the pattern, not an accusation: a private-in-intent call within minutes of the shooting, an immediate move to a non-public channel (Discord — the same platform central to the [Tyler Robinson](/Killer/overview) narrative), and the scrubbing of a Kirk-focused website within hours. Those are timing facts that a [trustworthy investigation](/Fix/overview) could resolve in either direction with subpoenaed records.
