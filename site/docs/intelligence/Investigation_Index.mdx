Charlie Kirk was killed at UVU on September 10, 2025, and none of the threads below have been tested against [the disputed cause-of-death record](/Cause_of_Death/overview). U.S. agency threads live under [U.S. Intelligence](/US_Intelligence/overview); Israel-specific motive pages under [Israel](/Israel/overview).
