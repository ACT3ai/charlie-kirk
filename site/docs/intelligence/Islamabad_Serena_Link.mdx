# Islamabad Serena Link (Foreign Intel)

In **April 2026**, **[Tony Seruga](/People/tony-seruga)** ([@TonySeruga](/Influencers/x/tonyseruga)) and amplified X threads claimed **four** of the **16 Israeli-registered phones** allegedly present at [UVU](./Foreign_Phones_UVU) on **September 10, 2025** were later traced to the **Islamabad Serena Hotel** in Pakistan — the venue for high-stakes **US–Iran** negotiations ("Islamabad Talks").

> "The accident narrative is dead: You don't have four high-level Israeli-linked devices at a murder scene and then at a secret diplomatic summit by coincidence." — paraphrase of viral X framing ([Charlie_Kirk.txt](https://github.com/))

## Diplomatic context (public reporting)

News accounts described a secured hotel with US delegation figures (including **[JD Vance](/People/jd-vance)**; **Jared Kushner** and **Steve Witkoff** named in negotiation orbit) and Iranian leadership (**Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf**, foreign ministry officials). Israeli strategic interest in **any US–Iran deal** is the **motive layer** commentators attach — not proof any phone owner was an assassin.

## Open questions

1. Were the four devices the **same SIM/hardware** as the UVU set, or registration-country coincidence?
2. Did U.S. IC **continue** tracing after [NCTC inquiry halt](/CoverUp/WhiteHouse_Halt_IC) reporting?
3. Can **Bumblehive** logs be produced under [Fix Law 2](/Fix/overview)?

We do **not** claim diplomats, negotiators, or phone subscribers committed crimes.

Question 2 is sharpened by former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent)'s on-record statement that the center's review of potential international ties was ordered to stop once a suspect was in custody — before device-trace leads like this one could be run to ground.

## Why the Serena Hotel claim persists

Citizen investigators keep the **Islamabad Serena** phone-trace story alive as a bridge between **UVU Israeli phones** and later **US–Iran diplomacy** travel patterns (April 2026 commentary windows). It is a **foreign-intel framing device**, not a completed forensic attribution. Require provider-grade location evidence before treating hotel co-location as operational meeting proof.
