# Foreign Phones at UVU (Sept 10, 2025)

The strongest **foreign-presence** data point in open-source threads is analyst **[Tony Seruga](/People/tony-seruga)**'s ([@TonySeruga](/Influencers/x/tonyseruga) on X) claim that **raw GPS / SIGINT** showed **44 foreign-registered cellphones** in the immediate area when Charlie Kirk was shot at [UVU](/UVU/overview) on **September 10, 2025**:

| National registration (claimed) | Count |
|--------------------------------|-------|
| Israeli | **16** (some posts say **12**) |
| Chinese | **13** |
| Russian | **12** |
| Iranian | **3** |

No NSA, FBI, or court filing **confirming subscriber identities** has been released publicly. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and podcast segments amplified an **NSA tip** narrative — see [Israel — NSA Cellphones](/Israel/NSA_Cellphones_Bumblehive) and [U.S. Intelligence — NSA](/US_Intelligence/nsa) for the domestic-collection angle.

## Bumblehive deanonymization (claims)

Seruga tied the UVU count to NSA's **Utah Data Center** in Bluffdale (nicknamed **Bumblehive**), alleging proprietary algorithms can link **burner phones** to prior devices after ~30–60 days ([Charlie_Kirk.txt](https://github.com/) Bumblehive notes). That would let investigators — if they chose — move from **registration country** to **real owners**. Whether that happened is an **open question** — and former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) says the center chartered to chase exactly this kind of foreign-nexus lead was ordered to stand down once Tyler Robinson was in custody.

## Benign explanations (acknowledged in threads)

Even skeptical commentators note:

- UVU hosts **national-security** and policy programs with international visitors
- **Tourism**, **dual citizens**, and **synagogue** traffic exist in the Provo/Orem corridor
- Foreign registration does **not** equal foreign-government operations

We do **not** claim any phone holder committed a crime or knew of the assassination in advance.

## Downstream linkage

Four of the **16 Israeli** devices allegedly reappeared at the **Islamabad Serena Hotel** during April 2026 US–Iran talks — [Islamabad Serena Link](./Islamabad_Serena_Link) · [Israel deep dive](/Israel/Islamabad_Serena_Tracking).

Breakdown of non-Israeli counts: [Chinese, Russian & Iranian Presence](./Chinese_Russian_Iranian_Presence).

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

Subscriber identities behind foreign registrations, NSA query logs, and CBP entry-exit manifests are among disclosures [Fix Law 2](/Fix/overview) targets.

## Counts circulating on X (11 / 12 / 16 Israeli and wider tallies)

- Posts citing **Candace Owens** mention **~11–16 Israeli-linked phones** at UVU on September 10 (counts vary by thread: @JosetteCaruso_ "12", [@Cayla_Patricia](/Influencers/x/cayla-patricia) "11", site research "16 Israeli" inside **Tony Seruga's 44 foreign** claim).
- Some July 2026 posts pair phone claims with statements that an **IDF-linked security team** was in contact with Charlie's detail while he was dying — **attributed hearsay**, not verified ops logs.
- Benign explanations and **deanonymization limits** remain mandatory on this page. Phone presence ≠ operational guilt.
