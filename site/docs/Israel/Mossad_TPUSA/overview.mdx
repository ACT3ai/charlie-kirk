# Mossad and TPUSA

<div style={{border:'1px solid #f0c36d', background:'#fcf8e3', borderRadius:'6px', padding:'1rem 1.25rem', marginBottom:'1.5rem'}}>

**Read this first — what this page is and is not.** This page documents an allegation made publicly by a former [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) (TPUSA) representative. It is presented as a **reported claim, not established fact**. We are not stating that the events described are true. **We do not claim that Joshua Peterson's testimony is correct or accurate — we ask only that it be properly investigated so the truth can come out.**

We are **not** claiming that Turning Point USA, its leadership, or any of its employees knew of, approved, or caused anything described here. We are **not** claiming that anyone at TPUSA was a foreign-intelligence officer, or that anyone at TPUSA was Mossad. No such thing has been proven, and nothing here should be read as a finding of guilt against any person or organization.

We publish it for one reason: to call for a **trustworthy investigation**. We want the proposed disclosure and investigation laws to be used so that U.S. intelligence services can determine whether any foreign-intelligence actor was interfacing with TPUSA personnel — and so the truth, whatever it turns out to be, can come out. See the [Fix Laws](../../Fix/overview).

</div>

This reporting comes from **Project Constitution** ([@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)), which published the video and relayed the on-camera account of former Turning Point USA representative **Joshua Peterson** (@RagingPatriot20). Peterson alleges that during a TPUSA training trip, former representatives took new hires out and that some of them later identified themselves as members of a foreign intelligence service who said they had been working at TPUSA for several years. These are his stated allegations and remain uncorroborated.

The following summarizes the account as published by **Project Constitution**, relaying statements attributed to former TPUSA representative **Joshua Peterson**. Every item below is a reported allegation by Peterson — not an established fact — and several reference living persons and an organization whose actions and statements should be independently verified.

> Former Turning Point USA Rep BLOWS the Whistle: STRIPPER Vans, Getting New Hires Drunk, and FOREIGN Intelligence Recruitment
>
> A former Turning Point USA representative named Joshua Peterson (@RagingPatriot20) came forward with a disturbing account from his time at TPUSA training. According to him, after the first few days of intense training, new hires were taken out by former representatives in rented stripper vans — complete with stripper poles inside — to bars in Arizona.
>
> Once there, the reps got the new hires drunk and began pulling people outside one by one. When it was Peterson's turn, they asked him about his views on "Our Greatest Ally" and "Our Greatest Allies Military." He says they then admitted they were part of "Our Greatest Allies" intelligence agency and revealed they had been working at Turning Point USA for the past four to five years.
>
> This is a serious allegation. If true, it suggests that foreign intelligence operatives were not only embedded inside Turning Point USA but were actively trying to recruit and vet new staff while engaging in highly unprofessional behavior with new hires.
>
> This needs to be investigated.
>
> — *Project Constitution, relaying the account of Joshua Peterson (@RagingPatriot20)*

The phrase **"Our Greatest Ally"** is a euphemism that, in this context, is widely understood by commentators to refer to Israel; the original account frames the alleged operatives as belonging to that ally's intelligence service. We note that framing as the source's interpretation, not as a proven fact.

This reporting comes from **Project Constitution** ([@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)), relaying the on-camera account of **Joshua Peterson** (@RagingPatriot20). The transcript below is Joshua Peterson's own words from the video. **We do not claim his testimony is true or accurate — we publish it only so that it can be properly investigated.**

> **Joshua Peterson — in his own words (video testimony, via Project Constitution):**
>
> "My name is Joshua Peterson. I'm a former representative for Turning Point USA and I have a story to tell about when I was out training at Turning Point.
>
> So the first couple nights went as you would expect. It's pretty long and strenuous work. We were being trained very vigorously and after the first few days we were told that we were going to have some group time and the first thing that I thought about that was that's going to be awesome. We get a group bond and stuff like that and I get a hangout with my leader.
>
> And however this was not the case. We were going to be going out with our former representatives that had shown up from previous years and had been at training in previous years. And they were going to be the ones that were going to show us around the city.
>
> So I get paired up with these two specific people who I cannot name currently. But they take us out. And the first thing that I found very odd about this Christian conservative company is they rented two stripper vans to take us out to the city. They were strippers but there were stripper poles inside the vans — very odd, very unlike their values. But okay.
>
> And then once we got down to the bars in Arizona, they started getting us drunk. The former reps were very drunk at that. And then they started singling out people and they would pull them out one by one to go outside and smoke like a cigarette.
>
> Now when it was finally my turn to go and talk to them, they approached me and they asked me, hey, what are your views on Israel? And I just told them, you know, I think Israel is a good country — at the time. I thought so at least.
>
> And they told me, okay, well, what do you think about, like, you know, Mossad and like Israeli forces? And I was like, they're all right. Like, yeah.
>
> And they were like, what if we told you we're part of it, like Israeli groups and forces? And I was like, oh, that's interesting. And I'm like, you work for Turning Point, right? And they were like, yeah, we do. We've been working for Turning Point for the past four or five years now."
