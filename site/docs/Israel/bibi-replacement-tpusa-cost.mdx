Two posts published a day apart in **July 2026** make the same underlying claim from opposite
directions: that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had specifically weighed calling for
**Benjamin Netanyahu** to step down, and specifically understood what that would cost him.
Neither is proof of anything about September 10, 2025. Together they are the sharpest version
yet of the **motive-layer** argument this section tracks — and they arrive with a piece of
contemporaneous-looking evidence attached that also **cuts against** the harder reading.

## The claim (Carrie Prejean Boller, July 25, 2026)

> "Seth Dillon and I spoke back stage at the religious Liberty commission hearing. We exchanged
> numbers and I followed up on text per our conversation… Seth admitted that Charlie told him if
> he were to say that Bibi should be replaced that TPUSA would be done. Charlie told Seth that
> saying certain things would come at a cost. Charlie knew the cost of criticizing Israel and
> Bibi. Charlie died a hero. He knew the risk of saying the things he was saying and he said it
> anyway. It eventually cost him his life."
>
> — [@CarriePrejean1](https://x.com/CarriePrejean1/status/2081030259257102348).
> 3,623 likes · 477 reposts · 261,804 views · 139 replies.

The venue she names is the **religious liberty commission hearing** — the same setting where
Prejean Boller has been reported as putting questions to Dillon about the [Bridgehampton
weekend](./israel-hamptons), the exchange in which Dillon's account of that weekend reportedly
shifted from "enjoyable summer retreat" to "heated and at times spirited debate."

## What the published screenshot actually contains

The image she attached is a text thread headed **"Seth"**:

> "He never said to me at least that he was bullied or threatened by anyone. just that saying
> certain things would come at a cost. He was right about that, but that doesn't make anyone
> that he was dealing with evil."

Set the two side by side and the gap is obvious:

| Claim | In the screenshot? |
|---|---|
| Charlie used "cost" language about saying certain things | **Yes** — verbatim |
| Charlie said calling for Bibi's replacement would end TPUSA | **No** — attributed to the backstage conversation only |
| Charlie said he was bullied or threatened | **No** — the text explicitly **denies** this |
| The people Charlie dealt with were acting in bad faith | **No** — the text explicitly **rejects** this ("doesn't make anyone… evil") |

The "TPUSA would be done" line is the load-bearing part of the claim and it is the part with
**no document behind it**. It is Prejean Boller reporting what Dillon reportedly said Charlie
said — two removes from the source, with the source dead. The message-level analysis of this
distinction lives at [Seth Dillon's Text — "A Cost"](/Messages/seth-dillon-text-exchange).

## The Bilzerian post it answers

Prejean Boller's post quote-tweets a July 24, 2026 post from
[@ElectBilzerian](https://x.com/ElectBilzerian/status/2080798822783918557) (471,907 views),
which itself quotes [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens):

> "In the hamptons on August 4th Charlie asked the Zionist influencers there a question: 'what
> would you say if I tweeted right now for Netanyahu to resign?'
>
> Imo He wasn't helping them come up with retorts... He was preparing to call for Netanyahus
> resignation.
>
> A month later he was assassinated."

The post is careful about its own status — "**Imo**" flags the second paragraph as
interpretation. The **question** at the Hamptons is presented as reported fact; the claim that
Charlie was **preparing** to call for Netanyahu's resignation is presented as the author's
inference from it. The final line places the two events in sequence without asserting causation,
which is exactly where the reader has to do the work.

## Why the two accounts reinforce each other — and where that stops

The reason these posts travelled is that they **converge without obviously being copies of each
other**. Bilzerian's account has Charlie *testing the question* in a room of pro-Israel
influencers in early August. Prejean Boller's has Dillon — one of the reported attendees of that
same weekend — saying Charlie had *priced the answer*: TPUSA would be done. If both are accurate,
Charlie both floated the move and knew what it would cost, roughly five weeks before he was
killed.

That is a coherent motive narrative. It is **not** an execution narrative. Nothing in either
post identifies a person who acted, a channel through which anything was arranged, or any link to
Utah on September 10, 2025. Financial and reputational cost — losing donors, losing an
organization — is the ordinary currency of political disagreement and is **not** the same thing
as a threat to someone's life. Dillon's own text says so explicitly.
