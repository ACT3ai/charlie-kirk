# People Investigation Index

This index routes to **individual profiles** for everyone discussed in the Charlie Kirk assassination investigation — September 10, 2025 at [UVU](/UVU/overview), the [Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview) capital case, motive threads around donors who wanted to suppress Charlie's Israel-policy shift, and citizen investigators who challenged the official narrative. Full topical depth lives in peer sections; this hub avoids duplicating [court](/court/overview), [Security Team](/Security_Team/overview), or [key_individuals](/key_individuals/overview) cluster pages.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime, did anything wrong or illegal, or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. Names appear only in the context of public discussion, reporting, and open questions. Nothing here is a finding by this website.
