# Andrea Burkhart

**Andrea Burkhart** is a **legal commentator** whose Substack has served as a **document repository** for primary source materials cited in coverage of the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) case — including legal filings and a summary described in commentary as an ATF-related report.

Her document archive is one of the more useful public windows into the state's [court case](/court/overview) against Tyler Robinson.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Burkhart's role here is as a **publisher and analyst of source documents**, not as a subject of the investigation. Several pages on this site reference documents hosted on her Substack (for example, the "Charlie Kirk legal documents" post) when discussing ballistics and forensics questions. Citing where a document is hosted does not imply any endorsement of, or responsibility for, the claims others draw from it.

## Background

Andrea Burkhart is publicly known as a defense attorney and legal commentator who publishes case analysis and hosts primary documents for readers to review directly.
