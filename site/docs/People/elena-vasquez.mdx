Elena Vasquez is a private individual whose name has surfaced in unverified online speculation surrounding the September 10, 2025 events at Utah Valley University. This site does **not** endorse, adopt, or detail those claims.

These profile threads circulated in the chaotic weeks after Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University; nothing in them is established, and the evidence the site does stand behind is under [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

The only basis for the name's appearance is uncorroborated online commentary — including claims that the name was listed on university-affiliated profiles that were later removed, and that it appears in search-term compilations circulated by anonymous researchers. Profile changes and name matches of this kind have ordinary, innocent explanations and prove nothing.

There is **no evidence**, and there are **no charges**, connecting this individual to the assassination or to any wrongdoing.
