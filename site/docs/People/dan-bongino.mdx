## Investigative Value

Dan Bongino is the Deputy Director of the FBI. In public commentary he appears in two ways: as one of the officials listed in attendance at the September 11, 2025 press conference/briefing, and as a named recipient of citizen appeals asking the FBI to seize and ground a private aircraft for forensic review. See the [FBI](/FBI/overview) and [Planes](/Planes/overview) sections.

Both mentions describe his public role and the demands citizens directed at the bureau — not any allegation against him personally. Nothing here asserts wrongdoing by Bongino.

## What is reported

- According to public commentary, Bongino appears on a list of officials in attendance at the September 11, 2025 Utah County press conference alongside other federal and local officials.
- Per commentary attributed to "MonkeyWerx," citizens called for "the FBI (Director Kash Patel and Dan Bongino) to seize the aircraft, interview the pilots/owner, and ground it for forensic review" in connection with a flagged private-jet flight (tail number [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview)). This is a citizen demand addressed to the FBI, not an allegation about Bongino's conduct.
- **Counterpoint:** Being named as the addressee of a public request, and attending an official briefing, are ordinary parts of a Deputy Director's role. No evidence in the file connects Bongino to the shooting or to any misconduct.

## Deputy FBI Director Role in Public Commentary

**Dan Bongino**, as **Deputy FBI Director**, appears in citizen lists of officials at post-arrest briefings alongside [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel) (as reported), Utah Governor Cox, LG [Deidre Henderson](/People/deidre-henderson), and others. MonkeyWerx-style aircraft threads **call on** Patel and Bongino to ground private aircraft for forensic review — **advocacy**, not proof of what the Bureau did.

Some posts note his **wife's past TPUSA connection** as conflict-of-interest **questions** — unproven as case-determinative. Institutional FBI critique is allowed; personal criminal guilt is not asserted.

Institutional questions about Bureau decision-making also include the account of former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent), who says his center's parallel review of potential foreign ties was ordered to stop once Tyler Robinson was in custody — before, he says, those leads were exhausted.

### Context of the Public Claims

> Commentary attributed to "MonkeyWerx" emphasizes the aircraft owner's local Utah ties as suspicious and calls for the FBI (Director Kash Patel and Dan Bongino) to seize the aircraft, interview the pilots/owner, and ground it for forensic review.

> Officials reported in attendance at the post-arrest briefing included Utah Governor Spencer Cox, Utah LG Henderson, FBI Director Kash Patel, Deputy FBI Director Dan Bongino, Utah County Sheriff Mike Smith, and FBI Special Agent In Charge Robert Bohls.
