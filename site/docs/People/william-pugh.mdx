# William Pugh

William Pugh is the name of a surgeon associated with an Utah-area hospital that appears in an unverified list circulated by citizen investigators. His name enters this investigation only through that online speculation, which this site does **not** endorse or detail.

No authority has adopted the list, and the name appears nowhere in the state's [court case](/court/overview) against the accused.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

As documented on the [Doctors](/Medical/doctors) page, Pugh's name is one of seven surgeons that appear in unverified citizen-investigator notes alleging online search activity around July 2025. As those pages state plainly, the allegation concerns **who reportedly searched for the names — not anything the doctors did**, and none of the listed surgeons is accused of any wrongdoing.

There is **no evidence** presented here that this individual did anything improper, and no charges of any kind are known. The presence of a name on such a list carries no implication of misconduct, and ordinary professional or privacy explanations fully account for it.

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

*(The following concerns alleged **search activity by third parties**, not conduct by Dr. Pugh. No wrongdoing is alleged against him as a site finding.)*

- Investigation notes under a “Timpanogos Surgeons” list claim that, in a roughly two-week window around **July 20, 2025**, IP addresses associated with Israel (in the commentators’ telling) queried multiple Utah emergency/surgical names, including **William Pugh** (noted around July 30 in that list), alongside Steven Neilman, Robert Patterson, Lee Trotter, Lee Patterson, Brian Gill, and Richard Rasmussen.
- Some posts tie that July window rhetorically to the same period when Charlie Kirk platformed **Tucker Carlson** at Florida events — a chronological juxtaposition by commentators, not proof of a plot involving any surgeon.
- Direct high-engagement X hits naming William Pugh alone are scarce; the name travels inside the multi-doctor search-pattern list. See [Doctors](/Medical/doctors) and medical pages for the fuller list and caveats.
