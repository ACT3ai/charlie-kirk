## Investigative Value

Abdel Fattah El-Sisi, the President of Egypt, appears in this investigation only through anonymous social-media commentary that strung together routine diplomatic contacts — calls and meetings with the French president and a U.S. military commander around September 2025 — and asked whether they might be "coincidence or collusion." Diplomatic calls between heads of state are commonplace, and nothing in the public record connects them to the events at Utah Valley University. See [People](/People/overview) and the [Cover-Up overview](/CoverUp/overview) for how such commentary circulated.

The framing in the source material is explicitly rhetorical (a series of leading questions). No evidence establishes any link to the shooting, and no charges exist. This page records that his name was invoked in speculation, not that any wrongdoing occurred.
