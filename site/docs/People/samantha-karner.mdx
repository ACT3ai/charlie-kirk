## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Karner's report sits at the center of the forensic fight over whether the **bullet that killed [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** can be **toolmark-matched** to the rifle the State attributes to [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson).

### The inconclusive jacket comparison

* Item: **bullet jacket fragment** recovered at autopsy / from the body (as described in filings and hearing coverage).  
* Comparison: seized **Mauser-pattern .30-06** bolt-action rifle from the wooded area near UVU.  
* Result: **inconclusive** — the fragment **could not be identified or excluded** as having been fired from that rifle.  
* Karner's live testimony (CNN / pool): she was **"unable to say one way or another"** because of a **lack of microscopic marks** on the jacket fragment.  
* The defense called her so that limitation would be sworn in open court, not buried in a sealed paper.

### What is **not** the same as the jacket result

Do **not** collapse all ATF work into the jacket outcome:

* ATF work on **cartridge casings** and the **Dremel** tool (home search items presented via [Jennifer Faumuina](/People/jennifer-faumuina)) is reported separately and used by the State for **rifle association**.  
* ATF **DNA** on the rifle itself is **[Caitlin Oliver](/People/caitlin-oliver)**'s domain (Day 5), not Karner's toolmarks.  
* FBI **DNA** on towel/screwdriver is **[Amanda Bakker](/People/amanda-bakker)** (Day 2).

## March 2026 sealing fight

Before the prelim, defense filings and a **~03/10/2026** seal fight ([calendar page](/court/Days_in_Court/2026-03-10-seal-motion)) focused public attention on the ATF ballistics paper. A **media coalition** opposed sealing. Later coverage describes the report as **unsealed** while remaining **inconclusive**. That sequence is why Karner's July live appearance is treated as a transparency and reliability stress test — not as proof of a plot by Karner or ATF.

## Defense strategy context

CNN's week takeaways note the defense called **three** forensic experts (Bakker, Karner, Oliver) to attack DNA and ballistics reliability. Karner is the **toolmark** leg of that triangle. [Michael Burt](/People/michael-burt) is repeatedly named as the defense attorney driving forensic semantics and quality challenges.
