# Walid Mahmoud

Walid Mahmoud is a name that appears in unverified citizen-investigator notes in connection with a "foreign-lead" aircraft thread — specifically as a claimed passenger aboard an Egyptian-registered jet that flew into Provo, Utah in 2025. This site includes the name only to document that it circulates in online speculation, which this site does **not** endorse or treat as established fact.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

The name enters the case solely through the aircraft-tracking discussion on this site — see the [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview) and Egyptian Armada pages. Those pages record that investigation notes name "Walid Mahmoud" as a passenger, while explicitly cautioning that this is an unverified online claim about a living person, that the same name appears elsewhere tied to a different aircraft, and that no wrongdoing has been established.

There is **no evidence** presented here connecting this individual to the shooting, and no charges of any kind are known. A named passenger on a flight is not, by itself, an indication of any involvement in a crime, and innocent business or travel explanations would be the ordinary expectation.

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X / Online Notes

*(High-speculation foreign-lead material. Presence of a name in flight chatter is **not** evidence of participation in any crime.)*

- Investigation notes claim a passenger name **“Walid Mahmoud”** appears in connection with Egyptian-registered jet **SU-BTU** activity into **Provo** (notes also reference a May 27, 2025 Provo arrival in broader Egyptian-armada timelines).
- Some online write-ups conflate that passenger string with career Egyptian diplomat **Walid Mahmoud Abdel Nasser** (public bio notes: WIPO Arab bureau, prior ambassadorships, Foreign Ministry posts). **Identity match is unverified.** This site does not assert they are the same person or that any diplomat flew into Provo for the assassination.
- Viral X Egyptian-plane threads ([Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) and amplifiers on [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) / [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) shadowing claims) usually focus on tail numbers and hotel tips, not on authenticated passenger manifests. Manifests are not public on this page.
