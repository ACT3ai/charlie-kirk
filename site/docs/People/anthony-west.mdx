# Anthony West

> **Disclaimer:** Inclusion on this page does not imply that this individual had knowledge of, participated in, or committed any illegal or immoral act. This page documents their publicly known connection to the events of September 10, 2025 for investigative reference only.

> **Living Person Notice:** Anthony West is a living person. Prior unrelated acting credits exist for other individuals with similar names. This page addresses **only** the UVU AV roster entry. We do **not** assert foreknowledge of the attack or evidence tampering.

## Investigative Value

**Anthony West** appears on the **sound/AV crew close proximity** list for the September 10, 2025 [UVU](/UVU/overview) event — alongside [Robert Golo](/People/robert-golo), [Jayden Wilson](/People/jayden-wilson), and [Terryl Farnsworth](/People/terryl-farnsworth). AV personnel may provide technical testimony on **mic routing**, **monitor mixes**, and **real-time stage conditions** that audience witnesses cannot replicate.

West's investigative value rises when AV logs, IEM configurations, and stage-side audio are compared against [Witnesses](/Witnesses/overview) claims and [Mic — Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview) research. See [Phillip Goldsberry Jr.](/People/phillip-goldsberry-jr) for microphone-placement questions on the same event.
