:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing on this page constitutes a finding of wrongdoing, criminal conduct, ethical violation, or participation in any crime by Dr. Deirdre Amaro or any other living person. This site documents questions and claims that have circulated in public commentary — not findings of fact. All persons named are presumed innocent. **Witness stand:** Her autopsy report was laid as **foundation through Agent [David Hull](/People/david-hull)**, not from the stand. See the full roster at [People on Witness Stand](/court/people-on-witness-stand) (Section C) and the calendar at [Days in Court](/court/Days_in_Court/overview).



Dr. Deirdre Amaro is identified in investigation material as **Utah's chief medical
examiner**, having taken the role at the state **Office of the Medical Examiner (OME)**
not long before the September 10, 2025 shooting of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) at Utah Valley
University. Holding a statewide medical-examiner post is an ordinary official role and,
as the source material itself states, implies no wrongdoing. She is **not accused of any
crime**, and no charge or official finding connects her to any misconduct.

The autopsy is the single most consequential document for establishing how Charlie Kirk was killed; the competing accounts are laid out under [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Utah does not use county coroners; deaths under criminal investigation are handled by the
statewide OME, based in **Taylorsville**. As the office's chief, Dr. Amaro oversaw the
office that handled Charlie Kirk's autopsy. Some investigation notes describe her more
directly as "the new Utah state medical examiner who performed the autopsy," though the
**specific pathologist who performed the hands-on examination** was named in preliminary-hearing
testimony as **Dr. Guajardo** (see [Charlie Kirk Medical Examiner Name](/Medical/charlie_kirk_medical_examiner_name)
and [Medical Examiner](/Medical/medical_examiner)). Her name is catalogued because commentators
have raised general questions about institutional continuity given her recent start date — the
site explicitly notes this is an ordinary role that implies no wrongdoing. Related documents:
[Charlie Kirk Autopsy Report](/Medical/charlie_kirk_autopsy_report) and
[Charlie Kirk Medical Examiner Report](/Medical/charlie_kirk_medical_examiner_report).

On **July 27, 2026**, X account **[@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu)** (Project
Constitution, ~294K followers) posted a clip from that evening's [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)
episode reporting that Dr. Amaro **has resigned** as Utah's chief medical examiner. The claim is
presented here as **reported commentary**, attributed to its source. It has **not** been independently
confirmed by this site, and as of this writing no statement from the Utah Office of the Medical
Examiner or from Dr. Amaro has been located confirming, denying, or explaining the departure.

Resigning a state post is a **lawful and ordinary** act. A resignation is **not** evidence of
wrongdoing, and nothing in this report establishes that Dr. Amaro did anything improper.

**The fact that cuts against the claim:** Utah DHHS organizational charts as of **May 2026**
still listed Dr. Amaro in the role, and no state announcement of a departure has appeared in
news coverage. The resignation may have happened; it is **not established**. Separately, and
confirmed by public record, the pathologist who performed the Kirk autopsy —
**Dr. Andrew R. Guajardo** — did leave Utah in early 2026, starting as
[Dutchess County, New York's medical examiner on March 23, 2026](https://www.dutchessny.gov/Departments/County-Executive/Sue-Serino-Appoints-New-Medical-Examiner.htm).

## Background & Qualifications

Dr. Amaro — referred to in some online discussion as **Deirdre Weiss Amaro** — is a
**quadruple board-certified pathologist**, certified in **anatomic and clinical pathology,
forensic pathology, and neuropathology**. That is a rare combination and it makes her
highly qualified to lead an office handling forensic homicide examinations.

Her documented career record:

| Years | Position |
|---|---|
| **2009** | **M.D.** — Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University |
| **2009–2013** | Residency, anatomic and clinical pathology — **UC San Diego** |
| **2013–2014** | Fellowship, forensic pathology — **University of New Mexico** Office of the Medical Investigator |
| **2014–2016** | Fellowship, **neuropathology** — UC San Diego |
| **~2016** | Forensic pathologist, **Shasta County, California** |
| then | **Missouri** — Chief Medical Examiner for **Boone and Callaway Counties**; faculty, **University of Missouri**, Columbia |
| **May 9, 2024** | Utah DHHS announces her hire |
| **July 1, 2024** | Begins full-time in-person work as **Utah's Chief Medical Examiner** |

She **replaced Dr. Erik Christensen**, who retired after nearly **16 years** with the Utah
office — first as a deputy, then as chief from **2016**. The office received **National
Association of Medical Examiners accreditation** around the time of the transition.

As chief she oversees the statewide Office of the Medical Examiner and its staff of roughly
**fifty** — investigations, recruiting, and operations. Publicly she has spoken about the
physically demanding reality of autopsy work, about death investigation as a **public-health**
instrument for tracking overdose and suicide trends, and about the work ultimately serving the
living. Interviews and a 2025 podcast describe her combining hands-on forensic work with
running the office.

**She did not personally perform the Charlie Kirk autopsy** according to available court
records. Preliminary-hearing testimony named **Dr. Andrew R. Guajardo**, then the office's
Deputy Medical Examiner, as the pathologist who performed the examination. Full account:
[Who Was the Medical Examiner for Charlie Kirk](/Medical/who_was_the_medical_examiner_for_charlie_kirk).

## Start Date — Resolved

Investigation notes on this site have carried **two** start dates, July 1 **2024** and July 1
**2025**, and the difference matters: a chief two months into the job reads very differently
from one fourteen months in.

**It is 2024.** Utah DHHS announced the hire on **May 9, 2024** and stated she began full-time
in-person work on **July 1, 2024**. By September 10, 2025 she had been running the office for
**fourteen months**. The "brand new chief" framing that circulates online is not correct.

Either way, appointment to a state ME office is routine, and a recently installed chief handling
a high-profile case does not, by itself, indicate anything improper.

## Court-Referenced Autopsy Record

At the [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) preliminary hearing (around **July 6–7, 2026**), Utah Department of
Public Safety agent **David Hull** testified that an autopsy **was conducted by the Utah
OME**, that the **cause of death was homicide by gunshot wound to the neck**, and that a
**bullet fragment was recovered** during the autopsy. The defense objected to the report
as **hearsay** absent direct testimony from the performing examiner. This official record
contradicts early "no autopsy" rumors while leaving the individual performing examiner
unnamed.
