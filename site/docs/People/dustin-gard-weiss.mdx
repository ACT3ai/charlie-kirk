## What is reported

- According to a citizen-research timeline in the project file, "Dustin Gard-Weiss was announced as the new executive director of the CIA, replacing [John Ratcliffe](/People/john-ratcliffe)" on July 11, 2025.
- Per the same notes, he was "previously the undersecretary of defense for intelligence and security" and was reportedly succeeded in that role by another official — a personnel description, not an allegation.
- Commentary flags the July 11 date as coinciding with other events the author is tracking; this is a noted coincidence, not a claim of causation or involvement.
- **Counterpoint:** Nothing in public reporting ties Gard-Weiss to the September 10 shooting or to any misconduct. A leadership appointment two months earlier is not evidence of anything by itself.
