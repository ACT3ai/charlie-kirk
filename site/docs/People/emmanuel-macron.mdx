## Investigative Value

Emmanuel Macron, the President of France, appears in this investigation solely because commentator [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) publicly alleged a plot she attributes to "the Macrons." According to Owens, an unnamed French government official warned her that the Macron family had paid for and green-lit an assassination attempt against her, and further claimed that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s alleged killer "trained with the French Legion's 13th Brigade." These are Owens's characterizations of what she says a source told her — not verified facts, and the French presidency has been reported to dispute the underlying premises. See [People](/People/overview) and the [Cover-Up overview](/CoverUp/overview) for how this thread fits the broader commentary.

There is no evidence in the public record tying President Macron to the events at Utah Valley University, and no charges exist. This page exists to record that his name was raised in reported allegations, not to endorse them.

## What is reported

- According to a public statement by Candace Owens ([@RealCandaceO](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens)), an unnamed "high-ranking employee of the French Government" warned her that "the Macrons have executed upon and paid for" an assassination plan targeting her.
- Owens further claimed the same source said Charlie Kirk's alleged assassin "trained with the French legion 13th brigade with multi-state involvement." This is presented as her account of a source's claim, not as established fact.
- Separate citizen-research commentary circulating in public discussion speculated about El-Sisi–Macron diplomatic contacts around September 2025 and a French state company's Tennessee investment. These items are speculation and do not establish any connection to the shooting.
- **Counterpoint:** These are attributed, unverified allegations from a single commentator relaying an anonymous source. They have not been proven, no court has found them true, and reporting indicates the French side disputes the premises. Presence of Macron's name here is not evidence of any act by him.

## Open Questions

1. What, if any, independent corroboration exists for the anonymous "French official" whom Owens cites — and can the claim be tested at all?
2. Have French authorities issued any formal response to the specific allegations, and what did it say?
3. Do any of the cited diplomatic-calendar items have a documented link to the case, or are they unrelated routine state business?

## Public commentary

The following are attributed claims that have circulated in public discussion — not findings of criminal guilt.

Macron's name also surfaces in broader geopolitical commentary online, sometimes alongside unrelated conspiracy threads about the Macron family. Such foreign-leader speculation does not amount to any finding about the assassination; at most, it reflects that some commentators mention France or geopolitics as backdrop. As with all claims on this page, these remain unproven and are attributed to their sources.

Among the specific claims reported in public commentary: Candace Owens has said that in 2025 roughly $1.5 million was allegedly funneled through an elite French dinner group referred to as the Club des Cent for an assassination plot targeting her, which she links to President Emmanuel Macron. Owens has said the warning came directly from a high-ranking official inside the French government. These figures and details are her characterizations of an anonymous source's account, are contested, and have not been established in any court.
