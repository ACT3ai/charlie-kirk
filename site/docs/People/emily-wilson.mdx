# Emily Wilson

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing on this page constitutes a finding of wrongdoing, criminal conduct, or ethical violation by Emily Wilson or any other living person. Wilson is **not accused** of involvement in Charlie Kirk's death. Meeting attendance and motive allegations cited in commentary are **reported claims**, not court findings. **We do not claim** that Wilson or any other person named here knew of or planned the assassination.
:::

**Emily Wilson** — known online as **"Emily Saves America"** — is a conservative influencer and podcaster named among the reported participants in the **Bridgehampton gathering of August 4-5, 2025**, the event [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and others later called the **"Hamptons intervention"** involving [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) and pro-Israel figures.

The Bridgehampton material is reported context from the weeks before Charlie Kirk was killed; the site's analysis of the killing itself sits under [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

## Background

Wilson produces conservative commentary as a podcaster and social-media personality. She operates within the same influencer circle as several other figures cited in the [Israel Hamptons](/Israel/israel-hamptons) accounts.

## Connection to the Investigation

### Reported Bridgehampton Gathering (Aug 4-5, 2025)

According to photos and *The Grayzone* reporting:

- Wilson is placed in the Hamptons around **August 8, 2025** via photographs, and is named among the **reported attendees** of the gathering convened by Bill Ackman.
- She is pictured with other attendees including [CJ Pearson](/People/cj-pearson) and [Xaviaer DuRousseau](/People/xaviaer-durousseau).
- When contacted by *The Grayzone*, she **declined to comment**.

Her specific role and anything said at the gathering remain **unconfirmed** and rest on **photographic placement and secondary sourcing**, not primary documents.

## What We Do Not Claim

- Wilson is **not** identified by any official investigation as a suspect.
- **Reported presence** at a gathering is **not** evidence of knowledge of threats against Charlie Kirk.
- **We do not claim** that Wilson or any attendee knew of or planned the assassination on September 10, 2025.

## Why Her Name Appears in This Investigation

Her mention reflects coverage of the reported summer 2025 gathering — she appears in attendee photos — not any accusation of wrongdoing.

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

*(The following are unverified claims and commentary from public posts, not findings of fact.)*

Citizen investigators treat the **Bridgehampton / Hamptons gathering of early August 2025** as a **political-pressure node** rather than a crime scene. According to commentary recirculated on X — including summaries of *The Grayzone* reporting and posts by accounts such as **[@realstewpeters](/Influencers/podcasts-stew-peters)** and **[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)** — Bill Ackman hosted or underwrote a gathering of conservative and pro-Israel influencers that [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) attended about five weeks before the UVU shooting. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and allied commentators have framed the meeting as an **"intervention"** over Israel-related platforming; Ackman and other attendees have **publicly denied** blackmail, threats, or a hostile setup and have pointed to **friendly post-event texts**.

Within that thread, **Emily Wilson** ("Emily Saves America") is typically discussed as a **photographically placed attendee**, not as an operational figure. X commentary volume about Wilson is **lower** than volume about higher-profile names such as Ackman or [Arynne Wexler](/People/arynne-wexler). After the assassination, her public account **@emilysavesusa** continued ordinary political commentary (including posts criticizing celebration of political violence). That is **public speech after the fact**, not evidence about what was said at Bridgehampton.

**Open investigator questions that still name her only as a listed attendee:**

1. Who paid travel/lodging for each influencer at the gathering?
2. Was there a shared agenda or talking points on TPUSA Israel policy?
3. Do contemporaneous photos, flight, or hotel records confirm the Aug 4–5 / ~Aug 8 placement windows?

None of those questions converts **reported presence** into knowledge of the September 10, 2025 killing. No court has made any finding of wrongdoing by Wilson.
