## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

During the July 2026 prelim, Neiman asked [Judge Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf) to make evidence public **without redactions**, arguing closed or shielded presentation fuels speculation. After an oral denial, he filed written notice the same night. The court again declined full public republishing mid-hearing ([07/08–07/09](/court/Days_in_Court/overview)).

## Why listed here

Neiman is **not a fact witness** on the murder elements, but his on-record advocacy shaped **what the public could see** of witness exhibits — directly relevant to the [People on Witness Stand](/court/people-on-witness-stand) and [Days in Court](/court/Days_in_Court/overview) transparency story.
