## Investigative Value

Jack Hibbs is a Christian pastor and commentator. The publicly available material about him is **thin and speculative**, so this page is kept deliberately modest. His name appears only because some X commentary connects him, as a relative, to [Butch Hibbs](/People/butch-hibbs), a person cited in discussions of backstage access at the UVU event, and characterizes his public advocacy on Israel policy. None of this describes any action by Jack Hibbs on or around September 10, 2025.

The relevant context lives in the [Witnesses](/Witnesses/overview) and [Israel](/Israel/overview) threads.

## What is reported

- According to X commentary, Jack Hibbs is described as a pastor associated with PragerU and characterized as advocating pro-Israel, "Israel-first" positions. This is a **characterization by commentators**, not a documented action tied to the case.
- The same commentary **alleges** a family relationship between Jack Hibbs and [Butch Hibbs](/People/butch-hibbs), and further **alleges** unverified claims about the family's background. The commentary itself expresses uncertainty ("I thought you were all… crazy but now I'm not so sure").
- **Counterpoint:** These are **unverified allegations and characterizations** posted online. Nothing in the file describes any conduct by Jack Hibbs connected to the shooting, and there is no evidence he was present or involved. Public advocacy on policy is lawful expression and is not evidence of wrongdoing.
