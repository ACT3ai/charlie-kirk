## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Hull is one of two SBI **case agents** permitted to remain in the courtroom for the prelim (with **[Brian Davis](/People/brian-davis)**). He is the State's primary **video and investigation-overview** stand witness tying [UVU](/UVU/overview) surveillance to the person the State believes is [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson).

On Day 1 the State called him after **[Chris Bagley](/People/chris-bagley)** ("The state calls David Hull" — [Ryan McBride](/People/ryan-mcbride)). His examination continued into Day 2.

## Reported testimony (attributed)

### Surveillance timeline (CNN / pool)

Hull walked the court through footage the State says shows Robinson on campus **multiple times** on **09/10/2025**:

* ~**8:30 a.m.** — vehicle into parking garage; courtyard / TPUSA interaction; leave ~**9:25 a.m.**  
* ~**10:30 hour** — food; wooded area; backpack missing on return; leave before **11 a.m.**  
* ~**11:53 a.m.** — different clothes, limp  
* ~**12:22 p.m.** — rooftop run/crawl prone near shot time; escape toward woods with an object  
* ~**12:30 a.m. 09/11** — vehicle stop on campus road; plate tied to Robinson / mother  

### Other Day 1–2 threads

* Authentication of eyewitness, [UVU surveillance](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance), Ring doorbell video  
* [Medical examiner](/Medical/trial_and_autopsy_report) report foundation  
* In-court **identification** of Robinson for prelim purposes (over defense objection — judge finding limited to the hearing)  
* Cross by [Kathryn Nester](/People/kathryn-nester): chain of custody, rooftop search, **empty holster vs gun-in-holster** report conflict, **unfired** round on another roof (officer-rifle account), people cleared early, event security posture  

### Crime-scene paving

Citizen clips attribute to Hull that he learned of courtyard **paving** via **news reports**, without prior notice to him as lead. That is **attributed testimony commentary** used in cover-up threads; it is not a finding that Hull approved or participated in any improper destruction of evidence. See [Dirt Removed](/CoverUp/Dirt_Removed) and related pages.
