## What is reported

- According to a video account by commentator Russell Brand — who says he was relaying two unnamed sources — figures at a cabinet meeting (a list into which he places the Vice President) stated that foreign involvement and domestic accomplices were "not being looked at." The transcription itself flags the name as uncertain ("Joe Kin [JD Vance?]"), so even the identification is unconfirmed.
- Separately, the file notes a speculative "vancekirk2028.com" domain timeline that some commentators raise; the source presents this only as "a fun timeline" of domain searches and registrations, not as evidence of anyone's conduct.
- **Counterpoint:** These are uncorroborated, second-hand, and in one case explicitly uncertain claims. Nothing in the file establishes any statement or action by Vice President Vance, and no charge or court finding exists.

## Open Questions

1. Can the cabinet-meeting account be independently verified, including the identity of those present?
2. Who registered and searched the domain referenced in the commentary, and is there any connection to any official?
3. Do any on-the-record statements exist that match the second-hand account?
