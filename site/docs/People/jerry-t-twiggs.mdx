Jerry T Twiggs is named in citizen research as a member of the extended Twiggs family and, per Texas business-registry records, as a director and registered agent of a nonprofit corporation. He enters the investigation only through that family and public-record connection, not through any alleged act.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

[Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) is a person referenced in the case as an associate of the accused, [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson). Researchers noted the surname overlap and reviewed public filings for a related charity. There is **no evidence** connecting Jerry T Twiggs to the events of September 10, 2025, and no accusation of wrongdoing.

## Background

According to Texas Secretary of State-style public records cited in research notes, the **Faith and Family Foundation Charity** is a Texas domestic non-profit corporation filed March 9, 2026, listing Jerry T Twiggs as Director and Registered Agent, with [Amy R Twiggs](/People/amy-r-twiggs) and [Mikayla R Twiggs](/People/mikayla-r-twiggs) as directors, at a Houston, TX address. These are routine public-record incorporation details.
