# Blake Bednarz

**Blake Bednarz** (also referred to as **Douglas "Blake" Bednarz**) is a **private individual** whose name appears in **unverified online speculation** connected to the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) case. This site does **not** endorse, adopt, or detail that speculation.

His footage is one of the most-examined video records of the moment Charlie Kirk was assassinated, and forensic questions about it recur in coverage of the state's [case against Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview).

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

An anonymous online claim asserts that a person by this name was present at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. There is **no evidence**, no corroboration, and **no charges** supporting that claim or connecting any identifiable person by this name to any wrongdoing. Being present at, or near, a public campus event would be an ordinary and innocent circumstance in any case. The name is recorded here only because it circulated online; nothing about it is established as fact.
