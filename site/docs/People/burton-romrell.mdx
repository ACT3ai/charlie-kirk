# Burton Romrell

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing on this page constitutes a finding of wrongdoing, criminal conduct, ethical violation, or participation in any crime by Burton Romrell or any other living person. This site documents questions and claims that have circulated in public commentary — not findings of fact. All persons named are presumed innocent. Allegations referenced here are unproven and have not been established in any court.
:::


Burton Romrell is publicly listed as a co-owner of **Hardscape Specialties LLC**
and is described in circulating notes as former Utah Valley University
operations staff. His name enters the case only through unverified online
speculation about post-event courtyard/paving work at UVU.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Mr. Romrell's name appears in uncorroborated online commentary concerning the
company associated with hardscape work in the tent/courtyard area after
September 10, 2025. This site does **not** endorse, repeat, or detail that
speculation. There is **no evidence** of wrongdoing by Mr. Romrell, no finding
by any investigative body, and **no charges**. Ordinary contractors performing
scheduled or repair work is the routine, innocent explanation for any such
company's presence.

## Open Questions

1. Is there any verified public record establishing what work, if any, was
   performed and when — distinct from online inference?

## Status

**Status:** Unknown


## Hardscape Entity Context (Entity Facts vs. Personal Allegations)

Public commentary about **Hardscape Specialties LLC** (see [Hardscape Specialties](/Tent/Hardscape_Specialties)) has included claims that:

- The company was long dormant and **revived in early 2025**
- Co-owner **Burton Romrell** is described as **former UVU operations staff**
- Another listed owner, **Michael Powell**, is described in the same threads as a Utah AG-related special agent (entity-level claim — verify independently)
- A work order **two days after** the shooting and paving **days later** near the tent/courtyard area attracted suspicion online

**Defamation boundary:** Corporate timing questions are **not** proof that Romrell destroyed evidence or participated in a cover-up. Scheduled hardscape work and emergency repairs have innocent explanations. **No charges** against Romrell appear in public case dockets reviewed for this page.

## Open Questions (Process, Not Guilt)

1. What public procurement or work-order documents describe the post-9/10 courtyard work?
2. What is the documented corporate ownership history of Hardscape Specialties LLC?
3. Do any UVU facilities records name Romrell personally after September 10, 2025?
