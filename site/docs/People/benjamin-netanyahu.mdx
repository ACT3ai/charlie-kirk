## Investigative Value

Benjamin Netanyahu, the Prime Minister of Israel, is named in this investigation only in passing: through a reported claim, attributed to commentator [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), that a member of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s SUV/security detail had previously served on Netanyahu's protective team. This is a statement about a third party's alleged past employment, not any assertion of action by Netanyahu himself. See [People](/People/overview) and the [Israel overview](/Israel/overview) for the surrounding commentary.

The connection is thin and indirect. Nothing in the public record ties Netanyahu to the events at Utah Valley University, and no charges or allegations of wrongdoing exist against him here. This page records only that his name surfaced via a staffer's alleged résumé.

## What is reported

- According to commentary attributed to Candace Owens, a member of Charlie Kirk's security who was near him was said to have "previously" served on Benjamin Netanyahu's security team.
- Broader public commentary includes unverified quotes attributed to Kirk expressing fear about Israel; these are presented as reported statements, not proven facts, and none is an act by Netanyahu.
- **Counterpoint:** Even if a security professional once worked a foreign protective detail, that is a common career path and implies nothing about the shooting. There is no evidence tying Netanyahu to the case, no allegation of any crime against him, and this is commentary, not a court finding.
