## Investigative Value

Benjamin Netanyahu, the Prime Minister of Israel, is named in this investigation only through commentary: reported 2025 outreach to [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) — an invitation to visit Israel and a funding offer that Kirk reportedly declined — and attributed claims about a high-pressure phone call in Kirk's final days. Those threads are tracked at [Israel PM](/Israel/israel-pm) and [Netanyahu / Iran Intervention Call](/Charlie/Phone_Calls/netanyahu-iran-call). See [People](/People/overview) and the [Israel overview](/Israel/overview) for the surrounding commentary.

The connection is thin and indirect. Nothing in the public record ties Netanyahu to the events at Utah Valley University, and no charges or allegations of wrongdoing exist against him here. This page records only that his name surfaced in commentator accounts of outreach and calls.

## What is reported

- Commentators describe an alleged **invitation to visit Israel** that Kirk reportedly declined, and an alleged **funding offer** tied to TPUSA that Kirk reportedly rejected — see [Israel PM](/Israel/israel-pm). No readout, correspondence, or named witness documents either.
- [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and amplifiers claim Kirk's final high-pressure call involved Netanyahu or Netanyahu-aligned pressure over Iran — see [Netanyahu / Iran Intervention Call](/Charlie/Phone_Calls/netanyahu-iran-call). No call record or transcript has been published.
- Broader public commentary includes unverified quotes attributed to Kirk expressing fear about Israel; these are presented as reported statements, not proven facts, and none is an act by Netanyahu.
- **Counterpoint:** Netanyahu and Israeli officials have publicly denied any involvement in Kirk's death, and a head of government reaching out to a prominent foreign ally is ordinary. There is no evidence tying Netanyahu to the case, no allegation of any crime against him, and this is commentary, not a court finding.
