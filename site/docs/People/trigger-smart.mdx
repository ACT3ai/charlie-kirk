# Trigger Smart

Trigger Smart is the name used by an independent online commentator credited within citizen-investigator circles for research and analysis of theories about the September 10, 2025 shooting of Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University. The name appears to be an online handle rather than a confirmed legal identity.

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

According to the [Ian Carroll](/People/ian-carroll) profile on this site, Carroll is credited "alongside [Jon Bray](/People/jon-bray), Baron Coleman, Trigger Smart, and Stew Peters for deep investigation work into multiple theories about the shooting, including close-range theories and alternative shooter hypotheses." Trigger Smart's involvement is therefore as one of several independent researchers publishing commentary and theories, not as a witness or participant in the events.

## Background

Publicly verifiable biographical information about the person behind the "Trigger Smart" name is limited. The name circulates primarily through independent social-media commentary about the case. This site does not have confirmed details on the commentator's identity, background, or credentials.

## Reported Claims (Unverified)

- Independent commentary credits Trigger Smart with analysis supporting close-range and alternative-shooter theories. These are contested interpretations, not established findings, and are disputed by official accounts. Readers should weigh them as one set of opinions among many.

## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

*(Attributed commentary about an online handle — identity unconfirmed.)*

- Investigation notes and the [Ian Carroll](/People/ian-carroll) page credit **Trigger Smart** alongside Jon Bray, Baron Coleman, Ian Carroll, and Stew Peters for deep work on close-range and alternative-shooter theories after September 10, 2025.
- High-engagement X search results during this research pass more often name peer investigators (Ian Carroll, Baron Coleman, Stew Peters) than Trigger Smart by string; the handle still circulates in credit lists rather than as a single viral primary source.
- Theories associated with this research cluster include challenges to a pure rooftop/.30-06 lone-actor account and interest in stage/tent acoustics — contested interpretations, not established findings. See [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and [Acoustic Two-Event Split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split).
