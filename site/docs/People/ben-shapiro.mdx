## Background

Shapiro graduated from UCLA at age 20 and Harvard Law School at 23. He founded The Daily Wire in 2015 and built it into one of the largest conservative media companies. He is an Orthodox Jew and a vocal advocate for strong U.S.-Israel relations.

## Connection to the Investigation

### September 9, 2025 — Kirk on Shapiro's Show
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) appeared on Ben Shapiro's show on **September 9, 2025** — less than 24 hours before his death — and discussed Israeli policy. According to public reporting and video, this was one of Kirk's more direct public statements on the topic within the conservative movement.

Commentary threads (including **[@based_sam_parker](/Influencers/x/based-sam-parker)** compilations) place this appearance at the end of a rapid public escalation:

| Date | Kirk's reported public trajectory |
|---|---|
| **Aug 4–6, 2025** | Hamptons intervention — pro-Israel figures **reportedly** confront evolving stance |
| **Sept 1–5** | Group-chat statements: "no choice but to leave pro-Israel cause" |
| **Sept 8** | Public post leaving the pro-Israel movement |
| **Sept 9** | Shapiro show — criticized Israeli policy on air |
| **Sept 10, 12:23 PM** | Shot at UVU |

On the same day as the Shapiro appearance, sources **allege** Kirk told confidants during a high-pressure meeting: **"THEY ARE GOING TO KILL ME."** ([KM-07](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps)) These are **attributed statements**, not independently verified facts. Kirk's donor disputes and international connections in this escalation window are the same lead areas former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) says his team was ordered to abandon once Tyler Robinson was in custody.

### Hamptons Context (Indirect)
Shapiro is **not** among the names most commonly cited as a **reported attendee** of the August Hamptons intervention ([Josh Hammer](/People/josh-hammer), Bill Ackman, [Seth Dillon](/People/seth-dillon), [Rabbi Pesach Wolicki](/People/rabbi-pesach-wolicki), [Natasha Hausdorff](/People/natasha-hausdorff)). His relevance is primarily as the **September 9 broadcast platform** where Kirk's Israel-policy break became nationally visible — the day after his public exit post and the day before the assassination.

The Hamptons layer is documented separately in [Israel Hamptons](/Israel/israel-hamptons) and Charlie — Israel, Donors, and Motive.

### Intra-Conservative Media Friction
Via a **Megyn Kelly** clip circulated in X threads, Shapiro **reportedly** called [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)'s questioning of the FBI version **"evil"** — an example of **intraconservative media conflict** over the investigation narrative. This is **reported commentary** about a living person, not a finding of wrongdoing.

Public commentary and reporting also note that Shapiro **produced a show the day after** the assassination and, per one circulated clip, appeared alongside [Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet)'s "an army is rising" remark with language about being willing "to put boots on the ground… to do anything Charlie would have wanted." Separately, some threads reference an earlier period in which **Shapiro attacked Kirk on Mark Levin's program** over Kirk's shift on Israel. These are **reported commentary and clips**, cited to show the intra-movement friction, not offered as evidence of any involvement — Shapiro is **not accused** of any role in the death.

Shapiro is a prominent figure within the broader conservative media ecosystem that intersects with [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) and the donor networks discussed in the investigation's motive analysis sections.
