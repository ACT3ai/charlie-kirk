# Amy R Twiggs

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

The Twiggs family name enters the investigation only through **[Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs)**, who is associated with the case. Her name is listed in the [Lance Twiggs family records page](/People/Lance_Twiggs_Family) as a relative appearing in public documents — not as a participant in any alleged conduct.

Because the seller and buying trust share the family name, this is consistent with an **ordinary estate-planning transfer into a family trust** — a routine, lawful step.
