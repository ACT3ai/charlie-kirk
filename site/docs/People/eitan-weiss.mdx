Eitan Weiss is named in this investigation only as an Israeli Consul General who, per a social-media "timeline" post, reportedly met with the Tennessee governor in a publicized November 19 meeting described as being about "continued support for Israel." That is the entirety of the connection. A consul general meeting a governor is standard diplomatic activity.
