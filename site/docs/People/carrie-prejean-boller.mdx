Carrie Prejean Boller is a public figure whose name enters case-adjacent
discussion through online commentary reporting that she questioned
[Seth Dillon](/People/seth-dillon) under oath about a so-called "Hamptons
meeting."

## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

According to circulating online commentary, Prejean Boller "asked Seth Dillon,
while he was under oath, about the infamous Hamptons meeting with [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)."
This site documents that reported exchange as **part of the broader
donor-meeting and Israel-friction discussion**, not as evidence of any
wrongdoing by any party. The nature, forum, and accuracy of the reported
questioning are **not independently verified here**.

According to circulating X and commentary notes compiled in the investigation file, **Carrie Prejean Boller** publicly questioned **Seth Dillon under oath** about the reported **Hamptons meeting** connected to Charlie Kirk's evolving Israel-donor tensions. File summaries claim Dillon's account **shifted** toward admitting a "heated and at times spirited" exchange.

These are **attributed reports of sworn or public questioning**, not independent proof of what was said at any private meeting. See [Israel Hamptons](/Israel/israel-hamptons) and Charlie — Israel, Donors, and Motive. **No wrongdoing is alleged against Prejean Boller** for asking questions.
