# Butch Hibbs

> **Disclaimer:** Inclusion on this page does not imply that this individual had knowledge of, participated in, or committed any illegal or immoral act. This page documents their publicly known connection to the events of September 10, 2025 for investigative reference only.

> **Living Person Notice:** Butch Hibbs is a living person. The object in his front pocket in event photographs looks about the size of a RØDE wireless microphone receiver. We do **not** claim that it is one. We do **not** claim that he knew anything ahead of time, and we do **not** claim that he was involved in any way. We only say that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated.

Cleanup-crew commentary drew attention because the courtyard where Charlie Kirk was killed was reportedly washed down quickly — a point developed in the [cover-up section](/CoverUp/overview).

## Investigative Value

**Butch Hibbs** — reported in full as **Burton "Butch" Hibbs** — is listed in Query 6 research among witnesses tied to **aftermath and response** discussions at the September 10, 2025 [UVU](/UVU/overview) event — grouped with [Jason Behunin](/People/jason-behunin) and [Phillip Goldsberry Jr.](/People/phillip-goldsberry-jr) as **witnesses to aftermath**.

Hibbs also appears in pre-event photos near [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). Investigation-file notes describe him as the brother of Pastor **[Jack Hibbs](/People/jack-hibbs)** and a **Heber City, Utah** resident with long drone-industry experience — background context only, not proof of wrongdoing. Any sworn testimony on what Hibbs saw during response and tent-area activity could intersect [Tent](/Tent/overview) and [Witnesses](/Witnesses/overview) timelines.

> **We make no claim here.** This site does **not** assert that Butch Hibbs works for, or has ever
> worked for, any intelligence service — American or foreign. We do **not** assert that he did
> anything wrong, that he knew anything in advance, or that he committed any crime. He has not been
> charged with anything. The clip below is published because it is his **brother** speaking, and
> because what a brother says about a family member in the middle of recounting September 10 is
> worth hearing for yourself.

The interesting thing is not an accusation from a stranger online. It is that **[Jack Hibbs](/People/jack-hibbs)**
— Butch's brother, a Calvary Chapel pastor and a familiar face in [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) circles — is the one
speaking, on camera, and volunteers the subject himself while talking about the day Charlie Kirk was killed.

In the thirteen seconds captured here, Jack Hibbs says his brother has lived in Utah for years, then adds:

> "We have to make sure that this doesn't get out, but, you know, some people would say that he is a
> covert military operative for the CIA, and in conjunction with the Mossad."

Note carefully what that sentence is and is not. It is **hedged by the speaker himself** — "some people
would say" — and it is delivered in a conversational register that the poster who circulated it describes
as having been dressed up as a joke about a mountain man. It is not a claim of fact, and this page does
not treat it as one.

### What we are actually trying to work out

The open question this page holds — as a question, not a finding — is simply: **what did he mean?**
A short list of readings, none of them established:

* **A family joke.** Brothers tease each other about being mysterious. A rural, outdoorsy sibling gets
  called a spook. Nothing more is meant, and the "we have to make sure this doesn't get out" line is part
  of the bit rather than a real request for discretion.
* **Loose talk about ordinary work.** Utah has a large defense, aerospace, and unmanned-systems economy.
  Someone with a long career in that world can be described loosely as "military" by a relative who does
  not know or care about the distinctions between a contractor, a supplier, and an agency.
* **Something the speaker regretted saying.** The poster's reading is that the line slipped out and was
  immediately walked back. We record that this is one reading and that we cannot see inside anyone's head.

We do not adopt any of the three. The clip is short, we do not have what came before or after it, and a
thirteen-second excerpt is the weakest form of evidence about intent that exists. **Full context would
settle it, and full context is exactly what we do not have** — which is why it is presented as an open
question rather than a conclusion.

### Factual points we do carry from the coverage

These are the checkable, non-accusatory details the reporting around this clip contains:

* His full name is reported as **Burton "Butch" Hibbs**, and he is described as a longtime **Utah** resident.
* He is the **brother of Pastor [Jack Hibbs](/People/jack-hibbs)**.
* He was **present at the [UVU](/UVU/overview) event on September 10, 2025**, which is consistent with the
  photographs already discussed elsewhere on this page.
* Jack Hibbs says the last formal photograph Charlie Kirk posed for that day was **with the two of them** —
  a detail about a photograph, and nothing more than that.

Being present at a public event, being photographed with the speaker, and having a brother who says an
odd thing on a podcast are **not** wrongdoing, and nothing on this page should be read as suggesting they are.

On **September 2, 2026**, a Google Trends screenshot was posted showing a **single image-search spike** for the term
`"Burton Hibbs Calvalry Church"` — his legal first name plus an apparent misspelling of **Calvary Chapel**, his
brother's denomination — in the **Tucson (Sierra Vista) AZ** metro on **September 7, 2025**. The line is flat at zero
for the whole of the preceding eight months.

**Fort Huachuca sits next to Sierra Vista, and that is where the interest in the chart comes from.** It is also where
the argument stops. Google Trends reports **metro-wide buckets** covering roughly a million people across southern
Arizona, so the chart cannot show that any search came from the base, from a government network, or from any
particular person. **This site does not claim that Butch Hibbs went to, was at, or was expected at Fort Huachuca.**
Nothing published so far — no manifest, gate log, or travel record — supports any such thing.

The narrower point is that a Trends spike is an act by **whoever did the searching**. It is not an act by the person
whose name was typed in, and it places him nowhere. The full breakdown, including the control tests the original post
does not show, is on **[Butch Hibbs — Sierra Vista Trends Spike](/GoogleSearches/butch-hibbs-sierra-vista-trends)**.

The day after that screenshot circulated, the same query reportedly stopped returning a chart at all. On
**September 2–3, 2026**, [Stew Peters](/Influencers/podcasts-stew-peters) posted that the result "was wiped in real
time" while the host who had been discussing it was live on air, and published her message to him saying so. In the
clip here she pulls the term back up mid-broadcast, finds Google's standard **"your search doesn't have enough data to
show here"** panel where the curve had been, and asks on camera whether she simply mistyped it.

**That is an observation about a chart, not about a person.** No control term was run at the same moment, and a
very-low-volume string returns the identical empty panel whether it was removed or was always near the reporting
threshold. The full account, including the three checks that would settle it and which of them nobody has performed,
is on **[Trends Result Wiped Live On Air](/CoverUp/Trends_Wiped_Live_On_Air)**.
