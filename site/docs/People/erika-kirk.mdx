## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

Erika Kirk is Charlie Kirk's widow. National press covered her presence at the **[07/06–07/10/2026](/court/Days_in_Court/overview)** preliminary hearing in [State v. Robinson](/court/case-overview) in Provo — including visible emotional reactions when shooting descriptions and rooftop surveillance were presented (pool reporting, CNN/AP). Family counsel **[Jeffrey Neiman](/People/jeffrey-neiman)** pressed the court for **public, unredacted** display of hearing exhibits.

A family statement after **[07/10/2026](/court/Days_in_Court/2026-07-10-prelim-day-5)** called the hearing an important step and described the evidence presented as overwhelming from the family's perspective — **advocacy, not a court finding**.
