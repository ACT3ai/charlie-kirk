## Connection to the Charlie Kirk Case

According to Sergeant [Jennifer Faumuina](/People/jennifer-faumuina)'s sworn testimony at the [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) preliminary hearing ([Day 4 transcript](/court/Preliminary_Hearing/Day_4)), Special Agent Michelle Cammack was the **FBI Evidence Response Team lead** who responded to the [wooded area near UVU](/Locations/Wooded_Area_Gun_Recovery) after officers reported finding a gun. Faumuina testified: "myself and FBI ERT team, the lead, Special Agent Camack, would be en route."

## Reported Role in the Rifle Recovery (attributed)

All of the following comes from Sgt. Faumuina's testimony as recorded in the Day 4 transcript:

* When the scene was first observed, "all you could really see was just a dark towel there" in the bushes; photographs were taken before anything was moved.
* Faumuina testified that it was **Special Agent Cammack who physically moved the wrapped rifle from the bushes**: "We moved it from the bushes ... Or say FBI Special Agent Camack moved it from the bushes."
* "When Special Agent Cammack reached in, she grabbed not only the towel, but also the gun. And the towel and the gun remained basically intact... and then was placed onto the cardboard box."
* The rifle was then photographed on a cardboard box, the towel packaged separately, additional pictures taken, and the weapon rendered safe on scene.
* Faumuina stated she was present with Cammack the entire time: "She did [reach into the bushes], but I was there with her and we were communicating that entire time."

## Why Citizen Investigators Track This Role

The wooded-area rifle recovery is one of the most scrutinized moments in the case — its timing relative to [bomb-dog sweeps](/FBI/Bomb_Dogs), the [chain of custody](/FBI/Ballistics_Forensics) from the bushes to the labs, and the [gun drop and search sequence](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/gun-drop-and-search-sequence) are all active lines of citizen inquiry. Identifying which officials handled the alleged murder weapon, and when, is part of documenting that chain. These are questions about process, not findings of misconduct — **no court has found that Special Agent Cammack did anything improper**, and this page records her reported role only.

## Open Questions

1. Cammack's full ERT report and photo log for the wooded-area scene — not yet public.
2. Exact time the ERT team arrived at the bushes relative to the first dog searches of that area.
3. Whether Cammack testified or will testify directly, rather than being described through Faumuina's testimony.
