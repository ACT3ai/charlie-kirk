## Who He Is

**Jeremy Gallman** is a professional camera operator and video editor based in **Phoenix, Arizona**. Since **January 2025** he has worked as a Camera Operator and Video Editor for [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview), the organization Charlie Kirk founded. He joined TPUSA as an independent contractor in **November 2024** and moved into the staff role two months later.

Before TPUSA, he spent roughly eight years at **Seed Media** and Seed Media AK, working his way up through production and post-production: production assistant, freelance video editor and videographer, director of photography and lead video editor, internship supervisor, and post-production manager. He also owns a small production company, **Pine Films**. He studied outdoor leadership at the **University of Alaska Anchorage**, earning an Associate of Applied Science.

Photographs circulated online show him at Turning Point events, including "Prove Me Wrong" campus segments, doing the job he is paid to do — running a camera.

## Not To Be Confused With

Several people share this name, and they are **different people** with no relationship to one another:

* **Major Jeremy Gallman, DVM, MS, DACVPM, DACVIM (SAIM)** — a U.S. Army Veterinary Corps officer with Auburn University credentials, known for continuing-education lectures on canine hypoadrenocorticism, diabetic ketoacidosis, and the transfer of battlefield trauma skills to veterinary practice. He has been associated with Joint Base Lewis-McChord and Joint Base Andrews and with Yelm, Washington. He is not the subject of this page.
* **A hospital executive of the same name** — again a separate individual, unrelated to this page.

Neither namesake has any connection to Charlie Kirk, to Turning Point USA, or to anything documented on this site. Search results that mix them together are conflating three unrelated people.
