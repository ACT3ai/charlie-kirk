## What Is Claimed, And What We Actually Know

**What we say:** one X account, @MuppetMasher, made a claim. **What we do not say:** that the claim is correct.

The claim, in full, is this: an X user published a composite graphic that boxes a bearded man in a black polo and lanyard in a Turning Point Action group photograph taken in Phoenix, Arizona, draws an arrow to a frame of a camera operator working an event, and labels both **"Jeremy Gallman — TPUSA camera operator."** That label is @MuppetMasher's assertion, not ours.

**What we have not established:**

* That the man boxed in the group photo and the operator behind the camera are the same person. The graphic offers visual resemblance and nothing else.
* That either man is the Jeremy Gallman whose career listing appears below. The name on the graphic is a caption, not a verified attribution.
* Where any of these people were on **September 10, 2025**. This site has not established that, and does not claim it.

**What is separately on the public record:** a professional-profile listing under the name Jeremy Gallman, in Phoenix, Arizona, describing a career in video production. That listing is set out in the next section. It is a career history that exists publicly under this name — it does **not** by itself prove the identification in the graphic is correct.

Turning Point USA has not published a crew list, has not confirmed the identification, and has not denied it. Until one of those things happens, or someone who was there tells us, this stays exactly where it is: **an open, unresolved question.**

## Not To Be Confused With

Several people share this name, and they are **different people** with no relationship to one another:

* **Major Jeremy Gallman, DVM, MS, DACVPM, DACVIM (SAIM)** — a U.S. Army Veterinary Corps officer with Auburn University credentials, known for continuing-education lectures on canine hypoadrenocorticism, diabetic ketoacidosis, and the transfer of battlefield trauma skills to veterinary practice. He has been associated with Joint Base Lewis-McChord and Joint Base Andrews and with Yelm, Washington. He is not the subject of this page.
* **A hospital executive of the same name** — again a separate individual, unrelated to this page.

Neither namesake has any connection to Charlie Kirk, to Turning Point USA, or to anything documented on this site. Search results that mix them together are conflating three unrelated people.

## Can You Help Confirm Or Rule This Out?

This page is a question, not an answer. We would rather publish an unresolved question honestly than repeat an identification as though it were settled. If you can help settle it, please get in touch.

**What would confirm it:**

* A TPUSA staff member, contractor, or event crew member who can say plainly whether Jeremy Gallman worked as a camera operator and, if so, which events he was assigned to.
