## His Role At Turning Point USA

**Jeremy Gallman works for Turning Point USA as a camera operator and video editor.** This is not an allegation and it is not somebody else's inference — **he reports it himself on his own LinkedIn profile.** A person's self-published account of where they work is the most direct evidence available for that fact, and this site treats it as such.

He did not start there as staff. He came in as an **independent contractor** doing video work, and then moved onto the payroll: from January 2025 the listing reads as a **full-time employee** rather than contract work.

On **April 20, 2026**, an X account, [@MuppetMasher](https://x.com/MuppetMasher/status/2046200409476489659), published a composite graphic naming him — setting a group photograph taken in front of a Turning Point Action backdrop beside a frame of a camera operator working an event, and labelling both **"Jeremy Gallman — TPUSA camera operator."** That is the identification, and his own professional profile is consistent with it.

**What that does and does not mean.** It means a named video professional worked cameras for the organization. It does **not** mean he was at any particular event, on any particular date, and this site makes no claim about where he was on **September 10, 2025**. Turning Point USA has not published a crew list, so which events any individual operator was assigned to remains unpublished.

## Not To Be Confused With

The full detail behind the warning at the top of this page:

* **Major Jeremy Gallman, DVM, MS, DACVPM, DACVIM (SAIM)** — a U.S. Army Veterinary Corps officer with Auburn University credentials, known for continuing-education lectures on canine hypoadrenocorticism, diabetic ketoacidosis, and the transfer of battlefield trauma skills to veterinary practice. He has been associated with Joint Base Lewis-McChord and Joint Base Andrews and with Yelm, Washington. **He is not the subject of this page, and he is a completely different man from the TPUSA camera operator.**
* **A hospital executive of the same name** — again a separate individual, unrelated to this page and unrelated to the Army officer.

Neither namesake has any connection to Charlie Kirk, to Turning Point USA, or to anything documented on this site. Search results that mix them together are conflating three unrelated people, and any page, post or thread that treats an Army officer's biography as though it were a TPUSA video employee's has made exactly that error.
