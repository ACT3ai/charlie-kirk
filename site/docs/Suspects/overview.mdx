The strongest **documented mismatch** with the FBI story is the black-clad suspect: police dispatch broadcast a
male in all black with a tactical helmet and mask while [Dylan Hope](/People/dylan-hope), a construction worker at **785 College Dr**,
Orem, independently described a trench-coated figure with greasy hair who crossed the site before sirens sounded.
Pre-shooting rooftop video shows a prone all-black figure; the sheriff photo shown to workers reportedly did not
match the FBI's public release. **[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)** (April 2026) repackaged that dispatch/Hope/roof-video cluster for a large audience, arguing the FBI public package abandoned the original description — an **investigation-process claim**, not a court ruling. That thread is developed on Black Clothing Suspect
and [FBI Black Clothing Photo](/FBI/Black_Clothing_Photo).

Parallel **location and vehicle** theories argue the fatal event did not require a lone rooftop sniper at 150 yards.
[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution) traced an IR laser on [Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek)'s phone to a grey van under the
tent tunnel; @jonaaronbray and [@troofevades](/Influencers/x/troofevades) published
acoustic analyses placing the crack much closer to Charlie than Losee-rooftop geometry allows. Published ballistics
commentary questions whether a **5–9°** downward shot from Losee would match observed neck/chest wounds.
[Possible Shooter Locations](./Shooting_Locations) and [Grey Van Shooter POI](./Grey_Van_Shooter) hold those lanes.
Readers should keep the **official Losee rooftop charging theory** visible beside these alternatives; this section maps claims, it does not pick a winner.

**Persons of interest** near the stage and SUV — including Rick Cutler ("arm puller" / viral "palm gun" claims on X) and social-media
allegations about grey-van occupants ([Van Man Bednarz](./Van_Man_Bednarz)) — are documented as reported allegations, with attribution and known counterpoints. A separate unidentified figure, dressed head to toe in black, is
filmed reaching the open rear hatch of the transport SUV in the Terryl Farnsworth camera angle
([Black-Clad Figure — Transport SUV](./Black_Clad_Figure_Transport_SUV)); who that person is has not been established. Palm-gun and second-shooter threads (e.g. high-engagement October 2025 Project Constitution posts) are **unverified video interpretations**; only Robinson is charged in the public case discussed here. We attribute allegations; we
do not assert any living person committed the assassination. Whether anyone beyond Robinson was ever seriously
examined is itself contested: former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said on the record
that his center's parallel search for other actors — specifically foreign ties — was ordered to stop once

## Charlie Kirk: The Threat He Said Actually Got His Attention

Speaking on stage before September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk drew a line between two kinds of people who hated him. The stranger on a campus who wanted him dead, he said, did not bother him. The ones who did were the "close proximity" people — the ones he already knew, who had some sort of relationship with him, and who he says had betrayed or deceived him. Clip surfaced by [@NihiloX](https://x.com/NihiloX/status/2089076950174158960) on X, quoting [@unhealthytruth](https://x.com/unhealthytruth/status/2088853084998865178).
