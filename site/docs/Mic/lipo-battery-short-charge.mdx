# LiPO Short Charge / Battery Runaway — Non-PETN Mic Failure

:::caution Attributed theory, not a finding
Nothing on this page establishes that a battery failed on stage, that any living person sabotaged a pack, or that a rifle shot did not occur. This page separates one **mechanism hypothesis** — catastrophic failure of the [RØDE Wireless PRO](./rode-wireless-mic) **lithium-polymer cell** — from the **military high-explosive** stack. Official accounts still describe a rooftop gunshot. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted.
:::

Not every “exploding mic” post means **PETN in a shaped cone**. A large strand of citizen analysis argues the pack’s **own LiPO battery** went into **thermal runaway** — sometimes framed as an **internal short**, a **damaged cell**, or a **“short charge” / abuse condition** — venting hot electrolyte, metal, and **PCB fragments** under the shirt. That story can be true, false, or mixed with a rifle shot; it is forensically **different** from a [high-explosive micro-charge](./high-explosive).

## Why this fork exists

Proponents of a body-worn energy event still need a **physical source**. Two incompatible fills get proposed for the same RØDE envelope:

| Mechanism | What fails | What a lab would look for | Common public sources |
|-----------|------------|---------------------------|------------------------|
| **LiPO runaway / short** | Cell + housing | Fluorinated salts, copper/aluminum melt, PCB solder, no organic HE | [@ZREIKMIESTER](https://x.com/ZREIKMIESTER) diagrams; battery-path posts |
| **PETN / RDX micro-charge** | Intentional fill + initiator | Organic HE on fabric/soil; possible liner metal | AES / DoD timeline; pager analogies |

Diagrams recirculated in July 2026 claim a **molten LiPO path into the neck** and **battery / clip parts melted into polyester**. Those images appear on the [Mic overview](./overview). They are **illustrative claims**, not lab photographs of recovered fragments.

## How proponents describe the sequence

Attributed steps (composite of public posts, not a verified timeline):

1. A RØDE-class **belt / shirt transmitter** is on Kirk (see [hardware page](./rode-wireless-mic) and [AV under-shirt placement](./AV_Team_Mic_Setup)).
2. At the critical moment the **cell ruptures** — thermal runaway, internal short, or deliberate electrical abuse.
3. **Hot gas and solid fragments** exit the housing; fabric [puffs outward](./mic-explosion-video-analysis); black plastic [shards fall](/Videos/Vid_Exploding_Microphone/Vid_Microphone_Footage/Vid_Project_Constitution_Black_Shards_2084239).
4. Debris later appears as [glass-like shards and a burnt object](./suv-transporting-charlie) in the transport vehicle; the charred item is sometimes claimed as a [melted transmitter](./burnt-object-rode-transmitter).

Some posters explicitly reject the need for [AES](./AES) or a [DoD contract](./DoD_Contract): a commercial pack already contains stored chemical energy. Others argue the cell was **pre-damaged or over-discharged** so it would fail on cue — still a sabotage claim, still **unproven**.

## Counter-arguments in public discussion

- **Energy scale.** Automated and human replies on X (including threads citing rifle kinetic energy vs pack watt-hours) argue a consumer LiPO **cannot** deposit a .30-06-class wound. Proponents reframe the claim as **fragments + local overpressure**, not pure joule equivalence.
- **Smoke / flash.** Battery fires often produce **smoke and flame**; skeptics who already use the [shaped-charge demo](./Demo_of_shape_charge) against PETN also ask why no clear battery fireball appears. Proponents point to under-shirt confinement and short duration.
- **Official narrative.** The charging documents and public medical framing remain a **gunshot**. Battery failure is a citizen hypothesis until residue and device custody exist.

## What would settle this fork

Cheap tests (none publicly released as of the notes in this investigation file):

- **HPLC / organic HE panel** on shirt, necklace, SUV carpet → supports or kills PETN/RDX path ([residue on fabric](./explosive-residue)).
- **SEM-EDS / ion chromatography** for LiPO electrolyte markers and melted copper on recovered fragments.
- **Transmitter recovery** with serial numbers and battery state-of-charge logs from the event kit ([table jumpers / missing device](./Table_Jumpers_Device)).
- **Raw multi-channel audio** ([Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Audio)) compared to known LiPO vent spectra vs HE overpressure.

Until then, “the mic exploded” is a **slogan covering at least two mechanisms**. This page holds the battery/short-charge half so the [high explosive](./high-explosive) page is not forced to absorb it.
