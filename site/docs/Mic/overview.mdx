concealed a small explosive — not a rooftop .30-06 — as the primary kill mechanism. Start with
the [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index), then follow video, audio, procurement, or
post-event debris depending on your question.

The exploding-mic thread matters because it attacks the government's physical premise directly:
if Charlie died from a **stage-origin detonation**, [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s attributed rooftop shot
becomes narratively optional. [Jon Bray's video work](./mic-explosion-video-analysis) argues shirt
fabric expands **before** the neck wound with **spherical** not rifle-vector motion; the
[Canon XA55](./Canon_XA55_Audio) recording reportedly separates a **120 m muzzle blast** from a
**louder 46 m tent detonation**; [Rob O'Neill](./rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) and Gen. [Blaine
Holt](/People/blaine-holt) told NEWSMAX they saw an **explosion**, not a rifle wound signature. June 2026
[SUV interior photos](./suv-transporting-charlie) added fragment and reported glass-debris readings
investigators tie to a fragmented transmitter.

Substance clusters into several paths. **Hardware and placement:** [Phillip Goldsberry](./AV_Team_Mic_Setup)
reportedly routed the mic **under** the shirt — unlike prior TPUSA outdoor events.
**Mechanism forks:** military [high explosive](./high-explosive) vs commercial [LiPO short / battery runaway](./lipo-battery-short-charge)
vs [crucifix pendant](./exploding-pendant-shaped-charge) vs [in-ear IEM](./exploding-earpiece-iem) — compared in part under [Mic vs Pendant](./Mic_vs_Pendant).
**Immediate aftermath:** [table jumpers](./Table_Jumpers_Device) and [chest-shot witnesses](./Chest_Shot_Witnesses)
raise chain-of-custody and scripted-narrative questions (framed as hypotheses).
**Procurement:** the [April 2025 AES contract](./DoD_Contract) for miniaturized anti-personnel
charges and the [October factory explosion](./aes-explosion-factory) sit on the same timeline as
the [Aug 25 Heber–Nashville courier flight](./aes-heber-nashville-flight). **Skeptic balance:** [shaped-charge demo](./Demo_of_shape_charge)
documents smoke-signature and geometry objections. **Ordering argument:** the August 2026
[six forensic physics phenomena](./six-forensic-physics-phenomena) thread tries to bind the
strands into one sequence — heat, then radial expansion in fabric, then the wound, then the
necklace's final position.

Read [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index) first, then [Video](./mic-explosion-video-analysis)
+ [Canon audio](./Canon_XA55_Audio) if you want physics, [SUV photos](./suv-transporting-charlie)
for post-transport debris, and [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) to connect to the
Tyler Robinson frame. [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) list explosive-residue
and AES disclosure as leverage for answers not yet public.


## Explosive Residue Coverup

If a [high-explosive](./high-explosive) charge detonated on Charlie Kirk's chest, it left
[**explosive residue**](./explosive-residue) — on fabric, on skin, on
soil, on the SUV's upholstery, on a necklace that sat on his chest. One swab would confirm or kill the
theory outright. Item by item, the things that could still carry that residue were reportedly seized,
hauled away, sealed under pavers, cleaned, resold, or destroyed, and **no public record shows any of
them being tested**. Section hub:
[Removing Explosive Residue Cover-Up](./Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview).

Each of these steps has an ordinary explanation on its own — collecting bloodied clothing and towing a
vehicle are routine in any homicide investigation, and evidence inventories in a pending capital case
are not normally public. **No wrongdoing by any agency, any member of the security detail, or
[TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) is established here.** What the section documents is narrower and harder to
dismiss: the **absence of a single released residue result** for any item, and the fact that the
[Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) and [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s defense
are currently the only two mechanisms that could compel one.

In August 2026, [@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2084239546867130638) published a slow-motion, frame-by-frame analysis of the shooting footage in which **you can see tiny black plastic shards falling from underneath Charlie's shirt** as he collapses — small dark fragments dropping from his left side, near his belt line, in line with the neck wound. The poster identifies the shards as shrapnel from the [RØDE wireless microphone transmitter](./rode-wireless-mic) on Charlie's belt and argues the pattern shows "an explosive force radiating upward from the device, not an external projectile entering from a rooftop" — in their framing, a [PETN detonation](./high-explosive) disguised as audio equipment rather than a rifle shot.

These are the poster's attributed claims, not findings of fact; the official account remains a rooftop rifle shot. But the clip sits squarely on the thread the rest of this section documents: the falling debris matches the [black shards photographed on the SUV floor](./suv-transporting-charlie), the [explosive residue](./explosive-residue) question, the [burnt object recovered from the SUV](./burnt-object-rode-transmitter), and the fabric motion in the [video frame analysis](./mic-explosion-video-analysis).
