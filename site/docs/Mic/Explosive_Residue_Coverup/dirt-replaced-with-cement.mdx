# Landscaping Crew Replaced Dirt With Cement / Pavers

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Installing pavers or concrete after a campus homicide is not, by itself, a crime. This page documents **reported urgency, authority, and residue implications** of hardscaping the tent courtyard. No finding of obstruction is made against any contractor, UVU employee, or federal agent.
:::

Once the [soil was gone](./dirt-hauled-immediately), the next step sealed the pad: a **landscaping / hardscape crew** put **pavers** (in online shorthand often called **cement** or **concrete**) over the area where [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) had been sitting. That is a **different act** from the haul-away — different crew phase, different residual surface, different open records target (work orders and invoices rather than dump manifests).

## What was installed

Public photos and campus observation show the former grass courtyard under the event tent converted to a **hard paved surface** within days of September 10, 2025. Circulating language varies:

- **"Pavers"** — Dan Merrell's term; interlocking units on a prepared base.
- **"Concrete pad" / "re-poured concrete"** — shorthand used by [Alex Jones](https://x.com/RealAlexJones/status/2057879916025376990) and many X threads.
- **"Slapped down a concrete pad"** — viral construction-company posts in the investigation file.

For residue purposes the distinction is secondary: **porous blood-soaked soil is gone and a permanent hard surface sits where citizen investigators wanted to re-sample**.

## Merrell's "paver emergency"

On **Sunday, September 14, 2025**, Merrell says he received an unexpected **"paver emergency"** call. Elements that appear again and again in the clips:

- Caller had **"never done pavers before."**
- Deadline **"by Monday"** attributed by on-site coordinators to the **Governor of Utah** and the **FBI**.
- **"We're not talking to the media."**
- Merrell assembled a crew within ~90 minutes and worked Sunday — noted as culturally unusual in heavily LDS Utah County.
- He arrived to find **~10 inches already excavated** by a prior team ([dirt page](./dirt-hauled-immediately)).

Primary write-ups: [Daniel Merrell — Hardscape Utah](/Tent/Daniel_Merrell_Hardscape_Utah), [Paving Timeline Sept 14](/Tent/Paving_Timeline_Sept_14), [FBI Crime Scene Paving](/FBI/Crime_Scene_Paving).

## Residue and cover-up framing

Citizen investigators put the hardscape on the residue checklist because:

1. **Irreversibility.** You cannot re-swab the original sod after pavers are down.
2. **Speed.** "Within days" / "by Monday" is far faster than ordinary capital-case scene holds in the public imagination (whether or not the scene was legally "released").
3. **Authority opacity.** Lead investigator **David Hull** reportedly testified he was **not consulted** and learned of the paving from **news** — a process failure that amplifiers treat as intentional.
4. **Same package as soil dig.** Owens-aligned commentary presents dig + pave as one **PETN cleanup** program; Merrell's "ordinary over-excavation for base" is the benign half of the same physical work.

Security head commentary amplified by [@MJTruthUltra](https://x.com/MJTruthUltra) urged FOIA on **who ordered the tear-up** and when — including pushback against trap-door rumors. The demand for work orders stands independent of any device theory.

## Ordinary explanations

- **Campus reopening and trauma mitigation** after a mass-attended killing.
- **Biohazard + aesthetics**: grass replacement vs hardscape is a facilities choice.
- **Scene release**: once investigators release property, the owner (UVU) controls construction.
- **Proper paver engineering** really does require base depth — Merrell says so himself.
- Photos of a finished courtyard **prove alteration**, not motive.

## What has not been established

- That any contractor was told to hide explosive residue.
- That Governor [Spencer Cox](/People/spencer-cox) or any FBI official personally ordered the pavers for improper reasons (the Merrell-relayed quote is secondhand).
- That Tyler Robinson's family company performed the work.
- That the official rifle account is false.

## Open questions

2. Whether **any forensic clearance memo** authorized alteration.
3. Pre/post photos with timestamps and UVU camera coverage of the install.
4. Whether [Fix laws](/Fix/overview) would compel production of facilities records and federal tasking emails.

According to @TarynEllez7, **Judicial Watch** published an article covering **GRAMA (open records) requests** about the paving work at UVU, and the released material bears directly on this page. The video reads the records aloud. The claims below are hers and are reproduced as reported; the underlying documents should be checked against the Judicial Watch release itself.

**The original plan was reportedly to wash, not to dig.** She reports a **September 12, 2025** text exchange — two days after the shooting — in which one person writes: *"The cops just called me into the fountain area to get them some water. We're not gonna need to cut out any soil."* The reply: *"Okay great news. Let me know and I'll pass it along."* If accurate, this indicates that as of Sept 12 **no soil removal was planned at all** — which would place the [10-inch excavation](./dirt-hauled-immediately) and the Sunday **Sept 14** paving as a decision made *after* that point, by someone not identified in the texts.

The same exchange, as read in the video, continues with what she describes as discussion of remaining material on the ground — *"there's still a ... eight inch puddle ish"* — followed by *"the solution to pollution is dilution"*, *"I'll just grab a hose and spray it"*, and a deferral: *"no, tomorrow, the FBI is still there, I guess."* She also reads **Sept 14** messages attributed to the paver crew: *"my guys are well trained in keeping quiet,"* and an exchange about UVU offering **bright red barriers** that were declined in favor of *"any other color."* This is consistent with the *"we're not talking to the media"* instruction already reported by Dan Merrell above.

**The invoice says "crime scene cleanup" — and UVU reportedly did not pay it.** She states the GRAMA response included the **invoice for the courtyard paving work**, whose description line reads **"crime scene cleanup"**, made out to **Utah Valley University**. She further reports that UVU's position was that it had **no invoices to produce** for the courtyard work **because it did not pay for it**. If that holds up, it raises the question this page has been asking under Open Questions: *who authorized and who funded the alteration of the site?* She also notes a records entry showing a **call with the Governor's office on September 15** at 3 p.m., the day after the paving.

The Utah Attorney General's office (attributed in the video to **Nicole Ferguson**) is described as the responder to the records requests.

**The second half of the video covers the September 9 warnings** — GOP representatives who reportedly presented themselves at UVU as advance staff for two senators, an internal message reading *"[redacted] wants us to get a message to Charlie Kirk"* with his phone number, and the 11:19 PM walk-back identifying the two men as *"GOP reps who played like they knew more than they did."* That material is covered in detail on [UVU Sept 9 Foreknowledge](/Proof_Intel_Services/UVU_Sept_9_Foreknowledge).

Nothing in these records, as read, establishes that anyone was instructed to destroy evidence. The wash-not-dig text is equally consistent with an early, informal assessment that was later overruled by investigators for ordinary forensic or biohazard reasons. What the records do establish, if authentic, is a **documentary trail** — texts, an invoice, and a payer — that FOIA and GRAMA can be pushed further on.
