This is the first courtroom date in the case brought after Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University on September 10 2025 — six days earlier — and long before the [dispute over what actually caused his death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) reached the public record.

## What happened

Six days after the **09/10/2025** shooting of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview), Robinson made his **first court appearance**. Reporting describes a **video-feed** appearance with the court ordering him held **without bail** on **aggravated murder** and related felonies with **capital exposure**. Prosecutors publicly indicated intent to seek the **death penalty**.

Public charging narratives (not proven facts) allege a rooftop shot from the [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) with a **Mauser-pattern .30-06** recovered in a wooded area, DNA and digital-message links, and post-arrest communications. The defense has not conceded those allegations; Robinson remains **presumed innocent**.

## People and institutions in the room (as reported)

* **Bench:** [Judge Tony F. Graf Jr.](/court/judge-tony-graf) — recently sworn after [Robert Lunnen](/court/judge-robert-lunnen)'s retirement.
* **Prosecution (Utah County):** [Jeffrey S. Gray](/People/jeffrey-s-gray), deputies including [Chad E. Grunander](/People/chad-e-grunander), [Christopher D. Ballard](/People/christopher-d-ballard), [Ryan McBride](/People/ryan-mcbride), [David Sturgill](/People/david-sturgill), [Lauren Hunt](/People/lauren-hunt).
* **Defense:** Capital counsel later led by [Kathryn Nester](/People/kathryn-nester) with [Michael Burt](/People/michael-burt), [Richard G. Novak](/People/richard-g-novak), [Staci Visser](/People/staci-visser) (appointments/reporting evolved after this date).
* **Custody:** [Utah County Jail](/Locations/Utah_County_Jail) (Spanish Fork), Booking **#460956**.

## Why this day matters

1. It is the **start of the public adversarial case** after the arrest/surrender sequence of **09/11–09/12/2025**.
2. **No-bail** capital posture sets the stakes for every later [discovery](../discovery-brady-disputes), [gag](../gag-orders-sealing), and [preliminary hearing](../Preliminary_Hearing/overview) fight.
3. Early charging documents and probable-cause materials become the baseline later compared to hearing testimony (screwdriver descriptions, text-message versions, etc.).

## Public / X commentary themes (attributed)

Citizen investigators and press repeatedly return to **09/16** as the day the state's written theory froze into charging form — including disputes over whether the **screwdriver** and certain **text versions** appear as expected in the first papers. Treat commentary as **attribution**, not findings.
