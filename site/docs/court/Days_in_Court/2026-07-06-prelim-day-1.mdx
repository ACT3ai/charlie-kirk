## What happened

Week-long **probable-cause** hearing opens. Appearances, camera/decorum protocol, [Erika Kirk](/People/erika-kirk) present. State opens with four planned law-enforcement witnesses.

### Witnesses on the stand (Day 1)

* **[Chris Bagley](/People/chris-bagley)** (Spanish Fork PD; former UVU) — heard the shot ~**12:23 p.m.**; reached [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) roof ~**12:44 p.m.**; described disturbed gravel/"sniper pad," screwdriver, shoe print; empty pistol holster in grass; **body-worn camera** stopped recording (battery account). Cross on security posture and holster.
* **[David Hull](/People/david-hull)** (SBI / Utah DPS case agent) — begins authentication of [UVU surveillance](/cameras/UVU_Surveillance), eyewitness/Ring video, and [medical-examiner](/Medical/trial_and_autopsy_report) materials; in-court identification of Robinson for prelim purposes. Testimony continues Day 2.

### Other people in the record

* Prosecution: [Chad Grunander](/People/chad-e-grunander), [Ryan McBride](/People/ryan-mcbride), [David Sturgill](/People/david-sturgill), [Lauren Hunt](/People/lauren-hunt); [Jeff Gray](/People/jeffrey-s-gray) / [Chris Ballard](/People/christopher-d-ballard) present.
* Defense: [Kathryn Nester](/People/kathryn-nester), [Richard Novak](/People/richard-g-novak), [Michael Burt](/People/michael-burt), [Staci Visser](/People/staci-visser).
* Case agents permitted to remain: Hull and [Brian Davis](/People/brian-davis).
