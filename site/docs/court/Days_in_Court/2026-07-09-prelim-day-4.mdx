## What happened

### [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) — recorded interview (not live stand)

April-recorded video played: roommate/romantic partner. Alleged remorse ("wishes he hadn't done it"); claims he had **not** heard Robinson talk about Kirk specifically before the shooting; politics/Trump talk only in general. Twiggs' counsel publicly says he **cooperated**. Limited-immunity commentary exists on this site — cooperation is **not** proof of crime by Twiggs.

### Texts and note (via [Brian Davis](/People/brian-davis) / exhibits)

Alleged **09/10–09/11** thread: "look under my keyboard"; note claiming opportunity to "take out Charlie Kirk"; "you weren't the one who did it right????" / "I am, I'm sorry." Defense challenges authentication and publicity of [confession materials](../discord-evidence-court).

### [Jennifer Faumuina](/People/jennifer-faumuina) (return)

Wooded-area rifle: one spent + three unfired engraved rounds; home Dremel and "TEST SHOT" casing; ATF toolmark links on casings/Dremel as reported.

### [Samantha Karner](/People/samantha-karner) (ATF) — defense call

Bullet jacket fragment from autopsy **cannot be identified or excluded** as from the seized rifle — **inconclusive** for lack of microscopic marks. Central to [ballistics](../ballistics-atf-cbla) defense theme.
