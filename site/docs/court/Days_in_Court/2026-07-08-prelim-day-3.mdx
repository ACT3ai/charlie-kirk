## What happened

Afternoon start (**1 p.m.** per pool reporters). State calls **[Brian Davis](/People/brian-davis)** (SBI):

* Robinson's **09/11/2025** surrender at [Washington County Sheriff's Office](/Locations/Washington_County_Sheriff) with parents and family friend **[Mike Mitchell](/People/mike-mitchell)** (~**9 p.m.** arrival as Davis was told; Davis arrived later).
* Booking into [Utah County Jail](/Locations/Utah_County_Jail); warrants; interviews of parents and Mitchell; Mitchell's later **handwritten Rule 1102** statement (**03/31** meeting reported in press).
* [Lance Twiggs](/People/lance-twiggs) interviewed twice; foundation for recorded interview.
* Rooftop **.223** round discussion.

### Legal fight of the day

Defense ([Richard Novak](/People/richard-g-novak)) vs. prosecution ([Lauren Hunt](/People/lauren-hunt)) over **publishing** Twiggs' recorded **Rule 1102** interview and associated [Discord/texts](../discord-evidence-court) to cameras — fair-trial vs. transparency (*Estes v. Texas* arguments). [Jeffrey Neiman](/People/jeffrey-neiman) (Kirk family) presses for public evidence display.
