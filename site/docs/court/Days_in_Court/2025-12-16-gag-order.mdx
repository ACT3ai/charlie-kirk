## What happened

Reporting and this site's [Gag Orders & Sealing](../gag-orders-sealing) page document a **December 16, 2025** gag order issued **on the court's own motion**. Public accounts describe a **broad** restriction on extrajudicial statements by lawyers, and commentary about knock-on effects for **witnesses** and media coverage of [State v. Robinson](../case-overview).

The order became a permanent backdrop for 2026 events: the **June 2026** civil **contempt** finding against [Christopher Ballard](/People/christopher-d-ballard) for public comments on case strength after the **inconclusive ATF** publicity, and repeated fights over what exhibits could be **broadcast** during the July preliminary hearing.

## Why investigators watch this day

1. It is the legal hinge between **open public debate** and **court-controlled speech** about the assassination prosecution.
2. Citizen investigators argue the gag chilled potential witnesses; the court frames it as **fair-trial** protection. Document both claims; do not treat either as proven misconduct.
