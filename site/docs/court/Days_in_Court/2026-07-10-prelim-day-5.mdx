## What happened

Final day of the week-long prelim.

### [Caitlin Oliver](/People/caitlin-oliver) (ATF DNA section chief)

DNA statistics on rifle samples — press reports **≥1 trillion** times more likely Robinson contributed than not (lab max statistic). Defense ([Michael Burt](/People/michael-burt)) elicits that policies bar calling DNA "infallible" / "zero error rate," and that **transfer** via third party is possible. [Chad Grunander](/People/chad-e-grunander) argues the court has already heard **overwhelming** evidence for probable cause.

### Closing of evidence

State rests. Robinson **does not testify**. Court sets **bind-over briefing** with **oral argument 09/01/2026**. Kirk family statement: process "important step," evidence "overwhelming," continue transparency prayer. Family had pushed mid-week for unredacted public exhibits.

### Emotional moment

Surveillance of figure on roof played more openly for gallery at family request; [Erika Kirk](/People/erika-kirk) and Charlie's mother react visibly (AP/ABC pool).

## What the hearing does **not** decide

Probable cause only — **not** guilt. Full trial (if bound over) would re-litigate forensics, [Miranda/Discord timing](../mirandize/mirandize-overview), and alternative leads under higher stakes.
