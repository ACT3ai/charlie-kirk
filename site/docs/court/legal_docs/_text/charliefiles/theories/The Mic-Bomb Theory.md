# The Mic-Bomb Theory

> **The claim:** Someone on Kirk's team detonated a "mic-bomb" by **grabbing his shirt** — the shot was an inside job triggered up close.

**Verdict: FALSE / UNSUPPORTED.**

## What fuels it
A downstream elaboration of [[The Microphone Exploded|the exploding-mic theory]], built entirely on **slow-motion readings of low-quality video** — a hand near Kirk's shirt read as "triggering" a device.

## What the evidence shows (the primary record)
- There is **no explosive residue and no forensic finding** of any device or blast anywhere in the case file.
- The same primary evidence that, **on the current record**, contradicts the exploding-mic theory contradicts this one: the **medical examiner's gunshot-wound finding** ([[2025-09-17 - The Search Warrant Affidavit|The Search Warrant Affidavit]]), the recovered **[[The Rifle|rifle]]** and **[[The Engraved Casings|casings]]**, and the **bullet fragment recovered at autopsy**. See [[The Casing Match and DNA]].
- The "grab" is a video-interpretation artifact, not evidence of a mechanism.

## If the defense raised it
> Like the exploding-mic theory, this would go **to innocence** if a jury believed it — but it requires the same step of discrediting the autopsy, the weapon, and the casings, and **on the current record** nothing supports it. If the defense later put on an expert who could impeach that evidence, that would have to be weighed; the hearing is ongoing, but **as the record stands** it has nothing to stand on. See [[Insufficiency of the Evidence]].

## Bottom line
A theory stacked on a theory. Once the gunshot is established by the autopsy and the recovered weapon — and it is — the mic-bomb elaboration has nothing left to stand on.
