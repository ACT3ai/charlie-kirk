# Legal Documents — State v. Robinson

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
**Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted**, and is **presumed innocent**. Nothing on this page states as fact that any living person committed a crime, destroyed evidence, or acted improperly. Court filings in this case are subject to a **gag order** and **sealing**, so the public record is incomplete by design.
:::

## What This Directory Is

**Legal Documents** is the primary-source layer under [Court & Trial](/court/overview). Everything else in the court section — the [day pages](/court/Days_in_Court/overview), the [witness roster](/court/people-on-witness-stand), the [AI defense attorney](/court/ai_attorney/overview) exercise — is written from press accounts, pool reporting and transcripts. This directory is where the **filings themselves** go, so that any claim made elsewhere can be checked against the document rather than against a report of the document.

Case: **State of Utah v. Tyler James Robinson**, Case No. **251403576**, Fourth Judicial District Court, Provo Department, before the **Honorable Tony F. Graf Jr.**

## Documents Obtained

These are hosted on this site and can be opened directly.

### Defendant's Motion To Preserve The Bullet Jacket Fragment

**April 2026 · 29 pages · defense filing**

Full title, as captioned: *Defendant's Motion For An Order Prohibiting Any Further Alteration Or Testing Of A Bullet Jacket Fragment Pending The Outcome Of This Motion, And Permitting A Defense Expert To Examine And Photograph This Evidence Before It Is Further Altered Or Destroyed, Or, In The Alternative, Permitting Either Defense Expert Observation Of Any Further Alteration Or Testing Of This Evidence, Or Video Recording Of Such Alteration And Testing, and Memorandum In Support.*

Signed by all four counsel of record: **Kathryn N. Nester** (Nester Lewis PLLC, Salt Lake City), **Richard G. Novak** (Pasadena, pro hac vice), **Michael N. Burt** (San Francisco, pro hac vice), and **Staci Visser** (Brown, Bradshaw & Moffat, Salt Lake City).

The motion is brought under **Utah Rule of Criminal Procedure 16** and the Sixth, Eighth and Fourteenth Amendments, plus Article I sections 7, 9 and 12 of the Utah Constitution. It asks for three things in order: a temporary order stopping further testing of the fragment; an order letting a defense expert examine and photograph it before it is further altered or destroyed; and, failing that, defense observation or video recording of any further testing.

This is the single most important document currently hosted here. It shows the defense moving directly on the [inconclusive ATF comparison](/court/ballistics-atf-cbla) and on the risk that a fragment is consumed by an examination the defense never sees.

**[Open the PDF](/court/filings/tyler-robinson-ballistics-motion.pdf)** · source: Fox News document host

### Redacted Transcript Of Sealed Motion Hearing

**October 24, 2025 · 97 pages · court transcript**

Certified transcript of a **sealed motion hearing** before Judge Graf at the Fourth District Court, 137 North Freedom Boulevard, Provo. Reported by Phoebe S. Moorhead, RDR, CRR, Utah CSR, for Noteworthy Reporting LLC. Released in redacted form.

This is one of the few windows into the sealed side of the docket in the first months of the case, and it predates the [December 2025 gag order](/court/Days_in_Court/2025-12-16-gag-order).

## How Documents Are Stored In This Repository

Two locations, and they are not interchangeable:

* **The PDF itself** goes in `site/internals/static/court/filings/`, which Docusaurus copies verbatim into the build. It is then served at `/court/filings/FILENAME.pdf` and can be linked from any page.
* **The page describing it** goes in `site/docs/court/legal_docs/`, which is this directory.

A PDF placed under `site/docs/` is **not** served and will 404 for every real visitor while appearing to work locally.

## Honest Statement Of Completeness

Two documents out of a docket approaching a year old is a start, not a collection. The gap is not for want of trying: the Utah County Attorney host was down at the time of the attempt, DocumentCloud blocks scripted access behind Cloudflare, and the authoritative route — Utah XChange — is a subscription service that has to be worked by a person.

The list above is written so that anyone can pick up a row and close it.
