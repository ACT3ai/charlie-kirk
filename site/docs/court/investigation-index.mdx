## Highest-signal court disputes

1. **ATF inconclusive** on autopsy bullet jacket vs seized Mauser — defense cites as exculpatory; prosecution still pursues capital charges ([ballistics page](./ballistics-atf-cbla)).
2. **Discovery stall** — FBI/ATF files since September 2025; reported 600k-document dump; defense continuance vs prosecution push to hearing ([discovery page](./discovery-brady-disputes)).
3. **Gag order Dec 16, 2025** — issued on court's own motion per reporting; June 2026 **Ballard contempt** for media statements ([gag page](./gag-orders-sealing)).
4. **Miranda Sept 11 vs Sept 12** — Bates **003996-R2** at **6:25 PM**; Discord **~7:57 PM** ([mirandize hub](./mirandize/mirandize-overview)).
5. **March 10, 2026 seal motion** — media coalition opposes sealing ATF-related defense filing.
