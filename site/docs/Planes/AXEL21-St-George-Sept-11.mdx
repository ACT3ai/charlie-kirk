On 2 September 2026, [John Cullen](/Influencers/overview) posted a Google Earth render of an aircraft
track over southwest Utah with the caption: *"As Tyler Robinson was surrendering, at 9 pm at the
Washington County Sheriff's station, near St. George, Utah.. ...HADES AXEL21 was doing this."*

**The track is real, and this site's own primary archives confirm it.** **The 9 PM surrender time in the
caption is not.** [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) was in Washington County Sheriff's Office
custody and had been read his rights at **6:25 PM** that evening — or earlier — which is now in the
court record. What was happening around 9 PM was two other things: **this aircraft**, and the
**Discord messages** the state's narrative attributes to Robinson.

## The correction: 6:25 PM, not 9 PM

Robinson **surrendered at 6:25 PM on September 11, 2025, or before**. The source is the court file,
not a social-media claim: **page 70 of a defense filing quotes discovery Bates 003996-R2 at
timestamp 03:36:53, showing Miranda read at 06:25 PM and counsel invoked one minute later at
06:26 PM**. A separate discovery index row — "Video at WCSO on 9/11/2025", Bates 3996-R1 — is the
recording that timestamp comes from. The full documentation of that filing is on
[September 11 Turn-In Testimony](/Tyler_Robinson/discord/september-11-turn-in-testimony), and the
custody question is worked through on [the 6:25 PM custody
timeline](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession) and the
[Mirandize section](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview).

The 9 PM figure is the **federal** framing, not the local record. It descends from the
**33-hour manhunt** number, which counted back from the shooting lands near **9:23 PM on September 11**,
and from surrender-arranging calls reported at **8:02 PM and 8:04 PM** — hours after Robinson had
already been Mirandized in the building the calls were supposedly arranging his arrival at. That
conflict is laid out on [Discord & Custody Timeline](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Discord_Custody_Timeline).

**Cullen's picture does not need the 9 PM figure.** Correcting it does not weaken the post — it
sharpens it, because it separates three things that were all happening in the same two-hour window
and were not the same event.

## What 9 PM actually was

By 9 PM on September 11, 2025:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tyler Robinson** | Already in WCSO custody roughly 2½ hours, Mirandized at **6:25 PM**, counsel invoked at **6:26 PM**, phone seized per county protocol |
| **The Discord messages** | Confession traffic from account **zealous_monkey_55095** timestamped **7:57 PM**, and continuing traffic through the evening |
| **AXEL21 / [N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview)** | Flying the track in the picture — **8:28 PM to 9:15 PM MDT**, over southwest Utah |

**The Discord conversation was not written by Tyler Robinson.** He had been in a sheriff's facility for
hours by the time those messages posted, with his phone in custody. Whoever was typing from that
account that evening, it was not the person the account is attributed to. This site does not name who
it was — nobody has established that, and no forensic extraction of the seized electronics was offered
at the preliminary stage. What is established is the **exclusion**: the timeline rules him out. See
[Discord Messages](/Tyler_Robinson/Discord_Messages) and
[Discord & Custody Timeline](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Discord_Custody_Timeline).

**The plane is the part of Cullen's post that survives the correction intact**, and it is the part worth
the work.

## The track, checked against primary data

This is not a case where a render has to be taken on trust. **AXEL21 is the callsign this investigation
already catalogues against tail [N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview)** — ICAO hex **a1bbe5** — a Bombardier
Global 6500 registered to [LASAI Aviation II LLC](/Planes/LASAI-Fleet/overview), the fleet-mate of
[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) (callsign AXLE10), the [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) ISR jet
that made the low passes near UVU on 10 September. This site holds **recovered ADS-B traces for that hex
on that date from two independent archives**, adsb.lol and airplanes.live, pulled during the
[flight-data recovery effort](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview).

**Both archives carry the flight, and they agree to the second and to four decimal places of position.**
The render's burned-in timestamp lines up: at **8:41:37 PM MDT** the traces put the aircraft at
**37.60 N, 113.61 W** at **13,050 ft**, descending on a 030° track — the northeast lobe of the ribbon in
the picture. The picture is a faithful playback of a real track.

### What the aircraft actually did

The archives say something more specific than "was doing this", and it is worth stating precisely,
because the specific version is stronger than the vague one.

| Time (MDT, 11 Sep 2025) | What the position data shows |
|---|---|
| 6:39 PM | **Lands at Ontario, California** (KONT), touching down on the runway centreline |
| 6:39 – 8:00 PM | On the ground at Ontario, about **80 minutes** |
| 8:00 PM | Departs Ontario |
| 8:28 PM | Crossing into Nevada at 20,000 ft, tracking **030°** — northeast, toward Utah |
| 8:41 PM | **37.60 N, 113.61 W at 13,050 ft** — the render's timestamp |
| 8:45 – 8:53 PM | Turns back southwest and descends through the Cedar City corridor |
| 8:57 PM | Passing the Hurricane / Washington area at **5,575 ft**, about **2,600 ft above the ground**, at **127 knots**, on a steady **210°** line |
| 8:59 – 9:00 PM | **Levels at 4,050 ft — 1,109 ft above the field — at 123 knots**, tracking 201°, on the extended centreline of **runway 19** |
| 9:00:45 PM | **Goes around.** Adds power and climbs away without landing |
| 9:00:53 PM | Crosses the **runway 19 threshold at 0.08 km** — directly overhead — already climbing through 4,325 ft |
| 9:07 PM | Passes back over the Hurricane area at **21,525 ft**, climbing |
| 9:15 PM | Through 35,500 ft southeast-bound, accelerating away toward Arizona |

**It flew one instrument approach to [St. George Regional Airport](/Planes/Airports/KSGU) at 9 PM, to
about a thousand feet above the runway, and did not land.** Closest approach to the airport reference
point: **0.10 km**. It then left to the southeast and was near **Biggs Army Airfield**, its home field,
the following afternoon.

### The detour is real, and it is large

Ontario, California to Biggs Army Airfield at El Paso is **1,075 km** direct. Routed via St. George it is
**1,368 km** — **293 km and 27% further**. St. George is not on the way to anywhere this aircraft went
that night. **The leg into southwest Utah was flown on purpose.**

## What this does not show, stated at the same size

**The picture is a plane, not a purpose.** A trace proves presence. It never proves intent, and it never
proves occupancy — nobody has published a manifest for this leg, and nothing here places any person
aboard.

**"Orbit over the sheriff's station" is not what the data says.** The tight turn near the placemark in
the render is the descent and the go-around at the airport. The single closest pass to the Washington
County Sheriff's Office and the Purgatory Correctional Facility complex is at **21,525 ft** — four miles
straight up, on the climb-out. The aircraft was **low** near the sheriff's office, at roughly 2,600 ft
above ground, only on the way down the approach line, and that line goes to a runway. Reading the ribbon
as a loiter over the jail is reading the graphic, not the numbers.

**A single low approach at a quiet field at night is an ordinary thing for a crew to fly.** Practice and
currency approaches are routine, they are commonly flown at uncongested airports after dark, and a
repositioning leg is exactly when a crew would fit one in. That explanation covers the go-around
completely. What it covers less comfortably is the **293-kilometre detour** to reach the field, and the
date.

**The distances to the sheriff's complex are approximate.** The airport figures come from the
OurAirports database this investigation already holds — KSGU at 37.036389 N, 113.510306 W, field
elevation 2,941 ft, runway 19 threshold at 37.0481 N, 113.5041 W. The sheriff's-office figures use an
approximate position for the Hurricane complex and are not sourced from a case document. Altitudes are
barometric as transmitted, so the height-above-ground numbers carry the usual pressure-setting error.

**Nothing here is an accusation against any person.** Tyler Robinson is charged and is presumed innocent.
The Discord finding on this page runs in his favour, not against him.

## Why it matters anyway

Strip out the parts that do not hold and three things are left standing, all of them documented:

1. **Robinson was in custody and Mirandized at 6:25 PM**, from the court file.
2. **The Discord "confession" posted at 7:57 PM and after**, while he was in that building without his
   phone — so he did not write it.
3. **A HADES-fleet ISR jet flew 293 km out of its way to fly one approach to the St. George runway at
   9 PM that same night, went around, and left.**

Each of those is checkable independently. The middle one is the reason the case's central document is in
question. The third is the one Cullen found, and it is the one that no amount of correcting his caption
makes go away.
