**The claim:** on **April 29, 2025** — **134 days, more than four months, before Charlie Kirk was killed** — someone in the **Lincoln, Nebraska** area typed **Tyler Robinson's exact apartment address, including the unit number, into Google in Hebrew**. That week, an **Egyptian Air Force jet** was parked at Lincoln. The claim was made on the [Stew Peters Show](/Influencers/overview) and posted by [@realstewpeters](https://x.com/realstewpeters/status/2084745267962585553) on **4 August 2026**.

**One detail in the query is worth noticing.** Most of it is English sounded out in Hebrew letters — *sauth river ro'ad*, *sant jorg*. But the word for "apartment" is not sounded out. It is **דירה (*dira*)**, the ordinary Hebrew word. Someone merely using a Hebrew keyboard would spell "apartment" phonetically; someone who *thinks* in Hebrew writes *dira*. That is an observation about the text on screen, not proof of who typed it.

**And one about the other two terms.** Robinson's **name** in Hebrew reads **0** on April 29 in that market. Only the **address** registers. Whoever produced that spike was not looking him up by name; they already had the street, the number and the unit.

## The address, and what was known about it on April 29

* **Robinson and Twiggs were probably already living there.** Reporting cited on [Lance Twiggs' page](/People/lance-twiggs) says they lived together for "approximately one year before his arrest" — which puts the move around **September 2024**, before April 2025. No lease or move-in date has been published; the warrants establish residence only as of September 2025.
* **On April 29, 2025 Tyler Robinson was a private 22-year-old in St. George.** Nobody outside his own circle had a public reason to know his street, let alone his **unit number**. After September 12, 2025 the address was in court records. On April 29 it was not.
* **This is not the only claim of early interest in that address.** This investigation's case file carries an unsourced note that the River Road townhouse was "searched NONSTOP from Dec '24 to Sep '25" — see [Tyler Robinson and Lance Twiggs searches](/GoogleSearches/tyler-robinson-lance-twiggs-searches) and [Israel IP search patterns](/GoogleSearches/israel-ip-search-patterns). If that is right, April 29 is one point in a longer series, and the Lincoln spike stands out because of **where** and **in what language**, not because it is the first.
* **The same "Hebrew near the Egyptian jets" family** has two more dated entries, both in Nebraska: Hebrew searches for Erika Kirk's maiden name, *Erika Lane Frantzve*, on **July 11, 2025** ([Omaha, EXTRA-004](/Planes/following/overlap/20250711_NE_omaha_extra_004/overview)) and **July 24, 2025** (Omaha), when [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) was at Lincoln 20–26 July. Those are on [Hebrew-Language Searches and the Egyptian Jets](/GoogleSearches/hebrew-searches-erika-kirk-egyptian-jets). **April 29 is the earliest date in the family and the only one tied to Robinson's address.**

## Why this one matters

* **The date: April 29, 2025.** 134 days before the shooting at Utah Valley University. The day before SU-BTU left Lincoln for Delaware.
* **The dates line up exactly.** The guest's "inbound April 24, leaves April 30 for Delaware" is SU-BTU to the day, confirmed by four records she did not produce.
* **The language.** Hebrew is written almost nowhere but Israel. The word *dira* suggests a native speaker.
* **The place.** A Nebraska TV market with no large Hebrew-speaking population, during a six-day window when a foreign military jet had just flown in from Cairo via Paris.
* **The specificity.** Not a name — a street number and an apartment unit. The name alone reads zero.
* **The window.** The chart ends September 9, 2025. Nothing in it can be the news.

## What cuts against it — stated at the same size

* **Lincoln is where Egyptian Air Force jets go for maintenance.** [Duncan Aviation](https://www.duncanaviation.aero/locations/lnk)'s main plant is at Lincoln, it is a Dassault Falcon service centre, and it has held the Egyptian Air Force account since 1999. The [Lincoln arrival log](/Planes/following/Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview) shows **nine** Egyptian visits between February 2024 and October 2025 — SU-BTT, SU-BTU and SU-BTV — almost every one the same shape: Cairo → Paris → Omaha → Lincoln, six days, out to Wilmington, home to Cairo. SU-BTU at Lincoln on April 29 is that rotation doing what it always does. **It is the search that would be unusual, not the jet.**
* **Google Trends is a sample, and small numbers are noise.** Google says Trends uses a *sample* of searches and scales everything 0–100 within the chosen place and time. For rare terms in a small region the classic result is exactly this picture — flat zero with **one isolated spike** — and re-pulling the same query can move or erase it. See [The Conversation on misreading Trends](https://theconversation.com/how-misreading-google-trends-is-fuelling-bondi-attack-conspiracy-theories-272251), and this site's own [Trends re-pull](/GoogleSearches/nester-israel-trends-repull) and [Trends deletion](/GoogleSearches/trends-deletion-suppression) pages. A spike of 100 can be a handful of searches.
* **The region is a TV market, not an airport.** "Lincoln & Hastings-Kearney" spans much of Nebraska. A spike there says nothing about the ramp at KLNK.
* **Nothing ties the search to the aircraft.** No device, account, IP, subscriber record, crew list or passenger manifest connects it to SU-BTU or to anyone who flew on it. The guest herself calls it a hypothesis — "someone who may or may not have been on that jet."
* **Hebrew is not a nationality or a tasking order.** Israeli students, visitors and US residents who read Hebrew live in Nebraska too, and the University of Nebraska–Lincoln is in that market.
* **Nobody has swept Lincoln that week.** This investigation's area-wide ADS-B sweep covers event cities only; April 26–29 were never swept, and no Lincoln or Omaha circle exists for April 2025. **What else was on the ground at Lincoln that week is simply unknown.**
* **The segment belongs to a genre that has been wrong before.** Earlier "searched from Israel" Trends claims have been disputed and mocked on X. That does not make this one wrong, but it is why it has to be re-run before it is relied on.

## Check it yourself

Anyone can re-run the chart. It is the single most useful thing a reader can do with this page.

1. Open [Google Trends Explore](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore).
2. Add the three terms exactly as shown, **with the quotation marks**:
   * `"טיילר ג'יימס רובינסון"`
   * `"טיילר רובינסון סנט ג'ורג"`
   * `"סאות' ריבר רואד 3419, דירה 73, סנט ג'ורג"`
3. Region: **United States → Nebraska → Lincoln & Hastings-Kearney NE**.
4. Time: **Custom, 2025-01-01 to 2025-09-09**. Category: All. **Web Search**.
5. Hover the chart at **April 29, 2025**.
6. **Then run the controls.** Same three terms, same dates, but region set to **Nebraska** (whole state), to **Omaha NE**, and to **United States**. Then the **same address in English**. If the Hebrew address spikes only in the Lincoln market and only on April 29, that is a result. If it spikes all over the place, or not at all, that is also a result — publish either one.
7. Screenshot everything with the date and time of your pull. Trends samples change between pulls.

That would list every aircraft on the ground at Lincoln that week — and answer whether anything **else** foreign was there. The current sweep tool only builds circles from TPUSA events; it needs an ad-hoc circle option first.
