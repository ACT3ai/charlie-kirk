# Planes

## What the flight data actually shows

**On 10 September 2025 — the day Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University — two Egyptian-registered government jets were on the ground at Provo Municipal Airport, about 5 miles from Orem, the event city.** [SU-BTT](./SU-BTT/overview) and [SU-BND](./SU-BND/overview) are put there by **primary ADS-B position data**, not by a reading of a tracking website: closest fixes **1.23 km** and **1.29 km** from [KPVU](/Planes/Airports/KPVU). SU-BTT had been parked on that ramp since **4 September**, six days before, and lifted off at **07:14 MDT** that morning. **SU-BND did not move at all that day** — its first and last fixes are the same Provo position — and it stayed until **13 September**.

**Of the 29 claimed overlap rows that primary position data can actually decide, 25 put the aircraft at the field the claim names. Three are refuted.** A widely-quoted X audit of the same spreadsheet scored two rows in three as wrong and three in five as on the wrong continent. Where that audit can be checked against position data — **5 of its 44 negative verdicts** — it is **wrong on 3 and right on 2**.

**[N102DZ](./N102DZ/overview), the aircraft reported as Charlie Kirk's, had its FAA registration certificate re-issued on 16 October 2025 — 36 days after the assassination** — to a Cheyenne, Wyoming holding company, with a further registry action on 23 December 2025. It is [the only case-central aircraft whose registration moved after 10 September 2025](./Deleted-Flight-Records). Certificate re-issues are ordinary events — sale, transfer between owner LLCs, renewal, address change — and **are not evidence of anything by themselves**.

This section catalogs aircraft whose movements deviate from routine civilian patterns near
UVU in September 2025: a US Army [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) ISR jet making low passes, five Egypt-registered
VIP jets cycling through Provo, US government SAM flights touching [Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview) the day
before the shooting, and private jets with transponder gaps or tight timeline conflicts.
The area matters because flight data is **primary-source-adjacent** — ADS-B is public — yet
manifests and mission tasking remain sealed.

Several patterns recur. **[N1098L](./N1098L/overview)** looped at low altitude the day of the
event; **[SU-BTT](./SU-BTT/overview)** was on the ground Sept 4–10 with competing departure narratives;
the **armada** allegedly shadowed Kirk travel for years per
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens); **[SAM 99-0404](./SAM-99-0404/overview)** stopped at Fort
Huachuca Sept 8–9. The [Sept 10 timeline](./Sept10-Flight-Timeline) page merges transponder
windows with hospital-timing claims. Each thread has innocent explanations (training, MRO
contracts, VIP logistics) documented on the per-tail pages.

New readers should start with **[Planes Investigation Index](./Planes_Investigation_Index)** for
the full map. For foreign-intel framing go **[SU-BTT](./SU-BTT/overview)** → **[Israel Planes](./Israel-Planes)**;
for US military ISR go **[N1098L](./N1098L/overview)**; for command-and-control go
**[SAM 99-0404](./SAM-99-0404/overview)**. Ground truth requires manifests the
[Fix Laws](/Fix/overview) seek to compel. The foreign-aircraft threads cataloged here are
also the kind of leads former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) says his
center's parallel foreign-nexus review was ordered to abandon once Tyler Robinson was in custody.

## How to check any claim on these pages in five minutes

Every dispute in this section reduces to one of four things, and each has a fast test.

**1. Match on the hex code, never the registration.** The ICAO 24-bit Mode S address is what raw ADS-B archives index on and it does not survive retyping the way a tail number does. The identifiers for the aircraft in this investigation are collected in the [investigation index](/Planes/Planes_Investigation_Index).

| Tail | Hex | Type |
|---|---|---|
| [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) | 0101D3 | Falcon 7X |
| [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview) | 0101D0 | Falcon 7X |
| [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) | 0101D1 | Falcon 7X |
| [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) | 01003E | Gulfstream 4SP |
| [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) | 010070 | Gulfstream IV |
| [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) | AC3C75 | Challenger 300 |
| [N59906](/Planes/N59906/overview) | A7C14D | Piper PA-31 (1974) |
| [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) | A0299E | Global 6500 |

**2. Convert at the right offset.** Utah was on **UTC−6** on September 10, 2025 — Mountain *Daylight* Time. Arizona is **UTC−7 year-round** and does not observe daylight saving. More false claims in this investigation come from this single error than from any other. It generated the "time zone mistake" attack on the Owens presentation, and it was independently conceded and corrected by an analyst sympathetic to the investigation.

**3. Ask what kind of gap it is.** "Off the tracker" has at least four ordinary causes and they are not the same thing: an owner's **LADD privacy block** (a legal FAA opt-out that hides an aircraft from FlightAware and Flightradar24 while it keeps broadcasting normally — this is what [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) has); **terrain and receiver coverage gaps** in mountain valleys; **radar service termination** on approach to a non-towered field, which is a controller action, not a transponder action; and an actual **transponder switch-off**, which is the only one that would be illegal. Only the last supports any inference.

**4. Ask whether a maintenance relationship already explains it.** **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a five-year direct contract announced in October 2015, with its main plant at **Lincoln, Nebraska** and a facility at **Provo, Utah**. That single documented fact accounts for the Omaha→Lincoln hops, the Wichita visits, the recurring six-to-eight-day dwells, and the Provo relationship — which between them are most of the "overlap" entries in the shadowing spreadsheets. See [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) for the press release in full.
