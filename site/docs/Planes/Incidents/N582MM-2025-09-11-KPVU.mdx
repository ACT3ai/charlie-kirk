## What this establishes, and what it does not

**It establishes that the airframe was there.** The positions are on-ground reports from a volunteer receiver network, held in a public archive, and recorded here with the exact window they cover.

**It does not establish purpose, and it does not establish occupancy.** No ADS-B record anywhere places any person aboard any aircraft. An aircraft near an event is an airframe near an event.

**And for this aircraft, proximity is the expected thing.** This is a Kirk- or TPUSA-associated airframe. An aircraft that carries the man showing up where the man is is not surveillance — this row is a check that the method works, not evidence of anything.
