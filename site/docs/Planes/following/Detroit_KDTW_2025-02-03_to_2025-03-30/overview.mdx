# Detroit (KDTW) — February and March 2025

**[SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) entered the United States at Detroit twice in early 2025, and on both occasions flew onward the same day** — once to [Lincoln](../Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview), once to [Wichita](../Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview).

**No Kirk or TPUSA pairing has ever been offered for either date, by anyone.** Michigan is not among the five states in the geographic clustering. This page exists because the completeness rule for this section requires it: **an airport this fleet used is documented here whether or not it helps the claim.** Recording the locations that do not support the pattern is what makes the ones that do worth reading.

## The numbers at this field

**The only aircraft in this investigation the archives put on the ground at Detroit is not an Egyptian jet. It is [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview), the TPUSA-associated Learjet 60** — recorded at KDTW on **15 June 2024**, 20:25–20:29 UTC, **1.39 km** from the field, **38 position fixes**, the **day after** the [Turning Point Action People's Convention](/Planes/following/speaking/20240614_detroit) in Detroit, 16.6 miles away. **Two independent recovery routes hold that contact** — the per-tail pull and a blind geographic sweep that was not told what to look for — and they agree. It has its own page: [N582MM at KDTW, 15 June 2024](/Planes/Incidents/N582MM-2024-06-15-KDTW).

**That is a Kirk-side aircraft at a Kirk-side event. It is not an overlap and it is not evidence of following.** It is here because this page documents what the archives hold at this field, and what they hold at this field is that.

### How unusual is a foreign government jet here

Not unusual at all. **Detroit is a major international gateway with full customs facilities.** A transatlantic business jet clearing into the United States here is the airport operating exactly as intended.

If anything, Detroit is the *most* conventional entry point this fleet used anywhere in its record — more so than [Omaha](../Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview), and far more so than [Minot](../Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04/overview).

| **3 Feb 2025** | [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) | Paris–Le Bourget | **8:29** | **Lincoln (LNK)** | **1:46** | 6-day stay, then Wilmington 9 Feb, Cairo 10 Feb |
| **30 Mar 2025** | [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) | Paris–Le Bourget | **8:03** | **Wichita (ICT)** | **1:55** | 4-day stay, then Wilmington 3 Apr, Cairo 6 Apr |

Both are the same rotation template this fleet flies everywhere: **Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → US entry → onward hop → sit → Wilmington → Cairo.** The only variable is which US field.

Note that neither hop is the 15-to-20-minute repositioning flight seen at [Omaha](../Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview) and [St. Louis](../StLouis_KSTL_2022-12-20_to_2025-02-23/overview). These are genuine 700-mile transits, because Detroit is nowhere near either destination. **The aircraft cleared customs at a big hub and then flew properly across the country** — which is what an operator does when the convenient entry point and the shop are not in the same region.

## Why an empty page is worth having

Three reasons, and they apply across this whole section.

**1. It shows the entry points vary and the destinations do not.** Across 2024–2025 this fleet cleared into the United States at **Omaha, Detroit, St. Louis, Minot and Williston** — five different fields on both coasts of the interior. But it went to a much shorter list of places afterwards: **Lincoln, Wichita, Provo, St. Louis.** The entry point looks chosen for convenience on the day; the destination looks fixed. That is the signature of an aircraft going somewhere specific for a reason, and the reason at Lincoln and Wichita is documented — they are Falcon service centres.

**2. It tests the clustering.** The five-state clustering (Missouri, Delaware, Utah, Nebraska, Kansas) is offered as evidence that the aircraft followed the Kirks. But the fleet also went to **Michigan twice**, and Michigan is not in the cluster — **because nothing was happening in Michigan.** That is what you would expect if the aircraft's itinerary were driven by maintenance and the "cluster" were an artefact of where TPUSA happened to have activity. It is not what you would expect if the itinerary were driven by a target's movements.

**3. It is the honest way to publish a pattern.** A location log that only contains the locations supporting the claim is not a log; it is a case. If the Detroit arrivals had produced a same-day Kirk appearance in Michigan, that row would be on this page in bold. They did not, and this page says so.

## A note on the unrelated Michigan claim

A separate assertion circulating on X — that **"the same small UVU aircraft seen loitering over Utah Valley University"** were also flying over **Grand Blanc, Michigan** during a later incident at an LDS church on 28 September 2025 — has nothing to do with these arrivals.

That claim involves small single-engine trainers (**N114UV**, a Diamond DA40; **N908PA**, a Diamond DA20-C1) operating out of Provo and Spanish Fork, matched against an unregistered **Cessna 172** in Michigan. It fails on the aircraft type printed in the poster's own screenshot, and it is set out and assessed on the [Planes Investigation Index](/Planes/Planes_Investigation_Index).

It is mentioned here only so that a reader searching "Michigan" in this investigation finds both items and does not confuse them. **The Detroit arrivals on this page are Egyptian-registered Falcon 7X flights; the Grand Blanc claim is about flight-school trainers.**

## The counterargument

There is no claim on this page to argue against. For completeness:

* **Detroit is a major international customs port.** Clearing here is ordinary.
* **Both arrivals departed the same day** for destinations with documented Falcon maintenance facilities.
* **No pairing has been alleged by anyone**, including by the researchers making the strongest claims elsewhere.
* **The rotations fit the same fixed template** flown at every other location in this section.

## What we do not know

1. **Why Detroit on these two occasions and Omaha on the others.** Slot availability, weather, fuel pricing and crew duty limits all produce this kind of variation, and none of it is public.
2. **The CBP entry records** for both arrivals exist at Detroit and have never been requested.
3. **Whether anyone joined or left the aircraft at Detroit.**
4. **Whether Charlie or Erika Kirk was in Michigan on either date.** Nobody has claimed either was.
