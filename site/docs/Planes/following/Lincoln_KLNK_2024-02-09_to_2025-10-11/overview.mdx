# Lincoln (KLNK) — February 2024 to October 2025

**Three Egyptian-registered Falcon 7X jets — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU) and [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV) — made seven documented arrivals at Lincoln Airport, Nebraska between February 2024 and October 2025, each staying six to eight days.** Lincoln is the most-visited destination in the entire Egyptian fleet's US record.

**It is also the location of Duncan Aviation's main plant, and Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999.** This page sets out both facts in full, because Lincoln is where the shadowing claim is at its numerically strongest and its evidentially weakest at the same time.

## The airport

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Lincoln Airport |
| **Identifiers** | **KLNK** (ICAO) / **LNK** (IATA) |
| **City / State** | Lincoln, Lancaster County, Nebraska |
| **Class** | Commercial service / joint civil-military, with a Nebraska Air National Guard presence |
| **Distance from Omaha (KOMA)** | ~50 miles — a 15-to-20-minute flight, which is exactly what the record shows |
| **MRO on field** | **Duncan Aviation** — the company's **headquarters and main plant**, one of the largest privately-owned business-aviation MRO facilities in the world |

### How unusual is a foreign government jet here

Less unusual than at [Provo](./Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13), and for a documented reason. Lincoln is a mid-sized Midwestern airport that would not ordinarily see Egyptian state aircraft — except that **Duncan Aviation's global headquarters sits on the field**, it does heavy maintenance on exactly this class of aircraft, and it has a named, published, decades-long relationship with this specific customer.

## The contract that explains this page

A **Duncan Aviation** press release, dated **Thursday, 10/29/2015, 14:31**, carried by the trade publication *DOM — Director of Maintenance*, headlined **"Duncan Aviation Signs a Five-Year Contract With the Egyptian Air Force."** Reproduced in the parts that matter:

> …announces the company has entered into a five-year direct contract agreement with the Egyptian Air Force (EAF) for aircraft maintenance services. **Duncan Aviation has been managing the maintenance and repair of the EAF fleet of Falcon 20 aircraft since 1999.**
>
> Duncan Aviation's Manager of Government & Special Programs Dave Shipperbottom says the fact that **the EAF has chosen to fly over closer maintenance companies and bring their aircraft to Duncan Aviation** "reveals a lot about our relationship with them. All the technicians at the squadron level are great to work with. They know us and we know them. It is a good relationship."
>
> **The first EAF aircraft arrived at Duncan Aviation's Lincoln, NE, facility on October 20 for a Z-check. The next will arrive in November for a C-check, Major Corrosion Inspection and complete paint.**

Four points, each of which lands directly on this page's flight record:

* **The relationship dates to 1999** — twenty-six years, and fifteen years before Charlie Kirk's public career began.
* **The destination named in the release is Lincoln, Nebraska**, the exact field in the table below.
* **The company's own manager anticipates and answers the obvious objection**: the EAF flies *past* closer facilities to get there. The long ferry from Cairo is the documented norm for this customer, not an anomaly requiring explanation.
* **The named work types — Z-check, C-check, Major Corrosion Inspection, complete paint — are multi-day to multi-week events.** A six-to-eight-day dwell is at the light end of that range and entirely ordinary.

### The pattern is a rotation, and the numbers say so

Read down the columns rather than across the rows and the regularity is overwhelming:

* **The dwell is 5 or 6 days. Every single time.** Nine visits, nine dwells of five or six days. A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.
* **The repositioning hop is 15 to 19 minutes.** Seven of the nine arrive at Omaha and hop to Lincoln in a quarter of an hour. **A Falcon 7X does not fly a 15-minute sector between two Nebraska cities for any reason except to move to a facility.** Omaha is the international arrival airport; Lincoln is the destination.
* **Wilmington is on the outbound leg of all nine, and only the outbound leg.** It is a customs and fuel stop, not a place these aircraft go.
* **The whole rotation is Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → US entry → hop → sit six days → Wilmington → Cairo.** Flown identically nine times by three different aircraft over twenty months.

## The Kirk / TPUSA side

Two pairings are offered in the researcher timeline. Both are set out here at their real strength.

|---|---|---|---|
| **11 Feb 2024** | **"Turning Point USA begins a chapter in Lincoln, Nebraska"** | **SU-BTT on the ground at Lincoln, day 3 of a 5-day stay** | **Same city, inside the stay** |
| 8 Feb 2024 | Charlie Kirk's campus tour publicly announced (with Candace Owens) | SU-BTT arrives Omaha 10:57 am on **9 Feb**, hops to Lincoln | Day after |
| 25 Aug 2023 | **Charlie Kirk speaks in Lincoln, Nebraska** | **Aircraft in Egypt** per the independent audit | **Different continent** |

### The February 2024 pairing, weighed honestly

**This is the best Lincoln pairing that exists, and it is not strong.** A TPUSA chapter opening in Lincoln on 11 February 2024, while an Egyptian jet sat on the Lincoln ramp mid-way through a five-day maintenance-shaped stay, is a same-city, same-week co-location. It is exactly the kind of row a **±3-day overlap window** produces.

Three things weaken it further:

* **A chapter opening is not a Kirk appearance.** Nothing in the record places Charlie Kirk in Lincoln on 11 February 2024.
* **The aircraft was there before the event and after it**, having arrived on the 9th and left on the 14th. It did not arrive for the event or leave with it.
* **The 2023 counter-case is stronger than the 2024 case.** On **25 August 2023, Charlie Kirk actually spoke in Lincoln, Nebraska** — a genuine, in-person, named appearance in this exact city. The [independent audit](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) of the overlap sheet places the aircraft **in Egypt** on that date. **The one time Charlie Kirk is documented in Lincoln, the plane was on another continent.**

## An error worth correcting in the source graphics

The most-circulated overlap timeline graphic, watermarked **@AlsaceTX**, carries a footnote reading:

> ² Stops in Wilmington, **NC** for one day, leaves Wilmington for Cairo on 8/24

**Wilmington, North Carolina is ILM. Wilmington, Delaware is ILG.** The flight-history exports for all three aircraft show **ILG — Delaware**, on every rotation including the 23–24 August one this footnote describes. The same graphic elsewhere writes the September 10 leg as **"PVU-ILM"**, which is the North Carolina code.

This is a two-letter transposition, but it moves a claimed destination five hundred miles and into a different state, and the [Wilmington page](./Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12) explains why the destination matters. Anyone auditing that graphic should read **ILG** throughout.

## The counterargument

Stated at full strength, because on this page it is the stronger reading:

* **Duncan Aviation's headquarters is on this field, and it has held the Egyptian Air Force account since 1999.** That is a published fact from a 2015 trade press release, not an inference.
* **The 15-to-19-minute Omaha hop is unexplainable any other way.** No surveillance purpose is served by flying a large business jet a quarter of an hour between two Nebraska cities. It is a repositioning flight to a shop.
* **The dwell is fixed at five to six days across all nine visits.** Fixed turnarounds are what maintenance produces.
* **The rotations continue after the assassination.** SU-BTV flew the identical Lincoln rotation on **5–11 October 2025**, a month after Charlie Kirk's death. Whatever this pattern is, it did not stop when he did.
* **The pre-2025 Lincoln claims fail the audit.** The 25 August 2023 Lincoln appearance — the one date Charlie Kirk is genuinely documented in this city — has the aircraft in Egypt.

## And the counter to it

Two things the maintenance reading does not settle, kept here because a page without them is propaganda:

1. **Nine visits in twenty months is a lot of heavy maintenance for three aircraft.** Z-checks and C-checks are periodic, not monthly. Whether nine six-day visits by three airframes over twenty months is a plausible inspection cadence is an aviation question that no one in this investigation has actually asked a maintenance engineer.
2. **A real relationship is also good cover.** A twenty-six-year maintenance contract explains why these aircraft *can* be at Lincoln without anyone asking. It does not establish what any given trip carried.

Neither point is evidence. Both are open.

## What we do not know

1. **No work orders, no invoices, no shop records.** Duncan Aviation has not commented publicly and its customer records are not public. **A single confirmation of which airframes were in the shop on which dates would close this page.**
2. **No manifests for any of the nine arrivals.**
3. **Customs happened at the entry airport** — Omaha, or Detroit for the February 2025 rotation — not at Lincoln.
4. **The TPUSA Lincoln chapter opening on 11 February 2024 is uncited**, coming from a researcher timeline circulated on X.
5. **Whether Charlie or Erika Kirk was ever in Lincoln during any of the nine stays is unknown**, and no source claims either was.
