# Erika Kirk's flights — what the X posts actually claim

**The register behind the following claim holds [70 rows](./cuts/erika-any-70) that name Erika Kirk — [61](./cuts/erika-only-61) of them name her and not Charlie.** They run from 5 October 2022 to 13 September 2025, across **57 dated occurrences**, **12 US states** and **15 airports**. On **18** of those rows, primary ADS-B position data puts the claimed aircraft **on the ground at the field the claim names**. Three rows the same data flatly contradicts.

**The closest of them is the day of the assassination.** On [10 September 2025](./overlap/20250910_UT_orem/overview) the Egyptian-registered jets [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) are placed by primary position data on the ground at [Provo Municipal Airport](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) — closest approach **1.23 km** and **1.29 km** — about 5.4 miles from the UVU event. Six days earlier SU-BTT was at the same field; three days later [SU-BND was back](./overlap/20250913_UT_provo_owens_042/overview).

**What none of that data can do is put Erika Kirk aboard anything, or anywhere.** Every row below is two claims stacked: a claim about an aircraft, and a claim about her location. The aircraft half can be tested against the archives. The Erika half never has been — see [what these rows do not establish](#what-these-rows-do-not-establish) below, which is kept in full.

## What these rows do not establish

This page is the first dedicated public log of **Erika Kirk's flights** as they appear in X posts, broadcasts, and the overlap spreadsheet. It is **not** a finding that she boarded any foreign-registered aircraft, tasked anyone, or did anything wrong. **Status: Alive.** She is named here because commentators named her, and because she answered them on camera.

The headline number travelling on X — **73 overlaps with Erika Kirk** — is a **tracker tally**, not a record. This repo cannot reproduce a dated list of 73 Erika locations. Her [flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Of **139** sourced Turning Point appearances in [the speaking catalog](./speaking/overview), **exactly one** places her at an event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. **Do not treat Charlie Kirk's itinerary as a stand-in for hers.** The tallies themselves say the two counts differ by a factor of three.

**Erika Kirk's presence is not established on a single one of the 70 rows above.** No dated itinerary for her has ever been published — not by the compilers, not by Turning Point, not by anyone. What the sheet has instead is a column called *Erika Location*, and across the 61 rows that claim her alone that column carries:

* **42** name a single **US state** — not a city, not a venue, not an address.
* **11** name **two places at once** — *"Kansas / Delaware"*, *"Delaware / Nebraska"*, *"Missouri / Cairo"*.
* **8** name **nowhere at all.**
* **2** get as specific as a city or a venue. **Two, out of sixty-one.**

**So this page applies a rule, and states it plainly: where it cannot be established that Erika Kirk was somewhere, she is treated as not having been there.** That is the ordinary standard for every other claim on this site, and there is no reason this one gets an exception. Applying it leaves **[zero](./cuts/erika-established-0)** established Erika overlaps. That is not a finding that she was never near these airports. It is a finding that **the published evidence does not place her**.

**A jet's movements do not establish who was aboard.** Occupancy and ownership are different questions. Nothing on this page treats a tail number as a passenger list.

## Why her flights are the weak side of the largest number in this investigation

**Register update.** The paragraph below was written against an earlier 78-row reconstruction of the compilation. The register now holds **85** rows, **[70](./cuts/erika-any-70)** of which name Erika Kirk and **[61](./cuts/erika-only-61)** of which name her alone — the figures used in the table at the top of this page. The older breakdown is kept here as published.

The compilation reconstructed in [overlaps.csv](./overlap/overview) has **78** claimed rows. On the sheet's own attribution:

* **60** claim Erika Kirk alone.
* **13** claim Charlie Kirk alone.
* **1** claims both, on 10 September 2025.
* **4** claim a Turning Point event with neither Kirk.

**Seventy-eight percent of the entire count rests on her.** And that is the part nobody can check against a flight log. The sheet's `Erika Location` cells are mostly **states**, sometimes **two states joined by a slash**, and the Notes column names **Instagram**. That substitution is unpacked on [How the spreadsheet placed her](./Erika_Location_From_Instagram).

Applying the ordinary standard used everywhere else on this site — where presence cannot be established, treat the person as not having been there — leaves **zero** established Erika overlaps. That is not a finding that she was never near these airports. It is a finding that **the published evidence does not place her**.

## Counterargument

* **Erika Kirk has denied the Egyptian-plane story in her own words**, invited a log check, and offered a dated hospital photo for at least one row. A denial is not a record, but it is the strongest public statement from the person the tally is about.
* **Blocking N102DZ from a public tracker is ordinary.** Private owners request FAA LADD / tracker privacy as a matter of routine. A May 2026 removal, even if confirmed, does not establish who requested it or why.
* **[@Liz_Wheeler](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler)** looked at the same spreadsheet and said that under a same-airport, same-day rule the Erika overlap **dwindles to one**, and that one is Utah on 10 September **after** the shooting, with the Egyptian jets **already on the ground**. See [What "overlap" was defined as](./Overlap_Window_Definition).
* **[@KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat)** said **66 percent** of the *aircraft* locations were wrong. An overlap cannot be an Erika overlap if the jet was in Egypt.
* **The aircraft side has a service explanation that does not need Erika Kirk to be anywhere.** Duncan Aviation's Egyptian Air Force account is documented to about **1999** — main plant in [Lincoln](./Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview), a station at Provo — so a leg into either field can be accounted for without reference to her movements at all. **Which specific legs it actually accounts for is unresolved:** no work order or invoice for any individual visit has ever been produced.
* **JD Vance**, quoted by [@EricLDaugh](https://x.com/EricLDaugh/status/2044193709370011851), publicly rejected the idea that Erika Kirk was "involved" in Charlie Kirk's death. That is a named public official's denial of a class of attacks this page does not make.

## What we do not know

1. **Erika Kirk's actual itinerary** for 2022–September 2025. No dated list has been published by her, by Turning Point, or by the compilers.
2. **Whether N102DZ's public history was in fact removed in May 2026**, by whom, and under which privacy program.
3. **What the saved screenshots [@hurtfeelingzday](https://x.com/hurtfeelingzday) says he holds actually show.** They have not been published in full here.
4. **Who was aboard N102DZ, N560TW, or any Egyptian-registered jet on any claimed date.** Tails are not manifests.
5. **Which of the competing 10 September stories is true.** Arizona-all-day, afternoon inbound on N102DZ, and a 10:06 a.m. N560TW theory cannot all be right. See [Sept 10 whereabouts](./Erika_Sept10_Whereabouts).
6. **Which Instagram posts the spreadsheet's Notes column is pointing at.** No URLs, dates, or archives were published.

What would settle the flight half: operator charter records, FBO logs, and a published N102DZ history for the claimed dates. What would settle the person half: Erika Kirk's itinerary, or the sheet's underlying citations. **Neither has been produced.**

### What these tables cannot tell you

* **A trace proves presence. It never proves purpose, and it never proves occupancy.** No ADS-B record anywhere places any person aboard any aircraft. An aircraft near an event is an airframe near an event.
* **Erika Kirk's flight logs are reported erased, and no archive anywhere produces her itinerary.** Every row above is anchored to a sourced *event* she is recorded as attending, not to a document saying where she was. This is the single largest hole in the whole overlap claim. See [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).
* **The sourced Erika event calendar is short.** These tables can only test the events this repository can source. That is nowhere near every place she went, so an empty row is a statement about our calendar as much as about any aircraft.
* **An absence is not a finding.** A day with no row may be a day a volunteer receiver network heard nothing. Transponder off, outside coverage, and genuinely elsewhere all look identical from here.
* **2022 is effectively blank.** No free archive covers it, so no row above and no gap above can speak to that year.
