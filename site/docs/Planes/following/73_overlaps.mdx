# The 73 overlaps — every city, date, and tail the list actually named

The number **73** is a **tracker tally**, not a record. It is the number [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) said out loud on **17 November 2025** after starting from **68**. This page is the reconstruction of the *list* those numbers were counted from: every date, city, airport, and tail number that has been published on a spreadsheet screenshot, named on a broadcast, or posted on X. The machine-readable version is `overlaps.csv` in this directory — 78 rows, one per claimed pairing, including the five extras that were never dated in public. Docusaurus does not serve that file as a page; it is the research spine this page is written from.

**No full 73-row dated table was ever released.** What exists in public is a Google Sheet Owens displayed on air, screenshots of that sheet and of a rebuild of it, a colour-coded audit of 65–67 of those rows, and a handful of cities named in posts. The five rows that turned 68 into 73 have no dates. That hole is the finding, and it is recorded as five empty rows rather than filled in.

Every disputed claim below is **alleged** and is a **reported claim**, not an established fact. No public record establishes who tasked any aircraft. [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) are the two tails in the 73-count; [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV) and [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM) are a later five-plane armada and are **not** in the 73. Erika Kirk's side of every pairing is the weak side — her flight logs are reported erased. See **[Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)**.

## Where the number came from, in the words that created it

**17 November 2025, "Operation Mocking-Plane"** (Candace Ep 266, circulating YouTube `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JQjCAsfoXEE`). Transcript:

> "It turns out that she was correct. She discovered that between 2022 and September uh of 2025, both of those aircrafts, SUBTT, the yellow plane, and SUBND, the blue plane, showed 68 overlaps with Erica's documented loca locations. Okay, 68 overlaps. I have since been able to add now uh 73 times that this overlapped. Okay.
>
> And 29 of those instances also happened to include Charlie. Charlie was also present. These are all the instances um uh in which this plane 60 all 68 instances that this plane crossed with Erica.
>
> And I'm also going to say this includes overseas as well. So my instant question becomes, why didn't Turning Point acknowledge that this plane was familiar to them?
>
> And when it comes to these Egyptian flights, the math on this is indisputable. There is less than a 0.000000001% probability. Okay, under random distribution models that these Egyptian planes coincidentally landed around Erica at these times.
>
> Like I said, this plane was not just with her in America. It was also tracking her overseas as well."

Four things in that passage, pulled apart:

1. **The count was 68 and became 73 in the same breath.** 68 is credited to a "pregnant mommy sleuth." The extra five are Owens's own additions. **They were never published as dates.**
2. **29 of 68 also included Charlie.** On her own arithmetic, roughly **39 of the 68 did not involve Charlie Kirk at all.** The investigation file's split is *"Egyption Planes had 73 overlaps with Erika. Only ~23 with Charlie."* 29-of-68 and ~23-of-73 have never been reconciled.
3. **"Overseas as well."** No overseas pairing has ever been published as a dated row except the sheet cell that put Erika "potentially in Giza" on 20 April 2023.
4. **The 0.000000001% figure has no model.** No denominator, no airport-population, no list of compared locations. It is quoted because she said it.

The flights, she said, "were majority clustered within five states, **Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska, Delaware, and Utah**." Cities later named around that cluster: **Omaha, Wichita, Provo**, plus **Lincoln, St. Louis, Wilmington, Salt Lake City, Lawrence, Jefferson City, Topeka, Bangor, Williston, Minot**, and the claimed-and-audited-away **New York, Chicago, Boston, Atlanta, Sacramento**.

## How the sheet defined an "overlap"

A screenshot of the Owens-side sheet, circulating with the Kanekoa audit and reproduced on **[Planes Following Charlie or Erika](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika)**, shows these column headers:

`Index` · `Date` · `Flight` · `Route` · **`Erika Location`** · `Event/Reason` · **`Overlap Window`** · **`Charlie Present`** · `Notes`

Four facts about those columns, from the visible extract (about 35 rows on one screenshot, 65–67 in the audit rebuild):

* **`Overlap Window` reads "±3 days" on every visible row.** A seven-day window. An aircraft arriving Monday counts against an event the previous Friday or the following Thursday.
* **`Charlie Present` reads "No" on 32 of 35 visible rows**, and **"Yes" on only the last three** — 15 Mar 2025, 22 Mar 2025, 23 May 2025. On the sheet's own data this is an Erika sheet.
* **`Event/Reason` is a category label**, not an event. Visible entries: "Media appearance / TPUSA," "TPUSA event / campus," "TPUSA engagement / local media," "TPUSA / media appearances," "TPUSA appearances / media," "TPUSA / campus engagement." **Not one visible cell names a venue.**
* **`Erika Location` is often a state.** Visible values: New York, Illinois, Massachusetts, Kansas, Missouri, Utah, Nebraska, California, "Delaware / Nebraska," "Kansas / Delaware," "Missouri / Cairo," "Wichita / DE / potentially Giza." Notes cells cite **"Instagram confirms presence"** without a link, a date, or an archive.

Liz Wheeler's reading of the same sheet, **17 December 2025**, [status/2001081318705168809](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler/status/2001081318705168809):

> "The spreadsheets define 'overlap' as the Egyptian planes landing within +-3 days of Erika and within the same 'region' which they define as within 50-100 miles. So, not the same day at the same airport. Not even close."

Owens, quoted by Kanekoa on **17 December 2025**, [status/2001099364304085159](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat/status/2001099364304085159), said a column readers took as Erika's location "was actually saying where the planes landed on that day, because it was ±3 days." That is an admission that the column the 73 was counted against was not a verified Erika itinerary.

## The full reconstructed list

Rows `OWENS-001` through `OWENS-067` are the Kanekoa rebuild of the sheet Owens displayed. Colour in the source: **A** = accurate, **P** = partial, **✗** = inaccurate, **—** = archive does not go back that far. `Charlie Present` is filled only where the visible extract or the hub page states it. `Erika Location` is filled from the visible extract, from @liztalny1023's cell-reads, or from the route when the extract named that state. Nearest sourced Kirk/TPUSA event is from this directory's [speaking catalog](./speaking/overview) and `tpusa_events.csv`.

A row can be **route-accurate and still not an overlap with anybody.** The audit tests whether the aircraft flew the route. It does not independently place Erika or Charlie.

## How to use the CSV

`overlaps.csv` in this directory is the spine. Columns match the sister-directory spec, plus `owens_index`, `audit_verdict`, `claimed_erika_location`, `charlie_present_claimed`, `overlap_window`, `nearest_sourced_kirk_event`, `nearest_event_gap_days`, and `notes`.

* `OWENS-001` … `OWENS-067` are permanent IDs. **Do not renumber them.** They join this page to the Kanekoa screenshots and to the hub audit.
* `UNPUB-001` … `UNPUB-005` are the five undated extras.
* `EXTRA-001` … `EXTRA-006` are pairings named on air or on X that are not a numbered sheet row.
* `gap` is `same_day` / `day_before` / `day_after` / `pm3` (the sheet's ±3-day window) / `other` / `unknown`.
* `confidence` is `audited_accurate` / `audited_partial` / `audited_inaccurate` / `claimed` / `unpublished`.

Join `foreign_tail` to `planes.csv`. Join dates and cities to `flights.csv` and `tpusa_events.csv`. An overlap is not a row in either of those files on its own — it is a pairing, and the gap has to be written down.
