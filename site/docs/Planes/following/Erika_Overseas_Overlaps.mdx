# The overseas-overlap claim — Erika Kirk and the Egyptian jets abroad

This page isolates one clause that travelled with the 17 November 2025 pivot: that the alleged overlaps were **not only domestic**. It is a record of what X accounts posted. It is **not** a finding that Erika Kirk was tracked abroad, boarded a foreign jet, or did anything wrong. **Status: Alive.** Parent: **[Erika Kirk's flights](./Erika_Kirk_Flights)**.

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing on this page constitutes a finding of wrongdoing by Erika Kirk or any other living person. Overseas-tracking claims quoted here are other people's allegations. They are unproven.
:::

## What the posts said

On 17 November 2025, [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) said on air, as transcribed on [The 73 overlaps](./73_overlaps):

> "And I'm also going to say this includes overseas as well. So my instant question becomes, why didn't Turning Point acknowledge that this plane was familiar to them? … this plane was not just with her in America. It was also tracking her overseas as well."

Amplifiers repeated the clause the same night:

* **[@itsmorganariel](https://x.com/itsmorganariel/status/1990557512043803109):** "including trips she took outside of the country."
* **[@ShadowofEzra](https://x.com/ShadowofEzra/status/1990557868396089532):** "the planes followed Erika internationally."
* **[@ninoboxer](https://x.com/ninoboxer/status/1990588451566334184):** "The flight paths line up on both domestic and international routes."
* **[@Ryanmatta](https://x.com/Ryanmatta/status/1990562438908162474):** "trailed Erika Kirk to over 73 locations all over the world."
* **[@Mia_Stretch](https://x.com/Mia_Stretch/status/1990565803331289285), 17 November 2025**, went further than the broadcast: "tracking Erika Lane Frantze's (Kirk) movements across 70+ documented flights around the world … even before she ever met Charlie." **No source for a pre-marriage dated list has been produced.** The clause is recorded as that account's allegation.

**[@KAGdrogo](https://x.com/KAGdrogo/status/2001087067712024960), 17 December 2025**, later posted that Owens said the same jets "were also flying in and out of ISRAEL." That is a routing claim about the *aircraft*, which already has its own page at **[Israel Planes](/Planes/Israel-Planes)**. An aircraft that visits Israel between US maintenance stops is not, by itself, an overlap with Erika Kirk.

## What has actually been published as a dated overseas row

**One cell.** The overlap spreadsheet extract transcribed on [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) includes the value **"Wichita / DE / potentially Giza"** for 20 April 2023. Three places on two continents, with the word *potentially* doing the work in a cell that was counted as a location match.

**No other overseas pairing has been published as a dated row** — not a city, not an airport, not a tail, not a gap. The [73 overlaps](./73_overlaps) page records that hole as the finding. Cairo appears in this directory as the [home field](./Cairo_HECA_2022-11-13_to_2025-10-12) every rotation begins and ends at, which is also what the "wrong continent" audits mean. [Sharm el-Sheikh](./SharmElSheikh_HESH_2025-10-13_to_2025-10-13) is a 13 October 2025 tarmac shot of SU-BTT beside Air Force One — **no Kirk presence, no overlap, not a 74th row.**

## Counterargument

* **An unpublished overseas list cannot be audited.** The domestic sheet was. [@KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat) found a majority of those aircraft positions wrong, often because the jet was in Egypt — which is the opposite of an overseas *Erika* overlap.
* **Erika Kirk's international movements have not been published as a flight log.** Treating Instagram, press photos, or TPUSA overseas programming as a coordinate is the same substitution problem unpacked on [How the spreadsheet placed her](./Erika_Location_From_Instagram).
* **"Flying in and out of Israel" is a claim about the jets' own routing**, not about her. Do not merge those threads.
* **She denied** being on an Egyptian plane. See [the invitation](./Erika_Flight_Log_Invitation).

## What we do not know

Which overseas cities, dates, airports, and tails the "includes overseas as well" clause was pointing at. Until those rows are dated, the international half of the 73 is a sentence, not a list.
