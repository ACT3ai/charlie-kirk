# What the compilers counted as an "overlap"

**The sheet's own `Overlap Window` column reads "±3 days" on every visible row, and the region rule was 50–100 miles.** That is a seven-day window and a hundred-mile radius. **[Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) tightened it to same airport, same day and the count fell from 65–75 to one** — 10 September 2025, Utah, where on her account Erika Kirk arrived after the shooting and the jets were already on the ground.

**This directory ran the same tightening against its own data and got a different small number.** Six dates survive a same-day-or-adjacent test at a shared field: **three involve Charlie Kirk, two are a Turning Point event with neither Kirk at it, and the sixth is an Erika cell that fails the presence rule.** Two of the three Charlie dates are Utah. **Neither result is averaged into the other.**

| The rule | Radius | Window | Count it produces | Who applied it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The sheet's own rule** | 50–100 miles | **±3 days** (a seven-day window) | **65–75**, published as 68, then 73 | The compilers — [the broadcast](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) |
| A looser restatement of the critique | **±150 miles** | ±3 days | — | [@paramounttactcl](https://x.com/paramounttactcl/status/2000717550104064272), 16 Dec 2025. **Even the critique of the window does not agree on the radius** |
| **Same airport, same day** | 0 | 0 days | **1** — 10 Sep 2025, Utah | [Liz Wheeler](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler/status/2001081318705168809), 17 Dec 2025 |
| Same-day-or-adjacent at a shared field | shared field | ±1 day | **6 dates** — 3 Charlie, 2 TPUSA-only, 1 failing Erika row | [This directory's reconstruction](./overview) |
| Strict same-ICAO, ±3 days against 139 sourced events | same airport | ±3 days | **1** | [TPUSA events](./TPUSA_events) |
| Loose same-metro, ±3 days against 139 sourced events | same metro | ±3 days | **2** — both Utah, both Charlie | [TPUSA events](./TPUSA_events) |
| Primary ADS-B position data, per claimed row | 15 km of the named field | same day only | **25 of the 29 decidable rows at the claimed field** | [The 73 overlaps](./73_overlaps) |

**Read the last two rows together, because they are the honest shape of this page.** The aircraft half of the claim is far better corroborated than the audits suggested — 25 of 29. **The pairing half collapses**, because a wide window matched against a state-level location note is not a match with a person. **The wide 73 and the narrow six are different claims, and debunking the window does not dissolve the September Provo presence of [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview).**


This page is the definition fight. **The 73 is only as strong as the rule that produced it.** [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) (@Liz_Wheeler) published that rule in December 2025. This page quotes her, quotes the amplifiers who had used a tighter-sounding word, and keeps the counter-count next to the count. It is **not** a finding that Erika Kirk was or was not followed. **Status: Alive.** Parent: **[Erika Kirk's flights](./Erika_Kirk_Flights)**.

## The word people heard, and the rule the sheet used

Amplifiers on 17–18 November 2025 used verbs like **followed**, **tracking**, **trailed**, **coincided with Erika's flights**. [@JustTheTweets17](https://x.com/JustTheTweets17/status/1990785423623262490) wrote "72 times those exact planes followed Erika Kirk." [@BasedSamParker](https://x.com/BasedSamParker/status/1990567019851464866) wrote "73 of the flights … coincide with Erika Kirk travel locations." Readers who heard "followed" heard **same airport, same day**.

**[@Liz_Wheeler](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler/status/2000710646292267009), 15 December 2025:**

> "Were Egyptian planes following Erika Kirk? It's been alleged that 65-75 times, these Egyptian planes' travel overlapped with Erika's travel. 'Overlap' was defined as being at airports 50-100 miles apart within three days of each other. Do you know how many times they were at the same airport on the same day? One time."

**[@Liz_Wheeler](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler/status/2001022256382714069), 16 December 2025**, video attached, same reduction: "One time. Once in three years."

**[@Liz_Wheeler](https://x.com/Liz_Wheeler/status/2001081318705168809), 17 December 2025**, the long version:

> "The spreadsheets define 'overlap' as the Egyptian planes landing within +-3 days of Erika and within the same 'region' which they define as within 50-100 miles. So, not the same day at the same airport. Not even close. … Theoretically that could mean Erika landed on location A on Monday, left Tuesday, the Egyptian plane didn't land til Wednesday and in a completely different state 100 miles away… but that was counted as overlap?! … When you narrow the parameters to Egyptian planes landing on the same day in the same location, the overlap dwindles to… one. On 9/10 in Utah. Where Erika arrived after Charlie's murder and the Egyptian planes were already on the ground."

**[@paramounttactcl](https://x.com/paramounttactcl/status/2000717550104064272) (Gary Melton), 16 December 2025**, restated her as "±3 DAYS and ±150 MILES" — a looser mile figure than Wheeler's 50–100. **Even the critique of the window does not agree on the radius.** This site does not pick 100 or 150. It records that both numbers were posted.

## The second cut: the aircraft was often not there at all

A wide window only matters if the jet was in the window. **[@KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat/status/2001099364304085159), 17 December 2025**, said **66 percent** of the Egyptian planes' locations on the original sheet were wrong, and that Owens "has never provided verifiable evidence of where Erika actually was." **[@EYakoby](https://x.com/EYakoby/status/2003551800537018613) (Eyal Yakoby), 23 December 2025:** "60% of the flight logs she cited weren't just inaccurate—they placed planes on the literal wrong continent."

The line-by-line is on [Planes Following Charlie or Erika](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) and [The 73 overlaps](./73_overlaps). The person-side hole is on [How the spreadsheet placed her](./Erika_Location_From_Instagram). **A ±3-day, 100-mile match against a state-level Instagram note, scored on dates the jet was in Egypt, is not an Erika-Kirk overlap.**

## What survives the tighter rule

Wheeler's same-day, same-airport remainder is **one date: 10 September 2025, Utah**, and on her account Erika **arrived after**, with the jets **already down**. That remainder is itself contested on [Sept 10 whereabouts](./Erika_Sept10_Whereabouts). This directory's own reconstruction finds **six** dates that survive a same-day-or-adjacent test at a shared field, of which **three are Charlie Kirk**, **two are a Turning Point event with neither Kirk**, and the sixth is the Wilmington 10 September Erika cell that fails the presence rule. **Two of the three Charlie dates are in Utah.** That working is on the [overview](./overview).

**The wide 73 and the narrow six are different claims.** Debunking the window is not the same as dissolving the September Provo presence of [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview).

## Counterargument

* **Wheeler is reading the sheet's own rule**, not inventing a straw man. The `Overlap Window` column on the extract reads "±3 days."
* **Owens later described the Erika Location column as having been misread**, per Kanekoa's 17 December quote of her. If the column was the *plane's* landing region, the 73 was never an Erika-location count in the way the 17 November posts sold it.
* **Duncan Aviation's 1999 account** still explains why the jets are at Lincoln and Provo at all, window or no window. [Lincoln](./Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview).
* **Erika Kirk denied** the tracking story. [The invitation](./Erika_Flight_Log_Invitation).

## What we do not know

Whether the compilers will publish a same-airport, same-day table. Whether Owens's promised "interactive timeline" of broader TPUSA Faith events — which Kanekoa says she shifted to — was ever released. Whether Wheeler and Kanekoa were working from the same export of the same sheet.
