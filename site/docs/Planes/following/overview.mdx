The claim is not one dramatic flight. It is the **repetition**: [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) (the "yellow plane"), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) (the "blue plane"), and the rest of the Israel/Egyptian armada showing up at the same fields the Kirks and TPUSA used, often small regional or Army airfields, across a window researchers date from **2022 through September 2025**. The existing hub for that allegation is **[Planes Following Charlie or Erika](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika)**. This directory is the public location-by-location log of that same claim; the first page in it is the X-discussion dump, because the counts, the dates, and the disputes all started as posts.

The published tallies do not agree, and the disagreement is itself the finding. Owens's team and amplifiers have used **73 Erika / about 23 Charlie**, **72**, **"70+"**, **68 Erika of which 29 also hit Charlie**, **65–75**, and **77**. [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) says that under a same-airport, same-day rule the overlap **dwindles to one** — Utah on September 10. [KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat) says **66 percent** of the spreadsheet locations are wrong and **60 percent** put the jets on the wrong continent. Those numbers are recorded side by side on the [X discussions page](./x_discussions); they are not averaged.

What to read next: start with the [X discussions page](./x_discussions) if you want every sourced post, then the [hub](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) for the compressed claim, then the tail-number pages — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU) — and **[Erika Kirk's flights](./Erika_Kirk_Flights)**, the first dedicated page for her alleged itinerary, plus [Erika's erased flight logs](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), because the Erika side of every tally is the weak side. **The location pages are now live** — Provo, Lincoln, Omaha, Wichita, Wilmington, St. Louis, Minot, Detroit, and the two non-US fields Cairo and Sharm el-Sheikh, each with its own date range, arrival log, overlap table and counterargument. They are indexed in the table below.

## The numbers, before anything else

**Every one of these is a claim made by the compilers, reconstructed row by row into [78 pages, one per claimed overlap](./overlap/overview). None of them is a finding by this site.**

| The question people actually ask | The number |
|---|---|
| **Overlaps with an Egyptian-registered jet, all told** | **78** |
| **Overlaps with Charlie Kirk where Erika is *not* claimed with him** | **13** |
| **Overlaps claiming Charlie *and* Erika together** | **1** — 10 September 2025, and only that one |
| **Overlaps involving Charlie at all** (13 + the shared row) | **14** |
| **Overlaps involving Erika at all** (60 alone + the shared row) | **61** |
| **Overlaps at a Turning Point event where neither Kirk is claimed present** | **4** |
| **Overlaps where the aircraft was [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), the yellow plane** | **54** |
| **Overlaps where the aircraft was [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), the blue plane** | **16** |
| **Overlaps where even the tail number is unresolved** | **8** — 5 logged as "SU-BTT or SU-BND", 3 with no tail at all |

**Now the part that changes how the whole thing reads.**

**Erika Kirk's presence is not established on a single one of those 61 rows.** Her [flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). No dated itinerary for her has ever been published — not by the compilers, not by Turning Point, not by anyone. Of **139 sourced Turning Point and Kirk appearances** in [the speaking-events catalog](./speaking/overview), **exactly one** places her at an event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. What the sheet has instead is a column called *Erika Location*, and of the 61 rows that column carries:

* **42** name a single **US state** — not a city, not a venue, not an address.
* **11** name **two places at once** — *"Kansas / Delaware"*, *"Delaware / Nebraska"*, *"Missouri / Cairo"*.
* **8** name **nowhere at all.**
* **2** get as specific as a city or a venue. **Two, out of sixty-one.**

**So this page applies a rule, and states it plainly: where it cannot be established that Erika Kirk was somewhere, she is treated as not having been there.** That is the ordinary standard for every other claim on this site, and there is no reason this one gets an exception. Applying it:

| Under that rule | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Overlaps where Erika Kirk's presence is established** | **0** |
| **Overlaps with Charlie Kirk alone** — the 13, plus the shared row, where Charlie is documented at UVU and Erika is a Delaware cell | **14** |
| **Overlaps at a Turning Point event with neither Kirk present** | **4** |
| **Rows where no Kirk can be placed at all** | **60** |

**Read that against the headline. The number in circulation is "73 times those planes followed Erika Kirk." The number of those that can be shown to have involved Erika Kirk is zero, and the number that can be shown to have involved Charlie Kirk is fourteen.** The 73 is not a lie — it is a real count of rows on a real spreadsheet — but it is counting a column nobody has ever been able to check.

**This does not make the pattern go away, and it is important to say why.** Foreign-government jets did land at these small fields, repeatedly, and the aircraft side of the record is checkable — 18 of the 78 aircraft positions were confirmed accurate by a hostile audit. What collapses is the *attribution to Erika*, which is three-quarters of the count. What is left standing is smaller, and Charlie-shaped, and harder to dismiss: of the **six dates** that survive a same-day test at a shared field, **three are Charlie Kirk**, **two are a Turning Point event with no Kirk at it**, and the sixth — Wilmington, 10 September — is an Erika claim that fails this rule. **Two of the three Charlie dates are in Utah.**

The full working is in [Who the overlaps are actually with](#who-the-overlaps-are-actually-with--the-count-broken-open) further down, and every row has [its own page](./overlap/overview).

## The four source layers

Every figure on this page came out of a broadcast, spread as posts, was then written up by outlets, and is now restated by AI assistants to anyone who asks. **Those are four different kinds of evidence and they are held on four different pages, because collapsing them is how one source starts to look like twelve.**

**[The Candace Owens broadcasts](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts)** are the origin. That page is the show-by-show record of what was actually said on air: the **68 overlaps that became 73 in the same breath**, the **29 that also included Charlie** — which means, on the show's own arithmetic, that roughly forty of them did not — the **0.000000001% probability** offered without a model, the **second-level [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) transponder timings for 10 September** (on at 09:05:36, off at 10:34:05, back on at 12:40:53, off at 13:29:40, aircraft never moved), the **contractor and FBO rental-car claims**, and the **920 N King Street** allegation. It also carries the ledger of what none of it established — no manifest, no work order, no stated time base, no published model.

**[X Discussions of the Following Planes](./x_discussions)** is the spread: every post the topic queries returned, from [@BasedSamParker](https://x.com/BasedSamParker)'s Inshas Airbase thread through the Erika-not-Charlie pivot to the [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) and [KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat) audits that cut the count apart — and a seventh section for the posts a later pass surfaced, including the longest and least sourced version of the whole theory.

**[Press coverage](./Press_Coverage)** is the write-up layer, and it is the one that should change how you read the numbers. **Sportskeeda, IBTimes, Factually, the Economic Times, the Times of India, the Hindustan Times, Document, and the Daily Wire are all reporting the same single broadcast.** Not one outlet in that record pulled the flight history and published its own dated list. Twelve bylines, one source — and the count arriving rounded as "nearly 70" or "around 70" on the way through. That page also carries **Erika Kirk's dispute of the location claims**, including her CBS town-hall response, which matters more than a denial usually does: she is the only person who could produce the itinerary the 73-count was measured against, and the count was assembled without it.

**[AI assistant answers](./AI_Assistant_Answers)** is the fourth layer and the newest one: what Google Gemini, Grok and the rest return when a member of the public asks the question directly — which for most readers is now the first answer and often the only one. That page carries one such answer verbatim and audits it. **Twelve of its assertions carry the same citation, and that citation is a single public note page** — no ADS-B pulled, no analyst named, no document quoted, and **not one YouTube or podcast source returned even though the question asked for them by name**. It also holds the one factual sentence in that answer this site's own primary-record page contradicts outright: that the [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) HADES jet was "nowhere near Utah" on 10 September, when the El Paso-to-Kalispell leg it cites as the alibi **is the leg that carried it over Orem**. What the assistants get right — the 66 percent spreadsheet audit, the time-zone correction, the unpublished Erika itinerary — is on the page too, with the attribution they stripped restored.

**And two locations in the index below are not overlap locations at all.** [Cairo](./Cairo_HECA_2022-11-13_to_2025-10-12) is the home field where every rotation begins and ends — which is also, precisely, what the auditors mean when they say a majority of the spreadsheet rows put the aircraft on **the wrong continent**. It is where the [Inshas Airbase and El Sa'ka Forces argument](./Cairo_HECA_2022-11-13_to_2025-10-12) belongs, and where the reasons that argument does not hold are set out. [Sharm el-Sheikh](./SharmElSheikh_HESH_2025-10-13_to_2025-10-13) is the 13 October 2025 tarmac shot of SU-BTT beside **Air Force One** — **no Kirk presence, no overlap, and not a 74th row on anybody's tally.** What it does is remove "some charter operator sent a jet" from the list of explanations for Provo. That is a real narrowing, and it is the only thing it narrows.

## Who the overlaps are actually with — the count broken open

The headline is "73 times those planes followed them." **They are not 73 of the same thing, and most of them are not about Charlie Kirk at all.** Every claimed row has now been reconstructed into [overlaps.csv](/Planes/following/overlap/overview) — 78 entries, each with its own page — and sorted by *whose* presence the compilation actually asserts. This is the sheet's own attribution, not a verdict by this site.

| Who the sheet claims was there | Rows | Share | What that means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Erika Kirk alone** | **60** | **77%** | The overwhelming majority. Charlie Kirk is **not** claimed present on any of these. |
| **Charlie Kirk alone** | **13** | 17% | Erika is not claimed present on these. |
| **Both Kirks together** | **1** | 1% | **One row in seventy-eight** claims the two of them in the same place at the same time — 10 September 2025. |
| **A TPUSA event, neither Kirk present** | **4** | 5% | Turning Point activity — a chapter opening, a Faith-tour stop — with no Kirk claimed at it. |
| **Total claimed overlaps** | **78** | | |

**Read across that table before reading anything else about this claim.** The number that travels — 73, or 77, or "more than 70" — is being heard by the public as *seventy-three times foreign jets followed Charlie Kirk*. On the compilation's own attribution it is nothing of the kind:

* **Overlaps with Charlie Kirk: 14 of 78** — the 13 rows claiming him alone, plus the single row claiming both Kirks. That is **18%** of the count. The Owens team's own companion figure is "about 23 with Charlie" — **the reconstruction does not reach it**, and nobody has published the list that would.
* **Overlaps with Erika Kirk: 61 of 78** — the 60 claiming her alone, plus the shared row. **78%** of the entire count rests on her.
* **Overlaps without Erika Kirk: 17 of 78.** Take her out and the pattern loses three-quarters of its mass.
* **Overlaps at a Turning Point event where neither Kirk is claimed to have been present: 4.** These are the rows where the jet is being matched to the *organisation* rather than to a person.

**And that 78% is the part of the ledger nobody can check.** Erika Kirk's flight logs are [reported as erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Of **139 sourced Turning Point and Kirk appearances** catalogued in [the speaking events index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), **exactly one** places Erika Kirk at an event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. So the largest component of the largest number in this investigation is measured against an itinerary that has never been published by anyone — its rows come from a spreadsheet column that often names a whole state, sometimes two states at once, and sometimes hedges with the word "potentially."

**Then the audit cuts it again.** An independent line-by-line check of the same sheet against paid flight-history archives found:

| Audit verdict on the aircraft's position | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Accurate** — the jet really was there that day | **18** |
| **Inaccurate** — the jet was somewhere else, usually another continent | **44** |
| Partially accurate — right aircraft and date, wrong route or duration | 5 |
| Not assessable — the flight archive does not reach back that far | 2 |
| **Never published** — a number with no date, city, airport or tail attached | **5** |
| Not reached by the audit | 4 |

**Forty-four of seventy-eight claimed overlaps put the aircraft in the wrong place**, including nine claimed Wichita visits on dates the plane was in Egypt, three 2022 rows invalidated because the aircraft was in France from 25 September to 10 November 2022, and **duplicate rows counted twice** — index 61 duplicates 31, index 63 duplicates 33, and index 56 is index 14 re-entered with the year advanced.

**What survives is small, and it is worth more than what was lost.** Of all 78 rows, only **nine** fall within three days of a Turning Point or Charlie Kirk appearance this site can independently source in the same state — and only **six** survive a same-day-or-adjacent test at a shared field. **Two of those six are in Utah.** One is 23 April 2024, when SU-BTT landed at Provo the same day Charlie Kirk spoke at the University of Utah. The other is 10 September 2025.

**That is the honest shape of it: not seventy-three, and not zero.** A six-date pattern concentrated on one state, standing inside a three-year record that otherwise resolves into maintenance rotations, is a narrower claim than the one being broadcast — and a much harder one to dismiss. The inflated version is what lets a hostile reader throw out the September window along with the spreadsheet.

**Four of the five states resolve to a maintenance or clearance function. Utah is the one that does not resolve cleanly**, and it is where this section's attention belongs. That is not a concession that the other four are innocent; it is a statement about where the remaining work is.

**And the clustering omits the two busiest places the fleet actually went.** [Paris–Le Bourget](./Paris_LFPB_2022-10-05_to_2025-10-05) takes **18 recorded stops** — more than any American field except [Wilmington](./Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12) — and [Goose Bay](./GooseBay_CYYR_2023-05-09_to_2025-09-13) takes the Gulfstreams every time they cross. Neither is in France or Labrador because of anything either Kirk did. **A geography claim that leaves out the two most-visited nodes is describing where Turning Point had activity, not where the aircraft went.**

There is one geographic fact the clustering misses in the other direction, and it is the one worth chasing: **every Utah-bound rotation entered the United States through North Dakota** — [Williston](./Williston_KXWA_2024-04-23_to_2024-04-23) in 2024, [Minot](./Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04) four times in 2025 — while every other rotation used a conventional hub.

The clustering also omits what does not fit. **[Detroit](./Detroit_KDTW_2025-02-03_to_2025-03-30) is not in it** — the fleet used it twice as an entry point, and nothing was happening in Michigan. That is what you would expect if the itinerary were driven by maintenance and the cluster were an artefact of where TPUSA happened to have activity.

## The route template, which never varies

Every US rotation by every Falcon in this fleet, across eleven months and three aircraft:

> **Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US entry field → a 15–20 minute repositioning hop → sit six to eight days → [Wilmington](./Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12) → Cairo.**

* **The dwell is fixed at five to eight days**, at every location, regardless of city. A tasking following a person would produce dwells tracking that person's schedule.
* **The repositioning hops are 15 to 19 minutes** — Omaha→Lincoln seven times, St. Louis Lambert→Downtown once. **No surveillance purpose is served by flying a Falcon 7X a quarter of an hour between two Nebraska cities.** It is an aircraft moving itself to a shop.
* **The two Gulfstreams do not use Wilmington at all.** [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) and [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM) route through **[Goose Bay, Labrador](./GooseBay_CYYR_2023-05-09_to_2025-09-13)** instead — a range split, not a mission split. The Falcon 7X can reach Cairo from Wilmington; the older Gulfstream IVs need the North Atlantic tech stop.
* **[Paris–Le Bourget](./Paris_LFPB_2022-10-05_to_2025-10-05) is westbound only, and always an overnight.** Sixteen recorded Cairo–Paris crossings all fall between **3:56 and 4:22**; every one is followed by a morning departure to a US field the next day. Going home, all three Falcons fly [Wilmington](./Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12) → Cairo nonstop and skip Paris entirely. **That asymmetry is the jet stream, not a mission** — and it has to be conceded before the September window can be argued honestly.
* **Every Utah-bound rotation entered the United States through North Dakota.** [Williston](./Williston_KXWA_2024-04-23_to_2024-04-23) in April 2024, [Minot](./Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04) four times in 2025. Five flights, two years, no exceptions — while the Nebraska, Michigan and Missouri rotations used ordinary hubs. **Nobody has explained it, and the customs records for all five are in a state nobody has contacted.**

## What the audit did and did not dissolve

The [full row-by-row audit is on the hub](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika). Its structural findings, which apply to every page in this directory:

* **The source list contains duplicated rows that are counted.** Index 61 duplicates 31; 63 duplicates 33; 65 duplicates 35; and **56 is index 14 re-entered with the year advanced** — the same claimed event, counted twice, with the second instance describing an aircraft 1,200 miles from where the sheet places it.
* **The matching window is ±3 days on every single row**, per the sheet's own `Overlap Window` column. That is a seven-day window, conceded in a column of its own.
* **The sheet's own `Charlie Present` column reads "No" on 32 of 35 visible rows.** On its own data, these aircraft were overwhelmingly not overlapping with Charlie Kirk.
* **Erika Kirk's location is sourced to "Instagram confirms presence"** in the `Notes` column — undated, unlinked, unarchived.
* **The `Event/Reason` column names no actual events.** Every entry is a category label — "TPUSA appearances / media," "TPUSA / campus engagement" — with no venue, city, or date a reader could check.

**And the September core survives all of it.** The auditor marks the **4 September Provo arrival accurate**, the **13 September SU-BND departure accurate**, the **23 May SU-BND Provo landing accurate**, and confirms the **10 September departure** while correcting its stated origin from "Paris" to Provo. **The multi-year pattern does not survive. The September window does.**

The investigation is better served by the narrow surviving claim than by the wide one. A seven-day-window spreadsheet with duplicate rows is what a hostile reviewer reaches for first, and reaching for it lets them dismiss the September facts along with it.

## What we do not know

Named, so the holes are visible rather than papered over:

1. **No manifest for any leg has ever been published**, by anyone, for any of these aircraft, at any location.
2. **The customs records are in North Dakota, not Utah.** Every Provo arrival came in on a domestic leg from **[Minot](./Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04)**. Whoever was aboard was processed there — and **nobody has asked North Dakota for anything.** This is the largest unexploited lead in the aircraft angle of the case.
3. **The Kirk-side event dates are largely uncited**, coming from a researcher timeline circulated on X.
4. **The 27 May 2025 Provo arrival is attributed to two different aircraft** — [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV) in the flight-history export, [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU) in the Owens account. A named-passenger inference rests on which is right. **SU-BTV is hex 0101D1, SU-BTU is hex 0101D0**; the raw archive would settle it in minutes.
5. **The window itself is unresolved.** The earliest overlap that survives audit is **13 November 2022** (Wichita) — but the auditor also notes the archive "doesn't go back this far" for some 2022 rows, so the true start is unknown in **both** directions.
6. **No Duncan Aviation work order, invoice, or shop record has been produced** for any of these visits. A single confirmation of which airframes were in the shop on which dates would close most of this directory.
7. **The 0.000000001% probability claim has no published model** — no assumptions, no denominator, no list of compared locations. It is quoted on the [broadcast page](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) because it was said, not because it computes.
8. **The 10 September transponder timings have no stated time base.** Local Mountain Time or UTC changes their meaning entirely — and a UTC-versus-local confusion is exactly the error that produced the disputed SU-BTT departure time on the same morning. A second tracker's reading of the same cycles puts the power-up **21 minutes before** the shot rather than 17 minutes after.
9. **No outlet independently pulled the flight data.** Every write-up in the [press record](./Press_Coverage) is summarising one broadcast. The volume of coverage is not corroboration.
10. **The 68→73 revision was never reconciled** into a single sourced list, and the compilation it rests on — credited to [@alsacetx](https://x.com/alsacetx) — is a set of ADS-B reads, not a primary record.
