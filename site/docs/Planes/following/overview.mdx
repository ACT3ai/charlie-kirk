# Planes That Followed Them

Trackers on X, led in public by [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), say Egyptian-registered VIP jets repeatedly landed at the same airports Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, or a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) event used — the same day, the day before, or the day after — and they count that coincidence in the high sixties to the high seventies. Every figure on this page is a **researcher tally read from public ADS-B history**, not a confirmed record. The full X discussion, one topic at a time, is on **[X Discussions of the Following Planes](./x_discussions)**.

The claim is not one dramatic flight. It is the **repetition**: [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) (the "yellow plane"), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) (the "blue plane"), and the rest of the Israel/Egyptian armada showing up at the same fields the Kirks and TPUSA used, often small regional or Army airfields, across a window researchers date from **2022 through September 2025**. The existing hub for that allegation is **[Planes Following Charlie or Erika](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika)**. This directory is the public location-by-location log of that same claim; the first page in it is the X-discussion dump, because the counts, the dates, and the disputes all started as posts.

The published tallies do not agree, and the disagreement is itself the finding. Owens's team and amplifiers have used **73 Erika / about 23 Charlie**, **72**, **"70+"**, **68 Erika of which 29 also hit Charlie**, **65–75**, and **77**. [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) says that under a same-airport, same-day rule the overlap **dwindles to one** — Utah on September 10. [KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat) says **66 percent** of the spreadsheet locations are wrong and **60 percent** put the jets on the wrong continent. Those numbers are recorded side by side on the [X discussions page](./x_discussions); they are not averaged.

What to read next: start with the [X discussions page](./x_discussions) if you want every sourced post, then the [hub](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) for the compressed claim, then the tail-number pages — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU) — and [Erika's erased flight logs](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), because the Erika side of every tally is the weak side. Location pages for Provo, Omaha, Wilmington, Wichita, and Minot are the next pages this directory will carry.

## The count dispute, shown as a dispute

Do not pick a winner from this table. The [X discussions page](./x_discussions) quotes the posts that produced each number.

* **73 / ~23** — Owens-team headline, also in the investigation file: Egyptian planes had 73 overlaps with Erika, only about 23 with Charlie.
* **72** — "@JustTheTweets17, 18 November 2025: "72 times those exact planes followed Erika Kirk, not a plane like it, those 2 planes.""
* **70+ / around 70 / more than 70** — Shadow of Ezra, David Nino Rodriguez, and later Grok-on-X restatements.
* **68 / 29** — Morgan Ariel, 17 November 2025: blue and yellow jets together, 2022–September 2025, 29 of them also including Charlie.
* **65–75** — Liz Wheeler's paraphrase of the same spreadsheet.
* **77** — two November/December 2025 posts treat 77 overlaps over 44 months as a "fact" in a debate.
* **1** — Liz Wheeler's same-day, same-airport reduction: Utah, 9/10, after the shooting, jets already on the ground.

## Counterargument

The innocent reading, stated on every page in this directory: Duncan Aviation at Provo has serviced Falcon and VIP jets under a relationship trackers themselves date to about **1999**; Wichita is a Falcon-maintenance hub; Wilmington is a mid-Atlantic tech stop; Army fields are ordinary for a foreign government VIP jet. ADS-B has holes. Erika Kirk's flight logs are reported as erased, so most Erika "overlaps" are the foreign jet's track plus a claimed location for her. A page that hides that is propaganda. The [X discussions page](./x_discussions) carries the Wheeler methodology critique, the Kanekoa spreadsheet audit, Erika Kirk's on-camera denial, John Cullen's maintenance argument, and the UTC-versus-local departure-time correction in full.
