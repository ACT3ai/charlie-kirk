# Provo (KPVU) — April 2024 to September 2025

**Primary ADS-B position data places two Egyptian-registered government jets on the ground at Provo Municipal Airport on 10 September 2025 — the day Charlie Kirk was killed about 5 miles from Orem, the event city away at Utah Valley University.** [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)'s closest recovered fix that day is **1.23 km** from [KPVU](/Planes/Airports/KPVU); [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)'s is **1.29 km**. **SU-BTT had been parked on that ramp since [4 September](/Planes/following/overlap/20250904_UT_provo_owens_040/overview) — six days before, closest fix 0.16 km — and lifted off at 07:14 MDT that morning. SU-BND never moved that day, and stayed until [13 September](/Planes/following/overlap/20250913_UT_provo_owens_042/overview).**

That is not a reading of a tracking website. It is the archived position record, pulled from independent ADS-B archives and held in this repo. **It places an airframe. It places nobody aboard, and no archive anywhere puts Charlie or Erika Kirk on any of these aircraft.**

**All five Egyptian-registered aircraft in this investigation — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) and [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) — landed at Provo Municipal Airport, a regional field seven miles from Utah Valley University, across a seventeen-month window ending three days after [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed there.** For 113 consecutive days of that window, at least one of them was continuously parked on the ramp. This is the one location in the shadowing claim that survives the sceptics' own line-by-line audit intact.

Everything below is drawn from Flightradar24 flight-history exports compiled by **@WeAre_TheElite** and from a researcher timeline circulated on X. It is public ADS-B data read by independent researchers. **It is not an official record, and no manifest for any of these flights has ever been published.**

### How unusual is a foreign government jet here

Genuinely unusual, and the reason has nothing to do with runway length. Provo has a runway that will take a Falcon 7X or a Gulfstream IV without difficulty. What Provo does not have is the thing a foreign state aircraft normally needs: **it is not a routine international port of entry for this class of movement.** Diplomatic and state aircraft ordinarily arrive at major hubs where customs, protocol and handling infrastructure exist.

The flight record shows how that was solved, and the answer is the most-overlooked fact about this whole cluster: **every one of these aircraft entered the United States somewhere else and reached Provo on a domestic leg.** Four of the five came in via [Minot, North Dakota](../Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04/overview); one came via Salt Lake City. **US customs processing for every person aboard happened at the entry field, not at Provo.**

So the honest form of the "why a small airport" question is not *how did they clear customs there* — they didn't — but *why choose a Utah regional field as the domestic destination.* And there the ordinary answer is on the airport diagram: **Duncan Aviation runs an MRO on the field**, and the same company has held the Egyptian Air Force's maintenance account since 1999.

### Three things in that table that are new

**1. Two Egyptian jets were parked here simultaneously in April 2025.** [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview) was on the ground 8–14 April. [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) flew a Provo→Provo sortie on 10 April and did not leave Utah until 16 April. **Five months before the assassination, two foreign-registered jets shared this ramp for the better part of a week.** That has not previously been set out anywhere on this site, and it cuts hard in both directions — it deepens the Provo relationship, and it makes the September stay look like the fifth in a series rather than a singular event.

**2. From 23 May to 13 September 2025 — 113 days — a foreign-registered jet was continuously parked here.** SU-BND arrived on 23 May and did not leave until 13 September. Every other arrival in that period, including SU-BTT's September stay, happened at an airport where SU-BND was already sitting.

**3. In those 113 days, SU-BND flew exactly twice.** Both were **Provo→Provo round-robins** that landed where they started: one on **10 June** (no duration logged) and one on **5 September, lasting 1 hour 8 minutes** — five days before the assassination. A jet that sits for four months and flies twice is not conducting operations. But a 68-minute closed-loop sortie by an aircraft that had not moved in three months is also not nothing, and it is the single flight on this page that nobody has adequately explained.

**A fourth point, which is the counter to the third.** [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) flew the *same* signature on **10 April 2025** — a **55-minute Provo→Provo round-robin**. Two different Egyptian jets, five months apart, each flew exactly one closed-loop local flight out of Provo. The ordinary explanation for both is a **post-maintenance functional check flight**, which is what an aircraft does after a heavy inspection before it is released: roughly an hour, and back to the shop that did the work. Two completed maintenance visits produce exactly two of these. If the 5 September flight is treated as anomalous, consistency requires treating the 10 April one the same way — and an April closed-loop five months before the event is very hard to fit into any narrative about the assassination.

### The pairing that actually holds, and the one that does not

**23 April 2024 is the strongest documented pairing anywhere in the pre-2025 record.** Charlie Kirk was at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City doing a *Prove Me Wrong* event. SU-BTT landed at Provo — 45 miles south — at 12:13 pm the same day, having flown Cairo → Paris → Williston, North Dakota → Provo. Same day, same metropolitan region, documented on both sides. The [independent audit](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) marks this flight **accurate** (its index 26: *"Acc 4/22-23. Williston code XWA"*).

**The September 2025 pairing does not hold in the way it is usually presented.** During the six days SU-BTT sat at Provo, Charlie Kirk was **in Seoul, South Korea** on 5 and 6 September, per the same researcher timeline that supplies the April pairing. Erika Kirk has reportedly said she **stayed home in Arizona on 10 September**. Neither Kirk is documented at Provo at any point during the fleet's September presence. What is documented is that **an event was scheduled at UVU on 10 September** and that the aircraft was on the field for six days beforehand and left that morning.

That is a real fact and it does not need inflating. It is also not the same claim as "the plane was following him."

## The 27 May tail-number dispute

The 27 May – 2 June 2025 row is attributed to two different aircraft by two different sources, and something substantial rides on which is right.

* **The Flightradar24 export** puts **[SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview)** at Provo from 27 May to 2 June, and puts **[SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview)** at Provo six weeks earlier, 8–14 April.
* **[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)'s August 2026 segment** puts **SU-BTU** at Provo on 27 May, departing 2 June, and builds a chain of inference on it: a name allegedly in use aboard the 10 September flight was, she says, also in Provo in late May aboard SU-BTU — which she then links to the Egyptian presidential fleet via a claimed record of President el-Sisi flying aboard SU-BTU to Riyadh in April 2023.

**If the tail is wrong, that entire chain dissolves.** SU-BTU and SU-BTV differ by one character; they are consecutive serial numbers (243 and 246) of the same aircraft type, delivered a year apart, flying near-identical routes. Owens's own segment acknowledges tail numbers being conflated in this coverage.

This page takes no position on which is correct. It records that **the question is open, has not been asked publicly before, and is settleable in minutes** by anyone with archive access: **SU-BTV is hex 0101D1, SU-BTU is hex 0101D0**, and the raw ADS-B record for 27 May 2025 into KPVU carries one of them.

## The counterargument

Every innocent explanation for this location, stated at full strength.

* **Duncan Aviation operates a full-service MRO on this field**, and the same company has held the **Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a five-year direct contract announced in an October 2015 trade press release. Duncan's own manager anticipated the objection in that release: the EAF *"has chosen to fly over closer maintenance companies"* to reach its facilities. A foreign VIP fleet routing through a Duncan facility is a documented, decades-old arrangement.
* **A 113-day stay is a maintenance stay, not an operation.** A 1998-build Gulfstream IV in for a heavy check, paint and an avionics upgrade looks exactly like SU-BND's record: arrive, sit for four months, fly one functional check flight, leave. The independent auditor of the overlap sheet — a hostile reviewer — volunteers the same explanation unprompted, writing **"ACCURATE POSS BROKEN JET GROUNDED IN PROVO."**
* **The two Provo→Provo round-robins fit post-maintenance check flights.** 55 minutes and 68 minutes, by two different aircraft, five months apart, each landing where it started.
* **The six-day dwell is constant across every visit and every city.** Provo, Lincoln, Lincoln, Wichita — six to eight days every time regardless of destination. A tasking following a person would produce dwells that track that person's schedule.
* **Neither Kirk is documented here.** Charlie Kirk was in Seoul during part of the September stay; Erika Kirk reportedly stayed in Arizona on 10 September.

## And the counter to the counterargument

Fairness runs in both directions, and three things the maintenance reading does not settle:

1. **Why Provo rather than Lincoln in September.** Every other 2025 rotation by this fleet that was clearly maintenance went to **Lincoln** — the main plant, three times — or to **Wichita**. The September visit went to Provo. So did April and late May. Something changed about Utah in 2025, and no published account explains what.
2. **The 5 September SU-BND sortie.** A 68-minute closed-loop flight by an aircraft dormant for three months, five days before the event, has been characterised by one analyst as consistent with a corridor mapping run or an electronic sweep. It is also consistent with a check flight. Nobody has published the raw track that would distinguish them.
3. **The 07:14 departure on the morning of the shooting.** The maintenance explanation covers why the aircraft was at Provo. It does not address the timing of its departure, which is the fact that started all of this.

## What we do not know

Named, so the holes are visible:

1. **No manifest exists in public for any of these seven arrivals.** Not one.
2. **The customs records are in North Dakota.** Every 2025 Provo arrival came in on a domestic leg from **Minot (MOT)**. Anyone seeking entry records, passport data or a passenger list is looking in the wrong state if they are looking in Utah.
3. **SU-BGM's arrival date is unknown.** The export begins with the aircraft already at Provo on 10 April 2025. When it arrived, and via where, is not in the published record.
4. **SU-BND's July 2024 departure is reported, not exported.** The "sat there until 12 July" note comes from the audit's commentary rather than from a leg-by-leg export.
5. **The 10 June 2025 Provo→Provo flight has no duration logged**, meaning the tracker captured a departure and an arrival without a continuous track. What happened in between is not in the data.
6. **The Kirk-side event dates are uncited.** They come from a researcher timeline on X. Every one of them needs a primary source before it should be relied on.
7. **The Duncan Aviation work orders would settle most of this**, and are not public.

## Where this page fits
