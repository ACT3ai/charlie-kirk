# Sharm el-Sheikh (HESH) — 13 October 2025

**On 13 October 2025, [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) — the yellow plane that sat at [Provo](./Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13) from 4 September through the morning of 10 September — was photographed on the tarmac at Sharm el-Sheikh beside Air Force One, during the Gaza peace summit.** The sighting is in Fox News live coverage of the summit, in the shot of Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi's motorcade as Air Force One landed.

This page is the odd one out in this directory. **Nobody claims Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, or TPUSA was ever at Sharm el-Sheikh.** There is no overlap here and no gap to state. It is not a 74th row on anybody's tally, and it must not be counted as one.

What it is instead: **the single strongest piece of 2026-era corroboration for one specific sub-claim** — that the aircraft at Provo was a state aircraft from the Egyptian presidential fleet, not a chartered business jet. That is a real narrowing, and it is narrower than what the posts around it say.

### How unusual is a foreign government jet here

**Not unusual at all — and that is the point of the counterargument.** Sharm el-Sheikh during an Egypt-hosted heads-of-state summit is exactly where an Egyptian presidential-fleet aircraft belongs. The anomaly the posts are pointing at is not the Sharm appearance. It is the **Provo** appearance, seen in the light of the Sharm one.

## What this sighting supports, and what it does not

**Supports — one thing, and it is worth having.** If the Fox News frame is what these accounts say it is, then SU-BTT is a state aircraft operating in the Egyptian government's summit fleet. That removes "some charter operator sent a jet" from the list of available explanations for [Provo](./Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13) and makes the operator a government. It also lines up with the separate identification of **[SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU)** as President el-Sisi's personal jet, sourced to an Egyptian Presidency archive record of a 3 April 2023 Riyadh arrival.

**Does not support — everything else in the posts above.**

* **It is not a new overlap.** Zero Kirk-side presence, zero TPUSA event, wrong continent, five weeks after the assassination. Any tally that grows because of this sighting has been padded.
* **It does not confirm the contractor story.** "Carrying Egyptian military contractors," "deplaned in Provo," "got rental cars from Duncan Aviation," "left with only four people on board" — none of that is established by a photograph of the aircraft in Egypt in October. Those are [Candace Owens broadcast claims](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) and they still rest on the same unpublished manifest they rested on before.
* **It does not resolve the departure-time dispute.** The FlightRadar time-zone argument the posts dismiss as an attack is a real unreconciled conflict, recorded on the [Provo page](./Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13) and on [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT): the 10 September departure has been given as **07:08 MT**, **07:14**, and in one account as a Dugway rather than Provo takeoff.
* **It does not defeat the maintenance explanation.** Presidential fleets need maintenance too. A state VIP jet is *more* likely, not less, to be ferried to a factory-authorised service centre — and [Duncan Aviation's Egyptian Air Force account is dated by trackers themselves to 1999](./Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11).
* **The Tyler Robinson line in the reposts is not this site's position.** Tyler Robinson is **charged, not convicted**.
* **"Vindicated" is doing too much work.** The word appears in the video titles around this sighting. What was vindicated is the tail number's fleet status. The overlap count, the contractor manifest, and the tasking question are all exactly where they were.

## Counterargument

The innocent reading of this sighting is strong and short: **an Egyptian presidential-fleet aircraft at an Egypt-hosted international summit is the most ordinary thing in this entire directory.** Grok-on-X's reply to these posts said exactly that — "normal VIP traffic" — and it is correct.

Both sentences can be true at once, and this page holds both:

* A presidential-fleet jet at Sharm el-Sheikh during a summit Egypt is hosting is ordinary.
* A presidential-fleet jet parked at **Provo Municipal Airport** for the six days before the assassination is the thing that still needs a same-day, same-airport log and a manifest.

The second sentence is not made truer by the first. It is made *harder to wave off*, which is a different and smaller thing.

## What we do not know

1. **The frame has not been independently archived on this site.** The claim rests on reposted clips of a live broadcast. A timestamped archive of the Fox News segment, or a wire photo of the same tarmac, would settle whether the tail reads SU-BTT.
2. **Whether the aircraft was there in a fleet role or was simply parked.** A tarmac position beside Air Force One is a ramp assignment, not a mission.
3. **Who was aboard on 13 October 2025** — the same unanswered question as every other leg in this directory.
4. **Whether SU-BTT's fleet status was different in September than in October.** Nothing in the record establishes continuity of role across the five weeks.
