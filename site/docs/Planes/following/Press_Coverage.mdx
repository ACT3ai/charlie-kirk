# Press Coverage of the Egyptian-Plane Claim

The overlap claim has three layers and this is the third. **[The broadcasts](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts)** are where it was said. **[The X posts](./x_discussions)** are where it spread. This page is where it was **written up by outlets** — and the write-ups matter for a reason that has nothing to do with whether they are sympathetic.

**They are how the numbers drifted.** A claim that left the studio as "68, and I can now add 73" arrived in aggregator copy as "nearly 70," "around 70," "more than 70," and eventually as a flat "73" with no origin attached. **Every one of those outlets is reporting the same single source.** Independent coverage would mean an outlet that pulled the ADS-B history itself and published its own list. **No outlet in this record did that.** What looks like corroboration across a dozen publications is one broadcast, re-narrated a dozen times.

**Nothing on this page is a verified total.** The counts are a researcher's tallies read from public flight-tracking history. Where sources conflict, the conflict is the finding.

## The coverage, and what each layer actually adds

| Layer | Outlets | What it adds | What it does not add |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Origin** | The broadcast itself | The **68 → 73** revision, the **29-with-Charlie** split, the transponder timings, the 0.000000001% claim | — |
| **Aggregators** | **Sportskeeda**, **IBTimes**, **Factually.co** summaries | Reach; a written record of the numbers as of a date | **Zero independent verification.** They summarise the show. |
| **International press** | **Economic Times**, **Times of India**, **Hindustan Times** | Reach far outside the US audience; the claim becomes an internationally reported story | No flight data of their own. The numbers arrive already rounded — "nearly 70," "around 70." |
| **European** | **Document.se / Document.no** (Scandinavian) | Coverage in a second language, which is how the claim entered non-English discussion | Same single source. |
| **The reply side** | **The Daily Wire** | **Erika Kirk's response**, disputing the specific trip dates and locations, and noting she was pregnant across several of the cited periods | It is a response, not an audit. It does not publish her itinerary either. |
| **Filings and transcripts** | **CourtListener** PDFs of related discussion | That the claim surfaced in documents attached to litigation | A document quoting a claim is not a finding about the claim. |

**Read that table as one sentence:** twelve bylines, one source, no new data.

## What the coverage consistently says

Stripped to the common core, the write-ups agree on this much:

* The two aircraft are **[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)** and **[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)**, Egyptian-registered.
* The claimed window is **2022 through September 2025**.
* The count is **68 initially, later 73**, against **Erika Kirk's** documented locations, with **about 29** of those also including **Charlie Kirk**.
* The overlaps are described as **near TPUSA events, or at airports where the Kirks' aircraft were**.
* **[Provo](./Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview)** and the **4 September 2025** arrival are in nearly every version.
* Later coverage adds the **[Sharm el-Sheikh tarmac sighting](./SharmElSheikh_HESH_2025-10-13_to_2025-10-13/overview)** of 13 October 2025 as a "presidential fleet" confirmation.
* **Erika Kirk publicly addressed and disputed the location claims**, including in a CBS town hall.

**And the numbers do not match across the write-ups.** "Nearly 70," "around 70," "more than 70," "68," "73." That spread is not sloppiness at the margin — it is what happens when an unpublished tally is paraphrased by people who cannot check it. **This site does not average them.** The [count dispute is displayed as a dispute](./overview), which is the only honest way to hold five incompatible figures.

## Erika Kirk's dispute, on the record

Erika Kirk **publicly disputed the claims** about her locations. Her response, as reported:

* She **disputed the specific trip dates and locations** attributed to her.
* She noted she was **pregnant across several of the periods** in question.
* She addressed the matter **in a CBS town hall** and in an on-camera remark, quoted and answered in **[the broadcast record](./Candace_Owens_Broadcasts)**.
* Separately, she reportedly told an interviewer she had **no knowledge of the aircraft**, and has reportedly said she **stayed home in Arizona on 10 September 2025**.

**Her denial deserves more weight than a denial usually gets here, and the reason is structural.** She is the only person who could produce the itinerary the 73-count is measured against, and **the count was built without it.** Her flight logs are reported as erased — see **[Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)** — so nobody, on either side, is working from her actual travel record. **A tally assembled from a person's *claimed* whereabouts is not corroborated by the fact that the person disputes those whereabouts. It is undermined by it, unless the compiler can show the source.** No compiler has.

That does not settle the matter in her favour either. A denial is not a record. **What would settle it is her itinerary or the FBO logs, and neither has been published by anyone.**

**Erika Kirk is a living person who has not been charged with anything, has denied the claims, and is named here only as the person who responded.** Nothing on this page or in this directory asserts that she knew of, arranged, or benefited from any flight.

## The claims this site declines to carry from the coverage

Some material travelling alongside the plane story in posts and comment threads does not belong on a page about flight records, and the reason is worth stating rather than leaving as a silent omission:

* **Speculation about a personal relationship between Erika Kirk and someone aboard an aircraft.** This appeared in at least one widely-read post. **There is no evidence for it of any kind, no source has produced any, and this site does not carry it as a claim about a living person.** It is noted as having been said, and that is all.
* **The life-insurance-as-motive argument.** The policy itself is a documented matter with its own page — **[GGLF life insurance](/Suspicious/Israel/gglf-life-insurance)** — and belongs there, with its own sourcing. **Attaching it to a flight-tracking page turns a records question into an accusation of murder against a living person, on no evidence.** [Do not merge the threads.](./overview)
* **"Who called the hit."** Broadcast billings used the phrase. **No public record establishes who tasked any of these aircraft, in either direction**, and this section never asserts tasking.
* **Tyler Robinson as convicted.** Several reposts of the plane story assert the lone-shooter account is false because of the planes. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted**, and the plane record neither charges nor clears him.

## Counterargument

* **Volume of coverage is not corroboration.** Twelve outlets reporting one broadcast is one source with twelve URLs. **No outlet in this record independently pulled the flight history and published a dated list.**
* **The rounding runs one way.** "68, and I can add 73" becomes "more than 70" becomes "73." Numbers that drift upward in retelling are a sign of a claim travelling faster than its evidence.
* **The strongest audits are not in the press at all — they are on X**, from [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) (a same-day, same-airport rule reduces the overlap to **one**) and from [KanekoaTheGreat](https://x.com/KanekoaTheGreat) (**66 percent** of the listed locations wrong, **60 percent** on the wrong continent). **The press layer largely missed the critique layer.** Both are on the [X discussions page](./x_discussions).
* **The maintenance explanation was reported as a dismissal rather than examined.** Coverage repeats that Owens "was told it was just maintenance" as if that settles it in her favour. **It does not settle it in either direction** — and the specific, checkable version of the maintenance case, the [Duncan Aviation Egyptian Air Force account dated to 1999](./Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview), appears in almost none of the coverage.
* **A CourtListener PDF that quotes the claim is not a court finding about it.**

## What we do not know

1. **Whether any outlet obtained flight data of its own.** Nothing in this record indicates one did.
2. **What Erika Kirk's actual itinerary was** for any of the disputed dates. Neither side has published it.
3. **Whether the underlying spreadsheet was ever shared with any outlet** in full, with sources per row.
4. **Whether a fact-check ever addressed the narrow September claim** — a foreign state jet parked at a small Utah field for six days — as distinct from the wide 73-overlap claim. **The two get debunked together, and only the wide one deserves it.**
5. **The exact publication dates and headlines** for several of the aggregator items are not archived on this site, and are recorded above as outlets rather than as citations. That is a gap, and it is named rather than hidden.

## Where this page fits

A dozen outlets reported the Egyptian-plane claim about [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) and not one of them pulled the flight history itself, which is why this page sits below [the broadcasts](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) and [the X posts](/Planes/following/x_discussions) in the [Following log](/Planes/following/overview) rather than beside them as corroboration. Numbers drift when they are re-narrated, and the same thing has happened to nearly every figure in this case — [the phone counts at UVU](/intelligence/Foreign_Phones_UVU), [the drone counts](/Drones/overview), [the shot-timing dispute](/Timeline/12-23-shooting-time) — which is why [the media section](/Media/overview) tracks the retelling separately from the claim. Read [the AI assistant answers page](/Planes/following/AI_Assistant_Answers) next for the newest layer of the same problem: a chatbot asked about the overlaps returns one confident paragraph, in the logged case sourced to a single note on a public wiki, and most readers will never see anything else.
