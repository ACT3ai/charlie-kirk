# X accounts that posted about Erika Kirk's flights

This page is the speaker list for **[Erika Kirk's flights](./Erika_Kirk_Flights)**. Every claim in that cluster is a **reported claim by a named account**. Living people named *in* the posts stay unnamed as accused. Erika Kirk is **alive** and is named because she is the person the posts are about, and because she answered them. **This site does not adopt any poster's accusation.**

:::caution Legal Disclaimer
Nothing on this page constitutes a finding of wrongdoing by Erika Kirk or any other living person. Accounts listed here are quoted as the people who posted. Quoting them is not an endorsement.
:::

The 15 Grok / X queries that surfaced these accounts are listed on the [parent page](./Erika_Kirk_Flights). Engagement numbers are what the query returned at capture.

## Accounts this page will not launder into the flight record

Some replies returned by the same queries are attacks on Erika Kirk that are **not about flights** — trafficking claims, "honeypot" claims, personal insults. **They are not evidence of an aircraft overlap.** They are recorded only to mark that the search returned them, and they are **not carried forward as claims**. See the [press-coverage page](./Press_Coverage) for the class of material this directory declines to convert into an accusation of murder against a living person.

**[@grok](https://x.com/grok)** replies appeared in almost every query. They are relayed restatements, usually of the 66% audit plus the Duncan Aviation maintenance reading. An assistant answer is a fourth source layer, already audited on **[AI assistant answers](./AI_Assistant_Answers)**. It is not an independent flight record.

## Counterargument

* **Volume of posters is not corroboration.** The 17 November cluster is one broadcast, restated.
* **Named accounts can still be wrong.** Wheeler and Kanekoa are the reason the 73 cannot be repeated as a verified total.
* **Erika Kirk's own words** sit above every row in the first two tables. [The invitation](./Erika_Flight_Log_Invitation).
