This page is the public log of what people on X actually said about Egyptian-registered jets overlapping [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk), Erika Kirk, and [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) events. It is **not** a finding that any crew, passenger, or government tasked those aircraft. It is the discussion: every distinct claim, count, correction, denial, and counter-count that came back from one Grok/X query per row of the research topic list. The compressed version of the same allegation lives on **[Planes Following Charlie or Erika](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika)**. The tail numbers live on **[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT)** and **[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND)**.

The pattern people are arguing about, stated as they state it: a foreign-government-registered jet lands at **the same airport** Charlie, Erika, or a TPUSA event used, on the **same day, the day before, or the day after**, often at a **small** field, and it happens again and again. Trackers count that in the high sixties to the high seventies. One overlap is a coincidence. Seventy-three is the claim. **The counts are trackers' tallies, not records.** Where they conflict, the conflict is the finding.

## How this page was built

Six research topics. One Grok/X query per topic. Every distinct post those queries returned is on this page. Duplicate Grok-on-X replies that repeat the same paragraph are grouped and quoted once, with the repeat noted.

1. **Candace talks about planes.**
2. **People talk about Egyptian-registered planes that stopped at airports near a TPUSA event or Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk where their planes were at the same airport on the same day or one day off, up to 77 times.**
3. **People talk about Egyptian-registered planes that stopped at airports near a TPUSA event up to 77 times (or a number close to that).**
4. **People talk about Egyptian-registered planes at airports where Charlie Kirk's plane was, up to 77 times (or a number close to that).**
5. **People talk about Egyptian-registered planes at airports where Erika Kirk's plane was, up to 77 times (or a number close to that).**
6. **People had debates about more or less than 77 (or a number close to that) related to Egyptian-registered planes at airports where Charlie Kirk's plane was (or Erika Kirk).**

Queries ran against public X posts. Engagement numbers below are what the query returned at capture, not a permanent score. Author names are the public display names on those accounts. Living people named in the posts stay unnamed here as accused — they are quoted as speakers, not as perpetrators.

Erika Kirk's side of every pairing is the weak side. Her flight logs are reported as erased. See **[Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)**. This repo cannot reproduce a dated list of 73 Erika locations. Do not treat Charlie's itinerary as a stand-in for hers. The tallies themselves say the two counts differ by a factor of three.

The September 10 minute-by-minute, the [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) HADES passes, and [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG) are **different claims about different aircraft**. They are linked where a post mixed them into the same sentence. They are not merged into the following-pattern count.

## Counterarguments, gathered in one place

Every page in this directory has to carry the innocent reading. These queries produced these:

1. **Loose "overlap" definition.** Wheeler: plus or minus three days, 50–100 miles (later "same state"). Same-day, same-airport cut leaves one. @truecrimejes loosens it further to 500 miles.
2. **The spreadsheet locations are wrong even under the loose definition.** Kanekoa: 66 percent of plane locations wrong, 60 percent on the wrong continent. @CBarrett47: 50 of 70. @truecrimejes: 70 percent. Glamhamradio, per Kanekoa, independently flagged the sheet and got no reply.
3. **No published Erika itinerary.** Kanekoa's question — "How would she even know Erika's location?" — is the same hole as [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Pregnancy / "I was home in Arizona on 9/10" / In-N-Out is Erika's denial, not a dated counter-log.
4. **The column was never Erika's location.** Kanekoa quoting Owens, 17 December 2025: people assumed a plane-landing column with a plus-or-minus-three-day window was where Erika had been that day, "and that was inaccurate."
5. **UTC versus local on September 10.** Brick Suit caught it the morning of 12 October 2025. Owens stood corrected the same day. "Left after the shot" as a wheels-up claim does not survive that correction. The 9:05 AM power-up / go-dark story is a separate clock.
6. **Duncan Aviation maintenance.** Cullen, restated by Grok-on-X: Falcon/VIP work at Provo since about 1999. A four-month SU-BND stay is a maintenance-visit length. Wichita is a Falcon hub. Army fields are ordinary for a foreign government VIP jet.
7. **Sharm el-Sheikh is ordinary.** Grok-on-X: a presidential-fleet jet at an Egypt-hosted summit next to Air Force One is routine VIP traffic.
8. **The jets were not the airborne murder platform.** Cullen: they were not at 200 feet AGL, they do not deploy UAVs, HADES does. That is an argument for **splitting** the claims, which this directory already does.
9. **The promised interactive timeline was not posted.** Wheeler 6 January 2026; Kanekoa through 19 July 2026. Owens on 19 July 2026 still said "The Egyptian planes were involved" without publishing the update.

A page that stopped at the 73 would be propaganda. A page that stopped at Wheeler's one would also be propaganda. The claim that has to be tested is the **same-airport, same-day or one-day-off** log at specific fields — which is what the location pages in this directory are for.
