## Where this page fits

St. Louis sits in one of the five states the shadowing claim clusters on, which makes this Grace Church date one of the more load-bearing rows in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) — and it is a faith stop, not a campus hall, so it tests whether the [following pattern](/Planes/following/overview) tracks Kirk or tracks universities, a question the [event-by-event tally](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) is built to answer. The church network that grew out of stops like this is documented under [TPUSA Faith](/Charlie/Leader_of_Churches) and in the [organizations map](/organizations_groups/overview), and it is the same network that filled the stadium at the [memorial](/After/overview) in September 2025. Then read [the Jefferson City pairing](/Planes/following/overlap/20230402_MO_jefferson_city/overview): the TPUSA Faith tour launched there on 2 April 2023, the day after SU-BTT landed at [St. Louis](/Planes/following/StLouis_KSTL_2022-12-20_to_2025-02-23/overview) and while SU-BND was already parked — the tightest Kirk-side coincidence anywhere in the reconstructed sheet.
