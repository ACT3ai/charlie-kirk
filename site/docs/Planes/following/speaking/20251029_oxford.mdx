# Turning Point Tour / This Is the Turning Point — University of Mississippi

**TPUSA** at **Sandy and John Black Pavilion at Ole Miss, 762 All American Drive, Oxford, MS** (Oxford, MS) on **2025-10-29**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

**Nothing foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered was on the ground here.** A blind sweep of the entire open ADS-B archive — every aircraft flying that UTC day, roughly 74,000 to 95,000 of them, not a tail-number lookup — kept only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on Oxford, MS and found none of those four categories parked inside it on the day of the event.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Oxford, MS**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 2.5 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (12 circle-days) | 64.6 | 10.8 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

This is the last date on the original fall list and the end of the catalog's coverage — a Vice President on stage with Erika Kirk, launching a tour under her name — and it is AFTER_DEATH, which keeps it out of the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) even though it is the best-documented [Erika location](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights) in the whole file after [Glendale](/Planes/following/speaking/20250921_glendale).
