## What was actually on the ground near this event

**This date was not covered by the geographic sweep.** The sweep streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive and filters it to a 50-mile circle on the event city. It has not been run for 2022-12-17 to 2022-12-20 at Phoenix, AZ, so this page cannot say what was or was not parked nearby.

That is a **gap in our coverage, not a finding**. It is not evidence that nothing was there, and it must never be quoted as though it were.
