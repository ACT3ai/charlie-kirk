## Where this page fits

A rally at the Arcada Theatre — a few miles from where Kirk grew up — is the kind of non-campus stop that keeps the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) honest, because the [following-pattern claim](/Planes/following/overview) is usually argued off university dates and [conference weeks](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events). Illinois returns to this case later through [Chicago in 2023](/Planes/following/speaking/20230309_chicago) and through the Egyptian jets' Midwest routing, but a February 2022 mask rally sits before all of it — well before the [donor and Israel pressure](/political_context/overview) that the [motive theories](/Motive/overview) turn on. The most useful next click is [what the compilers counted as an overlap](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition): Liz Wheeler's own posts put the threshold at 50-100 miles and plus or minus three days, which is how a rally in one state gets scored against a jet in another, and then [only 23 of 69 pairs survived a primary-trace test](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Overlap-Recovery).
