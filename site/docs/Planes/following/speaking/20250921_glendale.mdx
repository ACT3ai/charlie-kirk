# Charlie Kirk stadium memorial

**Erika** at **State Farm Stadium, Glendale, AZ** (Glendale, AZ) on **2025-09-21**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **4 foreign-registered**, **5 military**, **2 government-operated** and **10 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Glendale, AZ.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: XA-FON (LJ75) at KPHX; XA-WOW (GLF5) at KPHX. 2 of the foreign-registration flags sit on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**224 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**2 recorded ground contacts by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N560TW](/Planes/N560TW/overview), [N708JH](/Planes/N708JH/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. ## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.
