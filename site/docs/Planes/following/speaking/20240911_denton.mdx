**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Denton replaces a September 26 podcast-proxy row with a real event date, and every such swap tightens the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) by one entry — which matters because the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) counted a jet as matching within plus or minus three days, so a wrong date can manufacture a pairing that the [following file](/Planes/following/overview) and the [TPUSA event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) would then carry forward. Texas is not one of the clustering states, and a row like this is most useful as counter-evidence — the tour going where the jets did not — which is the same discipline the [cover-up section](/CoverUp/overview) needs when it separates a [withheld record](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records) from a record that was never made, and the [FBI section](/FBI/overview) needs when it describes [blocked foreign leads](/FBI/Foreign_Leads). Joe Kent, then running the National Counterterrorism Center, [says on the record](/Proof_Intel_Services/Joe_Kent_Foreign_Inquiry_Shutdown) that his foreign-nexus review was stopped once a suspect was in custody — the clearest statement in this file that a lead was closed rather than exhausted.
