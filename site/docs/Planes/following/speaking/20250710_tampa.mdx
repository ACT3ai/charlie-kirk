## Where this page fits

Three Turning Point summits ran on top of each other in Tampa that week — this one, the [Chapter Leadership Summit](/Planes/following/speaking/20250709_tampa) and the [Student Action Summit](/Planes/following/speaking/20250711_tampa) — and the Educators' row is here because an August podcast had been carrying it as a separate 10 August event in an unknown city, a duplicate the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) had to collapse before it could inflate the [event list](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) behind the [following claim](/Planes/following/overview). Turning Point Academy is the K-12 arm, which puts it beside [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview)'s faith and campus networks in the [organizations map](/organizations_groups/overview) and the [companies and organizations index](/Companies_Organizations/overview) — the structure Kirk was reportedly auditing in the weeks before he died. That audit is the hook: he reportedly ordered [a DOGE-style internal financial review](/TPUSA/doge-audit) of his own organization days before his death, and citizen investigators say the leadership that replaced him quietly dropped it.
