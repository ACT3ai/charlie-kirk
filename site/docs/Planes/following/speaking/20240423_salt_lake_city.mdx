# Prove Me Wrong — University of Utah

**Charlie** at **University of Utah** (Salt Lake City, UT) on **2024-04-23**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

**A foreign-registered jet this investigation tracks has a recorded ground contact at this event:**  [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) at [KPVU](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) (Provo, UT) on [2024-04-23](/Planes/Incidents/SU-BTT-2024-04-23-KPVU), same day. These are the tails the whole following claim is about. The contact comes from the recovered primary position data for that airframe, not from the city sweep — which is why it is not in the sweep table below.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **1 foreign-registered**, **3 military**, **2 government-operated** and **10 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Salt Lake City, UT.

1 of the foreign-registration flags sits on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**157 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**3 recorded ground contacts by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview), [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview), [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. **What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

This is the tightest pre-2025 pairing anyone has produced: SU-BTT landed at Provo at 12:13 p.m. on 23 April 2024 having entered the United States at [Williston, North Dakota](/Planes/following/Williston_KXWA_2024-04-23_to_2024-04-23/overview), and Charlie Kirk did a Prove Me Wrong table at the University of Utah 45 miles north the same day — with the [Salt Lake City overlap page](/Planes/following/overlap/20240423_UT_salt_lake_city/overview) and the [Provo airport record](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) carrying the two halves. Even at its tightest it remains one coincidence on one day, and the Kirk-side citation is a researcher-compiled timeline rather than a primary document, so it is read against the [UVU material](/UVU/overview), the [Provo Municipal Airport page](/Locations/Provo_Airport) and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) rather than treated as settled — and a trace never places any person aboard an aircraft. Seventeen months later the same airport produces the reported account of four to six men boarding an Egyptian Falcon on Egyptian passports wearing U.S. DoD Liaison badges, carried with its sourcing problems on the [SU-BTT page](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and the [Egyptian foreign-operations record](/intelligence/Egyptian_Foreign_Ops).
