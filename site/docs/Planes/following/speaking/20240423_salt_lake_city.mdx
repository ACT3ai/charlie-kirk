## Where this page fits

This is the tightest pre-2025 pairing anyone has produced: SU-BTT landed at Provo at 12:13 p.m. on 23 April 2024 having entered the United States at [Williston, North Dakota](/Planes/following/Williston_KXWA_2024-04-23_to_2024-04-23/overview), and Charlie Kirk did a Prove Me Wrong table at the University of Utah 45 miles north the same day — with the [Salt Lake City overlap page](/Planes/following/overlap/20240423_UT_salt_lake_city/overview) and the [Provo airport record](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) carrying the two halves. Even at its tightest it remains one coincidence on one day, and the Kirk-side citation is a researcher-compiled timeline rather than a primary document, so it is read against the [UVU material](/UVU/overview), the [Provo Municipal Airport page](/Locations/Provo_Airport) and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) rather than treated as settled — and a trace never places any person aboard an aircraft. Seventeen months later the same airport produces the reported account of four to six men boarding an Egyptian Falcon on Egyptian passports wearing U.S. DoD Liaison badges, carried with its sourcing problems on the [SU-BTT page](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and the [Egyptian foreign-operations record](/intelligence/Egyptian_Foreign_Ops).
