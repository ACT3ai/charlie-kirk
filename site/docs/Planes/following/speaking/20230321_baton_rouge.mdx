# Live Free Tour — LSU (announced; Kirk unconfirmed)

**TPUSA** at **Louisiana State University** (Baton Rouge, LA) on **2023-03-21**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Baton Rouge, LA**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023-03-20 | Day before | 793 | 3 | 0 |
| 2023-03-21 | **Same day** | 788 | 4 | 0 |
| 2023-03-22 | Day after | 736 | 2 | 1 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2023-03-20 | 2023-03-21 | 2023-03-22 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 0 | 0 | 1 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**1 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 3.0 | 0.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 19.3 | 3.3 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

This is an announcement, not an attendance record: LSU appeared on the press list for 21 March 2023, Kirk was at [Ohio State the following night](/Planes/following/speaking/20230322_columbus), and his presence at Baton Rouge is unconfirmed — so the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) logs it as TPUSA and it must not be used as a Kirk location in the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) or the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). TPUSA returned to LSU on [27 October 2025](/Planes/following/speaking/20251027_baton_rouge), after his death, on a tour that continued without him — the same continuation the [aftermath section](/After/overview), the [leadership succession record](/TPUSA/TPUSA_Replacement_Timeline) and the [list of reversals since](/TPUSA/TPUSA_Opposite_Charlie) document. Read [what the compilers counted as an overlap](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition): with a window of plus or minus three days and 50 to 100 miles, an announced-but-unattended campus date sitting one night before a confirmed Ohio appearance is precisely the row that turns into a false positive.
