## Where this page fits

Dream City Church on 4 January 2022 is the earliest sourced Charlie Kirk appearance anywhere in this [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), which makes it the floor date for the [following-planes claim](/Planes/following/overview) — everything the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) alleges has to begin at or after a night like this one. A megachurch booking is also the [TPUSA Faith](/Charlie/Leader_of_Churches) side of [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) that rarely survives into coverage of the killing, and the pastor network built through nights like this is why [the organization](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/turning-point-usa) could fill a courtyard at [UVU](/UVU/overview) three and a half years later.
