A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Manhattan, KS**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 0.7 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 42.6 | 6.6 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). ## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

Kansas is one of the states the shadowing claim actually clusters in, so a firm Kansas date that was never in the original spreadsheet is the most useful kind of addition to the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) — testable against the [Wichita record](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview), where [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and its sister Falcons appear repeatedly between 2022 and 2025 in the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Wichita also supplies the strongest innocent explanation in the whole file — an FAA Part 145 Dassault service centre — and that is exactly the sort of alternative the [theories hub](/Theories/overview) and the [suspicious index](/Suspicious/overview) are meant to weigh before anyone reaches for [foreign-intelligence claims](/Theories/Foreign_Intelligence_Claims). The cleanest worked example is Lawrence: Kirk spoke at the University of Kansas on 3 April 2024 and [SU-BTT departed Wichita at 8:43 that morning](/Planes/following/overlap/20240403_KS_lawrence/overview), leaving the state on the day of the event.
