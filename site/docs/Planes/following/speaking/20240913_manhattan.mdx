## Where this page fits

Kansas is one of the states the shadowing claim actually clusters in, so a firm Kansas date that was never in the original spreadsheet is the most useful kind of addition to the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) — testable against the [Wichita record](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview), where [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and its sister Falcons appear repeatedly between 2022 and 2025 in the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Wichita also supplies the strongest innocent explanation in the whole file — an FAA Part 145 Dassault service centre — and that is exactly the sort of alternative the [theories hub](/Theories/overview) and the [suspicious index](/Suspicious/overview) are meant to weigh before anyone reaches for [foreign-intelligence claims](/Theories/Foreign_Intelligence_Claims). The cleanest worked example is Lawrence: Kirk spoke at the University of Kansas on 3 April 2024 and [SU-BTT departed Wichita at 8:43 that morning](/Planes/following/overlap/20240403_KS_lawrence/overview), leaving the state on the day of the event.
