Missouri is one of the five clustering-claim states, so a firmly dated Springfield stop with a named venue is worth more here than a bigger event elsewhere: it gives the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) and the [St. Louis airport page](/Planes/following/StLouis_KSTL_2022-12-20_to_2025-02-23/overview) something falsifiable, and it replaced a proxy row seventeen days late on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview).
