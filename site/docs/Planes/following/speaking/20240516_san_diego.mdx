## Where this page fits

Second San Diego State stop of 2024, carried on a podcast-release date rather than a sourced one, which is why it appears in the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) as evidence of where the tour went and not as a testable date against the [Egyptian tails](/Planes/Israel-Planes) logged on the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Campus tabling like this is what [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) was built to do, and the [donor pressure](/Amfest/donor-pressure-bookings) that later reshaped who could stand on a Turning Point stage runs straight through the [political context](/political_context/overview) investigators read as bearing on [motive](/Motive/overview). The clearest illustration of a proxy date going wrong sits on the [overlap-window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition): the compilers counted an aircraft as overlapping within 50 to 100 miles and plus or minus three days, and the [per-aircraft recovery status](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status) shows how little primary data survives to check any of it.
