# Turning Point Action Georgia For Trump rally

**Charlie** at **Gas South Arena, Duluth / metro Atlanta** (Duluth, GA) on **2024-10-23**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **3 foreign-registered**, **8 military**, **3 government-operated** and **23 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Duluth, GA.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: OE-IGG (GLEX) at KFTY, 0.1 mi out. 2 of the foreign-registration flags sit on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**217 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. **What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy.
