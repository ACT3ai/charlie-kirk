### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 149.0 | 24.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 51.8 | 8.2 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A police crowd estimate of roughly 350 is the kind of independent number that makes this row useful: venue, date and size all come from outside the show feed, so the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) can carry it as a fixed point rather than as the [podcast proxy](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) the October 17 episode would have made it for [Turning Point's](/TPUSA/overview) fall tour. Oregon appears nowhere in the [clustering claim](/Planes/following/73_overlaps), and pages like this one keep the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file honest about how much of the tour has no aircraft beside it at all — the same discipline the [narrative section](/Narrative/overview) applies to how September 10 was framed in real time and the [media hub](/Media/overview) applies to who framed it. For the sharpest example of a first-hour framing that later collapsed, read [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart): a televised [witness](/Witnesses/overview) said one shot, right directly to the heart, and that police had arrested the wrong guy.
