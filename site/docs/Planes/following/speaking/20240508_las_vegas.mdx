# Prove Me Wrong tabling — UNLV

**Charlie** at **University of Nevada Las Vegas** (Las Vegas, NV) on **2024-05-08**. Date certainty: the date is a podcast release date — a PROXY. The live event is normally 0–7 days earlier. Never compute a same-day overlap off this date.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What the sources say

Podcast 2024-05-08 is a PROXY. Kirk returned to UNLV on Oct 24, 2024 (his own X recap: 1,500+ students). Do not collapse the two UNLV visits.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

A podcast-release date is not an event date, and that is the whole point of this row in the [speaking-event index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview): the UNLV tabling is carried as a PROXY, so it sits inside the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) record without ever being paired against an [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) movement the way a firm date on the [TPUSA event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) can be. Campus tabling was the retail end of [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) — the same [campus-event model](/campus_university/overview) that put Charlie Kirk under a tent at [UVU](/UVU/overview) on September 10, 2025 with a private detail and [no counter-snipers](/Security_Team/no-counter-snipers). If you want the reason these dates are shaky in the first place, [Apple Podcasts removed 1,793 episodes](/TPUSA/apple-podcasts-removed-episode-list) of the show this row is dated from — the primary index for half the tour is itself gone.
