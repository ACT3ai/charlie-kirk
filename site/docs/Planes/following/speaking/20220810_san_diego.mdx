## What was actually on the ground near this event

**This date was not covered by the geographic sweep.** The sweep streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive and filters it to a 50-mile circle on the event city. It has not been run for 2022-08-10 to 2022-08-12 at San Diego, CA, so this page cannot say what was or was not parked nearby.

That is a **gap in our coverage, not a finding**. It is not evidence that nothing was there, and it must never be quoted as though it were. The sweep reaches 2023 onward and covers none of 2022; see [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) for exactly which days are held.
