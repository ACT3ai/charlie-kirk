## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Orlando, FL**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023-10-09 | Day before | 2248 | 56 | 7 |
| 2023-10-10 | **Same day** | 2145 | 51 | 5 |
| 2023-10-11 | Day after | 2112 | 54 | 8 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2023-10-09 | 2023-10-10 | 2023-10-11 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Unregistered / non-ICAO address | 0 | 1 | 0 | No registration resolved, or a transponder address outside the ICAO block. |
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 6 | 4 | 7 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**17 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

| Date (UTC) | Registration | Type | Operator | Field | Mi from field | Ground window (UTC) | Why flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 2023-10-10 | ***(none)*** | — | — | **FA83** — | 2.9 | 14:07–14:40 | Unregistered / non-ICAO address |

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 53.7 | 6.7 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 30.2 | 3.7 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Three days after October 7, 2023, Kirk was on a Florida campus — a date worth fixing precisely because of what it sits next to, and the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) replaced a proxy row ten days late to get it right for the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) and the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). That week begins the [Israel thread](/Israel/overview) that runs through the rest of this investigation: the public rhetoric, the [donor pressure on the lineup](/Amfest/donor-pressure-bookings), and eventually the [reported break with the pro-Israel cause](/Charlie/Comments/israel-break-public-rhetoric) that commentators put at the centre of [motive](/Motive/overview). The single most-quoted line in that arc has never been produced in any form — see [KM-07, the all-caps September 9 text](/Charlie/Text_Messages/kill-me-07-sept-9-all-caps), where no screenshot, carrier record or filing has ever authenticated it.
