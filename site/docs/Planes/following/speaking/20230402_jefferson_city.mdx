**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. **What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.
