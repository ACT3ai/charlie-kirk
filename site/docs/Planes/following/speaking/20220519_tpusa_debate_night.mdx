## Where this page fits

A streamed debate has no city, and that is exactly why it stays in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview): a row with UNKNOWN in the city column can never be paired with an airport, so it cannot contribute to the [following-planes tally](/Planes/following/overview) no matter how the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) is drawn. The debate format is what Kirk was doing when he was killed — a [Prove Me Wrong tent](/Tent/overview) in the [UVU courtyard](/Locations/Public-Speaking-Event-UVU) — and TPUSA later [erected a replica of that tent at AmericaFest](/Amfest/murder-tent-replica). Read the [21 May 2022 podcast row](/Planes/following/speaking/20220521_tpusa_debate_night) beside this one: it is the same Briahna Joy Gray debate, released two days later, and it is precisely how one event becomes two rows in a spreadsheet that is then counted as a pattern in the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps).
