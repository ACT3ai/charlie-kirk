## Where this page fits

Eleven days separate the real UT San Antonio date from the podcast row it replaced, the largest correction in this stretch of the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and it is a reminder that proxy dates run late and never early — which skews every uncorrected row on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) and the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) in one direction. Texas appears repeatedly on the tour but barely at all in the aircraft record, and that asymmetry is the point: the [clustering claim](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) rests on five states while the [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) campus schedule covered far more ground, which the [locations index](/Locations/overview) keeps straight. For where the jets actually did concentrate, [Wilmington, Delaware](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) is the most-visited airport in the entire Egyptian fleet record and appears on the outbound leg only, every single time, for three years — with [Paris-Le Bourget](/Planes/following/Paris_LFPB_2022-10-05_to_2025-10-05/overview) staging almost every inbound.
