## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **San Antonio, TX**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023-10-17 | Day before | 801 | 50 | 7 |
| 2023-10-19 | Day after | 898 | 46 | 5 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2023-10-17 | 2023-10-19 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Foreign registration | 2 | 1 | **The category this investigation is about.** A non-US civil registration. |
| Military | 2 | 1 | A military serial or a military database flag. Mostly routine — see the note below. |
| Unregistered / non-ICAO address | 1 | 0 | No registration resolved, or a transponder address outside the ICAO block. |
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 2 | 3 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**5 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 48.0 | 6.0 |
| Control circles (12 circle-days) | 32.4 | 5.0 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Eleven days separate the real UT San Antonio date from the podcast row it replaced, the largest correction in this stretch of the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and it is a reminder that proxy dates run late and never early — which skews every uncorrected row on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) and the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) in one direction. Texas appears repeatedly on the tour but barely at all in the aircraft record, and that asymmetry is the point: the [clustering claim](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) rests on five states while the [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) campus schedule covered far more ground, which the [locations index](/Locations/overview) keeps straight. For where the jets actually did concentrate, [Wilmington, Delaware](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) is the most-visited airport in the entire Egyptian fleet record and appears on the outbound leg only, every single time, for three years — with [Paris-Le Bourget](/Planes/following/Paris_LFPB_2022-10-05_to_2025-10-05/overview) staging almost every inbound.
