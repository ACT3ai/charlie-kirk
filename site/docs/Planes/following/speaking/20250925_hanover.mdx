## Where this page fits

A debate that never happened — Kirk against Hasan Piker at Dartmouth, fifteen days after he was killed — and the reason a non-event gets a page at all is that public calendars keep publishing: an unflagged calendar row is the easiest way for a phantom location to enter the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) or the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) from the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), which is why this one sits beside [Colorado State](/Planes/following/speaking/20250918_fort_collins) under the same flag. The debate format is also what made these campus events what they were — the "Prove Me Wrong" table, the open microphone, the crowd pressed close — a set-up that the [UVU tent](/UVU/Tent), the [security decisions](/Security_Team/overview) around it and the [proposed event-security law](/Fix/Event_Security) all turn on, and that [Campus and University](/campus_university/overview) records across the tour. TPUSA reportedly erected a replica of that debate tent at AmericaFest three months later — [the "Prove Me Wrong" tent replica](/Amfest/murder-tent-replica) — while the original courtyard had already been paved over.
