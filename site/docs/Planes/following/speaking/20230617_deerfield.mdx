## Where this page fits

Two sources disagreed on this one — the Illinois Family Institute said June 15-17, Turning Point Education's own page said June 17-19 — and the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) takes the organiser's date, which is the sort of two-day swing that decides whether a row survives the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) and in the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events). Turning Point Academy is the K-12 education arm, a fourth wing beside the campus chapters, [the nonprofits](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/overview) and [the LLCs](/TPUSA/LLCs/overview) of [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview), and the full corporate picture sits in the [companies and organizations index](/Companies_Organizations/overview) rather than anywhere in the flight data. The July 2 podcast release is a proxy, not an event — the same trap that produced most of the bad rows here — and the scale of that catalogue problem is on [every Apple Podcasts episode removed, all 1,793 of them](/TPUSA/apple-podcasts-removed-episode-list).
