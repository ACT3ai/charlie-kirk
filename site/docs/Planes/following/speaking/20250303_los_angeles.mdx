## Where this page fits

USC was not on the original six-stop tour list and not in the source spreadsheet — the spring tour expanded past what anyone published in advance, which is a direct warning against treating a pre-announced itinerary as the [complete event record](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) the [overlap tally](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) needed, and the reason the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file keep gaining rows. California does not appear in the aircraft claim at all, so rows like this one mostly establish where the jets were not — the same negative-space work the [maps section](/maps/overview) does for UVU sightlines and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) does for every address in the case. Where that turns concrete is [Provo Municipal Airport](/Locations/Provo_Airport): Duncan Aviation sources reportedly say four to six men boarded an Egyptian Falcon on Egyptian passports wearing U.S. DoD Liaison badges — a claim about people, not just about [SU-BTT's](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) tail number.
