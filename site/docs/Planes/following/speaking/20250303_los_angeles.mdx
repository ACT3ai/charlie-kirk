# American Comeback Tour — USC

**Charlie** at **University of Southern California** (Los Angeles, CA) on **2025-03-03**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **5 foreign-registered** and **3 military** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Los Angeles, CA.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: 9H-VJY (GLEX) at KLAX; G-RNDX (E550) at KLAX; HB-JLF (GL7T) at KLAX; B-2099 (B773) at KLAX, and 1 more below. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**246 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**1 recorded ground contact by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N872RA](/Planes/N872RA/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

USC was not on the original six-stop tour list and not in the source spreadsheet — the spring tour expanded past what anyone published in advance, which is a direct warning against treating a pre-announced itinerary as the [complete event record](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) the [overlap tally](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) needed, and the reason the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file keep gaining rows. California does not appear in the aircraft claim at all, so rows like this one mostly establish where the jets were not — the same negative-space work the [maps section](/maps/overview) does for UVU sightlines and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) does for every address in the case. Where that turns concrete is [Provo Municipal Airport](/Locations/Provo_Airport): Duncan Aviation sources reportedly say four to six men boarded an Egyptian Falcon on Egyptian passports wearing U.S. DoD Liaison badges — a claim about people, not just about [SU-BTT's](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) tail number.
