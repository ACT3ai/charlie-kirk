## Where this page fits

A February announcement in the campus paper fixed this date at 22 April, replacing an 11 May row that was only a podcast-release proxy — the kind of correction the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) has to make repeatedly before a date is allowed anywhere near the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) or the [overlap rule](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition). Proxy dates are not a housekeeping detail: the tallies of 68, 72 and 73 overlaps that reached [press coverage](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) and the [Candace Owens broadcasts](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) were built on location claims of exactly this quality, and they now sit beside the [withheld foreign-lead records](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records), the [intelligence threads](/intelligence/overview) and the [media coverage](/Media/overview) as things a reader cannot independently check.
