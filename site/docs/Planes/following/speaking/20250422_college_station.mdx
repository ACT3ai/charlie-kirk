# American Comeback Tour — Texas A&M

**Charlie** at **Texas A&M University** (College Station, TX) on **2025-04-22**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day before or the day after: **2 foreign-registered** and **2 military** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on College Station, TX. Nothing in these four categories was on the ground inside the circle on the day of the event itself.

2 of the foreign-registration flags sit on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**1 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **College Station, TX**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 2.3 | 1.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 54.8 | 8.4 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

A February announcement in the campus paper fixed this date at 22 April, replacing an 11 May row that was only a podcast-release proxy — the kind of correction the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) has to make repeatedly before a date is allowed anywhere near the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) or the [overlap rule](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition). Proxy dates are not a housekeeping detail: the tallies of 68, 72 and 73 overlaps that reached [press coverage](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) and the [Candace Owens broadcasts](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) were built on location claims of exactly this quality, and they now sit beside the [withheld foreign-lead records](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records), the [intelligence threads](/intelligence/overview) and the [media coverage](/Media/overview) as things a reader cannot independently check. Read [the AI assistants' answers](/Planes/following/AI_Assistant_Answers) next — asked the same question, Gemini and Grok return different overlap counts, which is a fair measure of how unstable the underlying claim still is.
