# You're Being Brainwashed Tour — UW-Madison

**Charlie** at **University of Wisconsin-Madison** (Madison, WI) on **2024-09-05**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **1 military** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Madison, WI.

None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**14 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**1 recorded ground contact by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

This row is the fall 2024 tour's opening night and it does a second job: it demotes the December UW-Madison episodes to delayed releases, which removes a phantom December date from the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), from the [73-overlap tally](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) built on top of it, and from anything measured against the [plus-or-minus-three-day window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) the compilers used for [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) dates. The Brainwashed format is the one that ended at [UVU](/UVU/overview) — an outdoor quad, [a tent](/UVU/Tent), a queue of students at a microphone, and on September 10 a [perimeter that left nearby rooftops uninspected](/Security_Team/perimeter-rooftop-failures), which is the through-line of the [campus and university](/campus_university/overview) material. The single most concrete thing on the other side of that link is on the [pre-shooting warnings page](/UVU/pre-shooting-warnings): two men reportedly walked into a UVU office at 10:35 PM the night before asking to tell police about a plan, and the [GRAMA release](/CoverUp/UVU_GRAMA_Records) shows the chain dismissing them as GOP reps forty-four minutes later.
