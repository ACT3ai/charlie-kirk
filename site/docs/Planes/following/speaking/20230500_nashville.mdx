## Where this page fits

A month with no day in it is its own kind of hole: this row places Charlie Kirk in Nashville in May 2023 but cannot place him on a date, so it can never pair with a jet movement on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) no matter how the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) is drawn, and both the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) carry it as a location fact only.
