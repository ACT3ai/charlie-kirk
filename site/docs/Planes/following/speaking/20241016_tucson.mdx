### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 68.7 | 12.7 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 45.1 | 7.9 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

The Tulsi Gabbard episode released on November 26 is very likely this October 16 stop arriving late, which is why the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) carries the campus date and flags the episode as a probable delayed release rather than letting one appearance become two rows in the [TPUSA event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events), the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file or the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Sharing a stage with a future Director of National Intelligence is the sort of detail that connects the campus tour to the [intelligence threads](/US_Intelligence/overview) this investigation runs, including the reported cabinet-level moment when [officials are said to have closed off foreign-involvement questions](/Israel_Main_Suspect/coverup-foreign-involvement) six days after Kirk was killed. Russell Brand attributes that account to two cabinet sources; the [Israel-as-main-suspect hub](/Israel_Main_Suspect/overview) sets it beside [Joe Kent's on-camera account](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) of being told to stop, which is by far the better documented of the two.
