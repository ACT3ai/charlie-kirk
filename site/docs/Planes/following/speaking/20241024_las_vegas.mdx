# Prove Me Wrong — UNLV (plus TP Action Trump rally)

**Charlie** at **University of Nevada Las Vegas; TP Action 'United for Change' rally Las Vegas** (Las Vegas, NV) on **2024-10-24**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **3 foreign-registered**, **3 military**, **1 government-operated** and **4 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Las Vegas, NV.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: C-GSEN (C172) at KLAS; C-GGAQ (CL35) at KLAS; XA-AFA (BE40) at KLAS. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**33 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**1 recorded ground contact by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 83.3 | 23.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 46.9 | 7.7 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

A campus morning with a crowd figure Kirk published himself, then a [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) rally the same day in the same city — and resolving it corrected the source spreadsheet, which had filed this date as a Georgia rally, meaning one row feeding the [overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) pointed at the wrong state before the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file reworked it. Wrong-state rows are why the [audit verdicts](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) matter more than the tally, and why the [press-coverage page](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) tracks the number drifting from 68 to 73 as the claim travelled — the same drift the [media section](/Media/overview) documents in its [mainstream-versus-alternative](/Media/Mainstream-vs-Alternative-Media) framing of everything else here. The broadcast record behind that number sits on the [Candace Owens page](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts), including the on-air claim that the odds of the overlaps being coincidence were 0.000000001 percent.
