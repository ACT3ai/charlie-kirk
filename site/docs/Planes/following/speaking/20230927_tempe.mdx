### The same days, at cities with no event

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Arizona State appears twice in one year and only the September stop has a real date — the February appearance is a podcast proxy — which is exactly the split the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) was built to make visible before anything reaches the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) or the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Tempe is home turf: [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) runs out of Phoenix, which is where [AmericaFest](/Amfest/what-is-americafest) fills the convention centre every December and where [the people around the organisation](/TPUSA/people-related-to-tpusa) mostly lived and worked. Phoenix is also where the December 2025 AmFest went ahead without him — [the first AmericaFest without Charlie Kirk](/Amfest/amfest-2025-december), complete with a [replica of the Prove Me Wrong tent](/Amfest/murder-tent-replica) reportedly erected on the floor.
