**Charlie** at **Krach Lawn, Purdue University** (West Lafayette, IN) on **2025-04-10**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). ## Where this page fits

Purdue was an add-on rather than an original six-stop tour date, and the page exists so the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) carries the whole itinerary — including the fact that Kirk's next Indiana date, [Bloomington on 21 October](/Planes/following/speaking/20251021_bloomington), was kept by [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) after he was dead. A calendar that outlived the man is the seam between the [before](/Before/overview) material and the [after](/After/overview) material, and it runs through the [memorial at State Farm Stadium](/Planes/following/speaking/20250921_glendale), the [institutional resignations](/After/Institutional_Resignations) and the [organizations mapped around him](/organizations_groups/overview). The most concrete item on the other side of these links is a memo dated 2 September 2025: Kirk reportedly [replaced his COO and ordered an internal audit](/TPUSA/charlie-replace-coo) eight days before he was killed.
