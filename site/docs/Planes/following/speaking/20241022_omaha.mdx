### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Omaha is a clustering city in the aircraft claim, and this row is where the arithmetic gets stated plainly: [SU-BTT's Omaha landings](/Planes/following/Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview) are roughly nine months away from this October 2024 evening, so it is not a same-window overlap under [any definition the compilers used](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition), and the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) says so on the page rather than in a footnote about [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). Six Egyptian arrivals at Omaha were each followed within twenty minutes by a hop to Lincoln, which reads far more like a customs-and-service routine than a shadow, and separating that from the [Provo cluster](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) is what the [cover-up file](/CoverUp/overview) needs if its [foreign-flight-records claim](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records) is going to hold. Lincoln holds the closest thing to a documented pairing anywhere in this material: TPUSA opened a Lincoln chapter on [11 February 2024 while SU-BTT sat on the Lincoln ramp](/Planes/following/overlap/20240211_NE_lincoln/overview), day three of a five-day stay.
