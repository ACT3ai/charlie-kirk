## Where this page fits

Omaha is a clustering city in the aircraft claim, and this row is where the arithmetic gets stated plainly: [SU-BTT's Omaha landings](/Planes/following/Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview) are roughly nine months away from this October 2024 evening, so it is not a same-window overlap under [any definition the compilers used](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition), and the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) says so on the page rather than in a footnote about [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). Six Egyptian arrivals at Omaha were each followed within twenty minutes by a hop to Lincoln, which reads far more like a customs-and-service routine than a shadow, and separating that from the [Provo cluster](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) is what the [cover-up file](/CoverUp/overview) needs if its [foreign-flight-records claim](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records) is going to hold.
