**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A single theatre booking in South Carolina, twenty days before UVU, and the reason a one-night speaker series gets a page is the flat contradiction with [Win the Storm in Dallas](/Planes/following/speaking/20250820_dallas) running 20-22 August on the same events page — the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) marks the collision so nobody later builds a [following claim](/Planes/following/overview) on whichever row turns out to be wrong. The last three weeks of Kirk's life are the densest part of the record: the [donor group chat of 8 September](/Messages/sept-8-donor-group-chat), the [reported death warnings](/Charlie/Fear_Kill_Warnings/overview), the [Asia trip](/Planes/following/speaking/20250905_seoul), and the [political pressure meetings](/Before/Political_Pressure_And_Meetings_2025) that [Before](/Before/overview) tracks day by day. The most quotable of those is [the message to Dan Flood](/Messages/message-to-dan-flood) — Kirk reportedly told his own head of security he thought he would be "executed tomorrow," while UVU fielded six officers for a crowd of about three thousand.
