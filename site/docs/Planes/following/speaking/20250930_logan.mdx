# Turning Point Tour — Utah State University

**TPUSA** at **Utah State University, Logan** (Logan, UT) on **2025-09-30**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

Originally on Kirk's fall list with UVU. After his death: Alex Clark, Sen. Mike Lee, Gov. Spencer Cox, Rep. Andy Biggs, former Rep. Jason Chaffetz. Charlie did not appear.

Do not treat this row as a Charlie Kirk location for the following-pattern. The Egyptian-jet overlap claim is about the years *before* September 10.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). ## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Logan, UT**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025-09-29 | Day before | 629 | 120 | 19 |
| 2025-09-30 | **Same day** | 594 | 94 | 19 |
| 2025-10-01 | Day after | 733 | 144 | 30 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2025-09-29 | 2025-09-30 | 2025-10-01 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Foreign registration | 0 | 1 | 1 | **The category this investigation is about.** A non-US civil registration. |
| Military | 0 | 1 | 1 | A military serial or a military database flag. Mostly routine — see the note below. |
| Government operator | 0 | 0 | 1 | The registered operator string names a government body. |
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 19 | 17 | 28 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**64 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 119.3 | 22.7 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 62.9 | 8.9 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Logan was on the same fall list as UVU — the two Utah dates were announced together in one local report — and this one went ahead without him three weeks later, which is why the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) marks it AFTER_DEATH and keeps it out of any [Charlie pairing](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview) in the [following record](/Planes/following/overview). The guest list is the interesting part: Governor Spencer Cox spoke here nine days after the [closed-door Gold Room meetings](/Planes/Cox-Foreign-Meetings) reported with Egyptian and French diplomatic figures, and a contractor separately says on camera he was told ["the governor and the FBI said they want this done by Monday"](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/courtyard-paved-over) — an account [UVU's Pavers page](/UVU/Pavers), the [FBI section](/FBI/Crime_Scene_Paving) and the [crime-scene handling](/Law_Enforcement/crime-scene-handling) page all carry with attribution. Follow [10 Inches of Dirt Removed Before the Pavers](/Tent/Dirt_Removed_Before_Pavers): roughly ten inches of soil were reportedly excavated and hauled off the kill site by a crew still described only as men working for the state.
