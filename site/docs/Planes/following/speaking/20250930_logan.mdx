## Where this page fits

Logan was on the same fall list as UVU — the two Utah dates were announced together in one local report — and this one went ahead without him three weeks later, which is why the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) marks it AFTER_DEATH and keeps it out of any [Charlie pairing](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview) in the [following record](/Planes/following/overview). The guest list is the interesting part: Governor Spencer Cox spoke here nine days after the [closed-door Gold Room meetings](/Planes/Cox-Foreign-Meetings) reported with Egyptian and French diplomatic figures, and a contractor separately says on camera he was told ["the governor and the FBI said they want this done by Monday"](/Suspicious/Law_Enforcement/courtyard-paved-over) — an account [UVU's Pavers page](/UVU/Pavers), the [FBI section](/FBI/Crime_Scene_Paving) and the [crime-scene handling](/Law_Enforcement/crime-scene-handling) page all carry with attribution.
