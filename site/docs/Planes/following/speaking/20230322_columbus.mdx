# Live Free Tour — Ohio State University with Candace Owens

**Charlie** at **Ohio State University** (Columbus, OH) on **2023-03-22**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day before or the day after: **1 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Columbus, OH. Nothing in these four categories was on the ground inside the circle on the day of the event itself.

None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**1 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Columbus, OH**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|

| 2023-03-22 | **Same day** | 1081 | 5 | 0 |
| 2023-03-23 | Day after | 1101 | 1 | 1 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2023-03-21 | 2023-03-22 | 2023-03-23 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Unregistered / non-ICAO address | 1 | 0 | 0 | No registration resolved, or a transponder address outside the ICAO block. |
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 0 | 0 | 1 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**1 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 6.7 | 0.7 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 18.6 | 3.3 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). ## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview).
