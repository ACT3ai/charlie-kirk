## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Athens, GA**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024-10-21 | Day before | 1604 | 85 | 18 |
| 2024-10-22 | **Same day** | 1550 | 83 | 18 |
| 2024-10-23 | Day after | 1521 | 76 | 24 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2024-10-21 | 2024-10-22 | 2024-10-23 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 18 | 18 | 23 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |
| Already tracked by this investigation | 0 | 0 | 1 | An aircraft already on this site's list, found here by geography rather than by name. |

**59 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 81.3 | 20.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 43.1 | 6.8 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A Georgia morning and [an Omaha evening on the same date](/Planes/following/speaking/20241022_omaha), 1,100 miles apart, is the fact that should discipline every reading of the [overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) and of the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview): a man who crosses the country twice a week will share a state with almost any frequent flyer, which is what the [window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) actually encodes. That base-rate problem is the honest counterweight to the whole [following claim](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika), and the pages that survive it are the ones with a document behind them — the [UVU records showing a warning the night before](/UVU/pre-shooting-warnings), or the [ATF fragment that could not be matched to the rifle](/Gun_Bullet/ATF_Fragment_Inconclusive). That ATF page carries the most load-bearing document on this site: a September 17, 2025 report reportedly stating the fragment could not be identified or excluded as fired from the seized Mauser, which is where both [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) and the [.30-06 energy mismatch](/Gun_Bullet/Thirty_06_Energy_Mismatch) begin.
