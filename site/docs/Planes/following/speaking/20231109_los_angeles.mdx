### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 851.0 | 179.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 20.9 | 3.4 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

UCLA closes the Fall 2023 Live Free run with a three-day correction — the podcast row said November 12 — and small fixes like this are what make the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) usable as the Kirk side of any pairing on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) or the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Three days is inside the [plus-or-minus-three-day window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) the compilers used, which is the uncomfortable part: a wrong date can still register as an overlap, and that is why the [campus record](/campus_university/overview), the [TPUSA organisation pages](/TPUSA/overview) and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) are checked independently of the spreadsheet. The clearest statement of what was actually counted — a jet within 50 to 100 miles and plus or minus three days of a claimed location — comes from [Liz Wheeler's](/People/liz-wheeler) December 2025 posts, quoted in full on that window page.
