## Where this page fits

Auburn is the second half of a two-night Southern swing with [Fayetteville](/Planes/following/speaking/20220330_fayetteville), and like that page it exists to replace a bad row — the source sheet's 22 April 2022 Auburn entry was a podcast release three weeks adrift, which is fatal to any [same-day pairing](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) in the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) or the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps).
