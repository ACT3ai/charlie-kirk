## Where this page fits

Omaha is where the arithmetic gets stated out loud: SU-BTT flew into this city on 20 July and 17 August 2025, fifteen to sixteen months after this rally, and the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) logs that as not an overlap — the [Omaha airport page](/Planes/following/Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview), the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [overlap window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) all make the gap explicit rather than letting a shared city stand in for a shared date.
