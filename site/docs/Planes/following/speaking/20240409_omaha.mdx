# Win Every Vote / Winner-Take-All rally — Omaha

**Charlie** at **Lord of Hosts Church, 5351 S. 139th Plaza, Omaha** (Omaha, NE) on **2024-04-09**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **8 military** and **2 government-operated** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Omaha, NE.

None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**1 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Omaha is where the arithmetic gets stated out loud: SU-BTT flew into this city on 20 July and 17 August 2025, fifteen to sixteen months after this rally, and the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) logs that as not an overlap — the [Omaha airport page](/Planes/following/Omaha_KOMA_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-05/overview), the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [overlap window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) all make the gap explicit rather than letting a shared city stand in for a shared date. The event itself was [Turning Point Action](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/turning-point-action) with the Nebraska GOP over the winner-take-all electoral-vote fight, AP counted about 500 inside and 400 in overflow, and that political-operation side of the organisation sits in [political context](/political_context/overview) and [organizations and groups](/organizations_groups/overview) rather than in the campus material. Every Omaha arrival in the Egyptian record was followed within twenty minutes by a hop to [Lincoln](/Planes/following/Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview), where a 1999 Duncan Aviation maintenance contract is the innocent explanation that has to be answered first.
