## Where this page fits

Three-day summits give the [following-pattern test](/Planes/following/overview) a wide target rather than a single point — a jet has seventy-two hours to be counted, not one afternoon — which is exactly the looseness the [overlap window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) then adds three more days to on either side, so the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) flags multi-day rows for that reason. The Gaylord Texan lineup of [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk), [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) and [Marjorie Taylor Greene](/People/marjorie-taylor-greene) is the [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) speaker economy the investigation later treats as policy, and [who gets booked and who gets blocked](/Amfest/booking-as-policy) becomes one of the motive threads. The sharpest version of it is the [fight over Tucker Carlson's AmFest invitation](/Amfest/tucker-invitation-fight), where Grayzone, citing five sources, reports a Bridgehampton donor weekend that went off the rails.
