## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

This row carries an unresolved judgment on its face: a 15 April 2023 podcast titled as a live UIC speech is treated here as a delayed release of the 9 March stop rather than a second visit — and if that call is wrong, an extra Chicago date belongs in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), in the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) and possibly in the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps).
