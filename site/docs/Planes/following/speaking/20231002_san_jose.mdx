## Where this page fits

Six days separate the real San Jose State date from the podcast-release date it replaced, and six days is more than the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) allows on either side, so this correction alone can flip a row on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) from hit to miss on the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps), which is why the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) carries the press-release date instead. TPUSA's own events account claimed 750-plus at San Jose State, Albany, Central Florida and Georgia Tech, which is the crowd scale that made [campus events](/campus_university/overview) worth protecting and makes [the security questions](/Security_Team/overview) at [UVU](/UVU/overview) two years later so pointed. For how numbers behave once a claim leaves its source, read [how the press covered the Egyptian-plane claim](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) — the overlap tally drifts between 68, 70, 72 and 73 depending on who is repeating it.
