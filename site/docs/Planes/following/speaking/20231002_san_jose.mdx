# Live Free Tour — San Jose State University

**Charlie** at **San Jose State University** (San Jose, CA) on **2023-10-02**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **8 foreign-registered**, **3 military** and **3 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on San Jose, CA.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: 9H-VIM (GL7T) at KSFO, 0.2 mi out; VP-CVI (GLF6) at KSJC, 0.2 mi out; M-NBLA (EC35) at KSJC, 0.3 mi out; 9H-AMZ (GLEX) at KOAK, 0.7 mi out, and 1 more below. 3 of the foreign-registration flags sit on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**205 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

Six days separate the real San Jose State date from the podcast-release date it replaced, and six days is more than the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) allows on either side, so this correction alone can flip a row on the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) from hit to miss on the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps), which is why the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) carries the press-release date instead. TPUSA's own events account claimed 750-plus at San Jose State, Albany, Central Florida and Georgia Tech, which is the crowd scale that made [campus events](/campus_university/overview) worth protecting and makes [the security questions](/Security_Team/overview) at [UVU](/UVU/overview) two years later so pointed. For how numbers behave once a claim leaves its source, read [how the press covered the Egyptian-plane claim](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) — the overlap tally drifts between 68, 70, 72 and 73 depending on who is repeating it.
