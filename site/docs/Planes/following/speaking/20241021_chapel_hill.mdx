## Where this page fits

The afternoon half of a two-campus day and the second Chapel Hill stop of 2024 — repeat visits to one university are why the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [TPUSA event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) are keyed on date rather than venue, and why the [Atlanta morning](/Planes/following/speaking/20241021_atlanta) is a separate page instead of a note on this one. North Carolina appears three times in this month of the tour and none of those dates has an [Egyptian tail](/Planes/Israel-Planes) beside it — a negative result, and negative results are what let the [Israel section](/Israel/overview) and the [Israel-as-main-suspect hub](/Israel_Main_Suspect/overview) be argued from evidence rather than from a tally.
