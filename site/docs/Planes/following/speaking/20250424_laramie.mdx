A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Laramie, WY**. ### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 0.7 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 56.8 | 10.1 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Laramie belongs to the same American Comeback Tour whose fall leg opened at [UVU on 10 September](/Planes/following/speaking/20250910_orem), which is why a corrected date here matters more than a small-campus stop normally would — the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) had carried it as an 8 June podcast proxy until a February tour list fixed it, and only fixed dates go near the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview). Wyoming has no aircraft claim attached to it at all, and saying so plainly is the point: absence is the ordinary result across most of this catalog, and it is the baseline against which the loud rows — [Provo Municipal Airport](/Locations/Provo_Airport), the [Egyptian jets parked there](/Proof_Intel_Services/Egyptian_Jets_Provo), the [HADES pass over UVU](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU) — have to be judged by anyone reading the [intelligence-services case](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview).
