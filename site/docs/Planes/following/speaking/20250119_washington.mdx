## Where this page fits

An inaugural-eve ball is a political event rather than a tour stop, and the city here is inferred from the inauguration rather than sourced — a soft field the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) marks rather than hides, because inferred cities are how a [50-to-100-mile overlap radius](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) quietly stops meaning anything in the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) or the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file. Washington is also where the [July 31, 2025 White House strategy session](/TPUSA/July_31_Meeting_Without_TPUSA) on Israel and MAGA antisemitism took place — the meeting [Turning Point was reportedly absent from](/TPUSA/TPUSA-Absence-July-31) six weeks before Kirk was killed, and a fixture of the [political context](/political_context/overview) file. The related search claim is stranger than the meeting: researchers report [Google Trends interest in defense attorney Kathryn Nester spiking from Israeli geography in December 2024](/GoogleSearches/kathryn-nester-searches), nine months before the shooting she would go on to work, and the [Google Searches hub](/GoogleSearches/overview) carries the rest.
