## Where this page fits

Grand Canyon University was an added stop, absent from the original eight-campus press list, and added stops are where a [tour catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) assembled only from press releases goes wrong — every gap of that kind is a hole in the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) and in the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) built on top of it. Phoenix is Turning Point's home city: the [AmericaFest](/Amfest/what-is-americafest) venue, the [organization's](/TPUSA/overview) base, and the destination of [the flight that carried Kirk's casket](/Planes/Casket-Flight) in September 2025. Read [AmericaFest 2022 in December of this same year](/Planes/following/speaking/20221217_phoenix) — four days, the Phoenix Convention Center, a confirmed city — which makes it one of the few 2022 rows a [flight archive](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status) could in principle be checked against, though 2022 is the year the free archives reach least well.
