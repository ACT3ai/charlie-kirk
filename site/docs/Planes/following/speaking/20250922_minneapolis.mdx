## Where this page fits

Guest-speaker dates like this one are still evidence of something else — how quickly [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) recovered its operations while the [legal investigation](/legal_investigation/overview) narrowed — which is the same interval covered by the [after](/After/overview) section, the [media response](/Media/overview) and the [censorship](/Censorship/overview) fights over what could be said about the case.
