# Turning Point Tour (resumed) — University of Minnesota Twin Cities

**TPUSA** at **University of Minnesota Twin Cities** (Minneapolis, MN) on **2025-09-22**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

**123 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 554.7 | 66.0 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

The tour restarted twelve days after the killing under a new name — American Comeback became the Turning Point Tour — and the rebrand is why this row is flagged AFTER_DEATH in the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview): Charlie was not there, so it cannot serve as a [Charlie location](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview) in the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) whatever the tour is called. Guest-speaker dates like this one are still evidence of something else — how quickly [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) recovered its operations while the [legal investigation](/legal_investigation/overview) narrowed — which is the same interval covered by the [after](/After/overview) section, the [media response](/Media/overview) and the [censorship](/Censorship/overview) fights over what could be said about the case. The most concrete number waiting there is Reuters' November 2025 count of roughly [600 Americans punished for Kirk comments](/CoverUp/Purge_600_Fired) — teachers, nurses, flight attendants — fired or disciplined over what they posted.
