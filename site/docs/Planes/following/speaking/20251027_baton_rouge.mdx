# Turning Point Tour — Louisiana State University

**TPUSA** at **Louisiana State University** (Baton Rouge, LA) on **2025-10-27**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Baton Rouge, LA**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 11.0 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (12 circle-days) | 57.2 | 5.4 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

LSU appears twice in this catalog — [March 2023](/Planes/following/speaking/20230321_baton_rouge), where the listing itself is marked "announced; Kirk unconfirmed," and this October 2025 return he did not live to make — and two soft rows on one campus is a fair illustration of why the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) publishes certainty flags rather than handing a clean list to the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) or the [overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Weak rows are not a defect to be tidied away; they are the honest half of a claim that reached [press coverage](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) as a hard number, and the same discipline runs through the [defamation section](/Defamation/overview), the [influencer sourcing pages](/Influencers/overview) and the [witness-reliability](/Other/Witness-Reliability) material elsewhere on this site. For a worked example of a claim tested rather than repeated, read [Wrong Witnesses — Why the Same Wrong Detail?](/Witnesses/wrong_witnesses): multiple unconnected witnesses used near-identical heart-wound wording, and the page asks what would produce that.
