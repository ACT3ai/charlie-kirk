## Where this page fits

The venue name carries a thread of its own: ASU's T.W. Lewis Center is named for the donor foundation whose aviation LLC owns a Cessna Citation XLS that turns up at Provo on 10 September 2025 — a coincidence of names, recorded here because the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) and the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) are meant to be checkable against each other rather than merged. The foundation side is documented under [the T.W. Lewis Foundation](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/tw-lewis-foundation) and [T.W. Lewis Aviation LLC](/TPUSA/LLCs/tw-lewis-aviation-llc), which is where donor money, a named campus centre and a private jet meet, and where [the wider LLC map](/TPUSA/LLCs/overview) belongs. Read [N560TW](/Planes/N560TW/overview) itself — Candace Owens flagged it publicly for a Scottsdale-Provo-Santa Barbara out-and-back that day — and note what the page says plainly about limits: a trace establishes an airframe's presence, never occupancy and never purpose, which is also the standing caveat on the [per-aircraft recovery status](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status).
