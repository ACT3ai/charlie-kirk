## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Missoula, MT**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 25.4 | 3.1 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Two events on one campus on one day, one of them a [Turning Point Action](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/turning-point-action) rally rather than a campus talk — a distinction the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) keep because the [overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) did not, and a doubled city can read as two independent hits. The 501(c)(3) and the 501(c)(4) doing different work on the same afternoon is the everyday version of the [entity structure](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/overview) citizen investigators later mapped into [thirteen LLCs](/TPUSA/LLCs/overview) and a set of [for-profit companies](/TPUSA/Companies/overview) on the [organizations map](/organizations_groups/overview). One of those entities is [GGLF 2023 LLC](/TPUSA/LLCs/gglf-2023-llc), the Wyoming company reported to have held a life-insurance policy on Charlie Kirk — the single most-cited financial item in the whole organizational file.
