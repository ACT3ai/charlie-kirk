## What was actually on the ground near this event

**This date was not covered by the geographic sweep.** The sweep streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive and filters it to a 50-mile circle on the event city. It has not been run for 2022-06-02 to 2022-06-04 at Grapevine, TX, so this page cannot say what was or was not parked nearby.

That is a **gap in our coverage, not a finding**. It is not evidence that nothing was there, and it must never be quoted as though it were. ## Where this page fits

Kirk called it Dallas; the Gaylord Texan is in Grapevine, twenty miles away — a small discrepancy in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) that decides which airport a summit gets matched to, and therefore whether a row survives in the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) or in the [event-by-event tally](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events). Multi-day summits like the Young Women's Leadership Summit are the [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) events with the biggest speaker lists, tracked year by year in the [AmFest and summit timeline](/Amfest/amfest-year-timeline) and name by name in the [speaker index](/Amfest/speakers-index).
