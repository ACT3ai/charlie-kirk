## Where this page fits

Another four-day correction — the source sheet's 5 March 2023 UCSB row was a podcast release, the event was 1 March — and by 2023 the Live Free Tour had three rotating headliners plus an opener, so neither the date nor the speaker can be assumed from a tour name in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), in the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) or in the [73-overlap list](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Santa Barbara turns up again on the afternoon of 10 September 2025 in the Provo arrivals, a Utah story with no connection to a 2023 campus booking — the sort of coincidence the [theory map](/Theories/overview), the [suspicious-activity hub](/Suspicious/overview) and the [people index](/People/overview) exist to keep separate from evidence. Read [N560TW](/Planes/N560TW/overview) and [N872RA](/Planes/N872RA/overview) for that afternoon: both are reported into Provo from California, and both pages state what a trace establishes — an airframe's presence, and nothing whatsoever about who was aboard.
