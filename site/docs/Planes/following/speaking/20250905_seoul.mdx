## Where this page fits

Five days before he was killed Kirk was on another continent, and that does real work here: an Asia trip removes him from United States airspace entirely for the window immediately preceding UVU, which is a hard constraint on any [following claim](/Planes/following/overview) and on the [Egyptian fleet thread](/Planes/Israel-Planes) the [overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) draws from. The Seoul and [Tokyo](/Planes/following/speaking/20250907_tokyo) dates also frame the last week politically — the [Iran-war fight](/Iran/overview), his [Epstein-files pressure](/Charlie/Epstein_Files_Push) and the [donor break](/Messages/sept-8-donor-group-chat) reported within days of his return all sit in the [motive](/Motive/overview) material. The hardest single item in that week is [Charlie's stand against the Iran war](/Iran/charlie-anti-iran-war): the timeline states he went to the White House on 18 June 2025 specifically to argue against regime change, and that Trump reportedly "barked" back.
