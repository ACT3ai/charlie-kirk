# You're Being Brainwashed Tour — UC Boulder

**Charlie** at **University of Colorado Boulder** (Boulder, CO) on **2024-09-10**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **3 foreign-registered**, **5 military**, **1 government-operated** and **1 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Boulder, CO.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: HB-VSZ (PC24) at KBJC; M-SFPL (GLEX) at KAPA; C-FIOV (CL30) at KDEN. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**301 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**2 recorded ground contacts by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview), [N872RA](/Planes/N872RA/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

Boulder is the second stop of the fall 2024 run and it carries an accident of the calendar worth naming plainly — September 10, one year to the day before [UVU](/UVU/overview) — with no aircraft claim attached to it anywhere in the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) or the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview). A coincidence of date is not evidence, and this site's own [September 10 flight timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline) is about 2025 — the [HADES ISR jet](/Planes/N1098L/overview), the [Egyptian transponder window](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), and the [day-of chronology](/Topic-Analyses/September_10_Event_Timeline) that belong to a different year and a different claim. The numbers on that day-of page are the reason to click: an Army surveillance jet reportedly at 203 feet over the campus at 9:16 a.m. and [back at 335 feet at 12:48 p.m.](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU), twenty-five minutes after Kirk was pronounced dead.
