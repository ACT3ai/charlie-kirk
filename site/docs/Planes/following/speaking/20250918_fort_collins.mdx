# American Comeback Tour — Colorado State University (scheduled, not held by Charlie)

**Charlie** at **Colorado State University** (Fort Collins, CO) on **2025-09-18**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk did not appear. He was killed on September 10, 2025. The date stayed on a public calendar.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2025-09-17 | What it means |

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Colorado State was the next stop after UVU — eight days later, never held — and keeping the date visible is the point: a scheduled row is not a location, and the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) or the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) would double-count it as one if the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) quietly dropped the certainty flag. What happened instead of a speech at Fort Collins was the [memorial at State Farm Stadium](/Planes/following/speaking/20250921_glendale) three days later and a tour resumed under a new name, while the investigation itself moved into [censorship and gag-order fights](/Censorship/overview), [sealed records](/Legal/Evidence-Sealing-2026) and the [after](/After/overview) material — including the [resignations](/After/Institutional_Resignations) that followed at UVU and the FBI. The item worth opening is [Court Gag Orders](/Censorship/Court_Gag_Orders): Judge Tony Graf reportedly issued a gag order on his own motion on 16 December 2025 — neither side asked for it — weeks after the prior judge had retired.
