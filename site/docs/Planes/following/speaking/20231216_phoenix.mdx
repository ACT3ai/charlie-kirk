## Where this page fits

AmericaFest 2023 is the weakest-sourced of the three AmFest rows on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview): the exact start and end dates are softer than 2022 or 2024, with an Arizona Republic gallery dated December 18 doing most of the work, and a four-day block already gives the [following test](/Planes/following/overview) a wide target before any date slack is added. Roughly thirteen thousand people in the Phoenix Convention Center is [AmericaFest](/Amfest/what-is-americafest) at full size, run by [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) through the operation described on [who actually runs AmFest](/Amfest/who-runs-amfest), with the year-by-year lineup tracked on the [speaker index](/Amfest/speakers-index).
