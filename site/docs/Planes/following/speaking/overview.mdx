This index exists so a location page under [Following](/Planes/following/overview) can be checked against a sourced speaking date instead of a guessed one. 139 appearances are listed. 118 carry a sourced **event date**. 8 are still **podcast-release proxies** — the live stop is normally 0–7 days earlier, and a "same day" overlap must not be published off those rows. 2 rows name Erika Kirk (alone or with Charlie). Her [flight logs are reported as erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), so the 73-overlap tally is measured against a set of Erika locations this repo still cannot list as a dated public record — see **[Erika Kirk's flights](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights)**; the June 2025 Young Women's Leadership Summit at the Gaylord Texan is the well-dated joint appearance before September 10.

Campus tours in this window had names: **Live Free** (2022–2024), **You're Being Brainwashed** (fall 2024), **American Comeback** (2025, then rebranded to **Turning Point Tour** after September 10). Flagship summits — [AmericaFest](/Amfest/overview) each December in Phoenix, Student Action Summit in Tampa, Young Women's Leadership Summit in Texas, TPUSA Faith pastors' summits in San Diego / Nashville / West Palm Beach / Rancho Palos Verdes — are the other axis. After September 10 the fall campus dates continued with guest speakers; those rows are marked `AFTER_DEATH` and are not Charlie locations.

What to do with this: start at the **June 13–15, 2025 YWLS** page if you need the one solid pre-assassination Erika date; the **February–May 2025 American Comeback** pages if you need the spring campus spine; **September 10 UVU** if you need the last Charlie stop; and any `PODCAST_PROXY` row only as a lead, not as an overlap key. Then go back to the [following overview](/Planes/following/overview) and the tail-number pages — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview) — and test pairings against `flights.csv`, stating the gap (same day, day before, day after) with both timestamps.

## Counterargument

A speaking calendar this dense makes coincidences easier, not harder: Kirk was on the road constantly, TPUSA ran multiple branded tours a year, and several "clustering" states (Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska) are also ordinary aviation-maintenance and campaign geography. A 15-month gap between an Omaha speech (April 2024) and SU-BTT's reported Omaha landing (July 2025) is not a shadowing event. Podcast-proxy dates will manufacture false same-day hits if they are treated as live dates. Erika's missing logs mean most claimed Erika overlaps cannot be checked from this catalog at all.

## Where this page fits

This catalog is one half of a two-sided claim and never both halves at once: it establishes where Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk or a Turning Point event actually was, so the [aircraft side](/Planes/following/overview) can be checked against something real rather than against itself — and every row carries a certainty flag because [podcast-proxy dates](/Planes/following/speaking/20250429_tpusa_student_town_h), [scheduled-not-held rows](/Planes/following/speaking/20250918_fort_collins) and [after-death tour stops](/Planes/following/speaking/20250922_minneapolis) will manufacture false same-day hits the moment they are treated as live locations. The reason to be this careful is that tallies built from these locations — 68, 72, 73 overlaps — travelled into [press coverage](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) and into the [Israel](/Israel/overview) and [intelligence-services](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview) arguments as hard numbers, sitting beside the [withheld foreign-lead records](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records) and the [motive](/Motive/overview) case as though they carried the same evidentiary weight. The clearest single page here is [what the compilers counted as an overlap](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) — a jet within 50 to 100 miles and plus or minus three days of a claimed location — a rule loose enough that a dense speaking calendar scores hits on its own, and Erika's half cannot be checked at all because her [flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).
