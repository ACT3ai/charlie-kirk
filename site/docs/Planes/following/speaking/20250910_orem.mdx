# The American Comeback Tour — UVU (first stop of the fall tour)

**Charlie** at **Utah Valley University, Sorensen Center courtyard** (Orem, UT) on **2025-09-10**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

**A foreign-registered jet this investigation tracks was on the ground inside the 50-mile circle on the day itself:** [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) at [KPVU](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) on 2025-09-10, 16:05–20:29 UTC (0.8 mi from that field's own reference point); [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) at [KPVU](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) on 2025-09-10, 13:07–13:27 UTC (0.8 mi from that field's own reference point). These are the tails the whole following claim is about, and the sweep found them by geography, without being given a tail number.

[Provo Municipal (KPVU)](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) is about **5.4 miles from Orem**, the event city, and roughly **4 miles from the UVU campus** itself.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **3 foreign-registered**, **8 military**, **2 government-operated** and **4 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Orem, UT.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: C-GZBT (P28A) at KPVU; SU-BND (GLF4) at KPVU. 1 of the foreign-registration flags sits on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet.

**109 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**9 recorded ground contacts by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N102DZ](/Planes/N102DZ/overview), [N40JD](/Planes/N40JD/overview), [N560TW](/Planes/N560TW/overview), [N59906](/Planes/N59906/overview), [N872RA](/Planes/N872RA/overview), [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

## What the sources say

THE EVENT. Charlie Kirk was assassinated here at 12:23:30 PM MDT. First stop of TPUSA's fall American Comeback Tour (15 dates Sept 10-Oct 29 announced). Erika Kirk's presence is DISPUTED: one compilation spreadsheet places her in 'Nebraska/Delaware' for TPUSA media appearances that day; she has said she stayed home in Arizona with her mother. Neither version is independently documented. SU-BTT departed Provo (~7 mi) that morning; SU-BND sat at Provo with its transponder on and never took off. Next scheduled Charlie stop was Colorado State Sept 18.

*On that distance.* The paragraph above says Provo is "~7 mi" from the event, and that sentence is left exactly as written. It is worth naming what each figure is measured **to**, because three different numbers for the same airport circulate on this site and all three are legitimate. Computed from this repo's own airport coordinates (KPVU 40.218894, -111.722445), [Provo Municipal](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) is about **5.6 miles from the Orem city centroid** and about **4.1 miles from the UVU campus itself**. The generated [Incidents](/Planes/Incidents/overview) pages print **5.4 miles**, straight-line to the event city. The "~7 mi" above is the loosest of the three. An unlabelled mileage is the defect, not the disagreement.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.
