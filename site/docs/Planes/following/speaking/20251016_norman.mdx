# Turning Point Tour — University of Oklahoma

**TPUSA** at **University of Oklahoma** (Norman, OK) on **2025-10-16**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **2 foreign-registered**, **6 military** and **1 government-operated** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Norman, OK.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: VH-LEP (GLEX) at KOKC. 1 of the foreign-registration flags sits on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**20 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 88.0 | 15.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 63.7 | 9.4 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. ## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). ## Where this page fits

An announced date with the guest list still blank — Oklahoma went on the schedule before anyone knew who would fill Kirk's slot — and the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) carries it exactly as announced, AFTER_DEATH, outside the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) and outside the [Egyptian fleet thread](/Planes/Israel-Planes).
