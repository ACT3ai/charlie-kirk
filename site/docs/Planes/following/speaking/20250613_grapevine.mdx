## Where this page fits

This is the one row in the whole catalog where both Kirks are documented in the same place on the same dates, which makes it the single best test case for a claim built almost entirely on [Erika Kirk's locations](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights) — and it exposes how thin that side is, because her [flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and the sheet that produced 73 overlaps [placed her from Instagram](/Planes/following/Erika_Location_From_Instagram) rather than from any log. The wider case leans on that same weak side whenever it argues the jets tracked her rather than him, so the [claim that the planes followed Erika](/Planes/following/Erika_Tracked_Not_Charlie) has to be read next to [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) itself, the [donor pressure](/Amfest/donor-pressure-bookings) of that summer, and the [key individuals](/key_individuals/overview) whose travel is documented in ordinary ways. The most concrete thing downstream is her own on-camera invitation to [check the flight logs](/Planes/following/Erika_Flight_Log_Invitation) — an offer that cannot now be taken up, because the records she pointed at are the ones researchers say were removed.
