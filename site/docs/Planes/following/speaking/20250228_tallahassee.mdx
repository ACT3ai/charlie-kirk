## Where this page fits

Third Florida campus in nine days and the third documented FSU visit going back to October 2022 — a density that matters because the [overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) counted state-level co-presence, and a speaker who returns to the same campus every eighteen months will co-locate with a frequent transatlantic flyer sooner or later without either following the other, as the [window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition), the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the [Following](/Planes/following/overview) file all make plain. The parts of this case that do not dissolve under that argument are documentary: the [sealed autopsy and unnamed examiner](/CoverUp/Autopsy_Sealed_Examiner), the [rooftop body camera that died mid-scene](/CoverUp/Bagley_Bodycam_Gap), the [600,000-file discovery dump](/CoverUp/Discovery_Document_Dump) — records that exist, or provably do not. The body-camera page is the sharpest of those: UVU Officer Chris Bagley's only recording on September 10 ran 27 minutes 35 seconds and [stopped before crime-scene tape went down](/Law_Enforcement/officer-bagley-bodycam), which is where the [law-enforcement section](/Law_Enforcement/overview) takes it up.
