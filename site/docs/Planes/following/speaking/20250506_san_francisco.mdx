### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 760.3 | 132.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 54.4 | 8.0 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

An outdoor field booking with a published two-and-a-half-hour window is about as well-sourced as a location gets in this catalog, and that precision is the whole reason [speaking pages](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) exist separately from the [aircraft pages](/Planes/overview) — one side has to be solid before the other is allowed to touch it in the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) or in the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). San Francisco State also carries campus-conflict history: Riley Gaines was reportedly barricaded in a classroom after a 2023 TPUSA chapter event on this campus, a precedent that belongs with the [free-speech and event-security context](/Before/Free-Speech-Campus-Issues), the [campus and university](/campus_university/overview) record and the [security decisions](/Security_Team/overview) that mattered later at [UVU](/UVU/overview). The line worth chasing is on [UVU's pre-shooting warnings](/UVU/pre-shooting-warnings) page: at 10:35 PM the night before, two men reportedly came to campus police about "a plan," and at 11:19 PM the chain dismissed them as "GOP reps."
