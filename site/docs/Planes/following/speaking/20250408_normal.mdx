# American Comeback Tour — Illinois State University

**Charlie** at **Milner Plaza / Lincoln Statue, Illinois State University** (Normal, IL) on **2025-04-08**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Normal, IL**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2025-04-07 | Day before | 1047 | 6 | 1 |
| 2025-04-08 | **Same day** | 498 | 3 | 1 |
| 2025-04-09 | Day after | 1018 | 8 | 2 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2025-04-07 | 2025-04-08 | 2025-04-09 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 1 | 0 | 1 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**2 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 5.7 | 1.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 20.4 | 3.4 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A morning stop at Illinois State only matters alongside its evening twin: the same-day [UIUC appearance](/Planes/following/speaking/20250408_urbana) makes 8 April 2025 a two-campus day in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and two documented locations inside one day is the resolution the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) needs before any pairing with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) is argued. American Comeback Tour dates like this one are the ordinary business the rest of the case is measured against — the fall leg of the same tour opened at [UVU on 10 September](/Planes/following/speaking/20250910_orem), and the campus-speech format, the [private protective detail](/Security_Team/overview) and the [free-speech fights](/Before/Free-Speech-Campus-Issues) around these events sit in [Before](/Before/overview) and in [Campus and University](/campus_university/overview). Worth the click: [what the compilers counted as an overlap](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) records a jet "within 50-100 miles and plus or minus 3 days" of a claimed location — a rule loose enough that a two-campus day in central Illinois would score twice.
