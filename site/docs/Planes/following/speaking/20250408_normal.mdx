**Charlie** at **Milner Plaza / Lincoln Statue, Illinois State University** (Normal, IL) on **2025-04-08**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). ## Where this page fits

A morning stop at Illinois State only matters alongside its evening twin: the same-day [UIUC appearance](/Planes/following/speaking/20250408_urbana) makes 8 April 2025 a two-campus day in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and two documented locations inside one day is the resolution the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) needs before any pairing with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) is argued. American Comeback Tour dates like this one are the ordinary business the rest of the case is measured against — the fall leg of the same tour opened at [UVU on 10 September](/Planes/following/speaking/20250910_orem), and the campus-speech format, the [private protective detail](/Security_Team/overview) and the [free-speech fights](/Before/Free-Speech-Campus-Issues) around these events sit in [Before](/Before/overview) and in [Campus and University](/campus_university/overview).
