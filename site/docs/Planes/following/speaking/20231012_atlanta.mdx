## Where this page fits

The livestream names Candace Owens and not Charlie Kirk, so this row is explicitly not a Charlie campus hit — the distinction matters because the [following-pattern claim](/Planes/following/overview) is about the Kirks' movements, and folding a co-speaker's stop into the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) inflates the [tally](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) for free. [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) is the person who later put SU-BTT and SU-BND on air, so keeping her tour dates separate from Kirk's is not pedantry but the difference between an independent claim and a circular one, which is why the [people map](/People/overview), the [organizations record](/organizations_groups/overview) and the [media coverage of her Israel controversies](/Media/Candace-Owens-Israel-Controversies) are read alongside it. Her broadcast record is rebuilt show by show on [the Candace Owens broadcasts page](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts), including the revision from 68 overlaps to 73, and every name in the material is catalogued on [everyone named in the following-planes record](/Planes/following/Named_People).
