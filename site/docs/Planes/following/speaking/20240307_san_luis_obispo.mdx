## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **San Luis Obispo, CA**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024-03-06 | Day before | 634 | 3 | 0 |
| 2024-03-07 | **Same day** | 696 | 5 | 1 |
| 2024-03-08 | Day after | 866 | 5 | 0 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2024-03-06 | 2024-03-07 | 2024-03-08 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 0 | 1 | 0 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**1 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 4.3 | 0.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 21.0 | 3.9 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A student newspaper gave this row something almost nothing else on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) has — a time of day, roughly 12:30 to 2 p.m. on Dexter Lawn — which is the resolution the [following-pattern claim](/Planes/following/overview) would need everywhere before a same-day landing could mean anything against a [three-day overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition). An open lawn, a table, hundreds of students and a protest march at noon is the exact format of the last event, and the [campus and university](/campus_university/overview) record, the [locations index](/Locations/overview), the [UVU courtyard](/UVU/Courtyward) and the [protective detail's perimeter gaps](/Security_Team/perimeter-rooftop-failures) are all arguments about how that format was secured. The most specific security finding on the site is [the claim that the next event had no plan](/Security_Team/next-event-no-plan) — an anonymous account that a detail which normally planned far in advance had nothing prepared for September 11.
