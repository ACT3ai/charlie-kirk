# Live Free Tour — University of Kansas

**Charlie** at **University of Kansas** (Lawrence, KS) on **2024-04-03**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

**Nothing foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered was on the ground here.** A blind sweep of the entire open ADS-B archive — every aircraft flying that UTC day, roughly 74,000 to 95,000 of them, not a tail-number lookup — kept only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on Lawrence, KS and found none of those four categories parked inside it on the day of the event.

**The 4 aircraft it did flag carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag.** That is an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. It is not a finding.

## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Lawrence, KS**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024-04-02 | Day before | 841 | 7 | 1 |
| 2024-04-03 | **Same day** | 876 | 12 | 0 |
| 2024-04-04 | Day after | 1084 | 32 | 3 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2024-04-02 | 2024-04-03 | 2024-04-04 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 1 | 0 | 3 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**4 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 17.0 | 1.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 23.4 | 4.1 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Kansas is a clustering-claim state, so this row gets tested rather than assumed: the [matching overlap page](/Planes/following/overlap/20240403_KS_lawrence/overview) has SU-BTT at Wichita that morning and departing at 8:43 a.m., leaving the state on the day of the event, which is a miss — and the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) and the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) both say so. The University of Kansas stop itself is ordinary [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) campus work, of a piece with the rest of the [campus and university](/campus_university/overview) record, and it only matters here because [Wichita](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview) hosts an FAA Part 145 Dassault service centre that gives Egyptian Falcons an innocent reason to be in the state at all. The later Wichita landing cited alongside this event is roughly sixteen months away, and the arithmetic for claims like it is worked through on [the 73 overlaps](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) and [the overlap window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition).
