## What was actually on the ground near this event

**This date was not covered by the geographic sweep.** The sweep streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive and filters it to a 50-mile circle on the event city. It has not been run for 2025-08-20 to 2025-08-22 at Dallas, TX, so this page cannot say what was or was not parked nearby.

That is a **gap in our coverage, not a finding**. It is not evidence that nothing was there, and it must never be quoted as though it were. ## Where this page fits

Win the Storm sits on Kirk's own events page rather than on any [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) calendar, and it collides with the [North Myrtle Beach date](/Planes/following/speaking/20250821_north_myrtle_beach) listed for the following night — a collision the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) records rather than resolves, because one of the two listings may simply be stale. Unresolved calendar collisions are exactly the material that produces false confidence downstream, the failure mode running through the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps), the [press coverage](/Planes/following/Press_Coverage) that carried them as hard numbers, and — well outside the aircraft thread — the [evidence contradictions](/Other/Evidence-Contradictions) and disputed timestamps catalogued across the [timeline](/Timeline/overview). Three weeks after these Dallas dates Kirk was dead, and the page that best carries what happened in between is [Pre-Assassination Context, September 3-9](/Topic-Analyses/Pre_Assassination_Context), where UVU records show two men asking campus police about "a plan" the night before.
