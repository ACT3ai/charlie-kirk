# American Comeback Tour — UC San Diego

**Charlie** at **UC San Diego Town Square** (San Diego, CA) on **2025-05-01**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **14 foreign-registered** and **15 military** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on San Diego, CA.

14 of the foreign-registration flags sit on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**171 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

**3 recorded ground contacts by aircraft this investigation already tracks fall on the day of the event** — [N40JD](/Planes/N40JD/overview), [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview), [N872RA](/Planes/N872RA/overview). Each has its own contact page, linked further down.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## The pairing, in one sentence

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. It is not itself an overlap with [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview). One campus stop is not evidence of shadowing; the claim, if any, is the repetition across many stops.

## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). Innocent explanations for any one pairing — maintenance hubs, diplomatic travel, a tracker misreading a nearest-public-airport label — are stated on those pages, not waived here.

## Where this page fits

Two outlets date the same UC San Diego appearance four days apart — Fox says 1 May, a CalMatters caption says 5 May — and this page records the disagreement instead of picking a winner, because a silently chosen date is exactly how a false pairing enters the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) and the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) built on top of the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview). Date disputes of this kind recur across the whole case: [Tokyo](/Planes/following/speaking/20250907_tokyo) is given as 6 or 7 September, the impact minute is disputed as 12:23 versus 12:27 in [Evidence Contradictions](/Other/Evidence-Contradictions), and the [timeline hub](/Timeline/overview) and the [narrative section](/Narrative/overview) both carry versions that changed after the fact. Worth the click: [Shot in the Heart](/Narrative/Shot_in_the_heart), where a TV witness said "one shot, right directly to the heart" and that police had "arrested the wrong guy" — a first account the official version later had to overwrite.
