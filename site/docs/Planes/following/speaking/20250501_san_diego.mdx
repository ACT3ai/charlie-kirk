## Where this page fits

Two outlets date the same UC San Diego appearance four days apart — Fox says 1 May, a CalMatters caption says 5 May — and this page records the disagreement instead of picking a winner, because a silently chosen date is exactly how a false pairing enters the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) and the [overlap tallies](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) built on top of the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview). Date disputes of this kind recur across the whole case: [Tokyo](/Planes/following/speaking/20250907_tokyo) is given as 6 or 7 September, the impact minute is disputed as 12:23 versus 12:27 in [Evidence Contradictions](/Other/Evidence-Contradictions), and the [timeline hub](/Timeline/overview) and the [narrative section](/Narrative/overview) both carry versions that changed after the fact.
