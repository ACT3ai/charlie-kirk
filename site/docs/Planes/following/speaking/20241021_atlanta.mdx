### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Two campuses in two states on one day — Georgia State in the morning, [Chapel Hill in the afternoon](/Planes/following/speaking/20241021_chapel_hill) — is precisely the movement pattern that makes a plus-or-minus-three-day, 50-to-100-mile [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) generous enough to catch almost anything, and why the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) records times of day wherever a [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) source gives them. Georgia recurs across this file: the [Duluth rally two days later](/Planes/following/speaking/20241023_duluth) and, further back, the [Atlanta row on the reconstructed sheet](/Planes/following/overlap/20230115_GA_atlanta_owens_008/overview) that the audit marked inaccurate — the sort of result the [suspicious index](/Suspicious/overview) and the [theories map](/Theories/overview) both need in front of them. Row-by-row audit verdicts are the real value of the [73-overlap page](/Planes/following/73_overlaps): it records which claimed pairings survive contact with the flight record and which do not.
