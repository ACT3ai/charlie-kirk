## Where this page fits

The Hillsdale seminar was held in Phoenix, not on the Michigan campus, and getting that right matters more than it sounds: the March 2 podcast proxy sits in the same calendar neighbourhood as [SU-BTU's reported Detroit-to-Provo leg](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview), and a mislocated venue would have manufactured a Michigan pairing out of nothing — which is what the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), the [Detroit airport record](/Planes/following/Detroit_KDTW_2025-02-03_to_2025-03-30/overview) and the [73-overlap audit](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) exist to prevent. No document ties the two, and saying so is the same discipline that governs the [cover-up section](/CoverUp/overview): a [record that was never made](/CoverUp/Foreign_Flight_Records) is not a record that was destroyed, and the [FBI file](/FBI/overview) has to be read the same way. Where a document does exist it is worth naming — former NCTC director [Joe Kent says on camera](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) his team was told to stop what you're doing now because we have a suspect, which is the spine of the [intel-services case](/Proof_Intel_Services/overview).
