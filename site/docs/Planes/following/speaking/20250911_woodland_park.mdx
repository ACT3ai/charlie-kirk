**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

A conference listed as a *past event* on Kirk's own website the day after he was killed — this row exists so a scraped calendar never becomes a location, and the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) flags it SCHEDULED_NOT_HELD alongside [Colorado State](/Planes/following/speaking/20250918_fort_collins), [Murfreesboro](/Planes/following/speaking/20250919_murfreesboro) and the [Dartmouth debate](/Planes/following/speaking/20250925_hanover) for the same reason. 11 September is a surrender date rather than a speaking date, and what the record actually holds for it is the [Sept 11 surrender and custody timeline](/After/Sept_11_Surrender_And_Custody), the [Miranda-timing conflict](/court/mirandize/mirandize-overview) and the [casket flight](/Planes/Casket-Flight) out of Salt Lake City — with [Tyler Robinson charged, not convicted](/People/tyler-robinson). The hook is a two-hour inversion: a defence filing quotes video exhibit Bates 003996-R2 showing [Robinson Mirandized at 6:25 PM](/court/mirandize/custody-timeline-fake-confession), before the 8:02 PM call that supposedly first identified him and roughly ninety minutes before the Discord "confession" appears.
