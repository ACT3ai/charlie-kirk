## Where this page fits

A seven-day span with a city but no venue is close to the limit of what the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) can do with a row: it replaced an entry whose city was simply UNKNOWN, which is progress, but a week-wide window cannot test anything against the [following record](/Planes/following/overview), the [event ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) or the [overlap definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition).
