### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 666.3 | 126.3 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 19.8 | 2.7 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Kirk announced this one himself and the podcast carrying it went out on April 24, forty-one days later, which is the largest proxy gap on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the clearest demonstration of why podcast dates cannot be fed into the [following record](/Planes/following/overview) or the [73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Forty-one days is more than thirteen times the [plus-or-minus-three-day window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) the compilers used, and it is why the [campus record](/campus_university/overview), the [TPUSA organisation pages](/TPUSA/overview) and the [locations index](/Locations/overview) are treated as primary rather than the spreadsheet. What that discipline produced on the aircraft side is [the per-aircraft recovery status page](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status): what every flight-data source still holds for each tail, and how far back each archive actually reaches.
