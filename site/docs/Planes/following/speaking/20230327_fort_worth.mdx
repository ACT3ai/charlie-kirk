## What was actually on the ground near this event

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Fort Worth, TX**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

| Sweep day (UTC) | | Aircraft entering the circle | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023-03-26 | Day before | 2317 | 58 | 17 |
| 2023-03-27 | **Same day** | 2100 | 34 | 11 |
| 2023-03-28 | Day after | 523 | 20 | 2 |

### What "flagged" is actually made of

| Category | 2023-03-26 | 2023-03-27 | 2023-03-28 | What it means |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Military | 1 | 3 | 0 | A military serial or a military database flag. Mostly routine — see the note below. |
| Government operator | 1 | 0 | 0 | The registered operator string names a government body. |
| FAA privacy program (LADD) | 12 | 6 | 1 | **Ordinary and not suspicious.** Thousands of US owners file for this; at a training field it is mostly Cessna 172s. |

**19 of the flagged aircraft here carry nothing but the FAA privacy flag.** They are excluded from the named table below. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site will not do it.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 37.3 | 10.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 11.9 | 1.7 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

## Where this page fits

Every row on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) fixed this way — a TPUSA press-release date replacing a podcast-release date five days later — narrows the window the [following-pattern claim](/Planes/following/overview) has to survive, and it feeds straight into the [event-by-event aircraft ledger](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) and the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Texas Christian sat on the same [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) spring tour that built the [campus operation](/campus_university/overview) Charlie Kirk ran until his last event, and the [political pressure field](/political_context/overview) that later attached to those bookings is documented separately from the tour dates themselves. If you read one page next, make it the [overlap window definition](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition): the compilers counted a jet within 50 to 100 miles and plus or minus three days as an overlap, which is why a five-day date error changes the answer for tails like [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview).
