# Live Free Tour / Prove Me Wrong — University of Washington

**Charlie** at **HUB Lawn then HUB Ballroom, University of Washington** (Seattle, WA) on **2024-05-07**. Date certainty: this is a sourced event date, not a podcast-release proxy.

Named in the table below on the day of the event: **2 foreign-registered**, **1 military**, **1 government-operated** and **3 unregistered or non-ICAO** aircraft on the ground inside a 50-mile circle on Seattle, WA.

The foreign-registered civil aircraft among them: C-GHAP (DHC6) at KBFI, 7.5 mi out. 1 of the foreign-registration flags sits on a **military serial** — a transponder address that resolves to no civil register, not a foreign civil jet. None of them is one of the Egyptian-registered tails this investigation tracks. Foreign registration on its own means only that the aircraft is not on the US register.

**53 of the flagged aircraft across the swept days carry nothing but the FAA's routine LADD privacy flag** — an ordinary filing thousands of US owners make, and at a training field mostly Cessna 172s. They are excluded from the named table. Counting them as suspicious is the single easiest way to inflate a number on this subject, and this site does not do it.

An aircraft inside a circle is not an aircraft at an event, and a trace places an airframe, never a person. The full sweep result, the control-city comparison and the limits are immediately below.

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. ## What we do not know

ADS-B holes, erased Erika logs, and podcast-proxy dates all cut against a clean overlap table. If the date certainty above is `PODCAST_PROXY`, do not publish a "same day as a foreign jet" claim off this row. If the city is `UNKNOWN` or `AMBIGUOUS`, do not fill it from memory.

## This is not an overlap claim

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). ## Where this page fits

Two venues in one day — the HUB Lawn in the afternoon, the HUB Ballroom at 6:30 p.m. — make this one of the better-resolved rows on the [speaking index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and it replaced a podcast entry five days late that would have put Kirk in Seattle on the wrong side of the [overlap window](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) used by the [following record](/Planes/following/overview). Washington State never appears in the Egyptian aircraft record at all, which is the useful part: the [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) campus schedule went nationwide while the tails clustered in a handful of states, and the [campus and university](/campus_university/overview) and [locations](/Locations/overview) material is where that mismatch is kept honest. The best single test of the whole claim is [overlap recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Overlap-Recovery), where all 69 testable aircraft-and-date pairs were pulled against both free daily ADS-B archives and twenty-three now have a primary trace behind them — with [what a 403 actually means](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/What-A-403-Means) explaining why an absent record is not a deletion.
