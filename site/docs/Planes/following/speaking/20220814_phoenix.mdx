## Where this page fits

This is Turning Point Action, not Turning Point USA — a different entity with a different legal form — and the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) holds the distinction because the aircraft question is asked of organizations by name, not of a movement, before anything reaches the [event-by-event tally](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events). The two arms are separated on this site under [the 501(c)(4) political arm](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/turning-point-action) and [the 501(c)(3) itself](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/turning-point-usa), with the wider [LLC web](/TPUSA/LLCs/overview) and the [for-profit companies around Turning Point](/TPUSA/Companies/overview) mapped alongside.
