## Where this page fits

Florida State is the seventh Live Free stop and one of the clearest reasons the speaker of record has to be logged: Kirk, Candace Owens and Jack Posobiec alternated hosting this tour, so a tour date is not automatically a Kirk date in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), the [following-planes record](/Planes/following/overview) or the [73-overlap reconstruction](/Planes/following/73_overlaps). Kirk came back to FSU on [28 February 2025](/Planes/following/speaking/20250228_tallahassee) during the American Comeback Tour — the tour he was still running when he was killed at [UVU](/UVU/overview), where a detail with [no counter-snipers and no Secret Service](/Security_Team/no-counter-snipers) covered a crowd of roughly three thousand. Read [the aircraft record placed beside every TPUSA event](/Planes/following/TPUSA_events) next: it is the page where a tour date either has a foreign-registered jet beside it or visibly does not, which is the honest form of the claim once [the counting rule](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition) is published.
