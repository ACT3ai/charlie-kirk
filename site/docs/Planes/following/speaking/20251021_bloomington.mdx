# Turning Point Tour — Indiana University Bloomington

**TPUSA** at **Indiana University Bloomington** (Bloomington, IN) on **2025-10-21**. Date certainty: Charlie Kirk was already dead. TPUSA and/or Erika Kirk continued the tour with guest speakers.

**Nothing foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered was on the ground here.** A blind sweep of the entire open ADS-B archive — every aircraft flying that UTC day, roughly 74,000 to 95,000 of them, not a tail-number lookup — kept only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on Bloomington, IN and found none of those four categories parked inside it on the day of the event.

A **blind geographic sweep** streams an entire UTC day of the open ADS-B archive — 74,000 to 95,000 aircraft — and keeps only what fell inside a 50-mile circle on **Bloomington, IN**. It is not given a tail number, so it cannot be steered toward a wanted answer. Here is what it found around this event.

### The foreign, military, government and unregistered aircraft, named

**None.** On the swept days around this event, no foreign-registered, military, government-operated or unregistered aircraft was on the ground inside the circle.

### The same days, at cities with no event

On these same sweep dates the run also covered six control cities with no Kirk or TPUSA event. Averaged per circle-day:

| | On the ground | Flagged on the ground |
|---|---:|---:|
| **This event circle** | 1.0 | 0.0 |
| Control circles (18 circle-days) | 63.4 | 9.7 |

**Do not read a bigger number here as a finding.** Event cities are large metros with large airports; several control cities are not. A raw per-circle comparison measures airport size, and once it is normalised for that, event circles hold a *lower* share of flagged aircraft than the controls. See [Investigating Deleted Flights](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) §4.5.

**What this section cannot tell you.** A trace proves presence — never purpose, and never occupancy. Military aircraft near Salt Lake City are routine: the Utah Air National Guard's refuelling wing is based at the airport and the Army Guard flies helicopters from South Valley Regional. An aircraft being in a circle is not an aircraft being *at* an event.

This page records a [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) / Charlie Kirk speaking appearance so the [following-pattern](/Planes/following/overview) can be tested against a real location, not a guessed one. ## What we do not know

A documented speaking date is the *Kirk / TPUSA side* of a pairing. The foreign side lives in `flights.csv` and on the location pages under [Following](/Planes/following/overview). ## Where this page fits

Bloomington had been booked for Kirk himself — an Indiana return after [Purdue in April](/Planes/following/speaking/20250410_west_lafayette) — and Turning Point kept the date after he was killed, which is why the row reads AFTER_DEATH and cannot support a [Charlie location](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview) in the [catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) or the [following record](/Planes/following/overview). An organisation continuing a dead founder's calendar is an organisational fact, and that thread is one of the strongest non-aircraft lines in the case: the [September 2 COO memo](/TPUSA/charlie-replace-coo), the [audit reportedly dropped](/TPUSA/DOGE-TPUSA-Connection) after him, the [organizations and groups map](/organizations_groups/overview) and the [companies index](/Companies_Organizations/overview) that traces the LLCs around them. The oddest of those is [GGLF 2023 LLC](/TPUSA/LLCs/gglf-2023-llc): a life-insurance policy on Charlie Kirk reportedly held not by TPUSA but inside an anonymous Wyoming LLC, with a reported $350,000 loan taken against it.
