## Location pages added from the ADS-B flight-history captures (Aug 2026)

The Large File Bridge OCR / AI-description pass over the four mirrored plane directories
(`Planes/`, `Plane_Egypt_SU_BTT/`, `Plane_N888KG/`, `Plane_Piper_N59906/`) recovered the full
Flightradar24 aircraft-history tables for all five Egyptian tails from the `5_Planes/` screenshot
set. Reading those tables as *stays* rather than *legs* surfaced three locations that the fleet
used repeatedly and that had no page of their own:

* **[Paris–Le Bourget (LFPB)](./Paris_LFPB_2022-10-05_to_2025-10-05/overview)** — 18 recorded stops, every
  westbound rotation, always an overnight, never eastbound. The busiest node in the dataset after
  Cairo and the one nobody alleges anything about.
* **[Goose Bay (CYYR)](./GooseBay_CYYR_2023-05-09_to_2025-09-13/overview)** — Gulfstreams only, Falcons
  never. Where SU-BND left North America on 13 September 2025 on a ~50-minute turn.
* **[Williston (KXWA)](./Williston_KXWA_2024-04-23_to_2024-04-23/overview)** — the 2024 Utah entry field,
  and the reason "every Provo rotation entered the US through North Dakota" is a two-year pattern
  rather than a 2025 one.

`flights.csv` was rebuilt from the same tables at the same time: its five `mdx_page` join keys had
all been pointing at filenames that were never used, and several of its tail attributions
(notably the 27 May – 2 Jun 2025 Provo stay, which is **SU-BTV**, not SU-BTU) disagreed with the
ADS-B record.
