# The 12 Erika rows position data corroborates

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table headed
"The two ledgers do not behave the same way" has an **Erika Kirk** row reading 56 claimed, **12
corroborated**, 3 refuted, and "39 (+1 names no aircraft)" the archives cannot answer — the
drill-down behind that last cell holds 41 rows. This page is the **12**. The register holds
12 rows in this slice, matching the figure printed on the overview, and it is the number behind the
overview's sentence that on the Erika side "barely a fifth are corroborated".

## What this slice is

A row is here when **two things are both true**: the row's claim is about Erika Kirk (Charlie is not
claimed on it), and a raw ADS-B trace this investigation recovered and stored puts the **named
aircraft** within 15 km of the **named airport** on the **claimed date**. Corroboration is measured
against the aircraft half of the claim only. Nothing in this slice tests the person half — no trace
carries a passenger list. Filter `overlaps.csv` for rows with `attendee_class = ERIKA_ONLY` and an
ADS-B verdict of "At the claimed airport — corroborated" and you reproduce these 12 exactly.

## What the specifics show

**Corroborated here means the aircraft, not the person.** All 12 rows carry the same ADS-B verdict —
the named jet was inside 15 km of the named field on the claimed date — and all 12 are rows where
the compilers claim **Erika Kirk and not Charlie Kirk**. The register establishes Erika Kirk's
presence on **zero** of them. What has been corroborated on this page is the aircraft half of a
two-part claim whose other half has no document behind it.

**Only two of the 12 are the strongest evidence class.** OWENS-013 (20 April 2023) puts SU-BTT on
the ground, transmitting, 0.56 km from Wilmington New Castle; OWENS-025 (19 April 2024) puts SU-BND
on the ground 0.05 km from Provo Municipal. The other ten are airborne positions below 4,000 ft and
within 15 km of the field — an arrival, a departure or a low pass — and the register labels them
that way row by row rather than rolling them in with the ground records. Read strictly, the ground
count for the whole Erika-corroborated ledger is **2**.

**The two longest rows here argue against following rather than for it.** OWENS-025 records SU-BND
arriving Provo on 19 April 2024 and sitting there until 12 July — **84 days**. OWENS-042 records the
same aircraft on the Provo ramp from 23 May 2025 to 13 September 2025 — **113 days**. An aircraft
parked on one ramp for three or four months is not tracking anybody's itinerary, and both of those
rows are counted in this slice as corroborations. That is the honest shape of the corroboration: it
confirms where the metal was, and in these two cases where it was is *stationary*.

**The Kirk side of these rows is almost entirely empty.** Eight of the 12 carry no sourced Kirk or
TPUSA appearance within three days anywhere in the country. Three more have a nearest sourced
appearance exactly three days away — OWENS-013 against Topeka, OWENS-039 against Dallas, OWENS-042
against Orem. Exactly **one** row falls on a day this site independently sources a Kirk appearance:
SITE-005, 10 September 2025 — and on that row the corroborated position is **Wilmington, Delaware**
while the sourced appearance is **Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah**. The same
row records the spreadsheet placing Erika in "Nebraska/Delaware" against Erika Kirk's own account
placing her in Arizona. On the register's single best-dated day, the sheet's own Erika cell is
contradicted by the person it describes.

**The audit and the position data disagree on this slice, and both results stand.** Six of the 12
were called **Accurate** by the tracking-site audit, four **Partially accurate**, and two —
OWENS-021 and OWENS-025 — **Inaccurate**, yet the recovered traces corroborate the airport on all
12. The two methods were run against different evidence: one re-read tracking-site pages, the other
measured stored position traces. Where they disagree the disagreement is the finding, and neither is
quietly preferred here.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** Two of these rows say so
  in the data itself: the ground-flag entries for OWENS-013 and OWENS-025 both end with the phrase
  "It places NOBODY aboard."
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row** — not on these 12, not anywhere in the
  register. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated
  itinerary has ever been published. Every location attributed to her on this page is a cell on a
  circulated compilation spreadsheet, and the compilers' own cells frequently name two states at
  once.
* **Corroborating an airport is not corroborating a purpose.** Wilmington is a customs and fuel
  field, Provo and Lincoln both host Duncan Aviation facilities, and each of the 12 rows carries its
  own innocent explanation on its full row page. Nothing here establishes tasking, and no public
  record establishes tasking in either direction.
* **This slice contains no archive gaps, so none is being read as anything.** All 12 rows returned
  data. Where `NOT_HEARD` and `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE` appear on
  [the sibling undecided page](/Planes/following/cuts/ledger-erika-undecided-41) they are limits on
  what volunteer ADS-B archives hold — never deletions, never suppression.
