# Every overlap that involves Erika Kirk — all 70 rows

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), in the table headed *The question people actually ask*, the row **"Overlaps involving Erika at all (61 alone + the 9 shared)"** reads **70** rows across **57** distinct dates. This page is those 70 rows, one line each, with every field the register holds and both verdicts that have been passed on them. The count here agrees exactly with the figure printed on the overview.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list if the compilers' sheet names **Erika Kirk** on it — whether she is named alone or named together with Charlie Kirk. Nothing else qualifies a row: the aircraft, the airport, the date and the verdicts are all irrelevant to the filter. Reproduce it from `overlaps.csv` by taking every row whose Erika column is `claimed`, which returns the **61** rows resting on Erika alone plus the **9** rows claiming both Kirks. The five rows that were never published are inside this list, because the sheet's author attributed them to Erika even though she published no date, city, airport or tail for any of them.

**Erika Kirk's presence is established on none of these 70 rows.** Every one of them records a *claim* about where she was. Not one is supported by a passenger manifest, a boarding record, a photograph at the field, or a published itinerary.

## What the specifics show

**The Erika column is where nearly the whole register lives.** 70 of the 85 rows name her, so this slice is not a subset in any meaningful sense — it is the sheet. It also carries most of what recovered position data can speak to: **18 of the 25 rows** the archives put at the claimed field across the whole register are here, and so are **all three** rows the archives place somewhere else entirely.

**The two methods disagree, and both results stay on this page.** The tracking-site audit called **42** of these 70 rows inaccurate and only **16** accurate. Measured against stored ADS-B traces instead, **18** are at the claimed field and **3** are refuted. Those are different tests against different evidence — one a re-reading of tracking-site pages, one a measurement of position traces this investigation holds — and where they disagree the disagreement is the finding, not a thing to be averaged away.

**The strongest rows are concentrated on one field and one stretch of 2024–2025.** Seven of the eight ground-position confirmations are at [Provo](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview): `OWENS-025` on 19 April 2024 at 0.05 km, `SITE-004` on 23 April 2024 at 0.00 km, `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` on 23 May 2025 at 0.03 km, and `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` on 10 September 2025, the day of the shooting, at 1.23–1.29 km. The eighth is `OWENS-013` at [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) on 20 April 2023 at 0.56 km.

**The three refutations are all Erika-only rows, and they are far off.** `OWENS-015` (9 May 2023, claimed Missouri) is 1,062 km from the claimed field; `OWENS-050` (12 May 2023, KSTL) is 7,034 km away; `OWENS-036` (18 June 2025, KOMA) is 10,140 km away. A row that is wrong by ten thousand kilometres is not a near miss.

**The list restates itself.** Five of the 70 rows carry no date at all, and the remaining 65 dated rows resolve to just 57 dates because six dates carry more than one row: 12 January 2025, 15 March 2025, 23 May 2025 and 7 July 2025 each appear twice as index pairs the register itself flags as duplicates (31/61, 33/63, 35/65, 37/66); 20 July 2025 appears twice at two different Nebraska fields (`OWENS-038` at KOMA, `OWENS-067` at KLNK); and 10 September 2025 carries four rows. Any headline built on the row count is inflated relative to the date count by those eight rows.

**Where the list is weakest.** **48 of the 70** rows cannot be decided by position data at all — 32 not heard, 10 with no free archive coverage of the date, 5 with no date to ask about, and 1 with no aircraft to look up. Only **9** rows fall on a calendar date this site can independently source a Kirk or TPUSA appearance for, and only **4** of those put the appearance and the aircraft in the same state. Of those four, **3** are at the same field — `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006`, all at Provo on 10 September 2025, which is also the nearest field to the UVU venue. The fourth, `SITE-004` on 23 April 2024, has the aircraft at Provo while the sourced appearance is at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City — the same state and the same day, but a different airport. On the other five, the sourced appearance is in a different state entirely: Tallahassee against Chicago, Fort Worth against Wilmington, San Diego against Wichita, Tampa against Omaha, and Orem against Wilmington.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** Every corroboration on this page, including the eight ground-position confirmations, establishes only that an Egypt-registered jet transmitted positions at or near a field. Not one of them shows who was on it, or that anyone got on or off.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any of these 70 rows.** Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary has ever been published. The sheet's Erika column is a claimed cell — often a state rather than a city, sometimes two states, sometimes qualified as "potentially" — and on several rows here it is blank or reads UNKNOWN. The count of rows where her presence is actually established is [zero](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-established-0).
* **The 32 "not heard" rows and the 10 "no archive coverage" rows are archive limits, not deletions.** "Not heard" means a volunteer archive that served other aircraft that day holds no trace for this airframe; a parked aircraft with its transponder off, an aircraft outside receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from outside. "No archive coverage" means no free archive reaches that date for any airframe probed. Neither says the aircraft was elsewhere, and neither is evidence that anything was suppressed.
* **An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence.** 42 of these rows are undecidable by position data for that reason alone.
* **Every claim on this page is the compilers'.** The rows come from a spreadsheet circulated by others and from posts amplifying it; the audit verdicts are a third party's re-reading of tracking-site pages. This site asserts no crime by any person named here and no purpose behind any flight.
* **On the counts, this page and the overview agree.** Both give 70 rows and 57 distinct dates. Where the overview's *audit* figures look smaller — 44 of 78 inaccurate site-wide — that is because the audit was carried out against a 78-row version of the register, while this register now holds 85 rows; the audit verdicts here are carried over unchanged and the rows it never reached are marked as such.
