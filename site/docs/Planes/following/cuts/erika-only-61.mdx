# The 61 rows resting on Erika Kirk alone

**This page opens the number 61 — the rows that rest on Erika Kirk alone, and they are 72% of the entire claim.** **Two ground confirmations out of 61. Five rows fall on a day this site can source a Kirk or TPUSA appearance, and on all five the appearance is in a different state from the aircraft.** This is the largest and the weakest block in the register at the same time.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table
counting who each claimed overlap is attached to carries a row reading **"Overlaps where Erika is
claimed and Charlie is not — 61, across 53 distinct dates."** This page is that cell, opened.
All **61** rows are below, with every field the register holds on each of them. The count here
and the count printed on the overview agree exactly.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the compilers' sheet claims **Erika Kirk** was somewhere the aircraft
went, **and does not claim Charlie Kirk alongside her**. In register terms that is
`erika_present = claimed` together with `charlie_present = not_claimed`, and both conditions hold
on all 61 rows with no exceptions or borderline cases. Rows claiming both Kirks are excluded and
live in [the nine shared rows](/Planes/following/cuts/charlie-and-erika-9); rows claiming Charlie
without her are in [the nine Charlie-only rows](/Planes/following/cuts/charlie-only-9). Reproduce
this list from `overlaps.csv` by taking every row whose *Erika Present* column reads claimed and
whose *Charlie Present* column does not.

## What the specifics show

**The aircraft side of this list is checkable and it is thin.** Of the 61 rows, position data
recovered by this investigation puts the named airframe at the named field on **12** — and only
two of those twelve are ground-flag records: `OWENS-013`, SU-BTT on the ground 0.56 km from
Wilmington New Castle on **20 April 2023**, and `OWENS-025`, SU-BND on the ground 0.05 km from
Provo on **19 April 2024**. Three rows are refuted outright by the same data, and the misses are
not small: on `OWENS-015` (9 May 2023, Goose Bay) the closest recovered position is 1,063 km away,
on `OWENS-050` (12 May 2023, St. Louis) it is 7,034 km, and on `OWENS-036` (18 June 2025, Omaha)
it is 10,140 km. One row, `OWENS-012` (14 April 2023, Wichita), lands 28 km from the named field —
inside the sheet's own stated tolerance, outside a same-airport claim.

**The person side of this list is not checkable at all, and that is the finding.** The register
records what the sheet's *Erika Location* column says, and on **42 of the 61 rows** that column
holds nothing but a bare US state — "Nebraska", "Kansas", "Utah". On a further **9** it names two
places at once, such as "Kansas / Delaware" or "Delaware / Nebraska". On **8** it reads UNKNOWN.
One row, `OWENS-013`, is the single case where a specific location cell was read out in public
("potentially Giza") and then checked against a dated Instagram post, and the check did not
support it. The 61st, `SITE-005` on **10 September 2025**, is the one row where the cell is itself
in dispute: the sheet reads "Nebraska/Delaware" while Erika Kirk's own account places her in
Arizona, and the register carries both rather than picking one. That is the whole column —
42 bare states, 9 double entries, 8 UNKNOWN, 1 hedged and 1 disputed. A state is not a location,
and none of these entries is an itinerary.

**Where the list is weakest is the same place it is largest.** Twenty-nine rows come back
**Not heard** — the archives cover the date and the volunteer receivers recorded nothing for that
airframe — and a further ten fall in windows **no free archive reaches**. Both are limits of the
archives, not findings about the aircraft, and neither is evidence of a deletion. Five rows carry
no date at any point, so no archive can be asked about them. The tracking-site audit, working the
same claims against paid flight-history archives, called **38 of the 61 inaccurate** and 11
accurate.

**Five rows fall on a day this site can independently source a Kirk or TPUSA appearance — and all
five point away from the claim.** On `OWENS-002` the jet is claimed at Chicago while the sourced
Charlie Kirk appearance is at Florida State in Tallahassee. On `OWENS-048` it is Wilmington against
Fort Worth. On `OWENS-022`, Wichita against San Diego. On `EXTRA-004`, Omaha against the Student
Action Summit in Tampa. On `SITE-005`, Wilmington against UVU in Orem on 10 September 2025. In
every case the aircraft and the one sourced person are in different states.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** The twelve corroborations on
  this page establish that an Egyptian-registered jet was where the sheet said it was. They
  establish nothing about who was inside it, and the register's own notes say so on each row.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero of these 61 rows.** Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), and no dated itinerary for her has ever been
  published by the compilers, by Turning Point USA, or by anyone else. Every row on this page rests
  on a spreadsheet cell naming a state, not on a record placing her anywhere.
* **An absence in an archive is not an absence in the world, and it is not a deletion.** The 29
  *Not heard* rows and the 10 *No archive coverage* rows are archive limits. A transponder switched
  off, a gap in volunteer receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from here.
  Nothing on this page should be read as suppression.
* **The three refuted rows and the 38 the audit called inaccurate are published as prominently as
  the twelve corroborations, deliberately.** This slice is the largest single block of the register
  and it is also its weakest block.
* **On the count.** The overview prints 61 rows across 53 distinct dates for this cell and the JSON
  behind this page returns the same 61 and the same 53, so there is nothing to reconcile here. Note
  separately that the tracking-site audit quoted elsewhere on the overview worked from a **78-row**
  version of the register; this register holds **85** rows, of which these 61 are a part. Where an
  audit percentage and a register count seem to disagree, that is why.
* Every claim above is attributed. The sheet is the compilers'; the audit verdicts are the
  auditor's; the ADS-B verdicts are this investigation's, measured off raw traces. No row here
  asserts that any living person committed any crime.
