# The nine rows claiming Charlie Kirk without Erika

**Where this came from.** [The Following overview](/Planes/following/overview) sorts all 85 register rows by *whose* presence the compilation asserts, and the row of that table reading **"Charlie Kirk alone — 9 — 11% — Erika is not claimed present on these"** links here. This page is those nine rows in full: the aircraft, the airport, the claimed date, the logged ground stay where one exists, the sourced Kirk appearance the row is paired against, and both independent verdicts. The count on this page and the count on the overview agree at nine.

## What this slice is

A row lands here when the compilation's own attribution says Charlie Kirk and **not** Erika Kirk — in `overlaps.csv` terms, the `charlie_present` column is anything other than "not claimed" while `erika_present` reads "not claimed". That is the sheet's attribution, not a finding by this site. Seven of the nine record Charlie as **claimed** present; the remaining two, `OWENS-024` and `OWENS-026`, record him as **unknown** and are grouped here because Erika is explicitly not claimed on them either. Rows naming both Kirks, rows naming Erika alone, and rows naming a Turning Point event with neither Kirk are excluded and live on their own pages.

## What the specifics show

**The corroborated rows and the Nebraska rows are two different lists, and they do not overlap.** All five rows where recovered position data puts the aircraft at the claimed airport are in Kansas or Utah — `OWENS-023`, `OWENS-024` and `SITE-003` at KICT, `OWENS-026` and `OWENS-040` at KPVU. Not one of the four Nebraska rows produces a corroboration, and they fail in two different ways that must not be merged. `EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-001` and `EXTRA-007` name no tail number, so there is no airframe to look up and **the question was never asked**. `EXTRA-005` **was** asked and came back **Not heard** — an archive that served other aircraft on 25 August 2023 holds no trace for SU-BTT, which is an archive limit and not a deletion, and does not show the aircraft was elsewhere. An unasked question and an archive that answered with silence are different things, and only the second is a coverage fact.

**Only two of the nine put the aircraft on the ground.** `OWENS-026` (23 April 2024, Provo, 0.00 km, on-ground flag set, a logged five-day stay) and `OWENS-040` (4 September 2025, Provo, 0.16 km, on-ground flag set, a logged six-day stay) are the strongest aircraft-side rows in this slice. The three Wichita corroborations are airborne positions 1.43 to 1.83 km from the field — an arrival, a departure or a low pass, as the note on each row says.

**The tightest same-day pairing in this list argues against the claim it is filed under.** Two rows, `OWENS-024` and `SITE-003`, both record 3 April 2024, the day Charlie Kirk spoke at the University of Kansas in Lawrence. The register's own counterargument on `SITE-003` says the aircraft **departed** Wichita at 08:43 that morning, that Wichita is about 160 miles from the venue, and that the nearest fields to Lawrence are KLWC and KMCI. The two rows are also the reason nine rows resolve to eight dates: `SITE-003` was reconstructed from an overlap page that had no row in the file and restates the same claim.

**`OWENS-040` is the September window, and its gap is six days.** SU-BTT is confirmed on the Provo ramp from 4 September 2025, but the sourced Kirk itinerary in the register puts Charlie Kirk in Seoul on 5–6 September and Tokyo on 7 September — the aircraft was parked before he was back in the country. That is on the page because it weakens the pairing, not despite it.

**The audit reached only six of the nine.** Three are marked accurate, two partially accurate, one inaccurate, and three — all of them the undated-tail Nebraska amplifier claims — were never reached by the auditor at all. Six of the nine fall on a day this site can independently source a Charlie Kirk or Turning Point appearance; three cannot.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** The two on-ground confirmations show a Falcon 7X at Provo. They do not show who boarded it, who met it, or whether anyone did.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows** — here and across the whole register. That is definitionally true on this page, because these nine are the rows where the compilation does not claim her; but it is also true everywhere else. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no itinerary was ever published, so no row anywhere can be checked on her side.
* **Charlie Kirk's presence is the sheet's claim, not a verified fact, on every row here.** Seven rows record him as claimed present and two as unknown. Where this site can independently source the appearance, the sourcing is shown in Table B and the link goes to the event page; where it cannot, Table B says so.
* **An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence.** The one **Not heard** row means a volunteer receiver network holds no trace for that airframe that day. Parked with the transponder off, outside receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from the outside.
* **Three rows name no aircraft at all** and are untestable as published — not refuted, not corroborated, simply not checkable in the form they were circulated.
* **No count on this page differs from the overview.** The overview's attribution table prints 9 for "Charlie Kirk alone" and this register holds 9. Note separately that the audit figures elsewhere on the overview are stated against the **78 rows the auditor worked from**, not the 85 this register now holds; the three rows on this page that read "Not reached by the audit" (`EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-001`, `EXTRA-007`) sit outside the Owens sheet he was checking, so no verdict of his exists for them. `EXTRA-007`, sourced from posts of August 2026, is also one of the seven rows added to the register after the auditor's pass.
