# Utah — every overlap, every landing, every event

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the geographic
table gives Utah one line — one field, Provo (KPVU), and one number. This page is that cell opened
up: **15 claimed overlap rows**, plus the **8** separately logged ground presences in the state and
the **3** sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearances held in Utah, so the aircraft side and the people side
can be read against each other on one page. The overview's own summary of this state is that four of
the five clustered states resolve to a maintenance or clearance function and **Utah is the one that
does not resolve cleanly** — which is why it gets the most careful reading of the five.

## What this slice is

Every row of the overlap register whose claimed location resolves to the state of Utah, on any date,
for any aircraft, regardless of what either verdict says about it. Nothing is filtered out for being
refuted, unheard, or audited inaccurate — this is the whole state, warts included. Filter
`overlaps.csv` on `state = UT` and you get these fifteen, with one boundary case: `OWENS-041` is
logged `UT/DE` because it is a single claimed movement **Provo then Wilmington** on 10 September
2025, and it is counted here on its Utah half.

Utah is unusual among the five states in having exactly **one** field. All 15 rows name **KPVU**,
Provo Municipal — a regional/municipal airport with a **Duncan Aviation** full-service facility on
the field and no routine international customs function. Table D then adds the state's rows from
`flights.csv` (logged aircraft ground presences that are not overlap claims) and Table E adds the
state's rows from `tpusa_events.csv` (sourced appearances).

## What the specifics show

**Utah is where the aircraft side of this register is strongest, by a wide margin.** Ten of the
fifteen rows are corroborated by recovered position data and **nine of those ten carry the on-ground
flag** — the aircraft transmitting its own position from the surface, not passing overhead. The
whole register holds only ten ground-flag rows; **nine of them are on this page**, and the tenth is
`OWENS-013` at Wilmington on 20 April 2023. The distances are not marginal either: `OWENS-026` and
`SITE-004` both measure **0.00 km** from KPVU on 23 April 2024, `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` both
**0.03 km** on 23 May 2025, `OWENS-025` **0.05 km** on 19 April 2024, and `OWENS-040` **0.16 km** on
4 September 2025. Not one Utah row is refuted, and not one falls in a window no free archive reaches.

**The 10 September 2025 rows are the tightest aircraft-to-event pairing in the whole register, and
they are also where the people column falls apart.** Three rows share that date — `EXTRA-006` at
1.29 km, `OWENS-041` at 1.23 km and `SITE-006` at 1.23 km, all three with the ground flag set — and
the same-day sourced appearance is Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University in **Orem, five miles from
KPVU**. That is as close as anything on this site gets. But read the Erika column on those same three
rows: the sheet places her in "Nebraska / Delaware" while the register records that **Erika Kirk has
reportedly said she stayed home in Arizona that day**. The register carries the disagreement on the
row rather than choosing a side. On the strongest aircraft date in the file, the sheet's own claim
about her is contradicted by her own reported account.

**The other same-day pairing is 23 April 2024**, `OWENS-026` and `SITE-004`, both ground-confirmed at
Provo, against Charlie Kirk sourced at a "Prove Me Wrong" event at the University of Utah in Salt
Lake City. The register flags its own weakness there in the flight log: that Kirk-side event is
recorded as **uncited**, with no ticket page or venue confirmation behind it. The pairing is
published with that caveat attached, not without it.

**The five Not-heard rows are where this state's list is thin, and two of them are the same claim
twice.** `OWENS-016` (12 June 2023) is the first claimed Utah row and the audit places the aircraft
in Egypt that day, noting that the sheet's Provo cluster starts a year early — this site's own Utah
record begins April 2024. `OWENS-030` (8 December 2024) and `OWENS-064` (19 April 2025) return
nothing. `OWENS-037` and `OWENS-066` are a duplicated pair on 7 July 2025, both claiming a
Provo-to-Goose-Bay-to-Paris-to-Cairo departure; the register's flight log records SU-BND as sitting
on the Provo ramp continuously from 23 May to 13 September 2025, so the sheet has the aircraft flying
home on a day it did not move, and it has that error entered twice.

**Table D is the part of this page that was never a claim to begin with, and it is the heart of the
Utah question.** Eight logged ground presences, five different Egyptian-registered tails, all at
Provo: **SU-BND for 84 days** (19 April to 12 July 2024) and then **113 continuous days** (23 May to
13 September 2025, the longest published stay by any aircraft in this investigation, covering
10 September); **SU-BTT** for 5 days in April 2024 and 6 days from 4 to 10 September 2025, departing
the morning of the assassination; **SU-BTU** for 6 days in April 2025; **SU-BTV** for 6 days from 27
May to 2 June 2025; and **SU-BGM** on the ramp until 16 April 2025 with its arrival date not
captured. One of the eight, the SU-BTT stay dated from 1 April 2024, is marked
**disputed and unverified** — a relayed month-only claim with no primary screenshot — and is flagged
rather than built on.

**And the counter-reading is on the same page, in the same table.** Duncan Aviation is on the Provo
field, the register records its Egyptian Air Force account, and a **four-month stay is a
maintenance-visit length, not a surveillance length**. The audit's own note on the 113-day stay reads
"ACCURATE POSS BROKEN JET GROUNDED IN PROVO", with only two local Provo-to-Provo flights flown in the
entire window — the classic post-maintenance test-flight profile. The SU-BGM entry opens the same way,
with a 55-minute Provo-to-Provo local. The register also records that during SU-BTU's six days in
April 2025 Charlie Kirk was touring Illinois, Indiana and Michigan, and that **no pairing is claimed
by anyone for those dates**. Three of the five sourced Utah aircraft stays have no Kirk documented in
the state at all.

**The two verdict passes disagree on this state more than on most.** The audit called 8 rows accurate
and 7 inaccurate; the position data corroborated 10 and refuted none. `OWENS-025` (19 April 2024,
0.05 km with the ground flag set) and `OWENS-041` (10 September 2025, 1.23 km with the ground flag
set) were both called **inaccurate** by the audit and are **corroborated** here. Both results stay on
the row. And the final Utah row, `OWENS-042` on 13 September 2025 at 0.62 km, closes the September
window for the blue plane — corroborated in the air, without a ground position.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** The nine ground-flag rows on this
  page are the strongest evidence class this investigation has, and they establish that an
  Egyptian-registered jet was physically on the Provo ramp. They establish nothing whatsoever about
  who was inside it, on 10 September 2025 or on any other date here.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any of these 15 rows**, and it is established on
  [zero rows anywhere in the register](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-established-0). Her flight logs
  are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary was ever published,
  so the *Erika Location* column is the compilers' claim rather than a source for it. On the three
  10 September rows that claim reads "Nebraska / Delaware" and is contradicted by her own reported
  statement that she was in Arizona — printed here because it weakens the register's own strongest
  date.
* **Charlie Kirk's presence in Utah on 10 September 2025 is sourced independently of this
  register**, from the Utah Valley University event. What no page can supply is a link between a man
  standing in a courtyard in Orem and a jet parked five miles away. Proximity is not connection.
* **The five Not-heard rows are archive limits, not deletions.** *Not heard* means the volunteer
  archives covered those dates and served other airframes on them while holding no trace for this
  one. Parked with the transponder off, outside receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look
  identical from outside. None of the five is evidence of absence and none is evidence of
  suppression.
* **The maintenance explanation is not disposed of by anything on this page.** Duncan Aviation
  Provo is on the field; the stays are of maintenance length; the flights inside them are local
  out-and-backs. That is the ordinary reading of five foreign-registered aircraft rotating through
  one regional field, and it is published here as prominently as the pairing.
* **One Table D row is disputed and one Table E pairing is uncited.** The 1 April 2024 SU-BTT entry
  is a relayed month-only claim with no primary source, and the 23 April 2024 University of Utah
  event carries no ticket page or venue confirmation. Both are flagged on their own rows and neither
  should be leaned on.
* **No crime is asserted against anyone.** The sheet is the compilers'; the audit verdicts are the
  auditor's; the ADS-B verdicts are this investigation's, measured off raw traces; the reported
  Arizona statement is attributed to Erika Kirk's own account as the register records it.
* **On the count.** The overview prints Utah as **15 rows** and the data behind this page returns the
  same 15, so there is nothing to reconcile for this cell. Note separately that the tracking-site
  audit worked from a **78-row** version of the register while this register holds **85**; where an
  audit percentage and a register count appear to disagree, that is usually why.
