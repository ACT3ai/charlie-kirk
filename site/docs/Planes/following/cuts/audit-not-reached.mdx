# Rows the audit never reached

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table headed "Audit verdict on the aircraft's position" — the line-by-line check of the compilers' sheet against paid flight-history archives — ends with a row reading **"Not reached by the audit"**. The overview prints **4** in that cell. **This register holds 5**, and this page is those five rows. The difference is explained under "What this list cannot tell you" below; nothing has been removed from either count.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the register carries **no audit verdict of any kind** — the auditor never assessed it, in either direction. These are rows the audit could not have reached: they were reconstructed by this site from overlap pages, from a Candace Owens broadcast, from a reply on X, and from later amplifier posts, and were not on the published sheet of 78 rows the auditor worked from. "Not reached" is therefore **an absence of a verdict, not a verdict** — it is neither a pass nor a fail, and it must not be read as either.

## What the specifics show

**Four of the five name no aircraft at all.** `EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-001`, `EXTRA-007` and `EXTRA-004` carry no tail number, no type and no ICAO hex, so there is no airframe to look up — untestable as published, by the auditor or by anyone else. That is why the ADS-B re-test also returns **No aircraft named** on four of these five rows. A claim that does not name the plane cannot be checked against flight data in either direction, and the register says so on the row rather than quietly dropping it.

**Only one row names an aircraft, and its own counterargument is the strongest thing on this page.** `EXTRA-003`, 23 April 2023 at Topeka, names [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). Its ADS-B verdict is **Not heard** — an archive limit, meaning an archive that served other airframes that day holds no trace of this one. But the register does not need the archives to weaken this row: it records that SU-BTT had **departed Wichita on 20 April, three days earlier and about 135 miles away**, which puts the pairing outside the register's own one-day rule. This row is the one Kansas entry on the page.

**The geography is narrow.** Four of the five rows are in Nebraska — `EXTRA-002` and `EXTRA-004` at Omaha (KOMA), `EXTRA-001` and `EXTRA-007` at Lincoln (KLNK) — and the fifth is Topeka, Kansas. There are **zero ground confirmations** on this page.

**Four of the five do fall on a day with a sourced appearance, and one of those four does not hold together.** `EXTRA-003` sits on a sourced TPUSA Faith event in Topeka, and the register notes that Charlie Kirk is not named as its speaker. `EXTRA-002` sits on a sourced Charlie Kirk rally in Omaha the same day, at 19:00 CT, four miles from the claimed field. `EXTRA-007` sits on a sourced Charlie Kirk town hall with no published city. But `EXTRA-004` claims Omaha, Nebraska on 11 July 2025 while the sourced Charlie Kirk appearance that day is the Student Action Summit in **Tampa, Florida** — the same-day match is a date match only, and it points away from the claimed field, not toward it. `EXTRA-001` has no same-day sourced appearance at all; the nearest is Manhattan, Kansas, the day before.

**The sourcing is the thinnest in the register.** Two of the five rest on X posts from August 2026 crediting a broadcaster — `EXTRA-007` (posts of 5–6 August 2026) and `EXTRA-004` (a post of 19 August 2026 citing an X post of 5 August 2026 and a guest). `EXTRA-001` rests on a single reply posted by Candace Owens on 1 November 2025 stating that Charlie Kirk was in Lincoln, Nebraska on 14 September 2024. `EXTRA-002` and `EXTRA-003` come from a Candace Owens broadcast episode together with this site's own event catalog. None of these is a primary flight record, and each is attributed above so a reader can go and look.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **"Not reached" is not a finding.** It records that the auditor never assessed these rows. It is not a pass, not a fail, and nobody should read it as either. On four of the five, no method could reach them as published, because they name no aircraft.
* **On the count — the overview prints 4 and this register holds 5.** The overview's audit table is stated against the **78 rows the auditor worked from**, and it says so on that page; this register now holds 85 rows in total, of which 80 carry a date. The seven later additions were reconstructed by this site from overlap pages and later posts, and the auditor never saw them. Counted straight out of the register, **five rows carry no audit verdict of any kind, and they are the five above** — the other reconstructed rows do carry one. That is why five appear here and four on the overview. **The overview figure is the auditor's; the five above are the register's.** No row has been removed from either.
* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard** — and on four of these five rows there is not even an aircraft to trace.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero of these rows**, as it is on zero rows anywhere in this register. She is claimed on one row, `EXTRA-004`, where the sheet never stated a location for her at all. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary has ever been published.
* **Charlie Kirk's presence is not established by anything on this page either.** Where a sourced appearance is shown it comes from event listings and press coverage in [the speaking-events index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), and it places a person at a venue — never on an aircraft. No Kirk-side aircraft is on record on any of these five rows.
* **The one Not heard verdict is an archive limit, not a deletion and not a refutation.** An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence.
* **Nobody here is accused of a crime.** Every claim above is attributed to the broadcaster, the poster, or the compilers who made it, and reproduced so it can be checked.
