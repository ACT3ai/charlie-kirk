# The Charlie/TPUSA rows the archives cannot decide

**This page opens the number 11 — the Charlie/TPUSA rows the archives cannot decide.** Eight were asked for and not heard; three name no aircraft to ask about. **Five of the 11 still land on a day this site can independently source a Kirk or TPUSA appearance**, which is what makes them open questions rather than dead ends.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the ledger table
that splits the Charlie Kirk and TPUSA side of the register into corroborated, refuted and undecided
carries a cell reading "Charlie Kirk + TPUSA — archives cannot answer". This page is that cell, and
it holds **11 rows**. The same ledger holds 24 rows in total: 13 that recovered position data
corroborates, 0 that it refutes, and these 11 that it can neither corroborate nor refute.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list if two things are true. First, the claim involves **Charlie Kirk or a Turning
Point USA event** — either Charlie's presence is claimed on the row, or both Kirks are claimed, or
the row is matched to a TPUSA appearance with neither Kirk named. Second, the ADS-B pass came back
**undecided**: either the free archives cover the date but hold no trace for the named airframe
(**Not heard**, 8 rows), or the claim names no tail number at all, so there is nothing to look up
(**No aircraft named**, 3 rows). Rows that came back corroborated or refuted are in the sibling
cells, not here. Reproduce the list from `overlaps.csv` by keeping every Charlie-or-TPUSA row whose
ADS-B verdict is neither "at the claimed airport" nor "elsewhere".

## What the specifics show

This is the part of the Charlie and TPUSA ledger where the evidence runs out, and it runs out in two
different ways. Eight rows are **Not heard**: the free volunteer archives cover the date and served
other airframes, but hold no trace for the tail on the row. Three rows — EXTRA-002 (9 April 2024,
Omaha), EXTRA-001 (14 September 2024, Lincoln) and EXTRA-007 (29 April 2025, Lincoln) — name **no
aircraft at all**, so there is nothing to look up. Table C calls those three untestable as published,
which is the honest description.

The list has a sharp internal paradox, and it is the most useful thing on this page. Five of the 11
rows land on a same-day, independently sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance — SITE-001 at the TPUSA
Faith tour launch in Jefferson City, EXTRA-003 at TPUSA Faith's Kingdom to the Capitol in Topeka,
EXTRA-005 at Charlie's Lincoln appearance, EXTRA-002 at the Win Every Vote rally in Omaha, and
EXTRA-007 at a TPUSA student town hall. Those are the best-sourced *events* in the whole ledger. They
are also, twice over, the rows with the worst aircraft sourcing: EXTRA-002 and EXTRA-007 name no
tail. (The third no-tail row, EXTRA-001, has no same-day sourced appearance at all.) A
well-documented Kirk appearance does not make an aircraft claim testable.

EXTRA-005 goes further and cuts against the register. Charlie Kirk's Lincoln appearance on 25 August
2023 is sourced, but the same audit places SU-BTT in Egypt that day, and Table A records that the jet
was elsewhere per the audit. The row is kept in the register so the negative is not lost. It is
recorded here as **Not heard** on the ADS-B pass and **Inaccurate** on the tracking-site audit.

Kansas and Nebraska carry this list almost entirely — five rows each, one Missouri row. Four of the
Kansas rows are Wichita (KICT), and three of those four are the March 2025 pair-plus-one: OWENS-033
and OWENS-063 are the same 15 March 2025 claim carried under two sheet indices, which is why 11 rows
resolve to 10 distinct dates, and OWENS-034 repeats the pattern a week later on 22 March 2025. All
three are **Not heard** and all three were marked **Inaccurate** by the audit.

Nothing in this list is confirmed on the ground: **ground-position confirmations are zero**. And the
tracking-site audit did not reach four of these 11 rows at all, so on those four there is neither an
audit verdict nor an ADS-B verdict to lean on. The newest row, EXTRA-007, traces to amplifier
accounts posting on 5–6 August 2026 and crediting a broadcaster, rather than to any flight record;
the next newest, EXTRA-001, traces to a single X reply of 1 November 2025.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **Nothing here is a deletion.** Every one of the eight **Not heard** rows means a volunteer ADS-B
  network's receivers did not hear that airframe on that date. A parked aircraft with the
  transponder off, an airframe outside receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical
  from the outside. **No archive gap on this page is being read as suppression.**
* **The three "no aircraft named" rows are not weak evidence — they are absent evidence.** With no
  tail number there is no airframe to query, so there is nothing to be right or wrong about.
* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** No row in this list even
  reaches that point, because no row here has a trace.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row.** Three of these rows claim both Kirks, and
  on all three the Erika side is a location cell on the compiler's spreadsheet reading "Kansas /
  Delaware". Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated
  itinerary was ever published.
* **A sourced event proves the event, not the pairing.** The five same-day appearances above are
  sourced to this site's own speaking-events catalog. They say where a Kirk or a TPUSA event was.
  They say nothing about any aircraft.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from 78 rows; this register holds 85. Four rows here were never reached by that audit
  at all.
