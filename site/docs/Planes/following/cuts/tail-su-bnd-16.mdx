# Every row naming SU-BND, the blue plane

**This page opens the number 16 — every row naming SU-BND, the blue plane.** It is the Utah aircraft: **10 of its 16 rows are Provo**, it sat there 113 days from 23 May to 13 September 2025, and it is the jet that did not take off on 10 September. Six of the 16 are corroborated by position data and four are ground confirmations.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that
splits the overlap register **by aircraft** carries a cell reading "Overlaps where the aircraft was
SU-BND, the blue plane". This page is that cell. It holds **16 rows** — every row in the register
whose named airframe is SU-BND, the Gulfstream carrying ICAO hex `01003E`. The register's other
large aircraft cell, SU-BTT, holds 57 rows; three further rows name both tails at once and sit in
their own cell rather than in either of these two.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list if the compiler's sheet, or the site's own reconstruction of it, names
**SU-BND alone** as the aircraft for that claimed overlap. Rows naming only SU-BTT are excluded, and
so are the three rows that name both tails on the same line — those carry the tail value
"SU-BTT; SU-BND" and are held in [their own cell](/Planes/following/cuts/tail-both-3), which is why
the per-aircraft cells add to the register's row count exactly rather than double-counting. Nothing
about a Kirk, a date or an airport enters the filter: reproduce the list by taking `overlaps.csv` and
keeping every row whose tail field is SU-BND on its own.

## What the specifics show

SU-BND is a Utah aircraft in this register, not a touring one. Ten of the 16 rows are Provo
Municipal (KPVU) and ten of the 16 are in Utah; the remaining six split five Missouri and one
Delaware, and every one of those six falls in 2023 or 2024. Nothing in this list is in Nebraska or
Kansas at all — those states belong to the other tail, SU-BTT.

The corroborations are real and they are concentrated in two long parks. Six rows carry **At the
claimed airport — corroborated**, and four of those six are ground-position confirmations: OWENS-025
at 0.05 km from KPVU on 19 April 2024, OWENS-035 and OWENS-065 both at 0.03 km on 23 May 2025, and
EXTRA-006 at 1.29 km on 10 September 2025. Those four sit inside two logged stays — 19 April to 12
July 2024, 84 days, and 23 May to 13 September 2025, 113 days. The other two corroborated rows,
OWENS-014 at 2.07 km from KCPS and OWENS-042 at 0.62 km from KPVU, are airborne positions, which
Table C describes as an arrival, a departure or a low pass rather than a landing.

The long parks are also where this list is weakest as an argument about following. If an airframe
sits on one ramp for 113 days, every date inside that window will produce a hit, and the sheet
records several of them as separate events. OWENS-037 and OWENS-066, both dated 7 July 2025, claim
the aircraft was leaving Provo; the audit's reading is that it never left, and Table A records
"nowhere — still in Provo". The same doubling shows up on 23 May 2025, where OWENS-035 and OWENS-065
are the same landing carried under two sheet indices — which is why 16 rows resolve to only 14
distinct dates.

Two rows are affirmatively refuted by recovered position data. On 9 May 2023 (OWENS-015) the
airframe's closest recovered position was 1,062.56 km from the claimed field, and on 12 May 2023
(OWENS-050) it was 7,034.48 km away. The tracking-site audit had already marked ten of these 16 rows
**Inaccurate**, against six **Accurate**. Eight further rows are **Not heard** — the volunteer
archives cover the date but hold no trace for this airframe, which is a limit of what those archives
hold and not a deletion.

The one row that lands on a same-day, independently sourced Kirk appearance is EXTRA-006, 10
September 2025: SU-BND on the Provo ramp, 5 miles from Utah Valley University, on the day Charlie
Kirk was shot. That is the single strongest row in this list, and the position data corroborates the
airframe being there and nothing else. On the other 15 rows there is no sourced Kirk or TPUSA
appearance the same day at all — the nearest sourced appearances are two and three days away and in
other states.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The four ground
  confirmations above put SU-BND on a ramp to within tens of metres. They place nobody in the cabin,
  including on 10 September 2025.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row here.** Thirteen of these 16 rows rest on
  her alone, and every one of those thirteen sources her location to a cell on the compiler's
  spreadsheet. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated
  itinerary was ever published.
* **The eight Not heard rows are an archive limit, not a removal.** Volunteer ADS-B networks hear
  what their receivers hear. A parked aircraft with the transponder off, an airframe outside
  receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from the outside.
* **A long park inflates the count.** Ten Provo rows are not ten arrivals. Read the ground-stay
  columns before treating a row as a separate visit.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from 78 rows; this register holds 85. Counts sourced from the audit and counts
  sourced from the register will not match.
