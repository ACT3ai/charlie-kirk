# The four rows with no tail number at all

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), in the table headed *Which Egyptian plane, and how many each*, the row **"No tail number recorded at all"** reads **4** rows — 3 Charlie-involved, 1 Erika-involved, 0 TPUSA-only. This page is those four rows. The count here, **4**, and the Charlie/Erika split, **3 and 1**, both agree exactly with the figures printed on the overview.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list if the register holds **no aircraft identifier for it whatsoever** — no tail, no ICAO hex, not even an ambiguous pair. Reproduce it from `overlaps.csv` by taking every row whose tail field is empty or literally `UNKNOWN`: four rows, indexed `EXTRA-001`, `EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-004` and `EXTRA-007`. They are distinct from the [five rows logged as "SU-BTT or SU-BND"](/Planes/following/cuts/tail-either-btt-or-bnd-5), where the source named two candidate aircraft and could not choose between them; here the source named none.

These are the same four rows the ADS-B verdict table reports as [no aircraft named](/Planes/following/cuts/adsb-no-tail-4). One set of four rows, failing two tests for the same reason: with no airframe to look up, there is nothing to query an archive for.

## What the specifics show

**All four are Nebraska, and two of them do not claim a jet at all.** Every row sits at Lincoln or Omaha. `EXTRA-002` (9 April 2024, KOMA) and `EXTRA-001` (14 September 2024, KLNK) are recorded in the register as Kirk-location markers — the airport column on each says so in plain words, "no jet" and "no jet claimed that day". They mark where a Kirk is reported to have been, not where an aircraft was seen. Calling them overlaps at all is generous to the claim.

**The same-day count of 3 is weaker than it looks, and unpacking it is the point of this page.** `EXTRA-002` is the one row where the sourced appearance and the claimed field are in the same city: Charlie Kirk at the Lord of Hosts Church in Omaha on 9 April 2024, four miles from Eppley Airfield. `EXTRA-007` (29 April 2025, KLNK) matches to a TPUSA student town hall whose venue and city the register records as UNKNOWN, so there is no location to compare. `EXTRA-004` (11 July 2025, KOMA) matches to Charlie Kirk at the TPUSA Student Action Summit in **Tampa, Florida** — a same-day appearance most of a continent away from the Nebraska field the row claims. That row is the one of the four attributed to Erika Kirk, and its same-day event actively cuts against it.

**The remaining row has no same-day match.** `EXTRA-001` (14 September 2024, Lincoln) is nearest to a sourced Charlie Kirk appearance at Kansas State University in Manhattan, Kansas the day before — one day early and one state over.

**Sourcing thins out across the four.** `EXTRA-002` rests on an Owens episode and a contemporaneous AP report of the Omaha event. `EXTRA-001` rests on a single reply post by [@RealCandaceO](https://x.com/RealCandaceO/status/1984666106569900231) dated 1 November 2025. `EXTRA-007` and `EXTRA-004` rest on amplifier accounts posting in August 2026 and crediting Stew Peters — that is, on secondary posts about a claim, not on the claim's own documentation.

**None of the four was reached by the tracking-site audit.** The audit worked from a 78-row version of the sheet; these four were added to the register later, from amplifier claims and overlap pages that had no row in the file. Their audit column reads "not reached", which is a statement about the audit's scope, not about the rows.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** There are no traces here at all — with no tail number there is no airframe to query — but the rule is why even the corroborated rows elsewhere in this register cannot show who was flying.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row here.** One of the four, `EXTRA-004`, names her, and the register records her location on it as UNKNOWN. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary has ever been published. Site-wide, the number of rows on which her presence is actually established is [zero](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-established-0).
* **Charlie Kirk's sourced presence on three of these dates says nothing about any aircraft.** That he spoke in Omaha on 9 April 2024 is well sourced. It is not evidence that any jet was at Eppley Airfield that day, because no row here names a jet.
* **This is not an archive gap and must not be read as one.** These four are not "not heard" and not "no archive coverage" — those are limits of what an archive holds. These rows are untestable as published, because the claim itself withholds the aircraft. Nothing has been deleted, hidden, or suppressed here.
* **An untestable claim is not a false claim.** Four rows with no airframe are unverifiable, not disproved. They carry no evidentiary weight in either direction.
* **Every claim on this page is somebody else's.** The rows record what Candace Owens, named X accounts, and accounts crediting Stew Peters have stated. This site asserts no crime by any person named here and no purpose behind any flight.
* **On the counts, this page and the overview agree** — 4 rows, 3 Charlie-involved, 1 Erika-involved. Where wider figures differ, note that the audit ran against 78 rows and this register holds 85; all four of these rows are among the later additions the audit never saw.
