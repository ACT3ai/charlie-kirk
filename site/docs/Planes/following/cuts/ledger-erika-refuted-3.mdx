# The three Erika rows position data refutes

**This page opens the number 3 — every refutation in the entire register, and all three fall on Erika Kirk's side.** 9 May 2023, 12 May 2023 and 18 June 2025: the aircraft was tracked that day and was somewhere else. **The matching Charlie/TPUSA column is zero.**

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the ledger table
splits the register by whose presence the row claims and then by what recovered position data says
about it. The cell reading "Erika Kirk — refuted" holds **3 rows**. This page is those three rows,
in full.

## What this slice is

A row lands here when two things are both true. First, the compiler's sheet claims **Erika Kirk
alone** — Charlie Kirk is not claimed on the row, and no TPUSA event is attached to it. All three
rows carry the attendee class "Erika Kirk alone". Second, recovered ADS-B position data for the
named airframe on the claimed date exists **and puts it somewhere else**: the verdict `ELSEWHERE`,
which requires that the aircraft was actually heard that day, so the closest recovered position can
be measured against the claimed airport. That second condition is what separates this list from the
much larger undecided one — a row where the archives simply hold nothing is not refuted, it is
unheard. Filter `overlaps.csv` for `attendee_class = ERIKA_ONLY` and `adsb_verdict = ELSEWHERE` and
you reproduce these three.

## What the specifics show

The three refutations are not the same size. `OWENS-050` (12 May 2023, KSTL) and `OWENS-036`
(18 June 2025, KOMA) are refuted by 7,034 km and 10,140 km respectively — continental distances,
consistent with the Kanekoa audit's own conclusion that the airframe was in Egypt on both dates.
`OWENS-015` (9 May 2023, CYYR) is refuted by 1,063 km, which is a different kind of miss: an
aircraft on a transatlantic leg is 1,063 km from Goose Bay for part of the day almost by definition.
Two of these three rows are wrong about the continent; one is wrong about the waypoint on a routing
the sheet otherwise describes correctly.

`OWENS-015` is also the only row in the slice where the two passes disagree with each other. The
tracking-site audit called it **Accurate** overall; the recovered position data reads `ELSEWHERE`.
Both readings are published here rather than reconciled. The register does not silently pick a
winner when its two independent passes conflict.

The other pattern is that all three dates are Erika-only rows with nothing on the Kirk side. Table B
records the same result on every line: no sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance within three days,
anywhere, and no Kirk-side aircraft on record. Even before the position data is consulted, these
three rows have no confirmed person at either end. The `OWENS-036` row makes the point most
sharply — the register notes that the only well-dated joint Charlie-and-Erika appearance in the
whole window before 10 September 2025 is Grapevine, Texas, 13–15 June 2025, and that no aircraft in
this investigation was in Texas for it.

This slice weakens the compiler's claim rather than supporting it, and it is published on the same
footing as the corroborated slices for exactly that reason. Note also how small it is: 3 rows.
Position data refutes far fewer rows than it corroborates or fails to reach, so the honest headline
is that most of the register is still undecided, not that most of it is disproven.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** These three refutations
  work in the negative direction only: they show the airframe was elsewhere. Even a corroborated
  position would not have placed any passenger in the cabin.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row** — not on these three and not anywhere in
  the register, where the count of rows with established Erika presence is
  [zero](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-established-0). Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary was ever published, so
  the "Erika location" column here is the compiler's claim rather than a source.
* **"Refuted" refutes the claimed location, not the compiler's honesty.** A wrong cell in a
  volunteer spreadsheet is an error. Nothing on this page asserts that anyone committed a crime or
  falsified anything, and no such assertion should be read into it.
* **No archive gap appears in this slice, and none is being read as a deletion.** Every row here was
  *heard* — that is the precondition for `ELSEWHERE`. Where sibling pages carry `NOT_HEARD` or
  `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE`, those are limits of what free volunteer archives hold, never suppression
  and never evidence of absence.
* **Ground confirmations here are 0.** The 9 May 2023 Goose Bay row has a logged ground stay of 0
  days with no times captured; that is a logged transit, not a recovered on-ground position.
* **Where this count differs from the overview, both are printed.** The tracking-site audit worked
  from 78 rows; this register holds 85. Any audit-derived figure and any register-derived figure are
  counting different populations.
