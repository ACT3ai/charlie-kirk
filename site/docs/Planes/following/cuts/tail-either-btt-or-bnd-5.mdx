# The five rows logged as “SU-BTT or SU-BND”

**This page opens the number 5 — rows whose aircraft cell reads “SU-BTT or SU-BND” because the source could not tell which.** They also carry no date, no city and no airport. **Two jets, neither confirmed, on five rows that are still inside the count being broadcast.**

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that
splits the register by which aircraft was named carries a line reading **“SU-BTT or SU-BND” — the
source could not tell which**. That cell is the count **5**. These are those five rows.

## What this slice is

A row lands here when the register records the aircraft as one of the two Egyptian-registered jets
without saying which. Both tails are carried on the row and both ICAO hex codes are stored —
`0101D3` for [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and `01003E` for [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) —
because the source distinguished neither. To reproduce the list from `overlaps.csv`, take every row
whose following-tail field names both tails as alternatives rather than as a pair observed together.
That last distinction matters: three other rows in the register name **both** tails because both
aircraft were logged on the same day at the same field, and those three are a different slice
entirely. The five rows here name both because the compiler could not narrow it to one.

## What the specifics show

These five rows are identical to one another in every column. All five — `UNPUB-001` through
`UNPUB-005` — carry the same single source: Candace Owens, in the broadcast the register cites as
*Operation Mocking-Plane* of 17 November 2025, saying "68 overlaps. I have since been able to add
now 73 times that this overlapped." The register holds these five as placeholders for the
difference between the two figures she gave. Nothing beyond the count was ever itemised, which is
why every one of them has an empty date column, an empty airport column, an empty state column, and
an aircraft entry naming both jets because the source distinguished neither.

That makes this slice a statement about the sourcing rather than about any aircraft movement. All
five are attributed to Erika Kirk alone and none to Charlie Kirk; none has a sourced Turning Point
or Kirk appearance within three days, which is automatic, because with no date there is nothing to
compare an appearance against. The tracking-site audit records all five as **Never published**, and
the position-data pass records all five as **No date named** — the only two verdicts either pass
can return on a row with no date. They are counted in the register's 85 because the source asserted
them; they contribute nothing testable to it.

## What this list cannot tell you

* These five rows are **untestable as published**. With no date and no airport, no archive can be
  queried at all — this is not an archive gap and not a removal, it is a claim that was never
  itemised in the first place.
* Nothing here shows that either aircraft moved, or that either was anywhere in particular. The
  rows record an aggregate assertion, not an observation.
* A trace proves an aircraft was at a place on a date. It never proves who was aboard. On these
  five rows there is no trace and no date, so there is nothing to place anyone against.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows of the register**, and all five rows in this
  slice are logged as Erika Kirk alone. Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no itinerary was ever published, so the
  Erika column here records what the compiler asserted and nothing that has been shown.
* The aircraft columns must not be read as evidence about either airframe. Both tails and both hex
  codes appear on every row precisely because the source could not say which, so nothing here can
  be added to the case for or against [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) or
  [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) individually.
* Nothing here establishes that any person committed any offence. The claim is attributed above to
  the broadcast that made it.
* Counts on this page come from the 85-row register. The tracking-site audit worked from 78 rows
  and never covered these five, which is exactly what its **Never published** verdict records; a
  figure printed on the overview page from the audit side will therefore not include them.
