# The rows where Erika Kirk's presence is actually established

**This page opens a count of zero, and the zero is the finding.** Of 85 claimed overlaps — **70 of which involve Erika Kirk, 61 resting on her alone** — there is not one row on which her presence is established by any record. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), and no dated itinerary for her has ever been published by anyone on either side of this dispute.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that
splits the overlap register by *who the row claims was being followed* carries a cell reading
"Overlaps where Erika Kirk's presence is established". This page is that cell. The count in it is
**0**. Every other cell in that table drills down into a list of rows; this one drills down into an
empty list, and the empty list is the point.

## What this slice is

A row qualifies for this list only if some source independently places **Erika Kirk herself** at the
claimed city on the claimed date — a published itinerary, a dated photograph, a venue record, a
manifest, a named appearance. Being written in the "Erika location" cell of the compiler's
spreadsheet does **not** qualify: that cell is the claim, not evidence for the claim. An aircraft
being at the airport does not qualify either, because a trace locates an airframe and never a
passenger. Filter `overlaps.csv` for rows carrying independent, dated, sourced confirmation of Erika
Kirk's own physical presence and the filter returns nothing. That is how this page ends up with zero
rows.

## What the specifics show

There are no specifics, and that is the finding. The three tables above are the real generated
output for this slice — they carry their headers and not a single data row, because the join that
produced every other cuts page returned an empty set here.

This matters because of where the register's weight sits. In the same index of slices, the
Erika-side lists are the large ones: the register counts 70 rows that involve Erika Kirk at all and
61 that rest on her alone, against 18 that involve Charlie Kirk in any way. So the majority of the
"following" claim is carried by a person whose presence is confirmed on **zero** of its rows. The
supporting material for those 70 rows is a location cell on a circulated spreadsheet — sometimes a
state, sometimes two states, sometimes hedged — and no dated itinerary behind it.

The nearest thing the register has to a sourced Erika appearance is not in this register at all. On
the refuted-Erika slice, the row for 18 June 2025 records that the only well-dated joint Charlie-and-
Erika appearance in the whole pre-September-10 window is Grapevine, Texas, 13–15 June 2025 — and no
aircraft in this investigation was in Texas for it. That is the strongest available Erika datum, and
it points away from the claim rather than toward it.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** Every corroborated row
  elsewhere in this register corroborates a tail number and a position, nothing more. No ADS-B
  record in existence can put a named passenger in a cabin.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row.** Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary was ever published.
  Every claim about where she was on these dates is attributed to the compiler's spreadsheet.
* **Zero here is not proof she was elsewhere.** An empty list means nobody has produced sourced
  evidence of her presence to this register. It does not establish absence, and it is not an
  accusation against anyone. It is a statement about the state of the sourcing.
* **This page contains no archive verdicts, so no archive gap is being read as anything.** Where
  `NOT_HEARD` and `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE` appear on sibling pages they are limits of what volunteer
  ADS-B archives hold — never deletions, never suppression.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from 78 rows; this register holds 85. Counts sourced from the audit and counts
  sourced from the register will not match, and neither is being quietly preferred.
