# Missouri — every overlap, every landing, every event

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the by-state table
gives Missouri one line and one number. This page is that cell opened up: **9 claimed overlap rows**,
plus the **1** separately logged landing in the state and the **4** sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearances
held in Missouri, so the aircraft side and the people side can be read against each other on one
page.

## What this slice is

Every row of the overlap register whose claimed location resolves to the state of Missouri, on any
date, for any aircraft, regardless of what either verdict says about it. Nothing is filtered out for
being refuted, unheard, or audited inaccurate — this is the whole state, warts included. Filter
`overlaps.csv` on `state = MO` and you get these nine. Table D then adds the state's rows from
`flights.csv` (logged aircraft presences that are not overlap claims) and Table E adds the state's
rows from `tpusa_events.csv` (sourced appearances). Note one boundary problem the state filter cannot
solve, flagged on the register's own SITE-001 row: three separate St. Louis metro fields are in play
and **KCPS is physically in Illinois**, so a state-level tally cannot cleanly separate them.

## What the specifics show

Missouri is a St. Louis story and nothing else. All nine overlap rows resolve to the St. Louis metro
— six to KSTL, one each to KSUS and KCPS, and SITE-001 to the metro without resolving which of the
three fields — while the only sourced Kirk appearances in the state outside St. Louis are Kansas
City on 18 October 2022 and Springfield on 19 October 2023, and no overlap row touches either city.

The strongest rows are three airborne corroborations: `OWENS-010` at 0.41 km from KSTL on 1 April
2023, `OWENS-014` at 2.07 km from KCPS on 3 May 2023, and `OWENS-046` at 2.22 km from KSUS on 25
February 2023. Read them precisely — all three are recovered positions *in the air*, at 3,357 ft,
1,137 ft and -113 ft above the field respectively, and the ground-confirmation count for this whole
state is **0**. The aircraft was over the field. Whether it landed, and who if anyone was aboard,
these traces do not say.

The people side is thinner than the aircraft side, and the gap is the finding. Not one of the nine
rows claims Charlie Kirk: seven claim Erika Kirk alone and two are matched to a TPUSA event with
neither Kirk. Table B returns "none same-day" or "none within three days" on eight of nine rows. The
single same-day match is `SITE-001` on 2 April 2023, and the register's own note on that row says
the event was in Jefferson City — the compiler footnoted the two-hour drive himself, and Jefferson
City has its own airport, KJEF, three miles from the venue. Meanwhile the three dates on which
Charlie Kirk himself is sourced as being in Missouri — 3 April 2022, 18 October 2022 and 19 October
2023 — carry no following aircraft at the airport and none in the metro on any of them.

Both verdict passes are close to evenly split and they do not agree with each other. The audit called
4 rows accurate, 4 inaccurate and 1 partially accurate; the position data corroborated 3, refuted 1
(`OWENS-050`, 12 May 2023, 7,034 km from KSTL), heard nothing on 4 and had no archive coverage at all
for 1. `OWENS-056` and `OWENS-057` are the weakest pair in the state: both claim St. Louis in May
2024 while the register's own ground log has SU-BND sitting at Provo, Utah for 84 days from 19 April
to 12 July 2024, and `OWENS-056` is flagged as a year-advanced duplicate of `OWENS-014`.

Table D adds the one thing in this state that was not a claim to begin with: SU-BTU at KSUS on
1 December 2022, pulled by this repo from primary ADS-B position data. Its caveats belong in the same
breath as the row. The trace ends on final approach at 150 ft and holds zero on-ground points, so the
landing is an inference and not a recorded touchdown; the free ADS-B Exchange sample holds one day
per month, the 1st, so the row establishes that the aircraft was there on that date and says nothing
about how often it came; and no Kirk or TPUSA presence is documented in Missouri that day.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The three corroborated
  rows and the SU-BTU landing establish airframe positions. No passenger, named or otherwise, is
  placed in any cabin by anything on this page.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row** — not on the seven Missouri rows that rest
  on her, and not anywhere in the register, where the established-presence count is
  [zero](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-established-0). Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary was ever published, so
  the "Erika location" column is the compiler's claim rather than a source for it.
* **The 4 Not-heard rows and the 1 No-archive-coverage row are archive limits, not deletions.**
  `NOT_HEARD` means volunteer archives served other airframes that day and hold no trace for this
  one; parked with the transponder off, outside receiver coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look
  identical from outside. `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE` means no free archive reaches that date for any
  airframe probed. Neither is evidence of absence and neither is suppression.
* **A refuted row is an error in a spreadsheet, not misconduct.** Nothing here asserts that any
  living person committed a crime, and the counterarguments the register carries — Duncan Aviation's
  Egyptian Air Force maintenance account since 1999, post-maintenance test profiles, routine
  transatlantic customs stops — are published alongside the claims because they are ordinary
  explanations for the same movements.
* **The state filter is coarser than the geography.** KCPS sits in Illinois while KSTL and KSUS sit
  in Missouri, and SITE-001 does not resolve to a single field, so a state-level tally of "St. Louis
  visits" can double-count or mis-assign. The register flags this on the row itself.
* **Where this count differs from the overview, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site audit
  worked from 78 rows; this register holds 85. The 9 rows on this page are counted from the register.
