# The 13 Charlie / TPUSA rows position data corroborates

**This page opens the number 13 — the Charlie/TPUSA rows recovered position data supports.** Thirteen of 24, **eight of them wheels-down at [Provo](/Planes/Airports/KPVU)**, seven falling on a day this site can independently source an appearance, and the run reaches from 1 April 2023 to 10 September 2025.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the two-ledger
table — the one with the columns *Claimed · Corroborated · Refuted · Archives cannot answer* — has a
row reading **Charlie Kirk + TPUSA**, and its **Corroborated** cell prints **13**. This page is that
cell. All 13 rows are below, spread across **8** distinct dates. The overview's own count for this
cell and the count derived from the register agree: thirteen.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when two things are true of it. First, it belongs to the **Charlie / TPUSA
ledger** — the register records Charlie Kirk as claimed on the row, *or* records a Turning Point USA
event as claimed on it. Second, its **ADS-B verdict is `AT_CLAIMED_AIRPORT`** — a position trace this
investigation recovered and stored puts the named airframe within the corroboration radius of the
field the row names, on the date the row names. Rows whose verdict is `NOT_HEARD`,
`NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE`, `ELSEWHERE`, `SAME_METRO`, or that name no aircraft or no date, are excluded.
To reproduce it from `overlaps.csv`: filter to the Charlie/TPUSA ledger, then to the single ADS-B
verdict value `AT_CLAIMED_AIRPORT`.

## What the specifics show

**Thirteen rows resolve to eight dates, and the compression is the first thing to read.** 10
September 2025 carries three rows — EXTRA-006, OWENS-041 and SITE-006 — because the register keeps a
separate row for the blue plane, for the yellow plane's departure, and for the reconstruction built
from the overlap page. 3 April 2024 carries OWENS-024 and SITE-003; 23 April 2024 carries OWENS-026
and SITE-004. And 23 May 2025 carries OWENS-035 and OWENS-065, which the register's own note records
as the source sheet's double entry of a single landing. **The landing is one landing; counting it
twice inflates the tally, and the register prints both rows rather than quietly deleting one.**

**Provo is where this list lives.** Eight of the thirteen rows name KPVU, and **all eight ground
confirmations in this slice are at Provo** — OWENS-026 and SITE-004 (23 April 2024, 0.00 km),
OWENS-035 and OWENS-065 (23 May 2025, 0.03 km), OWENS-040 (4 September 2025, 0.16 km), and
EXTRA-006, OWENS-041 and SITE-006 (10 September 2025, 1.23–1.29 km). A ground confirmation is the
strongest class of corroboration available here: the airframe transmitting positions with the
on-ground flag set at the field the row names.

**The other five corroborations are weaker and are labelled as such on the row.** OWENS-010 (0.41 km
from KSTL), OWENS-020 (0.31 km from KOMA), OWENS-023 (1.83 km from KICT) and both 3 April 2024 rows
(1.43 km from KICT) are **airborne** positions — an arrival, a departure or a low pass. They confirm
the aircraft was over the field. They do not show it stopped there.

**Not one row in this ledger is refuted by position data**, which is the overview's own figure for
this cell and is worth stating plainly. Against that, **the audit and the position data disagree on
one row and both verdicts stay**: OWENS-041 is marked **Inaccurate** by the tracking-site audit
because the sheet gave the origin as Paris and Lincoln when the aircraft came off the Provo ramp,
while the recovered trace still puts the airframe on the ground 1.2 km from KPVU that day. A wrong
origin string and a correct location are both true at once.

**Where this list is weakest, and it cuts against the claim.** On SITE-003 — 3 April 2024, the one
day in the record Charlie Kirk is documented in Kansas — the register has SU-BTT **departing** Wichita
at 08:43 am and leaving the state, 160 miles from the University of Kansas venue in Lawrence, whose
nearest field is KLWC. OWENS-023 has no same-day sourced appearance at all; the nearest is Texas Tech
in Lubbock, Texas, two days earlier. Neither 23 May 2025 row has a sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance
within three days anywhere, and the audit's own note on that landing reads that the jet was possibly
broken and grounded in Provo for the 113 days that follow. And OWENS-040's nearest sourced Kirk
appearance is Seoul, the day after the aircraft parked at Provo.

**Two of the thirteen do not claim a Kirk at all.** OWENS-010 and OWENS-020 are Turning Point event
rows on which the register records neither Charlie nor Erika as claimed — they are in this ledger
because a TPUSA event is claimed, not because a person is. Of the remaining eleven, six claim both
Kirks and five claim Charlie alone.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** Every one of the eight
  ground confirmations above carries the register's own sentence: it places nobody aboard. A Falcon
  7X on the Provo ramp on 10 September 2025 is an aircraft on a ramp.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows.** Six of these thirteen record her as
  *claimed*, and on the 10 September rows the register prints the claim against her own account —
  the sheet places her in Nebraska or Delaware, she has said she was at home in Arizona. Her flight
  logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no itinerary was ever published.
  Nothing on this page establishes where she was.
* **Corroborated means the aircraft was where the row said it was. It does not mean the row's
  meaning is right.** A landing at Provo, Wichita, Omaha, Lincoln or St. Louis is also what a Falcon
  maintenance contract produces, and the register records that counterargument on every row.
* **An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence, and this cut hides those rows.** Every row
  here has a trace. The rows the archives could not answer for are a different cut — they are
  `NOT_HEARD` or `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE`, which are **archive limits**, not deletions and not
  suppression. Reading this page alone will make the record look better covered than it is.
* **Where the counts differ.** The overview's two-ledger table prints 13 corroborated and 0 refuted
  for the Charlie / TPUSA side, and the register returns exactly 13 rows here. The row-versus-date
  gap is the one number that will not reconcile by itself: 13 rows, 8 dates, with one pair
  (OWENS-035 / OWENS-065) flagged in the register as the same landing entered twice.
