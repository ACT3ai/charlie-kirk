# The three rows naming both tails at once

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table headed
*Which Egyptian plane, and how many each* carries a row reading **"Both tails named on the one
row — 3"**. This page is that cell. It holds **3 rows** across **3 distinct dates**, from
**2 April 2023** to **10 September 2025**.

## What this slice is

Most rows in the overlap register name one aircraft. These three name **two** —
[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), the Dassault Falcon 7X with ICAO hex `0101D3`, and
[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), the Gulfstream 4SP with hex `01003E` — on the same line, because
the compilers placed both airframes at the same field on the same date. Filter `overlaps.csv` for
rows whose tail column carries both registrations and you get exactly these three. Rows recorded as
*"SU-BTT or SU-BND"*, where the source could not tell which aircraft it was looking at, are a
different cell entirely and are **not** in this list; that uncertainty is the opposite of this
certainty.

## What the specifics show

**Three rows, three years, one row a year, and every one of them survived the tracking-site
audit.** All three carry the audit verdict *Accurate* — the only tail-breakdown cell on the
overview where that is true of every row in it.

**Two of the three are corroborated by recovered position data, and both of those are the
strongest evidence class the register has.** [SITE-004](/Planes/following/overlap/20240423_UT_salt_lake_city/overview),
23 April 2024, returns a recovered position **0.00 km from Provo Municipal with the on-ground flag
set**. [SITE-006](/Planes/following/overlap/20250910_UT_orem/overview), 10 September 2025, returns
**1.23 km from KPVU, also on the ground**. Those are the two ground confirmations in this slice, and
they are what makes Provo the concentration here: 2 of the 3 rows sit at one field.

**The third row is the weakest thing on this page and it should be read that way.**
[SITE-001](/Planes/following/overlap/20230402_MO_jefferson_city/overview), 2 April 2023, carries the
ADS-B verdict **Not heard** — the volunteer archives served other airframes that day and hold no
trace for these. That is an archive limit, not a deletion and not suppression. It is also the row
with the largest distance problem in the slice: the sourced TPUSA Faith tour launch that day was in
**Jefferson City, Missouri**, and the aircraft are placed in the **St. Louis metro**, roughly two
hours' drive away. The compiler footnoted that gap himself rather than hiding it, and the register
carries the footnote.

**Three separate airports are folded into one cell on the St. Louis row** — KSTL, KCPS and KSUS,
one of which (Cahokia/KCPS) is in **Illinois**, not Missouri. A state-level tally cannot tell them
apart, so the Missouri entry in the *States* table above is a label the source supplied, not a
measurement this site made.

**On who was supposedly being followed, the slice splits 2 to 1.** Two rows claim both Kirks;
one claims a Turning Point event with neither Kirk named. On [SITE-006](/Planes/following/overlap/20250910_UT_orem/overview)
the sheet's Erika cell reads *"Nebraska/Delaware"* while Erika Kirk's own account places her in
**Arizona** — the sheet and the person it names disagree, and both readings are printed on the row.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The two ground
  confirmations here put two airframes on the Provo ramp. They place **nobody** in either cabin, and
  no ADS-B record in existence can.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero of these rows.** Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary for her has ever been
  published. On the two rows that claim her, the claim is the compilers' spreadsheet cell and
  nothing else — and on 10 September 2025 her own account contradicts it.
* **"Not heard" on [SITE-001](/Planes/following/overlap/20230402_MO_jefferson_city/overview) is an
  archive limit, not a deletion.** Parked with the transponder off, outside volunteer receiver
  coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from the outside. An absence is not a
  finding.
* **Two ground confirmations do not make an intent.** Provo Municipal hosts a Duncan Aviation
  facility, and Duncan has held the Egyptian Air Force maintenance account since 1999. A Falcon and
  a Gulfstream at a maintenance field have an ordinary explanation that has to be stated alongside
  the pairing, and the register states it on both rows.
* **Nobody is accused of a crime on this page.** Tyler Robinson is charged in the killing of Charlie
  Kirk and has not been convicted; nothing in these three rows asserts otherwise, and no person named
  on them is alleged by this site to have done anything unlawful.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from **78** rows; this register holds **85**. The overview's tail table gives this
  cell as 3 rows with 2 Charlie-involved, 2 Erika-involved and 1 TPUSA-only, and the JSON behind this
  page agrees with all four numbers.
