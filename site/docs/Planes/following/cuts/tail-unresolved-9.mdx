# The nine rows that cannot name the aircraft

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that
breaks the overlap register down **by aircraft** ends with a cell reading "Overlaps where even the
tail number is unresolved", printed as **9 — 5 logged as 'SU-BTT or SU-BND', 4 with no tail at all**.
This page is that cell. The register holds **9** such rows and they agree with the overview exactly:
**4** name no aircraft, **5** name two.

## What this slice is

A row lands here when the register cannot resolve the claim down to one airframe. There are only two
ways that happens. Either the following-tail cell in `overlaps.csv` is **empty** — the claim was made
without a tail number, so there is nothing to look up — or it carries **both SU-BTT and SU-BND at
once**, because the source that produced the row could not tell the compilers which of the two jets
it meant. Filter `overlaps.csv` for rows whose following-tail cell is blank or lists both tails and
you get these nine and nothing else. Every other row in the register names exactly one aircraft, or
names both deliberately as a genuine two-aircraft pairing, and lives on a different page.

## What the specifics show

**These nine rows are untestable as published, and they split cleanly into two different kinds of
untestable.** The five `UNPUB` rows carry no date, no city, no airport and no single tail — the
register records them because Candace Owens said on 17 November 2025 that the count had grown from
68 to 73 while the published sheet stopped at 67, and the compilers logged the five-row difference
rather than hiding it. No archive on earth can be asked a question about a claim with no date, so
the ADS-B verdict on all five is **No date named** and the audit verdict is **Never published**.
The four `EXTRA` rows are the opposite shape: they carry a real date, a real city and a real airport,
but no tail number, so there is no airframe to look up. Their ADS-B verdict is **No aircraft named**
and the tracking-site audit never reached them at all.

**The dated four all sit in one state.** EXTRA-002 (9 April 2024) and EXTRA-004 (11 July 2025) are
Omaha, KOMA; EXTRA-001 (14 September 2024) and EXTRA-007 (29 April 2025) are Lincoln, KLNK. Nebraska
is four of the four. Three of them do fall on a day this site independently sources a Kirk or TPUSA
appearance — EXTRA-002 against the Omaha rally at Lord of Hosts Church, EXTRA-007 against the TPUSA
student town hall, EXTRA-004 against the Student Action Summit in Tampa — but a sourced Kirk
appearance without an aircraft is only half a pairing, and on all four rows the Kirk-side aircraft
column reads *none recorded*.

**Read against the claim, this is the weakest ninth of the register and it should be counted that
way.** Nothing here is corroborated: the ground-position count for this slice is **0**, and no row
carries an arrival time, a departure time or a logged ground stay. EXTRA-004 is the sharpest example
of the problem — the sheet claims Erika Kirk, the sourced same-day appearance is Charlie Kirk in
Tampa, Florida, and the airport on the row is Omaha, Nebraska. The row's own counterargument in the
register calls the 9 April 2024 Omaha entry a clean non-overlap, noting the nearest SU-BTT landings
at that field came 15–16 months later. **A 15-month gap is not a shadowing event, and this page is
where that is visible.**

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** On this slice the point
  is sharper still: there is no trace, because there is no airframe named to look one up for.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row** — here or anywhere in the register. Six of
  these nine rows are Erika-alone claims and her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), with no dated itinerary ever published. What
  the rows record is what a compiler's spreadsheet asserted about her location, not a document.
* **"Untestable" is not "false", and it is not an archive gap either.** These nine rows carry no
  `NOT_HEARD` and no `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE` verdict, because no archive was ever in a position to be
  asked. Where those two verdicts do appear on
  [sibling pages](/Planes/following/cuts/adsb-not-heard-37) they are limits on what volunteer ADS-B
  archives hold — never deletions, never suppression.
* **Nothing here names a person as having done anything.** Candace Owens made the 68-to-73 statement
  publicly and is named as the person who made it; the `EXTRA` rows are attributed to the X accounts
  and broadcasts that carried them. No crime is asserted against anyone on this page.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** They do not disagree
  on this cell — the overview prints 9, split 5 and 4, and the register holds 9, split 5 and 4. On
  the register as a whole they do: the tracking-site audit worked from **78** rows, this register
  holds **85**, and the four `EXTRA` rows above are part of why the audit's denominator is smaller.
