# Nebraska — every overlap, every landing, every event

**This page opens Nebraska — 19 claimed rows, the largest state block in the register, and the one with the most missing aircraft.** **Four rows name no airframe at all, zero carry a ground confirmation, and 15 separate ground presences are logged in the state by SU-BTT, SU-BTU and SU-BTV.** Lincoln is also Duncan Aviation's main plant, holder of the Egyptian Air Force maintenance account since 1999.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that
breaks the overlap register down **by state** carries a cell reading "Nebraska". This page is that
cell. It holds **19 claimed overlap rows**. Because Nebraska is also where the fleet's logged ground
stays are densest, this page carries two further tables the state cells elsewhere do not always
need: the **15 logged ground presences** in the state, and the **three sourced Kirk or TPUSA
appearances** the site can independently date to Nebraska.

## What this slice is

Three separate filters, kept separate on purpose. **Table A, B and C** hold every row in
`overlaps.csv` whose claimed location is in Nebraska — 19 rows, at Omaha Eppley (KOMA) or Lincoln
(KLNK). **Table D** holds every leg in `flights.csv` that logs an aircraft on the ground in Nebraska,
whether or not any overlap row was ever built on it — 15 legs. **Table E** holds every entry in
`tpusa_events.csv` for a Kirk or TPUSA appearance in Nebraska — 3 events. A row in Table D is not a
claim about anybody; it is a landing record. Reproduce all three by filtering each file on
`state = NE`.

## What the specifics show

Nebraska is the state where the aircraft records are strongest and the following claim is weakest,
and the gap between Table D and Table B is the reason. Table D holds **15 logged ground presences**
in the state, all of them adsb_captured, flown by **three different Egyptian-registered tails** —
SU-BTT four times, SU-BTU six times, SU-BTV five times. Two of those three tails, SU-BTU and SU-BTV,
appear on **no overlap row in this register at all**. They are in the landing log and nowhere else.

The shape of the Nebraska rotation is consistent to the point of being mechanical: a long Paris leg
into Omaha Eppley — 8:39, 8:48, 8:52, 9:10, 9:12, 9:20 hours across the six logged entries — then a
hop to Lincoln of 15 to 19 minutes, then a stay of five or six days, then out again via Wilmington to
Cairo. Table D's own note says Omaha is an entry field, not a destination, because every Omaha
arrival by this fleet is followed within twenty minutes by the Lincoln hop. Lincoln is Duncan
Aviation's main plant; Table D records that the Egyptian Air Force has held an account there since
1999, citing a Duncan Aviation press release of 29 October 2015.

**The single strongest counterargument on this page is in Table D, and it is published here as
prominently as anything that supports the claim.** SU-BTV flew the identical rotation on 5–11 October
2025: Paris to Omaha in 9:20, a 19-minute hop to Lincoln, six days on the ground. That is roughly a
month after 10 September 2025. Table D states it plainly — the rotations continue afterwards, which
is the strongest argument the pattern is a maintenance schedule rather than a tasking. Table D's
Omaha note also corrects a claim that circulated about the July 2025 arrival: @Borrowed7Time logged
Paris to Omaha Eppley, a **civil** field, and later compilations relabelled it "an Army base"; no
primary post naming Offutt, Camp Ashland or any Army installation has been found.

On the overlap side the results are thin. Of 19 rows, **10 are Not heard** and **4 name no aircraft
at all** (EXTRA-002, EXTRA-001, EXTRA-007, EXTRA-004), leaving 4 corroborated and 1 refuted.
**Ground-position confirmations among the overlap rows are zero.** All four corroborated rows —
OWENS-020 at 0.31 km from KOMA, OWENS-038 at 1.33 km, OWENS-067 at 7.20 km from KLNK, OWENS-039 at
0.50 km — are described in Table C as corroborated in the air, not on the ground: an arrival, a
departure or a low pass at 366, 591, 881 and 391 feet above the field. OWENS-036, dated 18 June 2025,
is affirmatively refuted; the airframe's closest recovered position that day was 10,139.55 km from
Omaha. The tracking-site audit called 10 of the 19 rows **Inaccurate** and reached only one as
**Accurate**.

The who-was-followed side splits against itself. Thirteen of the 19 rows rest on **Erika Kirk alone**,
sourced to a spreadsheet cell reading "Nebraska" — yet all **three** independently sourced Kirk
appearances in the state, in Table E, are **Charlie's**, and Erika is recorded `not_documented` on
every one of them. Only four rows land on a same-day sourced appearance, and one of those four,
EXTRA-004 on 11 July 2025, pairs an Omaha aircraft claim with a Charlie Kirk appearance in **Tampa,
Florida**. EXTRA-005 is stronger against the claim still: Charlie's Lincoln appearance of 25 August
2023 is sourced, and the audit places the jet in Egypt that day. Two rows, EXTRA-007 and EXTRA-004,
trace to amplifier accounts posting in August 2026 rather than to any flight record.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** Every leg in Table D is
  an airframe on a ramp. None of the 15 legs, and none of the four corroborated overlap rows, places
  any named person in a cabin.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row.** Thirteen of these 19 rows rest on her
  alone and all thirteen source her location to the compiler's spreadsheet. In Table E she is
  `not_documented` at all three sourced Nebraska events. Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary was ever published.
* **The ten Not heard rows are an archive limit, not a deletion.** Volunteer ADS-B networks hear what
  their receivers hear. A parked aircraft with the transponder off, an airframe outside receiver
  coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from the outside. **Nothing on this page is
  evidence that any record was removed.**
* **The four rows naming no aircraft are untestable as published,** not disproved.
* **A landing record is not an allegation.** Table D lists ground stays at a commercial airport and
  at a maintenance plant. Attributing a purpose to any of them goes beyond what the record shows, and
  the continuation of the rotations after September 2025 points toward maintenance.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from 78 rows; this register holds 85, and four of the 19 Nebraska rows were never
  reached by that audit.
