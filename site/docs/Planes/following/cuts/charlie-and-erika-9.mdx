# The nine rows claiming both Kirks together

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table that sorts the claimed overlaps by *whose* presence the compilation actually asserts carries a row reading **"Overlaps claiming Charlie and Erika together"**, and the cell beside it prints **9**. This page is those nine rows, one line each, with the aircraft, the airport, the claimed date, the sourced appearance if there is one, and both verdicts. The overview also states that the nine resolve to five dates; the register agrees — **9 rows, 5 distinct dates**.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the reconstructed register in `overlaps.csv` records **Charlie Kirk as claimed present and Erika Kirk as claimed present on the same row** — the attendee class `CHARLIE_AND_ERIKA`. "Claimed" is the compilers' assertion, reproduced here; it is not a finding by this site. On none of these rows is **Erika Kirk's** presence established by any evidence at all. Charlie Kirk's presence is established on some of them — but always from event listings and press coverage catalogued in [the speaking-events index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), never from an aircraft record. Rows naming only one Kirk, and rows matched to a Turning Point event with neither Kirk claimed, are excluded and sit in the sibling slices linked at the foot of this page.

## What the specifics show

**Nine rows, five dates.** Three of the five dates carry more than one row. `OWENS-033` and `OWENS-063` are the same 15 March 2025 Wichita claim entered twice. `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` are the same 23 May 2025 Provo landing entered twice. And three separate rows — `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` — all sit on 10 September 2025, two of them describing the two aircraft on the Provo ramp and one describing the Provo-to-Wilmington leg. Nothing has been deleted to tidy the arithmetic, but a reader counting "nine times both Kirks were followed" is counting restatements.

**The aircraft side is the strong half, and it is entirely Utah.** Six of the nine rows are corroborated by raw ADS-B traces this investigation pulled and stored, and all six of those confirmations carry the on-ground flag: `SITE-004`, `OWENS-035`, `OWENS-065`, `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` — every one of them at **KPVU, Provo**. The three rows position data does not corroborate are the three Wichita rows at **KICT**, and all three come back **Not heard**, which is an archive limit and not a refutation: an archive that served other aircraft those days holds no trace for this airframe, and parked-with-the-transponder-off, outside-receiver-coverage and a wrong claimed date all look the same from outside.

**The two methods disagree on one row, and the disagreement is kept.** `OWENS-041` was called **Inaccurate** by the tracking-site audit, which corrected the claimed origin — the register records that the aircraft left the Provo ramp, not Paris. Measured against stored position data the same row comes back corroborated, on the ground 1.2 km from KPVU. Both verdicts are printed above and neither is folded into the other.

**Where the list is weakest is the Kirk side.** On **five of the nine rows** there is no Kirk or TPUSA appearance this site can independently source on the claimed day: `OWENS-033` and `OWENS-063` (the nearest sourced appearance is Knoxville, Tennessee, two days earlier), and `OWENS-034`, `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065`, which have no sourced appearance within three days anywhere at all. Only **four of the nine** land on a sourced same-day appearance — `SITE-004` at the University of Utah, and the three 10 September rows at Utah Valley University.

**Not one row places both Kirks in the same city.** On `SITE-004`, `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` the sheet's Erika cell reads, in full, "Utah" — a state, not a location. On `OWENS-033`, `OWENS-063` and `OWENS-034` it reads "Kansas / Delaware", two states at once. On the three 10 September rows the sheet reads "Nebraska / Delaware" while Erika Kirk's own published account places her in Arizona; the register records that dispute rather than resolving it.

**And the ordinary explanation is on the record too.** The register's own counterargument on `SITE-004` notes that Provo hosts a Duncan Aviation MRO which has held the Egyptian Air Force maintenance account since 1999, so a Provo arrival has an ordinary explanation, and it puts that aircraft's 2024 stay at about **84 days** — a heavy-check length, not a surveillance orbit. On the 23 May 2025 rows the counterargument records a stay of **113 days** on the Provo ramp, which it calls a maintenance-visit length.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** Six ground confirmations here put two airframes on the ramp at Provo. They say nothing whatever about who was in them, or whether anybody was.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero of these nine rows** — as it is on zero rows anywhere in this register. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), no itinerary has ever been published by her, by the compilers, or by anyone else, and the aircraft-side recovery does not produce one. Every "Erika: claimed" cell above is the spreadsheet's assertion.
* **Charlie Kirk's presence is not established from flight data either.** Where a sourced appearance is shown, it is sourced from event listings and press coverage catalogued in [the speaking-events index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) — not from any aircraft record. No Kirk-side aircraft is on record on any of the nine rows.
* **The three Not heard rows are an archive limit, not a deletion and not a refutation.** An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence.
* **A same-day match is not a same-hour match.** Most of these rows carry no published event time, so "same day" cannot be narrowed to "before" or "after". The 10 September rows are the exception.
* **On the count.** The overview prints **9 rows across 5 dates** for this cell and the register agrees, so there is no discrepancy here. Note that the audit-verdict column above is drawn from a line-by-line check that worked from the compilers' published sheet of 78 rows, while this register holds 85 — a difference that matters on other cuts pages more than on this one.
* **Nobody here is accused of a crime.** Every claim above is attributed to the compilers, to the auditor, or to the stored position data, and is reproduced so it can be checked.
