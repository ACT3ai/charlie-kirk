# Delaware — every overlap, every landing, every event

**This page opens Delaware — 11 claimed rows, all at [Wilmington (KILG)](/Planes/Airports/KILG), and every one resting on Erika Kirk alone.** **Five are corroborated by position data, one is a ground confirmation, and only one sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance has ever been documented in the state — 3 October 2022, with neither Kirk present.** Wilmington appears on the outbound leg only.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the geographic
view carries a five-state clustering table — Missouri, **Delaware**, Utah, Nebraska, Kansas — whose
Delaware cell reads "Wilmington (KILG) — a major business-aviation customs and fuel field, used
outbound only". This page is that cell, opened up. The register holds **11** claimed overlaps in
Delaware, **16** logged ground stays at the state's one field, and **1** sourced Kirk-side event in
the whole state.

## What this slice is

Everything in the register that touches the state of Delaware, joined from three directions. A row
is in the overlap tables if `overlaps.csv` gives its state as DE — that is 11 rows, and every one of
them names the same airport, **KILG, Wilmington New Castle**. A row is in the landings table if
`flights.csv` logs a following aircraft on the ground at a Delaware field, whether or not anyone ever
claimed an overlap for it — that is 16 stays. A row is in the events table if `tpusa_events.csv`
places a sourced Charlie Kirk or Turning Point appearance in Delaware — that is one, in 2022. The
three tables are deliberately not filtered to agree with each other; the gaps between them are the
finding.

## What the specifics show

**Delaware is a one-field state in this register, and a one-person claim.** All 11 overlap rows name
KILG and nothing else, and all 11 are **Erika Kirk alone** — Charlie Kirk is claimed on none of them.
That is different from every other state in the clustering table, and it means the Delaware column of
the five-state argument stands or falls entirely on where Erika Kirk was, which is the half of the
claim with no published document behind it.

**The archives split the 11 three ways, and only one of those ways is a positive result.** Five rows
are corroborated by recovered position data — OWENS-011, OWENS-013, OWENS-021, OWENS-027 and
SITE-005. Four rows come back **Not heard**: the archives served other airframes those days and hold
no trace for this one, which is an archive limit, not a deletion. Two rows — OWENS-005 and OWENS-043,
both November 2022 — come back **No archive coverage**: no free archive we can reach holds that date
at all, for any airframe, which is again an archive limit and not suppression. Exactly **one** row in
the state is the strongest evidence class: OWENS-013, 20 April 2023, SU-BTT on the ground and
transmitting 0.56 km from the field.

**The two same-day pairings both point out of state.** OWENS-048 (27 March 2023) sits against a
sourced Charlie Kirk appearance at Texas Christian University in Fort Worth, Texas. SITE-005
(10 September 2025) sits against Charlie Kirk at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah — and on that
row the compilation spreadsheet places Erika Kirk in "Nebraska/Delaware" while Erika Kirk's own
account places her in Arizona. Seven of the 11 rows have no sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance within
three days anywhere in the country, and two more have their nearest sourced appearance two or three
days away, in Arizona and Kansas.

**The landings table is where the innocent explanation gets its teeth.** Delaware carries **16**
logged ground stays at KILG, and only four of them are the aircraft the overlap claims name: SU-BTT
appears four times, while **SU-BTU appears five times and SU-BTV seven** — and neither of those two
tails is claimed as a following aircraft on a single Delaware overlap row. Every one of those stays
but one carries the same note: an outbound stop, followed by a direct run to Cairo, timed between
9:36 and 11:11. The 1 December 2024 SU-BTU row states the pattern in full — "Wilmington appears on
the outbound leg only, every time, for three years." A field that the whole fleet uses on its way
home, on a fixed rhythm, whether or not anybody claims a Kirk was near it, is behaving like a customs
and fuel stop.

**The one Kirk-side event in the state cuts the same way.** Table E holds a single row: 3 October
2022, the Live Free Tour at the University of Delaware, 12 miles from KILG — and the register records
**Charlie present: no, Erika present: no**. The speaker of record was Candace Owens, who later
compiled the overlap sheet. It also predates all 11 overlap claims. Delaware has, in this register,
no sourced Charlie Kirk appearance at all.

**One row here is older than every claim and was not claimed by anyone.** The final line of Table D
records SU-BTT on the ground at Wilmington on **1 June 2022**, first fix 11:31:13 UTC at 0.71 km,
sourced by this investigation from primary data rather than from any tracker. It is the earliest US
position held for that aircraft. It supports the aircraft being in the United States earlier than the
published claims say, and it supports nothing at all about who was near it.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The one ground
  confirmation in this state, OWENS-013, says so in the register's own words: "It places NOBODY
  aboard."
* **Erika Kirk's presence is not established on any row** — and all 11 Delaware rows are Erika
  claims. Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated
  itinerary was ever published. Four of the 11 rows give her location as the single word "Delaware";
  **six name two or more places at once** — "Delaware / Nebraska" three times, plus
  "Delaware / Missouri", "Delaware / Utah" and "Wichita / DE / potentially Giza" — and the eleventh
  is contradicted by Erika Kirk's own account. A state is not a location.
* **The six rows that returned nothing are archive limits, not deletions.** Four **Not heard** and
  two **No archive coverage**. Parked with the transponder off, outside volunteer receiver coverage,
  and a wrong claimed date all look identical from the outside. None of the six is evidence the
  aircraft was elsewhere, and none of them is evidence anything was suppressed.
* **A customs stop is an explanation, not a verdict.** The 16 logged stays are consistent with
  Wilmington being an outbound clearance field, and this page reports that because it is what the
  record says. It does not rule out any other use of the field; it removes the surprise from the
  count.
* **Nobody is accused of anything here.** Candace Owens made the compilation claims publicly under
  her own name and is named as the person who made them; @KanekoaTheGreat is named as the auditor.
  Aircraft owners, crew, passengers and ground staff are unnamed living persons and stay unnamed.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** They disagree on
  Delaware. The overview's location table prints **21** landings for Wilmington and its closing
  paragraph says "twelve departures and zero arrivals"; the flights table behind this page holds
  **16** logged ground stays at KILG. This page's number is the flights table's, the overview's
  numbers are the location page's tallies, and neither has been quietly conformed to the other. On
  the register as a whole the same applies: the tracking-site audit worked from **78** rows, this
  register holds **85**.
