# The Charlie Kirk and TPUSA ledger — all 24 rows

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the ledger table
that scores each claim class against recovered position data carries the line
**"Charlie Kirk + TPUSA — 24 claimed, 13 corroborated, 0 refuted, 8 the archives cannot answer
(+3 that name no aircraft)."** This page is that line, opened. All **24** rows are below with every
field the register holds. The counts on this page and the counts on the overview agree exactly:
24 rows, 13 corroborated, 0 refuted, 8 not heard, 3 naming no aircraft.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the compilers' sheet or this site's reconstruction attaches it to
**Charlie Kirk, to a Turning Point USA event, or to both** — as opposed to the much larger block of
rows that rest on Erika Kirk alone. Three sub-classes make it up, and they are counted separately
in the register: **9 rows claiming Charlie Kirk alone**, **9 rows claiming Charlie and Erika
together**, and **6 rows matched to a Turning Point event with neither Kirk claimed present**. To
reproduce it from `overlaps.csv`, take every row where the *Charlie Present* column reads claimed,
and add every row where a Turning Point event is attached but neither Kirk is. Rows resting on
[Erika Kirk alone](/Planes/following/cuts/erika-only-61) are excluded.

## What the specifics show

**This is the strongest block in the register, and it is strong because it is small.** Thirteen of
these 24 rows are corroborated by raw position data this investigation recovered and stored, and
**not one is refuted** — the only claim class on the whole Following overview where the refuted
column reads zero. That zero is real, but it sits on a denominator of 24 against the register's 85,
and eleven of the 24 return no usable answer at all.

**Eight of the site's ten ground-flag confirmations are in this list, and every one of them is at
Provo.** `OWENS-026` and `SITE-004` put SU-BTT on the ground at KPVU on **23 April 2024** at 0.00 km;
`OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` put SU-BND there on **23 May 2025** at 0.03 km; `OWENS-040` puts SU-BTT
there on **4 September 2025** at 0.16 km; and `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` put both
airframes on the Provo ramp on **10 September 2025**. The register logs 7 of the 24 rows as Utah and
one more as Utah/Delaware, and those eight rows are exactly the eight ground records — the
concentration is not spread evenly across the list, it is one field.

**Twelve of the 24 fall on a day this site independently sources a Kirk or Turning Point
appearance, and on five of those the row places the aircraft at the field nearest the venue.**
`EXTRA-005` (25 August 2023) claims the jet at Lincoln on the day of a sourced Charlie Kirk
appearance in Lincoln, 4 miles from KLNK — but that row comes back **not heard**, so the archives
neither support nor contradict it. `EXTRA-002` (9 April 2024) claims Omaha on the day of a sourced
rally in Omaha, 4 miles from KOMA — and that row names no aircraft, so it cannot be tested at all.
The three 10 September 2025 rows are different: `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` are
corroborated by ground-flag position data at Provo, 5 miles from the UVU courtyard. Two further
pairings are corroborated but same-state rather than same-field — `OWENS-024` and `SITE-003` at
Wichita, 1.43 km from KICT, against the University of Kansas in Lawrence on 3 April 2024, and
`OWENS-026` and `SITE-004` on the Provo ramp against the University of Utah in Salt Lake City on
23 April 2024.

**Where the list is weakest.** Eight rows come back **Not heard** — including both 15 March 2025
Wichita rows and the 22 March 2025 Wichita row — which means the archives cover the date and the
volunteer receivers recorded nothing for that airframe. That is an archive limit, not a finding
about the aircraft and not a deletion. Three more rows (`EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-001`, `EXTRA-007`)
**name no aircraft at all**, so there is nothing to look up and they are untestable as published —
`EXTRA-002` is one of the five same-field same-day rows above, and it is untestable, which is worth
stating rather than glossing. The tracking-site audit reached only 20 of the 24; it called 10
accurate, 6 inaccurate and 4 partially accurate, and never saw the other 4.

**Several of these rows are the same claim counted twice, and the register keeps both.**
`OWENS-024` and `SITE-003` are the same 3 April 2024 Wichita claim and return an identical 1.43 km.
`OWENS-026` and `SITE-004` are the same 23 April 2024 Provo arrival. `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065`
are the same 23 May 2025 SU-BND arrival and return an identical 0.03 km. `OWENS-033` and
`OWENS-063` are the same 15 March 2025 Wichita claim and both come back not heard. A duplicated row
in a sheet whose headline is a count is a finding about the count, so no row is deleted to tidy the
arithmetic — but 24 rows are not 24 independent observations.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace locates an aircraft. It never places a person aboard.** The 13 corroborations here
  establish that an Egyptian-registered jet was at the named field on the named date. They establish
  nothing at all about who was inside it, on the ground or in the air, and nothing about why it was
  there.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows, including the nine here that claim her
  alongside Charlie Kirk.** Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)
  and no dated itinerary for her has ever been published. On the 10 September 2025 rows the sheet's
  own Erika cell reads "Nebraska / Delaware" while her own account places her in Arizona — the
  register carries the dispute rather than resolving it.
* **A sourced Charlie Kirk appearance on the same date is a coincidence of calendar until something
  connects the two.** These rows show an aircraft at a field and, separately, a person at a venue.
  Nothing in this data links them, and the register does not claim it does.
* **Zero refuted is not the same as verified.** Eight rows the archives cannot answer and three that
  name no aircraft would all have to come back clean before "nothing here is refuted" could carry
  the weight it looks like it carries.
* **An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence, and it is not suppression.** The 8 *Not
  heard* rows mean volunteer receivers recorded nothing. Transponder off, no receiver in range, and
  a wrong claimed date all look identical from here.
* **Lincoln and Omaha have a standing innocent explanation** that this site publishes alongside the
  claim: Duncan Aviation's main maintenance plant is at Lincoln and has held the Egyptian Air Force
  account since 1999. Rows at KLNK and KOMA must be read against that first.
* **On the count.** The overview prints 24 / 13 / 0 / 8 (+3 naming no aircraft) for this ledger line
  and the JSON behind this page returns the same figures, so there is nothing to reconcile. Separately,
  the tracking-site audit quoted elsewhere on the overview worked from a **78-row** version of the
  register while this register holds **85**; audit percentages and register counts are therefore
  measured against different denominators by design.
* Every claim above is attributed — the sheet is the compilers', the audit verdicts are the
  auditor's, the ADS-B verdicts are this investigation's, measured off raw traces. No row here
  asserts that any living person committed any crime.
