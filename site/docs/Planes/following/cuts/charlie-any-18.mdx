# Every row that involves Charlie Kirk at all

**This page opens the number 18 — every row of the register that names Charlie Kirk at all.** Eighteen out of 85. **Position data corroborates 11 of them, refutes none, and 10 fall on a day this site can independently source a Kirk or TPUSA appearance** — the highest same-day rate of any cut on this site.

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the table
that splits the register by which Kirk is claimed carries a row reading **"Overlaps involving
Charlie at all"**. That cell is the count **18**, and this page is the 18 rows behind it — every
row of the register on which Charlie Kirk is named, whether or not Erika Kirk is named with him.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the register records Charlie Kirk as claimed present, or as possibly
present, on that date and at that place. Nine of the 18 are classed as **Charlie Kirk alone** and
nine as **Both Kirks**; the nine both-Kirks rows appear here and also in the sibling cut for the
pair. Two rows — `OWENS-024` and `OWENS-026` — are logged as Charlie **unknown** rather than
Charlie **claimed**, and they are carried here because the compilers' own attendee class puts them
on Charlie's side of the ledger. Rows that name only Erika Kirk, and rows matched to a Turning
Point event with neither Kirk claimed, are excluded. An investigator can reproduce this list from
`overlaps.csv` by taking every row whose attendee class is "Charlie Kirk alone" or "Both Kirks".

## What the specifics show

This is the strongest-evidenced part of the register and also the smallest. Eleven of the 18 rows
carry recovered position data placing the named aircraft at the claimed airport, and eight of those
eleven are on-ground confirmations — `OWENS-026` and `SITE-004` at 0.00 km from KPVU on 23 April
2024, `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065` at 0.03 km on 23 May 2025, `OWENS-040` at 0.16 km on 4 September
2025, and `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006` on 10 September 2025. Eighteen rows collapse to
12 distinct dates because the compilers logged several days twice: 15 March 2025 appears as both
`OWENS-033` and `OWENS-063`, 23 May 2025 as both `OWENS-035` and `OWENS-065`, 3 April 2024 as
`OWENS-024` and `SITE-003`, 23 April 2024 as `OWENS-026` and `SITE-004`, and 10 September 2025
three times over as `EXTRA-006`, `OWENS-041` and `SITE-006`. Geography is narrow: eight rows sit at
Provo (KPVU), six at Wichita (KICT), and the remaining four across Lincoln and Omaha in Nebraska.

The list is weakest at both ends of the tail column. Three rows — `EXTRA-002`, `EXTRA-001` and
`EXTRA-007` — name no aircraft at all, so there is nothing to look up; all three are Nebraska rows
that the tracking-site audit never reached. Four more — `EXTRA-005` at Lincoln, Nebraska, and
`OWENS-033`, `OWENS-063` and `OWENS-034` at Wichita, Kansas — return **Not heard**, which is an
archive limit and not a finding either way. The tracking-site audit disagrees with the register on five rows, calling them
**Inaccurate**, and on one of those five, `OWENS-041`, the recovered position data nonetheless puts
SU-BTT on the ground at Provo — so audit and position data point in opposite directions on the very
date at the centre of this investigation, and both readings are printed above rather than one being
chosen. Ten of the 18 rows sit on a day this site can independently source a Charlie Kirk or Turning
Point appearance; the other eight do not, and three of those eight — `OWENS-034`, `OWENS-035` and
`OWENS-065` — have no sourced appearance within three days anywhere at all.

## What this list cannot tell you

* A trace proves an aircraft was at a place on a date. It never proves who was aboard. Every
  on-ground confirmation above places an airframe on a ramp and nobody in a seat.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows of this register**, including the nine rows
  here that claim both Kirks. Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no itinerary was ever published, so the
  "Erika: claimed" cells above record what the compilers asserted, not what anyone has shown. On
  three of those rows the sheet's own claimed location for her is disputed by her own account.
* The four **Not heard** rows mean the archives we can reach hold no trace for that airframe on
  that date. That is an archive limit. Parked with the transponder off, outside volunteer-receiver
  coverage, and a wrong claimed date all look identical from outside, and none of them is evidence
  the aircraft was elsewhere.
* The three **No aircraft named** rows are untestable as published rather than tested and failed.
  No archive can be queried without a tail number.
* Nothing here establishes that any person committed any offence. The audit verdicts are the
  auditor's readings of tracking-site records; the ADS-B verdicts are this site's readings of
  recovered position data; both are attributed above and neither is a legal finding.
* Counts on this page come from the 85-row register. Where the overview page prints a figure drawn
  from the tracking-site audit instead, that audit worked from 78 rows and its totals will be
  smaller. Where the two disagree, the register total is the one used here.
