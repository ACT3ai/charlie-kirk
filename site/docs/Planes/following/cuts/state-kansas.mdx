# Kansas — every overlap, every landing, every event

**Where this came from.** On [the Following overview](/Planes/following/overview), the five-state
clustering table names **Kansas** as one of the states the aircraft record clusters in, with
**Wichita (KICT)** as the field and **Yingling Aviation**, an FAA Part 145 Dassault Falcon service
centre, as the ordinary explanation sitting on it. This page opens that state out completely: all
**18 claimed overlaps** across **15 distinct dates**, from **13 November 2022** to **22 March
2025**, plus the **5 logged ground presences** and the **3 sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearances** the
site holds for Kansas.

## What this slice is

Everything in the register whose state is **KS**. Filter `overlaps.csv` on the state column for
`KS` and you get the 18 rows in Tables A to C — 17 of them claimed at **KICT**, Wichita Dwight D.
Eisenhower National, and one at **KTOP**, Topeka. Table D is every row of `flights.csv` logging an
aircraft on the ground at a Kansas field, whether or not any overlap was ever claimed for it. Table
E is every row of `tpusa_events.csv` placing a sourced Charlie Kirk or Turning Point appearance in
Kansas. The three tables are deliberately kept separate because they are three different kinds of
record, and the interesting result is what happens when you line them up.

## What the specifics show

**Kansas is one airport wearing a state's name.** Seventeen of the 18 rows are Wichita; the
eighteenth, [EXTRA-003](/Planes/following/overlap/20230423_KS_topeka_extra_003/overview), is a
Topeka row entered because a TPUSA Faith event was held there on 23 April 2023 — and the register's
own note records that the aircraft had **departed Wichita three days earlier**, 135 miles away.
Every one of the 18 names [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and no other aircraft.

**Three rows are corroborated by recovered position data, and they collapse into two dates.**
[OWENS-023](/Planes/following/overlap/20240329_KS_wichita_owens_023/overview) (29 March 2024)
returns **1.83 km** from Wichita Eisenhower;
[OWENS-024](/Planes/following/overlap/20240403_KS_wichita_lawrence_owens_024/overview) and
[SITE-003](/Planes/following/overlap/20240403_KS_lawrence/overview) are the *same claim entered
twice* for 3 April 2024 and both return **1.43 km**. So the entire corroborated Kansas
record is one aircraft, at one field, on two dates five days apart, inside a single spring-2024
rotation. **None of the three carries an on-ground flag — the ground-confirmation count for the
whole state is zero.**

**The 3 April 2024 pairing is the tightest thing Kansas has, and it is not tight.** Charlie Kirk
spoke at the [University of Kansas in Lawrence](/Planes/following/speaking/20240403_lawrence) that
day; the aircraft is placed at Wichita, about 160 miles away, and the overview records it as
**departing that morning**. Same day, same state, different city, and the aircraft leaving.

**Fourteen of the 18 rows cannot be tested at all.** Twelve come back *not heard* and two more *no
archive coverage* — fourteen rows on which the free volunteer archives simply have nothing to say.
`OWENS-012` (14 April 2023) lands *right area, wrong field* at **27.58 km** from Wichita: inside the
source sheet's own tolerance, outside a same-airport test, refuting nothing and confirming nothing.
**None of that is a deletion.** The tracking-site audit, working independently, called **12** of the
18 Kansas rows *inaccurate*.

**Now line Table A against Table D, because this is the strongest finding on the page.** The five
logged ground presences at Kansas fields are 1 September 2022 (SU-BTT), 8–10 December 2024,
30 March – 3 April 2025 and 30 July – 3 August 2025 (all [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview)), and an
unverified 1 October 2025 SU-BTT claim. **Not one of those five dates falls on any of the 18 claimed
overlap dates**, and **three of the five are SU-BTV — an airframe the overlap sheet does not
attribute a single one of the 85 claimed overlaps to.** The state that is being counted as a
clustering state is, in the logged ground record, mostly a different aircraft on different days.

**And Table E is the other half of that.** All three sourced Kansas appearances are at
[Topeka](/Planes/following/speaking/20230423_topeka) (TPUSA Faith, neither Kirk named as speaker),
[Lawrence](/Planes/following/speaking/20240403_lawrence) and
[Manhattan](/Planes/following/speaking/20240913_manhattan). **None of the three is in Wichita**,
where 17 of the 18 claimed overlaps are. The claim side and the event side of Kansas do not meet in
the same city on any date in the record.

**One row's same-day match is not in Kansas at all.** Four rows fall on a day this site can source a
Kirk or TPUSA appearance somewhere; on `OWENS-022` (18 March 2024) that appearance was **San Diego
State University in California**, while the aircraft is claimed at Wichita. It is counted in the
same-day column and it is worth nothing to the Kansas argument.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The three corroborated
  Kansas rows corroborate SU-BTT's position near Wichita. They place **nobody** in the cabin.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero of these rows** — including the 10 rows that rest
  on her alone and the 3 that claim both Kirks. Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no dated itinerary for her has ever been
  published. The *Erika location* cell on these rows reads *"Kansas"* or *"Kansas / Delaware"* — a
  state, or two states, never a city or a venue.
* **The 14 undecidable rows are archive limits, not deletions.** `NOT_HEARD` means volunteer
  receivers served other airframes that day and hold no trace for this one; `NO_ARCHIVE_COVERAGE`
  means no free archive reaches the date at all. Parked with the transponder off, outside receiver
  coverage, and a wrong claimed date are indistinguishable from the outside. An absence is not a
  finding, and it is not evidence the aircraft was somewhere else.
* **The counter-explanation for Kansas is strong and it is stated here, not buried.** Wichita is the
  American general-aviation manufacturing capital and KICT holds **Yingling Aviation**, an FAA Part
  145 repair station authorised on Dassault Falcon aircraft. SU-BTT is a Falcon 7X and SU-BTV is a
  Falcon. A Falcon at the Falcon shop is the ordinary explanation for every Kansas landing in
  Table D, and nothing on this page rules it out.
* **The one primary-data Kansas row carries a sampling caveat that must travel with it.** The
  1 September 2022 SU-BTT trace came from the free ADS-B Exchange monthly sample, which holds **one
  day per month, the 1st, and nothing else**. It establishes that the aircraft was at Wichita that
  day. It says nothing about how often it came, and its logged stay is zero days only because that
  is the only day the archive holds.
* **Nobody is accused of a crime on this page.** No living person named in these rows is alleged by
  this site to have done anything unlawful.
* **Where the overview and this register disagree, both figures get printed.** The tracking-site
  audit worked from **78** rows; this register holds **85**. Two Kansas-specific gaps are worth
  naming: the overview's location table gives the [Wichita](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview)
  page **6** visits, while `flights.csv` yields the **5** Kansas ground presences in Table D — the
  two are counted from different files at different times. And the overview notes that **nine**
  claimed Wichita overlaps put the aircraft in Egypt instead; that figure comes from the
  tracking-site audit pass, not from the position-data verdicts tabulated above.
