# Rows that land on a day this site independently sources a Kirk or TPUSA appearance

**Where this came from.** [The Following overview](/Planes/following/overview) reduces the whole
85-row register with a same-day test — the passage about the rows *"that survive a same-day test at a
shared field"* — and reports the survivors as a small number of dates rather than a long list of
rows. This page is the row-level version of that test. **It holds 10 rows across 7 distinct dates.**
The overview's own prose for the narrower version of the same test prints **11 rows resolving to six
dates**; the two figures are not the same and both are given below, in *What this list cannot tell
you*.

## What this slice is

A row is in this list when the **date the register claims for it is a date on which this site can
independently source a Charlie Kirk appearance or a Turning Point USA appearance** — sourced meaning
it has its own page in [the speaking-events catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), with a
citation, not merely an assertion in the source sheet. The test is applied to the **date**. It does
not require the aircraft to have been at the event's own airport, it does not require the aircraft to
have been in the same state, and it does not require anything at all about who was aboard. To
reproduce it from `overlaps.csv`: keep the rows whose same-day sourced Kirk or TPUSA appearance field
is populated.

**That rule admits rows that fail on the aircraft side**, and two of the ten do. They are kept
because the overview's whole argument is that a matching date is not a matching location.

## What the specifics show

**Ten rows, seven dates, and the strongest thing on the page is also the narrowest.** Three of the
ten — EXTRA-006, OWENS-041 and SITE-006 — are 10 September 2025 at Provo Municipal, which the
speaking catalog places five miles from the Sorensen Center courtyard at Utah Valley University. That
is the one date in this list where a following aircraft and a sourced Charlie Kirk appearance are at
the same field, and all three of those rows carry an on-ground position: 1.23 km, 1.23 km and 1.29 km
from KPVU. A fourth ground confirmation sits on SITE-004, 23 April 2024, 0.00 km from KPVU, on the
day the catalog sources a Prove Me Wrong appearance at the University of Utah — 45 miles away, in a
different city, served by a different airport.

**Two of the ten pass the date test and fail on the aircraft, and that is why they are printed here
rather than quietly dropped.** EXTRA-005 (25 August 2023, Lincoln) is the one date in 2023 the
catalog sources Charlie Kirk in Lincoln, and the tracking-site audit marks the row **Inaccurate**,
placing the aircraft in Egypt. EXTRA-002 (9 April 2024, Omaha) sources a Charlie Kirk rally at Lord
of Hosts Church at 19:00 CT and **names no tail number at all**, so there is no airframe to look up
and nothing to test. **On a same-day date test, a row with no aircraft still passes. That is the
weakness of a date test, stated as plainly as it can be stated.**

**The two Turning Point rows carry no Kirk.** SITE-001 (2 April 2023) and EXTRA-003 (23 April 2023)
match the TPUSA Faith tour's Jefferson City launch and its Topeka stop. On both the register records
neither Charlie nor Erika as claimed, so what is being matched is an organisation's event against an
aircraft's state. Both are **Not heard** — the archives were asked and returned nothing for that
airframe on that day — and on EXTRA-003 the register's own note has SU-BTT leaving Wichita three days
before the Topeka date, roughly 135 miles away.

**Kansas is the clearest case of the pairing pointing the wrong way.** OWENS-024 and SITE-003 both
carry 3 April 2024, the day the catalog sources Charlie Kirk at the University of Kansas in Lawrence.
The register has the aircraft **departing** Wichita at 08:43 am that morning and leaving the state,
160 miles from a venue whose nearest field is KLWC. Both rows are corroborated by position data at
1.43 km from KICT — an airborne position, not a ground stop — and the audit calls both **partially
accurate**. The date matches. The location does not.

**Verdict spread across the ten:** four Accurate, two Partially accurate, two Inaccurate and two the
audit never reached; six corroborated by recovered position data, three Not heard, one with no
aircraft to check. Eight of the ten claim Charlie Kirk in some form — four of those eight claim both
Kirks as well — and the remaining two claim neither Kirk at all.

## What this list cannot tell you

* **A trace proves an aircraft was there. It never proves who was aboard.** The four ground
  confirmations here put a Falcon 7X and a Gulfstream on the Provo ramp on two dates. The register's
  own note on each says it places nobody aboard, and that includes 10 September 2025.
* **Erika Kirk's presence is established on zero rows.** Four rows here record her as *claimed*, and
  on all three 10 September rows the register prints the claim against her own account — the sheet
  places her in Nebraska or Delaware, she has said she was at home in Arizona. Her flight logs are
  [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and no itinerary was ever published. No row on
  this page places her anywhere.
* **A same-day match is a date, not a location and not an intent.** Two of the ten have the aircraft
  in another country or absent entirely, and five more have it in a different city from the event.
  The pages this site publishes on the Duncan Aviation and Yingling Falcon maintenance accounts give
  an ordinary reason for a Falcon to be at Provo, Lincoln or Wichita on any given day.
* **An absence in an archive is not evidence of absence.** SITE-001, EXTRA-003 and EXTRA-005 are
  **Not heard**: the free archives were queried and held no trace for that airframe on that date.
  That is an **archive limit** — not a deletion, not suppression, and not proof the aircraft was
  elsewhere. Parked with the transponder off, outside volunteer receiver coverage, and a wrong
  claimed date are indistinguishable from the outside.
* **Where the counts differ, and they do.** This cut, run on the rule stated above, returns **10 rows
  across 7 distinct dates**. The overview's prose for its stricter version of the test prints **11
  rows resolving to six dates**, and elsewhere describes the survivors as **six dates** of which three
  involve Charlie Kirk and three are a Turning Point event with no Kirk at it. The two do not
  reconcile from this page's data alone, and neither figure is adjusted to fit the other. The
  difference is a difference of rule, not of arithmetic: this page keeps every row whose **date**
  matches — including EXTRA-005, where the aircraft is placed in Egypt, and EXTRA-002, which names no
  aircraft — while the overview's narrower figure is a **shared-field** test. Use this page for the
  rows; use the overview for the stricter claim, and treat the gap between them as the finding it is.
