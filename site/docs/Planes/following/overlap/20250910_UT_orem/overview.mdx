# 10 September 2025 — Orem, Utah

## The pairing, in one sentence

**Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University in Orem at 12:23:30 PM MDT on 10 September 2025. [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) had been parked at Provo Municipal Airport — seven miles from the courtyard — for six days and departed that morning, and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) had been parked on the same ramp for 110 days and switched its transponder on that day without ever taking off.**

**This is the only date in the entire record that is a same-day, same-airport overlap by any definition — including the sceptics'.** [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler), reducing the whole 70-plus tally to what survives strict criteria, arrives at **one**: this one.

**From 23 May to 13 September 2025 — 113 continuous days — a foreign-registered jet was parked at this field.** Every other arrival in that period, SU-BTT's September stay included, happened at an airport where SU-BND was already sitting.

**In those 113 days SU-BND flew exactly twice**, both Provo→Provo round-robins: one on **10 June** (no duration logged) and one on **5 September lasting 1 hour 8 minutes** — five days before the assassination. The 5 September sortie is the single flight in this record that nobody has adequately explained.

The counter to that, stated in the same breath: **[SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) flew the identical signature on 10 April 2025** — a 55-minute Provo→Provo round-robin. Two Egyptian jets, five months apart, each flying exactly one closed loop out of Provo, is the profile of a **post-maintenance functional check flight**, which is what an aircraft does after a heavy inspection before release. If the September loop is anomalous, consistency requires treating the April one the same way — and an April closed loop five months early fits no narrative about the assassination.

## The SU-BTT departure time is disputed three ways, and they cannot all be true

This is the most contested fact in the whole investigation and it is set out here as a dispute, not reconciled.

| Version | Claim | Source | What it would mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Wheels-up ~07:08–07:14 MT** | SU-BTT departed Provo for Wilmington roughly **five hours before** the shot | The Flightradar24 export compiled by trackers; the [Provo page](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) records **07:14 am** | The aircraft was airborne and eastbound long before anything happened |
| **(b) The "nuclear flight logs"** | Power-up at **9:05 am**, the track **goes dark**, reactivates **17 minutes after the shot**, idles until **1:29 pm** | [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)'s broadcast | The aircraft was still on the ground during the shooting with its track suppressed |
| **(c) It never left Provo at all** | Launched instead from **Dugway Proving Ground's Michael Army Airfield** with the transponder off until clear of restricted airspace **R-6404** | @ProjectConstitu / @nihilox | A different airport, a deliberate track gap, and a military field |

A fourth reading, from **@troofevades**, logs the takeoff at **~13:14 UTC** and the landing at **~16:52 UTC**. Part of the spread between these versions is a **UTC-versus-local conversion error** that has circulated uncorrected — 13:08 UTC *is* 07:08 MDT, and the same instant has been reported as both.

**This page does not pick a winner.** The versions are irreconcilable, they come from named sources, and the disagreement is itself the finding. The full treatment is on the [Candace Owens broadcasts page](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts) and the [X discussions page](/Planes/following/x_discussions).

## SU-BND's transponder

**The raw archive settles this one.** The trace for SU-BND (hex `01003E`) on 10 September 2025 was pulled from both free historical archives — `globe.airplanes.live` and `adsb.lol` — and they agree exactly. Read in the time zone Provo was actually in that day (MDT, UTC − 6):

| Event | Local time (MDT) | Relative to the shot at **12:23:30** |
|---|---|---|
| Transponder on | **10:05:35** | 2 h 18 min before |
| Transponder quiet | **11:34:09** | **49 min BEFORE** |
| Transponder back on | **13:40:53** | **1 h 17 min after** |
| Transponder off | **14:29:38** | 2 h 06 min after |

**The aircraft did not take off.** Readings of this day taken off tracker screenshots have circulated that do not match these four boundaries under any offset — a primary archive outranks a re-rendered screenshot, so this page uses the archive. Method and files: **[Flight Data Recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview)**; full treatment on the [Candace Owens broadcasts page](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts).

A transponder cycling on a parked aircraft has ordinary causes — avionics power-up, a maintenance check, ground testing. It also has no published explanation for this date. Both statements are true and both belong here.

## What else was at Provo that week

Recorded because it is publishable and because it is not aircraft data, and it should not be conflated with it:

* The **Provo airport badge access list was updated on 9/11/25** — the day after.
* **Counter-UAS equipment was reportedly tested at Provo airport between 4 and 10 September.**
* Reportedly **four to six contractors carrying claimed "US Department of Defense Liaison" badges** were dropped off and **not flown back out**.

**These are reported claims from researchers, not records this site holds.** No badge list, no test log, and no contract has been obtained. They are on the page because withholding a publishable claim is not the same as verifying it.

## The counterargument

* **A Falcon service relationship exists on the record and covers this fleet.** Duncan Aviation's Egyptian Air Force account is documented back to **1999** in a 2015 Duncan Aviation press release, and Duncan runs a station at Provo as well as its main plant at [Lincoln](/Planes/following/Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview). **Whether it covers this particular six-day stay is unestablished** — no work order or invoice for the September visit has been produced by anyone.
* **Both aircraft were already there.** Neither flew in for the event. SU-BND had been on that ramp for **110 days** by 10 September; SU-BTT arrived six days early. **Wheeler's reduction is exactly this**: the one same-day, same-airport row is a row where the jets were parked before anyone scheduled anything.
* **An aircraft departing five hours before the shot is not overwatch.** On version (a), SU-BTT was over the Midwest when it happened.
* **Neither Kirk is documented at Provo at any point.** Charlie Kirk was in **Seoul on 5–6 September**; Erika Kirk reportedly stayed in Arizona. The aircraft were at an airport near an event, not near a person.
* **ADS-B has holes, and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg, and a clean track is not proof of innocent intent.

## What we do not know

* **Which of the three departure versions is correct.** This is settleable by anyone with raw archive access and has not been settled publicly.
* **Whether SU-BTT's track was suppressed or simply misread.** No raw ADS-B export has been published alongside the claims.
* **Why SU-BND's transponder cycled**, and why the aircraft never moved.
* **Who was aboard SU-BTT's outbound leg.** [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) claims four people, one using the name *"Walid Mahmoud"* — an unverified online claim about a living person, and no wrongdoing is established by it.
* **Whether Michael Army Airfield at Dugway has any record for this date.** Nobody has requested it.
* **Where Erika Kirk actually was.** Her flight logs are reported erased — see [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).
* **This page is the 18-month run-up thread, not the day-of timeline.** The minute-by-minute of 10 September is [Sept10 Flight Timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline)'s job, and the N1098L, N888KG and drone threads are **separate claims about separate aircraft** — link across, never blend.

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

### This settles the three-way departure dispute above, from primary data

The section [above](#the-su-btt-departure-time-is-disputed-three-ways-and-they-cannot-all-be-true) sets out three irreconcilable accounts of when and from where SU-BTT left. **They are no longer irreconcilable.** Both archives independently record:

| Event | UTC | Local (MDT) |
|---|---|---|
| First fix of the day, **on the ground at Provo** | **13:07:53** | **07:07:53** |
| Closest approach to Provo, still on the ground | **13:08:57** | **07:08:57** |
| **First airborne fix** (4,475 ft) | **13:14:03.82** | **07:14:03** |
| Last fix flagged *on the ground* | **13:13:16** | **07:13:16** |
| Last fix of the day, **on approach to Wilmington, Delaware** | **16:50:37** | 12:50:37 EDT |

Against the three versions:

* **Version (a) — wheels-up around 07:08 to 07:14 MT — is corroborated exactly.** The aircraft is on the ground at Provo at 07:07:53 MDT and airborne at 07:14:03 MDT.
* **Version (b) — power-up at 9:05 am, track dark through the shooting, reactivating 17 minutes after it and idling until 1:29 pm — is not supported by the archives.** By 16:50:37 UTC the aircraft is on final approach to Wilmington, Delaware. The shooting was at 18:23:30 UTC. An airframe over Delaware at 16:50 UTC cannot be idling on the Provo ramp at 18:40 UTC.
* **Version (c) — that it never left Provo and launched instead from Michael Army Airfield at Dugway with the transponder off — is not supported either.** The day's first fixes are at Provo, on the ground, and the trace runs from there. No Dugway fix appears.
* **The fourth reading, ~13:14 UTC takeoff and ~16:52 UTC landing, was right all along.** It is version (a) stated in UTC.

**And that is exactly the error this page already named.** The spread between these accounts is in large part a **UTC-versus-local conversion error**: 13:14:03 UTC *is* 07:14:03 MDT, and the same instant has been published as both, then argued over as though it were two. **Primary data does not split the difference between the three versions. It picks one, and it is the least dramatic one.**

### SU-BND did not move

Every fix for SU-BND on 9 and 10 September resolves to Provo and to nothing else — one airport in the day's list, first and last fix the same field, **on the ground on both**. The primary data is consistent with an aircraft that sat on that ramp and never flew, which is what the claim says.

**What it does not settle: the transponder cycle times.** The archives show the airframe was *heard*, which is what a live transponder produces. **They are not a switch-on and switch-off log.** Nothing here corroborates or contradicts the specific cycle times published for that day, whose time base was never stated. That dispute is unchanged.

**This confirms the AIRCRAFT half and nothing else.** Position data locates an airframe. It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere, it does not establish that anyone aboard knew where either of them was, and it does not establish who tasked the flight. The Kirk half of this pairing is exactly as strong, or as weak, as it was before we pulled the data.

**Say it once more on this page in particular, because this is the page it matters most on.** Confirming SU-BTT on the Provo ramp at 07:08 MDT and SU-BND beside it confirms **where two airframes were**. It does not establish that anyone aboard knew about the UVU event, it does not establish tasking in any direction, and it does not bear on who killed Charlie Kirk. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** This directory is the 18-month run-up; the minute-by-minute of the day is [Sept10 Flight Timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline)'s job, and the N1098L, N888KG and drone threads are **separate claims about separate aircraft**.

**And Erika's side is the weak side.** Her flight logs are reported erased, so any Erika pairing rests on the foreign aircraft's track plus a *claimed* location for her. See [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

**The counterargument is untouched, and confirmation makes one half of it stronger.** Both aircraft were **already there** — SU-BND for 110 days, SU-BTT for six — and the primary data shows neither flew in for the event. Duncan Aviation has a Provo facility and has held the Egyptian Air Force account since 1999. **An aircraft airborne and eastbound five hours before the shot is not overwatch**, and the confirmed departure time is the version that says so most plainly.
