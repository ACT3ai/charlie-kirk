# 10 September 2025 — Provo then Wilmington, UT/DE

**On 10 September 2025 primary ADS-B position data places [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) on the ground at Provo Municipal Airport (KPVU), about seven miles from the Utah Valley University courtyard where Charlie Kirk was shot at 12:23:30 pm MDT. The closest recovered fix is 1.23 km from the field. The same day's trace ends at Wilmington, Delaware.**

**The audit scored this row inaccurate, and the primary data shows why — and what survives it.** The sheet gave the wrong origin, naming Paris or Lincoln for a departure that actually began at Provo. The Provo→Wilmington→Cairo substance is confirmed; the origin string is not. **This is also the one row of the register that claims both Kirks at once.**

**It places nobody aboard.** A recovered trace locates an airframe and nothing else. No archive anywhere puts Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk on any aircraft, and pulling this data did not make the person half of the claim testable. And the departure clock is contested three ways — roughly 07:08–07:14 MDT, a 9:05 am power-up with a track gap, and a launch from Dugway instead — versions that cannot all be true. They are laid out against each other below rather than reconciled.

## Counterargument

* **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release. Its headquarters plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska**; it has a second facility at **Provo, Utah**. Landings at Lincoln, at Omaha (a fifteen-minute hop from Lincoln), at Wichita (Yingling Aviation, an FAA Part 145 **Dassault Falcon** service centre) and at Provo are all what that relationship produces.
* **The rotation never varies.** Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US entry field → a short repositioning hop → sit five to eight days → [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) → Cairo. Three airframes flew it identically for three years. **A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.**
* **Wilmington appears on the outbound leg only, twenty-one times, never inbound.** It is a customs and fuel stop being scored as a destination.
* **ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of intent.
* **The rotations continued after Charlie Kirk's death.** SU-BTV flew the identical Lincoln rotation 5–11 October 2025. If the flights were tasked to follow a man, they would have stopped when he died.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Confirmed. The claimed aircraft really was at the claimed field.** SU-BTT was tracked to within **1.23 km of KPVU** at **2025-09-10T13:08:57.280Z** on 10 September 2025, **on the ground**. The trace is held by both archives, agreeing.

That day the airframe was first heard at KPVU/PVU (Provo Municipal Airport, Provo UT) 1.37km and last heard at KILG/ILG (Wilmington Airport, Wilmington DE) 1.75km.

**This confirms the aircraft half of the pairing and nothing else. It does not place Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere.** An overlap needs both halves. The Kirk half of this row is unchanged by anything below, and it is checked in the sections above.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2025-09-10 **(claimed)** | 200 | 200 | **yes** | 1.23 km (on ground) |
| 2025-09-11 | 200 | 200 | **yes** | 3,090.41 km |

**The times, stated and not interpreted.** SU-BTT's first fix at Provo that day is **13:07:53 UTC (07:07:53 MDT)**; its closest approach to the field is **1.23 km on the ground at 13:08:57 UTC**; **wheels-up is 13:14:03 UTC (07:14:03 MDT)**; and it was last tracked on approach to Wilmington, Delaware at **16:50:37 UTC (12:50:37 EDT)**, roughly ninety minutes before the shooting. Both archives agree on these to within ten milliseconds. ADS-B records a fix, not a rotation: the last fix flagged **on the ground** is **13:13:16 UTC (07:13:16 MDT)** and the first fix **not** flagged on the ground is **13:14:03.82 UTC (07:14:03 MDT)** at 4,475 ft, so the wheels left the runway somewhere inside that **47-second window**. Both archives agree to within ten milliseconds. **This settles the three-way disputed departure time from primary data.** What the times mean is not something a trace can tell anyone, and this page does not say.

**The method, so a reader can repeat it.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and airplanes.live — were queried for SU-BTT on 10 September 2025 and on the day either side, and every returned position that was on the ground or below 4,000 ft was measured against the published coordinates of KPVU. Within 15 km counts as at the field and within 80 km as the same metro; **nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is printed beside every field name rather than the name alone.

**The files.** This row's extract is `site/docs/Planes/following/overlap/20250910_UTDE_provo_then_wilmington_owens_041/data/adsb_verification.json`; the full 85-row run, its method string and its tally are in `site/docs/Planes/following/apis/public_open_source/data/overlap_verification/overlap_verification.json`. Both were written 2026-08-24T17:36:21.953Z.

**Against the audit verdict already on this page.** The audit recorded on this page marks the row **inaccurate**, having checked it against paid flight-history archives. **Primary position data disagrees, and we leave the disagreement standing.** Both readings are published here; we are not quietly overwriting one with the other, and a reader who trusts the paid archives over the free ones is entitled to say so.

**What this check cannot do.** It locates an airframe. It does not identify who was aboard, it does not establish why the aircraft was where it was, and it does not distinguish a shadowing flight from the maintenance rotation set out in the counterargument above. **Absence in an archive is not proof of a covert leg, and presence is not proof of intent.**
