## Counterargument

* **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release. Its headquarters plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska**; it has a second facility at **Provo, Utah**. Landings at Lincoln, at Omaha (a fifteen-minute hop from Lincoln), at Wichita (Yingling Aviation, an FAA Part 145 **Dassault Falcon** service centre) and at Provo are all what that relationship produces.
* **The rotation never varies.** Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US entry field → a short repositioning hop → sit five to eight days → [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) → Cairo. Three airframes flew it identically for three years. **A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.**
* **Wilmington appears on the outbound leg only, twenty-one times, never inbound.** It is a customs and fuel stop being scored as a destination.
* **ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of intent.
* **The rotations continued after Charlie Kirk's death.** SU-BTV flew the identical Lincoln rotation 5–11 October 2025. If the flights were tasked to follow a man, they would have stopped when he died.

## What we do not know

* **Who was aboard.** No manifest, FBO log, crew list, or airport badge record has been obtained for any leg of any of these flights, at any location, by anyone.
* **Whether the aircraft entered a maintenance hangar.** No Duncan Aviation or Yingling work order or invoice has been produced either — **the maintenance explanation is as unevidenced by primary records as the shadowing one.**
* **Where Erika Kirk actually was.** No dated itinerary for her has ever been published, by the compilers or by anyone else.
* **What time of day any event ran.** Without event times, "same day" cannot be narrowed into a sequence on any date except 10 September 2025.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used.** The Kirk side of nearly every row here is a claimed location, not a tail number.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

Run 24 August 2026. Every claimed row of the sheet was tested the same way, this one included.

**Refuted on the Goose Bay leg — and this row is where two of our own runs disagree.**

:::warning[Two 26 August 2026 runs reach different verdicts on this row. Both are published.]

**Not in dispute, and it is the part the earlier verdict buried:** the same-day trace **departs KCPS,
St. Louis Downtown, at 20:02Z**. That is the leg the row names first, and **primary position data
confirms it happened.**

**In dispute is the Goose Bay leg.** The trace ends over La Tuque, **Quebec** at 22:10Z at FL410,
heading north-east, **1,063 km from Goose Bay** — not the 3,057 km published here before, which was
the distance at a different point in the day. The aircraft is next seen over **Ireland at 04:18Z on
10 May**.

* **The automated same-day run scores this ELSEWHERE** — the closest recovered position is 1,063 km
  from the field the row names, and that is the rule applied to all 85 rows without exception.
* **The fix-by-fix read calls it untested** — a Goose Bay fuel stop fits that six-hour gap exactly,
  and the archives neither show it nor exclude it. Coverage runs out over Quebec while the aircraft
  is still tracking towards Labrador.

**Neither reading is folded into the other.** The site's headline tally uses the automated run, which
counts this row among its three refutations. See
[the correction](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Overlap-Recovery).

:::

On 9 May 2023 SU-BND's track begins in the **St Louis metro**, which the row names — Aero Estates
Airport, Belleville, Illinois at 1.78 km, with the departure out of KCPS at 20:02Z — and ends over
Quebec. The next day it is over Belmullet, Ireland and then Mouy, France. Both archives agree.

**This is a misattribution, not an invention.** The St Louis half of the row is corroborated and the eastbound transatlantic routing is real; the Goose Bay half is not in the data. A Labrador fuel stop is the obvious innocent reading of what the compiler was looking at, but the archives do not record one.

The **bold** row is the date the sheet claims. The other two are the day either side, which is the window the compilation itself used.

**Method, so a reader can repeat it.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and airplanes.live — were queried for **SU-BND** on **9 May 2023** and the day either side, with positions resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. Positions on the ground or below 4,000 ft were measured against the claimed field's coordinates: within **15 km** counts as at the field, within **80 km** as the same metro area — and nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record.

**Our verdict disagrees with the audit verdict already recorded for this row, and we are not resolving it silently.** `overlaps.csv` records this row as **audited accurate**. Primary position data refutes it. That is a correction running against the direction people expect from this directory — the audit and the compiler agreed on this row, and the position data agrees with neither.

**Erika Kirk's side is unaffected by any of this.** Her flight logs are [reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), so there is no itinerary for a confirmed or refuted aircraft position to be checked against. Testing the aircraft half does not make the Erika half testable.

## Where this page fits

This row is the clearest example in the directory of a claim that is half true: [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) really did begin 9 May 2023 in the [St Louis metro](/Planes/following/StLouis_KSTL_2022-12-20_to_2025-02-23/overview) and really did head east across the Atlantic, but the [Labrador fuel stop](/Planes/following/GooseBay_CYYR_2023-05-09_to_2025-09-13/overview) the sheet names is in neither [free archive](/Planes/following/apis/public_open_source/knowledge) — the trace runs out over Quebec, 1,063 km short of it and heading the right way, and picks up again over Ireland six hours later. The St. Louis departure is now **confirmed by primary position data** — that much the earlier verdict of *refuted* obscured entirely. The Goose Bay leg is where two of our own runs part: the automated same-day rule scores it **elsewhere** at 1,063 km, the fix-by-fix read calls it **untested**, and the [overlap index](/Planes/following/overlap/overview) records the disagreement rather than choosing. That distinction is worth carrying into the rest of the case, where the gap between a wrong detail and a fabricated one decides how you read the [foreign-intelligence claims](/Theories/Foreign_Intelligence_Claims), the [Egyptian operations reported at Provo](/intelligence/Egyptian_Foreign_Ops) and the [cover-up allegations](/CoverUp/overview) alike. Next: the [overseas-overlap claim](/Planes/following/Erika_Overseas_Overlaps) — X accounts said the jets matched Erika Kirk on international legs, no dated overseas pairing was ever published, and her [flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), so nobody outside can test it.
