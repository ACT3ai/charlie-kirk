* **Tyler Robinson's identity as the charged suspect was not public on 29 April 2025.** The shooting is 10 September 2025. A search for his apartment address four-plus months earlier, if it happened, is a different claim than a search after he was named. The posts do not address that.

* **The same story family produces a second date** — 11 July 2025, Omaha, "Erika Frantze" in Hebrew — [EXTRA-004](../20250711_NE_omaha_extra_004/overview). Two unsourced in-jet Google claims do not corroborate each other.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Cannot be checked from free data — and the reason is the row, not the archive.** The tail cell for this row reads literally `UNKNOWN`. The posts say "the Egyptian jet"; they do not say which one. Every free ADS-B archive is keyed on the ICAO hex code an aircraft transmits, so a claim with no airframe cannot be entered into one. **There is nothing to query.**

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

**This is not a coverage problem.** The archives cover April 2025 for the fleet — the same 85-row run pulled full traces for these airframes on dates either side of it. **The data exists. The claim does not name an aircraft to look it up with.**

**It agrees with what this page already says.** `overlaps.csv` records the row as not assessed, this site's own `flights.csv` holds no SU-BTT or SU-BND stay in Nebraska on 29 April 2025, and the documented 2025 Nebraska rotations are 20 July and 17 August. **A primary-data pass adds one more independent way of saying the same thing: there is no airframe here to test.**
