**This page is one row of the overlap sheet that produced the "73 times" figure — reconstructed, sourced, and checked.** It is not a finding by this site. It records what the compilation claimed for this date, who it says was there, and what an independent audit of the flight archive found when it looked.

## What the audit found

**NOT ASSESSABLE — ARCHIVE GAP.** The auditor reported that the flight archive **does not reach back this far**, so this row could be neither confirmed nor refuted. An unassessable row is not a confirmed one, and it is not a refuted one either.

## Counterargument

* **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release. Its headquarters plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska**; it has a second facility at **Provo, Utah**. Landings at Lincoln, at Omaha (a fifteen-minute hop from Lincoln), at Wichita (Yingling Aviation, an FAA Part 145 **Dassault Falcon** service centre) and at Provo are all what that relationship produces.
* **The rotation never varies.** Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US entry field → a short repositioning hop → sit five to eight days → [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12) → Cairo. Three airframes flew it identically for three years. **A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.**
* **Wilmington appears on the outbound leg only, twenty-one times, never inbound.** It is a customs and fuel stop being scored as a destination.
* **ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of intent.
* **The rotations continued after Charlie Kirk's death.** SU-BTV flew the identical Lincoln rotation 5–11 October 2025. If the flights were tasked to follow a man, they would have stopped when he died.

## What we do not know

* **Who was aboard.** No manifest, FBO log, crew list, or airport badge record has been obtained for any leg of any of these flights, at any location, by anyone.
* **Whether the aircraft entered a maintenance hangar.** No Duncan Aviation or Yingling work order or invoice has been produced either — **the maintenance explanation is as unevidenced by primary records as the shadowing one.**
* **Where Erika Kirk actually was.** No dated itinerary for her has ever been published, by the compilers or by anyone else.
* **What time of day any event ran.** Without event times, "same day" cannot be narrowed into a sequence on any date except 10 September 2025.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used.** The Kirk side of nearly every row here is a claimed location, not a tail number.

## Where this row sits in the whole count

This is **one of 78 reconstructed rows**. Across all of them the sheet claims **60 overlaps with Erika Kirk alone, 13 with Charlie Kirk alone, 1 with both of them together, and 4 with a Turning Point event at which neither Kirk is claimed to have been present.** Independent audit found the aircraft position **correct on 18 rows and wrong on 44**. **Six dates** survive this directory's same-day-or-adjacent test. The arithmetic is set out in full on [The 73 overlaps](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) and [Overlap dates](/Planes/following/overlap/overview).
