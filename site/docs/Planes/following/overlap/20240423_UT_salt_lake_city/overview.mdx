## The pairing, in one sentence

**Charlie Kirk did a *Prove Me Wrong* event at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City on 23 April 2024, and [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) landed at Provo — 45 miles south — at 12:13 pm the same day, while [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) had already been parked on that ramp since the 19th.**

**This is the tightest sourced pairing anywhere in the pre-2025 record.** Same day, same metropolitan region, documented on both sides, and the flight leg is marked **accurate** by the independent audit that dissolved most of the rest of the sheet.

## Why this one survives when the others do not

The [line-by-line audit](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) of the overlap spreadsheet is the most damaging document in this whole investigation for the shadowing claim — it puts the aircraft in Egypt on nine claimed Wichita dates, finds duplicate rows counted twice, and invalidates the three earliest 2022 entries outright.

**It marks this flight accurate.** Both sides of the pairing exist, both are dated, and the aircraft is where the claim says it is.

What that establishes and what it does not:

* **Established:** an Egyptian-registered Falcon 7X flew Cairo → Paris → Williston ND → Provo and touched down at 12:13 pm on a day Charlie Kirk was speaking 45 miles away, at an airport where a second Egyptian-registered jet was already parked.
* **Not established:** that anyone aboard knew he was there, that the flight was tasked, or who ordered it. **No public record establishes tasking in either direction, and this page does not assert it.**

## The counterargument

* **Duncan Aviation operates a facility at Provo**, and the same Egyptian Air Force maintenance relationship that explains [Lincoln](/Planes/following/Lincoln_KLNK_2024-02-09_to_2025-10-11/overview) — a contract trackers themselves date to **1999** — reaches Utah.
* **An 84-day stay is a maintenance stay.** SU-BND sat at Provo for roughly twelve weeks in 2024, then repeated the pattern with a **113-day** stay in 2025. That is a heavy-check duration, not a surveillance posture.
* **45 miles is a different airport in a different county.** Provo Municipal is not the airport serving a University of Utah event; Salt Lake City International is, and the Kirk party's own aircraft is undocumented for this date.
* **SU-BTT's five-day dwell matches its dwell everywhere else** — the same Cairo → Paris → US field → sit five or six days → Wilmington → Cairo rotation it flew at Wichita, Lincoln and St. Louis.
* **A single overlap is not evidence.** This page exists because it is one date in a claimed pattern. Standing alone it has an innocent reading and a reader who will find it.

## What we do not know

* **What time of day Kirk spoke.** SU-BTT landed at 12:13 pm; without the event time the "same day" cannot be tightened into a sequence.
* **Whether either Kirk ever went near Provo.** Nothing places Charlie or Erika Kirk at KPVU on any date in this record.
* **Who was aboard either aircraft**, and what work — if any — was performed during the stays.
* **Whether SU-BND's reported 12 July 2024 departure is right.** It is carried as *reported*, not audited.
* **Erika Kirk's location on this date is entirely unrecorded.** Her flight logs are reported erased, which is why the Erika side of the 73-overlap tally cannot be reproduced from any public source — see [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Confirmed.** Both free archives hold SU-BTT on 23 April 2024, they agree with each other, and they place the aircraft **on the ground at Provo Municipal Airport (KPVU) — 0.00 km, at 18:13:11 UTC.** The day's 659 fixes begin 4.05 km from Paris-Le Bourget and end 1.42 km from Provo.

**18:13:11 UTC is 12:13:11 MDT.** The time published for this arrival on this site and by the trackers is **12:13 pm**. Primary position data matches it to the second — a rare thing anywhere in this material, and worth recording precisely because so much of the rest of it turns on UTC-versus-local conversion errors.

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

**The day before is confirmed too, and it confirms the rotation rather than the pairing.** On 22 April the same aircraft is tracked from **Almaza Air Force Base, Cairo** — an Egyptian military field, not Cairo International — to **Paris-Orly**. Cairo, then Paris, then a US field the next day is the rotation this fleet flew everywhere, and the primary data shows it doing exactly that into Provo.

**This confirms the AIRCRAFT half and nothing else.** Position data locates an airframe. It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere, it does not establish that anyone aboard knew where either of them was, and it does not establish who tasked the flight. The Kirk half of this pairing is exactly as strong, or as weak, as it was before we pulled the data.

**Two things this pull does NOT establish, and they matter:**

* **SU-BND's presence at Provo on this date is not corroborated.** Neither archive holds a trace for that airframe on any of the three days, although both cover them. The claimed 84-day stay beginning 19 April rests on the researcher record, not on position data we hold. **Absence is not proof.** (The neighbouring row [OWENS-025](../20240419_UT_provo_owens_025/overview) *is* confirmed for SU-BND's 19 April arrival at 0.05 km — so the arrival is anchored even where these three days are silent.)
* **The claimed Williston, North Dakota entry stop does not appear in the trace.** The day's airport list resolves to Le Bourget, Charles de Gaulle and Provo, and no North Dakota field. That is a hole to be filled, **not a refutation** — the archives carry gaps, and a fix missing from a volunteer network is not a leg that did not happen.

**The counterargument is untouched.** Duncan Aviation has a Provo facility and has held the Egyptian Air Force account since 1999. A Falcon 7X landing at Provo is what that contract produces. Confirming the landing to the second confirms **where an airframe was**, and the University of Utah is 45 miles from that ramp.

**This agrees with the row's existing status.** `overlaps.csv` records it accurate, the independent audit marked the flight accurate from paid archives, and primary position data now agrees with both — including on the minute.

This row is one of **85 checked against primary ADS-B, first on 24 August 2026 and again — fix by fix — on 26 August 2026**. The corrected result: **25 corroborated at the claimed field, 1 same-metro-wrong-field, 3 refuted**, 37 asked-and-empty, 10 with no archive coverage, 4 naming no aircraft, and 5 naming no date. The first pass reported 12 refutations; **9 were retracted** on the re-read — six had been scored against the wrong calendar day, and two against the shorter of two archives while the longer one put the aircraft at the claimed field.
