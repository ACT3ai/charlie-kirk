## The pairing, weighed

**This is the strongest Kansas pairing in the record and it points the wrong way.**

On the one day Charlie Kirk is documented in Kansas, the aircraft **left**. If the pattern is surveillance, an aircraft departing the state on the morning of the target's appearance is a strange way to conduct it. The aircraft had by then been on the ground at Wichita for **five days**, arriving on 29 March — well before a 3 April appearance in Lawrence was operationally relevant to anything.

There is a second Kansas claim that does **not** qualify for this directory and should be named so it is not confused with this one: a **TPUSA Faith "Kingdom to the Capitol" event in Topeka on 23 April 2023**, three days after SU-BTT departed Wichita, 135 miles away. **Three days is outside the one-day rule this directory runs on.** It is the archetype of what a ±3-day window and state-level matching produce.

## The counterargument

* **Wichita is the "Air Capital of the World."** Yingling Aviation at KICT is an **FAA Part 145 Dassault Falcon service centre**. A Falcon 7X landing at Wichita is the single most ordinary thing a Falcon 7X can do.
* **The aircraft flew the same rotation it flew everywhere else** — Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US field → sit four to six days → Wilmington → Cairo — and it did not vary for this date.
* **The aircraft departed.** This cannot be read as overwatch of an event that had not yet happened in a city 160 miles away.
* **The Wichita tally is the clearest illustration of how these counts inflate.** A claim of *"no fewer than 8 stops"* by SU-BTT at Wichita resolves against the audit into **three confirmed visits** — November 2022, April 2023, March–April 2024 — plus **nine claimed Wichita entries on dates the aircraft was in Egypt** (indices 22, 31, 32, 33, 34, 45, 51, 54, 59, 61, 63), **two of which are literal duplicates of two others** (61 duplicates 31; 63 duplicates 33).

## What we do not know

* **What time of day Kirk spoke at KU.** Without it, "same day" cannot be narrowed to hours. The aircraft was wheels-up at 8:43 am regardless.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used** to reach Lawrence, and into which field. No Kirk-side tail number is published for this date.
* **Whether SU-BTT entered Yingling's facility** during the five-day stay. No work order or FBO record has been obtained.
* **Who was aboard on either leg.**

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

**A real US day, and the wrong field.** This is the same claim as [OWENS-024](../20240403_KS_wichita_lawrence_owens_024/overview), and both are refuted by the same trace. The aircraft was not in Kansas at any point the archives can see on 3 April 2024; it was crossing the country eastbound and landing in Delaware.

**The limit of that refutation, stated plainly.** The archived trace **begins over Missouri, already airborne and already eastbound.** It therefore does not record where the day started, and it neither confirms nor excludes an early-morning Wichita departure of the kind this page describes — 8:43 am, from a five-day stay that began on 29 March. **What is refuted is any claim that the aircraft was AT Wichita during the tracked window.** What is untouched is the 29 March arrival, which falls before the archives' reach for this airframe.

**Which is to say the primary data pushes this page's own conclusion further, not back.** The page already says that on the one day Charlie Kirk is documented in Kansas the aircraft **left**. Position data says it did more than leave: by mid-afternoon it was on final approach to Wilmington, Delaware, roughly 1,850 km away. **If the pattern is surveillance, this is not what it looks like.**

**This confirms the AIRCRAFT half and nothing else.** Position data locates an airframe. It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere, it does not establish that anyone aboard knew where either of them was, and it does not establish who tasked the flight. The Kirk half of this pairing is exactly as strong, or as weak, as it was before we pulled the data.

**Where this leaves the audit's verdict.** `overlaps.csv` records this row **partial**. Against primary position data it is **refuted** as a Wichita co-location. That is a correction to this site's own register and it is being made here rather than left implied — **stating our own refutations is what makes the twenty-four confirmations worth anything.**
