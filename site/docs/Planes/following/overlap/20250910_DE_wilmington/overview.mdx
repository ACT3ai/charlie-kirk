# 10 September 2025 — Wilmington, Delaware

## The pairing, in one sentence

**[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) flew Provo to Wilmington, Delaware on the day Charlie Kirk was killed, stayed overnight and left for Cairo the next morning — and one compilation spreadsheet places Erika Kirk in *"Nebraska/Delaware"* for TPUSA media appearances that same day, a claim she has contradicted.**

**The Erika side of this pairing is the weakest kind of claim in the whole directory, and this page exists mainly to say so precisely.** It is here because a Delaware location for Erika Kirk on 10 September has been publicly asserted and a foreign-registered jet did land in Delaware that day. Both halves need to be visible before anyone can weigh them.

## The Kirk / TPUSA side — and why it is contested

Three things follow, and each of them matters more than the claim itself:

* **"Nebraska/Delaware" is not a location.** It is two states 1,200 miles apart offered as alternatives in a cell that should hold one place.
* **No aircraft in this fleet was in Nebraska on 10 September 2025.** The Lincoln rotation ran **17–23 August** and did not resume until **5–11 October**. Half of the claimed location has no aircraft behind it at all.
* **The Delaware half has an aircraft, but it is the aircraft's ordinary destination.** Wilmington appears on the **outbound leg of twenty-one rotations across three years, and only ever the outbound leg.**

**This is the concrete form of the rule that runs through this whole directory: Erika's side is the weak side.** Her flight logs are reported erased, so nearly every "Erika overlap" in the 73-count tally is the *foreign* aircraft's track plus a claimed location for her. See [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

## The 920 North King Street claim

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) has claimed that **only four people were aboard the Provo → Wilmington leg**, one of them a foreign dignitary using the name **"Walid Mahmoud"**, with an alleged transfer to **920 North King Street, One Rodney Square, Suite 404** — a building publicly associated with DEA, DOJ and Secret Service offices.

What this page can say about it:

* **The flight happened.** The audit confirms it, with the origin corrected to Provo.
* **The passenger count, the name, and the transfer are unverified.** No manifest, FBO record, or badge log has been produced.
* **The building link is a researcher inference, not a manifest.** Nothing connects an aircraft landing at KILG to any address in Wilmington except the inference itself.
* **Walid Mahmoud is a living person.** The name is an unverified online claim and **no wrongdoing is established by it.**

The same name appears in the record eight months earlier, aboard a **27 May 2025** Provo arrival — and **even that attribution is disputed**, with one source recording that arrival as SU-BTU and another as SU-BTT or SU-BTV. See the [Provo page](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview).

## The counterargument

* **Wilmington is a customs and fuel stop and nothing else.** Across three years, **twenty-one appearances, every single one on the outbound leg.** These aircraft do not go to Delaware; they clear Delaware on the way home.
* **The 10 September leg is the same leg the aircraft flew after every other US stay** — Wichita → Wilmington → Cairo, Lincoln → Wilmington → Cairo, Provo → Wilmington → Cairo. It did not vary.
* **Not one of the twenty-one Wilmington appearances has a Kirk or TPUSA pairing offered by anybody** — including this one, unless the spreadsheet cell is accepted at face value.
* **The Erika-in-Delaware claim comes from the same document class the audit dismantled.** A sheet that placed the aircraft in Egypt on nine claimed Wichita dates is not a strong source for a person's whereabouts.
* **Erika Kirk has publicly disputed it.** Her account is on the record; the spreadsheet's is not attributed to anyone.

## What we do not know

* **Where Erika Kirk actually was on 10 September 2025.** Neither version is documented.
* **What "TPUSA media appearances" refers to** — no broadcast, venue, or timestamp has been cited for it.
* **Who was aboard SU-BTT**, and whether anyone left the airport.
* **The exact arrival time at Wilmington.** The published departure times conflict, so the arrival inherits the conflict.
* **Whether any FBO at KILG holds a record for this aircraft.** Nobody has requested one.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Confirmed — the aircraft leg, in full, on both days.** Both free archives hold SU-BTT on 10 and 11 September 2025, they agree with each other, and they record the whole rotation this page describes.

| Date | Tail | adsb.lol | airplanes.live | Heard | Closest approach to KILG |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-09-09 | SU-BTT | 404 | 404 | no — archives cover the date | not measurable |
| 2025-09-10 | **SU-BTT** | 200 | 200 | **yes — both agree** | **1.75 km, 16:50:37 UTC**, on approach (1,229 fixes) |
| 2025-09-11 | **SU-BTT** | 200 | 200 | **yes — both agree** | **0.92 km, 11:55:36 UTC, on the ground** (1,399 fixes) |

* **10 September.** The day's trace begins **on the ground at Provo (1.37 km)** and ends **1.75 km from Wilmington Airport**, descending across Maryland and Delaware — `KEVY` at 7.35 km, `1DE5` at 2.48 km, `DE34` at 5.45 km, `KILG` at 11.09 km, all below 4,000 ft. **Closest approach to Wilmington: 1.75 km at 16:50:37 UTC — that is 12:50:37 EDT.** Charlie Kirk was shot at Utah Valley University at 12:23:30 PM MDT, which is 18:23:30 UTC. The Wilmington approach fix is therefore **about 93 minutes earlier**. That is the arithmetic; this page does not read anything into it.
* **11 September.** The day's trace begins **on the ground at Wilmington (0.95 km)** and ends **4.42 km from Almaza Air Force Base, Cairo** — an Egyptian military field, not Cairo International. The last on-ground fix at Wilmington is **11:55:36 UTC, which is 07:55:36 EDT**. The departure has been published as **7:56 am** and as **~11:56 UTC**; primary data matches both, and they were always the same instant stated in two time bases.

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

**This confirms the AIRCRAFT half and nothing else.** Position data locates an airframe. It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere, it does not establish that anyone aboard knew where either of them was, and it does not establish who tasked the flight. The Kirk half of this pairing is exactly as strong, or as weak, as it was before we pulled the data.

**And Erika's side is the weak side.** Her flight logs are reported erased, so any Erika pairing rests on the foreign aircraft's track plus a *claimed* location for her. See [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

**Which is the whole point of this page.** The aircraft half of this pairing is now the best-evidenced thing on it — 2,628 position fixes across two days, from two independent archives that agree. **The Erika half is unchanged and is still a spreadsheet cell reading "Nebraska/Delaware", against her own account that she was home in Arizona.** Confirming a jet landed at Wilmington does nothing whatever to put a person there, and the gap between the two halves is now wider than it was, not narrower.

**The counterargument is untouched and is strengthened by the second day.** Wilmington appears on the **outbound leg of twenty-one rotations across three years and never on the inbound**. The trace for 11 September shows exactly that function: overnight at Wilmington, then straight out to Cairo. **These aircraft do not go to Delaware; they clear Delaware on the way home.** No manifest, FBO record or badge log has been obtained for either leg, and the passenger and address claims attached to this flight remain unverified.

**Where this leaves the audit's verdict.** `overlaps.csv` records this row **partial**. Against primary position data the **flight is confirmed end to end**, with the origin firmly at Provo. That is an upgrade to the aircraft half only; the row's weakness was never the aeroplane.

## Where this page fits

This page exists to state a weak claim precisely: [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) genuinely flew [Provo](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13/overview) to [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) on the day Charlie Kirk was killed and left for [Cairo](/Planes/following/Cairo_HECA_2022-11-13_to_2025-10-12/overview) the next morning, while the Erika Kirk half is a spreadsheet cell reading "Nebraska/Delaware" — two states 1,200 miles apart — that she has contradicted, and [her flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) so nothing can check either version. The 920 North King Street allegation attached to this flight — four passengers, a name, a transfer into a building associated with federal offices — is unverified in every part, the person named is living and no wrongdoing is established, and claims of this class have already produced [a defamation suit involving Candace Owens](/Defamation/Brian_Harpole_Candace_Defemation). The aircraft half of that day belongs on [Orem](/Planes/following/overlap/20250910_UT_orem/overview), where the departure is published three irreconcilable ways — wheels-up at 07:14 am, a 9:05 am power-up with the track going dark, or never from Provo at all — and this site declines to pick a winner; [her own broadcasts](/People/candace-owens) are where two of those versions come from.
