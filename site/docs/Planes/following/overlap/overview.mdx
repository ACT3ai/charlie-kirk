# Overlap dates

**On 10 September 2025 — the day Charlie Kirk was killed — primary ADS-B position data places two Egyptian-registered jets on the ground at [Provo Municipal Airport (KPVU)](/Planes/Airports/KPVU), about 5.4 miles from the Utah Valley University courtyard.** [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) at **1.23 km** from the field, [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) at **1.29 km**, both transmitting with the on-ground flag set. Not a tracking-site screenshot and not one person's reading of another person's reading — recovered trace files, measured fix by fix. [Open that date &rarr;](./20250910_UT_orem/overview)

**SU-BTT had been parked there since 4 September, six days before, at 0.16 km from the runway.** [SU-BND had arrived on 23 May and sat for 113 days.](./20250523_UT_provo_owens_035/overview) **SU-BND was still at Provo on 13 September, three days after the assassination**, and left that afternoon for Goose Bay, Paris and Cairo. Four recorded ground contacts by foreign state-registered aircraft at one small Utah field, bracketing the killing: [4 September](./20250904_UT_provo_owens_040/overview) · [10 September, SU-BTT](./20250910_UTDE_provo_then_wilmington_owens_041/overview) · [10 September, SU-BND](./20250910_UT_provo_extra_006/overview) · [13 September](./20250913_UT_provo_owens_042/overview).

**And the register those dates sit in holds up better than its critics said it would. Of the 29 claimed rows primary position data can actually decide, 25 put the aircraft at the field the claim names.** Three are contradicted. One is right metro, wrong field. This is the first time the sheet has been tested against position data rather than against another reading of a flight-tracking website.

**A widely-quoted X audit reported the opposite result, and where it can be checked it is the one that fails.** That audit marked **44 rows inaccurate** and said 66% of the location claims were wrong with 60% putting the jets on the wrong continent. **Primary position data reaches only 5 of those 44 negative verdicts. On those 5 the audit is wrong on 3 and right on 2.** The remaining 39 rest on subscription archives and an unpublished method; they are untested, not refuted, and this page says so plainly rather than counting them as wins.

**What none of this does is put a person aboard.** ADS-B locates an airframe. No archive anywhere places Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk on any of these aircraft, and Erika Kirk's side of the ledger — 61 of the 85 rows — rests on an itinerary [nobody has ever published](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). The full verdict arithmetic, every counterargument, the innocent maintenance explanations, and the disputed tallies are all further down this page, in full and unsoftened.

## Six against seventy-three

The published tallies are far larger than this list, they disagree with each other, and none of them has been reconciled:

* **73 / ~23** — the Owens-team headline: 73 overlaps with Erika, about 23 with Charlie.
* **72** — @JustTheTweets17, 18 November 2025.
* **70+ / "nearly 70"** — Shadow of Ezra, David Nino Rodriguez, later restatements.
* **68 / 29** — Morgan Ariel, 17 November 2025: both jets, 2022 – September 2025, 29 also including Charlie.
* **65–75** — [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler)'s paraphrase of the same spreadsheet.
* **77** — two late-2025 posts treating 77 overlaps over 44 months as settled.
* **1** — Wheeler's own strict reduction: same day, same airport. Utah, 10 September.

**Do not average these. The disagreement is the finding.** The [X discussions page](/Planes/following/x_discussions) quotes the posts that produced each number. The reconstruction of the *list* those numbers were counted from — every city, date and tail the sheet, the broadcasts, or X actually named — is on **[The 73 overlaps](/Planes/following/73_overlaps)**.

**Why six and not seventy-three.** The larger counts are built by counting *legs* rather than visits, by matching on *state* rather than airport, by using a **±3-day window**, and — per the independent audit — by including rows where the aircraft was on another continent. The audit found the plane **in Egypt** on nine claimed Wichita dates, found **duplicate rows counted twice** (index 61 duplicates 31; index 63 duplicates 33; index 56 is index 14 with the year advanced), and **invalidated the three earliest 2022 entries outright** because the aircraft was in France from 25 September to 10 November 2022. **These six are what is left when those errors are removed.**

**And six is not a small number.** It is six more than coincidence comfortably explains at fields this small — which is the argument, and it does not need inflating to seventy-three to be worth investigating.

## The counterargument, which every page here carries

**Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release, and its main plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska** — the most-visited field in this record. **Wichita is a Falcon service hub. Wilmington is a customs stop used outbound only. Minot is where customs actually happened.** The rotation never varies: Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US field → sit five or six days → Wilmington → Cairo, flown identically by three aircraft over twenty months, with a dwell of five or six days **every single time**. A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.

**ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of innocent intent.

**Erika's side is the weak side.** Her flight logs are reported erased, so nearly every "Erika overlap" is the foreign aircraft's track plus a *claimed* location for her. Of 139 catalogued Turning Point appearances, **exactly one** places Erika Kirk at a TPUSA event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. **The 73-overlap tally is measured against a set of locations nobody has published.** See [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

## What we do not know

* **What time of day most of these events ran.** Without event times, "same day" cannot be narrowed into a sequence on any date except 10 September 2025.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used** on any of these dates. The Kirk side of every pairing here is a location, not a tail number.
* **Who was aboard any foreign aircraft**, on any leg, on any date. No manifest, FBO log, badge record, or crew list has been obtained for a single flight in this record.
* **Whether any of these aircraft entered a maintenance hangar.** No work order or invoice has been obtained either — the maintenance explanation is as unevidenced by primary records as the shadowing one.
* **Which version of the 10 September departure is correct.** Three irreconcilable accounts, all from named sources, all settleable in minutes by anyone with raw archive access.
* **How far back the window really goes.** Trackers say 2022; the beat framing says 18 months; the earliest date this directory can source is **April 2023**, and the earliest *Utah* date is **April 2024**. A single unverified claim (@AuntLinda\_\_, December 2025) would push SU-BTT's first Provo appearance back to **April 2024** — month only, no day, no screenshot. **The window follows the evidence, and the evidence currently starts in 2023.**
