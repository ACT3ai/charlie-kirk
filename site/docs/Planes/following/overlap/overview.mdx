# Overlap dates

**This directory holds one page per date on which an Egyptian-registered jet was at, or near, the same airport as Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, or a Turning Point event — on the same day, the day before, or the day after.**

It is the intersection of the two halves of the case. The [location pages](/Planes/following/overview) hold the foreign side, airport by airport. The [speaking-events catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) holds the Kirk and TPUSA side, appearance by appearance. **This directory is what is left when you lay one on top of the other and keep only the dates that touch.**

**This directory holds two layers, and the difference between them is the whole story.**

* **All 78 claimed rows**, reconstructed from the compilation that produced the "73 times" figure — one page each, [indexed below](#every-claimed-row-one-page-each). **60 of them rest on Erika Kirk alone. One claims both Kirks. Four claim a Turning Point event with neither Kirk present. Forty-four put the aircraft on the wrong continent.**
* **The six dates that survive a same-day-or-adjacent test at a shared field** — listed first, because they are what is actually left.

**Six qualify out of seventy-eight claimed.** That gap is not a footnote. It is the most important thing on this page, and it runs in both directions: the published number is far too big, and six unexplained co-locations at fields this small is far from nothing.

## The test each date had to pass

A date gets a page here only if **both** are true:

* **A foreign-registered jet from the followed fleet** — [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV), [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM) — **was on the ground at, or repositioning through, an airport at or near the event location.**
* **A documented Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, or TPUSA event happened there on the same day, the day before, or the day after.**

Everything else is on the [location pages](/Planes/following/overview), where it belongs. **A three-day gap is not an overlap. A 15-month gap is not an overlap.** Both appear in the published tallies.

## Read chronologically

**2023 — Missouri.** [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) lands at St. Louis on **21 February 2023** and does not leave until May. [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) arrives **25 February**, leaves, and returns **1 April at 11:02 am**. The next day, **2 April**, TPUSA Faith launches its "Kingdom to the Capitol" tour with a Sean Feucht event in **Jefferson City** — 130 miles and two hours' drive away. Two foreign jets in one metro region across a week of Turning Point activity, and no Kirk placed in the state. [Read the page →](./20230402_MO_jefferson_city)

**2024 — Nebraska, then Utah.** SU-BTT reaches **Omaha on 9 February**, hops fifteen minutes to **Lincoln**, and sits five days. **TPUSA opens a Lincoln chapter on 11 February**, inside that stay — same city, same airport, no Kirk present. [Read the page →](./20240211_NE_lincoln) Seven weeks later SU-BTT is at **Wichita**, and on **3 April Charlie Kirk speaks at the University of Kansas** — the morning the aircraft **departs**. [Read the page →](./20240403_KS_lawrence) Three weeks after that, **Utah appears in the record for the first time**: SU-BND arrives at Provo **19 April** via Salt Lake City, SU-BTT lands at Provo at **12:13 pm on 23 April** via Williston, North Dakota, and **Charlie Kirk is at the University of Utah that same day**. [Read the page →](./20240423_UT_salt_lake_city)

**2025 — the fleet converges on Provo, and then 10 September.** SU-BND arrives at Provo on **23 May** and stays **113 days**. SU-BTT arrives **4 September** via Minot. On **10 September** SU-BTT departs that morning, SU-BND switches its transponder on and never moves, and **Charlie Kirk is killed at Utah Valley University, seven miles away, at 12:23:30 PM MDT**. [Read the page →](./20250910_UT_orem) That evening SU-BTT lands at **Wilmington, Delaware**, where a compilation sheet claims Erika Kirk was — and leaves for Cairo the next morning. [Read the page →](./20250910_DE_wilmington)

**The counter-chronology, in the same breath.** Charlie Kirk was in **Seoul, South Korea on 5 and 6 September 2025**. Erika Kirk has reportedly said she stayed home in **Arizona on 10 September**. **Neither Kirk is documented at Provo at any point during the fleet's September presence.**

## Who the overlaps are actually with — the count broken open

The headline is "73 times those planes followed them." **They are not 73 of the same thing, and most of them are not about Charlie Kirk at all.** Every claimed row has now been reconstructed into [overlaps.csv](/Planes/following/overlap/overview) — 78 entries, each with its own page — and sorted by *whose* presence the compilation actually asserts. This is the sheet's own attribution, not a verdict by this site.

| Who the sheet claims was there | Rows | Share | What that means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Erika Kirk alone** | **60** | **77%** | The overwhelming majority. Charlie Kirk is **not** claimed present on any of these. |
| **Charlie Kirk alone** | **13** | 17% | Erika is not claimed present on these. |
| **Both Kirks together** | **1** | 1% | **One row in seventy-eight** claims the two of them in the same place at the same time — 10 September 2025. |
| **A TPUSA event, neither Kirk present** | **4** | 5% | Turning Point activity — a chapter opening, a Faith-tour stop — with no Kirk claimed at it. |
| **Total claimed overlaps** | **78** | | |

**Read across that table before reading anything else about this claim.** The number that travels — 73, or 77, or "more than 70" — is being heard by the public as *seventy-three times foreign jets followed Charlie Kirk*. On the compilation's own attribution it is nothing of the kind:

* **Overlaps with Charlie Kirk: 14 of 78** — the 13 rows claiming him alone, plus the single row claiming both Kirks. That is **18%** of the count. The Owens team's own companion figure is "about 23 with Charlie" — **the reconstruction does not reach it**, and nobody has published the list that would.
* **Overlaps with Erika Kirk: 61 of 78** — the 60 claiming her alone, plus the shared row. **78%** of the entire count rests on her.
* **Overlaps without Erika Kirk: 17 of 78.** Take her out and the pattern loses three-quarters of its mass.
* **Overlaps at a Turning Point event where neither Kirk is claimed to have been present: 4.** These are the rows where the jet is being matched to the *organisation* rather than to a person.

**And that 78% is the part of the ledger nobody can check.** Erika Kirk's flight logs are [reported as erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Of **139 sourced Turning Point and Kirk appearances** catalogued in [the speaking events index](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), **exactly one** places Erika Kirk at an event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. So the largest component of the largest number in this investigation is measured against an itinerary that has never been published by anyone — its rows come from a spreadsheet column that often names a whole state, sometimes two states at once, and sometimes hedges with the word "potentially."

**Then the audit cuts it again.** An independent line-by-line check of the same sheet against paid flight-history archives found:

| Audit verdict on the aircraft's position | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Accurate** — the jet really was there that day | **18** |
| **Inaccurate** — the jet was somewhere else, usually another continent | **44** |
| Partially accurate — right aircraft and date, wrong route or duration | 5 |
| Not assessable — the flight archive does not reach back that far | 2 |
| **Never published** — a number with no date, city, airport or tail attached | **5** |
| Not reached by the audit | 4 |

**Forty-four of seventy-eight claimed overlaps put the aircraft in the wrong place**, including nine claimed Wichita visits on dates the plane was in Egypt, three 2022 rows invalidated because the aircraft was in France from 25 September to 10 November 2022, and **duplicate rows counted twice** — index 61 duplicates 31, index 63 duplicates 33, and index 56 is index 14 re-entered with the year advanced.

**What survives is small, and it is worth more than what was lost.** Of all 78 rows, only **nine** fall within three days of a Turning Point or Charlie Kirk appearance this site can independently source in the same state — and only **six** survive a same-day-or-adjacent test at a shared field. **Two of those six are in Utah.** One is 23 April 2024, when SU-BTT landed at Provo the same day Charlie Kirk spoke at the University of Utah. The other is 10 September 2025.

**That is the honest shape of it: not seventy-three, and not zero.** A six-date pattern concentrated on one state, standing inside a three-year record that otherwise resolves into maintenance rotations, is a narrower claim than the one being broadcast — and a much harder one to dismiss. The inflated version is what lets a hostile reader throw out the September window along with the spreadsheet.

## Six against seventy-three

The published tallies are far larger than this list, they disagree with each other, and none of them has been reconciled:

* **73 / ~23** — the Owens-team headline: 73 overlaps with Erika, about 23 with Charlie.
* **72** — @JustTheTweets17, 18 November 2025.
* **70+ / "nearly 70"** — Shadow of Ezra, David Nino Rodriguez, later restatements.
* **68 / 29** — Morgan Ariel, 17 November 2025: both jets, 2022 – September 2025, 29 also including Charlie.
* **65–75** — [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler)'s paraphrase of the same spreadsheet.
* **77** — two late-2025 posts treating 77 overlaps over 44 months as settled.
* **1** — Wheeler's own strict reduction: same day, same airport. Utah, 10 September.

**Do not average these. The disagreement is the finding.** The [X discussions page](/Planes/following/x_discussions) quotes the posts that produced each number. The reconstruction of the *list* those numbers were counted from — every city, date and tail the sheet, the broadcasts, or X actually named — is on **[The 73 overlaps](/Planes/following/73_overlaps)**.

**Why six and not seventy-three.** The larger counts are built by counting *legs* rather than visits, by matching on *state* rather than airport, by using a **±3-day window**, and — per the independent audit — by including rows where the aircraft was on another continent. The audit found the plane **in Egypt** on nine claimed Wichita dates, found **duplicate rows counted twice** (index 61 duplicates 31; index 63 duplicates 33; index 56 is index 14 with the year advanced), and **invalidated the three earliest 2022 entries outright** because the aircraft was in France from 25 September to 10 November 2022. **These six are what is left when those errors are removed.**

**And six is not a small number.** It is six more than coincidence comfortably explains at fields this small — which is the argument, and it does not need inflating to seventy-three to be worth investigating.

## The counterargument, which every page here carries

**Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release, and its main plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska** — the most-visited field in this record. **Wichita is a Falcon service hub. Wilmington is a customs stop used outbound only. Minot is where customs actually happened.** The rotation never varies: Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US field → sit five or six days → Wilmington → Cairo, flown identically by three aircraft over twenty months, with a dwell of five or six days **every single time**. A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.

**ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of innocent intent.

**Erika's side is the weak side.** Her flight logs are reported erased, so nearly every "Erika overlap" is the foreign aircraft's track plus a *claimed* location for her. Of 139 catalogued Turning Point appearances, **exactly one** places Erika Kirk at a TPUSA event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. **The 73-overlap tally is measured against a set of locations nobody has published.** See [Erika flight logs erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

## What we do not know

* **What time of day most of these events ran.** Without event times, "same day" cannot be narrowed into a sequence on any date except 10 September 2025.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used** on any of these dates. The Kirk side of every pairing here is a location, not a tail number.
* **Who was aboard any foreign aircraft**, on any leg, on any date. No manifest, FBO log, badge record, or crew list has been obtained for a single flight in this record.
* **Whether any of these aircraft entered a maintenance hangar.** No work order or invoice has been obtained either — the maintenance explanation is as unevidenced by primary records as the shadowing one.
* **Which version of the 10 September departure is correct.** Three irreconcilable accounts, all from named sources, all settleable in minutes by anyone with raw archive access.
* **How far back the window really goes.** Trackers say 2022; the beat framing says 18 months; the earliest date this directory can source is **April 2023**, and the earliest *Utah* date is **April 2024**. A single unverified claim (@AuntLinda\_\_, December 2025) would push SU-BTT's first Provo appearance back to **April 2024** — month only, no day, no screenshot. **The window follows the evidence, and the evidence currently starts in 2023.**
