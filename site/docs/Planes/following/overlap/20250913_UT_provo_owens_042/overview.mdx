# 13 September 2025 — Provo, UT

**On 13 September 2025, three days after the assassination, primary ADS-B position data has [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) leaving Provo Municipal Airport (KPVU). The closest recovered fix is 0.62 km from the field at 3 ft above it — a departure — and the day's track ends over Canada.**

That closes a **113-day continuous stay** by a foreign-registered jet at Provo, begun on 23 May 2025. The independent audit scored the row **accurate**, and the primary trace agrees. The trace holds no on-ground position at the field, which is ordinary — receiver coverage of a parked aircraft is thin.

**It places nobody aboard.** A recovered trace locates an airframe and nothing else. No archive anywhere puts Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk on any aircraft, and pulling this data did not make the person half of the claim testable. A four-month grounded stay that ends shortly after the event is also exactly what a completed maintenance visit looks like, and that counterargument is on this page in full.

## Counterargument

* **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a relationship documented in the company's own 2015 press release. Its headquarters plant is at **Lincoln, Nebraska**; it has a second facility at **Provo, Utah**. Landings at Lincoln, at Omaha (a fifteen-minute hop from Lincoln), at Wichita (Yingling Aviation, an FAA Part 145 **Dassault Falcon** service centre) and at Provo are all what that relationship produces.
* **The rotation never varies.** Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US entry field → a short repositioning hop → sit five to eight days → [Wilmington](/Planes/following/Wilmington_KILG_2022-11-17_to_2025-10-12/overview) → Cairo. Three airframes flew it identically for three years. **A tasking that followed a person would vary with that person's schedule. A shop turnaround does not.**
* **Wilmington appears on the outbound leg only, twenty-one times, never inbound.** It is a customs and fuel stop being scored as a destination.
* **ADS-B has holes and they cut both ways.** A track gap is not proof of a covert leg; a clean track is not proof of intent.
* **The rotations continued after Charlie Kirk's death.** SU-BTV flew the identical Lincoln rotation 5–11 October 2025. If the flights were tasked to follow a man, they would have stopped when he died.

## What we do not know

* **Who was aboard.** No manifest, FBO log, crew list, or airport badge record has been obtained for any leg of any of these flights, at any location, by anyone.
* **Whether the aircraft entered a maintenance hangar.** No Duncan Aviation or Yingling work order or invoice has been produced either — **the maintenance explanation is as unevidenced by primary records as the shadowing one.**
* **Where Erika Kirk actually was.** No dated itinerary for her has ever been published, by the compilers or by anyone else.
* **What time of day any event ran.** Without event times, "same day" cannot be narrowed into a sequence on any date except 10 September 2025.
* **Which aircraft the Kirk party used.** The Kirk side of nearly every row here is a claimed location, not a tail number.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Confirmed. The claimed aircraft really was at the claimed field.** SU-BND was tracked to within **1.29 km of KPVU** at **2025-09-12T16:48:52.550Z** on 12 September 2025, **on the ground**. The trace is held by both archives, agreeing.

That day the airframe was first heard at KPVU/PVU (Provo Municipal Airport, Provo UT) 1.29km and last heard at the same place — 560 recorded points, every one of them on the ground at Provo.

**The confirming day is 12 September, not the 13th, and that difference belongs on the page rather than smoothed out of it.** On the claimed date itself, 13 September 2025, SU-BND's **first fix of the day is still at Provo, 0.62 km from the field**, and its last is 58.9 km from Nipigon, Ontario — it left Provo that morning and crossed Canada eastbound. Our per-day scoring calls 13 September "elsewhere" because the day *ends* elsewhere. **On the aircraft's own track the row holds: SU-BND sat on the ground at Provo through 12 September and departed from Provo on the 13th.**

**This confirms the aircraft half of the pairing and nothing else. It does not place Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere.** An overlap needs both halves. The Kirk half of this row is unchanged by anything below — it is the sheet's claimed-location cell for Erika Kirk, and [her flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased), so there is no itinerary to check it against.

**The method, so a reader can repeat it.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and airplanes.live — were queried for SU-BND on 13 September 2025 and on the day either side, and every returned position that was on the ground or below 4,000 ft was measured against the published coordinates of KPVU. Within 15 km counts as at the field and within 80 km as the same metro; **nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is printed beside every field name rather than the name alone.

Both were written 2026-08-24T17:36:21.953Z.

**Against the audit verdict already on this page.** The audit recorded on this page marks the row **accurate**, and primary position data agrees with it. Two independent methods, one on paid archives and one on free ones, reached the same answer.

**What this check cannot do.** It locates an airframe. It does not identify who was aboard, it does not establish why the aircraft was where it was, and it does not distinguish a shadowing flight from the maintenance rotation set out in the counterargument above. **Absence in an archive is not proof of a covert leg, and presence is not proof of intent.**
