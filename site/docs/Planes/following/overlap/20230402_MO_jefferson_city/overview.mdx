# 2 April 2023 — Jefferson City, Missouri

## The pairing, in one sentence

**TPUSA Faith launched its tour with a Sean Feucht event in Jefferson City, Missouri on 2 April 2023 — the day after [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) landed at St. Louis at 11:02 am, and while [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) had already been parked in the same metropolitan area for six weeks — but the event was 130 miles and roughly two hours' drive from the aircraft.**

This is a **day-after, different-city** pairing. It qualifies for this directory because the foreign aircraft was on the ground in the same state and metro region within one day of a Turning Point event. It does not qualify as a same-airport overlap, and nobody has ever placed Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk in Missouri on this date.

## The compiler footnoted his own weakness, and that is worth recording

The timeline that produced this pairing carries its own note: **"Event is in Jefferson City, MO (2 hrs from St. Louis)."**

That is the person building the case writing down the distance that undercuts it. It is the single most creditable act of sourcing anywhere in this material and it belongs on the page rather than buried.

## The February 2023 cluster this sits inside

The five days spanning **25 February to 2 March 2023** are the most concentrated pre-2025 period in the record: SU-BND on the ground since 21 February, **SU-BTT arriving 25 February** and staying to 2 March. Two Egyptian jets in one metro area for a week.

Two TPUSA Faith announcements fall inside that window — **Sean Feucht announced as a TPUSA Faith contributor on 27 February**, and as **head of the "Kingdom to the Capitol" tour on 28 February**. Those are *announcements*, not located events, which is why they do not get pages of their own in this directory. They are recorded here because they are part of why the April date is claimed at all.

## The counterargument

* **130 miles is not an overlap.** Jefferson City has its own airport. An aircraft that wanted to be at a Jefferson City event would fly to Jefferson City.
* **An eleven-week stay is a maintenance stay, not an operation.** A 1998-build Gulfstream IV parked for that long and then ferried home via Goose Bay is what a heavy check looks like.
* **Neither Kirk is placed in Missouri on any date in this window by any source.** The Kirk side of this pairing is an organization's event, not a person's location.
* **SU-BTT's rotation is the same one it flew everywhere else** — Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US field → sit five or six days → Wilmington → Cairo. It did not vary for this event.
* **The clustering claim's Missouri leg rests substantially on this one date.** If the two-hour drive disqualifies it, Missouri thins out considerably.

## What we do not know

* **Whether any TPUSA or Kirk aircraft flew into the St. Louis area for this event.** No Kirk-side tail number has been published for 1–6 April 2023.
* **What time of day the Jefferson City event ran.** Without it, "next day" cannot be tightened.
* **Who was aboard either aircraft.** No manifest, FBO log, or crew record has been obtained for any leg on this page.
* **Which of the three St. Louis-area fields SU-BND actually sat at.** The audit note points at KSUS for a different visit; this one is unresolved.

## We checked this row against primary ADS-B data

**Confirmed — for SU-BTT.** Both free archives hold SU-BTT on 1 April 2023, they agree with each other, and they put the aircraft over St. Louis: **closest approach 0.48 km from Lambert International (KSTL) at 15:55:15 UTC**, descending, with the last fix of the day 3.21 km from Spirit of St. Louis (KSUS). The day's trace runs from Mouy Airfield in France to the St. Louis metro in 558 fixes, touching **KSTL at 5.09 km, K1H0 at 7.57 km and KSUS at 7.64 km** below 4,000 ft.

**Method.** Both free ADS-B history archives — adsb.lol globe history and the airplanes.live mirror — were queried for the claimed tail on the claimed date and on the day either side, and every position fix was resolved to the nearest field in the OurAirports public-domain gazetteer. **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record**, which is why the distance in kilometres is published beside every result rather than a bare airport name.

**This confirms the AIRCRAFT half and nothing else.** Position data locates an airframe. It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere, it does not establish that anyone aboard knew where either of them was, and it does not establish who tasked the flight. The Kirk half of this pairing is exactly as strong, or as weak, as it was before we pulled the data.

**SU-BND's eleven-week St. Louis stay is NOT corroborated by this pull, and it is not refuted either.** Neither archive holds a trace for that airframe on any of the three days. A parked aircraft with its transponder off produces exactly this, and so does a coverage hole. **Absence is not proof.** Half the foreign side of this page therefore remains where it was: on a researcher-compiled record, not on position data.

### Why the first pass got this wrong, and why that is worth writing down

**Our own first automated pass scored this row ELSEWHERE.** The row's airport cell holds three identifiers in one string — `KSTL/KCPS/KSUS` — because the St. Louis metro has three fields a state-level tally cannot tell apart. The matcher compared that whole string as a single identifier, found no airport called `KSTL/KCPS/KSUS`, and scored the aircraft as being somewhere else. Splitting the cell into its three codes and measuring distance to each fixed it, and the row came back **confirmed at 0.48 km**.

**A compound cell in a source spreadsheet is a hazard, and this is a concrete example of it.** It is exactly the class of error the published audits of this material have been finding in the other direction. We found it in our own tooling, and correcting it turned a false refutation into a confirmation. **Stating that is what makes the other eighty-four verdicts worth anything.**

**The counterargument is untouched.** Confirming that SU-BTT was over St. Louis on 1 April 2023 does not shorten the **130 miles and two hours' drive** to Jefferson City, and it was the compiler who footnoted that distance in the first place. Neither Kirk is placed in Missouri on any date in this window by any source, and the rotation SU-BTT flew — Cairo, Le Bourget, a US field, sit, Wilmington, Cairo — is the one it flew everywhere else.

**This agrees with the row's existing status.** `overlaps.csv` records it accurate, and the independent audit reached the same conclusion from paid archives. Two independent readings and one primary-data pull now agree on where SU-BTT was.

This row is one of **85 checked in a single run on 24 August 2026** — 24 confirmed at the claimed field, 12 refuted, 22 not heard, 20 with no archive coverage, 2 naming no aircraft, and 5 naming no date. The whole result is on [Overlap dates](/Planes/following/overlap/overview).

## Where this page fits
