# 2 April 2023 — Jefferson City, Missouri

## The pairing, in one sentence

**TPUSA Faith launched its tour with a Sean Feucht event in Jefferson City, Missouri on 2 April 2023 — the day after [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT) landed at St. Louis at 11:02 am, and while [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) had already been parked in the same metropolitan area for six weeks — but the event was 130 miles and roughly two hours' drive from the aircraft.**

This is a **day-after, different-city** pairing. It qualifies for this directory because the foreign aircraft was on the ground in the same state and metro region within one day of a Turning Point event. It does not qualify as a same-airport overlap, and nobody has ever placed Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk in Missouri on this date.

## The foreign side

Two Egyptian-registered aircraft were in the St. Louis area on 2 April 2023.

| Aircraft | Type | Arrived St. Louis | Route in | Departed | Onward | Audit verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT)** | Falcon 7X | **1 April 2023, 11:02 am** | Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget (31 Mar) → St. Louis | **6 April 2023** | Wilmington; Cairo 8 April | **Accurate** *(audit indices 10 and 11)* |
| **[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND)** | Gulfstream 4SP | **21 February 2023** | — | **~May 2023** | Goose Bay → Paris → Cairo, 9 May | **Accurate** *(audit index 15)* |

**SU-BND had been sitting in the St. Louis area for six weeks when the Jefferson City event happened, and it stayed for another five.** Its total stay was roughly eleven weeks.

The St. Louis record also touches **three separate metro airports** — Lambert (**KSTL**, Missouri), Downtown/Cahokia (**KCPS**, Illinois), and Spirit of St. Louis (**KSUS**, Chesterfield, Missouri). The audit's note on index 46 reads *"1 week in St. Louis (KSUS)."* A state-level tally cannot distinguish them.

## The compiler footnoted his own weakness, and that is worth recording

The timeline that produced this pairing carries its own note: **"Event is in Jefferson City, MO (2 hrs from St. Louis)."**

That is the person building the case writing down the distance that undercuts it. It is the single most creditable act of sourcing anywhere in this material and it belongs on the page rather than buried.

## The February 2023 cluster this sits inside

The five days spanning **25 February to 2 March 2023** are the most concentrated pre-2025 period in the record: SU-BND on the ground since 21 February, **SU-BTT arriving 25 February** and staying to 2 March. Two Egyptian jets in one metro area for a week.

Two TPUSA Faith announcements fall inside that window — **Sean Feucht announced as a TPUSA Faith contributor on 27 February**, and as **head of the "Kingdom to the Capitol" tour on 28 February**. Those are *announcements*, not located events, which is why they do not get pages of their own in this directory. They are recorded here because they are part of why the April date is claimed at all.

## The counterargument

* **130 miles is not an overlap.** Jefferson City has its own airport. An aircraft that wanted to be at a Jefferson City event would fly to Jefferson City.
* **An eleven-week stay is a maintenance stay, not an operation.** A 1998-build Gulfstream IV parked for that long and then ferried home via Goose Bay is what a heavy check looks like.
* **Neither Kirk is placed in Missouri on any date in this window by any source.** The Kirk side of this pairing is an organization's event, not a person's location.
* **SU-BTT's rotation is the same one it flew everywhere else** — Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → a US field → sit five or six days → Wilmington → Cairo. It did not vary for this event.
* **The clustering claim's Missouri leg rests substantially on this one date.** If the two-hour drive disqualifies it, Missouri thins out considerably.

## What we do not know

* **Whether any TPUSA or Kirk aircraft flew into the St. Louis area for this event.** No Kirk-side tail number has been published for 1–6 April 2023.
* **What time of day the Jefferson City event ran.** Without it, "next day" cannot be tightened.
* **Who was aboard either aircraft.** No manifest, FBO log, or crew record has been obtained for any leg on this page.
* **Which of the three St. Louis-area fields SU-BND actually sat at.** The audit note points at KSUS for a different visit; this one is unresolved.
