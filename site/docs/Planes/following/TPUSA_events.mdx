# TPUSA Events, With The Aircraft Record Beside Them

This page is the **event index** for the [following-plane claim](./overview). It lists **139 sourced Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, and Turning Point USA appearances** between January 2022 and October 2025 — every one of them linked to its own page under [Speaking events](./speaking/overview) — and it puts the **foreign-aircraft record for that exact date in the last column**, so a reader can test a pairing instead of being told about one.

The claim being tested is not this page's claim. Trackers on X, led in public by [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), say Egyptian-registered VIP jets shadowed the Kirks and TPUSA up to **73 times**. Those are researcher tallies read off public ADS-B history, they disagree with each other — 77, 73, 72, "70+", 68/29, 65–75, and one reduction to a single day — and this site does not restate any of them as a verified total. What this page can do is line the two records up in public and let the arithmetic be checked.

## What the line-up produces

Against the **63 documented ground presences** by [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) and [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM/overview) on U.S. soil, catalogued location by location in [this directory](./overview):

* **42 of the 139 events** fall on a date when at least one of those aircraft was somewhere in the United States. That is the raw number, and on its own it means very little — the fleet sat on U.S. ramps for long stretches, so any busy speaking calendar will intersect it.
* **Four of the 139 events** put an event and an aircraft **in the same state on the same day.**
* **None of the 139 events** put an event and an aircraft **in the same city.** Zero.

Those four same-state days are the ones the location pages already argue over, and each one has its distance stated:

**Two of those four are in Utah, and both are the ones that matter.** That is the honest shape of the pattern as this repo can currently source it: not seventy-three hits, but a Utah concentration that survives every audit aimed at it, sitting inside a three-year record where nothing else lands closer than 130 miles.

## How the last column was built, and what it cannot tell you

The aircraft column is **computed, not reported.** For each event date it asks one mechanical question — was any of the five tail numbers on the ground at a U.S. field that day, according to the arrival and departure logs on the location pages in this directory — and prints what it finds, with the city marked **same city** or **same state** when it matches the event.

That method has limits, and they run in both directions:

* **A jet being in the United States is not an overlap.** [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) sat at Provo for **113 consecutive days** in 2025. Every single TPUSA event in that window shows a jet in the last column, and almost none of them mean anything. Read the city, not the presence.
* **The logs are not complete.** They are built from published Flightradar24 histories and tracker exports. Legs nobody has published do not appear, and an absence in this column is not proof an aircraft was elsewhere.
* **The event list is not complete either.** It holds what this repo could source. A Kirk appearance nobody wrote down is not in it.
* **Erika Kirk is in exactly two rows** — the [June 2025 Young Women's Leadership Summit](./speaking/20250613_grapevine) with Charlie, and the [21 September 2025 stadium memorial](./speaking/20250921_glendale) after his death. **Her flight logs are reported as erased** ([see that page](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)), and no tracker has ever published the dated Erika itinerary the 73-count was measured against. The largest tally in this case rests on the side of the ledger this page cannot reproduce at all. Say that out loud on any page that uses her number.
* **Eight rows are podcast-release proxies, not event dates.** The live stop is normally 0–7 days earlier. They are marked in the "Date basis" column. **Never publish a "same day" claim off one of them.** Four more are events that were **scheduled and not held**, and nine fall **after 10 September 2025**, when Charlie Kirk was no longer present at his own tour.
* **Five rows have no sourced city** — mostly TPUSA Faith and Academy summits and debate-night recordings. They cannot be tested for location at all, and they are left as `UNKNOWN` rather than filled in from memory.

## Counterargument

The innocent reading of everything below, stated here as it is on every page in this directory. **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's aircraft maintenance account since 1999**, under a five-year direct contract announced in October 2015, with its headquarters plant at **Lincoln, Nebraska** and a facility at **Provo, Utah**. Wichita hosts **Yingling Aviation**, an FAA Part 145 Dassault Falcon service centre. Wilmington, Delaware is a routine East Coast customs and fuel stop, and it appears in this record on the **outbound leg only, twenty-one times, for three years**. Omaha, Minot and Detroit are customs entries followed within twenty minutes by a hop to a maintenance field.

That accounts for the itinerary without anybody following anybody. It does not account for the September 2025 Provo window, which is why that window — and not the tally — is where this section's attention belongs.

## What we do not know

1. **No manifest for any leg has ever been published**, by anyone, for any of these aircraft, at any location. Nothing in the table above establishes who was aboard.
2. **The Erika Kirk itinerary does not exist as a public record.** Two rows in 139. The 73-overlap tally was assembled against a location set nobody has published.
3. **The customs records are in North Dakota.** Every 2025 Provo arrival entered the U.S. at [Minot](./Minot_KMOT_2025-04-08_to_2025-09-04/overview). Whoever was aboard was processed there, and **nobody has requested those records.**
4. **The 27 May 2025 Provo arrival is attributed to two different aircraft** — SU-BTV in the flight-history export, SU-BTU in the Owens account. SU-BTV is hex `0101D1`, SU-BTU is hex `0101D0`; the raw archive would settle it in minutes.
5. **No Duncan Aviation work order has been produced** for any of these visits. A single confirmation of which airframes were in the shop on which dates would close most of this directory.
6. **The window itself is unresolved** — 18 months, two years, and "2022 through September 2025" are all in circulation, and the earliest overlap that survives audit is 13 November 2022.

## Where this page fits

This is the event index the aircraft record gets tested against — 139 sourced Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk and TPUSA appearances from January 2022 to October 2025, each with [its own page](/Planes/following/speaking/overview) and the foreign-aircraft position for that exact date beside it — and it is what turns [the 73-overlap claim](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) from an assertion into arithmetic anyone can check against the [Following log](/Planes/following/overview). The events themselves are the [Turning Point](/TPUSA/overview) record, so this page also reaches into [the organisation's nonprofit structure](/TPUSA/Nonprofits/overview), [the donor-pressure arc before September](/Before/overview) and [the leadership succession afterwards](/TPUSA/TPUSA_Replacement_Timeline). The number to look for is above: against 63 documented ground presences by the five Egyptian tails, [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) among them, four of the 139 events put an aircraft and an event in the same state on the same day and none put one in the same city — a result that cuts against the pattern and is published for that reason, and Erika Kirk's side is thinner still because [her flight logs are reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).
