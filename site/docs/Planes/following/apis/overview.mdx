# Getting The Data

The claim that foreign-registered aircraft shadowed Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk and TPUSA rests almost
entirely on **third-hand readings of flight-tracking websites**. Somebody photographed an
aircraft-history table. Somebody typed the rows into a spreadsheet. Somebody tallied the spreadsheet.
Somebody read the tally on television. **Every one of those steps is a place a row could be misread**
— and the published audits say a great many of them were.

This section replaces that chain with data we pull ourselves, from named sources, with the raw
response saved and timestamped. It exists so that a reader who does not believe a word of this site
can repeat the pulls and get the same answer.

A pass is never a substitute for another. **The same claim checked four ways and agreeing is the
strongest thing this investigation can produce. Checked four ways and disagreeing is the second
strongest — and it gets published too.**

## What the first pass found

478 pulls against the free historical archive on 24 August 2026. 178 traces retrieved. Three results
worth stating up front.

**The September 10 departure has a primary answer.** SU-BTT was on the ground at Provo Municipal at
13:07 UTC — 07:07 Mountain — lifted off at 13:14 UTC, and was on the ground at Wilmington New Castle,
Delaware at 16:50 UTC. That is a departure about five hours before the assassination and a landing
about ninety minutes before it, from a 1,229-point track. It matches the "7:08 AM MT" figure that has
circulated and is inconsistent with the competing accounts. **It says where an airframe was. It says
nothing about who was aboard or who tasked it, and no public record establishes tasking in either
direction.**

**An independent check agrees with the audit.** Running every exactly-claimed date through primary
traces, blind: pairings this repo's audit called **accurate** have a trace **75%** of the time, ones
called **partial 88%**, and ones called **inaccurate only 11%**.

**And the sharpest result splits the claim in two.** Individual flight legs — "this aircraft was at
this field on this date" — verify at **98%**. Overlap pairings — "and therefore it was following
them" — verify at **38%**. **The aircraft movements are largely real. The inference that they
constitute shadowing is where the spreadsheet falls apart.** That finding flatters neither side of
this argument, which is roughly the point.

## What none of it can do

**The counts remain trackers' tallies, not records.** 73, 72, 70+, 68, 29, 23 — amateur readings of
public data by independent researchers, never reconciled with each other. Nothing in this section
turns one of them into a verified total, and where they conflict this site shows the conflict rather
than averaging it.

**ADS-B has holes, and they cut both ways.** A missing trace means a volunteer network heard nothing.
It does not establish that an aircraft did not fly and it does not establish that a transponder was
switched off — a parked aircraft, a coverage gap over the Atlantic, and a wrong claimed date all look
identical. Every gap we hit is written down in a `MISSING_DATA.md` file beside the relevant aircraft
and overlap, **90 of them**, saying exactly that.

**Two doors are shut.** The free archive does not reach 2022, where the trackers date the start of
the pattern — so those rows are untestable without paying. And **Erika Kirk's side cannot be checked
at all**: her flight logs are reported erased, and this repo holds a single sourced location for her
before 10 September 2025, against a window somebody counted 73 overlaps across. See
[Erika Kirk's flight logs reported erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased).

## What this section will not do

It will not merge the threads. The 18-month following pattern, the September 10 day-of timeline, the
N1098L thread and the N888KG Wendover departure are **four different claims about four different sets
of aircraft**, and blending them is how the whole angle gets dismissed at once.

It will not name who tasked an aircraft, because no public record establishes it. It will not name a
living person as an accused. And it will not publish a page without the innocent explanation beside
the suspicious one — including the innocent explanation for our own results, which is that an
aircraft simply was not there.
