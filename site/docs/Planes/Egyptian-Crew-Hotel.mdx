# Egyptian Crew Hotel (Claims)

[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) reported receiving a tip from someone **directly
involved in the booking process** that occupants of the **Egyptian government private jets**
in Provo lodged at:

**La Quinta Inn & Suites — 521 W. University Parkway, Orem, UT 84058** (University Parkway location)

A second La Quinta exists at 1100 W 780 N, Orem; the tip specifies the **University Parkway**
property. This page documents the **allegation and investigative next steps** — not confirmed
guest identities or wrongdoing by any hotel employee or guest.

## Alleged stay window

Per Owens's public posts (summarized in CK_FILE):

- **Arrival context:** Egyptian jets including **[SU-BTT](./SU-BTT/overview)** flew into Provo **September 4, 2025** — six days before the assassination.
- **Departure context:** Fleet activity continued through **September 13, 2025** (e.g., [SU-BND](./SU-BND/overview) Sept 13 outbound leg).
- **Requested evidence:** Hotel lobby or exterior camera footage; employee memories of unusual guests.

Owens invited tips to **moretips@candaceowens.com** from nearby businesses or La Quinta staff.

## Why investigators care

If corroborated, hotel records would:

1. **Name passengers** linked to tail numbers already in ADS-B logs.
2. Connect **ground transport** ([Duncan Aviation](https://www.duncanaviation.aero/) rental-car claims on [Sept 10 timeline](./Sept10-Flight-Timeline)) to specific individuals.
3. Settle **how long the occupants were actually on the ground, and against what booking.** A multi-week presence and a dated service visit are different things, and a hotel folio dated against a Duncan Aviation work order would show which the September 4–13 window was. No service record covering these tails at Provo in that window has been produced.

Proximity to UVU (~minutes from campus) makes the location **geographically salient**; proximity alone is not proof of participation in violence.

## The rental-car allegation and the Duncan Aviation FBO

The lodging tip on this page has a companion claim about ground transport, and the two are usually circulated together. Both come from the same source type — an anonymous tipster relayed on air — and both should be weighed the same way.

In a segment circulated by **@MJTruthUltra** in late 2025, **[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)** stated:

> There's more. I know something else about the Egyptian flights. This time. Another mommy sleuth. I learned that oddly, Duncan Aviation, you know, where that plane was transponding from, the FBO, and Provo sourced rental cars for these quote unquote Egyptians when they landed a couple of days before this operation. I didn't just learn that, I actually had the license plates number for 4 of the vehicles that they rented.

She then displayed four vehicles and plates as full-screen graphics:

| # | Vehicle | Plate | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Silver 2025 Toyota RAV4 | T092ZS | Utah |
| 2 | White 2025 Toyota Camry | CWN9872 | Arizona |
| 3 | White 2023 Toyota RAV4 | T912KM | Utah |
| 4 | Black 2025 Toyota 4Runner | Z923DA | Utah |

Her ask was crowdsourced rather than accusatory:

> Now, to the students at UVU, please check your phones, your pictures from that day because if any of those cars were seen at UVU were around campus on that day, then we're going to be able to suspect something else. Check hotels, maybe gas station, maybe you work somewhere, look at those lights, look for those plates during that time frame from when those cars were there until they departed.

### How this bears on the hotel claim

The rental-car tip and the hotel tip fit together into a single, coherent, checkable scenario: **aircraft lands at Provo, crew and passengers clear through the FBO, the FBO arranges cars, the cars go to a nearby hotel, the party stays several days.** That is exactly what happens after every business-jet arrival at every regional airport in America, every day. It is also what a staging operation would look like. The scenario is not the evidence — the records are.

Three cautions carry from the vehicle claim to the lodging claim:

* **An FBO arranging cars is a service, not an anomaly.** Sourcing rental vehicles for arriving crew and passengers is a standard fixed-base-operator offering, and Duncan Aviation offers it to every customer at every facility. The same is true of recommending or booking crew hotels. Neither fact distinguishes this arrival from any other.
* **No document has been published.** No rental agreement, no FBO trip sheet, no hotel folio, no invoice. Both claims rest on anonymous tips relayed second-hand on a broadcast. Duncan Aviation has not commented; neither has the hotel.
* **Live plates on real vehicles are being circulated publicly.** Rental fleet plates rotate among unrelated customers constantly, so a plate photographed in Utah in that period may well have been on a different renter's car days later. **No person associated with any of these vehicles has been identified, charged, or accused of anything by any authority**, and nothing here should be read as suggesting otherwise. The plates are recorded to document what was broadcast, not to invite anyone to act on them.

### Where the checkable records actually are

If the arrival happened as described, it produced paperwork in at least four places, and any one of them would settle the matter faster than another round of tips:

1. **The FBO's trip sheet** at Provo — arrival handling, fuel uplift, catering, ground transport arrangements. Held by the FBO, not public, but obtainable by subpoena.
2. **U.S. Customs and Border Protection** clearance for an inbound international arrival. Note that per the [flight logs](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), these aircraft did **not** clear customs at Provo — they entered the United States at **Minot, North Dakota (MOT)**, then flew on to Provo as a domestic leg. That relocates the relevant customs record entirely, and it is the single most useful correction on this topic.
3. **The rental agreements** at whichever agency supplied the vehicles.
4. **The hotel's registration records** for the alleged stay window.

The customs point deserves emphasis because it changes where anyone looking should look. Every Provo arrival in the Egyptian fleet's 2025 record — [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview) on 8 April, [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV/overview) on 27 May, [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) on 23 May, [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) on 4 September — arrived on a **domestic leg from Minot**, having entered US airspace hours earlier and hundreds of miles away. **Whoever was aboard was processed at Minot, not at Provo.** Any manifest, passport record, or entry log for these arrivals is a North Dakota record.

## Where this page fits

What the flight record establishes is the airborne end: [SU-BTT arrived at Provo on 4 September](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), six days ahead of the assassination; [SU-BND stayed long enough for its transponder behaviour to become its own question](/Planes/SU-BND/overview); and the fleet's movements are the basis of [the following claim](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) and sit alongside [the reported Egyptian operations record](/intelligence/Egyptian_Foreign_Ops). This page is the ground-level end of that same thread — a single-source tip, from someone described as inside the booking process, putting the occupants of those jets in one named Orem hotel minutes from campus. Corroborated, it would do what no ADS-B trace can: put names to tail numbers. That is why it belongs next to [the Duncan Aviation ramp account at Provo](/Locations/Provo_Airport), [the claim of foreign-registered phones at the kill site](/intelligence/Foreign_Phones_UVU), and [the disclosure law written to force manifests out](/laws/US_Intel/Law_2_US_Intel). What is genuinely unproven is stated plainly above and does not change: the tip is uncorroborated, guest registers are private absent lawful process, and [the recovered ADS-B record](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Overlap-Recovery) can place an airframe on a ramp but never a person in a room — no itinerary yet produced places any named individual aboard any of these aircraft. Nor has any service record been produced for these tails at Provo in this window, while [the Falcon fleet's documented factory-authorised service stops run through Wichita](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview).
