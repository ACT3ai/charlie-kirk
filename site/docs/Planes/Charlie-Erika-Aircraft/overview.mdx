# Which Planes Charlie Flew, Which Planes Erika Flew

The following-planes claim rests on a pairing: a foreign-registered jet lands where a Kirk lands. To test it you need **both halves** — the foreign tail, and the Kirk tail. The foreign half is well documented. This page is about the other half, and about a gap in it that has not been stated plainly anywhere else on this site.

**Status: Erika Kirk — Alive.** Nothing on this page is a finding of wrongdoing by her or by anyone.

:::danger The tally that produced "73 overlaps with Erika Kirk" never established which plane she was on
**Across all 85 rows of the [overlap register](/Planes/following/overview), the column that would record which aircraft Erika Kirk flew — `kirk_tail` — is empty in 84 rows and reads `UNKNOWN` in the eighty-fifth. Not one row names an aircraft for her.**

**She was placed at each location by Instagram posts, not by flight records.** See [Erika's location from Instagram](/Planes/following/Erika_Location_From_Instagram). That means the counts are not aircraft-to-aircraft comparisons at all — they are **foreign-aircraft-to-claimed-human-location** comparisons, which is a weaker coincidence than two aircraft on the same ramp, and only the second was ever claimed.

**It is not evidence she hid anything.** Private aircraft manifests are not public for anyone. The absence of an Erika tail number is the normal state of the world for every private flyer in America — which is the entire premise of the [proposed disclosure laws](/Fix/overview). She **publicly invited** the check that would close it, telling a camera to go through her flight log. Nobody has published it.

**Status: Erika Kirk — Alive.** Nothing on this page is a finding of wrongdoing by her or by anyone.
:::

That last row is the finding. The tally that produced "73 overlaps with Erika Kirk" **never established which plane she was on**, because it was never built from her flight records. It was built from where she appeared to be.

## Charlie Kirk's aircraft — what is actually established

**N102DZ** is the only aircraft tied to Charlie Kirk's travel by a public flight record in this investigation. The FAA registry resolves what site commentary had left open:

* **Type:** Gulfstream Aerospace **G-V (Gulfstream V)** — not "reported to be a G5," but confirmed as one
* **Year built:** 1998 · **Serial number:** 555
* **Seats:** 20 (commentary describing "15–16 passenger capacity" was in the right range)
* **Registered owner:** Elderberry Chestnut LLC, Cheyenne, Wyoming — also listed: Sunny Blue Sky LLC, UST Enterprises LLC
* **Cost to buy new today:** **~$58.8 million** (see [Aircraft Costs](/Planes/Aircraft-Costs/overview))

The documented Charlie Kirk legs are the 10 September 2025 movements: Scottsdale into the Salt Lake City area that morning, and the afternoon legs into Provo. Those are laid out on the [N102DZ page](/Planes/N102DZ/overview) and the [September 10 flight timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline).

Beyond N102DZ, the overlap research attributes **17 rows to Charlie** as the subject, of which **8 are matched to a sourced, dated speaking event** — a real event, at a real venue, on the stated day. That is the strongest part of the whole dataset, and it is strong precisely because the events are independently checkable.

## Erika Kirk's aircraft — the gap, stated plainly

Across **85 rows** of the overlap spreadsheet, **61 name Erika as the subject** and **70 mark her as "claimed present."** The column that would record which aircraft she flew — `kirk_tail` — is **empty in 84 rows and `UNKNOWN` in the eighty-fifth**.

There is no Erika tail number to publish because the research never produced one. Three separate things follow from that, and they matter in different directions:

1. **The overlap counts cannot be aircraft-to-aircraft comparisons.** They are foreign-aircraft-to-claimed-human-location comparisons. A foreign jet landing at the same airport as a person who was in that city is a weaker coincidence than two aircraft on the same ramp — and only the second was ever claimed.
2. **The audit results reflect that weakness.** Of the rows with a verdict in the earlier line-by-line audit — an audit done by re-reading flight-tracking websites — **44 are marked inaccurate**, 21 accurate, 8 partial. The failures cluster on exactly the rows placed by social media rather than by a record.
   **Updated 26 August 2026.** Every row has since been re-tested against raw ADS-B position traces this investigation recovered and stored, and the picture separates rather than collapses: of the 29 rows position data can decide, **25 are corroborated and 3 refuted**, and the split between the two ledgers is stark — **13 of 24 Charlie/TPUSA rows corroborated with none refuted, against 12 of 56 on Erika's side carrying all three refutations**. Fifty-one rows no archive can answer at all. The full working is on [the following-planes overview](/Planes/following/overview#why-the-numbers-moved). It still locates aircraft only — it places no person aboard any of them.
3. **It is not evidence she hid anything.** Private aircraft manifests are not public for anyone. The absence of an Erika tail number is the normal state of the world for every private flyer in America, which is the entire premise of the [proposed disclosure laws](/Fix/overview).

Erika Kirk **publicly invited** the check that would close this: she told a camera to go through her flight log. That invitation, and the researchers' claim that the N102DZ history was later removed from FlightRadar24 in May 2026, are documented on [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and [her flight-log invitation](/Planes/following/Erika_Flight_Log_Invitation).

## Can Erika's flights be separated from Charlie's at all?

Yes — but only for a small number of dates, and only by evidence that is not flight data. Four methods exist, in descending order of strength:

1. **Dates where the two were provably in different places.** Where Charlie has a sourced speaking event in one city and Erika has a dated public appearance in another, the itineraries genuinely diverge. Of **139 sourced Turning Point appearances** in the [speaking catalog](/Planes/following/speaking/overview), **exactly one** places Erika at an event before 10 September 2025 with a firm date. One data point is not a travel pattern.
2. **The overseas legs.** The claim that the jets followed her *internationally* is the only part of the tally that is inherently Erika-specific, because those trips are described as hers alone. It is documented — and its weaknesses are documented — at [Erika Overseas Overlaps](/Planes/following/Erika_Overseas_Overlaps).
3. **Her own flight logs**, if produced. She has said they exist and invited scrutiny. Nobody has published them.
4. **N102DZ's full registration and movement history.** If the Gulfstream V is the family aircraft, its complete leg history would show which trips it made when Charlie had a documented event elsewhere. That history is the thing researchers say was removed.

**None of the four has produced a second tail number.** Until one does, "the planes followed Erika" and "the planes followed Charlie" are claims resting on different qualities of evidence, and this site should not present them as if they rest on the same.

## Where this page fits

Splitting the two itineraries is what makes [the following claim](/Planes/Following-Charlie-Erika) testable at all, and the split does not come out even — Charlie Kirk has [a confirmed tail number](/Planes/N102DZ/overview) and eight rows matched to sourced speaking events, while Erika Kirk's 85 spreadsheet rows carry no aircraft at all and [were placed from Instagram posts](/Planes/following/Erika_Location_From_Instagram) under [a window definition of 50 to 100 miles and plus or minus three days](/Planes/following/Overlap_Window_Definition). Her flight logs are reported erased and no backup anywhere has produced them — see [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) — which leaves the person half of every pairing resting on the same unavailable-record problem that [sealed digital-forensics warrants](/Legal/Evidence-Sealing-2026) and [unreleased manifests](/Fix/overview) create everywhere else in this case. The number to take with you is on [Overlap Recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Overlap-Recovery): of the 29 rows from [the 73-overlap sheet](/Planes/following/73_overlaps) that primary ADS-B can decide, **25 put the aircraft at the claimed field, 1 puts it in the right metro at the wrong field, and 3 put it somewhere else**. That corrects an earlier pass on this site which reported 12 refutations — **9 of the 12 were retracted** after every position fix in every recovered trace was re-read, six of them because they had been scored against the wrong calendar day. One confirmation was downgraded in the same pass, and it is published too, because a check that only ever moves one way is not a check.

### What these tables cannot tell you

* **A trace proves presence. It never proves purpose, and it never proves occupancy.** No ADS-B record anywhere places any person aboard any aircraft. An aircraft near an event is an airframe near an event.
* **139 sourced events is nowhere near every place Charlie Kirk went.** An aircraft that is absent from these tables was not necessarily absent from his travel.
* **An absence is not a finding.** A day with no row may be a day a volunteer receiver network heard nothing. Transponder off, outside coverage, and genuinely elsewhere all look identical from here.
* **2022 is effectively blank.** No free archive covers it, so no row above and no gap above can speak to that year.
