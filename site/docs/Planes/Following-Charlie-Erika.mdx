## The claim, and what primary position data did to it

**The compilation behind the "73 times" figure reconstructs to 85 claimed overlap rows. Twenty-nine of them can be decided by raw ADS-B position data. Twenty-five of those twenty-nine put the aircraft on or over the field the sheet names. Three are refuted.**

**The strongest row is the day itself.** On **10 September 2025**, [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) are both placed on the ground at [Provo Municipal Airport (KPVU)](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) — about 5 miles from Orem, the event city from Utah Valley University — at **1.23 km** and **1.29 km**. SU-BTT had been on that ramp since **[4 September](/Planes/following/overlap/20250904_UT_provo_owens_040/overview)**, six days before, and left at 07:14 MDT that morning. SU-BND never moved that day and stayed until **[13 September](/Planes/following/overlap/20250913_UT_provo_owens_042/overview)**.

**And the pattern is not only September 2025.** Confirmed ground positions run across 2023, 2024 and 2025 — Provo, Wilmington, Wichita, Omaha, Lincoln and St. Louis — including six dates on which the jet is **transmitting from on the ground** at the named field.

This page is the hub for the single biggest allegation in the aircraft angle of the case: that one or more aircraft did not merely happen to be near Utah Valley University on **September 10, 2025**, but had been **shadowing [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk), Erika Kirk, and [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) (TPUSA) events for years** — and that the pattern tightened in the **two months before the assassination**. The headline number, attributed to [**Candace Owens**](/People/candace-owens) and amplified by independent flight trackers, is that Israel/Egyptian government jets logged **up to 73 overlaps with Erika Kirk's travel** and roughly **23 with Charlie Kirk**. Every figure on this page is a **researcher claim built on public ADS-B data**, not a confirmed record, and the most prominent counterarguments are laid out in full below.

## The core claim

According to **Candace Owens**, two Israel/Egyptian Air Force VIP jets — the **"yellow plane" [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)** (a Dassault Falcon 7X) and the **"blue plane" [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)** (a Gulfstream) — repeatedly appeared near the Kirks' locations and near TPUSA events over a period researchers describe as **roughly 2022 through September 2025**. One widely shared post framed it as *"72 times those exact planes followed Erika Kirk… that is statistically near impossible without intent."* The investigation file records the same allegation tersely: *"Egyptian Planes had 73 overlaps with Erika. Only ~23 with Charlie."* The person-side of that tally — what X accounts actually posted about **[Erika Kirk's flights](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights)**, her on-camera invitation, and why the itinerary is unpublished — is now a dedicated page.

The claim is that this is not coincidence but **tasking** — that foreign aircraft were positioned to track the Kirk family and TPUSA's movements, and that this surveillance ran right up to the day Charlie Kirk was killed. Some posters note the overlaps **begin around 2022**, which they say **aligns with the Kirk marriage timeline**, and that several overlaps involve **international / overseas legs** alongside the Kirks rather than only domestic stops. The same researchers argue the Israel/Egyptian jets **regularly flew in and out of Israel** between these overlaps — the thread followed in detail on the [Israel Planes](/Planes/Israel-Planes) page.

## The overlap counts — and why they disagree

The "tracking" tally is the most cited and most disputed figure in the whole aircraft investigation. The exact number changes depending on which researcher and which post you read:

* **73 / ~23** — The headline split: roughly **73 documented overlaps near Erika Kirk** and about **23 near Charlie Kirk**, attributed to **Candace Owens**'s team, across U.S. cities including **Omaha, Wichita, and Provo**, plus domestic and international legs.
* **72, 70+, 68** — Different X posts cite **72**, **"70+"**, or **68** Erika-Kirk overlaps for the lead jets. The inconsistency between counts is itself part of the dispute.
* **68 / 29 (dual-plane count)** — A separate tally claims the blue ([SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)) and yellow ([SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)) jets *together* overlapped Erika Kirk's locations **68 times between 2022 and September 2025** — including overseas trips — of which **29 also coincided with Charlie Kirk's location.** This 68/29 figure is not reconciled with the 73/23 tally; both are independent researchers' numbers.
* **Geographic clustering** — One analysis cited by researchers concentrates the U.S. overlaps in **five states: Missouri, Delaware, Utah, Nebraska, and Kansas.** This clustering is offered as supporting evidence and has not been independently verified.

These are **trackers' allegations, not established fact.** They rest on amateur readings of public flight-radar history, and — as the counterarguments below show — several specific dates have been challenged.

## The TPUSA-event tie and the two-month window

Researchers connect the overlaps specifically to **TPUSA programming**, not just to the Kirks' personal travel. The flight-log thread that introduced **[SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU/overview)** summarized its 2025 movements as **"multiple NE/UT hops tied to TPUSA Faith tours"** (the researcher's characterization, not a confirmed itinerary). The **Israel/Egyptian Plane Armada** reporting likewise ties the spring-2025 Provo legs to **Kirk's Midwest push** and the recurring Northeast-to-Utah hops to **TPUSA Faith-tour stops**.

The two months before September 10, 2025 are the tightest part of the timeline. The lead jets are reported around Provo and TPUSA stops through the summer, and the fleet's presence peaks in early September: **[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)** landed at Provo on **September 4, 2025 — six days before the assassination** — and departed the **morning of September 10**, while **[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)** sat at Provo with its transponder **on but no takeoff** at the moment of the shooting before leaving on **September 13**. Because TPUSA's calendar of campus and faith-tour events ran continuously through that window, any flight overlap "near a TPUSA event" in the two months prior falls inside this same period.

## Counterarguments and fairness

The shadowing claim is **contested**, and several of the people closest to the flight data dispute it. These rebuttals are reported here for balance:

* **Erika Kirk's own denial.** Erika Kirk has reportedly pushed back on the narrative that the Israel/Egyptian jets shadowed her movements, including stating that she **stayed home in Arizona on September 10** rather than traveling. Her denial directly undercuts any "overlap" counted for that date and is reported here as her stated position.
* **[Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler)'s methodology challenge.** Commentator **Liz Wheeler** argues the tallies use **loose criteria** — counting a "match" when an Israel/Egyptian jet was within a broad region (tens of miles) and within a few days of an Erika Kirk location, rather than the same airport on the same day. Per Wheeler, *"when you narrow the parameters to Egyptian planes landing on the same day in the same location, the overlap dwindles to one — on 9/10 in Utah."*
* **The "5 of 8 are wrong" audit.** A reviewer who pulled a year of FlightRadar history said **five of the eight overlaps** Owens cited were inaccurate — claiming a plane "hadn't even left Egypt" on some listed dates. That reviewer found some entries accurate but could not source others.
* **The Provo maintenance context.** Provo's **Duncan Aviation** MRO facility has held foreign VIP-jet service work for decades, and the Egyptian Air Force contract there is documented on [the SU-BTT page](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview). That context is real and belongs on the record. What it does not do on its own is account for the September 2025 window: no work order or service booking covering these tails at Provo in that window has been produced, and [the fleet's documented factory-authorised Falcon service stops run through Wichita](/Planes/following/Wichita_KICT_2022-11-13_to_2025-08-03/overview).

### What the audit actually establishes — and what it does not

Four findings fall out of the table, and they are not all in the sceptics' favour.

**1. The list contains duplicate rows, and the duplicates are counted.** The auditor flags at least four: index 61 duplicates 31, index 63 duplicates 33, index 65 duplicates 35, and index 56 is *"a duplicate of number 14, plus one year"* — the same claimed event re-entered with the year advanced. Indices 66 and 37 both describe the same 7/7/2025 SU-BND departure. If a tally is built by counting rows, duplicated rows inflate it directly. This is a mechanical error, not an interpretive dispute, and it is the cleanest criticism on this page.

**2. The single largest failure mode the audit reports is the aircraft being in Egypt — and it is the part primary data supports least.** Row after row reads *"In Egypt," "Still in Egypt," "Cairo," "Yeppers egypt."* That column produces the 60%-different-continent figure. It is *plausible* on its face: the [SU-BTT flight log](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) shows the aircraft spending the overwhelming majority of 2025 flying 15-to-45-minute sectors around Cairo, Medina, Jeddah, Sharm el-Sheikh and El Arish, with only **three** trips to the United States all year.

But **"the jet is usually in Egypt" is a base rate, not a test of a date.** When the specific claimed days are pulled from the archives, the different-continent finding survives on **3 of the 29 rows primary data can decide**. On three others the aircraft is on the ground in the United States, at the field the sheet names, within **0.02 to 0.82 km** — while the audit has it in Egypt. The remaining 39 "Egypt" rows fall on dates no free archive can reach, so they stand unrefuted and untested at the same time.

**3. But the September 2025 core survives.** This is the part sceptics tend to skip and it belongs on the record. The audit marks **row 40 (9/4/2025, Cairo→Paris→Minot→Provo) as ACCURATE** and **row 42 (9/13/2025, Provo→Goose Bay→Paris→Cairo) as ACCURATE**, and it confirms row 41's substance while correcting its origin — the 9/10 flight happened, but it went **Provo→Wilmington→Cairo**, not Paris→Lincoln→Wilmington→Cairo. It also marks **row 35 (5/23/2025, SU-BND lands in Provo) as ACCURATE.** The audit's own verdict on row 41 puts SU-BTT departing Provo on 10 September and reaching Cairo via Wilmington on 11 September.

### A caution about the audit itself

The audit is an X posting, not a published methodology. It should be held to the same standard it applies:

* **No methodology is stated.** It does not say which tracking service supplied the "actual location," over what archive, or how gaps were treated.
* **Its own numbering is unstable.** The index runs to 67 while the summary bars count "of 65," and a second transcription pass of the same sheet, circulated separately as *"Flight Log Transcription, Page 1 / Page 2,"* assigns **different routes and different verdicts to the same index numbers** — its entry 38 reads "Leaves for Cairo via Goose Bay → Paris / Status: Correct," where the spreadsheet version has index 38 as the 7/20/2025 Omaha–Lincoln rotation. At least one of the two transcriptions is misaligned by a row.
* **It admits it cannot check the oldest rows.** Indices 43 and 44 are marked *"Data doesn't go back this far"* — so 2022 is partly outside the archive it used, in both directions.
* **It is checking routes, not overlaps.** The audit tests whether the aircraft flew the route the sheet claims. It does not independently establish where Erika or Charlie Kirk were on any date. A row can be route-accurate and still not be an overlap with anybody.

None of that undoes the duplicate-row finding or the continent finding, both of which are checkable by anyone with archive access. It does mean this audit is a strong prompt for a proper review, not a substitute for one.

## The audit has now itself been audited, against position data

The audit above is the most rigorous public test this claim has had. It is also, by its own admission, an X posting with **no stated methodology and no named archive** — it checks *routes* against somebody's flight-history screen. On **24 August 2026** every row of the reconstructed 85-row overlap register was checked a different way: against **raw ADS-B position traces** held by two independent free archives, on the claimed date and the day either side, measuring the aircraft's closest approach in kilometres to the claimed field. **This is the first time the sheet has been tested against position data rather than against another reading of a tracking website.** The per-row working is on the [overlap index](/Planes/following/overlap/overview); the archives and pulls are documented on [Flight Data Recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview).

That 24 August run was itself re-checked on **26 August 2026**, this time by reading **every position
fix in every recovered trace** rather than a summary of each day. It found the first run had refuted
ten rows it should not have. Both tallies are below, because a correction that is quietly applied is
not a correction.

| **At the claimed field** — corroborated | 24 | **25** |
| **Same metro, wrong field** — neither confirmed nor refuted | — | **1** |

archives** while the longer archive showed the aircraft **climbing out of the very airport it was
said to be 1,853 km from**. One refuted a two-leg claim whose first leg the trace confirms.

partially accurate, 60% on the wrong continent.** They still do not reconcile, and **they are not
reconciled here** — they were produced by different methods against different archives. What has
changed is that the gap is no longer symmetric. **Where both methods can reach the same row, primary
position data supports the sheet on 25 rows and the audit on 3.**

refuted by day D. Those six now read **not tested**.

Three refutations survive in the register, and only two of them are Egypt:

* **OWENS-050** — 12 May 2023, SU-BND airborne out of Paris-Le Bourget and descending into Inshas Air
  Base, Cairo, never closer than **7,034 km** to St. Louis. **Genuinely refuted.**
* **OWENS-036** — 18 June 2025, SU-BTT, **10,140 km** from Omaha — and **weaker than this site
  implied**: the entire day's holding is **five minutes of it**, 06:23 to 06:28 UTC at FL320 between
  Alexandria and El Alamein. That places the aircraft in Egypt that morning and leaves the rest of the
  day unobserved.
* **OWENS-015** — 9 May 2023, SU-BND, **1,063 km** from Goose Bay. Not Egypt, and **disputed between
  two of our own runs**: the same-day trace *confirms the St. Louis half of the row* and then runs out
  over Quebec while still tracking north-east, with the aircraft next seen over Ireland six hours
  later. The automated same-day rule scores it elsewhere; the fix-by-fix read calls it untested. The
  tally uses the automated run and the disagreement is published rather than resolved.

**The recovery work still earned its keep, but not the way this page previously said.** It was
credited here with *debunking* four rows on backup-archive data alone — OWENS-016, 029, 052 and 055.
**All four of those refutations have been retracted.** What the recovery actually delivered is the
opposite and larger result: reading the fuller archive **reversed three published refutations into
confirmations and dissolved seven more into untested.**

**And the duplicates are confirmed from the position data.** The auditor flagged index 61 duplicating 31, 63 duplicating 33, and 65 duplicating 35. All three pairs returned identical verdicts in this run — 35/65 both confirmed (SU-BND at Provo, 0.03 km, 23 May 2025), 31/61 and 33/63 both undecidable on the same claimed Wichita dates. **A duplicated row in a sheet whose headline is a count is a finding about the count**, and it is now checkable two independent ways.

### What this does not do

**It does not turn a confirmed aircraft into a confirmed overlap.** A verdict of *at the claimed airport* confirms **the aircraft half** of a pairing and nothing else. This data locates the aircraft. **It does not put Charlie Kirk or Erika Kirk anywhere**, and the Kirk half is exactly as weak as it was — thin for Charlie, close to empty for Erika, whose flight logs are [reported as erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and whose claimed locations still trace back to a `Notes` column reading *"Instagram confirms presence."*

**It does not settle the counts, and the counts stay disputed on this page.** **73 / about 23**, **72**, **"70+"**, **68 / 29** — none of those is confirmed by this run and none is averaged into another. What the run does is move 29 individual rows from opinion to record. The other 56 remain exactly where they were.

**And absence is still not proof.** The 37 *not heard* and 10 *no archive coverage* rows are holes. An empty archive response means the archive had nothing on that airframe that day — not that the aircraft was hidden, and not that it was absent. **Neither side of this argument may claim them, and that cuts against the sheet as hard as it cuts against the audit.** The corrected 25-of-29 figure is a rate on the rows that can be tested, not a claim that 25 of the 73 overlaps happened.

**Counterargument.** Twenty-five corroborated positions are twenty-five corroborated positions and nothing more. The innocent explanation for the strongest of them is the one the auditor volunteered himself: **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force maintenance account since 1999**, with its main plant at Lincoln and a facility at Provo, and *"possibly broken jet grounded in Provo"* remains the most economical reading of SU-BND's 113 days on the ground. Wichita is a Falcon service centre; Wilmington is a customs and fuel stop. A confirmed position at a maintenance field is evidence the aircraft was at a maintenance field.

## What the source sheet's own columns reveal about its method

A separate screenshot circulating with the audit shows the **Owens-side sheet itself**, and its column headers are more revealing than any of the rows. They are:

`Index` · `Date` · `Flight` · `Route` · **`Erika Location`** · `Event/Reason` · **`Overlap Window`** · **`Charlie Present`** · `Notes`

Four of those need comment.

**`Overlap Window` reads "±3 days" on every single row.** Not on some rows. On all of them, uniformly, for the full length of the sheet. This is the sheet conceding its own matching criterion in a column of its own — and it is precisely the criticism [Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler) made from the outside. A "±3 days" window means an aircraft arriving on a Monday counts as overlapping an event held the previous Friday or the following Thursday. Combined with the region-level rather than airport-level location matching, a seven-day window makes coincidental matches likely rather than remarkable — a business jet operating on a roughly monthly maintenance rotation will land inside *some* seven-day window near *some* event of a large national organisation almost every trip.

**`Charlie Present` reads "No" on the overwhelming majority of rows.** In the visible 35-row extract, it reads **"No" on 32 rows** and **"Yes" on only the final three** (3/15/2025, 3/22/2025 and 5/23/2025). That is the sheet's own answer to the question most readers assume it is about. **On its own data, these aircraft were not overlapping with Charlie Kirk.** The 73-versus-23 split reported elsewhere is visible right here in the structure: the sheet is an Erika Kirk sheet with a Charlie column that is almost always empty.

**`Event/Reason` is generic, not specific.** Across the whole visible extract the entries are variations on a single phrase: "Media appearance / TPUSA," "TPUSA event / campus," "TPUSA engagement / local media," "TPUSA / media appearances," "TPUSA appearances / media," "TPUSA / campus engagement." **Not one row names an actual event, a venue, or a city where a TPUSA event took place.** These are category labels, not citations. A reader cannot check a single one of them, because there is nothing in the cell to check against.

**`Erika Location` is often a state, not a place.** The values run "New York," "Illinois," "Massachusetts," "Kansas," "Missouri," "Utah," "Nebraska," "California," "Delaware / Nebraska," "Kansas / Delaware," "Missouri / Cairo," "Wichita / DE / potentially Giza." Some are cities; most are states; several are two states joined by a slash; one contains the word "potentially." No source is given for any of them.

**`Notes` restates the pattern instead of evidencing it.** The visible entries are: "Instagram confirms presence; state-level overlap," "Kansas is a recurring state for flights," "Multi-state overlap suggests repeated intersections," "Utah emerges as a new region in flight-overlap pattern," "Multi-stop alignment; recurring pattern," "Kansas repeated overlap," "Exact city-level overlap." Several cite **"Instagram confirms presence"** as the basis for placing Erika Kirk somewhere — a social-media post, undated and unlinked, is the sourcing for the location half of the overlap.

Taken together, the sheet's own structure answers the question the counts were meant to settle. **An overlap in this sheet is: an aircraft in the same state, within a seven-day window, during a period when TPUSA had programming, with Erika Kirk's location inferred from an unlinked Instagram post, and with Charlie Kirk recorded as not present.** That is the definition being counted 65 or 68 or 73 times.

## Where this leaves the claim

The honest summary, stating both halves:

* **The multi-year shadowing pattern does not survive audit.** Two-thirds of the claimed overlaps are wrong on the aircraft's own flight record, three-fifths put the plane on the wrong continent, the list contains duplicated rows, the matching window is a full week, the location data for the person being followed comes from Instagram, and the sheet's own `Charlie Present` column says "No" on nine rows in ten. The **73**, **72**, **70+**, **68** and **68/29** figures are all built on this sheet.
* **The September 2025 Provo window does survive it.** The audit's own verdicts confirm SU-BTT arriving Provo 4 September, departing 10 September for Wilmington and reaching Cairo on 11 September; SU-BND landing at Provo 23 May and departing 13 September; and the [full Flightradar24 exports](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) confirm the same. That is a real, documented set of facts about two foreign-registered aircraft at a small Utah airport in the days around the assassination. The location-by-location breakdown of every airport in the claim is in the **[Following section](/Planes/following/overview)**.
* **And there is an ordinary explanation for the surviving facts that has to be dealt with.** **Duncan Aviation has held the Egyptian Air Force's maintenance account since 1999**, under a five-year direct contract announced in October 2015, with its main plant at **Lincoln, Nebraska** and a second facility at **Provo, Utah**. That single fact accounts for the Omaha→Lincoln hops, the Wichita visits, the recurring six-day dwells, and the Provo relationship. It does not account for *why September, why Provo rather than Lincoln, and why that morning* — which is exactly where the remaining work is.

The investigation is better served by the narrow surviving claim than by the wide one. A seven-day-window spreadsheet with duplicate rows is what a hostile reviewer will reach for first, and reaching for it lets them dismiss the September facts along with it.
