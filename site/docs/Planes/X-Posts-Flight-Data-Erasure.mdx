# X posts on flight data reported erased

**The entire "they erased the flight data" conversation on X is one account and one amplifier.**
[@hurtfeelingzday](https://x.com/hurtfeelingzday) put *N102DZ / FlightRadar24 / May 2026 / I have
the screenshots* into circulation on **20 June 2026**. Stew Peters carried it to **279,454 views on
1 July 2026** with the line *"Innocent people don't behave like this."*

**The most-viewed post in the whole cluster is not about that jet at all.** It is
[@urinalwallpoet on N888KG](https://x.com/urinalwallpoet/status/1965960095239647254), posted the
morning after the assassination — **752,920 views** — saying a private plane left Utah, never filed,
transponder off from there on. The government answered that one directly: the FAA and the
Transportation Secretary both said the transponder was never turned off.

**And the aircraft at the centre of the following allegation are not in the erasure conversation at
all.** A keyword search for SU-BTT, SU-BND, SU-BTU, SU-BTV, SU-BGM, T7-ELL or EJM36 plus
erased / deleted / removed from a named tracker returned **zero posts**. So did a `from:RealCandaceO`
search for erased / deleted / wiped / removed plus flight / transponder / logs / flightradar.
**Four different "gone" claims are being treated as one**, and the table two screens down separates
them.


This page is the public log of **what X.com and Twitter posts say** about flight-tracking data being erased, blocked, wiped, or gone after the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) assassination. It is **not** a finding that any tracker deleted a record, that any living person requested a deletion, or that any aircraft was tasked. **Status of every living person named below: Alive.** They are quoted as speakers, not as perpetrators.

The tested version of the single biggest claim on this page — that [N102DZ](/Planes/N102DZ/overview) was taken off FlightRadar24 — already lives on **[Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased)** and **[What A 403 Actually Means](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/What-A-403-Means)**. The primary data this investigation actually pulled back lives on **[Flight Data Recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview)**. The overlap-count debate, a different claim, lives on **[X Discussions of the Following Planes](/Planes/following/x_discussions)**. This page is the X-side of the *erasure* conversation: which system, which plane, which person, what they thought they were looking at.

The topic list and the query rule (full post text; the word "summarized" never goes in a Grok search) are on **[X search topics — flight data reported erased](/Planes/X-Flight-Data-Erasure-Topics)**. Twenty topics, one query each, 26 August 2026.

Nothing on this page is a finding of wrongdoing by Erika Kirk, Kash Patel, Derek Maxfield, Baron Coleman, or any other living person. Owner-requested privacy blocks and seven-day free-tier windows are ordinary features of commercial flight trackers. Allegations that someone "deleted" a record are unverified claims circulating on X, not court findings.

## Cluster A — N102DZ, FlightRadar24, May 2026

**The claim, as the posts state it.** Sometime between April and May 2026, the public FlightRadar24 history for Gulfstream V tail **N102DZ** — described as Charlie Kirk's jet, then Erika Kirk's — stopped showing 2025 flights. Researchers who had taken screenshots from late September 2025 through 5 January 2026 say they still have the rows. Erika Kirk later invited the public to check "my flight log." The posters say that invitation is hollow because the app now returns "No flights found."

**The dates, in the originator's own posts, in order:**

* **20 June 2026** — first returned posts. [Screenshots Sept 2025–Jan 2026](https://x.com/hurtfeelingzday/status/2068304816581849384); [account locked after posting the thread](https://x.com/hurtfeelingzday/status/2068381246342713801); unlock after [@DocHolidayz75](https://x.com/DocHolidayz75) tagged Elon Musk, xAI, Barnes Law, CBS Austin, KVUE. Wipe dated *"as of May 2026"* and also *"some time after April 2026."*
* **24 June 2026** — [the "Erika Kirk deleted" wording](https://x.com/hurtfeelingzday/status/2069688064620843318) (4,608 likes, 151,489 views), a month before Tyler Robinson's preliminary hearing. Screenshot of the app saying **"No flights found."**
* **1–3 July 2026** — Stew Peters puts it on air. The "innocent people don't behave like this" line is his, not the originator's.
* **3 July 2026** — screenshots tied to a claimed Miami / Opa-locka (OPF) pattern and a Netanyahu-family geography claim. That geography claim is **not established here** and is recorded only because it is what the poster said they were using the logs to look at.
* **13 July 2026** — the FR24 wipe bundled with unrelated TPUSA video-edit claims (Hegseth clip, Aspen audio, YouTube takedowns). Different records, one sentence.
* **20 July 2026** — window narrowed to *"sometime between April & May of 2026."* Contrast aircraft named: Air Force Two still up; [SAM 99-0404](/Planes/SAM-99-0404/overview) still up.
* **21 July 2026** — the Bari Weiss invitation clip, 211,658 views.
* **31 July 2026** — a claimed taxiway coincidence with another private aircraft (Miriam Farbstein named by the poster; **living person, no wrongdoing established, recorded only as the reason the poster gave for caring about the wipe**). Window shown to Stew: **4 September 2025 – 5 January 2026**.
* **16–17 August 2026** — same sentences still being posted.

**What they say they were tracking.** Not "was the plane at UVU." The 2025 history, specifically: post-assassination legs, a 16 December 2025 Nashville appearance, Camarillo, and whether N102DZ sat on the same ramp as other private aircraft. Occupancy of any leg is a separate question. An aircraft track never establishes who was aboard. See [Charlie's and Erika's aircraft](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview).

**The replies that cut against the cover-up reading, from the same thread.**

On 24 June 2026 [Baron Coleman](https://x.com/baroncoleman/status/2069811968316236113) replied to the "Erika deleted" post:

> "All of the information from this plane and all other planes used by TPUSA and Paradise Charters are readily available at adsbexchange. Many planes fly under the LADD program, which limits data sharing to commercial sites (like the ones you are using). There's a lot wrong with this case. But this isn't one of them."

The same day [@researcherdaisy](https://x.com/researcherdaisy) said N102DZ flights were designated under **LADD** (the FAA's Limiting Aircraft Data Displayed program), that Elderberry Chestnut LLC holds a fractional share, and that Paradise Jets holds the charter certificate. The originator did not drop the claim; later posts say Coleman "acknowledged that the flight logs were deleted but said that they weren't relevant" and that "the same data can be retrieved from another site."

[@grok](https://x.com/grok/status/2073102650808119474) on the Stew Peters thread, 3 July 2026: the absence on FlightRadar24 is "standard: private owners routinely request FAA LADD privacy blocks, which FR24 respects. It filters display for security—not deletion or cover-up." (A different Grok reply in the same cluster mixed N102DZ up with [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) and said Kirk did not own a plane. That mix-up is recorded so it is not reused.)

**What this investigation already tested, restated so this page does not quietly disagree with its sisters.** Opened in a real browser on 24 August 2026, `flightradar24.com/data/aircraft/n102dz` returns **HTTP 200** with the identity block intact. So do all fifteen other case tails and four unrelated controls. The empty history table is the **seven-day free tier**; the unrelated control N628TS prints the identical "could not find data" message to within eight bytes. The HTTP 403 a script gets is bot protection for the entire site, including FlightRadar24's home page. **The claim "the page was taken down" is refuted.** A narrower claim that 2025 history was purged from the *paid* table and later restored is beyond that method in either direction, and this page does not assert it. The underlying ADS-B position record was **not** destroyed: this site holds 66 days of N102DZ traces, 35 of them inside the reported April–June 2026 wipe window. Method: [What A 403 Actually Means](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/What-A-403-Means). Per-tail counts: [Per-Aircraft Status](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status).

**The one thing the recovery does not produce is an Erika Kirk itinerary.** That is still the missing document. See [Erika Kirk's flights](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights).

**Do not merge those threads into this one.** Transponder-off is an aircraft behaviour. A privacy block is a website behaviour. A seven-day paywall is a product behaviour. The following-pattern is a pairing claim. This page is only the "a tracker dropped the record" sentence.

## Cluster C — N888KG: a gap, a block, and a government denial

[N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) generated the first "the plane disappeared" wave, in the 48 hours after the shooting, before anyone was talking about N102DZ's 2025 table.

[@urinalwallpoet](https://x.com/urinalwallpoet/status/1965960095239647254), 11 September 2025 — 4,019 likes, 752,920 views: *"A private plane with tail number N888KG left Utah after the shooting. landed in Wendover, never filed, no transponder from there on. Desert airstrip, cameras off."* Later reporting pointed the route at **Page, Arizona**, not Wendover. The owner, **Derek Maxfield**, said the outbound leg was two pilots and no passengers, radar services were cancelled by mutual agreement near a non-towered field, and the flight was pre-scheduled and unrelated. **Status: Alive. Naming him is not an accusation.**

[@DanceTheSkys](https://x.com/DanceTheSkys/status/1965994108138442808) the same night posted a tracker screenshot: *"This is supposed to be public info. The government is blocking the tracking of this flight."* Grok-on-X replies in January 2026 describe that block as an **owner privacy request on FlightAware**, "a common option."

On 21 September 2025 [Kash Patel](/People/kash-patel) wrote that after interviews with the pilot and the FAA, **the transponder was not turned off**. On 28 September 2025 [Secretary Sean Duffy](https://x.com/SecDuffy/status/1972439646849356018) said FAA radar still captured the aircraft, the transponder never went off, and the flight matched its plan. [Lead Stories](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/09/fact-check-private-jet-n888kg-is-not-part-of-investigative-inquiry-into-assassination-of-charlie-kirk.html) recorded the jet as not part of the investigation.

Two different "gone"s got stacked on one tail: a **mid-flight ADS-B gap** on 10 September, and a **FlightAware owner block** that made the public page look empty afterwards. Neither is the N102DZ / May 2026 / 2025-history claim. This site keeps them on [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview).

## Cluster D — N708JH: the block the press independently reported

[N708JH](/Planes/N708JH/overview) is a 2019 Gulfstream G550 registered to the United States Government, Department of Justice. It is in this investigation because of a tracking-block claim. It is **not** a Kirk-family or Egyptian tail.

After October 2025 coverage of FBI Director Kash Patel's use of the jet for personal travel, FlightAware's public page for N708JH started returning the owner/operator block message. [@TheRickWilson](https://x.com/TheRickWilson/status/1984977560988749913), 2 November 2025 — 14,955 likes: *"Yes, Kash Patel has ordered his use of the FBI jet's ADSB blocked."* [@willsommer](https://x.com/willsommer/status/1984775620752982077) the same day: *"the FBI has pulled the jet Kash Patel frequently uses from flight tracking database FlightAware."* The Guardian reported the on-site message: *"not available for public tracking per request from the owner/operator."* Kyle Seraphin said he paid to keep tracking it; The Intercept, 14 November 2025, said **ADS-B Exchange still showed the aircraft**.

**This is the one row on this page where a named tracker, a named tail, and a named requester line up in press reporting, not only in an anonymous research account.** The motive X assigned is personal-travel privacy after a news cycle, not hiding 10 September 2025. Government aircraft being off public maps is also the default for this category; see the [N708JH](/Planes/N708JH/overview) page. It belongs here because it is a documented example of the *mechanism* the N102DZ posts are guessing at.

## Cluster E — N1098L: the logs posters say were *not* deleted

[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview), the LASAI / HADES Global 6500, is the opposite of an erasure story on X.

No post returned by these queries claimed this tail's tracker history had been taken down. The claim on X about N1098L runs the other way: its FlightAware page stayed publicly reachable through October 2025, and the low-altitude passes near Orem sit in the public ADS-B record where anyone can pull them. Andy Loue's August 2026 note states it plainly — the N1098L data "is real, publicly archived." The complaint attached to this tail is that a still-public record is being **ignored**, not that a tracker removed it. See [ISR Operations](/Planes/ISR-Operations) and [LASAI Fleet](/Planes/LASAI-Fleet/overview).

## Counterarguments that have to travel with every row

* **A seven-day empty table is a paywall until a control aircraft proves otherwise.** FlightRadar24 says so under the table. N628TS matched N102DZ. SU-BTT rendered history on the same day because it had flown recently. See [What A 403 Actually Means](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/What-A-403-Means).
* **LADD is a real FAA program.** [File a block request](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/ladd/request). It is the mechanism Baron Coleman, researcherdaisy, and FlightAware's own engineering blog describe. Presence of a block is not evidence of a crime.
* **ADS-B Exchange, airplanes.live, and adsb.lol do not take LADD orders the way FR24 and FlightAware do.** That is why Coleman pointed at ADS-B Exchange, and why this investigation recovered N102DZ through May 2026 from archives FlightRadar24 does not control. [Per-Aircraft Status](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/Per-Aircraft-Status).
* **An aircraft track is not a passenger list.** Recovering N102DZ in full still does not place Erika Kirk on a leg. The 73-overlap tally is still measured against a set of her locations nobody has published as a dated list. [Erika Kirk's flights](/Planes/following/Erika_Kirk_Flights).
* **Absence of a volunteer-network trace is not a deletion.** Dates adsb.lol was asked about [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) and held nothing are a coverage fact, not a takedown. Parked, out of coverage, or a wrong claimed date all look the same from here.
* **N1098L cuts the other way:** some of the most-discussed tails in this case still have public FlightAware pages, and the live argument there is that investigators are not reading a record nobody took down.
