# N79SC Erika's Probable Plane

**N79SC** is a Learjet 60 — the same type as [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview), Charlie Kirk's
speaking-event plane. Added 17 September 2026, when the recovered flight archives were matched against
the few dated events Erika Kirk did on her own. **On those days N79SC was at the airport, and N582MM
was somewhere else.**

**Status: Erika Kirk — Alive.** Nothing on this page is a finding of wrongdoing by her or by anyone.

:::info Two Learjets, two cities, the same two days
**On 21–22 March 2025 Erika Kirk spoke at a women's conference in San Clemente, California, without
Charlie. N79SC flew Scottsdale → Santa Ana and back. On the same days N582MM was in Duluth, Minnesota.**

N79SC was also in **Washington, DC on 14 October 2025** and **New York on 8 December 2025**, both days
Erika Kirk had events there.

**The caveat, at the same size.** We could find only a few dated events of Erika's own before September
2025, so this rests on **3–4 dates**. It is a strong lead, not an established fact. **And a flight
record never places a person aboard** — this shows which plane was at the airport on her days, not
that she was on it.
:::

## What the geographic sweep recorded

The [geographic sweep](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview) asks which aircraft were on the ground
within 50 miles of every sourced Kirk or TPUSA event, without starting from a tail number. N79SC came
out of it on its own:

* On the ground near a sourced event on **26 event-days**, in **13 cities** across **8 states**
  (Arizona, California, Florida, Georgia, Pennsylvania, Texas, Utah, Washington).
* **17 of those hits are Phoenix**, which looks like its home base. A Phoenix plane near Phoenix events
  is expected and proves little.
* **It was never on the ground near any of the control cities** swept on the same days.
* The dates include **11 September 2025 near Orem, Utah** — the day after the assassination — and
  **21 September 2025 at Glendale, Arizona**, the day of the stadium memorial.

Those circles include Charlie's events as well as Erika's, so this list is not by itself evidence of
whose plane it is. The dated events of Erika's own are. Source:
`site/docs/Planes/following/apis/public_open_source/data/analysis/recurrence_hardtest.csv` and
`geo_recurrence_all_aircraft.csv`.

## Counterpoints

* **3–4 dates is a small base.** A charter or shared aircraft could line up with a few dates by chance.
  More of Erika Kirk's own dated events are needed to test it.
* **Being at the airport is not being aboard.** No ADS-B record carries a passenger list.
* **Going private is ordinary.** Owners lawfully ask the FAA and the tracking sites to hide their
  aircraft. It shows nothing about who asked, or why.
