# SU-BTT Israel/Egyptian Jet

An Egyptian-registered **Dassault Falcon 7X** (tail number SU-BTT) — referred to on [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)'s podcast as **"the yellow plane"** — is reported to have been on the ground in or near Provo, Utah from September 4 through the morning of September 10, 2025, departing the United States in the hours around [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s assassination. It is the lead aircraft in what X researchers call the **"Israel/Egyptian Plane Armada"** — a cluster of Israel/Egyptian government Falcons and Gulfstreams ([SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV), [SU-BGM](/Planes/SU-BGM)) that trackers say overlapped with [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) events for years. Every claim below comes from independent open-source researchers working from public ADS-B records; little of it has been independently verified, and the innocent explanations are laid out near the end of this page. See also **[Sept 10 Flight Timeline](./Sept10-Flight-Timeline)** (transponder window vs. hospital timing) and **[Egyptian Crew Hotel](./Egyptian-Crew-Hotel)** (La Quinta Orem lodging tip).

## Aircraft identification

* **Tail number:** SU-BTT
* **Type:** Dassault Falcon 7X (long-range business jet) — reported by trackers as built in **2014**
* **Registry / operator:** Egyptian-registered — described as Israel/Egyptian Air Force / Israel/Egyptian Government use
* **Nickname in coverage:** "The yellow plane" (Candace Owens's color-coded shorthand; SU-BND was "the blue plane")
* **Home base in Utah window:** Provo Municipal Airport (PVU), ~7 miles from Utah Valley University

## Flight history relevant to the case

Independent trackers, amplified by Candace Owens, describe two US visits in 2025. Dated bullets below reflect their published timelines:

* **July 20, 2025:** Reported as the jet's first-ever trip to the United States — to an Army base in Nebraska (Omaha / Lincoln corridor).
* **First US visit (August):** Paris → Omaha/Lincoln (Aug 17) → Wilmington, DE (Aug 23) → Paris (Aug 24).
* **September 4, 2025:** Second US visit — routing from **Paris-Le Bourget (LBG)** via **Minot, ND** into the **Provo/Orem, UT** area, then stayed. Trackers stress this full inbound arrival and alleged drop-off came **six days before** the assassination.
* **September 10, 2025:** Departed Utah for **Wilmington, DE** — the day of the UVU shooting. Trackers describe this outbound leg as visibly "lighter" than the inbound flight. One widely shared post summarized it bluntly: *"Egyptian military planes in Provo at Provo Municipal Airport on 9/10/25. Tail# SU-BTT flew in on 9/4/25 and departed the morning of 9/10/25."*
* **September 11, 2025:** Departed the United States for Egypt (Cairo) via Wilmington.

Owens's team claims **18 people flew in but far fewer flew out**, and that the same aircraft had earlier appeared near the **2025 Rotary "Peace 2025" conference in Istanbul (Feb 20–22, 2025)** before leaving the country ahead of the assassination.

### Conflicting accounts of the Sept 10 departure

Public sources do not agree on how SU-BTT left Utah on September 10. Three versions circulate, and they cannot all be true:

* **7:08 AM departure:** Some flight logs show SU-BTT leaving Utah for Wilmington at roughly 7:08 AM MT, hours before the shooting.
* **Power-up, go dark, reactivate:** Owens's "nuclear flight logs" presentation describes the jet powering up at Provo Municipal around **9:05 AM**, going dark, then reactivating **17 minutes after the shot** and idling on the tarmac until **1:29 PM**.
* **Dugway origin:** A separate OSINT thread claims it never took off from Provo at all (see below).

These accounts are reported here as competing allegations, not settled fact.

## Long-term tracking pattern (2022–2025)

Beyond the September 2025 window, Owens and independent trackers allege a multi-year pattern of SU-BTT overlapping with the Kirk family's movements:

* According to their tallies, the Israel/Egyptian jets logged roughly **73 overlaps with Erika Kirk** and about **23 with Charlie Kirk** across the United States (Omaha, Wichita, Provo, plus domestic and international legs).
* Owens has framed the odds of that many location overlaps happening by chance as vanishingly small — citing a "0.000000001%" coincidence figure — and her team has said it cross-referenced the flight legs against **rental-car records** for the Kirks' ground movements. The probability figure is a rhetorical estimate, not a calculated statistic, and is reported here as her framing rather than as a verified number.
* Some reported overlaps are said to include international flights and legs connected to Israel.
* One widely shared post framed the pattern in absolute terms: **"72 times those exact planes followed Erika Kirk, not a plane like it, those 2 planes. That is statistically near impossible without intent."** This is the trackers' rhetorical framing, not a verified statistic — the published counts themselves vary (68, 70+, 72, and 73 all appear across different posts).
* A separate analysis claims the Israel/Egyptian jets' US stops cluster in **five states — Missouri, Delaware, Utah, Nebraska, and Kansas** — which posters read as following the Kirks and TPUSA events. Critics note that several of those states (notably Kansas) host major aviation-maintenance hubs, which would also explain repeat visits.
* A separate **dual-plane tally** circulated on X claims that the blue (SU-BND) and yellow (SU-BTT) jets *together* overlapped with Erika Kirk's locations **68 times** between 2022 and September 2025 — including overseas trips — and that **29** of those overlaps also coincided with Charlie Kirk's location. This 68/29 figure is a different count from the 73/23 tally above and is reported here as an independent researcher's claim, not a reconciled number.
* Two of the most-shared verbatim framings of these tallies, reported here as the trackers' own words rather than as verified statistics:
  * *"Two specific Egyptian-registered planes (tail numbers SU-BTT and SU-BND…) showed repeated overlaps with Erika Kirk's locations and travel patterns. These overlaps occurred 73 times between 2022 and September 2025."*
  * *"The Egyptian military plane was tracking Erika Kirk NOT Charlie Kirk! Out of those 68 instances, 29 of them included Charlie."*
* Owens's timelines also single out **Turning Point Faith events** specifically, describing the Israel/Egyptian jets as allegedly **shadowing domestic political targets** in a systematic pattern. This is her characterization of the ADS-B data, not an official finding.
* Posters amplifying these tallies have repeatedly framed the silence around the Israel/Egyptian jets as a question — "why is the FBI silent?" — pointing to the aircraft being on the ground at Provo on the day of the assassination. This is rhetorical framing by the trackers, not an official finding.
* Trackers point to public ADS-B data they say shows the aircraft appearing near the family's locations with unusual frequency.

These claims rest on public ADS-B records circulated by independent researchers and have not been independently confirmed. They are reported here as allegations made by the named trackers, not as established fact.

## Reported flights to Israel

Independent compilers — credited on X to **[@nihilox](/Influencers/x/nihilox)** and amplified by **[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)** (Project Constitution) — claim SU-BTT made dozens of trips toward Israel across the year. They allege the public logs read **Cairo-to-Cairo (CAI → CAI)** or show no destination because, they say, the jet flew with its **transponder off** and actually landed in Israel. The dates they list (presented here as the trackers' compiled claims, not confirmed flights) include:

* **Jan:** 05, 12, 26 — **Feb:** 11, 16, 18, 23 — **Mar:** 18 — **Apr:** 05, 08 — **May:** 27
* **Jun:** 14, 18, 21, 22 — **Jul:** 05, 08 (two flights), 13 — **Aug:** 05
* **Sep:** 20 (two), 21, 30 (two) — **Oct:** 13, 21, 22, 26 — **Nov:** 18, 30 — **Dec:** 02, 07

[@nihilox](/Influencers/x/nihilox) summarized the pattern in absolute terms, posting what the account called **"irrefutable evidence SU-BTT regularly flies into Israel."** The alleged ADS-B signature, as described by these trackers, is consistent: the jet departs Cairo, **flies south, then goes dark** — dropping off public tracking — and reappears only after the leg into Israel would have been completed. One thread also cites an **eye-witness** said to place the **Egyptian chief of intelligence landing in Israel shortly after** one such blackout. [@nihilox](/Influencers/x/nihilox)'s stated conclusion is that **Egypt is lying about its relationship with Israel**, and that this points to a possible **Israel connection to the Israel/Egyptian jets that were on the ground the day of the assassination**. These are the trackers' claims and an unverified witness account, not confirmed flights.

A related interpretation circulating on X is the **"Paris meetings" theory** — that SU-BTT's repeated stops at **Paris-Le Bourget** functioned as **neutral ground for high-level government meetings** (described by some posters in "dead drop" terms). This is speculation offered by independent researchers, not an established finding.

One of these legs is **corroborated** by mainstream reporting rather than only by ADS-B screenshots: per **Israeli Channel 12 News**, SU-BTT flew **Cairo to Israel on October 21, 2025**, reportedly carrying **Hassan Rashad**, the head of Egyptian intelligence. Rashad is a foreign public official, and an Egyptian intelligence chief flying to Israel is consistent with the two countries' long-standing diplomatic and security contacts. This single corroborated trip does not establish that the earlier listed legs occurred as described, nor that any of them connect to the Charlie Kirk case.

## The Duncan Aviation / contractor claims

Multiple **unconfirmed** witness accounts shared on X (which the authors themselves flag as uncorroborated) allege the Provo stop was not routine maintenance:

* Sources said to be at **Duncan Aviation** (the MRO operator at Provo) reportedly described **4–6 subcontractors** boarding with Egyptian passports but carrying badges reading **"U.S. Department of Defense Liaison."**
* The contractors are alleged to have **not flown out again**, and to have **tested "counter-UAS" (anti-drone) gear** at Provo Airport between Sept 4–10.
* Provo Tower (PVU) logs are claimed to show a **"no-scan priority clearance"** federal override on Sept 4, with no passenger manifests recorded.
* The same threads name two alleged passengers ("Maj. [Ahmed Khalil](/People/ahmed-khalil)" and "Eng. [Omar Nassar](/People/omar-nassar)") as people who, the author says, *need to be investigated further* — these are unverified names floated online, not confirmed identities, and no wrongdoing has been established.
* A further claim alleges the jet **dropped off Egyptian military personnel or subcontractors miles from UVU** on the day of the assassination, framed by posters as a possible **staging area**. One widely shared post described the aircraft as *"sitting on the tarmac… dropped off members of the Egyptian military."* No evidence supports this; it is reported here strictly as an online allegation.

Treat this entire section as raw, unverified online testimony. None of it has been confirmed by any official source.

## Theories about the aircraft's role

Researchers covering SU-BTT have floated several interpretations, framed as open theories rather than conclusions:

* **Foreign surveillance** — that a foreign-government aircraft may have been monitoring Charlie Kirk, Erika Kirk, or TPUSA events (the "intel-vacuum" theory).
* **A possible joint operation** — tied to the Wilmington routing discussed below.
* **Counter-drone testing or a drone role** — [John Cullen](/Influencers/john-cullen) has speculated the aircraft was associated with drones; other threads tie it to the counter-UAS claims above.

Owens has stressed these were not, in her telling, simple maintenance stops. Skeptics disagree — see the counterpoints section.

Whether any federal body ever ran foreign-aircraft leads like these to ground is itself an open question: former NCTC director [Joe Kent](/US_Intelligence/joe-kent) has said on the record that his center's parallel review of possible foreign ties to the assassination was ordered to stop once Tyler Robinson was in custody, before such leads were exhausted.

## The Dugway departure claim

According to an X thread by **[@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution)** (Project Constitution) building on flight-tracker analysis, SU-BTT did not actually take off from Provo Municipal Airport on September 10. That account framed it as: *"The Mysterious Egyptian Government Jet SU-BTT Didn't Depart from Provo… It Took Off from DUGWAY PROVING GROUND… transponder OFF… Classic stealth protocol."* The specifics they list:

* Trackers showed SU-BTT "materializing" mid-air near Utah with no recorded takeoff from PVU.
* The reported origin was **Dugway Proving Ground's Michael Army Airfield** — a restricted US military test site west of the Great Salt Lake.
* The aircraft is reported to have flown with its **transponder OFF** until clearing the **R-6404 restricted airspace**, then re-acquired tracking, with public trackers defaulting to the nearest public airport (Provo) as the displayed origin.

If accurate, this would place an Israel/Egyptian government jet operating from a restricted US military airfield in the days around a high-profile political assassination. The claim is unverified.

## The Wilmington destination

SU-BTT's September 10 destination — **Wilmington, DE** — has been linked in public reporting to **920 North King Street, One Rodney Square, Suite 404**, an address publicly associated over the years with the DEA, the Department of Justice, and the Secret Service; some posters describe it as a **joint DHS/FBI/DEA office**. According to Candace Owens, an Israel/Egyptian plane **landed and stayed overnight on September 10** (assassination day), with an alleged **foreign-dignitary pickup transferred to 920 N King St**. Researchers connect this to claims that a foreign dignitary may have been shuttled from Provo to Wilmington for a joint US/foreign operation on the day of the assassination. Some X threads have gone further and speculated about the identity of a possible passenger, naming a veteran Egyptian diplomat — but that is unverified online speculation about a living person who has not been charged with anything, and no evidence ties any named individual to the flight.

The post was headlined: *"BOMBSHELL: The ASSASSINATION Plane From Sept 10th Is Back — EGYPTIAN Military Aircraft SU-BTT Just TOUCHED Down in Kansas."* The thread raised questions about possible **"special cargo"** and meetings, noting Wichita's status as an aerospace and defense hub.

### What is new in this segment

Four claims in the transcript above had not previously appeared on this page. All four are the speaker's assertions, sourced to an anonymous tipster, and none has been independently verified:

* **A named tip source.** Owens says the passenger information came from an unnamed foreign correspondent to her "DCIA tips box" who claimed proximity to someone aboard the flight, and she frames the resulting claim as a direct challenge to FBI Director **Kash Patel** to dispute it. An anonymous tip relayed second-hand is not evidence, and Owens does not publish anything that would let a reader check it.
* **An alleged passenger name.** She states that one of the four people aboard the September 10 Provo→Wilmington leg was **travelling under the name "Walid Mahmoud."** She herself stresses she is describing a *name in use*, not a verified identity, saying governments "just print passports" and calling such names "obviously dead ends." No public record confirms that any person by that name was aboard, no such person has been identified, charged, or accused of anything by any authority, and nothing here should be read as alleging that any individual — real or otherwise — did anything wrong. ## Counterpoints and innocent explanations

In fairness, several facts cut against the sinister reading:

* **Provo has a legitimate jet-service hub.** Duncan Aviation runs a full-service maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) facility at Provo Municipal Airport — airframe, engine, avionics, paint, and interior work. A foreign VIP Falcon stopping there for service is not inherently suspicious.
* **The Wichita stops fit aircraft maintenance, not surveillance.** Researchers note SU-BTT made **"no fewer than 8 stops"** in **Wichita, Kansas** — but Wichita is the **"Air Capital of the World,"** home to major aviation-maintenance operations including **Yingling Aviation**, an FAA Part 145 facility that services **Dassault Falcon** jets (with Dassault-Wichita State University ties dating to 2015). Repeated Wichita visits by a Falcon 7X are exactly what a routine maintenance relationship would produce.
* **Liz Wheeler's "loose criteria" rebuttal.** Commentator **[Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler)** argues the overlap spreadsheets count a match whenever an Israel/Egyptian plane is within roughly **±3 days** and the same broad **region (50–100 miles)** — not the same day at the same airport. In her telling, **"when you narrow the parameters to Egyptian planes landing on the same day in the same location, the overlap dwindles to… one. On 9/10 in Utah."** Other reviewers add that many entries contain **UTC-vs-local time errors** and wrong-location/wrong-continent mistakes, with one estimate that a large share of the listed overlaps are incorrect.
* **A skeptic's audit of the logs.** At least one reviewer who pulled a year of FlightRadar history said **"5 of the 8" overlaps Owens cited were wrong** — claiming the plane had not even left Egypt on some of the dates — and noted that **Erika Kirk herself said she stayed home in Arizona** on Sept 10 rather than being in "Nebraska/Delaware." That reviewer disputed the "73 overlaps" framing entirely.
* **SU-BND stayed for months.** The companion "blue plane" reportedly sat in Provo for nearly four months, which fits a long maintenance or storage stay more than a surgical operation.
* **John Cullen walked back the "strong association."** In later notes, investigator **John Cullen** — who had earlier speculated about a drone link — is reported to have **dismissed a strong association** between SU-BTT and the assassination, pointing to **long-standing Duncan Aviation maintenance contracts said to date back to 1999** and characterizing some legs as routine **ferrying flights**. Critics also note a **Dassault Falcon is not serviced every two weeks**, so the maintenance-versus-conspiracy question turns on how often a VIP jet realistically visits an MRO — a debate, not a settled point.
* **The extra evidence never fully materialized.** Skeptics say much of the additional corroboration Owens promised — beyond the screenshots — **never fully materialized** in the form critics could check.
* **Transponder gaps and "materializing" tracks** can result from terrain shadowing, receiver coverage gaps, and trackers snapping an origin to the nearest public field — mundane data artifacts, not proof of a stealth launch.

### What the shape of that record actually shows

Read as a whole rather than as a highlight reel, the log has a very regular structure, and it is not the structure of a surveillance mission:

* **Every US visit is the same trip.** Cairo → Paris-Le Bourget → one US city → *sit for six days* → Wilmington → Cairo. Three times in three months, to the day-count.
* **The 15- and 16-minute Omaha→Lincoln hops are positioning flights.** Omaha and Lincoln are about 50 miles apart. A wide-body-class business jet does not fly a quarter-hour hop between two Nebraska cities for any operational reason except to reposition to a facility. Both July and August visits do it.
* **Wilmington (ILG) is on the way out every single time**, and only ever on the way out. Wilmington is a well-known **customs and fuel stop** for transatlantic business aviation. Its recurrence is what an outbound clearance point looks like, not what a destination looks like.
* **The six-day dwell is constant** across all three visits regardless of city — Lincoln, Lincoln, Provo. A tasking that was following a person would produce variable dwells tracking that person's schedule. A tasking that is *work being done on the aircraft* produces a roughly fixed turnaround.

None of that is exculpatory in itself, and the September 4–10 Provo stay is the one visit that lands somewhere other than the two Nebraska fields. But it means the honest question on this page is narrower than the one usually asked: not *why was this jet in America*, but *why was this one visit to Provo instead of Lincoln.*

## Duncan Aviation has held an Egyptian Air Force maintenance contract since 1999 — and its main plant is in Lincoln, Nebraska

This is the single most consequential piece of context on this page, and it has been almost entirely absent from the discussion.

A **Duncan Aviation** press release dated **Thursday, 10/29/2015, 14:31**, carried by the trade publication *DOM — Director of Maintenance*, is headlined **"Duncan Aviation Signs a Five-Year Contract With the Egyptian Air Force."** Its text:

> …announces the company has entered into a five-year direct contract agreement with the Egyptian Air Force (EAF) for aircraft maintenance services. Duncan Aviation has been managing the maintenance and repair of the EAF fleet of Falcon 20 aircraft since 1999.
>
> Duncan Aviation's Manager of Government & Special Programs Dave Shipperbottom says the fact that the EAF has chosen to fly over closer maintenance companies and bring their aircraft to Duncan Aviation "reveals a lot about our relationship with them. All the technicians at the squadron level are great to work with. They know us and we know them. It is a good relationship."
>
> **The first EAF aircraft arrived at Duncan Aviation's Lincoln, NE, facility on October 20 for a Z-check. The next will arrive in November for a C-check, Major Corrosion Inspection and complete paint.**
>
> For more information, visit Duncan Aviation at booth #N4910 during this year's NBAA convention to be held Nov. 17-19 in Las Vegas…

Set that against the flight log above and the fit is exact:

* **Lincoln, Nebraska is the destination of every one of the repeated Nebraska visits.** Omaha is not the destination; it is the international arrival airport, and the jet immediately hops fifteen minutes west to Lincoln. Duncan Aviation's Lincoln plant is the endpoint.
* **The Egyptian Air Force has been flying its aircraft to that specific plant since 1999** — a twenty-six-year relationship, on the record, in a trade press release published a decade before this case.
* Duncan's own manager anticipates and answers the obvious objection in the quote itself: the EAF **"has chosen to fly over closer maintenance companies"** to get there. The long ferry is the documented norm, not an anomaly.
* **The visit types named — Z-check, C-check, Major Corrosion Inspection, complete paint — are multi-day to multi-week events.** A six-day dwell is at the short end of that range and entirely ordinary for a lighter scheduled inspection.

This does not explain the **Provo** stop, which is the only US destination in the 2025 record that is not Lincoln. But it substantially explains **Omaha, Lincoln, Wichita, and the recurring Wilmington clearance stop** — which between them account for the large majority of the "overlap" entries in the shadowing spreadsheets. Any tally that counts a Lincoln arrival as an overlap with a Kirk or TPUSA event in Nebraska is, on this evidence, counting a maintenance rotation that predates Charlie Kirk's public career by roughly fifteen years.

The counter-argument is available and should be stated: a long-standing maintenance relationship is also exactly the cover an operational flight would use, and Duncan Aviation runs a facility at **Provo Municipal Airport** as well as at Lincoln, which is the innocent explanation already offered for the September stay. Neither reading can be settled from the flight log. But a reader who did not know about the 1999 contract was being asked to find the Nebraska pattern mysterious, and it is not.

She calls SU-BTU "the purple plane." That May 27 – June 2 window is load-bearing for her argument, because it is what links a person across two aircraft.

If those exports are accurate, then **the aircraft in Provo on May 27 was SU-BTV, not SU-BTU**, and the dates Owens gives — arrival May 27, departure June 2 — match SU-BTV's stay to the day. SU-BTU was at Provo April 8–14.

This is a correction that can be checked by anyone, and it should be checked before it is relied on in either direction: two possibilities are open, and this page does not claim to know which holds. Either the tail number in Owens's account is transposed — **SU-BTU and SU-BTV differ by one character**, and her own segment acknowledges tail numbers being conflated in this coverage — or the compiled export is wrong. What is not open is treating the SU-BTU identification as settled. Everything downstream of it, including the inference that a person moved between the "purple plane" and the September 10 flight, and the further inference from the Egyptian presidency's website that SU-BTU is a presidential-fleet aircraft, rests on which tail was actually at Provo in late May. **If the tail is wrong, the person-across-aircraft link dissolves.**

Note the wider point this exposes: **five Egyptian-registered jets rotated through the same handful of US fields on the same route template in 2025** — SU-BTT, SU-BTU, SU-BTV, SU-BGM and SU-BND. When five near-identical aircraft fly near-identical routes to the same airports, attributing a given arrival to a specific tail from memory or from a summary is unreliable. The hex codes are the only safe handle: **SU-BTT is 0101D3, SU-BTU is 0101D0, SU-BTV is 0101D1**, and [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) is **01003E**.

## The "Walid Mahmoud" name and a caution about identification

Owens is explicit that the name she reports from her anonymous tipster is **a name in use, not a verified identity** — "we are dealing with governments… these people just print passports… these are obviously dead ends" — and she floats the innocent possibility that the person was the pilot. That caution needs restating because of what has happened to the name since.

Circulating alongside the SU-BTT material is a screenshot of a **Google Scholar** profile for **Waleed E. Mahmoud**, listed as being in the **Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, Suez Canal University, Egypt**, with a verified email at `science.suez.edu.eg` and a research area of "Natural science/Physics."

There is no evidence whatsoever connecting that academic to this case. "Waleed Mahmoud" is among the most common name combinations in Egypt — the equivalent of pulling a "James Smith" out of a US faculty directory. An Egyptian materials physicist at a public university has no apparent relationship to a government flight, and none has been alleged by anyone with knowledge. **This page names the profile only to mark it as a misidentification and to ask that it stop circulating.** He is a private individual, he has not been accused of anything by any authority, and no reader should draw any inference about him from its appearance in this material.

## The Provo Airport badge access list

A screenshot of a document hosted at **airport.provo.gov** circulates in this material — page 4 of 4 of a badge-holder roster, footed **"Exported 09/11/25"** and **"Exported by Inga Jones."** The export date is the day after the assassination, which is the reason it draws attention.

The visible rows list organisation, surname, given name, a uniform date of **9/30/2025**, and an "x" flag against some entries. The organisations shown on the visible page are ordinary airport tenants and services: **Pilot Makers** (a flight school), **Provo Fire Department**, **Simco Electric**, **Signature Flight** and Signature Flight/Hangar Tenant, **Smith Detection**, **Stone Security**, **Tie-Down**, **UVU** (the university operates a flight-training program at PVU), and **Westland Construction**.

What this document is, and is not:

* It is a **badge-holder roster**, i.e. a list of people cleared for movement-area access at a public regional airport. It is the sort of record any airport maintains and periodically exports.
* The **UVU entries are unremarkable** — Utah Valley University runs an aviation program based at Provo Airport, so university staff and students holding ramp badges is expected, not surprising.
* **The 9/30/2025 date in every row is not an event date**; it appears to be a uniform expiry or review date applied across the roster.
* The **09/11/25 export date** is the only genuinely notable feature, and it has at least two readings: someone pulled the list in response to the previous day's events, or the export is a routine periodic one that happens to be dated then. Nothing in the document distinguishes them.

**The names on this roster are private individuals — flight instructors, electricians, firefighters, security staff, students, contractors — who have not been accused of anything by anyone.** They are not reproduced here. The document is described so that readers encountering it know what it is: an airport badge list, not a manifest, not a visitor log, and not a record of anyone who was aboard any aircraft.

## Bright Star 25 — the US-Egypt exercise running two days before

A slide circulating in this material is a **U.S. Air Force news release** dated **Sept. 8, 2025** — two days before the assassination — from the **U.S. Central Command Area of Responsibility**:

> U.S. Air Force Airmen demonstrated aeromedical evacuation operations to Egyptian Air Force service members during exercise BRIGHT STAR 25, showcasing lifesaving skills in combat and humanitarian environments. The training scenario simulated patient loading and in-flight medical care, giving crews opportunities to rehearse complex, time-sensitive missions.
>
> BRIGHT STAR, first held in 1980, is one of the largest and longest-running military exercises in the Middle East. The aeromedical evacuation training highlighted the U.S.-Egypt partnership.
>
> "It is a great teamwork scenario that requires the crews to work together and mitigate challenges to effectively medically care for patients," said Lt. Col. Jerry M. Earl Jr., an aeromedical evacuation planner assigned to U.S. Air Forces Central Command.

Posters have circulated this release as context for why Egyptian military aircraft would be operating in a US-adjacent posture that week. It cuts in the direction of ordinariness rather than away from it: **Bright Star is a forty-five-year-old bilateral exercise**, it is conducted in Egypt rather than in the United States, and a routine US-Egypt military relationship is the baseline against which any Egyptian Air Force movement should be read. It establishes that the two air forces work together constantly. It establishes nothing about Provo.

## Track This Aircraft

Research this aircraft's flight history yourself on both major flight-tracking sites. They differ in one important way:

* [FlightRadar24 — SU-BTT](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/aircraft/su-btt) — large commercial tracker that honors the FAA's [LADD](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/ladd) block list, so flights an owner or the government has asked to hide can be missing.
* [ADS-B Exchange — SU-BTT](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/?reg=SU-BTT) — volunteer-fed, unfiltered tracker that does **not** honor LADD, so it often shows blocked aircraft and tracks the filtered sites leave out.

## Note on Registration & Control

This aircraft carries an Egyptian registration — the **SU-** prefix on its tail number is Egypt's national civil registry, so the Egyptian registration itself is real and not in dispute. What is *not* settled is **who ultimately controlled the jet.** If an Egyptian front company was registered as the aircraft's operator, that registration — Egyptian on paper — may have been arranged on behalf of an intelligence service from another country. That open possibility is why this page labels the aircraft **"Israel/Egyptian"**: the label flags the unresolved question of control. It is **not** a claim that the jet was Israeli-registered or Israeli-owned — the public record does not establish that, and nothing here asserts as fact that any government or person controlled it.
