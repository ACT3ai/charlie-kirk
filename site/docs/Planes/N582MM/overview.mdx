# N582MM TPUSA Aircraft

Tail number **N582MM** is an aircraft that public commentary describes as frequently used by **[Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) (TPUSA)** insiders. It is named in the proposed Charlie Kirk disclosure laws over a reported **October 2025 movement from Fort Huachuca, Arizona to Kalispell, Montana**. Standalone sourcing on this tail number is thin — the TPUSA-insider association is a **reported characterization**, not an established fact, and naming the aircraft is not an accusation against any person or organization. The page exists because citizen investigators have flagged the tail in OSINT threads and because the disclosure-law draft lists it among the aircraft for which it seeks records.

The counterpoint is that Glacier Park International is a normal destination and that two flights to one airport, weeks apart, can be unrelated.

## What the FAA registry says about this tail

Public commentary describes N582MM as "the TPUSA aircraft." The FAA Civil Aviation Registry does not support that description of its **ownership**:

* **Type:** **Learjet 60**, built 2001, serial 227 — an 11-seat midsize business jet. Earlier descriptions on this site called the type "not confirmed in public reporting." It is confirmed now.
* **Registered owner:** **DOJA Contracting LLC**, Mesa, Arizona. Also listed on the registration: TK Enterprises LLC and JRO Aviation LLC.
* **Registered to Turning Point USA?** **No.** A search of the complete FAA registration database returns **no aircraft registered to Turning Point USA, Turning Point Action, or any related Turning Point entity** — see [TPUSA Aircraft](/Planes/TPUSA-Aircraft/overview).

This narrows the claim rather than refuting it. "TPUSA insiders used this plane" is a statement about **who was aboard**, and private aircraft manifests are not public — the registry can neither confirm nor refute it. What the registry does establish is that the aircraft is owned by an Arizona LLC, not by the organisation. **Naming a registered owner is not naming a wrongdoer**, and nothing on this page asserts that DOJA Contracting LLC or anyone associated with it did anything improper.

### The counterargument

The plain reading of all of it is that N582MM is an ordinary midsize business jet with a fourteen-year
public flight record of ordinary business routes, operated over that period by more than one
registrant, and that its Provo appearances — in 2015 and since — are the same unremarkable thing.
Nothing we pulled shows otherwise, and the 2015 Provo legs cut directly against reading a Provo
arrival by this tail as meaningful in itself. **On this evidence the null hypothesis is the
best-supported one**, and the reported Fort Huachuca–Kalispell leg remains reported rather than
verified: we hold no primary trace of it.

The claim this section answers is not about N582MM. It is the broader claim that this category of
record has been erased. **We pulled four years of primary track and recovered fourteen-year-old
flight tables for this aircraft, from free archives, on demand.** What came back is unremarkable
business aviation. That is a finding in itself: a record that reconstructs this easily has not been
scrubbed, and the honest way to report a recovery is to report what it actually shows rather than
what we hoped it would.

Research this aircraft's flight history yourself on both major flight-tracking sites. They differ in one important way:

## Where this page fits

A tail number written into draft legislation deserves more scrutiny than the sourcing behind it currently supports, and documenting that gap is this page's job — an aircraft described in commentary as TPUSA-adjacent, named in the [disclosure-law drafts](/Fix/overview) over a reported [Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview) to Kalispell leg, catalogued alongside the [FAA answer about TPUSA-owned aircraft](/Planes/TPUSA-Aircraft/overview). Both endpoints of that reported leg recur elsewhere: Fort Huachuca as the Army counterintelligence school running through the [US intelligence section](/US_Intelligence/overview), and Kalispell as the destination [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) was repositioning toward when it dropped over Utah — a coincidence of geography that is not by itself a connection, and a separate claim about a separate aircraft. [Law 2, the intelligence services disclosure act](/laws/US_Intel/Law_2_US_Intel) is where this tail actually does work: it names specific numbers Congress would be forced to explain, the Egyptian aircraft SU-BTT, SU-BND, SU-BTU and SU-BGM alongside the HADES jet, which turns a rumour about an aircraft into a testable demand for a record — the same move [Law 1 makes against the DoJ and FBI](/laws/DoJ_FBI/Law_1_DoJ_FBI) and [Law 3 makes against the investigation itself](/laws/Require_to_Investigate/Law_3_Require_to_Investigate).
