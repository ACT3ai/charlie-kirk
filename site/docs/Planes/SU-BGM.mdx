# SU-BGM Israel/Egyptian Jet

**SU-BGM** is an Egyptian-registered **Gulfstream IV** that independent open-source researchers count as the fifth aircraft in what they call the **"Israel/Egyptian Plane Armada"** — a group of Israel/Egyptian government Falcons and Gulfstreams ([SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV), and SU-BGM) that public flight logs show routing through Provo, Utah during 2025. Standalone reporting on this specific tail is thin: SU-BGM has drawn far less individual attention than the lead jets SU-BTT ("the yellow plane") and SU-BND ("the blue plane"). Its significance in the case is almost entirely as a *member of the fleet pattern*, not as a separately documented aircraft. The full analysis of the armada theory lives on the hub page — see **Israel/Egyptian Plane Armada** — and everything below should be read as one tail within that larger, still-unverified picture.

## Aircraft identification

* **Tail number:** SU-BGM
* **Type:** Gulfstream IV (long-range business jet), per the flight-log thread that catalogued the fleet
* **Registry / operator:** Egyptian-registered — described in coverage as Israel/Egyptian Government / Israel/Egyptian Air Force use
* **Color nickname:** None. Unlike SU-BTT (yellow) and SU-BND (blue), [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)'s color-coded shorthand assigned **no color label** to SU-BGM; it "appears in flight logs" without one.
* **Role in coverage:** One of the "three more" Israel/Egyptian jets (with SU-BTU and SU-BTV) added to the original two-plane story to make the "five-plane armada" framing.

## Flight history relevant to the case

The only tail-specific legs published for SU-BGM come from a single raw-flight-log thread (attributed to [@WeAre_TheElite](/Influencers/x/weare-theelite) and amplified by [@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution) / Project Constitution and [Sam Parker](/Influencers/sam-parker)). They describe **spring 2025** activity, not the September window:

* **April 10, 2025:** A **Provo local flight** of roughly 55 minutes (a short out-and-back from Provo Municipal Airport).
* **April 16, 2025:** **Goose Bay → Paris → Provo**, reported as landing at Provo around **6:00 PM** — the classic transatlantic routing (Cairo/Paris via Canada) the researchers attribute to the whole fleet.

The thread characterizes this as a **"spring swarm during Kirk's Midwest push,"** placing SU-BGM in Provo months before the September 10 assassination rather than in the immediate days around it. That distance matters: a spring flight is a long way from the afternoon Charlie Kirk was killed, and readers should weigh it against the [account of how he actually died](/Mic/overview) rather than treat proximity in a log as proximity to the act. No passenger manifest, crew list, or purpose for either leg has been publicly established. These two legs are the entirety of the tail-specific public record for SU-BGM; the rest of its relevance is inherited from the fleet pattern described on the hub page.

It is worth flagging one absence: unlike SU-BTT, SU-BTV, and SU-BTU — for which [@nihilox](/Influencers/x/nihilox) and others published month-by-month lists of alleged Cairo-to-Israel flights — **no Israel-flight date list was ever compiled for SU-BGM**. Its only published role is being named as the fifth tail in the Provo "armada," and posters mainly cited it to urge Candace Owens to investigate the group further. No specific overlap between SU-BGM and a documented Erika Kirk, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk), or [TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) event location has been demonstrated.

## Theories about the aircraft's role

Because so little is published about SU-BGM on its own, the theories attached to it are the **fleet-level** theories, framed here as open allegations rather than conclusions:

* **Recurrent foreign-fleet presence** — that five distinct Israel/Egyptian government jets cycling through a single US municipal airport across 2025, overlapping with TPUSA event geography, is itself the anomaly worth investigating. SU-BGM is cited as evidence of the *pattern's breadth*, not for any single suspicious flight.
* **Surveillance / "intel-vacuum" reading** — the same foreign-monitoring theory applied to the lead jets is extended to the whole armada by association.
* **Pre-event staging** — the spring-2025 legs are read by the thread's author as part of a build-up ("circling Utah like predators pre-strike"). This is speculative narrative framing, not documented fact.

## Counterpoints and innocent explanations

Several considerations cut against reading SU-BGM as sinister, and they should be weighed prominently given how thin the tail-specific record is:

* **[John Cullen](/Influencers/john-cullen)'s maintenance-contract dismissal.** Independent investigator **John Cullen**, who has pushed hard on other aircraft in this case (notably [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) and [N55906](/Planes/N55906)), has *discounted* the Israel/Egyptian-jet armada theory, attributing the Provo visits to routine **aircraft maintenance contracts** rather than to an operation. Provo Municipal Airport hosts **Duncan Aviation**, a full-service maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) facility, and a foreign VIP Gulfstream cycling through for service — including short local post-maintenance test flights like the April 10 "Provo local" — is an ordinary explanation that fits the data.
* **Spring timing, not the kill window.** SU-BGM's documented legs are in **April 2025**, roughly five months before the assassination. They do not place this tail in Provo in the September 4–10 window that gives the lead jets their weight.
* **Data artifacts.** Transatlantic VIP routing via Goose Bay and Paris, and short local hops, are exactly what maintenance ferrying and crew positioning look like on ADS-B; none of it is unique to a covert mission.
* **Skeptic audit of the overlap counts.** At least one reviewer who pulled a year of FlightRadar history said most of the "overlaps" Owens cited for the lead jets were wrong. That skepticism applies with even more force to a secondary tail like SU-BGM, for which no specific Kirk-event overlap has been demonstrated at all.
* **Liz Wheeler's "loose criteria" rebuttal.** Commentator **[Liz Wheeler](/People/liz-wheeler)** argued that the overlap spreadsheets counted a "match" whenever an Israel/Egyptian jet was within roughly **three days** and the same broad **region** (50–100 miles) as Erika Kirk — not the same day at the same airport. Per Wheeler, when the parameters are narrowed to an Israel/Egyptian plane landing the same day in the same location, "the overlap dwindles to… one. On 9/10 in Utah." Under that stricter test SU-BGM produces no qualifying overlap at all.
* **Erika Kirk's denial.** Erika Kirk has pushed back on the broader Israel/Egyptian-plane theory, and in remarks attributed to her she said she "had no idea there were Egyptian planes" and that "it could very well just be a coincidence," disputing being in the claimed locations. Her response addresses the fleet narrative generally rather than this specific tail.

## Open questions

1. Who were the passengers and crew on SU-BGM's April 16, 2025 arrival into Provo, and what was the purpose of the April 10 local flight?
2. Was SU-BGM serviced at Duncan Aviation's Provo MRO, and do maintenance records account for both legs?
3. What is the operational relationship, if any, between SU-BGM and the other four Israel/Egyptian tails (SU-BTT, SU-BND, SU-BTU, SU-BTV)?
4. Is there any SU-BGM presence in the September 2025 window, or is its record genuinely confined to the spring?
5. Who authorized recurring Israel/Egyptian Air Force VIP access to a non-international US municipal airport across 2025?

SU-BGM pairs with [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) as the **two older Gulfstreams** in an otherwise Falcon 7X fleet. Like SU-BND, its export carries **no operator entry** — a database detail worth noting whenever this aircraft is described as "Egyptian military," which is an inference from the **SU-** registration prefix and from its company in the Provo cluster rather than something the registry states.

### The logged Provo legs

The export shows three consecutive legs, and all three involve Provo:

| Date | From | To | Duration | STD | ATD | STA | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **10 Apr 2025** | **Provo (PVU)** | **Provo (PVU)** | **0:55** | 4:11 PM | 3:16 PM | 4:11 PM | Landed 4:11 PM |
| 16 Apr 2025 | **Provo (PVU)** | Goose Bay (YYR) | 4:55 | 5:10 PM | 12:14 PM | 8:10 PM | Landed 8:10 PM |
| 16 Apr 2025 | Goose Bay (YYR) | Paris–Le Bourget (LBG) | 5:06 | 9:01 PM | 9:16 PM | 7:09 AM | Landed 7:22 AM |

Three details in that short table carry more weight than their length suggests.

### Two Egyptian jets were at Provo simultaneously in April 2025

[SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU) was on the ground at Provo from **8 April to 14 April 2025**. SU-BGM flew a Provo→Provo sortie on **10 April** and did not depart Utah until **16 April**.

So for roughly a week in April, **two Egyptian-registered jets were parked at the same small Utah regional airport at the same time** — and a third, [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND), would arrive on 23 May and stay until September. This is five months before the assassination, and it has not previously been set out on this site.

That simultaneity is genuinely double-edged, and both readings deserve stating:

* **It strengthens the maintenance explanation.** Operators send aircraft to the facility they have a relationship with. Two airframes from the same fleet overlapping at the same MRO is ordinary — it is what a shop with an account looks like.
* **It weakens the "one anomalous visit" framing in both directions.** The September 2025 Provo stay is not an isolated event to be explained; it is the fourth or fifth in a series stretching back to at least April. That undercuts the reading of September as uniquely significant, and it also means the Provo relationship is deeper and longer-running than the assassination-window coverage implies.

### The 10 April Provo→Provo round-robin

A **55-minute flight that departs Provo and lands back at Provo** is the same signature that [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) produced on **5 September 2025** with a 1-hour-8-minute Provo→Provo sortie. Two different Egyptian jets, five months apart, each flew one closed-loop local flight out of Provo.

The mundane reading is a **post-maintenance functional check flight** — an aircraft coming out of a heavy inspection is flown for roughly an hour to verify systems before release, and it returns to the shop that did the work. Two of them is exactly what two completed maintenance visits produce. The less mundane reading is a local pattern flight of some other kind. The tracker log alone cannot separate them, and this page does not claim to.

What can be said is that if the 5 September SU-BND flight is treated as anomalous, consistency requires treating the 10 April SU-BGM flight the same way — and an April closed-loop, five months before the event, is very hard to fit into any narrative about the assassination.

### Goose Bay, not Wilmington

SU-BGM leaves North America via **Goose Bay, Newfoundland**, not via Wilmington, Delaware. So does [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND) on both its inbound (23 May) and outbound (13 Sep) transits.

That is a clean split in the fleet's routing, and it maps onto aircraft type rather than onto anything else:

* **The three Falcon 7Xs** ([SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT), [SU-BTU](/Planes/SU-BTU), [SU-BTV](/Planes/SU-BTV)) route out through **Wilmington (ILG)** on every single rotation.
* **The two Gulfstream IVs** (SU-BGM, SU-BND) route through **Goose Bay (YYR)**.

The Falcon 7X is a long-range trijet with roughly 5,900 nm of range; the Gulfstream IV is an older design with materially less. **Goose Bay is the classic North Atlantic tech stop for aircraft that cannot make the crossing in one leg.** The split is a range-and-fuel split, not a mission split — which is worth knowing, because the recurring Wilmington stop has been read on this site and elsewhere as significant in itself. On this evidence it is significant only for the aircraft that need less fuel to reach it, and the fleet's other half does not use it at all.


## The April window, tested against the TPUSA event calendar

SU-BGM has the thinnest US record of the five Egyptian tails — one Provo local sortie on **10 April 2025** and a departure on **16 April 2025**. Testing it against Charlie Kirk's public itinerary takes one row, and the answer is unambiguous.

The calendar used is `site/docs/Planes/following/tpusa_events.csv` — **139 dated rows, 4 January 2022 to 29 October 2025**, built from TPUSA postings, campus press and event listings, each row carrying a certainty flag. It is the spine of the [Following section](/Planes/following/overview).

### The one ground window

| On the ground | Airport | Nearest TPUSA event **in Utah**, any year | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 Apr – 16 Apr 2025 | **Provo (PVU), UT** | 10 Sep 2025 — Charlie, Orem, UT — The American Comeback Tour, Utah Valley University | **147 days** |

**There is no overlap.** The nearest TPUSA event in the state of Utah is the UVU appearance five months later — the one at which Charlie Kirk was killed. In the whole 139-row calendar there is no Utah event in April 2025, and none within three months of SU-BGM being there.

### Where Charlie Kirk actually was that week

| Date | Where | Event |
|---|---|---|
| 8 Apr 2025 | Urbana, IL | American Comeback Tour — University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign |
| 8 Apr 2025 | Normal, IL | American Comeback Tour — Illinois State University |
| **10 Apr 2025** | **West Lafayette, IN** | American Comeback Tour — **Purdue University** |
| **11 Apr 2025** | **East Lansing, MI** | American Comeback Tour — **Michigan State University** |

On **10 April 2025** — the day SU-BGM flew its 55-minute closed loop out of Provo Municipal — Charlie Kirk was on stage at Purdue, in Indiana, roughly 1,500 miles away. The next day he was in Michigan. He was not in Utah at any point in that week, and neither was any TPUSA event.

That is worth stating plainly on a site that argues the following pattern is real: **for this tail, on this evidence, the pattern is not there.** SU-BGM's presence in the [Provo cluster](/Planes/following/Provo_KPVU_2024-04-19_to_2025-09-13) is a fact about the fleet's relationship with Provo Municipal Airport, not a fact about Charlie Kirk's movements. Adding it to a "five-plane armada" headline without this test attached is how a real anomaly — the September window — gets dismissed along with the padding around it.

### What this test does and does not settle

* **It tests Charlie's side, not Erika's.** Of 139 rows, exactly **one** places Erika Kirk at a TPUSA event before 10 September 2025. The tallies that produced the 68/73 numbers were measured against *her* claimed locations, and her flight logs are reported erased — see [Erika's Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Nothing here refutes those numbers; it records that **no public source reproduces them for this tail**.
* **The calendar records public events only.** It does not capture private travel, and it cannot rule out that someone connected to the case was in Utah that week.
* **A clean miss is still a finding.** It is the reason this page treats SU-BGM as evidence about the *breadth of the Provo relationship* and not as evidence about the assassination.

## Track This Aircraft

Research this aircraft's flight history yourself on both major flight-tracking sites. They differ in one important way:

* [FlightRadar24 — SU-BGM](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/aircraft/su-bgm) — large commercial tracker that honors the FAA's [LADD](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/ladd) block list, so flights an owner or the government has asked to hide can be missing.
* [ADS-B Exchange — SU-BGM](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/?reg=SU-BGM) — volunteer-fed, unfiltered tracker that does **not** honor LADD, so it often shows blocked aircraft and tracks the filtered sites leave out.

## Note on Registration & Control

This aircraft carries an Egyptian registration — the **SU-** prefix on its tail number is Egypt's national civil registry, so the Egyptian registration itself is real and not in dispute. What is *not* settled is **who ultimately controlled the jet.** If an Egyptian front company was registered as the aircraft's operator, that registration — Egyptian on paper — may have been arranged on behalf of an intelligence service from another country. That open possibility is why this page labels the aircraft **"Israel/Egyptian"**: the label flags the unresolved question of control. It is **not** a claim that the jet was Israeli-registered or Israeli-owned — the public record does not establish that, and nothing here asserts as fact that any government or person controlled it.
