# N1098L Spy Plane

**N1098L** is a **Bombardier Global 6500** business jet operated by **LASAI Aviation II LLC**, a US Army intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) contractor whose control fell under **Leidos** in 2021. The aircraft is publicly documented as part of the Army's **[HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) program** (High Accuracy Detection & Exploitation System), which is designed around the Global 6500 airframe and has been written about as a platform for **air-launched drones** and signals collection. On September 10, 2025 — the day [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was assassinated at Utah Valley University — N1098L flew an unusual low-altitude, low-speed profile near the Orem/UVU corridor, and it has become the single most-discussed aircraft in the citizen investigation. This page lays out what is documented, what is disputed, and what remains an open theory.
No ADS-B track, tasking order, or sensor product from this aircraft has appeared in the [prosecution of Tyler Robinson](/Tyler_Robinson/overview), even though the flight overlapped the shooting window almost exactly.

## Aircraft identification

* **Tail number:** N1098L (previously registered as **N911PV**)
* **Type:** Bombardier Global 6500 (G6500)
* **ICAO hex:** A0299E
* **Callsign on Sept 10, 2025:** AXLE10 (also rendered **AXEL10** in some posts)
* **Program affiliation:** US Army **HADES** (High Accuracy Detection & Exploitation System) ISR — sometimes referenced in the notes alongside the "Athena" program. The investigation notes also tie the aircraft to an **AXLE21**-style mission designation, framed by some posters as a "training" cover for the ISR tasking.
* **Operator:** LASAI Aviation II LLC — a defense contractor specializing in ISR missions for the US Army; control reportedly fell under **Leidos** in 2021
* **FAA-listed operator address:** 10660 Aviation Lane, Manassas, VA 20110 (per FAA registration records cited in the investigation notes)
* **Other reported contractor associations:** Citizen investigators have also tied the broader HADES ISR training/contracting context to **Sierra Nevada Corp** and **SAIC** (now part of the Leidos lineage)
* **Fleet sister ship:** [N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview), another LASAI Global 6500 supporting Army reconnaissance — indicating N1098L is one airframe in a fleet, not a one-off charter

A note on ownership: some online commentators pointed to a New York address (888 7th Ave, Fl 42) reportedly tied to LASAI's filings and noted it matched the offices of **Bill Ackman's** Pershing Square Capital Management, prompting speculation that Ackman "owns the plane." This claim is unverified and conflicts with the FAA registration and the LASAI/Leidos defense-contracting trail, which point to a government ISR operator rather than private ownership. It is noted here only because it circulated widely; there is no confirmation, and the address overlap alone establishes nothing.

## Flight history relevant to the case

According to publicly available ADS-B data circulated via Flightradar24 and ADS-B Exchange, and the investigation notes built on that data:

* **~07:48 MDT** — Departed **[Biggs Army Airfield](/Planes/Airport-Biggs-Army-Airfield) (KBIF)**, El Paso, TX.
* **~10:38 MDT** — Logged as arriving **Glacier Park International Airport (KGPI)**, Kalispell, MT (a roughly 2h50m repositioning leg). The Utah passes below fall along this corridor.
* **~11:47 AM MDT (≈3 hours before the shooting)** — A descent to roughly **600 ft AGL** over the Orem / Utah Lake area. Some accounts put this initial drop near **650 ft**.
* **12:23 PM MDT** — Charlie Kirk is shot at UVU.
* **~12:24–12:48 PM MDT (≈20–25 minutes after the shot)** — A second low pass, variously reported between **~335 ft and ~204 ft AGL** at roughly **118–129 knots**, within about **1–2 miles** of the UVU campus, before the aircraft continued out of the corridor. Some accounts describe this leg differently — the aircraft **descending to ~300 ft and slowing to ~150 knots** while **looping around Orem** — with one investigator timing the dive at roughly **30 seconds after the shot**.

Specific FlightAware legs have been shared by investigators as the open-source backbone of the timeline:

* **2025-09-10 / 1348Z — KBIF → KGPI** (Biggs Army Airfield to Glacier Park International), characterized by some posters as a possible setup/calibration leg.
* **2025-09-10 / 1732Z — KGPI → KBIF**, the return leg that includes the Orem dive; some posters frame it as a possible data-collection run.
* From there the aircraft is tracked continuing on to **KGPI**, **KHII** (Lake Havasu City, AZ), **KSNS** (Salinas, CA), and back to **KBIF**.

These leg labels (calibration, data collection) are the posters' interpretations, not confirmed mission descriptions.

The investigators framing this as suspicious emphasize the *repeated* low-and-slow geometry: a heavy business jet descending from cruise altitude to near stall speed twice over the same area on the day of a political assassination. The competing benign account (see Theories) describes the same two maneuvers as routine training passes.

Investigator **[@hurtfeelingzday](/Influencers/x/hurtfeelingzday)** summarized the profile this way in a widely shared post: *"ON 9/10/2025 air force jet N1098L mission call sign AXEL10 ... descended to 300 ft, slowed to 150k and looped around Orem. At 1127 MT, it left FCA and did the same low alt/speed loop around Orem."* (The "air force" label in that quote conflicts with the Army-ISR/LASAI-Leidos contracting trail documented above; it is reproduced as written.)

Source records on the altitude and speed numbers are not fully consistent — figures of 600/335 ft and 204 ft, and 116/118/129 knots, all appear across different posts. The discrepancy is itself an open question: the investigation notes flag that **some of N1098L's flight segments are not fully listed in public tracking data**, which makes a single authoritative track hard to assemble from open sources alone.

After September 10, investigators continued tracking the aircraft. The notes record it on the ground at **Corpus Christi International Airport (CRP), TX** as of early October 2025, and flag scrutiny of **later legs toward the Washington, D.C. area**. Separately, the notes claim LASAI Aviation aircraft were also observed **circling Washington, D.C. on and immediately after January 6, 2021** — offered by investigators as pattern context, not as proof of anything about September 10.

## Theories about the aircraft's role

These are competing, open interpretations. None is confirmed, and there is no official statement naming the crew, the mission authorization, or the tasking for the September 10 sortie.

* **Routine drone-retrieval training (benign account).** A widely circulated explanation in defense-aviation discussion holds that **drone-retrieval training** was the primary purpose of N1098L's Orem passes, characterizing the two low maneuvers as "textbook drop/recovery simulations." Commentators such as **[@TJPHager](/Influencers/x/tjphager)** have also argued the airframe was *not* built out to launch drones at all. This is the principal counterpoint to the surveillance theories.
* **Real-time ISR / SIGINT collection.** Citizen investigator **[John Cullen](/Influencers/john-cullen)** (@I_Am_JohnCullen) has, in his public commentary, described N1098L as a CIA-linked or military ISR asset capable of drone launch/recovery or signals-intelligence collection, and theorized it could have been conducting real-time surveillance of the event. Cullen and others stress that if the aircraft was ISR-tasked, the federal government would hold airborne sensor records of the kill zone. In Patreon and YouTube commentary titled *"Why Was a Pentagon HADES Spy Plane at the Scene of Charlie Kirk's Assassination, Twice That Day?"*, Cullen has gone further and promoted a drone launch/recovery theory in which an armed drone — rather than the named suspect — allegedly fired the shot. This is Cullen's stated theory, not a confirmed finding, and it has not been substantiated.
* **Drone deployment / "drone strike setup."** The most aggressive theory ties the low passes to **drones reportedly seen over the campus** that day — small, fast (~150 mph), hard to see but caught on phone cameras. Investigator [**Blake Bednarz**](/People/blake-bednarz) reported spotting 16 drones; the theory speculates the aircraft dropped drones before the shooting and recovered them after. Posts circulating on X also tie N1098L to **Fort Huachuca** (the US Army's intelligence and drone-operations base in Arizona), claiming the aircraft's "interesting" maneuvers alongside UVU connect it to Army drone and ISR programs based there. This Huachuca link is an unverified OSINT claim, not a documented tasking record, and is unproven.
* **Airborne command/relay station ("Task Force Charlie").** Investigator **[@raumgeist](/Influencers/x/raumgeist)** has theorized that N1098L served as an airborne command center or relay station passing data to **[Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview)** as part of what that poster calls "Task Force Charlie," allegedly providing software coordination for the operation, with a possible ground station on campus or carried in a backpack. The same thread chain alleges **pre-event meetings** linked to Fort Huachuca. This is [@raumgeist](/Influencers/x/raumgeist)'s stated theory and is unverified; no tasking record supports it.
* **"Spy/drone swarm plane on site."** In the wider X discussion, some users have flatly labeled N1098L a *"spy/drone swarm plane on site,"* tying it to the drones reportedly seen over campus. This is an OSINT characterization, not a documented capability claim, and is unverified.
* **Part of a broader "sophisticated operation" narrative.** Some citizen theories fold the aircraft into a larger alleged operation involving claims of **tunnels** and **wrong-direction shooting** at the scene, describing the day as a *"demonstration of latest cutting-edge warfare techniques."* These are speculative claims circulating online, presented here as reported allegations rather than established fact; none has been confirmed.
* **Skeptical refutation (for balance).** Other commentators reportedly dismiss the armed-drone-deployment theory outright as nonsense, arguing the airframe was never configured to launch weaponized drones and that the low passes are fully explained by routine ISR training. **[@TJPHager](/Influencers/x/tjphager)**, by his own account, initially set out to refute John Cullen's drone-deployment claim, and has debated the aircraft's ISR capabilities — noting it carried only interior equipment with no visible exterior modifications matching the full HADES **ME-11B** contracted specifications that critics say a true drone-launch/HADES platform would require. In this reading the jet was **not equipped to full HADES contracted specs**. This refutation is included so the aggressive drone theory is not presented as settled.
* **Warrants a federal investigation, not a dismissal.** Independent voices including [**Candace Owens**](/People/candace-owens) and [**Ian Carroll**](/People/ian-carroll) argue the geometry, timing, and ISR capability of the aircraft justify a formal federal investigation rather than a training-exercise dismissal — a position about process, not a conclusion of guilt.
* **Rogue-faction theory (attributed, unverified).** The notes recount an OSINT account's claim of a conversation with a **National Counterterrorism Center (NCTC)** contact whose informal working theory was that a "rogue faction" was responsible and that N1098L's maneuvers were hard to explain as routine. This is a secondhand, unverifiable account included for completeness, not as fact.

Because LASAI is a defense contractor, **crew identities for the September 10 flight have not been publicly confirmed**. Competing crew lists have circulated online, but none is verified, and standard trackers (FlightAware, Flightradar24) do not publish personnel for private special-mission jets; this page does not name unverified individuals. (For why the contracting layer is opaque, see the [ISR Program Context](/Planes/ISR-Operations) hub.)

## Open questions

1. Who authorized N1098L's low-altitude passes near a major political event, and under what tasking?
2. Were drones in fact deployed or recovered during the passes, as the most aggressive theory alleges?
3. Who crewed the aircraft, and were any of them in contact with US or foreign intelligence?
4. If N1098L was ISR-tasked, do airborne sensor records of the UVU area exist, and have they been preserved?
5. Why are some of the aircraft's September 10 flight segments missing from public ADS-B data?
6. What was the aircraft's tasking on the return leg ~25 minutes after the assassination, and on later legs toward Washington, D.C.?


## The aircraft record card

A Flightradar24 aircraft-history banner for this tail, widely circulated in the OSINT threads, gives the airframe identity:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Aircraft | **Bombardier Global 6500** |
| Type code | **GLEX** |
| **Mode S / ICAO hex** | **A0299E** |
| **Serial number (MSN)** | **60098** |
| Airline (as listed) | **Private owner** |
| Operator (as listed) | — *(no operator entry)* |
| Age | **2 years** (build date given as **Feb 2023**) |
| Type photo credit | © SpotterPowwwiii / Jetphotos |

Two entries there deserve attention.

**The operator field is blank and the airline field reads "Private owner."** For an aircraft alleged to be flying an intelligence mission, the public database says only that it belongs to a private party. That is not evidence against the ISR reading — it is the *expected* record for a contractor-operated special-mission aircraft, which is registered to a corporate or trust entity rather than to a government. But it means the ISR attribution on this page comes from flight behaviour and from the airframe type, not from any registry entry.

**The build date, February 2023, matters for the HADES timeline.** The US Army's High Accuracy Detection and Exploitation System program is built on the **Bombardier Global 6500** airframe, and its first aircraft were being delivered and modified in exactly this period. A two-year-old Global 6500 with no listed operator is the correct age and type for a HADES or HADES-adjacent airframe. It is also the correct age and type for an ordinary new corporate jet, of which several hundred exist. **The type and vintage are consistent with the claim; they do not establish it.**

**The Mode S hex A0299E is the identifier to audit on.** Any check of this aircraft's September 10 track against the raw ADS-B Exchange archive should match on A0299E, not on the registration.

## John Cullen: "the HADES spy plane… is for sale"

On **September 20, 2025 at 7:58 AM**, **[John Cullen](/Influencers/john-cullen)** (@I_Am_JohnCullen) posted a listing screenshot under the text:

> Did you know the HADES spy plane we flying around the scene of Charlie Kirk's murder is for sale?
>
> When the CIA can't fit all their crap in a plane, they sell it, and get a bigger plane..
>
> $50 million..
>
> Should we set up a GoFundMe?
>
> Or, should @elonmusk buy it, and we can find out who killed Charlie Kirk tomorrow?

The post reported **13.4K views, 39 replies, 109 reposts, 363 likes and 48 bookmarks**.

The attached images are a four-panel composite, watermarked `@I_Am_JohnCullen`, showing a genuine aircraft-marketplace listing:

* **The listing header:** `AIRCRAFT FOR SALE > BOMBARDIER > GLOBAL 6500 > 2023`, titled **"2023 BOMBARDIER GLOBAL 6500"**, priced **"Call for price"** — the $50 million figure is Cullen's own, not the listing's.
* **Aircraft location: Wilmington, Delaware.**
* **Seller: AeroAmerica Group, Contact: Sales Team, Manassas, Virginia.**
* A **grey CAD rendering of a cabin** fitted out with a row of three identical operator stations along one wall — flat-panel displays in bulky angular housings with control trays beneath, high-backed operator seats, cockpit door visible down the aisle. One of the display screens shows a circular green-and-yellow radar sweep. The panel carries a white banner reading **"FILE PHOTO / SAMPLE OF SPECIAL MISSION INTERIOR."**
* A cockpit photograph and a green head-up-display graphic labelled **CVS** (Combined Vision System), both stock imagery.

### What this actually shows, and what it does not

The listing is real and its details are checkable. Its evidentiary value, though, is narrower than the post implies, and the reasons matter:

* **The interior render is explicitly a file photo.** The banner across it says so in the seller's own words: *"FILE PHOTO / SAMPLE OF SPECIAL MISSION INTERIOR."* It is a stock illustration of what a special-mission conversion *can* look like, placed in the listing by a broker who converts and sells such aircraft. **It is not a photograph of the aircraft for sale, and it is not a photograph of N1098L.**
* **AeroAmerica Group is a broker, not an operator.** A Manassas, Virginia broker advertising a Delaware-parked Global 6500 with special-mission fitting illustrations is a company in the business of trading green and converted airframes. That business existing does not connect any particular airframe to any particular flight.
* **Nothing in the listing names N1098L.** The post asserts the identity between "the HADES spy plane… flying around the scene" and the aircraft on sale. The listing does not carry a tail number, and none of the four panels contains one.
* **The Wilmington, Delaware location is a coincidence worth flagging as a coincidence.** Wilmington also appears in this investigation as the destination of [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview)'s September 10 departure and as the recurring outbound customs stop for the entire Egyptian fleet. Wilmington is a major business-aviation and aircraft-trading hub; a jet for sale being parked there is what one would expect. Two unrelated threads of this investigation touching Wilmington is not a link between them.

The claim that this specific airframe was sold because "the CIA can't fit all their crap in a plane" is rhetorical framing with nothing behind it, and Cullen offers it as such.

### The reply worth following up

A reply on the thread from **@F1sT ("ai swarm")** raised a genuinely testable point:

> why doesn't anybody get the FAA logs for Butler Pennsylvania I bet you it's the exact same plane. That's even…

The suggestion is to pull FAA records for **Butler, Pennsylvania** — the site of the July 13, 2024 attempt on Donald Trump — and check whether the same airframe or the same class of ISR aircraft was operating there. Whatever one makes of the premise, this is the right *kind* of request: a specific records ask about a specific place and date, capable of returning a yes or a no. It appears not to have been pursued publicly by anyone.

* **Still built today?** Yes — still in production

Global 6500 remains in production. Manufacturer list ~$56-58M for a green airframe; the HADES ISR mission fit-out is a separate government cost not in that figure.

Three of the top four are **US Army airfields**, and the aircraft is flying almost nothing else.
[N2100L](/Planes/N2100L/overview) — same registered owner, consecutive serial — returned the same
shape: Robert Gray AAF 27, Biggs AAF 17.

**Sierra Vista Municipal is the civil half of Libby Army Airfield, on Fort Huachuca.**

This page has carried the Fort Huachuca connection as *"an unverified OSINT claim, not a documented
tasking record"*, sourced to posts on X. That characterisation was accurate when it was written.
**It is now partly sourced:** N1098L flew into Fort Huachuca's field three times in one week, and
the record is free, third-party and timestamped.

**Read the limits, because they are real ones.**

* This is **August 2026**, not 10 September 2025. It says where the aircraft operates now. It says
  nothing about where it operated, or why, on the day of the assassination.
* An Army-contracted surveillance aircraft basing at Army airfields is **exactly what that aircraft
  would do**. It is not remarkable in itself — what is worth having is confirmation of *which*
  fields, from a record rather than from a screenshot.
* An activity log is a tracking site's summary of flights. **It is not a tasking record.** Who was
  aboard, what sensors were fitted, and what the aircraft was ordered to do are outside every source
  available here — and are exactly the records the
  [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview) are written to force open.

## Flight data recovery

This is the tail most often described online as a hidden, blacked-out or blocked military asset, so
the result of the archive sweep deserves stating plainly: **nothing about N1098L is missing from any
archive we checked.** It is one of the best-covered aircraft in the fleet — **46 days** of raw track
on airplanes.live running to **3 June 2026**, and monthly samples reaching **1 August 2026**. Its
FlightRadar24 page loads in an ordinary browser, listed openly as a **Bombardier Global 6500 (GLEX),
"Private owner", Mode S A0299E**, and served **28 history rows** on 24 August 2026.

| Source | What it holds for N1098L |
|---|---|
| adsb.lol `globe_history` | 17 days, 2 Sep 2025 – 11 Oct 2025 |
| globe.airplanes.live | 46 days, 2 Sep 2025 – 3 Jun 2026 |
| ADS-B Exchange monthly sample | 19 days, 1 Nov 2023 – 1 Aug 2026 |
| FlightRadar24 page | HTTP 200 in a browser on 24 Aug 2026; 28 history rows on the free seven-day tier; older history paywalled |
| Internet Archive | 3 page snapshots held (FlightAware, May 2023 – Sep 2025); 0 flight rows recovered off them — those captures are JavaScript shells with no server-rendered rows |

**Nothing here was found to have been removed.** No tracking site was found to have taken this
aircraft's page down. Our scripted probe recorded HTTP 403 from FlightRadar24, RadarBox and
Planespotters — but the identical script got the identical 403 for five unrelated control aircraft
chosen for having nothing to do with this case, so a 403 is a robot block and says nothing about
this tail. See [What A 403 Actually Means](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/What-A-403-Means).

Where a day is absent from an archive, that means a volunteer receiver network heard nothing on that
day. It does not establish that the aircraft did not fly, and it does not establish that a
transponder was switched off. Both free daily archives begin in 2023, so every 2022 date is
untestable by construction rather than missing.

The raw files are on disk at `site/docs/Planes/N1098L/data/recovered/` — the source is in every
filename, and each file carries a `.meta.json` sidecar recording the source, the URL, the HTTP
status, the byte count and the date, so any line above can be checked without trusting this page.

## Track This Aircraft

Research this aircraft's flight history yourself on both major flight-tracking sites. They differ in one important way:

* [FlightRadar24 — N1098L](https://www.flightradar24.com/data/aircraft/n1098l) — large commercial tracker that honors the FAA's [LADD](https://www.faa.gov/pilots/ladd) block list, so flights an owner or the government has asked to hide can be missing.
* [ADS-B Exchange — N1098L](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/?reg=N1098L) — volunteer-fed, unfiltered tracker that does **not** honor LADD, so it often shows blocked aircraft and tracks the filtered sites leave out.
* [ADS-B Exchange — by ICAO hex A0299E](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/?icao=a0299e) — the Mode-S hex is this jet's permanent transponder ID; use it when the registration lookup comes up empty.

**Aircraft ID:** Bombardier Global 6500 (US Army ISR, HADES/ATHENA program) · registration **N1098L** · callsign AXLE10 · ICAO hex **A0299E**. Investigators note some of this aircraft's flights are missing from public tracking, so the unfiltered ADS-B Exchange links above are usually the most complete.
