# TPUSA Aircraft — What Turning Point Actually Owned

**Turning Point USA owns no aircraft. Not one.** The complete FAA registration database — every civil aircraft registered in the United States — returns **zero** aircraft held by Turning Point USA, Turning Point Action, Turning Point Faith, or any entity using the Turning Point name in that sense.

**Every tail this investigation has ever called "the TPUSA plane" is registered to a private LLC**, and the four of them sit in [the table below](#the-tails-commentary-attaches-to-tpusa-and-who-they-belong-to). There is no TPUSA fleet to build a page-per-plane catalogue from, because in the FAA's records the fleet does not exist.

**That is a finding about registered ownership, and only that.** Organisations charter, lease, borrow, and fly on donor-owned aircraft constantly, and none of that appears in an ownership registry. What it does mean is that **an organisation owning no aircraft leaves no aircraft paper trail of its own** — no depreciation schedule, no Form 990 aircraft entry, no maintenance records under its name. Every flight it took sits in somebody else's records, and none of those are public. That gap is exactly what the [proposed disclosure laws](/Fix/overview) are aimed at.

---

Commentary across this investigation refers repeatedly to "the TPUSA plane," "Turning Point's jet," and aircraft "TPUSA insiders" used. This page checks that language against the one record that settles aircraft ownership in the United States: the **FAA Civil Aviation Registry**.

The answer is short, and it is worth stating before anything else.

:::info The registry finding
A search of the **complete FAA aircraft registration database** — the full `MASTER.txt` release covering every civil aircraft registered in the United States — returns **no aircraft registered to Turning Point USA, Turning Point Action, Turning Point Faith, or any entity using the Turning Point name in that sense**.

The only registrants whose names contain "Turning Point" are three unrelated organisations: a Tennessee ministry flying two 1964 Cessna 172s (N370LA, N718DF) and a Washington state LLC flying a 1966 Cessna 150 (N818ML). None has any connection to Charlie Kirk or to Turning Point USA.
:::

That is a finding about **registered ownership**, and only that. It does not mean Turning Point never flew private aircraft — organisations charter, lease, borrow, and fly on donor-owned aircraft constantly, and none of that appears in the ownership registry. It means that **there is no TPUSA-owned fleet to build a page-per-plane catalogue from**, because in the FAA's records the fleet does not exist.

## Why this matters to the investigation

Several claims on this site and across X depend, quietly, on the idea that a particular aircraft belonged to Turning Point. Once ownership is checked, those claims have to be restated more carefully:

* A tail described as **"the TPUSA aircraft"** is, in every case checked here, registered to a **private LLC** — not to the organisation.
* "TPUSA insiders used this plane" is a claim about **who was aboard**, which the registry cannot confirm or refute. Passenger manifests are not public. That is exactly the gap the [proposed disclosure laws](/Fix/overview) are aimed at.
* An organisation that owns no aircraft leaves **no aircraft paper trail of its own** — no depreciation schedule, no Form 990 aircraft entry, no maintenance records under its name. Every flight it took sits in somebody else's records.

None of these four is registered to Turning Point USA. Two of them — N582MM and N102DZ — are registered to Wyoming and Arizona LLCs whose names appear nowhere else in this investigation. **Naming an LLC is not naming a wrongdoer**, and nothing here asserts that any of these owners did anything improper.

## The one thing the registry does show about N102DZ

The Gulfstream V most closely associated with Charlie Kirk's travel, **N102DZ**, has a registration record with a date worth noting plainly: its **current certificate was issued on 16 October 2025** — about five weeks after the assassination — with a last recorded registry action on **23 December 2025**.

A registration certificate reissue is an ordinary event. It happens on sale, on transfer between owner LLCs, on renewal, and on a change of registered address. It is **not evidence of anything by itself**. It is recorded here because the timing sits inside the window in which researchers also allege the aircraft's public flight history was removed from FlightRadar24 — a claim documented on [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased). Two ordinary-looking administrative events in the same window are worth writing down; they are not proof that either was arranged.

## What would settle the ownership and usage questions

1. **TPUSA Form 990 travel and transportation lines** for fiscal years 2022 through 2025 — what the organisation reported spending on air travel, and to whom.
2. **Charter and lease agreements** between Turning Point entities and the LLCs above. These are private documents; only a subpoena or a voluntary release produces them.
3. **Passenger manifests** for the flights already documented on this site. Not public for private aircraft. This is [Law 1 and Law 3](/Fix/overview) territory.
4. **FAA registration history** for N102DZ showing the chain of registered owners before Elderberry Chestnut LLC, which would show whether the October 2025 certificate reflects a genuine change of hands.

## Where this page fits

The finding here is an absence: a direct query of the FAA registry returns no aircraft owned by [Turning Point USA](/TPUSA/overview) or any Turning Point entity, which forces every "the TPUSA plane" line elsewhere on this site to be restated as a claim about [who was aboard somebody else's LLC-registered jet](/Planes/Charlie-Erika-Aircraft/overview), from [N102DZ](/Planes/N102DZ/overview) to [N582MM](/Planes/N582MM/overview). That is the same gap the wider investigation keeps hitting — [Kirk reportedly ordered a DOGE-style audit of his own organisation](/TPUSA/doge-audit) days before he died, [TPUSA's counsel later conceded a media drone flew before the event](/Drones/TPUSA_Production_Drone), and [the donor and pressure field around him](/political_context/overview) is documented far better than the money moving through it. The detail to carry forward is a date: the Gulfstream V's current registration certificate issued 16 October 2025, five weeks after the assassination, inside the same window researchers say its public flight history vanished — a claim set out at [Erika Flight Logs Erased](/Planes/Erika-Flight-Logs-Erased) and tested by [the recovery sweep](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview).
