# Utah Cox Foreign Meetings (Claims)

**Nineteen days before the Utah Capitol meetings alleged on this page, primary ADS-B position data puts an Egyptian-registered Falcon 7X, [SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), on the ramp at [Provo Municipal Airport](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) — 1.04 km from the field, 141 position fixes, 4 September 2025. An Egyptian Gulfstream, [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview), is recorded on that same ramp on seven separate days between 5 and 12 September, including [the day Charlie Kirk was killed](/Planes/following/speaking/20250910_orem) five miles away.**

**That is the aircraft half of this page, and it is documented. The meetings half is not.** The 23 September Gold Room sessions with Egyptian and French figures are **claims** circulating from calendar screenshots and social posts. No calendar entry, readout, photograph, or official statement has been produced. Governor [**Spencer Cox**](/People/spencer-cox) is a living public official, and **nothing on this page asserts that he, or any diplomat, committed any wrongdoing or knew anything about the killing in advance.**

Thirteen days after [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s death, commentary alleges Utah Governor [**Spencer Cox**](/People/spencer-cox)
hosted **back-to-back closed-door meetings** in the **Utah Capitol Gold Room** with
**Egyptian** and **French** representatives — the same foreign threads tied to
Egyptian jets in Provo. This page documents those **claims** from
CK_FILE and social posts. We do **not** assert Governor Cox or any diplomat committed
wrongdoing or knew of the killing in advance.

| **2025-09-23** | Salt Lake City | UT | — | — | **Closed-door Gold Room meetings with Egyptian and French figures** | **No primary record. Claim only — calendar screenshots and social posts, no readout, no photograph, no official statement** |

**The last row is the point of this page, and it is the one row with nothing behind it.** The aircraft record is real and checkable. The meeting record is not, and setting them side by side is the honest way to show the gap rather than letting the first row lend credibility to the last.

**A trace proves presence, never purpose, and never occupancy.** Nothing in the ADS-B record names a passenger on any of these aircraft, and no manifest for any of them has been published.

- **[Egyptian planes](./SU-BTT/overview)** were on the ground in Provo Sept 4–13 per ADS-B research.

  an allegation, not a finding.

French angle: separate research threads allege French intel interest in
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) coverage; those belong primarily to
[Israel/foreign leads](/Israel/foreign-leads), not this page.

## Sourcing caution

- Calendar screenshots and ambassador identities need **primary verification**.
- Governors routinely hold **undisclosed diplomatic meetings**; secrecy alone is not evidence of a cover-up.
- Spencer Cox is a **living person**; attribute all claims to cited posts, not as established fact.

## What corroboration would look like

- Governor's office readouts or FOIA for Sept 23 Gold Room bookings
- Ambassador travel records into Salt Lake City
- Overlap with Egyptian armada passenger names from manifests
