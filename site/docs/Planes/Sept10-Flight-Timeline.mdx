# September 10 Flight Timeline

**Charlie Kirk was shot at approximately 12:23 p.m. MDT on 10 September 2025. By then one of the two
Egyptian government jets that had been sitting at [Provo](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) was already on the
ground in Delaware, and the other had not moved all day.**

Both statements are now settled by **primary ADS-B position data held by two independent volunteer
archives that agree to within 10 milliseconds** — not by a screenshot of a tracking website.
[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) was on the Provo ramp at **07:07:53 MDT**, wheels up at
**07:14:03**, and on approach to [Wilmington, Delaware](/Planes/Airports/KILG) at **12:50:37 EDT —
about ninety minutes before the shooting**. [SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview)'s first and last fixes
of the day are the same Provo parking spot, 1.29 km from the field.

**Three of the five hex codes that would settle the rest of this page have still not been pulled** —
`A0299E` (the AXL10 low-pass claim), `A7C14D` ([N59906](/Planes/N59906/overview)'s survey grid) and
`AC3C75` ([N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview)'s departure and coverage gap). The two that were pulled
are the two the following-planes claim turns on.

This page correlates public flight-tracking claims with witness timing statements. **It does not
assert that any aircraft participated in the killing or that any living person lied.** It documents
reported conflicts, and it names the source of every line.

**The UTC−6 point is not a footnote.** More bad claims in this investigation trace to assuming UTC−7 for Utah on this date than to any other single error. It is the mistake conceded and corrected by the analyst quoted on the [SU-BTT page](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview), and it is the mistake that generated the "time zone" attack on the Owens presentation. Anyone re-deriving these figures should convert at **UTC−6**, and note separately that **Page, Arizona is UTC−7 year-round** because Arizona does not observe daylight saving — an offset that matters for the [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) legs.

### The three collisions in that table

**1. The shooting time itself is annotated two different ways.** The @stellarman22 graphic marks "Assassination: 18:10" UTC — **12:10 p.m.** local. The Rosenwald graphic prints "Charlie Kirk shot at 12:23pm." A thirteen-minute spread on the anchor event propagates into every "X minutes after" claim on this site. The 12:23 figure is the one used elsewhere on these pages; the 12:10 figure is recorded here because it is what a widely-shared graphic actually says, and because anyone auditing @stellarman22's arithmetic needs to know which anchor he used.

**2. Owner and tracker disagree on N888KG at both ends.** The owner has the aircraft departing nine minutes later and landing nine minutes later than the tracker does. Neither account has ever been reconciled against FAA data. See [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview) for the full comparison.

**3. SU-BTT's departure is logged at 07:14 with a scheduled time of 10:51 — and the departure half is now settled.** The Flightradar24 export gives **STD 10:51 AM, ATD 7:14 AM, STA 11:51 AM** for the Provo→Wilmington leg. The 07:14 actual is now confirmed to the second from the raw track (see the section below); the 10:51 scheduled figure remains an export column of unknown time reference and should not be treated as a second departure narrative. The reading hazard was in the *scheduled* column, not the *actual* one.

### The SU-BTT departure is settled from primary data

This page carried three competing departure accounts and said flatly that they could not all be true. **They no longer have to be argued about.** Both free ADS-B archives now hold the complete 10 September 2025 trace for SU-BTT, and **they agree to within 10 milliseconds** on every fix:

| | UTC | Local | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First position fix of the day** | **13:07:53.66** | **07:07:53 MDT** | On the ground at **[Provo (KPVU)](/Planes/Airports/KPVU)**, 1.37 km from the field reference point |
| **Wheels up** | **13:14:03.82** | **07:14:03 MDT** | Departing Provo |
| **Last tracked position** | **16:50:37.53** | **12:50:37 EDT** | On approach to **[Wilmington, Delaware (KILG)](/Planes/Airports/KILG)**, 1.75 km |

Files: `site/docs/Planes/SU-BTT/data/recovered/SU-BTT_2025-09-10_adsb-lol_trace_full.json` and `site/docs/Planes/SU-BTT/data/recovered/SU-BTT_2025-09-10_airplanes-live_trace_full.json`.

**Reconciling this with what this page already said, plainly:**

* **The 07:14 figure is right.** It came from a Flightradar24 export column and it matches the raw track to the second — **07:14:03 MDT**. It is no longer a tracker claim; it is a recorded position.
* **The "~7:08 AM" line is not a competing departure.** 13:07:53 UTC is the **first fix of the day** — the aircraft's transponder appearing while it was still **on the ground**. Both figures come from the same aeroplane, six minutes apart, and this page previously presented them as a conflict. **They are not a conflict, and that framing was wrong.**
* **The Dugway-origin claim does not survive.** The trace's first fix of the day puts SU-BTT at **Provo**, not at Dugway, and the aircraft is on the ground there when it appears. The reading that produced the Dugway claim is the nearest-public-airport label problem this section has warned about elsewhere.
* **Nearest-airport resolution is geometry, not a landing record.** The distances are published above for exactly that reason: 1.37 km and 1.75 km are how far the aircraft was from the field reference point, not a statement that a tower logged it.

**The consequence, stated flatly.** SU-BTT was on approach to Wilmington, Delaware at **12:50:37 EDT — 10:50:37 MDT**. Charlie Kirk was shot at approximately **12:23 p.m. MDT**. **The aircraft was on the ground in Delaware roughly ninety minutes before the shooting**, having left Utah about five hours and nine minutes before it. That is what the position data shows and this page states it without adding a reading to it.

**Counterargument, and it is the obvious one.** An aircraft leaving hours before an event is at least as consistent with a scheduled departure as with anything else, and the return leg — Wilmington to **Almaza Air Force Base, Cairo** on 11 September — is the same Wilmington-to-Cairo routing this fleet flew home on every previous rotation. **A confirmed departure time is a confirmed departure time. It establishes where the aircraft was; it establishes nothing about why.**

### The two aircraft that bracket the shooting

Stripped to the aircraft whose positions are best documented, September 10 looks like this:

* **N59906** — a fifty-one-year-old Piper piston twin under a discrete ATC squawk (4323), flying a **2h39m closed-loop survey grid out of and back into Provo** at **19,000 ft** and **212 kt**, with five metronomic passes 8–9 minutes apart, ending **48 minutes before** the shooting. It was **taxiing on the Provo ramp** at the moment the screenshot documenting it was taken, roughly at the time of the shooting.
* **AXL10** — a Bombardier Global 6500 tied by an independent analyst to a US Army deep-sensing ISR program, alleged to have made **two very low passes within ~3 miles of UVU**, one about three hours before and one about twenty-five minutes after.

The first is well documented and, on the evidence, ordinary. The second, if the altitude derivation holds, is genuinely extraordinary — and it is the one nobody has produced a raw track for. That asymmetry is the single most useful thing on this page: **the aircraft everyone can verify is the boring one, and the aircraft nobody has verified is the interesting one.**

**And two foreign-registered aircraft bracket the day in a different sense — one left, one stayed.** Both are now documented from raw traces held by two independent archives:

* **[SU-BTT](/Planes/SU-BTT/overview) — the one that left.** On the ground at Provo at **07:07:53 MDT**, wheels up at **07:14:03**, on approach to Wilmington, Delaware at **12:50:37 EDT**. Gone from Utah more than five hours before the shot, and on the ground on the east coast about ninety minutes before it.
* **[SU-BND](/Planes/SU-BND/overview) — the one that stayed.** On the ground at Provo, **1.29 km**, at **20:03:33 UTC on 9 September**; on the ground at Provo, **1.29 km**, at **16:05:35 UTC on 10 September**; and still on the ground at Provo, **1.29 km**, at **16:48:52 UTC on 12 September**. Its first and last fixes on 10 September are the same position. **The aircraft did not take off that day, and the primary data now corroborates that claim, which previously rested on a broadcast reading of a tracker.** What the traces do **not** settle is the transponder-cycling allegation — power on at 09:05:36, dark to 10:34:05, on again at 12:40:53, off at 13:29:40 — because those claimed timings still carry [no stated time base](/Planes/following/Candace_Owens_Broadcasts), and a stationary aircraft producing intermittent ground fixes is what an ordinary parked transponder looks like.

**Do not merge this with the other threads.** SU-BTT and SU-BND belong to the [18-month following-pattern claim](/Planes/following/overview); [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) and AXL10 belong to the drone/ISR thread; [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview)'s departure and coverage gap is a third and separate claim. They share a date and nothing else that anyone has established.

### The one query that would resolve most of this

Nearly every disputed line above is a **raw ADS-B Exchange archive query away** from being settled. For September 10, 2025, over the Utah Valley:

* **A0299E** — the Global 6500 hex, for the AXL10 altitude and pass claims.
* **A7C14D** — N59906, to confirm the five passes and the altitude band.
* **AC3C75** — N888KG, to settle the departure time and the coverage gap.
* **0101D3** — SU-BTT, to fix the Provo departure minute. ✅ **Done.** Full trace pulled from two independent archives; departure settled to the second.
* **01003E** — SU-BND, to confirm the ground pings and the absence of a takeoff. ✅ **Done.** First and last fixes of 10 September are the same Provo position; no takeoff in the trace.

Five hex codes, one date, one archive. **Two of the five are now done** — the two Egyptian tails, pulled on 24 August 2026 from `adsb.lol` and `airplanes.live`, with the raw JSON committed to this repo under `site/docs/Planes/SU-BTT/data/recovered/` and `site/docs/Planes/SU-BND/data/recovered/`. **Three remain open: A0299E, A7C14D and AC3C75** — the AXL10 altitude claim, the N59906 survey grid, and the N888KG departure and coverage gap. The two that were pulled are the two the following-planes claim turns on; the three still outstanding are the ones the day-of timeline turns on, and they are separate claims. Method and archive map are on [Flight Data Recovery](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview).

## Hospital timing conflict (claims)

**[Frank Turek](/People/frank-turek)** (former Navy officer) reportedly stated on the record that **Erika Kirk
arrived at Timpanogos Regional Hospital around 4:00 PM**. Commentary from
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) highlights a **logistical tension**:

- If Erika met **[Andrew Kolvet](/People/andrew-kolvet)** on the runway when **[N40JD](/Planes/N40JD/overview) landed at ~4:40 PM**, then
  deplaning, an emotional tarmac meeting, and a ~20-minute drive (with manhunt traffic)
  would place hospital arrival **no earlier than ~5:15 PM** — not 4:00 PM.
- Owens argues both Turek and Kolvet may be telling the truth only if Erika arrived on an
  **earlier flight** — and **[N102DZ at 3:31 PM](./N102DZ/overview)** is the tail that fits that math.

This is **citizen-investigator reasoning**, not a court finding. Erika Kirk is a living
person; we document the **timeline question** attributed to Owens and Turek, not a conclusion
of dishonesty.
