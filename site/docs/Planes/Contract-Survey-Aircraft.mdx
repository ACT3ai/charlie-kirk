# Contract Survey Aircraft

Most of the aircraft in this section fall into one of two buckets: **private jets carrying people**, or **military and government aircraft**. [N59906](/Planes/N59906/overview) is neither. It belongs to a third category that is unfamiliar to most readers and is repeatedly described wrongly in posts about this case — the **contract airborne-survey aircraft**.

This page is about **that category of aircraft**: what it is, what it carries, who hires one, and why an aircraft of this kind flying a grid over a city on a given morning is simultaneously (a) completely ordinary and (b) the reason the imagery question in this case will not go away. It also documents the **April 2026 re-registration** of N59906 to **Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC**, reported by [@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution) on 31 August 2026 — and checks that report against the FAA registry data this investigation already holds.

:::info The paperwork claim checks out. The framing around it does not.
**The FAA record confirms the specific, checkable part of the report.** N59906's airworthiness certificate was reissued on **4 April 2026** to **ALPHA WHISKEY 2 LLC** at **101 Highpointe Ct Ste C, Brandon, Mississippi** — the exact date and the exact street the post names. That is not disputed here; it is corroborated from the FAA master registry file this investigation downloaded independently.

**Four things around that fact do not survive checking**, and they are set out in full below. The post says **four** survey passes; the record shows **five**. It calls them **low-altitude**; they were flown at **19,000 ft MSL**, a standard high-altitude mapping band. It says the paperwork moved **"after people started asking"**; the post's own timeline puts the registration **pending in September 2025**, before the October questions it says caused it. And the framing that the aircraft **"did not disappear"** answers a claim the data never supported — N59906 was never missing from the archives, and this site's own suppression test scored it at **0.0%**.

**A registration change is not evidence of concealment.** Single-purpose LLCs are the ordinary way US aircraft are held, and an entry in a public, searchable federal registry — which is exactly how this was found — is a poor way to hide anything.
:::

## What a contract survey aircraft actually is

A contract survey aircraft is a **sensor platform flown for hire**. The airframe is usually old, cheap and unremarkable — a piston twin from the 1970s is typical, because what matters is endurance, a stable platform and a hole in the floor, not speed or luxury. The value is entirely in what is bolted into the cabin.

**N59906 fits that description exactly:** a **1974 Piper PA-31-350 Navajo Chieftain**, serial **31-7552027**, eight seats, ICAO hex **A7C14D**. A fifty-two-year-old piston twin. Any account describing it as a modern surveillance platform is describing a different aircraft.

| What defines the category | How it shows up in the data |
|---|---|
| **Flown for a client, not for its owner** | The operator sells flight hours; the person who tasked it is a customer whose identity is not in any public record |
| **Sensor payload, not passengers** | Large-format digital cameras, infrared and hyperspectral scanners, LiDAR |
| **Grid flight profile** | Long parallel legs at a constant altitude, evenly spaced, out of and back into one field |
| **High altitude, not low** | Mapping bands are typically 15,000–20,000 ft AGL — the higher the aircraft, the wider each strip and the fewer passes needed |
| **Old, cheap airframe** | 1974 Navajo; [~\$1.5M](/Planes/Aircraft-Costs/overview) to buy the closest new piston equivalent today |
| **Ordinary customers** | Federal and state agencies, utilities, oil and gas, civil engineering, universities, private mapping firms |

**The grid is the tell, and it cuts both ways.** Five passes eight to nine minutes apart at a constant 19,000 ft is what a programmed mapping run looks like — parallel lines flown at a fixed interval so the imagery overlaps. That regularity is the strongest single argument that the flight was routine survey work. It is also *not* what an aircraft loitering to watch one specific thing would fly. **But the same grid still produces high-resolution imagery of everything underneath it**, including a campus, a quad and a stage, and that is why the flight matters regardless of why it was flown.

## The re-registration, as reported

The following reproduces the substance of the 31 August 2026 post by [@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/podcasts-project-constitution). It is presented as the claim made, before checking.

> **"UPDATE: The SECOND UVU Spy Plane Did NOT Disappear — Marc Inc. Papered It To A New LLC"**
>
> On the morning of September 10, while Charlie Kirk was still very much alive, a twin-engine Piper Navajo Chieftain, tail number N59906, left Provo Airport and flew four passes over Utah Valley University. The plane was not a toy. It was a survey ship operated by MARC Incorporated, a Mississippi contractor that sells specialized aircraft and crews for mapping, LiDAR, and surveillance. It carries large-format cameras, infrared scanners, and LiDAR. Federal agencies hire this kind of work. So do states and private firms.
>
> The first pass started at 11:01 a.m. The second at 11:09. The third at 11:18, over the event area. The last at 11:35. At 11:50, campus cameras picked up the man they later called the shooter. Nobody from the company or The FBI has said who paid for those four runs, or why the specific timing.
>
> On October 13, photographs from that last pass were published on Tiktok by a whistleblower who goes by @universehop00. That account had no other posts and hasn't posted since. The pictures are high-resolution views of the quad and the stage during the event. The timestamps match the 11:35 flight. Those images exist. They are part of this case.
>
> Then the paperwork moved. For years the plane sat on the books of MARC Incorporated at John Bell Williams Airport in Bolton, Mississippi. [An individual] of Brandon was the name attached to the LLC. In September 2025 the Federal Aviation Administration showed the registration pending at an address on Highpointe Court in Brandon. On April 4, 2026, the certificate issued to Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC, also in Brandon. Same airplane. Same town. Same kind of flying. A new limited liability company is not a new operation.
>
> So you have a contractor plane photographing the campus before the killing, photographs from that flight, and a name change after people started asking who hired it. The Bureau can end the mystery. Name the client. Release the full image set. Until they do, the sequence stands.

**One redaction.** The post names a private individual in Brandon, Mississippi as "the name attached to the LLC." **This site does not repeat that name.** The FAA master registry record this investigation holds names only the limited liability company — no individual appears in it. Naming a private person as the human face of an alleged concealment, on the strength of one social-media post and against a federal record that does not name him, is not a standard this site is willing to meet. The corporate entities are named throughout, because they are what the record actually contains.

## Checked against the FAA registry

This investigation downloaded the FAA registry master data for every tracked tail independently of any post. The N59906 record reads:

| FAA field | Value on record |
|---|---|
| Registration | **N59906** |
| Mode S hex | **A7C14D** |
| Serial / year | **31-7552027** / **1974** |
| Registrant name | **ALPHA WHISKEY 2 LLC** |
| Street | **101 HIGHPOINTE CT STE C** |
| City / State / ZIP | **BRANDON, MS 39042-7041** |
| Certificate issue date | **2026-04-04** |
| Last action date | **2026-04-04** |
| Expiration date | **2033-04-30** |
| Status | **V — Valid** |
| Airworthiness date | **1974-12-24** |

**Three of the post's specifics land exactly:** the **4 April 2026** certificate date, the **Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC** registrant, and the **Highpointe Court, Brandon** address. The post describes the September 2025 *pending* registration as being at "an address on Highpointe Court in Brandon" — the same street the *issued* registration now carries, which is consistent with one continuous filing rather than a move between two unrelated parties.

**What the FAA record does not contain:** any individual's name, any reference to MARC Inc. as the prior registrant, any client, any tasking, and anything at all about 10 September 2025. A registry entry establishes who holds title. It establishes nothing about who hired the aircraft or what it photographed.

**The operator's own fleet is the strongest argument against the concealment reading.** The FAA registry shows MARC Inc. holding **32 aircraft** at Bolton, Mississippi, the majority of them PA-31-350 Navajo Chieftains — the exact fleet profile of a working aerial-survey contractor, which supports the survey explanation for the UVU passes rather than undercutting it. MARC also holds an aircraft registered at **Brandon, Mississippi**, the same town as the new registrant, so the transfer may be an internal or local one rather than an arm's-length sale to a stranger.

**Note also that this is not new to this site.** The [N59906 page](/Planes/N59906/overview) and the [Aircraft Costs table](/Planes/Aircraft-Costs/overview) have both carried "Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC, Brandon MS" as the registered owner. The post's contribution is the **date and the sequence**, not the discovery of the entity.

## Four corrections

Publishing the result that weakens a claim as prominently as the result that supports it is a standing rule in this section. These four all weaken it.

### 1. Five passes, not four

The post lists **four** — 11:01, 11:09, 11:18 and 11:35 — and drops **11:26**. The annotated ADS-B Exchange graphic compiled by Eric R Rosenwald, captured at `2025-09-10 18:06:10 Z`, lists the passes explicitly and there are **five**: 11:01, 11:09, 11:18, **11:26**, 11:35. Eight to nine minutes apart across a 34-minute block. The full pass-by-pass record is on the [N59906 page](/Planes/N59906/overview) and in the [Sept 10 Flight Timeline](/Planes/Sept10-Flight-Timeline).

The correction matters because the missing pass is the one that makes the interval regular. Four passes at 11:01, 11:09, 11:18 and 11:35 look like three quick runs and a straggler. Five at an even 8–9 minute cadence look like exactly what they are: a programmed grid.

### 2. Not "low-altitude"

An earlier post in the same thread called these "low-alt passes (15k ft AGL)." **Fifteen thousand feet above ground is not low.** The passes were flown at roughly **19,000 ft MSL**, which over Provo's 4,497 ft field elevation is about 15,000 ft AGL — a standard high-altitude aerial-mapping band. The aircraft was three miles above the campus. It came near UVU because **UVU sits inside the survey block**, not because it descended to look at it.

### 3. The paperwork moved *before* the questions started, not after

The post's closing argument is "a name change after people started asking who hired it." **Its own timeline contradicts this.** By the post's account the FAA showed the registration **pending in September 2025** — before the 13 October 2025 TikTok images, before the November 2025 thread that first raised the aircraft publicly, and therefore before the asking began. What issued on 4 April 2026 was the completion of a filing already in motion. FAA registrations also run on a fixed renewal cycle and routinely sit "pending" for months; the new certificate expires **30 April 2033**, a standard seven-year term.

### 4. It never disappeared

The headline answers a disappearance claim the data never supported. N59906 was not missing from the flight archives. This site's [deleted-flights investigation](/Planes/investigating_deleted_flights) ran the recovery against control aircraft and found **N59906's archive coverage rose from 3.31% to 6.89%** once the backup routes were added, with the suppression rate for N1098L, N2100L and N59906 together scoring **0.0%** across the days both archives genuinely covered. On FlightAware the aircraft returns **`blocked: false` with full activity logs** — unlike [N888KG](/Planes/N888KG/overview), which is the one genuinely withheld record in the whole effort.

An aircraft that was always in the archives and is listed under a valid, searchable registration has not been made to disappear.

## What survives, and it is the part worth pressing

Strip the four corrections out and something real is left, and it is the same thing the [N59906 page](/Planes/N59906/overview) has said all along:

**A contract survey aircraft flew a mapping grid over the Utah Valley on the morning of 10 September 2025 and landed at [Provo](/Planes/Airports/KPVU) at 12:08 p.m., roughly fifteen minutes before [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was shot.** Aircraft in this category carry large-format cameras and LiDAR. If that payload was fitted and running, high-resolution imagery of the campus that morning exists, and it is held by a private contractor and its client rather than by any public body.

**Nobody has said who the client was.** Neither the operator nor the [FBI](/FBI/overview) has publicly identified who commissioned the flight. That is the unanswered question, and it does not depend on the pass count, the altitude, the LLC or the registration date being right.

**A trace proves presence, never purpose and never occupancy.** Nothing on this page alleges wrongdoing by MARC Inc., by Alpha Whiskey 2 LLC, by any person associated with either, or by any crew member. No ADS-B record places any person aboard any aircraft. The [alleged leaked TikTok imagery](/Planes/N59906/overview) remains **unverified** — its authenticity, source and metadata have never been independently confirmed.

## Open questions

1. Who contracted and paid for the N59906 survey flight of 10 September 2025?
2. Which sensors were actually fitted that day, and was any imagery captured over the campus?
3. Where is that imagery now, and who has custody of it?
4. Was this a scheduled survey block — the Interstate-15 corridor, a utility or county contract — with a delivery date that would place it on the calendar long before September 2025?
5. Does the FAA registry hold the **prior** registrant record showing when MARC Incorporated's registration ended and the pending filing began?
6. Are the [alleged leaked images](/Planes/N59906/overview) authentic, and if so what is their provenance?
