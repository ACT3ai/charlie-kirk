# Paving Timeline — September 14

The UVU **tent courtyard** was reportedly **stripped of grass and paved within days** of
[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)'s death on **September 10, 2025**. This page assembles **date claims** from
public reporting and Merrell's on-camera account. Timeline disputes ("next day" vs "four days")
remain open pending release of work orders and security video.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime or knew of
the assassination in advance. Names appear only in the context of public discussion, and nothing
here is a finding by this website.

## Anchor dates

| Date | Event (reported) |
|------|------------------|
| **Sept 10, 2025** | Charlie Kirk shot at UVU courtyard tent/stage ([Timeline](/Timeline/overview)) |
| **Sept 11, 2025** | Emergency payment request, **$6,090.52**, "Clean up for shooting incident," vendor redacted (Judicial Watch GRAMA release) |
| **~Sept 12, 2025** | Work order issued **~two days** post-shooting ([Hardscape Specialties](./Hardscape_Specialties) notes); UVU texts plan to **wash** the area — *"We're not gonna need to cut out any sod"* |
| **Sept 13, 2025** | Scene reportedly **released** back to the university |
| **Sept 13–14 (window)** | An **unidentified state crew** reportedly removes **~10 inches of soil** ([dirt removal](./Dirt_Removed_Before_Pavers)) — no hour, no crew name, no disposal record in the public file |
| **Sept 14, 2025 (Sunday)** | **[Dan Merrell](/Tent/Daniel_Merrell_Hardscape_Utah)** arrives to an already-excavated courtyard and says he **installed pavers**; paving reportedly **started ~four days** after killing |
| **By Monday** | On-site coordinator told Merrell **governor and FBI** wanted completion **by Monday** (per Merrell) |

Sunday hardscape on a university courtyard is widely described in citizen research as **unusual**
for Utah County — most campus/state maintenance runs weekdays or Saturday at latest.

## Competing "speed" narratives

Two framings appear in public commentary and X threads:

1. **"Four days later"** — aligns with Merrell's **9/14/25** paver date and landscaping accounts.
2. **"The next day"** — a widely circulated claim states: *"They covered up the evidence THE NEXT DAY, demolishing and repaving the crime scene."*

These may reflect **multiple work phases** (early excavation vs Sunday pavers) rather than a single
error. [State Workers](./State_Workers_Groundskeepers) commentary describes a **first government
team** removing soil before Merrell arrived — see
[10 Inches of Dirt Removed Before the Pavers](./Dirt_Removed_Before_Pavers), which narrows that dig
to the **Sept 13–14** window. Disclosure of **dated photos, invoices, and delivery logs** should
reconcile the sequence.

## UVU "pre-scheduled" claim vs emergency framing

Public reporting indicates UVU characterized work as **pre-scheduled** in some postures, while citizen
compilations argue **evidence contradicts** routine scheduling given the homicide timing. UVU's
president reportedly cited **pending external review** when declining specifics (per AP reporting). KUTV/KATV noted courtyard changes without always naming contractors.

## Strange-events list

A widely asked question in citizen research is plainly: **"Why paved over Tent area?"** That question
assumes the pave-over is **anomalous** relative to normal post-event preservation — not that any
official admitted wrongdoing.

## Forensic window

Whatever the exact hour-by-hour log, the critical investigative issue is whether **ballistics,
blood-pattern, and explosive-residue collection** finished **before** topsoil removal and paver
base installation. [Site Alteration Evidence](./Site_Alteration_Evidence) lists trace categories
at risk. [FBI Crime Scene Paving](/FBI/Crime_Scene_Paving) frames federal authorization questions.

## Laws (Charlie Kirk)

Dated work orders, emergency authorizations, and pre-paving forensic clearance records are among
targets of the [Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview).


## Citizen Investigator Claims on X

The **September 14, 2025** paving window is the calendar anchor for alteration claims.

- Merrell-linked viral posts (May 2026) allege an **emergency Monday deadline** driven by Governor + FBI requests — clashes with “long-planned beautification” messaging.
- Timeline investigators ask: when was the last independent photo of the **original substrate**, and who controlled the courtyard after LE released the scene?
- Related context appears in the [Site Alteration Evidence](./Site_Alteration_Evidence) and [Cover-Up](/CoverUp/overview) sections — still **allegations about preservation**, not proven courtroom spoliation findings unless adjudicated.

## Timeline Claims (As Reported)

| Date | Claim |
|------|-------|
| Sept 10 | Assassination in courtyard / tent area |
| Scene release | LE control then return to university (details disputed) |
| ~Sept 14 | Paving / hardscape work window |
| 2026 | Merrell statement videos recirculate with “Governor + FBI” quote claims |

*Commentator coverage:* Citizen journalist [Ian Carroll](/People/ian-carroll) has questioned this paving sequence in appearances on the Jimmy Dore Show and in his own posts, describing the rapid courtyard work as what he calls a "scrubbing" of the scene and pressing for the work orders and forensic-clearance records to be released.
