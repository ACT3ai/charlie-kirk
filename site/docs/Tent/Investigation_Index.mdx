# Tent Investigation Index

The **tent and courtyard directly under [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** are where the killing occurred — and where
**grass was removed and pavers installed within days**. This memo routes the contractor names,
government-worker accounts, forensic-preservation questions, and proximity-shot theories without
treating any allegation as proven fact.

## Paving timeline at a glance

- **Sept 10** — Charlie Kirk shot at the UVU courtyard tent
- **~Sept 12** — work order issued (~two days post-event, per Hardscape Specialties notes); UVU texts still plan to **wash** the area, not cut sod
- **Sept 13** — scene reportedly released back to the university
- **Sept 13–14 (window)** — an **unidentified state crew** reportedly removes **~10 inches of soil** ([dirt removal](./Dirt_Removed_Before_Pavers))
- **Sept 14 (Sunday)** — **Dan Merrell** arrives to an already-excavated courtyard and installs pavers
- **By Monday** — coordinator tells Merrell **"the governor and the FBI"** want it done; UVU later calls the work **"pre-scheduled"** (CK: "evidence contradicts")
