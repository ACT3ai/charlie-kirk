# Switchblade & DARPA Drone Claims

Two **U.S. military UAS families** get named whenever this case turns to drones — the **Switchblade**
loitering munition and DARPA's **micro-airframe (X-65 class)** work. Both are real, publicly documented
programs. Neither has any record placing it at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025. This page sets
out what the hardware actually is, so that a reader meeting the names on X can tell the documented program
from the speculation attached to it.

## Switchblade (loitering munition)

The **Switchblade** family is an Army-fielded **kamikaze drone** — tube-launched, GPS-guided, designed for
precision strike, and small enough to be carried by one soldier. It is the platform people reach for when
they want a low-signature attack in a crowded environment, and it is fielded, not hypothetical.

The connection people draw to this case runs through the airframe, not through any sighting: defense-trade
reporting on **[air-launch trials from business-jet platforms](https://www.twz.com/air/army-wants-to-test-air-launched-drones-from-a-global-express-business-jet)**
describes Army interest in launching small UAS from the Global Express family — the same family as
**[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)**. That establishes doctrine for the airframe type. It establishes
nothing about this flight.

**Status:** No public record places a Switchblade at UVU that day, and nothing in the physical evidence
recovered from the scene points to one.

## DARPA micro-UAS (X-65 class)

**DARPA** has publicly run micro-air-vehicle work in the **2–5 kg** class — small, glide-capable airframes
that can carry roughly a kilogram of payload. Because that weight class is what a business-jet air-launch
trial would plausibly carry, the X-65 name recirculates in discussion of this case alongside Switchblade.

The program is real and the weight class is documented. What is **not** documented is any deployment near
Provo: there is no recovered airframe, no wreckage, no radar or ADS-B return for a small UAS, and no
record of any glide path, drop point, or retrieval. Where people have modelled specific approach geometry
over Provo, they were modelling — not reporting an observation.

Some posts go further and speculate that such a platform **was the shooter** or **masked a ground shooter**.
That is speculation with nothing under it, and it competes with far better-supported routes: the
[Mic](/Mic/overview) shaped-charge line, which is where the majority of citizen investigation has actually
landed, and the [alternative kill mechanisms](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Alternative_Kill_Mechanisms) page.
