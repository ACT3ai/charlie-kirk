# N1098L and the Drone Question Over UVU

Two things happened in the same sky on **September 10, 2025**, and they are two different records.
**[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)** — a **U.S. Army [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES)** ISR jet, a Bombardier Global 6500 —
is recorded on **public ADS-B** making extremely low, slow passes near **Utah Valley University**, before and after
the shooting. Separately, witnesses and posters reported **drones over the campus** that day. This page keeps them
apart: what the flight data actually documents, what the drone reports actually are, what connects them, and which
records would settle it. Nothing in the public record places a drone under that airframe, and the Army has published
no mission explanation for the passes.

## What the flight record documents

These figures come from the **ADS-B** record itself — ADS-B Exchange and FlightRadar24 history for the tail, plus
the [recovered traces](/Planes/Flight-Data-Recovery/overview) — not from any single commentator:

| Pass | Altitude / speed as recorded | Timing vs the shooting |
|------|------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Morning pass** | ~**600–650 ft** AGL, ~**118 kt** | roughly **3 hours before** (~9:16 AM MT cited) |
| **The shooting** | — | **12:23 PM MT**, [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) shot at UVU |
| **Afternoon pass** | ~**204–335 ft** AGL, ~**129 kt** | roughly **20–25 minutes after** (~12:48 PM MT cited) |

**ICAO:** A0299E · **Callsign:** AXEL10 · **Departure Sept 10:** Biggs Army Airfield (KBIF). The passes put the
aircraft within roughly **1–2 miles** of campus, the second of them out over **Utah Lake**.

That is the documented part, and it is not small. A Global 6500 does not normally sit at two to three hundred feet
and near its stall speed a couple of miles from a major campus event — and it did so **twice on the same day**,
bracketing the killing. The flight profile stands on the flight data alone.

## Army air-launch program context

[The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/army-wants-to-test-air-launched-drones-from-a-global-express-business-jet)
reported Army interest in **air-launched drones from Global Express–class jets** — the same airframe family as HADES.
That is a real, publicly reported program interest, and it is the reason the question gets asked at all. It
establishes **doctrine**, not this flight: it does not place a drone under this aircraft on this day, and it should
not be read as if it does.

## What the airframe itself shows

The most substantive check on the drone-launch question came from **[@TJPHager](/Influencers/x/tjphager)**, who
examined the **ME-11B** configuration and found the airframe carried **interior mission equipment only** — no
external pylons, hardpoints, launch rails, or recovery gear of the kind an air-launch-and-recovery platform would
have to carry. He also cites an alternate crew roster (Cooper Brown, Michael Reynolds, Sarah Kline). That refutation
is the strongest thing anyone has put on the record about the aircraft's physical fit-out, and it points away from
this jet as a drone platform.

**No FOIA release** has settled the crew manifest, the mission orders, or the fit-out on that specific date.

## The drone reports over UVU are a separate record

Drone sightings around the campus that day come from other named witnesses, phone video, and posters — a body of
reporting that exists independently and is examined on its own pages:

- **[Military Drones on Video](./Military_Drones_On_Video)** — streak-and-fast-object claims from phone footage, still unverified.
- **[TPUSA Production Drone](./TPUSA_Production_Drone)** — the one drone anybody admits flew: media B-roll before the event.
- **[UVU Security & No Drones](./UVU_Security_No_Drones)** — the university runs a drone program and did not use it to cover the event.
- **[Switchblade & DARPA Claims](./Switchblade_DARPA_Claims)** — the small-UAS platforms that get named in discussion, with no record placing either at UVU.

Nothing ties those sightings to N1098L except that both are in the same airspace on the same afternoon. Treat the
two as separate until a record joins them.

## The records that would settle it

If N1098L was flying an **ISR tasking** over Orem — and a HADES jet at that altitude and speed is not doing anything
else — then **records of that afternoon exist**: the tasking order, the sensor product, the crew manifest, the
mission log. None of it has been produced, to this investigation or to the prosecution. The presence of an Army ISR
jet over the homicide window is a **[Proof Intel Services](/Proof_Intel_Services/N1098L_HADES_Over_UVU)** indicator
on its own terms, and it is the request for those records — not any claim about what the jet carried — that this
page ends on.

### Focus of this page

**The documented N1098L flight profile near UVU on Sept 10, 2025, the separately reported drone sightings over the campus, and the ISR records that would connect or separate the two.**

### Claims and discussions circulating (do not treat as proven)

Whether the Army ISR passes and the campus drone reports are the same event. The flight profile is in the public ADS-B record; the drone reports rest on witness accounts and phone video; the airframe check points away from a launch platform; no released record joins them.
