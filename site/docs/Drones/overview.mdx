# Drones

Start with the [Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index). Drone claims here range from **documented
ADS-B flight paths** to **unverified weaponized-UAS theories**. **We do not claim any operator used a
drone to kill [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** unless citing a court record or attributed allegation. Drone activity is tracked here because aerial platforms bear on where any device came from on the day Charlie Kirk was killed — the wider question handled in [Cause of Death](/Cause_of_Death/overview).

The drone angle matters because **independent researchers treat airspace as evidence** — not a sideshow.
**[N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview)** [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) passes, **[Counter-UAS tests at Provo airport](/Proof_Intel_Services/Provo_Airport_Counter_UAS)**
the same week, and **fast objects on phone video** all sit in the **same Sept 10 window** as the shooting.
If any **small UAS** provided overwatch, strike capability, or **acoustic cover**, the rooftop **.30-06**
story may be incomplete (see [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview)).

**Documented vs. disputed:** **[UVU campus police did not fly security drones](/Drones/UVU_Security_No_Drones)**
per AP-aligned reporting, while **[TPUSA](/TPUSA/overview) later acknowledged** that a **media production drone** flew before the event
— contradicting earlier **"no drones"** statements attributed to Turning Point figures such as [Tyler Bowyer](/People/tyler-bowyer). **[Frank Turek](/Drones/TPUSA_Production_Drone)** reportedly
warned Charlie about **building exposure** after reviewing that feed. Military threads (the [N1098L low passes](./N1098L_Air_Launched_Drones) and the
[Switchblade/DARPA platforms](./Switchblade_DARPA_Claims)) rest on **public ADS-B and program documents** — the
flight profile is in the record, no released mission order explains it, and nothing places a drone under that jet. **[Israeli quadcopter](./Israeli_Armed_Quadcopter)** and **[Butler PA parallel](./Butler_PA_Drone_Parallel)**
theories are **citizen hypotheses** — real Gaza-documented tech, **unproven Utah application**.

Read **[Investigation Index](./Investigation_Index)** first, then **[N1098L over UVU](./N1098L_Air_Launched_Drones)** +
**[TPUSA Production Drone](./TPUSA_Production_Drone)** for the strongest mix of **data and admissions**, then
**[Military Drones on Video](./Military_Drones_On_Video)** for citizen sightings. Weaponized theories last.
Disclosure targets — **feed recipients, FAA authorizations, N1098L manifests** — are in
[Charlie Kirk Investigation Laws](/Fix/overview).

## Public commentary

Public X/Twitter and hearing-adjacent commentary continues to discuss the drone angle. The recurring themes for **Drones** are attributed claims and reported allegations, not findings by this website.

Related public coverage includes the July 2026 preliminary-hearing reporting (defense DNA/ballistics challenges and media exhibit rules under Judge Graf) and [Candace Owens](/Influencers/podcasts-candace-owens) / TPUSA succession-donor threads where they intersect this area. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) remains **charged, not convicted**. This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime or knew of the assassination in advance.
