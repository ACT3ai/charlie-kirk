# RØDE Mic

This page documents a reported allegation, not an established finding. Citizen investigators argue that the **RØDE wireless lavalier microphone** clipped to [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview) on **September 10, 2025** sits at the front end of a hardware chain that — according to their posts — runs through defense- and DoD-connected suppliers. Nothing below is a court finding, and [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) remains charged, not convicted, in an official case still framed as a domestic lone-suspect shooting. Every accusatory claim here is attributed to the source that made it.

## The Microphone on Kirk's Shirt

According to citizen-investigator analysis, the device on Kirk was a **RØDE Wireless PRO** (also cited in some posts as a RØDE Wireless GO II) — a professional wireless lavalier system consisting of a small clip-on transmitter, a receiver at the mixing board, and a capsule microphone. Reporting describes the transmitter as roughly **two inches by two inches**.

Master-file notes that circulate among researchers add physical dimensions: the Wireless PRO TX is listed as approximately **44 × 45.3 × 18.5 mm**, with an internal LiPo battery and sealed housing. The exploding-mic theory holds that this transmitter is the physical object investigators focus on because it is:

- Commercially common, so it would not draw attention at a sound check.
- Large enough internally to conceal a very small device, per that theory.
- Fitted with a **magnetic clasp** that analysts such as **[Jon Bray](/People/jon-bray)** argue could act as a projectile if a charge inside the housing detonated.

The mechanics of that theory are documented elsewhere. This page is narrower: it follows the claim that the mic and the materials around it trace back to a **defense/intel supply chain**, which is why the topic appears in the US Intelligence Assisted section. For the full device analysis, see [RØDE Wireless PRO — Full Analysis](/Mic/rode-wireless-mic) and the [exploding mic overview](/Mic/overview).

## MagClip GO and Under-Shirt Placement

Investigators flag an anomaly in how the mic was worn. According to their posts and measurement notes preserved in the investigation file, the RØDE transmitter was routed **under Kirk's shirt** rather than clipped on the outside — a departure from what they say was the standard TPUSA event setup visible in prior outdoor footage. Proponents argue that an under-shirt placement would both conceal a device and, if one were present, position it against the body.

The clasp system is repeatedly identified as a **MagClip GO**: a magnetic sandwich in which a small external clip (notes cite roughly **35.1 × 19.7 mm**) sits on the outside of the fabric while a neodymium magnet (notes cite roughly **26 × 17 mm**) holds the transmitter housing from the inside. Pre-event multi-camera stills, according to the same notes, show a **rectangular bulge** on the upper right chest where the 18.5 mm-deep housing pushed fabric outward, with the MagClip visible as a small dark rectangle. Post-event stills are said to show the clasp still present while the surrounding case deformation has vanished — the shirt lying nearly flat. Proponents treat that change as evidence the housing was no longer intact under the fabric.

This is a reported interpretation, not a confirmed forensic finding. It is included because the chain of custody of the hardware is what ties the hardware discussion to the supply-chain claims below. We assume that every TPUSA employee and every audio-visual contractor working at UVU that day had no knowledge of any explosive that may have been in the mic, even if they handled the mic.

Pre-event photos show an object in the front pocket of [Butch Hibbs](/People/butch-hibbs) that looks about the size of a **RØDE wireless mic receiver**. We do not claim that it is one. We do not claim he knew anything ahead of time or that he was involved in any way. We note only that whatever was in his pocket could continue to be investigated.

## The DoD / Defense Supply-Chain Tie

The reason this hardware discussion lands in an intelligence-assisted framing is a set of **defense-contract claims** that citizen investigators attach to the mic theory. According to a Las Vegas paralegal posting as **[@mistressdivy](/Influencers/x/mistressdivy)**, who says she has handled DoD procurements, **USASpending.gov** shows a contract to **[Accurate Energetic Systems (AES)](/Mic/AES)** for **"miniaturized-XS demolition charges, anti-personnel"** — described in researcher notes as **contract N0016425PJ538**, a **Navy / NSWC Crane** award, valued at about **$440,494** (popularized on X as roughly **$425k**), signed in **spring 2025** with delivery reported for **August 25, 2025**.

Researcher notes cited in the master file add:

- The award was, they say, the only **"XS"** device contract to AES since records began.
- It reportedly carried a high-hazard classification and allowed additive/3D-printed manufacturing on a tight timeline.
- [@mistressdivy](/Influencers/x/mistressdivy) flagged it as unusually **short-term**, a **single-bidder** simplified acquisition, and placed **off** the large indefinite-delivery vehicles AES normally uses.

She is explicit that "correlation doesn't mean causality" and asks others to verify or correct the record. Investigators have concluded that the device at Kirk's microphone appears to have come from this DoD contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges, delivered two weeks before September 10. See the [DoD contract analysis](/Mic/DoD_Contract) for the full walkthrough.

## The Heber City to Nashville Flight (Reported)

The supply-chain claim continues with a flight. Charlie_Kirk.txt notes that on **August 25, 2025** — the reported AES delivery date — a **rare private flight departed Heber City, Utah, for Nashville, Tennessee** (described as the closest major airport to the AES facility), with **no return flight logged**. Some posts ask whether that flight could have carried a device or a courier; nothing ties any named person to the flight.

Every step of this is an open question raised by independent researchers, not a verified movement of any physical object. It is documented so readers can see the full shape of the alleged hardware chain: vendor contract, then delivery date, then unreturned flight, then the mic worn at UVU. Related aircraft context lives under [Planes & Flight Analysis](/Planes/overview).

## The Factory Explosion That Followed

The chain the theory draws does not end at the event. The AES facility in **McEwen, Tennessee**, suffered a catastrophic explosion on **October 10, 2025** — roughly a month after the assassination — destroying Building 602 with reported worker deaths. Proponents cite this as removing the people and records that could confirm the contract claims. The official explanation for the blast is an industrial accident; no court has examined whether it was anything else.

The explosion is documented on this hub's [AES Factory Explosion](/US_Intelligence_Assisted/aes-factory-explosion) page and on the [Mic section's AES page](/Mic/aes-explosion-factory) in fuller detail.

### Named critics, and the investigators' answer

A few named accounts argue against the device conclusion. Their arguments are kept here, attributed, with the answer investigators give:

- **[@irinamanna114k](https://x.com/irinamanna114k)** (July 11, 2026) states there is "nothing of fact about exploding microphone," that a tiny mic blast cannot produce deep neck cavitation and a crushed vertebra, that video still shows the mic "fully intact" on the Freedom shirt, and that Robinson's texts, Discord admissions and identification are "direct evidence." *Investigators' answer:* a directed micro-charge focuses its energy rather than spreading it, as the pager charges in [Operation Grim Beeper](/Israel/Operation_Grim_Beeper) showed; the pre-event housing bulge disappears after the blast while only the external clasp remains, which is not what an intact unit looks like; and the texts and Discord material are themselves disputed ([faked confession](./faked-confession)) and do not explain the shirt expanding outward first. See the [Mic hub](/Mic/overview).
- **[@alexboge](/Influencers/x/alexboge)** (July 9, 2026) argues that shaped charges need standoff distance and rigid fixation; that a ~32 g RØDE Wireless PRO on fabric would accelerate *away* from the body under Newton's third law; and that PETN-class charges leave flash, smoke, burns and torn fabric, which multi-angle 4K footage does not show. *Investigators' answer:* the transmitter sat under the shirt in a magnetic MagClip stack pressed against the chest, which is the fixation; the outward shirt expansion and the housing and battery debris are what a unit blown outward would leave; a gram-scale charge under fabric in full daylight gives little visible flash; and the burn evidence is the [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and the [charred neck fragment](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic), while the clothing and residue were [removed before any public test](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview).
- **[@angertab](/Influencers/x/angertab)** (Matt Tardio) and other veterans argue that charge diameter is limited by the mic's width, that a poor standoff to the neck would dissipate a shaped jet, and that a real PETN detonation would have been visually obvious. *Investigators' answer:* the wound needs a gram-scale directed charge, not a military shaped jet, and the [Canon XA55 audio](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) and the [frame-by-frame shirt expansion](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) are the detonation signature that was captured. ## Fragment and Transport Claims

A separate cluster of posts focuses on debris rather than the shirt bulge alone. Investigation notes and Candace Owens–linked SUV photography are cited for **glass/tempered-face fragments** and electronic pieces on the floorboard of the vehicle that transported Kirk, framed by some accounts as consistent with a shattered RØDE housing rather than a single rifle round. Master-file notes also describe a **blue-solder-mask PCB** fragment alleged in the left collar area — something proponents say cannot leave a sealed transmitter without catastrophic case failure.

X posts in mid-2026 continue to ask, in substance, why there is "Rode Mic glass in the car" if the official story is a single rifle round with no secondary device. Other accounts speculate that recovered metal or polymer fragments labeled generically in early reports could be housing shrapnel rather than bullet jacket. None of that is laboratory-confirmed in sources cited here.

The transport vehicle itself is a second evidence problem. Cover-up pages track claims that the SUV was later **destroyed or melted** — which, if true, would remove a primary location where glass and electronic debris were allegedly photographed. That is why mic-hardware questions and [signs-of-cover-up](/US_Intelligence_Assisted/signs-of-cover-up) evidence-disposal questions travel together.

None of these fragments have been authenticated in a public laboratory report cited here. They remain open physical-evidence questions that sit beside the [Cover-Up](/CoverUp/overview) section and the [Mic](/Mic/overview) forensic cluster.

## Why the intelligence-assisted reading focuses on the mic

A rifle-only domestic case does not need a DoD energetics vendor, a MagClip under-shirt stack, or a Tennessee plant disaster to be coherent. The intelligence-assisted reading focuses on the mic because that is the only everyday object on Kirk's body that:

1. Sits against the chest/neck zone where video shows the first fabric disturbance;
2. Is small, sealed, and **commercially unremarkable** (ideal cover for a micro-device if one existed);
3. Has a documented **magnetic clasp system** that holds the housing flat against the body under the shirt;
4. Sits, in citizen procurement research, one hop away from a **DoD mini-charge contract** and a **deadly plant explosion**.

That is why researchers who reject the lone-gunman package treat the RØDE chain as a possible **hardware pathway for state-capable assistance** — the defining theme of this Level 2 section — rather than as a random audio-gear footnote. ## Dual-mic and "second redundant mic" claims

Several citizen analysts emphasize that September 10 may have been **unusual in Kirk's own AV pattern**. Posts attributed to **[@GoldenOne1980](/Influencers/x/goldenone1980)** and Candace Owens commentary claim Kirk was running **two microphones** — a standing or primary path plus the RØDE lav — and that this dual setup was rare or unique for him outdoors. In the exploding-mic theory, the "second" or "redundant" lav is the one that mattered: the under-shirt unit closest to the body.

If true, the question investigators ask is about the hardware itself, not the people who handled it: where the transmitter units came from before they reached the venue, and whether a unit could have been altered upstream in the supply chain. That is why this page sits next to [Fort Huachuca](/US_Intelligence/Fort_Huachuca/overview) "signals assembly" leads rather than only under pure audio geekery. Nothing indicates anyone on the security or AV team knew of any device.

Dual mics can be ordinary redundancy at a large outdoor event; investigators' point is that the redundant unit is the one routed under the shirt, against the body, which is why they want the equipment purchase and inventory records produced.

## Why Investigators Conclude It Was the Exploding Mic

The **official narrative** remains a domestic lone-suspect **gunshot wound to the neck** from a .30-06 rifle, and posts about the July 6, 2026 preliminary hearing say the autopsy lists GSW to the neck. Citizen investigators have largely concluded instead that Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive at his microphone. The main reasons:

* **The shirt and fabric move first** — [video frame analysis](/Mic/mic-explosion-video-analysis) shows the shirt expanding outward, spherically, before the neck wound appears.
* **The audio separates two blasts** — the [Canon XA55 recording](/Mic/Canon_XA55_Audio) reportedly shows a louder detonation at the tent distinct from a distant muzzle report.
* **Eyewitness veterans called it an explosion** — [Rob O'Neill and Gen. Blaine Holt](/Mic/rob-oneill-navy-seal-testimony) said it looked like an explosion, not a rifle wound.
* **No exit wound, and an energy mismatch** — a .30-06 at that range would be expected to exit and destroy tissue; see [six forensic physics phenomena](/Mic/six-forensic-physics-phenomena).
* **The mic was routed under the shirt** — [unlike prior events](/Mic/AV_Team_Mic_Setup), placing the transmitter against his chest and neck.
* **Physical debris** — a [burnt object in the SUV](/Mic/burnt-object-rode-transmitter) and a [neck fragment that may be charred plastic](/Mic/neck-fragment-charred-plastic).
* **The residue evidence was removed, not tested** — dirt, clothing, the SUV and the necklace were [hauled, cleaned or seized](/Mic/Explosive_Residue_Coverup/overview) with no public residue test.
* **Means existed** — an [AES contract for miniaturized anti-personnel charges](/Mic/DoD_Contract) sits on the same timeline.

Full evidence hub: **[Microphone — The Exploding Mic Theory](/Mic/overview)**.

In the government's model the lav is ordinary event AV, AES is an unrelated industrial tragedy, and the fragments are bullet fragments. Investigators answer that the official model fails the shirt dynamics, the speed of scene disposal, and the closure of the foreign lane, and that no fragment has been publicly shown to be bullet jacket rather than housing material. This page's role is to keep the **mic + MagClip + AES + flight** chain visible so it cannot be memory-holed while the rifle narrative monopolizes headlines.

## Open Questions

1. What is the **authenticated make/model** of the transmitter on Kirk (Wireless PRO vs GO II) from inventory or purchase records?
2. Which inventory records document the **MagClip GO** and the under-shirt stack issued the morning of September 10?
3. Can primary **USASpending / award PDFs** for N0016425PJ538 be published with end-use and delivery confirmation?
4. Are any **mic fragments** from the shirt, table, or transport SUV forensically catalogued and DNA/ballistically separated from rifle debris?
5. What **chain-of-custody records** exist for the transmitters, batteries, and MagClips from purchase to the event?
6. Will the **autopsy fragments** be released for independent testing, so investigators can check whether they are bullet jacket or transmitter-housing material?
7. Were spare transmitters, batteries, or MagClips **inventoried after** the event, and were any sent for testing?

These disclosures are exactly the kind of records the [Fix](/Fix/overview) proposals aim to compel. Until they are produced, the official record cannot answer the RØDE-centered supply-chain evidence investigators have assembled.
