# Suspect Before Actions

## Overview
This directory contains information about actions taken by suspects before the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) incident.

## Goal
To investigate the pre-incident actions of all suspects, including [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)'s possible recruitment by intelligence agencies, the Israel/Egyptian aircraft's arrival and positioning, and the surveillance aircraft's deployment, and to assess the argument citizen investigators make that these show planning and coordination behind Charlie Kirk's assassination. This includes analyzing communications, movements, and preparatory activities that those commentators argue show an international, intelligence-run operation rather than a lone-actor attack. Their conclusion is unproven.

All of it is examined backwards from September 10, 2025, the day Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University; the competing accounts of [how he died](/Cause_of_Death/overview) shape which pre-incident actions look meaningful.
