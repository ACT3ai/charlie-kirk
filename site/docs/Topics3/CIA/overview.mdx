# CIA

## Overview
This directory contains information and analysis related to [CIA](/US_Intelligence/cia) involvement in the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) incident.

## Goal
To examine the claims raised in public commentary about a CIA role in Charlie Kirk's assassination — in particular the theory that rogue factions within the agency coordinated with Israeli and Egyptian intelligence after Kirk's criticism of Israeli influence in U.S. politics. No evidence establishes any of this, and it is documented here as an unproven allegation. This includes examining what commentators allege about the agency's connection to the [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) surveillance aircraft and drone operations, and their argument that Tyler Robinson served as a patsy. Nothing here is a finding that any agency or any person did any such thing. The agency-role question also depends on mechanism: an operation of the kind alleged fits far better with the view that Charlie Kirk was killed by [a planted explosive device](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie) than with a lone rifleman.
