# Other

## Overview
This directory contains miscellaneous information and evidence that doesn't fit into other specific categories. It exists because Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025 and many loose evidentiary threads never entered the [state's court case](/court/overview) — leaving the [mechanism of death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) itself among the open questions.

## Goal
To collect and analyze miscellaneous evidence and information relevant to the alternative theories raised about the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) assassination, including overlooked details, unclassified information, and supplementary data that citizen investigators argue points to other perpetrators. Those theories are unproven and are documented here as claims, not findings. This includes investigating any evidence that commentators say does not fit the official narrative and that they argue points to broader intelligence coordination or cover-up efforts.
