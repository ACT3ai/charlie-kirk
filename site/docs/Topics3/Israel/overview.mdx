# Israel

## Overview
This directory contains information about Israeli connections to the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) incident. Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025, and while the state has charged Tyler Robinson alone, the [proceedings against him](/court/overview) have not addressed the foreign-intelligence questions gathered here.

## Goal
To examine the claims raised in public commentary alleging Israeli intelligence ([Mossad](/intelligence/Mossad)) involvement in Charlie Kirk's assassination — including the motive those commentators attribute to it after he publicly criticized Israeli influence in U.S. politics and called for the unredacted release of Jeffrey Epstein's files. This includes examining what those commentators allege about coordination with Egyptian intelligence, Israeli weapons technology (CornerShot), and the use of diplomatic immunity. These are unproven allegations, not established findings, and no evidence presented here shows that any intelligence service carried out an operation on American soil.
