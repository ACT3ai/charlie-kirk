# Drones

## Overview
This directory contains information about drone activity related to the [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) incident. Any drone theory has to answer the same question the rest of the site works on — how Charlie Kirk was killed — and the majority citizen reading favours an [explosive device at the podium](/Mic/overview) over any airborne weapon.

## Goal
To investigate the 16 drones observed at the scene and their connection to the [N1098L](/Planes/N1098L/overview) [HADES](/US_Intelligence/US_Army_HADES) surveillance aircraft, and to assess the theory advanced by citizen investigators that Charlie Kirk's assassination was carried out using drone technology rather than a traditional sniper rifle — an unproven claim. This includes analyzing drone capabilities, flight patterns, and what those commentators allege about their deployment and about plausible deniability through the [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) patsy narrative.
