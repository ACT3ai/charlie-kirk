# Miscellaneous Evidence Leads

These leads appear across multiple sections of the investigation. They are **collected here** so that cross-cutting threads stay in one place.

This website does not assert as fact that any living person named here committed a crime or had any knowledge of or involvement in the assassination. Names appear only in the context of public discussion. Nothing here is a finding by this website.

## Sealed and Withheld Records

- **Digital-forensics warrants** reported sealed until **March 2026** — limits public review of devices, searches, and ballistics chain
- **UVU GRAMA** — DCNF obtained 112 pages; UVU acknowledges **additional unreleased** documents ([Pre-Shooting Warnings](/UVU/pre-shooting-warnings))
