# U.S. Intelligence Investigation Index

Citizen investigators argue U.S. intelligence agencies had **capability** (signals, ISR aircraft,
campus programs) and **institutional motive** to manage — not genuinely solve — the Kirk case. This
memo maps threads **without** asserting any agency ordered the assassination. The underlying fact is
not in dispute: Charlie Kirk was murdered in Orem, Utah on September 10, 2025, and the
[cause of that death](/Cause_of_Death/overview) is still contested by the citizen investigation.
