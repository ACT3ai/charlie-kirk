# AmFest Speaker Index by Year

The lineup is the falsifiable core of the AmFest thesis, so it is worth keeping as a plain list. Everyone named here is named for having **appeared at or been invited to a public conference** — nothing on this page alleges that any speaker did anything wrong, and no speaker is accused of any crime.

## AmericaFest December 2025 — Phoenix

The lineup was **announced by flyer on August 25, 2025** — sixteen days before Kirk was killed — and the event itself was held after his death. That split is the whole story of this year: **Kirk programmed it, and someone else ran it.**

Announced on the August 25 flyer, per master-file commentary on the graphic:

| Figure | Placement on the flyer as described |
|---|---|
| [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson) | Prominent, beside Kirk, turned toward him |
| [Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon) | Below Carlson |
| Donald Trump Jr. | Near the bottom |
| Jack Posobiec | Bottom right, described as the last face on the graphic |

Reported as speaking or featured at the event itself:

| Speaker | What is reported |
|---|---|
| [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson) | Told the audience that taking AIPAC money is an America-last policy and that criticizing AIPAC should not be labeled antisemitism — [detail](./tucker-aipac-speech) |
| [Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro) | Criticized Carlson and Kelly by name for not denouncing Owens's theories, calling the silence "cowardice" — [detail](./shapiro-amfest-2025) |
| [Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon) | December 19, 2025: "We are at war," and called Kirk potentially "the greatest American produced since the Revolution" — [detail](./bannon-we-are-at-war) |

Reported as **invited or floated but not confirmed on any published 2025 lineup**:

* **[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)** — per the September 9, 2025 text claim ([detail](./candace-invitation)). Whether an invitation was ever formally issued after Kirk's death is **not documented anywhere in this file**.
* **[Governor Spencer Cox](/People/spencer-cox)** — one circulated post objects to Cox being made "a guest speaker to any TPUSA events" after Kirk's death, arguing Kirk would have opposed it. That is the poster's opinion. Cox is a living person and this site makes no claim that he was involved in any crime.

## What is missing from this index

No authenticated **contract trail** is public for any year. No **booking-department record** of vetoes, donor requests, or rescinded invitations has been released. The lineups above come from published playlists, promotional graphics, and event coverage — which is enough to compare years, and not enough to prove why any single name moved. The document requests that would close that gap are on [Open Questions and Checklist](./open-questions-checklist).
