# Year-by-Year AmFest Timeline

Every AmericaFest and TPUSA summit date the investigation file touches, in chronological order. Dated items are drawn from the master file, the site's video index, and event coverage; interpretive items are marked as claims.

## 2023 — AmericaFest 2023

* **December 2023 — AmericaFest 2023.** The event itself is not a focus of the case, but the **"AmericaFest 2023" set banner** appears in the studio dressing behind [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) in a widely circulated clip of *The Charlie Kirk Show* in which he addresses political donors directly. The banner is what dates that clip; the clip is held in the [video archive](/Videos/overview) as a "Charlie's own words" record of a position he took publicly.
* **2023 onward — Bannon as a fixture.** [Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon) spoke at AmericaFest events across **2023–2025**, closing the 2024 event with a "Victory or Death!" call to action.

## 2024 — AmericaFest 2024 (December 19–24, 2024)

* **December 19–24, 2024 — AmericaFest 2024.** A six-day program. Speakers included **[Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson)**, **[Donald Trump](/People/donald-trump)**, **[Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro)**, **Glenn Beck**, **[Patrick Bet-David](/Influencers/podcasts-patrick-bet-david)**, **[Donald Trump Jr.](/People/donald-trump-jr)**, **Dr. Ben Carson**, **[Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon)**, and **Rob Schneider**, among others. TPUSA published the sessions as YouTube playlists, which is why this lineup is documentable rather than recalled.
* **December 19, 2024 (first night, closing speaker) — Carlson on Epstein.** Carlson reportedly discussed Jeffrey Epstein's alleged involvement in a blackmail operation potentially conducted on behalf of foreign intelligence services. Detail: [AmFest 2024: Tucker on Epstein](./amfest-2024-tucker-epstein).
* **December 8–14, 2024 — an adjacent anomaly.** In the same month, Google Trends screenshots circulated by researchers show a one-week spike in searches for **[Kathryn Nester](/People/kathryn-nester)** — later Tyler Robinson's court-appointed defense attorney — attributed to an Israeli IP. This has nothing directly to do with the conference; it is listed because commentators place both items in the same December 2024 window. Trends data does not establish who searched or why.

## 2025 — the year of the booking fight

* **July 11–13, 2025 — TPUSA Student Action Summit, Tampa, Florida.** [Megyn Kelly](/Influencers/podcasts-megyn-kelly) appeared with Kirk. On **July 13**, [Dave Smith](/Influencers/podcasts-dave-smith) debated [Josh Hammer](/People/josh-hammer) on the United States, Israel, Epstein, and Mossad — the public face of the intra-coalition fight. Detail: [July 2025 Summit](./sas-2025-tampa).
* **July 14, 2025 — Carlson speaks at the same event** and, in circulated accounts, is encouraged to "go all the way." Much of the commentary that calls this "AMFEST in Florida" is referring to this summer summit, not the December Phoenix event.
* **July 16, 2025** — A podcast episode titled *Inside the Turning Point USA Fallout* is published, documenting that the summit produced visible internal fallout.
* **July 20, 2025 — the anchor week.** Commentators date a cluster of Google Trends spikes to the same week Kirk platformed Carlson, and separately allege a **military/intelligence meeting** on this date. Detail: [The July 20, 2025 AmFest Week](./july-20-2025-amfest-week).
* **August 4–6, 2025 — the Hamptons donor weekend.** Per *Grayzone*-attributed reporting amplified by [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), one anonymous source says Kirk was pressed to **rescind Carlson's AmFest invitation**. **Bill Ackman** has said Kirk was at no time threatened. Detail: [The Fight Over Tucker's Invitation](./tucker-invitation-fight); full dispute at [Hamptons Intervention Meeting](/Timeline/hamptons-intervention-meeting).
* **August 25, 2025 — the AmFest December 2025 flyer drops.** Master-file commentary reads Carlson's prominent placement beside Kirk as deliberate. Detail: [The August 25, 2025 Flyer](./august-25-flyer).
* **September 9, 2025 — the Candace text.** Kirk reportedly texts that he will invite Owens to speak at AmFest. Detail: [September 9: The Candace Invitation](./candace-invitation).
* **September 10, 2025** — Kirk is killed at [Utah Valley University](/UVU/overview).
* **September 21, 2025 — the stadium memorial**, produced by the same vendor that produces AmericaFest. See [Funeral and Memorial Timeline](/Timeline/funeral-memorial-timeline).
* **December 2025 — AmericaFest 2025, Phoenix.** The first without Kirk. Carlson's AIPAC remarks, Shapiro's "cowardice" criticism, Bannon's "we are at war," the tent replica, roughly 30,000-plus attendance. Detail: [AmericaFest December 2025](./amfest-2025-december).

## 2026 — the disputes that trail the stage

* **Spring 2026 — authentication arguments.** Citizen accounts argued that TPUSA's public use of **AI voice cloning**, including Dream City Church / AmFest-style "Charlie from beyond the grave" content, makes unverified audio harder to accept at face value. Detail: [AI Voice and Memorial Tech](./ai-voice-and-memorial-tech). These are arguments about authentication standards, not findings that anyone faked anything.
* **June–July 2026 — the Israel-motive claim restated.** Carlson said publicly, "All of us who knew Charlie Kirk believe he was murdered because his views on Israel were changing." Attributed to Carlson; not a finding.

## Reading note

Two naming problems recur across every source on this subject. First, the summer **Student Action Summit** is frequently called "AmFest," which shifts events by five months. Second, "AmFest 2025" can mean either the December 2025 event or the summer 2025 summit depending on the writer. Where the master file is ambiguous, this section says so rather than guessing.
