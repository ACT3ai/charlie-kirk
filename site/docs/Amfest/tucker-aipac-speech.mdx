# Tucker's AIPAC Remarks on Stage

At **AmericaFest December 2025** in Phoenix — the first AmFest without [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) — **[Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson)** delivered remarks that became one of the highest-engagement AmFest clips in the investigation file. Circulating video and commentary summarize the on-stage argument as follows (paraphrase of widely shared coverage; check primary video for exact wording):

* Taking money from **AIPAC** is an **America-last** policy posture.
* **Criticizing AIPAC** should not be labeled **antisemitism**.
* Kirk had been **falsely attacked as antisemitic** for speaking out against AIPAC / related foreign-policy pressure.

Those are **political speech claims** made on a public stage. They are useful as **motive context** if accurate. They are **not** evidence of who fired a shot on September 10.

## Why this speech sits inside the AmFest case file

1. **Continuity of the fight Kirk programmed.** Carlson was on the [August 25, 2025 flyer](./august-25-flyer) beside Kirk after an alleged [August demand to rescind](./tucker-invitation-fight) his invitation. Hearing him attack AIPAC-labeling on Kirk's stage after Kirk's death is the opposite of a quiet purge — or it is a single allowed safety valve. Both readings circulate; the video is the datum.
2. **Falsifiability.** Unlike private donor threats, a main-stage speech is **archived, clipped, and timestamped**. Transcript requests are ordinary open-source work.
3. **The antisemitism-branding weapon.** Much of the pre-death pressure narrative is about who gets to define "antisemitism" inside the American right. Carlson's AmFest line is a direct counter to that weapon. See [Antisemitism Branding](/CoverUp/Antisemitism_Branding) and [Tucker Carlson Israel](/Israel/Tucker_Carlson_Israel).

## How X used the clip

High-engagement posts treated the AmFest AIPAC segment as:

* Proof Kirk's **America First** lane survived him (continuity reading).
* Proof the movement still needed **permission structures** to say what Kirk had already paid for saying (capture reading).
* Fuel for **"Israel killed Charlie"** reply spam. **This site rejects stating that leap as fact.** A speech about lobbying influence is not a homicide confession by any state or donor.

Accounts that recycle the Candace+Tucker AmFest pivot narrative (**@RealTheForce** and others) pair this speech with the claim that Kirk intended an **anti-Zionist / America Only** conference culture. Pairing is commentary. The speech itself is the primary object.

## What is not established

* Exact **verbatim transcript** held in this repo (use official livestream / TPUSA uploads).
* Whether any **donor contract** tried to bar AIPAC criticism after September 10.
* Whether Carlson's booking was **contested internally** after Kirk's death or was automatic because the August flyer already sold tickets on his name.
* Any causal link between this December speech and the September crime.

## Counter-reading

Carlson has made AIPAC and foreign-lobby arguments on **his own platforms** for years. Repeating them at AmFest is brand-consistent provocation that sells clips. TPUSA booking a star who draws youth audiences is **business**. Reading a December speech backward into a September murder requires the murder theory to already be assumed.

**Status note:** Tucker Carlson is alive. Nothing on this page alleges he committed or conspired in any crime. Criticizing a lobby is protected political speech.
