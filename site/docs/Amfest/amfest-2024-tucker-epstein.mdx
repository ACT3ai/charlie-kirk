# AmFest 2024: Tucker on Epstein

**AmericaFest 2024** ran **December 19–24, 2024** — nearly nine months before [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was killed. It matters to this investigation for one reason: it is the documented moment when the AmFest main stage was used to raise **Jeffrey Epstein as a foreign-intelligence blackmail question**, in front of a TPUSA audience, with Kirk's approval as the man who programmed the event.

## What is documented

* The event ran **December 19–24, 2024**, and TPUSA published the sessions as **YouTube playlists** — which is why the lineup and the fact of the appearances are documentable rather than recalled.
* **[Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson)** was the **closing speaker on the first night**.
* Per the site's video index and event coverage, Carlson **reportedly discussed Jeffrey Epstein's alleged involvement in a blackmail operation potentially conducted on behalf of foreign intelligence services**.
* The same conference featured **[Donald Trump](/People/donald-trump)**, **[Ben Shapiro](/People/ben-shapiro)**, **Glenn Beck**, **[Patrick Bet-David](/Influencers/podcasts-patrick-bet-david)**, **Donald Trump Jr.**, **Dr. Ben Carson**, **[Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon)**, and **Rob Schneider**.
* Bannon closed the 2024 event with a **"Victory or Death!"** call to action.

The characterization of Carlson's Epstein remarks is drawn from coverage summaries in the investigation archive, not from a verified transcript held here. The playlists are the primary source and should be checked directly by anyone building on this.

## Why investigators flag it

Two arguments get built on this appearance, and they should be kept separate.

**The weaker argument** is sequencing-as-motive: that Carlson said something about foreign intelligence on a TPUSA stage in December 2024, and Kirk was dead by September 2025. Nine months is a long gap and a conference speech is not a threat to anyone. This does not stand on its own.

**The stronger argument** is about **precedent and permission**. AmFest 2024 establishes that by December 2024 Kirk was already willing to put a speaker on his main stage to talk about Epstein and foreign intelligence services — which undercuts any account of 2025 that treats Kirk's Israel-critical turn as sudden, out of character, or invented after the fact by commentators. The direction the 2025 booking fight ran in was already visible on the 2024 stage.

## Where it connects

* **Carlson's continued presence.** Carlson appeared at the July 2025 TPUSA summit, was on the **August 25, 2025** December-AmFest flyer beside Kirk, and spoke at AmericaFest 2025 after Kirk's death. He is the one name that runs through every AmFest in this file. See [The Fight Over Tucker's Invitation](./tucker-invitation-fight).
* **The Epstein thread itself.** Whether Epstein's operation had a foreign-intelligence dimension is a separate investigation with its own body of claims, none of which this page adjudicates.
* **Bet-David's proximity.** The archive notes that Bet-David's AmFest 2024 appearance gave him **direct familiarity with the TPUSA network and its key personnel** in the months before Kirk's death — relevant to why his later commentary is treated as informed rather than distant. Network proximity is not participation in anything.

## Counter-reading

A conference books provocative speakers because provocative speakers sell tickets and generate clips. Carlson had been making Epstein and foreign-intelligence arguments in public for years before this booking, on his own platform, at scale. Nothing about the 2024 slot required special permission or signalled a new alignment — and reading it as the start of a chain that ends in a homicide requires assuming the chain, not finding it.
