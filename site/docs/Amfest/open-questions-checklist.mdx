# Open Questions and Checklist

This page is the **work order** for the AmFest section. Every item below is something a document, transcript, or sworn statement could settle. None of them requires assuming a foreign plot up front. **Tyler Robinson is charged, not convicted.** No item here is an accusation that a living person committed murder.

## A. Lineups and contracts

1. Authenticated **speaker shortlists and contracts** for AmericaFest 2023–2026 and SAS 2025.
2. Any **written veto, hold, or rescind** request naming [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson), [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens), [Dave Smith](/Influencers/podcasts-dave-smith), or Marjorie Taylor Greene.
3. Design **revision history** for the [August 25, 2025 flyer](./august-25-flyer).
4. Post-September 10 **delegation of booking authority** (board minutes, CEO directives, events SOP).

## B. Donor weekend and texts

5. Primary **date, venue, and guest list** for the August 2025 Hamptons / Bridgehampton gathering ([timeline](/Timeline/hamptons-intervention-meeting)).
6. Full export of the **private messages** Owens publicized (device extraction, not screenshots), including the "lost donor over Tucker" and "leave the pro-Israel cause" threads ([leaked texts](/Media/Leaked-Kirk-Texts)).
7. Ackman / Dillon / other attendees' **contemporaneous texts** they have been urged to release.
8. Any readout or log of a **Netanyahu–Kirk** call in the August window beyond public "invited to Israel" remarks.

## C. Stage content archive

9. Complete **main-stage transcripts** for Israel / AIPAC / Iran / Epstein segments at AmFest 2024, SAS 2025, AmFest 2025.
10. Official TPUSA **clip-boost logs** (which segments were promoted within 72 hours).
11. Verbatim files for Carlson's [AIPAC remarks](./tucker-aipac-speech), Shapiro's ["cowardice" remarks](./shapiro-amfest-2025), and Bannon's [December 19 war language](./bannon-we-are-at-war).

## D. Memorial and production

12. Approvals and budget for the [Prove Me Wrong tent replica](./murder-tent-replica).
13. **Mosaic Pro Events** (and subcontractor) contracts for AmFest, SAS, memorial ([Who Runs AmFest](./who-runs-amfest)).
14. Synthetic **Kirk voice** vendor contracts and disclosure policy ([AI Voice](./ai-voice-and-memorial-tech)).
15. Analytics vendors and retention policy for **memorial phone tracking** claims (Kolvet / 270k+ devices narrative).
16. Primary documents for any **Show Faith by Works** / Israeli-government contract alleged in X threads.

## E. Motive discourse hygiene

17. Separate **booking politics** from **homicide authorship** in every public brief — this checklist exists so the former can be proven without faking the latter.
