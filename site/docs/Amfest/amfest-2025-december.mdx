# AmericaFest December 2025

**AmericaFest 2025** in **Phoenix** is the first AmFest held without [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk). It is also the conference whose **promotional flyer dropped on August 25, 2025** — sixteen days before he was killed. That split is the whole story of the year: **Kirk programmed the public face of the event; someone else ran it.**

## Programming split: flyer vs stage

* **August 25, 2025** — TPUSA releases the December AmFest flyer. Master-file commentary places **[Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson)** prominently beside Kirk, with **[Steve Bannon](/Influencers/podcasts-steve-bannon)**, **Donald Trump Jr.**, and **Jack Posobiec** lower on the graphic. Detail: [August 25 Flyer](./august-25-flyer).
* **September 10, 2025** — Kirk is killed at UVU.
* **December 2025** — The event runs. Circulating attendance figures are on the order of **30,000-plus**. Main-stage moments that entered this investigation file:

| Speaker | Reported content | Detail page |
|---|---|---|
| **Tucker Carlson** | Criticizing AIPAC / taking AIPAC money as America-last; criticizing AIPAC should not be labeled antisemitism; referenced Kirk's experience being called antisemitic | [Tucker's AIPAC Remarks](./tucker-aipac-speech) |
| **Ben Shapiro** | Criticized Carlson and Megyn Kelly by name for not denouncing Candace Owens's theories; called the silence **"cowardice"** | [Shapiro at AmFest 2025](./shapiro-amfest-2025) |
| **Steve Bannon** | December 19, 2025 remarks including **"We are at war"** and praise of Kirk as potentially among the greatest Americans since the Revolution | [Bannon: We Are At War](./bannon-we-are-at-war) |

## Why investigators call it a natural experiment

Whatever one believes about the homicide, December 2025 is a **test of stage direction** after the founder's death:

1. **Did Israel-critical speech still reach the main stage?** Carlson's AIPAC segment, if accurately reported, says **yes** — at least for one headliner Kirk had fought to keep.
2. **Did the pro-Israel lane still dominate discipline language?** Shapiro's "cowardice" attack on other right-wing commentators says the **intra-coalition police function** remained live on the same stage.
3. **Did war-and-martyr framing take over the emotional center?** Bannon's "we are at war" and the [Prove Me Wrong tent replica](./murder-tent-replica) say the event became a **grief-and-mobilization** machine as much as a policy conference.
4. **Who decided?** Events executive control and production vendors are tracked on [Who Actually Runs AmFest](./who-runs-amfest). Leadership succession is a separate TPUSA thread.

Answering those four still does **not** identify a shooter. It tests the **"capture of the stage"** half of the conference-control motive story.

## What X treated as the story of the week

Post-event X discourse (2025–2026) clusters into predictable camps — logged here as **discourse**, not findings:

* **Continuity camp:** Carlson still spoke; therefore "Israel didn't capture AmFest."
* **Capture camp:** Owens not confirmed; Shapiro centered; tent-as-merch; claims Erika "replaced" America-First headliners — therefore the org reversed Kirk. See recycle posts from accounts such as **@RealTheForce**.
* **Optics camp:** Focus on the [tent replica selfies](./murder-tent-replica), merch timing, and donor-floor sponsorships as tasteless or strategic.
* **Groyper / Fuentes track:** Attacks Carlson for insufficient ethnonationalism after AmFest exchanges with Shapiro-lane arguments (**@groyper_quant** and related clips).
* **Motive leap camp:** Treats AmFest lineup politics as proof of foreign homicide. **This site rejects stating that leap as fact.**

Broader motive discourse dump: [Murder Motive and AmFest on X](./murder-motive-amfest-x).

## Counter-reading

A movement that just lost its founder will stage a **memorial conference**. War language, courage language, and a replica of the death tent can be grief theater without foreign direction. Shapiro and Carlson arguing on the same weekend is also **brand competition** between media empires that use TPUSA's audience as a prize. Measure speeches; do not invent a conspiracy from the existence of a conference.
