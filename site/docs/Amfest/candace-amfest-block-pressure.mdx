Among the names X treats as **speakers pro-Israel donors did not want on AmFest**, **[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens)** is second only to [Tucker Carlson](/People/tucker-carlson). ## Investigators' use

Assassination researchers use the Owens AmFest node to argue:

1. Kirk was **re-opening** the stage to the loudest Israel-critical woman on the right.
2. That re-opening, paired with Tucker and Dave Smith summer bookings, was **intolerable** to a funding base.
3. Therefore motive exists for someone who valued TPUSA's pro-Israel youth machine.

Motive existence ≠ perpetrator identity. **Tyler Robinson remains the charged suspect, not convicted.**

## Counter-reading

Kirk and Owens had a **publicly rocky** history; distance can be personal and brand-risk management, not a donor veto. Non-invitation after a founder's death can be **legal risk, grief politics, or simple scheduling**. Owens's own incentive is to center herself in the story.
