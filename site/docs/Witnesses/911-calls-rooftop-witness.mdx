# 911 Calls and the Rooftop Witness — "It Wasn't Tyler"

:::caution Charged, not convicted
**[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted**, and is presumed innocent. The
witness on this page gave an **interview, not sworn testimony**. The 911 and radio audio below is a
compilation posted to X; its chain of custody has not been published by any agency. Nothing here
states as fact that any living person committed a crime or acted improperly.
:::

A compilation posted to X on **September 13, 2026** by [@ProjectConstitu](/Influencers/x/project-constitution)
(Project Constitution) puts three kinds of first-hour audio side by side: **911 callers** at Utah Valley
University, **police dispatch radio**, and the man who says he filmed the figure on the Losee Center roof
before the shot — speaking, according to the post, the same account he has since given to
[Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens). That witness describes **all-black tactical clothing, a face mask,
and tactical movement** — not the t-shirt and jeans associated with the charged defendant.

**Source:** [@ProjectConstitu on X · 2026-09-13](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2099122738099011597)

## The 911 calls — the first voices after the bang

The opening minutes of the clip are callers to 911 from the courtyard and the parking lots. In the
audio, one caller says [Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk) was shot **in the neck**, that he was
**"rushed off in … a black SUV,"** and — asked whether they saw it happen — answers **"Yes, I saw
everything."** Other callers say only that they heard a gunshot and that everyone started running.

A father tells the call-taker his daughter has texted him "there's a shoot." The dispatcher says one
person has been reported injured, the school is on lockdown and buildings are being cleared, and advises
him not to text back, because a phone making noise could give away where she is hiding.

These calls matter for a simple reason: they are the least rehearsed record of the afternoon. They were
made before any press conference, before any charging document, and before any account of the day had
been settled on.

## The rooftop-video witness — "It was all tactical"

The centre of the clip is a man who says he filmed the figure on the roof. This site does not name him.
In his own words, from the audio:

> He was in our line of view for about 30 seconds … we saw him clear the edge of the building, and … he
> would go down, look around, and then run, stand up, and run, maybe 6 feet forward, and then go down, look
> around again … so it looked like a sort of a tactical movement, I would say, which is why I started
> recording.

He says he first assumed the person was **event security** heading toward the main tent, until the
movement with a long gun struck him as strange. His account of timing:

* His video is **about 20 seconds** long, filming only head and shoulders once the figure was in position.
* It took the figure **about 10 seconds** to get from the edge of his view to where he lay down.
* The shot came **about five seconds after he stopped recording**.
* The figure stayed down **maybe five more seconds**, then "stood up and absolutely sprinted off the roof."

On what the figure looked like:

> It was all tactical. He definitely had a face mask on. At the range we were at, we were about 175 yards
> from him … I couldn't tell race because all of his skin was covered, but I know he was wearing completely
> all black.

### What he told Candace Owens

The narrator in the clip — presented in the post as [Candace Owens](/People/candace-owens) — says the man
who filmed the lone video of the rooftop figure **told her he does not think Tyler Robinson is the
person**. According to her, when he reported the rooftop figure on campus that day, the outfit he described
was entirely different from the one attributed to Robinson: "Tyler was just supposed to be wearing a
t-shirt and some jeans … he says, no, he was in tactical gear."

She adds that the witness has experience with firearms and teaches people to shoot, and that he says the
rifle he saw **does not match** the one presented in court. In her words, if he is called in the case, "he's
going to testify and say, no, that doesn't look like the gun. That doesn't look like the person." She also
states that there is **no video showing Tyler Robinson firing the weapon or taking aim** at Charlie Kirk.

These are the witness's statements as relayed in an interview. He has not testified under oath in
[State v. Robinson](/court/hearing-witnesses), and his video and campus report have not been released by
any agency. For how his description fits the wider clothing dispute, see
[Black Clothing vs Jeans](/Roof/black-clothing-vs-jeans) and [Tactical Roof Run](/Roof/tactical-roof-run).
The state's narrative of the weapon is on [Mauser .30-06 Rifle](/Gun_Bullet/Mauser_30_06_Rifle).

## Dispatch radio — "all black … black tactical helmet"

The radio section of the clip carries the look-out descriptions broadcast in the first minutes. As
rendered in the post, one transmission describes a suspect **"all black, black long gun, black tactical
helmet, a black mask, possibly a tactical vest,"** and another unit places the person **"on top of the
building on the far north side, just east of the library … jeans, black shirt, black mask, long rifle."**
Units report **one shot** and **one victim**, and staging at the front of the school **by the roundabout**.

The machine transcript below renders some of these words differently ("possibly wearing a tactical mask in
jeans," "black vests"), which is normal for compressed radio audio. Readers should listen to the clip
rather than rely on either written version. The same radio traffic is laid out minute by minute on the
[Dispatch & Radio Scanner Timeline](/Timeline/dispatch-radio-scanner-timeline), and the case for
subpoenaing the full record is on [Subpoena the Dispatch and CAD Record](/court/ai_attorney/dispatch-and-cad-records).

## "Clear direction not to interview"

Later in the radio audio, a unit says that, per the FBI, officers have been given **"clear direction not
to interview"** until federal agents arrive, and another unit acknowledges "we were not going to
interview." A further transmission says the FBI wants officers to stand by with a man at a downtown café
until agents reach them. That man was a private individual who has not been charged with anything in this
case; he is not named here.

An instruction to hold interviews for federal investigators can have ordinary procedural reasons. What it
also means is that the first local officers on scene were told not to take statements from witnesses —
which is the context for why so few first-hour witness accounts exist in any public record. See
[Witness Interviews Blocked](/CoverUp/Witness_Interviews_Blocked) and
[FBI Witness Coordination](/FBI/Witness_Coordination).

## "He did not go in for an autopsy"

In another exchange, an officer reports having made a records request and being told that Charlie Kirk
**did not go in for an autopsy** and that **the death certificate was signed by the doctor at the
hospital**. The officer is asked to add a note to the case documenting that. For the wider record, see
[No-Autopsy Claims](/Medical/Autopsy/no_autopsy_claims) and [Death Certificate](/Medical/Autopsy/death_certificate).

## The side channel — Signature or Duncan

The clip closes on hospital-security radio: an officer is requested to stage at the emergency-room bay,
and the traffic says **Charlie's wife is coming to land** and that **"Jess"** (rendered "Jesse" in the
post) is going to pick up a car for her. One speaker says she is landing at **Signature**; another says
they were told **Duncan**, and they decide to "play it by ear." The audio does not say which airport or
FBO either name refers to. For the separate reconstruction of where Erika Kirk was that day, see
[Erika Kirk's September 10 Whereabouts](/Planes/following/Erika_Sept10_Whereabouts).

## Why this clip matters

The post's argument is that the earliest audio from September 10 — callers, dispatchers and the one person
who says he filmed the rooftop figure — describes a **masked, all-black, tactically moving** person, and
that this description never became part of the public case. This site's own reading of the record, set out
across the [Roof](/Roof/overview) and [Proof Not Tyler](/Proof_Not_Tyler/overview) sections, is that the
figure on the roof and the harm at the stage are separate questions, and that citizen investigators on X
have by a wide majority concluded Charlie Kirk was killed by an explosive rather than a .30-06 round — the
state's narrative. A rooftop witness who says the figure he filmed does not match the charged defendant, in
clothing or in rifle, bears directly on both questions.

Every item in this clip can be checked against records that already exist: the 911 recordings and
call-taker notes, the CAD log and radio recordings, the witness's own video and his campus report, and the
records request the officer describes about the autopsy. None of those records has been released.
