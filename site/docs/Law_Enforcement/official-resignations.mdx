# Official Resignations

This page tracks the reported cluster of resignations among officials connected to the **[Charlie Kirk](/People/charlie-kirk)** case. It is presented as a noted pattern and an open question — not as proof of any wrongdoing by any named person.

## Washington County Sheriff Nate Brooksby

According to **[@SteveCameronPr1](/Influencers/x/stevecameronpr1)** and others, **Washington County Sheriff [Nate Brooksby](/People/nate-brooksby)** resigned, with no stated reason, after handling the surrender of the charged suspect, **[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)**. His role in the surrender is covered on the [Washington County Sheriff](./washington-county-sheriff) page.

**Status:** Alive

## UVU President Astrid S. Tuminez

Per the same sourcing, **UVU President [Astrid S. Tuminez](/People/astrid-s-tuminez)** reportedly resigned the same year. The shooting occurred on UVU's campus, which is what places her departure within this reported pattern.

**Status:** Alive

## A Related FBI Personnel Change

The research notes flag a personnel change that is a **dismissal/reassignment rather than a resignation**, but that researchers group into the same pattern of timing. Per the notes, decorated FBI agent **[Mehtab Syed](/People/mehtab-syed)** was **quietly dismissed as head of the Salt Lake City FBI field office** around **August 4, 2025** and replaced by **[Robert Bohls](/People/robert-bohls)** — the same period [Judge Tony Graf](/court/judge-tony-graf) was sworn in to preside over the Robinson case. This is attributed reporting, presented as a noted coincidence of timing rather than proof of anything, and it asserts no wrongdoing by any named official. The court-side detail is covered on the [Prosecution & Court Proceedings](./court-proceedings) page.

## Why This Is Framed as a Question

The pattern that researchers point to is the timing: officials directly connected to the event or its aftermath leaving their posts within the same period. This is attributed to **[@SteveCameronPr1](/Influencers/x/stevecameronpr1)** and others and is presented as a noted cluster worth questions.

Resignations happen for many reasons, and no public statement has tied these departures to the case. This page asserts no wrongdoing by Sheriff Brooksby, President Tuminez, or anyone else named. Both are living officials, and the reported facts — that each resigned in the relevant period — are presented as an open question, not a conclusion.


## Citizen Investigator Claims on X — Brooksby Cluster (March 31, 2026)

On **March 31, 2026**, multiple high-engagement X posts clustered **Sheriff Nate Brooksby's resignation** with a reported **ATF inability to match** the recovered bullet fragment to the **Mauser** rifle tied to the charged suspect. **[@EvanAKilgore](/Influencers/x/evanakilgore)** wrote that Brooksby, "who helped Tyler Robinson to turn himself in, just resigned… after 30 years," and that "the ATF was also allegedly unable to conclusively match the bullet," adding sarcastically that it was "surely a coincidence." **[@BowesChay](/Influencers/x/boweschay)** framed the same day as a court-filing bullet non-match plus a "sudden" resignation. **[@daviddunn177](/Influencers/x/daviddunn177)** posted "We have questions" with similar pairing.

These posts treat **temporal clustering** as suspicious. Temporal clustering is **not proof** of causation or guilt. Brooksby is a living official; his resignation is a **public personnel fact** reported in mainstream and social media; any suggestion he resigned to avoid scrutiny is **commentary**, not a court finding.

Counter-narrative color also exists: **[@brenda11831](/Influencers/x/brenda11831)** amplified a **Police1** account in which Brooksby credited retired colleague **Mike Mitchell** with enabling a calm surrender because "the family trusted him." That official framing emphasizes **de-escalation**, not concealment.
