This page is about one map and one timing claim attached to it. The map is the
widely-circulated annotated aerial of the [UVU](/UVU/overview) campus that news
outlets and citizen researchers both use to explain the September 10, 2025 event
geography. The timing claim is a reply posted on X in June 2026 arguing that two
September 11 events — the announced recovery of the rifle in the wooded area, and
a trespass arrest inside the taped-off scene — happened on the same morning.

## What the map shows

The map is an overhead satellite view looking across the eastern edge of the
campus, with red numbered markers and white arrows drawn on top. The legible
labels are:

- **"Charlie Kirk"** with a marker box on the west side, at the courtyard where
  the event was held.
- **"Where shot was taken"** — marker **7**, on the structure adjacent to the
  courtyard.
- **Losee Center**, **Parking garage**, and **Campus Drive**, which orient the rest
  of the sequence.
- Markers **6** and **8** on the roof and sidewalk immediately below it, and a long
  arrow east from **8** toward marker **9**.
- **"Wooded area"** — marker **9**, on the east side of the roadway, backing onto the
  residential block.

The sequence runs right to left and back again: markers **2**, **3** and **4** come in
from the residential streets and parking on the east, cross toward the parking
garage, and continue to **5**, then to the roof at **6**/**7**, then down and east to
the wooded strip at **9**. That is the movement path used in the mainstream
walkthrough of the case, not an alternative theory — the same numbered graphic
appears in press coverage of the campus geography.

What the map is useful for is narrow but real: it shows that the **wooded area at
marker 9 is a short distance from the roof at marker 7**, on the far side of a
roadway, and that it backs directly onto residential lots rather than sitting deep
in open country. Anything found there was found within a couple of minutes' walk of
the shot position and within sight of houses.

## The same-morning claim

On **June 29, 2026**, **[@Mia_Stretch](https://x.com/Mia_Stretch/status/2071688818802266425)**
posted this map as a reply to **[@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2071678483839754580)**.
Her post reads, in full:

> "Russell Kim Kennington was arrested on the same morning (September 11, 2025)
> that authorities announced finding the weapon in the wooded area
> That connects to Morgan Vandagriff home."

The reply she was answering asked why
[Russell Kennington](/distraction_people/Russell_Kennington) — described in that
thread as having been at the scene in medical dress — was released and had the
[charges dropped](/Suspicious/Trial_Legal/kennington-charges-dropped) without a
public explanation.

**The checkable half.** The timing element can be checked against documents already
covered on this site. The Utah court paperwork circulated in July 2026 puts
Kennington's arrest at **11:30 AM on 09/11/2025**, with a
[Provo District Court](/court/overview) release order signed at **3:35 PM the same
afternoon**. The public announcement that the rifle had been recovered from the
wooded area also came on the morning of **September 11, 2025** — see
[Gun Discovery Sequence](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Gun_Discovery_Sequence) for how that
recovery unfolded after the earlier K-9 searches. So the two events do fall on the
same morning. That is a coincidence of timing, and this page records it as exactly
that. It is not evidence of a connection between them, and no agency or court has
stated that there is one.

**The unverified half.** The second sentence — that the wooded area "connects to
Morgan Vandagriff home" — is **unsourced as posted**. No supporting record, address,
document, or reporting was offered with it, and no independent source connecting
that name to this case was located. This site therefore does not map, locate, or
identify any private residence in connection with the wooded area, and makes no
suggestion that any resident of the surrounding neighborhood had knowledge of or
involvement in the shooting. The claim is recorded here only because it was made
publicly, and it is recorded as unverified.

## What this settles and what it does not

Maps can rule geometries in or out. This one supports two modest conclusions: the
rifle-recovery location was close to the shot position, and it was adjacent to
occupied homes rather than isolated. Both are relevant to the standing question of
how the weapon was not located during the earlier searches of that strip.

What the map cannot do is establish intent, sequence of custody, or any
relationship between the people named in the thread. [Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson)
has been charged, not convicted. Russell Kennington was arrested for trespass and
released the same day, the charges were later dropped, and this site makes no claim
that he committed any crime at UVU or had any knowledge of the assassination.
Cross-check this map against [UVU Campus Maps](/UVU/maps),
[Possible Shooter Locations](/Suspects/Shooting_Locations), and the
[Timeline](/Timeline/overview) before drawing anything from it.
