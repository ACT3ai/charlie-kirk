# Dating the Ground-Side Roof-Jumper Video

:::caution An open provenance question, not a finding
Nobody on this page establishes when the ground-side roof-jumper video was filmed. What is
documented here is that its **claimed date changed** as it circulated, and that at least one
account that followed it from the start says so publicly. Treat the date as unresolved.
:::

The clip usually called the **ground-side roof-jumper video** — the view from below of a figure
coming off the corner of the [Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) roof — is one of the most-shared
pieces of footage in this case. It is the ground-level counterpart to the enhanced rooftop
sequences catalogued under
[The Roof Jump Without Hesitation](/Videos/Vid_UVU_Venue/Vid_Roof_Runner_Clips/Vid_Roof_Jump_Without_Hesitation_2082286)
and [Trained Roof Drop / Egress](./trained-roof-drop).

The question this page holds is narrower than any of those, and it comes before all of them:
**what day was it filmed?**

## The Claim: The Date Changed As It Spread

[@realhonestash](/Influencers/x/realhonestash) (Honest Ash) posted the following on
**September 8, 2026**, reproduced in full:

> I remember this story. When the ground-side video of the roof jumper first came out, it was
> claimed to be from before 9/10. Now it's all over as being from 9/10.
>
> I was shocked when [@RealCandaceO](/People/candace-owens) shared it not long ago as being
> filmed on 9/10. Is the source of the video still available?

That is an attributed recollection from an account that has been circulating scene footage since
the first week, not a documentary finding. But it is a specific, checkable assertion with two
parts: that the video's **first** attribution was to a date **before** September 10, 2025, and
that its **current** attribution is to September 10 itself. Both cannot be right.

The second half of the post — *"Is the source of the video still available?"* — is the part that
matters most for anyone trying to settle it. A clip's original upload carries its own date, its
own caption, and often its own metadata. Once it has been screen-recorded and re-uploaded a few
hundred times, all three are gone. This site has repeatedly found that the original post is the
only thing that answers a provenance question, and that originals go missing — see
[Rooftop Video Reportedly Trimmed (Claims)](/CoverUp/Rooftop_Video_Trimmed) and the
[Censorship](/Censorship/overview) section.

## Why "Before 9/10" Is Not An Idle Alternative

The reason a pre-September-10 date is even plausible is the story shown in the screenshot above.

On **September 12, 2025**, two days after the assassination, **Derrick Evans**
([@DerrickEvans4WV](/Influencers/x/derrickevans4wv)) posted a report that reached roughly
**170,000 views, 2,805 likes and 561 reposts**. His post, in full, as he wrote it:

> BREAKING: A Utah Valley University (UVU) student contacted the FBI after the assassination of
> Charlie Kirk.
>
> The student reported seeing a man resembling the person of interest near the roof of the Losee
> Center twice in the weeks before the assassination.
>
> The shooter is believed to have fired a single shot from the roof of the Losee Center, which
> overlooks the campus courtyard where Charlie Kirk was speaking.
>
> The student says they saw a tall, skinny, dark-haired man in dark clothes on the roof of the
> Losee Center on two separate occasions.
>
> The most recent sighting was at noon on the Wednesday a week before the shooting.
>
> The student found the man's presence on the roof unusual. A professor attempted to speak with
> the man, but he was ignored.
>
> After seeing the FBI's photos of a person of interest in connection with the shooting, the
> student reported the sightings to the FBI tipline.
>
> The student has NOT heard back from the FBI regarding the tip.

This is a second-hand account of an anonymous student's tip, relayed by an X account. Nobody
named in it has been identified publicly, and the FBI has not confirmed the tip exists. It is
recorded here as a reported claim.

### The Text In The Image

The screenshot @realhonestash attached carries only the header and the first two paragraphs of
that post, with one line marked in red. Extracted by OCR, the visible text is:

> Derrick Evans · @DerrickEvans4WV · X.com
>
> BREAKING: A Utah Valley University (UVU) student contacted the FBI after the assassination of
> Charlie Kirk.
>
> The student reported seeing a man resembling the person of interest near the roof of the Losee
> Center **twice in the weeks before the assassination.**

The red underline is under that last clause. That is the whole argument of the post compressed
into one editing mark: **there were reportedly people on that roof on days that were not
September 10**, so footage of a figure on that roof is not self-evidently footage of the
assassination.

## The Dates That Would Have To Line Up

<div style={{clear: 'both'}} />

| Item | Date | What it would mean |
|---|---|---|
| Assassination | Wednesday, **September 10, 2025** | The date the video is now widely captioned with |
| "The Wednesday a week before the shooting" (reported most recent sighting) | Wednesday, **September 3, 2025**, around noon | A roof figure matching the description, one week early |
| Earlier reported sighting | Unspecified, "twice in the weeks before" | A second pre-event roof presence |
| Derrick Evans post relaying the tip | Friday, **September 12, 2025** | Two days after the assassination |
| @realhonestash raising the dating conflict | **September 8, 2026** | Roughly a year of circulation later |

If the ground-side video is from **September 3**, then a large share of the frame-by-frame
argument built on it — descent technique, clothing, timing against the shot — is being applied
to the wrong day, and the figure in it is not necessarily the figure in the
[roof timeline](./roof-timeline) at all. If it is from **September 10**, the pre-event sightings
are a separate and still-unanswered matter, and the tip in the screenshot describes a person who
was scouting the position in advance.

Either answer is significant. The one thing that is not available is the ability to skip the
question, which is what a caption does when it asserts the date without the source.

## What Would Actually Settle It

* **Recovering the original upload.** The first public post of the ground-side clip, with its
  own timestamp and caption. This is what @realhonestash is asking for.
* **Sun angle and shadow direction** in the footage, compared against the courtyard geometry —
  a noon September 3 shadow and a 12:23 PM September 10 shadow are close but not identical.
* **Foliage, signage, and construction state** at [785 College Dr](/Locations/overview) and the
  surrounding campus, which changed across those weeks.
* **The UVU camera record**, which would show whether a figure was on the Losee roof on
  September 3 at noon at all — material the [Fix laws](/Fix/overview) are written to force out.
* **Whether the FBI ever worked the tip.** The student reportedly never heard back. See
  [FBI Blocking Investigations](/FBI/overview).

None of these have been done publicly. Until one of them is, the correct description of this
clip's date is *disputed*, and this site labels it that way.
