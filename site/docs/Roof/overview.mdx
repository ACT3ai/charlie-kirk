## Why the Roof Is Its Own Section

The roof is the single load-bearing surface in the State's case. If the shot came from the Losee Center roof,
then the person on that roof is the person who killed [Charlie Kirk](/Charlie/overview), and every other question —
motive, associates, foreign involvement — hangs off identifying that figure. If the shot did not come from the
roof, or if the figure on the roof did something other than fire the rifle, the case reorganizes completely.
That is why the roof draws more frame-by-frame attention from citizen investigators than any other location in
the investigation, and why the material had grown too large to keep scattered across Timeline, Cover-Up, and
Law Enforcement alone. Late **July 2026** X traffic — Candace Owens roof releases, Sam Parker annotations, and
hundreds of amplify posts about crawl, flash, clothing, and helicopter tape — made a dedicated section
necessary.

## What the Disputes Actually Are

Almost none of the published objections dispute that *someone* was on a roof near the courtyard. They dispute
**sequence, geometry, clothing, discharge signatures, and identity**. Reported testimony places a screwdriver
past the railing and yellow tape at the access point; citizen review of **KSL helicopter** footage is said to
show neither. Surveillance frames put a runner into a **lateral crawl** behind a low roof lip, which raises
line-of-sight and "military training" arguments. Older and newer threads insist the figure is **all black /
black pants**, not jeans. Separate camps say there is **no muzzle flash or recoil**, that a **bright object**
was a pre-shot signal, that the roof person was a **decoy**, or that **other buildings** (Woodbury, Sorensen,
Sparks) also had figures. These are measurable, checkable claims once continuous footage and evidence logs are
public — not automatic proof of a multi-person conspiracy.
