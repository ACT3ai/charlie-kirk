# Pre-Shot Signal Claims

:::caution Speculative coordination claims
Nothing on this page establishes that any living person coordinated a multi-shooter or timed
device attack. **Bright-object / signal** claims come from citizen readings of compressed video.
They are open questions, not proven command-and-control evidence.
:::

Once Candace Owens–linked **Losee Center** roof footage circulated widely in **July 2026**, a
second layer of commentary appeared on top of the crawl and clothing debates: that the prone
figure **flashed a bright object** — phone, optic, mirror, or light — **just before** the public
shot time. Amplify posts framed it as a **signal** to other actors: stage team, second shooter, or
device trigger.

## What Circulating Posts Claim

A high-amplification example
([@DiligentDenizen](https://x.com/DiligentDenizen/status/2082247127745356187) quoting Candace
Owens footage):

> "The alleged shooter does a MILITARY crawl into position & appears to SIGNAL w a BRIGHT object
> just before taking the shot… WHO was the shooter SIGNALING?!"

Related threads say the figure **never took a shot**, only **flashed a signal**, then fled — a
variant of the [decoy](./decoy-no-shot-from-roof) theory
([@RS17Pg](https://x.com/RS17Pg/status/2082456431152771138): "Never took a shot. Flashed a
signal.").

Older commentary used "go signal" language without the same bright-object still: for example,
claims that a roof .30-06 was **decoy noise** that **signaled** a quieter shot from another
building, or that a patsy on the roof waited for a cue before believing he had fired a kill shot.

## Why Signal Claims Matter (If True)

A deliberate light or optic flash at T-minus seconds would imply:

* **Coordination** with at least one other party who could see the roof or a relay.
* That the roof figure's role might be **timing / distraction**, not sole lethal actor.
* That "lone wolf with a hunting rifle" is incomplete even if the roof person is correctly
  identified.

That is why the claim is popular in intel-service and multi-actor narratives. It is also why it
needs **frame-level proof** rather than a single amplify caption.

## Ordinary Explanations

Before treating a glint as a command signal, ordinary sources of a bright frame must be listed:

* **Scope or phone screen** catching sun.
* **Compression artifact** or sensor bloom on a dark jacket.
* **Reflection off gravel, vent metal, or railing**.
* **Accidental phone check** with no tactical meaning.
* **Mis-synced** highlight from a different second of the clip.

None of these require a conspiracy. Distinguishing them from a deliberate flash needs the original
file, not a re-encoded social clip.

## Open Questions

1. Can any **original camera file** show a bright object with stable screen coordinates across
   consecutive frames?
2. What is the **timestamp** of the alleged flash relative to 12:23:30 impact?
3. Is the bright pixel **on the figure's hand**, on the gravel, or on a building edge?
4. Do any **other cameras** (courtyard, tent, Woodbury) show a corresponding visual or radio cue?
