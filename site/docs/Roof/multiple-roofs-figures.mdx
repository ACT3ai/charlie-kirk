# Multiple Roofs and Other Figures

:::caution Unproven multi-position claims
Figures on other roofs — if real — can be workers, students, security, or coincidences. This page
documents **citizen claims** of multi-roof activity around the UVU courtyard. It does not
establish a multi-shooter team.
:::

The official public story centers on **one roof**: the **[Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center)**.
Citizen investigators argue that fixation is the problem — that **other buildings** around the
amphitheater also show dark figures, head pops, or crawling silhouettes the same morning, and that
the investigation never publicly cleared them with equal rigor.

## The Multi-Roof Claim

[@ProjectConstitu](https://x.com/ProjectConstitu/status/2075186943487344993) (July 9, 2026),
amplifying @Black_Scout analysis, is a clear statement of the thesis:

> Person in all black seen moving on the **Woodbury Building** roof ~**20 minutes before** the
> assassination… video proof of people on the roofs of the **Losee Center, Sorensen Center, and
> Sparks Center** that same day… "They had you staring at the wrong roof while unknown figures
> were moving across several others."

Earlier October **2025** threads already floated a **Woodbury** rooftop figure ~**17 minutes**
before the shot as a potential breakthrough for second-shooter or patsy theories.

Witness-facing posts also claimed **two people in all black at the bottom of stairs** to a roof
while another was on top ([@TwoSides444](https://x.com/TwoSides444/status/1976596253200019674)) —
support/security reading vs team reading, both unproven.

## Why Multiple Roofs Change the Geometry

If only Losee is manned, the case is a single perch ~**430 feet** from the stage (public figure
range). If **Woodbury** or another edge is occupied at T-minus 20 minutes:

* **Trajectory** arguments may need re-mapping ([Wound trajectory](/Gun_Bullet/Wound_Trajectory_Angle)).
* **Acoustic** direction claims gain alternative sources
  ([Acoustic split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split)).
* **Security failure** expands from "one unguarded roof" to "ring of roofs"
  ([UVU campus police](/Law_Enforcement/uvu-campus-police),
  [Security team](/Security_Team/overview)).
* The [black-clad early lead](/CoverUp/Black_Clad_Suspect_Dropped) may attach to a **different
  building** than the jeans/stairwell package.

## Relationship to Losee-Only Analysis

This section's other pages mostly assume the figure of interest is on **Losee** — run, crawl,
screwdriver, lip geometry. Multi-roof claims do not erase Losee activity. They ask whether Losee
was the **only** loaded position or the **decoy / primary / secondary** role in a larger layout.

## Counterpoints Worth Stating

* **Campuses have people on roofs** — HVAC, IT, photographers, students. A silhouette is not a
  rifleman.
* **Resolution at distance** turns backpacks and vents into "prone shooters."
* **Official case** may have cleared other roofs in unreleased reports; absence of a public
  briefing is not proof of a cover-up, though it is a transparency failure.
* **Crime-scene trespass** cases (e.g. people later entering taped areas) are not the same as
  pre-shot roof occupation.

## Open Questions

1. What **continuous multi-camera** coverage exists for Woodbury, Sorensen, and Sparks roofs for
   11:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m. on Sept 10?
2. Did bomb dogs, snipers, or UVU police **log roof checks** for each building that morning?
3. Can any figure on a non-Losee roof be **identified** (worker badge, student, contractor)?
4. Do **maps** used in court include alternate roof LOSs to the tent position?
