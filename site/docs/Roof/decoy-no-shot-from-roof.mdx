# Decoy / No Shot From Roof Claims

:::caution Charged, not convicted
**[Tyler Robinson](/People/tyler-robinson) is charged, not convicted.** Decoy theories are
**unproven hypotheses** in public commentary. This page documents them so readers can test each
link in the chain — not so anyone treats them as established fact.
:::

A large branch of roof skepticism does **not** deny that a person ran onto the
**[Losee Center](/UVU/Losee_Center) roof**. It denies that that person **fired the fatal shot**.
In that framing the roof is **theater**: a visible sniper story for cameras, radio, and later
prosecution packaging, while the actual kill mechanism was closer to the stage — or from another
building.

## Core Claim Bundle

As circulated on X (especially around Candace Owens roof releases in **July 2026**):

1. Footage does **not** show a full **rifle assembly** on the roof.
2. Therefore the **screwdriver** is not corroboration of assembly — some call it a
   **DNA plant** or "smoking gun" that evaporates without assembly video
   ([@duxxTales](https://x.com/duxxTales/status/2082262069181600248),
   [@SwannD199413](https://x.com/SwannD199413/status/2082259895621956018)).
3. The figure **throws or places a bag**, goes prone, then leaves with a bag — interpreted as
   prop behavior, not a bolt-action shoot cycle
   ([@RealTheForce](https://x.com/RealTheForce/status/2082293261893271912)).
4. [No muzzle flash or recoil](./no-muzzle-flash-or-recoil) at public shot time.
5. Line-of-sight and roof-lip geometry make a real shot from the final prone hard
   ([Line of Sight](./roof-line-of-sight)).
6. Therefore the roof person is a **decoy** (who may or may not be Tyler), and the government
   case "rooftop .30-06 lone actor" is incomplete.

## Assembly Timing vs Immediate Run

A separate official-side point cuts the other way. Commentators aligned with the prosecution
(example: [@JackPosobiec](https://x.com/JackPosobiec/status/2074720143301820429)) argue the figure
**runs immediately after the shot**, leaving **no time to disassemble**, which is why a
screwdriver left behind could still fit "assembled, fired, fled with rifle intact." That is the
opposite of "never assembled." The public video set is incomplete enough that both camps quote
different gaps.

[Sam Parker](/Influencers/sam-parker)'s sequence still assumes assembly at the screwdriver point
**before** the sprint and crawl — and then attacks that sequence as weird and uncorroborated by
the camera's field of view ([Tactical Roof Run](./tactical-roof-run)).

## Where the "Real Shot" Is Said to Be

Decoy claims almost always point off-roof:

* **Microphone / stage device** theories — [Mic](/Mic/overview),
  [Explosive mic](/Tyler_Robinson_Not_Assassin/explosive-mic-killed-charlie).
* **Acoustic two-event** — distant crack plus nearer boom
  ([Acoustic split](/Proof_Not_Tyler/Acoustic_Two_Event_Split)).
* **Other roofs** around the amphitheater — Woodbury, Sorensen, Sparks claims
  ([Multiple Roofs](./multiple-roofs-figures)).
* **Wound / energy mismatch** for a .30-06 neck hit from ~430 feet
  ([Gun & Bullet](/Gun_Bullet/overview)).

This page does not pick a mechanism. It records that the **roof-as-decoy** theory is the bridge
people use between "there is a roof guy" and "roof guy didn't kill Charlie."

## Counterpoints Worth Stating

* **Partial video is not zero shot.** Missing flash frames do not erase casings, DNA claims, or
  acoustic work — each of those is contested on its own pages.
* **Decoy still requires coordination.** A planned decoy is a multi-person operation; that is a
  high bar and needs positive evidence, not only gaps.
* **Confession / text / DNA package** (as publicly summarized) still exists; decoy theories must
  explain that package without circular "everything is planted" reasoning.
* **"No assembly on camera"** may mean "assembly happened off-frame," not "assembly never
  happened."

## Open Questions

1. Does any released continuous clip cover **the full prone interval** through public shot time
   without a cut?
2. What object is in the figure's hands or bag at entry vs exit — rifle, towel wrap, or soft bag?
3. How do **hearing exhibits** describe what the roof video shows at the moment of the shot?
4. If the roof shot is decoy noise only, what is the **court-admissible** alternative mechanism?
