## Audience

A reader who has already heard the headline — "Egyptian jets followed Erika Kirk
73 times" — from X, from a Candace Owens broadcast, or from a friend forwarding a
screenshot. They arrive with one of two prior beliefs and the graphic has to work
for both:

* The believer, who has heard 73 and treats it as a proven count of shadowing.
* The skeptic, who has heard Liz Wheeler's rebuttal — that "overlap" meant within
  100 miles and plus-or-minus three days, and that same-airport-same-day happens
  once — and has already written the whole angle off.

Both of them are scanning. Neither has read the 79-row table. Neither will.

What they are scanning for is the same thing: is there a pattern here, or is there
not. They want the shape, not the spreadsheet.

## What they should learn

The sentence the reader should be able to say out loud after four seconds:

> "The claimed overlaps run for three straight years and most of them do not hold
> up — but the ones that do hold up bunch together, on Charlie's side, at the end."

Not "it is all real." Not "it is all fake." The distribution is the finding.

## Conceptual framing

**A three-year seismograph with two traces, read against an audit.**

Time runs left to right. Two horizontal bands share one time axis: Charlie Kirk
and TPUSA above the line, Erika Kirk below it, mirrored, like a two-channel
recording. Each month is one bar. Bar height is how many claimed overlaps the
compilers logged in that month.

Then the second layer, which is what makes this an argument rather than a chart:
**every bar is filled according to whether an independent audit found the aircraft
was actually where the spreadsheet said it was.** Solid means confirmed. Hollow
outline means the audit put the jet on another continent that day.

The reader sees a long, noisy, mostly-hollow Erika trace running the full three
years — and a short, sparse, mostly-solid Charlie trace that wakes up at the end.

The picture argues the same thing the site argues: the wide claim collapses, the
narrow one does not, and confusing the two is how the whole thing gets dismissed.

## What we are educating on

1. That there is a claimed count, that it is a researcher tally from public ADS-B
   history, and that it spans January 2022 to October 2025.
2. That the count is overwhelmingly an *Erika* count — 55 of the 74 dated rows —
   and that the Erika side is the side with no published itinerary behind it.
3. That a hostile line-by-line audit checked the aircraft's position on each row
   and found 44 of 78 wrong, usually by a continent.
4. That what survives is small, concentrated, and Charlie-shaped: six dates pass a
   same-day test at a shared field, three of them Charlie Kirk, two of those in Utah.
5. That September 2025 is the densest surviving cluster, and it sits four days
   before the assassination.

## Perspective — what the reader currently cannot see

A number cannot show distribution. "73" hides the three facts that matter most:

* **Where in time they fall.** 73 sounds continuous. It is not — 15 of the 46
  months in the window have zero, and the tail of 2023 has a nine-month gap.
* **Whose side they are on.** 73 sounds like one pattern. It is two very different
  ledgers with very different evidentiary strength stacked into one figure.
* **Which ones held up.** 73 sounds audited. Most of it is not.

The graphic makes all three visible at once, which no sentence on the page can do.

## Polarity and scope

Both directions, deliberately, and the tension between them is the graphic.

* **Wide:** the full 46-month axis, edge to edge, showing three years of rhythm and
  the dead months inside it. This is the zoom-out that makes "73" look thin.
* **Tight:** a call-out lens on the last eight weeks — Jul through Sep 2025 — pulled
  out and enlarged at frame right, showing the surviving September cluster and the
  4 Sep Provo arrival / 10 Sep departure. This is the zoom-in that makes the
  remainder look dense.

Expressed visually by literal scale change: the main timeline is one continuous
low band across the full width; the September call-out is a boxed, magnified
excerpt with a thin leader line back to its position on the main axis. The reader
should feel the eye pulled from "three years of noise" to "one week that survives."

## Sizing — what is bigger and what is smaller

Driven by the order-of-understanding list below. Larger equals more important.

1. **The two-trace timeline** is the largest object on the canvas by a wide margin.
   It is the whole point; everything else annotates it.
2. **The title** is second — it has to survive being seen as a thumbnail.
3. **The September call-out lens** is third, and it is the only element allowed to
   compete visually with the main timeline. It is what the argument narrows to.
4. **The stat block** (74 / 19 / 55 / audit split) is fourth — legible but quiet.
5. **The legend** is fifth and deliberately small; the fill styles should be
   self-evident enough that most readers never look at it.
6. **The source line** is smallest. Present, honest, not competing.

The Erika trace is physically longer and busier than the Charlie trace, and that
asymmetry is left alone rather than balanced — the imbalance IS the information.

## Framing and placement

**Unframed / bleeding:** the main timeline band and its time axis. It runs nearly
edge to edge, deliberately breaching the margins on both sides, so the three-year
span reads as long. The background is unframed and continuous.

**Framed / boxed:** the September call-out lens (a hard-edged rectangle with a
visible border, thin leader lines back to Sep 2025 on the main axis), the stat
block (a light card, top right), and the legend (a small card). Framing marks
these as commentary about the timeline rather than part of it.

**Vertical order, top to bottom:**

* Title, upper left, on its own line.
* Subline directly beneath the title, smaller.
* Stat block, upper right, at title height — so the eye crossing the top of the
  image collects the headline and the numbers in one sweep.
* The **Charlie Kirk / TPUSA** band, bars growing UPWARD from the centre axis.
* The **centre time axis**, with year ticks 2022 / 2023 / 2024 / 2025 and light
  month gridlines.
* The **Erika Kirk** band, bars growing DOWNWARD from the centre axis, mirrored.
* The September call-out lens, lower right.
* Legend, lower left. Source line, bottom edge, full width.

**Horizontal:**

* Frame left is the beginning of time and the weakest evidence — 2022, all hollow.
* Frame right is the end and the strongest — September 2025, mostly solid.
* Left-to-right therefore doubles as weak-to-strong. That is not an accident and
  the design should lean on it.
* Band labels ("CHARLIE KIRK + TPUSA — 19 claimed", "ERIKA KIRK — 55 claimed") sit
  at frame left, inside their bands, so the reader knows which trace is which
  before reading a single bar.
* The 5 undated never-published rows hang off frame right, past the axis end,
  ghosted — literally outside of time.

## Order of understanding

Numbered by priority, most important first. Number 1 introduces the concept and
the issue; number 2 is the first thing the reader should actually understand.

1. **The concept and the issue.** Foreign-government jets are claimed to have kept
   landing where the Kirks and TPUSA were, for three years — and the count being
   broadcast has never been reconciled with an audit of it. Delivered by the title,
   the subline, and the shape of the two traces.
2. **This is three years, not a moment.** The axis runs Jan 2022 to Oct 2025 and
   there are 46 months on it, with real gaps. Delivered by the width and the ticks.
3. **There are two ledgers, not one, and they are wildly unequal.** 55 Erika, 19
   Charlie/TPUSA. Delivered by the mirrored bands and the band labels.
4. **Most of the claimed overlaps did not hold up.** The Erika trace is mostly
   hollow. Delivered by the fill styles, reinforced by the audit bar in the stat block.
5. **What did hold up is clustered, and it is on Charlie's side, and it is late.**
   Delivered by the solid bars at Apr 2023, Mar–May 2025 and Sep 2025, and the six
   diamond markers.
6. **September 2025 is the point of the whole thing.** SU-BTT on the ground at
   Provo from 4 Sep, departing 07:14 on 10 Sep. Delivered by the call-out lens.
7. **Five of the claimed overlaps were never published at all.** Delivered by the
   ghosted detached block off frame right — the last thing a thorough reader finds.

## Counterargument carried in the image itself

Required by `site/docs/Planes/following/CLAUDE.md` — every page carries its
counterargument, and so does every graphic that goes on one. In this design the
counterargument is not a footnote, it is the primary visual encoding: the hollow
bars ARE the rebuttal, drawn at the same scale as the claim. A reader who believes
the audit and a reader who believes the tally are looking at the same picture and
both find their case in it.

The source line additionally states the published overlap window (50–100 miles,
±3 days) so the reader knows the definition that produced the count, and states
that the Erika side has no published itinerary.

## What this graphic must NOT do

* Must not merge threads. This is the 18-month/3-year following pattern only. No
  N1098L, no drones, no HADES, no N888KG Wendover, no day-of ballistics.
* Must not show a single averaged or reconciled tally. 73 and 74 both appear.
* Must not depict Tyler Robinson, any suspect, any weapon, or the assassination
  itself. No blood, no crosshairs, no gore, no crime-scene imagery.
* Must not name or depict aircraft owners, crew, passengers or ground staff.
* Must not assert who tasked an aircraft.
* Must not imply the Erika count is verified.
