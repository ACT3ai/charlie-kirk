[Image text - infographic, image 1 of 3]: "Likelihood of Explosive Residue Transfer &
Detectability on Synthetic Fibers."
* TRANSFER - HIGH. "Handling or proximity to detonation readily transfers particles to
  synthetic surfaces. Fingerprint-level and post-blast residues common."
* TRAPPING IN FIBERS - HIGH. "Nylon, polyester, and auto carpet (typically nylon or
  polyester blends) are porous and fibrous - particles lodge between fibers and in the
  pile. Better retention than many smooth surfaces."
* TESTABILITY / RECOVERY - MEDIUM-HIGH. "Solvent extraction (acetone/methanol) + HPLC-UV
  or LC-MS recovers residues effectively. Nylon often shows higher recovery than cotton;
  polyester/auto carpet also workable. Detection at low ug/g to ng levels possible when
  properly sampled."
* Ranking strip: Nylon (high recovery) > Polyester / Auto Carpet (good-high) > Cotton (lower).
* Footer caveat: "Based on forensic studies of post-blast and handling residues (RDX, PETN,
